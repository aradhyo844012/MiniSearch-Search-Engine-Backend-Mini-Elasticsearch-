Load Testing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Load Testing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn virtual users, throughput, latency percentiles, and ramp-up.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a stress test that fills a stadium to see how the exits cope. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through capacity planning, finding bottlenecks, and validating autoscaling.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for testing unrealistic traffic and ignoring warm-up, then try k6 for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
