Database Constraints Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Database Constraints.

Do:
- Learn primary keys before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
- Test the case of preventing bad data early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: skipping constraints for speed.
- Ignore this: missing indexes on foreign keys.
- Ignore this: rules enforced only in the app.
- Adopt Database Constraints just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Database Constraints, a little patience with foreign keys pays off more than any clever shortcut.
