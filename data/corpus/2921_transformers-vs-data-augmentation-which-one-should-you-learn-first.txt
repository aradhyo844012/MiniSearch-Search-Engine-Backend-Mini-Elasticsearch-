Transformers vs Data Augmentation: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Transformers or Data Augmentation first. Both sit in the area of AI and Machine Learning, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other, and it is closely tied to self-attention and positional encoding. Data Augmentation, by contrast, is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize, and it is closely tied to transformations and label preservation.

Choose Transformers first if you care about language translation or text generation. Choose Data Augmentation first if you care about image models or text classification.

Think of Transformers as a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word, and of Data Augmentation as practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Transformers, avoid huge compute requirements. With Data Augmentation, avoid changes that alter the label.

A good habit for Transformers is to begin with small pretrained models. A good habit for Data Augmentation is to augment only the training data.
