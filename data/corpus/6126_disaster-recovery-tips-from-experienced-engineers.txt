Disaster Recovery Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Disaster Recovery regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit recovery point objective before anything else.
2. Do not skip recovery time objective; everything else builds on it.
3. Test the case of surviving regional outages early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
5. Be careful with this one: untested plans.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to meeting compliance needs as well.
