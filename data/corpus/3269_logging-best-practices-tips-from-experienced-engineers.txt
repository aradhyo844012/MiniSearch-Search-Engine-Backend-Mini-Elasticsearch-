Logging Best Practices Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Logging Best Practices regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of debugging production issues early, before the design hardens.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit structured logs before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Logging Best Practices so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: logging sensitive data.
5. Do not skip log levels; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to auditing actions as well.
