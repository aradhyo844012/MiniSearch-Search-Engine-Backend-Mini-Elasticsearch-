JWT Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of JWT.

- Definition: a compact token format that carries signed claims between a client and a server.
- Core ideas: header, payload and signature; claims; expiry; signing keys.
- Use it for: stateless API authentication; service-to-service calls; single sign-on.
- Watch out for: storing tokens in insecure places; skipping signature checks; very long expiry times.
- Tools: jwt.io, PyJWT, jsonwebtoken.
- Quick tip: practice header, payload and signature until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: JSON.
