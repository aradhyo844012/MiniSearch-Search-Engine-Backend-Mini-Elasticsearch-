The Pros and Cons of Package Managers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Package Managers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports installing system tools
- Makes keeping servers updated easier
- Helps with reproducing environments

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing sources that conflict
- Risk: ignoring signature checks
- Risk: upgrading without reading the changes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as installing system tools, and you can manage the risks, Package Managers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, update the package index before installing anything.
