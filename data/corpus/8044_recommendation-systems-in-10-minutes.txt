Recommendation Systems in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Recommendation Systems.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, ranking, and the cold start problem.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through streaming services, online shops, and news feeds.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for feedback loops and popularity bias, then try Surprise for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
