Why Graph Theory Basics Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Graph Theory Basics make better decisions, because Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them.

Three situations show why it matters. First, social networks. Second, maps. Third, dependency analysis. In each case, a solid grasp of vertices and edges and degree separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: mixing directed and undirected graphs, ignoring disconnected parts, and choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs.

Good tools exist, including NetworkX, Graphviz, and Gephi, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
