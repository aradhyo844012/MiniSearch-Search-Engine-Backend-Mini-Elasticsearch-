Why Apache Spark Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Apache Spark make better decisions, because Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines.

Three situations show why it matters. First, large ETL jobs. Second, machine learning on big data. Third, log analysis. In each case, a solid grasp of DataFrames and transformations and actions separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: data skew, too many small partitions, and collecting huge results onto the driver.

Good tools exist, including PySpark, Databricks, and the Spark UI, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
