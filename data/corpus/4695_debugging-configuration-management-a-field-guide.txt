Debugging Configuration Management: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Configuration Management, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: committing secrets, inconsistent defaults, and undocumented settings.
Step 3: revisit environment variables and config files, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use dotenv or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Configuration Management in work such as separating development and production come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
