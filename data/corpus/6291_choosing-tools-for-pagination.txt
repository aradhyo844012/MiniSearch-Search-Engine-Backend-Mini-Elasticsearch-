Choosing Tools for Pagination

There is no single best tool for Pagination. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

SQL LIMIT and OFFSET is a common first choice, especially for search results. keyset queries suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Django REST Framework is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle activity feeds well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: offsets getting slow on huge tables.

As a rule of thumb, prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data, no matter which tool you use.
