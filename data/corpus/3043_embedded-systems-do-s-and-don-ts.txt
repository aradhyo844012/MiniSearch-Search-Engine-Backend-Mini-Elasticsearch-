Embedded Systems Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Embedded Systems.

Do:
- Learn microcontrollers before anything else.
- Try C on a small example.
- Make it a habit to plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
- Test the case of appliances early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: memory leaks on tiny devices.
- Ignore this: no plan for updates.
- Ignore this: ignoring power use.
- Adopt Embedded Systems just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Embedded Systems, a little patience with firmware pays off more than any clever shortcut.
