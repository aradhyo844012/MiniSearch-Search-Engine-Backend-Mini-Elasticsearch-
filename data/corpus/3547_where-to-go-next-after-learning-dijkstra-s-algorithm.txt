Where to Go Next After Learning Dijkstra's Algorithm

You have met the basics of Dijkstra's Algorithm. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Greedy Algorithms, which belongs to the same area as Dijkstra's Algorithm (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of priority queue, distance table, edge relaxation or visited set, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around network routing with Python heapq, and note every problem you meet, starting with using it with negative edge weights.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Dijkstra's Algorithm to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
