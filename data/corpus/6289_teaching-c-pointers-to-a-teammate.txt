Teaching C Pointers to a Teammate

Explaining C Pointers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a street address written on a card instead of the house itself. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: C Pointers is variables that store the memory address of another value. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving dynamic memory allocation, naming addresses and dereferencing as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain pointer arithmetic in C Pointers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, dereferencing null pointers, and suggest this habit: set pointers to NULL after freeing them.
