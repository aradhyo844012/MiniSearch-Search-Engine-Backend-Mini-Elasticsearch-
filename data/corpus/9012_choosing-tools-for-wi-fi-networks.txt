Choosing Tools for Wi-Fi Networks

There is no single best tool for Wi-Fi Networks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Wi-Fi analyzer apps is a common first choice, especially for home internet. Wireshark suits teams that already work with similar technology, and router admin pages is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Wi-Fi Networks by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle office coverage well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Wi-Fi Networks, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: overlapping channels.

As a rule of thumb, use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase, no matter which tool you use.
