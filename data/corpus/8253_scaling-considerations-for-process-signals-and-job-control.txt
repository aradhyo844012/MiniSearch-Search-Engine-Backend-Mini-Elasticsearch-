Scaling Considerations for Process Signals and Job Control

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Process Signals and Job Control changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding SIGTERM and SIGKILL and getting stopping runaway programs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using kill -9 first and orphaned child processes, and measure with kill before changing anything.

Large scale: kill and trap and process groups move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around shutting down gracefully at this stage.

Before you scale, try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
