Where to Go Next After Learning Vector Databases

You have met the basics of Vector Databases. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about MongoDB Aggregation, which belongs to the same area as Vector Databases (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of embeddings, approximate nearest neighbors, distance metrics or metadata filters, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around recommendations with FAISS, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing embedding models.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Vector Databases to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
