The Trade-offs of Database Transactions

Every design choice has a cost, and Database Transactions is no exception. When people talk about Database Transactions, they usually mean groups of operations that succeed or fail together. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

On the positive side, Database Transactions is well suited to transferring money between accounts and placing orders. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: holding transactions open too long; ignoring isolation levels; forgetting to roll back on errors. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much atomicity and consistency matter to your project.
