Discrete Mathematics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Discrete Mathematics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Discrete Mathematics in your own words.
2. Describe sets, logic, relations, and proofs.
3. Give examples from algorithm analysis and database theory.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs. Then draw the grammar underneath every program on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is skipping proofs a problem? How does logic relate to relations? When would you choose Python sets or proof assistants?

Study habit: practice writing small proofs by hand.

When you feel ready, compare Discrete Mathematics with Information Theory and Entropy and write down three differences.
