A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Data Quality Checks

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Data Quality Checks.

Morning: Meera starts by reviewing a ticket about protecting dashboards. Before touching any code, Meera re-reads the notes on null checks to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Meera pairs with a teammate to explore uniqueness with Great Expectations. A small test exposes the problem of alerts nobody reads, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving validating ingestion. Meera sketches a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Meera writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to check data as early as it enters the pipeline.

Days like this are typical. Data Quality Checks is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
