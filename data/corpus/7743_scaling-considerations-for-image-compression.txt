Scaling Considerations for Image Compression

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Image Compression changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding lossy and lossless formats and getting web images right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for repeated recompression and the wrong format for graphics, and measure with ImageMagick before changing anything.

Large scale: transform coding and quality settings move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around photo storage at this stage.

Before you scale, pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
