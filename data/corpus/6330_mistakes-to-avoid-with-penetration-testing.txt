Mistakes to Avoid with Penetration Testing

Everyone learns Penetration Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: testing without written permission. It usually appears when someone skips scoping and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring the findings afterward. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of pre-launch security checks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: relying only on automated scans. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on reporting to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: get written authorization before any test.
