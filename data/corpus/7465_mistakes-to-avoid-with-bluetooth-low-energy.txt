Mistakes to Avoid with Bluetooth Low Energy

Everyone learns Bluetooth Low Energy partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring connection limits. It usually appears when someone skips advertising and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: weak pairing security. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of fitness trackers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming long range. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on pairing to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test with several phone models.
