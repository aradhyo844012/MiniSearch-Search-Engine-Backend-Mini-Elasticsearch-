AI Agents Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with AI Agents.

Do:
- Learn planning before anything else.
- Try LangGraph on a small example.
- Make it a habit to limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
- Test the case of research assistants early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: runaway loops.
- Ignore this: unchecked tool permissions.
- Ignore this: hard-to-debug behavior.
- Adopt AI Agents just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with AI Agents, a little patience with tool calls pays off more than any clever shortcut.
