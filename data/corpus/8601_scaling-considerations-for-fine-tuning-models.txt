Scaling Considerations for Fine-Tuning Models

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Fine-Tuning Models changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding pretrained weights and getting domain-specific language tasks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for overfitting on small datasets and forgetting general abilities, and measure with Hugging Face before changing anything.

Large scale: the learning rate and evaluation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around style adaptation at this stage.

Before you scale, start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
