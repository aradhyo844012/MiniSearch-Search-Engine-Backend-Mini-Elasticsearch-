Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with MQTT

This path is for a learner who has never studied MQTT before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks) and learn publish and subscribe and topics.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Mosquitto, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving sensor networks.

Stage 3, depth: study brokers and quality of service levels, then deliberately explore this problem: unsecured brokers.

Stage 4, application: apply MQTT to smart home devices, compare paho-mqtt with MQTT Explorer, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach MQTT to someone else, and then move on to Push Notifications.
