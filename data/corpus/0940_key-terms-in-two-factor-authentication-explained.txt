Key Terms in Two-Factor Authentication Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Two-Factor Authentication much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Two-Factor Authentication, they usually mean requiring a second proof of identity in addition to a password. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

one-time codes: a key term in Two-Factor Authentication. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
authenticator apps: a key term in Two-Factor Authentication. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
hardware keys: a key term in Two-Factor Authentication. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
backup codes: a key term in Two-Factor Authentication. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
