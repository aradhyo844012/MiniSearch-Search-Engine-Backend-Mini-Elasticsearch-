A Practical Checklist for Linked Lists

Use this checklist before you rely on Linked Lists in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers nodes.
2. Review the core ideas: nodes, head and tail pointers, singly and doubly linked lists, and pointer updates.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Linked Lists should accomplish.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on head and tail pointers.
5. Guard against this risk: poor cache locality.
6. Review how Linked Lists interacts with Heaps and Priority Queues.
7. Guard against this risk: losing the head pointer.
