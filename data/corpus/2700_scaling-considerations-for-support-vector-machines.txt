Scaling Considerations for Support Vector Machines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Support Vector Machines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding margin and getting text classification right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow training on large data and poor kernel choices, and measure with scikit-learn before changing anything.

Large scale: kernels and regularization move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around small high-dimensional datasets at this stage.

Before you scale, scale features and try a linear kernel first. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
