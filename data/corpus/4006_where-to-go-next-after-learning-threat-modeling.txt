Where to Go Next After Learning Threat Modeling

You have met the basics of Threat Modeling. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Encryption at Rest, which belongs to the same area as Threat Modeling (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of assets, attackers, trust boundaries or mitigations, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reviewing architecture with STRIDE, and note every problem you meet, starting with doing it once and never again.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Threat Modeling to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
