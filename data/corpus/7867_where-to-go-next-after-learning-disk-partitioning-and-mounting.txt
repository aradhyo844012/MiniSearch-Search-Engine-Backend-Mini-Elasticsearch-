Where to Go Next After Learning Disk Partitioning and Mounting

You have met the basics of Disk Partitioning and Mounting. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Cron Jobs and Scheduling, which belongs to the same area as Disk Partitioning and Mounting (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of partitions, file system types, mount points or fstab, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around separating data from the system with lsblk, and note every problem you meet, starting with wrong device names.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Disk Partitioning and Mounting to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
