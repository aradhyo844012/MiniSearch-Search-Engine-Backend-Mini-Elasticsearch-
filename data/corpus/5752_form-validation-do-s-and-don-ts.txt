Form Validation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Form Validation.

Do:
- Learn client-side checks before anything else.
- Try HTML constraint validation on a small example.
- Make it a habit to validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
- Test the case of sign-up forms early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: validating only on the client.
- Ignore this: vague error messages.
- Ignore this: rejecting valid but unusual input.
- Adopt Form Validation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Form Validation, a little patience with server-side checks pays off more than any clever shortcut.
