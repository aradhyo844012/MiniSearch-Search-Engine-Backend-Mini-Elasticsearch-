Teaching Prompt Engineering to a Teammate

Explaining Prompt Engineering to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: giving clear directions to a very literal helper. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving drafting content, naming instructions and examples as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain context in Prompt Engineering back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, vague requests, and suggest this habit: specify the output format and include one or two examples.
