Debugging MLOps Pipelines: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around MLOps Pipelines, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: untracked data changes, manual deployment steps, and no testing of data.
Step 3: revisit data versioning and training pipelines, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use MLflow or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving MLOps Pipelines in work such as repeatable model releases come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, version both the code and the data. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
