Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Fine-Tuning Models

This path is for a learner who has never studied Fine-Tuning Models before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Fine-Tuning Models is adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data) and learn pretrained weights and training data.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Hugging Face, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving domain-specific language tasks.

Stage 3, depth: study the learning rate and evaluation, then deliberately explore this problem: overfitting on small datasets.

Stage 4, application: apply Fine-Tuning Models to style adaptation, compare PyTorch with LoRA libraries, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Fine-Tuning Models to someone else, and then move on to Model Drift and Monitoring.
