A Practical Checklist for Data Visualization

Use this checklist before you rely on Data Visualization in a project.

1. Measure results before and after any change.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example explaining results.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Data Visualization should accomplish.
4. Guard against this risk: truncated axes.
5. Review the core ideas: chart types, axes and labels, color choices, and annotations.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers chart types.
7. Document your decisions in the project README.
8. Guard against this risk: too many colors.
