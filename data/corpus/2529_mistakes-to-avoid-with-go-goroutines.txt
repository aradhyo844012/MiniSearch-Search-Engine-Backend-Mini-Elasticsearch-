Mistakes to Avoid with Go Goroutines

Everyone learns Go Goroutines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: goroutine leaks. It usually appears when someone skips goroutines and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: data races. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of servers handling many connections. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unbuffered channels causing deadlocks. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on wait groups to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run your tests with the race detector turned on.
