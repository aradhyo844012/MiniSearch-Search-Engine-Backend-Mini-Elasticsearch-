Debugging End-to-End Testing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around End-to-End Testing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: flaky tests, slow runs, and brittle selectors.
Step 3: revisit browser automation and user flows, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Playwright or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving End-to-End Testing in work such as checking login and checkout flows come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, cover only the most important user journeys. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
