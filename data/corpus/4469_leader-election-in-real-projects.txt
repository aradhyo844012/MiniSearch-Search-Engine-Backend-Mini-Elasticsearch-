Leader Election in Real Projects

Reading about Leader Election is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: database primaries. Here, heartbeats drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against split brain.

Scenario two: job schedulers. This time terms or epochs matters most, and tools such as ZooKeeper help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: cluster managers. The key idea is candidates, and the biggest risk is flapping leaders.

Across all three scenarios, Leader Election stays the same idea: a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work. Only the details change.
