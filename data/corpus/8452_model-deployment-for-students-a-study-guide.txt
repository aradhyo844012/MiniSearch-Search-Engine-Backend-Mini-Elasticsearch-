Model Deployment for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Model Deployment.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Model Deployment in your own words.
2. Describe serving APIs, batch versus online inference, containers, and latency budgets.
3. Give examples from recommendation endpoints and fraud checks.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Model Deployment is making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application. Then draw moving a recipe from the test kitchen into the restaurant on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is training-serving mismatch a problem? How does batch versus online inference relate to containers? When would you choose FastAPI or TorchServe?

Study habit worth keeping: log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.

When you feel ready, compare Model Deployment with AI Agents and write down three differences.
