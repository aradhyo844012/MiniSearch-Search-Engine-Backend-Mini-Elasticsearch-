Scaling Considerations for Blue-Green Deployment

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Blue-Green Deployment changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding blue and green environments and getting zero-downtime releases right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for database schema mismatches and doubling infrastructure costs, and measure with AWS CodeDeploy before changing anything.

Large scale: rollback and database compatibility move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around quick rollbacks at this stage.

Before you scale, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
