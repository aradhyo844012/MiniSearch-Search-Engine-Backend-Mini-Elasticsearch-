Is Regular Expressions Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Regular Expressions, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text.

It is a good fit when your work involves validating input or searching logs, and when your team understands character classes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn quantifiers, or when you cannot manage these risks: catastrophic backtracking, patterns that are too greedy, and using them to parse HTML.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether groups and anchors are part of the answer. If they are, Regular Expressions is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with extracting fields from text and build patterns in small steps and test each one.
