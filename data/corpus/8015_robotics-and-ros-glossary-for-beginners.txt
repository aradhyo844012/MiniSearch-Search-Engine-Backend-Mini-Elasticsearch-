Robotics and ROS Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Robotics and ROS feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Robotics and ROS: building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.
sensors: a core term, most visible in warehouse robots.
actuators: a core term that beginners often confuse with nodes and topics.
nodes and topics: a core term that connects to control loops.
control loops: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ROS 2: a tool commonly used for drones.

Come back to this Robotics and ROS list whenever documentation for Gazebo uses a word you do not recognize.
