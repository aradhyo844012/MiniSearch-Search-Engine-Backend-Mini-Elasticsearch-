The Trade-offs of Zipf's Law

Every design choice has a cost, and Zipf's Law is no exception. When people talk about Zipf's Law, they usually mean the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

On the positive side, Zipf's Law is well suited to analyzing text corpora and sizing indexes. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: testing it on random text; reading too much into a perfect fit; ignoring small samples.
