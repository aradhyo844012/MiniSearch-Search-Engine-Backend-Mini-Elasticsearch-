Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Offline-First Apps

This path is for a learner who has never studied Offline-First Apps before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Offline-First Apps is apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns) and learn local storage and sync queues.

Stage 2, hands-on: install SQLite, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving field service tools.

Stage 3, depth: study conflict resolution and optimistic updates, then deliberately explore this problem: unresolved sync conflicts.

Stage 4, application: apply Offline-First Apps to note-taking apps, compare PouchDB with Room, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Offline-First Apps to someone else, and then move on to Push Notifications.
