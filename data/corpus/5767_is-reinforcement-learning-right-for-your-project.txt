Is Reinforcement Learning Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Reinforcement Learning, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

It is a good fit when your work involves game playing or robot control, and when your team understands agent.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn environment, or when you cannot manage these risks: badly designed rewards, slow training, and unstable results across runs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether reward and policy are part of the answer. If they are, Reinforcement Learning is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with recommendation strategies and start with simple environments.
