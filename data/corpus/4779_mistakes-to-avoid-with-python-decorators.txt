Mistakes to Avoid with Python Decorators

Everyone learns Python Decorators partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: losing function metadata. It usually appears when someone skips higher-order functions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: stacking decorators in the wrong order. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of logging calls. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hiding side effects. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on decorator arguments to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator.
