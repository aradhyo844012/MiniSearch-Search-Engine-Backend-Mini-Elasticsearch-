Teaching Mocking and Stubbing to a Teammate

Explaining Mocking and Stubbing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving isolating units, naming mocks and stubs as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain spies in Mocking and Stubbing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, over-mocking, and suggest this habit: prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
