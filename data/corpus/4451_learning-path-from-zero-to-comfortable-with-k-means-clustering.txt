Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with K-Means Clustering

This path is for a learner who has never studied K-Means Clustering before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (K-Means Clustering is an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points) and learn centroids and distance measures.

Stage 2, hands-on: install scikit-learn, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving customer segmentation.

Stage 3, depth: study choosing k and iterations, then deliberately explore this problem: choosing k blindly.

Stage 4, application: apply K-Means Clustering to image compression, compare NumPy with matplotlib, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach K-Means Clustering to someone else, and then move on to Reinforcement Learning.
