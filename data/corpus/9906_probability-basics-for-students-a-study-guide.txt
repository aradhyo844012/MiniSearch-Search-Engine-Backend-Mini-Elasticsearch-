Probability Basics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Probability Basics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Probability Basics in your own words.
2. Describe events, conditional probability, independence, and expected value.
3. Give examples from randomized algorithms and machine learning.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Probability Basics is the study of how likely events are to happen. Then draw predicting how often a dice roll will land on each face on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is confusing independent and exclusive events a problem? How does conditional probability relate to independence? When would you choose Python random or NumPy?

Study habit worth keeping: simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.

When you feel ready, compare Probability Basics with Linear Algebra for Programmers and write down three differences.
