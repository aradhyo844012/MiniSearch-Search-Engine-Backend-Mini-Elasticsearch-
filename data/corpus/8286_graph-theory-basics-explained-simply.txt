Graph Theory Basics Explained Simply

Here is Graph Theory Basics without the jargon: it is the study of nodes and the connections between them.

Think of Graph Theory Basics as a map of towns and the roads between them. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: vertices and edges, degree, paths and cycles, and connectivity. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with vertices and edges, then add degree.

People reach for Graph Theory Basics when they work on social networks, maps, and dependency analysis.

One practical tip is to draw a small example before writing code.
