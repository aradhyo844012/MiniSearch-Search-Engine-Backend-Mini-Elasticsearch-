Form Validation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Form Validation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: validating only on the client.
2. Reach for HTML constraint validation first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Zod.
3. Do not skip client-side checks; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
5. Test the case of sign-up forms early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to payment pages as well.
