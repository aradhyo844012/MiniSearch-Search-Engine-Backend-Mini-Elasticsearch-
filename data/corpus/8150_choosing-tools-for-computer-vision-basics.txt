Choosing Tools for Computer Vision Basics

There is no single best tool for Computer Vision Basics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

OpenCV is a common first choice, especially for face detection. Pillow suits teams that already work with similar technology, and scikit-image is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Computer Vision Basics by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle document scanning well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Computer Vision Basics, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: lighting changes.

As a rule of thumb, visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks, no matter which tool you use.
