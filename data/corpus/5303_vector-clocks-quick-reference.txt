Vector Clocks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Vector Clocks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: counters that let distributed processes track the order of events without a shared clock.
Key terms: logical time, per-process counters, the happened-before relation, and conflict detection.
Typical uses: detecting concurrent edits, replicated databases, and collaborative tools.
Risks: clocks that grow with many nodes, confusing them with wall-clock time, and never pruning old entries.
Tools: Riak, the Amazon Dynamo paper, and CRDT libraries.

Mental picture: each person in a group chat keeping a tally of messages seen from everyone.

Habit to build: compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
