Practice Exercises for Arduino Basics

These exercises build real understanding of Arduino Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how setup and loop functions and the serial monitor differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Arduino Basics and include the term sketches.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble reading sensors or driving motors and LEDs.
Exercise 4: Compare PlatformIO and Tinkercad for learning electronics and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: wiring errors. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Arduino Basics is a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices.
