Service Mesh Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Service Mesh regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Istio first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Linkerd.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit mutual TLS before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Service Mesh so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip sidecar proxies; everything else builds on it.
5. Be careful with this one: added latency and complexity.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to retries and timeouts as well.
