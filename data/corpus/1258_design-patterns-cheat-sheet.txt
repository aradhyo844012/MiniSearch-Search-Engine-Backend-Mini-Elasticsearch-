Design Patterns Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Design Patterns.

- Definition: reusable solutions to common problems in software design.
- Core ideas: singleton; factory; observer; strategy.
- Use it for: structuring applications; decoupling components; communicating design ideas.
- Watch out for: forcing patterns where they are unnecessary; over-engineering; copying patterns without understanding the problem.
- Tools: UML diagrams, refactoring guides, code examples.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for factory before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice singleton until it feels automatic.
