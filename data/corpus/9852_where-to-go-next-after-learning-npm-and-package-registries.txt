Where to Go Next After Learning npm and Package Registries

You have met the basics of npm and Package Registries. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about WebAssembly, which belongs to the same area as npm and Package Registries (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of package.json, semantic versions, lock files or registries, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around sharing internal packages with npm, and note every problem you meet, starting with unpinned versions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining npm and Package Registries to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
