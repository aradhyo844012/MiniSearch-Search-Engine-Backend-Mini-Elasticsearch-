VPNs in Real Projects

Reading about VPNs is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: remote work access. Here, tunneling drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against assuming it makes you anonymous.

Scenario two: linking offices. This time encryption matters most, and tools such as WireGuard help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi. The key idea is authentication, and the biggest risk is slow routing through distant servers.

Across all three scenarios, VPNs stays the same idea: encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet. Only the details change.
