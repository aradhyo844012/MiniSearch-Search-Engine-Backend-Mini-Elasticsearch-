Choosing Tools for Vector Databases

There is no single best tool for Vector Databases. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

FAISS is a common first choice, especially for semantic search. pgvector suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Pinecone is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Vector Databases by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle recommendations well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Vector Databases, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: mixing embedding models.

As a rule of thumb, measure recall on a small labeled test set, no matter which tool you use.
