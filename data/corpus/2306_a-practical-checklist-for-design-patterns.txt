A Practical Checklist for Design Patterns

Use this checklist before you rely on Design Patterns in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Guard against this risk: copying patterns without understanding the problem.
3. Guard against this risk: over-engineering.
4. Guard against this risk: forcing patterns where they are unnecessary.
5. Measure results before and after any change.
6. Review the core ideas: singleton, factory, observer, and strategy.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers singleton.
8. Confirm that your team agrees on what Design Patterns should accomplish.
9. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on factory.
