A Practical Checklist for Database Replication

Use this checklist before you rely on Database Replication in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: reading stale data from replicas.
2. Review how Database Replication interacts with Relational Databases.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers primary and replica.
4. Measure results before and after any change.
5. Pick a tool to start with, such as PostgreSQL streaming replication or MySQL replication.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Database Replication should accomplish.
7. Review the core ideas: primary and replica, replication lag, failover, and read scaling.
8. Guard against this risk: untested failover plans.
