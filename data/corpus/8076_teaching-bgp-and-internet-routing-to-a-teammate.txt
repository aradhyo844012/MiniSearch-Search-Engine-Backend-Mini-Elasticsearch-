Teaching BGP and Internet Routing to a Teammate

Explaining BGP and Internet Routing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: BGP and Internet Routing is the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving connecting ISPs, naming autonomous systems and route announcements as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain peering in BGP and Internet Routing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, route leaks, and suggest this habit: filter route announcements strictly.
