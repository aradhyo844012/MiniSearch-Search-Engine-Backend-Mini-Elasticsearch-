Skills to Build Around BGP and Internet Routing

Knowing the definition of BGP and Internet Routing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with autonomous systems and route announcements, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with bgpq4 on a BGP and Internet Routing task, and try at least one alternative such as BIRD.

Judgment: learn to decide when BGP and Internet Routing fits a problem such as connecting ISPs, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region.

Habits: filter route announcements strictly, and write down what you learn after each project.
