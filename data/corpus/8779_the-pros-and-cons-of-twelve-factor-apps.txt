The Pros and Cons of Twelve-Factor Apps

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Twelve-Factor Apps is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports building web services
- Makes deploying to platforms easier
- Helps with keeping environments consistent

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: storing state on local disks
- Risk: hard-coded config
- Risk: long start-up times

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as building web services, and you can manage the risks, Twelve-Factor Apps is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep config out of the code.
