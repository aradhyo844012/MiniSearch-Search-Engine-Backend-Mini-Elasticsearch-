Mistakes to Avoid with Support Vector Machines

Everyone learns Support Vector Machines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow training on large data. It usually appears when someone skips margin and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor kernel choices. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of text classification. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unscaled features. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on regularization to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: scale features and try a linear kernel first.
