API Versioning in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through API Versioning.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: API Versioning is the practice of evolving an API without breaking existing clients.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn version in the URL, header versioning, deprecation, and backward compatibility.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine keeping old editions of a textbook available while a new one is published. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through public APIs, mobile apps that cannot update instantly, and partner integrations.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for versioning too often and removing fields without notice, then try OpenAPI for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to announce deprecations with a clear sunset date.
