Packet Switching Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Packet Switching regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit headers before anything else.
2. Write down why you chose Packet Switching so the next person understands the decision.
3. Make sure you can capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
4. Do not skip packets; everything else builds on it.
5. Be careful with this one: packet loss.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to voice over IP as well as to Packet Switching in general.
