Scaling Considerations for Pagination

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Pagination changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding offset pagination and getting search results right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for offsets getting slow on huge tables and unstable sort order, and measure with SQL LIMIT and OFFSET before changing anything.

Large scale: page size and stable ordering move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around activity feeds at this stage.

Before you scale, prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
