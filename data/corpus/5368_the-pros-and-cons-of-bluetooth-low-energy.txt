The Pros and Cons of Bluetooth Low Energy

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Bluetooth Low Energy is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports fitness trackers
- Makes smart locks easier
- Helps with proximity beacons

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring connection limits
- Risk: weak pairing security
- Risk: assuming long range

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as fitness trackers, and you can manage the risks, Bluetooth Low Energy is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test with several phone models.
