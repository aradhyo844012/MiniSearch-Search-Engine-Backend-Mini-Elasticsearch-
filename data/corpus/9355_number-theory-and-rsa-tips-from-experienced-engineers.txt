Number Theory and RSA Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Number Theory and RSA regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
2. Do not skip prime numbers; everything else builds on it.
3. Reach for Python pow first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as OpenSSL.
4. Test the case of public key cryptography early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit modular arithmetic before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to hash functions as well as to Number Theory and RSA in general.
