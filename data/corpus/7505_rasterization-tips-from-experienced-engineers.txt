Rasterization Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Rasterization regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for OpenGL first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Vulkan.
2. Test the case of real-time games early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit pixel coverage before anything else.
4. Write down why you chose Rasterization so the next person understands the decision.
5. Make sure you can draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to user interfaces as well as to Rasterization in general.
