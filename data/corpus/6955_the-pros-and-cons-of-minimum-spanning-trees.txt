The Pros and Cons of Minimum Spanning Trees

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Minimum Spanning Trees is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports designing cable networks
- Makes clustering data easier
- Helps with reducing infrastructure cost

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using it on directed graphs
- Risk: forgetting that the graph must be connected
- Risk: treating ties as errors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as designing cable networks, and you can manage the risks, Minimum Spanning Trees is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, sort the edges once and reuse the order when testing variations.
