Digital Audio Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Digital Audio feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Digital Audio: representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.
sampling rate: a core term, most visible in music production.
bit depth: a core term that beginners often confuse with waveforms.
waveforms: a core term that connects to the Nyquist limit.
the Nyquist limit: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Audacity: a tool commonly used for voice calls.

Come back to this Digital Audio list whenever documentation for SoX uses a word you do not recognize.
