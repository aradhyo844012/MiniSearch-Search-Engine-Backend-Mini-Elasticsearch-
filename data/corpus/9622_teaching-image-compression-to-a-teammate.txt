Teaching Image Compression to a Teammate

Explaining Image Compression to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving web images, naming lossy and lossless formats and quantization as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain transform coding in Image Compression back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, repeated recompression, and suggest this habit: pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
