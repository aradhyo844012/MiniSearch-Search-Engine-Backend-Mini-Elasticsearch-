Choosing Tools for High Availability Design

There is no single best tool for High Availability Design. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

load balancers is a common first choice, especially for online stores. Kubernetes suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Patroni is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for High Availability Design by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle banking systems well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for High Availability Design, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: hidden single points of failure.

As a rule of thumb, list every component and ask what happens if it dies, no matter which tool you use.
