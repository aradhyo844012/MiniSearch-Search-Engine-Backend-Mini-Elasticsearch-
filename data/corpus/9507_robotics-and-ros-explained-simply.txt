Robotics and ROS Explained Simply

If Robotics and ROS sounds intimidating, relax. It is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Robotics and ROS as a body with senses and muscles controlled by a nervous system of messages. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sensors, actuators, nodes and topics, and control loops. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sensors, then add actuators.

People reach for Robotics and ROS when they work on warehouse robots, drones, and educational robots.

One practical tip is to test in simulation before moving real hardware.
