Is Zero Trust Security Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Zero Trust Security, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified.

It is a good fit when your work involves remote work access or protecting internal services, and when your team understands identity verification.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn least privilege, or when you cannot manage these risks: treating it as a product to buy, poor identity hygiene, and breaking usability.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether micro-segmentation and continuous checks are part of the answer. If they are, Zero Trust Security is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with limiting breach damage and start with strong identity and device checks.
