Threat Modeling Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Threat Modeling regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for STRIDE first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as OWASP Threat Dragon.
2. Test the case of designing new features early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit attackers before anything else.
4. Make sure you can draw the data flow diagram first.
5. Be careful with this one: doing it once and never again.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to reviewing architecture as well.
