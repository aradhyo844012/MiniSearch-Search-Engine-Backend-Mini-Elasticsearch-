Choosing Tools for Database Migrations

There is no single best tool for Database Migrations. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Alembic is a common first choice, especially for adding columns safely. Flyway suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Django migrations is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle renaming tables well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: locking large tables.

As a rule of thumb, split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back, no matter which tool you use.
