Practice Exercises for Reading Documentation

These exercises build real understanding of Reading Documentation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how examples and version numbers differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Reading Documentation and include the term search.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: reading the wrong version. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble learning new libraries or solving errors.
Exercise 5: Compare the Python docs and man pages for verifying answers from other sources and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Reading Documentation is the skill of finding accurate answers in official guides and references.
