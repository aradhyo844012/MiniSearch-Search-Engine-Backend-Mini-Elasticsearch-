Mistakes to Avoid with GraphQL

Everyone learns GraphQL partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: the N+1 query problem. It usually appears when someone skips schema and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unbounded query depth. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of mobile apps with limited bandwidth. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: weak caching. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on types to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
