Everything You Need to Know About Backtracking

This page collects the essentials about Backtracking in one place. In short, Backtracking is a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends.

Core concepts:
- decision tree
- pruning
- state restoration
- constraint checking

Where it is used:
- solving Sudoku
- generating permutations
- N-Queens puzzles

Common problems:
- forgetting to undo changes
- missing pruning rules
- copying large state at every step

Tools to know:
- Python recursion
- itertools
- debuggers

Remember this above all: walking a maze and returning to the last fork whenever you hit a wall is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
