Penetration Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Penetration Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Penetration Testing in your own words.
2. Describe scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation, and reporting.
3. Give examples from pre-launch security checks and compliance audits.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Penetration Testing is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do. Then draw hiring a locksmith to test your doors on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is testing without written permission a problem? How does reconnaissance relate to exploitation? When would you choose OWASP ZAP or Burp Suite?

Study habit worth keeping: get written authorization before any test.

When you feel ready, compare Penetration Testing with Hashing vs Encryption and write down three differences.
