Choosing Tools for Property-Based Testing

There is no single best tool for Property-Based Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Hypothesis is a common first choice, especially for testing parsers. QuickCheck suits teams that already work with similar technology, and fast-check is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle checking sorting and math functions well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: vague properties.

As a rule of thumb, save failing examples as regular tests, no matter which tool you use.
