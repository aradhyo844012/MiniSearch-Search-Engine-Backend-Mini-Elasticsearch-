Model Drift and Monitoring Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Model Drift and Monitoring.

Do:
- Learn data drift before anything else.
- Try Evidently on a small example.
- Make it a habit to monitor input data as well as accuracy.
- Test the case of catching degrading models early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: monitoring only system health.
- Ignore this: having no ground truth labels.
- Ignore this: alert fatigue.
- Adopt Model Drift and Monitoring just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Model Drift and Monitoring, a little patience with concept drift pays off more than any clever shortcut.
