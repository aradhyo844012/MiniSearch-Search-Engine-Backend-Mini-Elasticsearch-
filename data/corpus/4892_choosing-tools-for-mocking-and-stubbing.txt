Choosing Tools for Mocking and Stubbing

There is no single best tool for Mocking and Stubbing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

unittest.mock is a common first choice, especially for isolating units. Mockito suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Sinon.js is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle simulating failures well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: over-mocking.

As a rule of thumb, prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains, no matter which tool you use.
