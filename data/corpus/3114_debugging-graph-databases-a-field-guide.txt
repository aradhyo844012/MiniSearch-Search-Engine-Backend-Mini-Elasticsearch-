Debugging Graph Databases: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Graph Databases, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: using them for simple tabular data, unbounded traversals, and supernodes with millions of edges.
Step 3: revisit nodes and edges, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Neo4j or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Graph Databases in work such as social networks come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, limit traversal depth in every exploratory query. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
