Where to Go Next After Learning MQTT

You have met the basics of MQTT. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Flutter, which belongs to the same area as MQTT (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of publish and subscribe, topics, brokers or quality of service levels, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around smart home devices with Mosquitto, and note every problem you meet, starting with unsecured brokers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining MQTT to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
