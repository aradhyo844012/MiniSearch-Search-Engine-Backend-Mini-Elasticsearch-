Mistakes to Avoid with Graph Databases

Everyone learns Graph Databases partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using them for simple tabular data. It usually appears when someone skips nodes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unbounded traversals. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of social networks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: supernodes with millions of edges. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on traversal queries to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
