Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Data Augmentation

This path is for a learner who has never studied Data Augmentation before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize) and learn transformations and label preservation.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Albumentations, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving image models.

Stage 3, depth: study synthetic samples and augmentation pipelines, then deliberately explore this problem: changes that alter the label.

Stage 4, application: apply Data Augmentation to text classification, compare torchvision with nlpaug, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Data Augmentation to someone else, and then move on to Support Vector Machines.
