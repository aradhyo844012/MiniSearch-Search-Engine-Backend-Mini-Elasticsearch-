A Practical Checklist for Threads and Concurrency

Use this checklist before you rely on Threads and Concurrency in a project.

1. Review how Threads and Concurrency interacts with Virtual Memory.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers threads.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Threads and Concurrency should accomplish.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on locks.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Pick a tool to start with, such as Python threading or Java ExecutorService.
7. Try the approach on a small case, for example handling many requests.
