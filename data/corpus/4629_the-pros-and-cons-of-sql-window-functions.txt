The Pros and Cons of SQL Window Functions

No technology is free of trade-offs, and SQL Window Functions is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running totals
- Makes ranking rows easier
- Helps with comparing a row with the previous one

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: forgetting ORDER BY inside the window
- Risk: confusing them with GROUP BY
- Risk: slow sorts on huge partitions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running totals, and you can manage the risks, SQL Window Functions is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with ROW_NUMBER and LAG on a small table.
