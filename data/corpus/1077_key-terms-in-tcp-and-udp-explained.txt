Key Terms in TCP and UDP Explained

Clear vocabulary makes TCP and UDP much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. Put simply, TCP and UDP covers the two main transport protocols, one reliable and ordered and the other fast and connectionless. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.
