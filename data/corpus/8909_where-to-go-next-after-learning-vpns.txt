Where to Go Next After Learning VPNs

You have met the basics of VPNs. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Firewalls, which belongs to the same area as VPNs (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of tunneling, encryption, authentication or split tunneling, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around linking offices with WireGuard, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming it makes you anonymous.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining VPNs to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
