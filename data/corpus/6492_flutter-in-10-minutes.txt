Flutter in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Flutter.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn widgets, state, hot reload, and the render tree.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a box of customizable building blocks drawn by its own engine. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through cross-platform mobile apps, desktop apps, and custom user interfaces.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for deep widget trees and poor state organization, then try Dart for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to split big widgets into small reusable ones.
