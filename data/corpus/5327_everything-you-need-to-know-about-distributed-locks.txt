Everything You Need to Know About Distributed Locks

This page collects the essentials about Distributed Locks in one place. In short, Distributed Locks is mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines.

Core concepts:
- lease times
- fencing tokens
- lock owners
- timeouts

Where it is used:
- running scheduled jobs once
- protecting shared resources
- selecting a leader

Common problems:
- locks that never expire
- clock drift
- assuming the lock holder is still alive

Tools to know:
- Redis
- ZooKeeper
- etcd

Remember this above all: a single key to a shared meeting room that expires automatically is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
