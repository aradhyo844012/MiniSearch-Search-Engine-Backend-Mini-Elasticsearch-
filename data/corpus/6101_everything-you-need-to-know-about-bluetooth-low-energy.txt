Everything You Need to Know About Bluetooth Low Energy

This page collects the essentials about Bluetooth Low Energy in one place. In short, Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

Core concepts:
- advertising
- GATT services
- characteristics
- pairing

Where it is used:
- fitness trackers
- smart locks
- proximity beacons

Common problems:
- ignoring connection limits
- weak pairing security
- assuming long range

Tools to know:
- nRF Connect
- BlueZ
- Android Bluetooth APIs

Remember this above all: a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test with several phone models.
