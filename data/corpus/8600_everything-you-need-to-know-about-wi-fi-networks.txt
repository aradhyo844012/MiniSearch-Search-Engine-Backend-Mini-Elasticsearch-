Everything You Need to Know About Wi-Fi Networks

This page collects the essentials about Wi-Fi Networks in one place. In short, Wi-Fi Networks is wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.

Core concepts:
- SSIDs
- channels and bands
- WPA encryption
- signal strength

Where it is used:
- home internet
- office coverage
- public hotspots

Common problems:
- overlapping channels
- weak passwords
- poor access point placement

Tools to know:
- Wi-Fi analyzer apps
- Wireshark
- router admin pages

Remember this above all: a conversation in a crowded room where everyone must find a quiet channel is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
