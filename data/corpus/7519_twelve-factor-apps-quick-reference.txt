Twelve-Factor Apps Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Twelve-Factor Apps. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly.
Key terms: config in the environment, stateless processes, backing services, and disposability.
Typical uses: building web services, deploying to platforms, and keeping environments consistent.
Risks: storing state on local disks, hard-coded config, and long start-up times.
Tools: Heroku, Docker, and Cloud Foundry.

Mental picture: a checklist for packing a suitcase that fits any airline.

Habit to build: keep config out of the code.

See also: High Availability Design, which sits close to Twelve-Factor Apps in the area of Cloud Platforms and Architecture.
