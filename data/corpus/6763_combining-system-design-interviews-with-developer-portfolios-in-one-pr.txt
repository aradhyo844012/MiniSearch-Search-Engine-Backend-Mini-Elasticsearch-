Combining System Design Interviews with Developer Portfolios in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how System Design Interviews and Developer Portfolios can work together in a single project.

System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices. Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.

Imagine a project that involves preparing for senior roles and applying for internships. System Design Interviews brings requirements, while Developer Portfolios brings projects.

A sensible plan is to start with whiteboard sketches for the System Design Interviews part and GitHub Pages for the Developer Portfolios part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: ignoring trade-offs on one side, and no explanation of decisions on the other.

Picture the whole as sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists, working alongside a shop window displaying your best work.

Two habits help: first, clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes; second, feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
