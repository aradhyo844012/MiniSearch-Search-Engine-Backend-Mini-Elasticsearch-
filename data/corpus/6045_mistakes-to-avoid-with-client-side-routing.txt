Mistakes to Avoid with Client-Side Routing

Everyone learns Client-Side Routing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: broken deep links. It usually appears when someone skips history API and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unhandled 404 pages. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of single-page apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: lost scroll position. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on route guards to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test refreshing the page on every route.
