Service Mesh Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Service Mesh feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Service Mesh: an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.
sidecar proxies: a core term, most visible in securing service traffic.
mutual TLS: a core term that beginners often confuse with traffic policies.
traffic policies: a core term that connects to observability.
observability: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Istio: a tool commonly used for retries and timeouts.

Come back to this Service Mesh list whenever documentation for Linkerd uses a word you do not recognize.
