Practice Exercises for Package Managers

These exercises build real understanding of Package Managers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick apt and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Package Managers and include the term repositories.
Exercise 3: Compare dnf and Homebrew for reproducing environments and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Explain how versions and signed packages differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mixing sources that conflict. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Package Managers is tools that install, update and remove software along with its dependencies.
