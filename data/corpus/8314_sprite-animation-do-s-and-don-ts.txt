Sprite Animation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Sprite Animation.

Do:
- Learn sprite sheets before anything else.
- Try Aseprite on a small example.
- Make it a habit to pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
- Test the case of 2D platformers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: inconsistent frame timing.
- Ignore this: huge uncompressed sheets.
- Ignore this: animation states that never end.
- Adopt Sprite Animation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Sprite Animation, a little patience with frames pays off more than any clever shortcut.
