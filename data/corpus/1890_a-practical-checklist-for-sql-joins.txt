A Practical Checklist for SQL Joins

Use this checklist before you rely on SQL Joins in a project.

1. Pick a tool to start with, such as PostgreSQL or MySQL Workbench.
2. Guard against this risk: forgetting the join condition.
3. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on left join.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what SQL Joins should accomplish.
6. Measure results before and after any change.
7. Guard against this risk: filtering in the wrong clause of a left join.
8. Write at least one automated test that covers inner join.
9. Try the approach on a small case, for example linking customers to orders.
