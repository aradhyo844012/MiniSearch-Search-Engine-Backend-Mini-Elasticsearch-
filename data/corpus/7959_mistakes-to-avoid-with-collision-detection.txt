Mistakes to Avoid with Collision Detection

Everyone learns Collision Detection partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: tunneling through thin objects. It usually appears when someone skips bounding boxes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: checking every pair of objects. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of platformers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: inconsistent hitboxes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on collision response to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
