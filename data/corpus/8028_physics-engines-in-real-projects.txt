Physics Engines in Real Projects

Reading about Physics Engines is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: vehicles and ragdolls. Here, rigid bodies drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against variable time steps.

Scenario two: puzzle games. This time forces and impulses matters most, and tools such as Box2D help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: training simulations. The key idea is constraints, and the biggest risk is unstable stacks of objects.

Across all three scenarios, Physics Engines stays the same idea: software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects. Only the details change.
