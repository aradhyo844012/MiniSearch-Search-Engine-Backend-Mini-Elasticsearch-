Where to Go Next After Learning Prompt Engineering

You have met the basics of Prompt Engineering. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Model Deployment, which belongs to the same area as Prompt Engineering (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of instructions, examples, context or output format, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around extracting data from text with model playgrounds, and note every problem you meet, starting with vague requests.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Prompt Engineering to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
