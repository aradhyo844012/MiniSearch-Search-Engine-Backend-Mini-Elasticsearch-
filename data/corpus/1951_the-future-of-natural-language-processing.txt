The Future of Natural Language Processing

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of Natural Language Processing is easy to describe in general terms. Put simply, Natural Language Processing covers the field of teaching computers to work with human language. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Simple baselines are worth building first because they show what improvement is realistic.

Expect tools such as spaCy and NLTK to keep improving, and expect newer ideas to keep building on tokenization. The basics, though, tend to stay the same, so time spent on embeddings and language models is rarely wasted. Anyone working on search engines will keep meeting Natural Language Processing for years to come.

Read the official documentation for NLTK; it often explains embeddings better than blog posts do. Practice tokenization with small exercises until it feels natural. Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Natural Language Processing.
