Choosing Tools for Integration Testing

There is no single best tool for Integration Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Testcontainers is a common first choice, especially for verifying API and database interaction. pytest suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Postman is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle catching contract mismatches well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow suites.

As a rule of thumb, use disposable containers for test dependencies, no matter which tool you use.
