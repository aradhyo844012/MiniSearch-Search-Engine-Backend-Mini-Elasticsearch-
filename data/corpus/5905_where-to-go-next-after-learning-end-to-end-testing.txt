Where to Go Next After Learning End-to-End Testing

You have met the basics of End-to-End Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Integration Testing, which belongs to the same area as End-to-End Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of browser automation, user flows, test data or selectors, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around release verification with Playwright, and note every problem you meet, starting with flaky tests.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining End-to-End Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
