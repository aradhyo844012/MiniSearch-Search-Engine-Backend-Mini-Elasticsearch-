Is Responsible AI and Bias Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Responsible AI and Bias, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.

It is a good fit when your work involves hiring tools or lending models, and when your team understands bias in data.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn fairness metrics, or when you cannot manage these risks: assuming data is neutral, testing only on average performance, and giving people no way to appeal.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether transparency and human oversight are part of the answer. If they are, Responsible AI and Bias is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with healthcare decisions and test performance separately for different groups.
