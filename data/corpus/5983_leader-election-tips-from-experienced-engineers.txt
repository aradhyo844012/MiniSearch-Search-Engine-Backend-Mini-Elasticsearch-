Leader Election Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Leader Election regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Leader Election so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: split brain.
3. Do not skip heartbeats; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for ZooKeeper first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as etcd.
5. Make sure you can tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to job schedulers as well.
