Everything You Need to Know About Leader Election

This page collects the essentials about Leader Election in one place. In short, Leader Election is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work.

Core concepts:
- heartbeats
- terms or epochs
- candidates
- failover

Where it is used:
- database primaries
- job schedulers
- cluster managers

Common problems:
- split brain
- flapping leaders
- slow failure detection

Tools to know:
- ZooKeeper
- etcd
- Kubernetes leases

Remember this above all: a classroom picking a monitor when the teacher leaves is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
