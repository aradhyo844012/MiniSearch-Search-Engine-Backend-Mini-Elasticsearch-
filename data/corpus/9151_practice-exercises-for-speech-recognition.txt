Practice Exercises for Speech Recognition

These exercises build real understanding of Speech Recognition. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Kaldi and cloud speech-to-text services for dictation and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: accents and background noise. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble voice assistants or captioning.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Speech Recognition and include the term audio features.
Exercise 5: Explain how language models and word error rate differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning.
