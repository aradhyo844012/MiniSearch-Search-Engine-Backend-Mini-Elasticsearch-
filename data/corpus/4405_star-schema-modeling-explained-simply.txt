Star Schema Modeling Explained Simply

If Star Schema Modeling sounds intimidating, relax. It is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Star Schema Modeling as a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: fact tables, dimension tables, surrogate keys, and grain. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with fact tables, then add dimension tables.

People reach for Star Schema Modeling when they work on business intelligence reports, data warehouses, and fast aggregations.

One practical tip is to state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.

Once Star Schema Modeling feels comfortable, read about Feature Stores, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
