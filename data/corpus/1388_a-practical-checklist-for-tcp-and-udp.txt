A Practical Checklist for TCP and UDP

Use this checklist before you rely on TCP and UDP in a project.

1. Review how TCP and UDP interacts with HTTP and HTTPS.
2. Review the core ideas: handshakes, acknowledgements, ports, and congestion control.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example web traffic.
4. Guard against this risk: opening too many connections.
5. Guard against this risk: ignoring timeouts.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on acknowledgements.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers handshakes.
