Skills to Build Around Push Notifications

Knowing the definition of Push Notifications is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with device tokens and push services, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Firebase Cloud Messaging on a Push Notifications task, and try at least one alternative such as Apple Push Notification service.

Judgment: learn to decide when Push Notifications fits a problem such as order updates, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives.

Habits: ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value, and write down what you learn after each project.
