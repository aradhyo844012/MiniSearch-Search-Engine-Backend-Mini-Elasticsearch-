Combinatorics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Combinatorics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the art of counting arrangements and selections.
Key terms: permutations, combinations, the pigeonhole principle, and inclusion-exclusion.
Typical uses: analyzing algorithms, probability, and estimating password strength.
Risks: double counting, mixing order and no-order cases, and ignoring repetition.
Tools: Python itertools, math.comb, and SymPy.

Mental picture: counting the ways to seat guests at a table.

Habit to build: test counting formulas on tiny cases first.

See also: Linear Algebra for Programmers, which sits close to Combinatorics in the area of Mathematics for Computing.
