Where to Go Next After Learning Chaos Engineering

You have met the basics of Chaos Engineering. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Static Analysis, which belongs to the same area as Chaos Engineering (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of steady state, hypotheses, blast radius or fault injection, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around validating failover with Chaos Monkey, and note every problem you meet, starting with experimenting without safeguards.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Chaos Engineering to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
