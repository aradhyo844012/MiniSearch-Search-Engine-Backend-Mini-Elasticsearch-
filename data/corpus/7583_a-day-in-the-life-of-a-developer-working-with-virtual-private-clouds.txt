A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Virtual Private Clouds

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Virtual Private Clouds.

Morning: Rahul starts by reviewing a ticket about separating public and private services. Before touching any code, Rahul re-reads the notes on subnets to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Rahul pairs with a teammate to explore route tables with AWS VPC. A small test exposes the problem of overlapping address ranges, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving connecting to offices. Rahul sketches a gated neighborhood inside a big city on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Rahul writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to keep databases in private subnets.

Days like this are typical. Virtual Private Clouds is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
