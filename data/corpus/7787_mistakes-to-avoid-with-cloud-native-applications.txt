Mistakes to Avoid with Cloud-Native Applications

Everyone learns Cloud-Native Applications partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: treating the cloud like a data center. It usually appears when someone skips containers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: too many services. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of web platforms. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring costs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on elastic scaling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: design every service to be stateless where possible.
