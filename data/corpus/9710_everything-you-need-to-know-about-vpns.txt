Everything You Need to Know About VPNs

This page collects the essentials about VPNs in one place. In short, VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.

Core concepts:
- tunneling
- encryption
- authentication
- split tunneling

Where it is used:
- remote work access
- linking offices
- protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi

Common problems:
- assuming it makes you anonymous
- slow routing through distant servers
- leaking DNS requests

Tools to know:
- WireGuard
- OpenVPN
- Tailscale

Remember this above all: a private tunnel under a public road is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test for DNS leaks after connecting.
