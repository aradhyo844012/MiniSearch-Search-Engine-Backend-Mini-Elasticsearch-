Is Embeddings Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Embeddings, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.

It is a good fit when your work involves semantic search or recommendations, and when your team understands vectors.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn cosine similarity, or when you cannot manage these risks: mixing models between indexing and querying, ignoring normalization, and stale embeddings after data changes.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether dimensionality and nearest neighbor search are part of the answer. If they are, Embeddings is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with clustering text and use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
