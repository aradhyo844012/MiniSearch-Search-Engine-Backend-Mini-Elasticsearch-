Physics Engines for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Physics Engines.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Physics Engines in your own words.
2. Describe rigid bodies, forces and impulses, constraints, and fixed time steps.
3. Give examples from vehicles and ragdolls and puzzle games.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects. Then draw a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is variable time steps a problem? How does forces and impulses relate to constraints? When would you choose Box2D or Bullet?

Study habit: use a fixed time step for stable results.

When you feel ready, compare Physics Engines with Game AI Behavior Trees and write down three differences.
