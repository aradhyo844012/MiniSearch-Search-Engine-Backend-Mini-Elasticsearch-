Technical Debt Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Technical Debt.

Q: Explain Technical Debt in simple words.
A: Technical Debt refers to the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation. I would add an example involving deciding when to cut corners.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: SonarQube, issue trackers, and code metrics.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with planning cleanup work, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: never paying it down.

Q: How does it connect to Version Control Workflow?
A: Both belong to the area of Software Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: never paying it down; hiding it from stakeholders; and unclear ownership.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: shortcuts, interest, refactoring backlog, and code smells.
