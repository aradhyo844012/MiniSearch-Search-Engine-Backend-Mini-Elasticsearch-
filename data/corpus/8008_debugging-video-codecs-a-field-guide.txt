Debugging Video Codecs: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Video Codecs, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: mismatched codec support, too low a bitrate, and ignoring hardware decoding.
Step 3: revisit frames and motion compensation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use FFmpeg or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Video Codecs in work such as streaming services come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test playback on the weakest target device. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
