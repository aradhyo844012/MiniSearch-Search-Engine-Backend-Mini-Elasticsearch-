Mistakes to Avoid with Makefiles

Everyone learns Makefiles partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: tabs versus spaces. It usually appears when someone skips targets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing phony declarations. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of compiling C projects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hidden environment assumptions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on phony targets to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.
