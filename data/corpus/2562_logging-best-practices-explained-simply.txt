Logging Best Practices Explained Simply

If Logging Best Practices sounds intimidating, relax. It is recording useful, structured information about what an application is doing, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Logging Best Practices as a ship's logbook that explains what happened and when. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: log levels, structured logs, correlation IDs, and log rotation. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with log levels, then add structured logs.

You will meet Logging Best Practices in work such as debugging production issues, auditing actions, and measuring usage.

One practical tip is to include a request ID in every log line.
