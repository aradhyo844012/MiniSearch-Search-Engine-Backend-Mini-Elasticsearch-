How to Learn Android Basics in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Android Basics. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Android Basics covers the core building blocks used to create apps for Android phones. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn activities and layouts, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Android Studio on a small task such as building simple apps.
Week 3: build a mini project around accessing sensors.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as ignoring lifecycle events, and explain Android Basics to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include Android Studio, Kotlin, and Gradle.

In short, careful study of Android Basics pays off. Spend time on activities and layouts, and the rest will follow.
