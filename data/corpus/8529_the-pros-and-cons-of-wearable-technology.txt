The Pros and Cons of Wearable Technology

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Wearable Technology is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports fitness tracking
- Makes notifications easier
- Helps with health monitoring

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: battery drain
- Risk: inaccurate sensor readings
- Risk: privacy of personal data

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as fitness tracking, and you can manage the risks, Wearable Technology is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, design interactions that take only a few seconds.
