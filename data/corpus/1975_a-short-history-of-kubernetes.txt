A Short History of Kubernetes

Ideas behind Kubernetes developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. When people talk about Kubernetes, they usually mean a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Cloud resources are easy to create and just as easy to forget, which makes costs grow quietly.

To understand Kubernetes, start with pods. Then move on to deployments, and finally look at services and ingress. Understanding deployments first often makes services much easier to reason about.

A solid grasp of Kubernetes also makes Cloud Storage easier to understand.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Kubernetes whenever a new project gives you a reason.
