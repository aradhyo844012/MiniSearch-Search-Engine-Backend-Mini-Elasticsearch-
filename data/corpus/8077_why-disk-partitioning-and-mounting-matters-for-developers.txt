Why Disk Partitioning and Mounting Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Disk Partitioning and Mounting make better decisions, because Disk Partitioning and Mounting is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree.

Three situations show why it matters. First, adding a new drive. Second, separating data from the system. Third, encrypting volumes. In each case, a solid grasp of partitions and file system types separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: wrong device names, mounting without fstab entries, and formatting the wrong disk.

Good tools exist, including lsblk, fdisk, and mount, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
