Digital Twins Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Digital Twins feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Digital Twins: virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.
sensor data: a core term, most visible in factory monitoring.
simulation models: a core term that beginners often confuse with synchronization.
synchronization: a core term that connects to analytics.
analytics: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Azure Digital Twins: a tool commonly used for building management.

Come back to this Digital Twins list whenever documentation for Unity uses a word you do not recognize.
