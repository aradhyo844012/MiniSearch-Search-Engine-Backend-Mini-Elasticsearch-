Mistakes to Avoid with 5G Networks

Everyone learns 5G Networks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: limited coverage on the fastest bands. It usually appears when someone skips spectrum bands and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: battery drain. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of mobile broadband. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: marketing exaggeration. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on small cells to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds.
