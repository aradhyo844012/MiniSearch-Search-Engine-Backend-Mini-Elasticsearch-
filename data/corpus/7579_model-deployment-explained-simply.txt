Model Deployment Explained Simply

If Model Deployment sounds intimidating, relax. It is making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Model Deployment as moving a recipe from the test kitchen into the restaurant. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: serving APIs, batch versus online inference, containers, and latency budgets. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with serving APIs, then add batch versus online inference.

You will meet Model Deployment in work such as recommendation endpoints, fraud checks, and mobile app features.

One practical tip is to log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.

Once Model Deployment feels comfortable, read about Anomaly Detection, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
