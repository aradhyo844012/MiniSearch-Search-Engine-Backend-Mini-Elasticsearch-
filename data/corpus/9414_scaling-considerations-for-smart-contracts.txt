Scaling Considerations for Smart Contracts

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Smart Contracts changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding contract code and getting token systems right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for bugs that cannot be patched and reentrancy attacks, and measure with Solidity before changing anything.

Large scale: gas fees and immutability move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around automated escrow at this stage.

Before you scale, audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
