Image Compression in Real Projects

Reading about Image Compression is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: web images. Here, lossy and lossless formats drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against repeated recompression.

Scenario two: photo storage. This time quantization matters most, and tools such as ImageMagick help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: sending pictures over slow networks. The key idea is transform coding, and the biggest risk is the wrong format for graphics.

Across all three scenarios, Image Compression stays the same idea: reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail. Only the details change.
