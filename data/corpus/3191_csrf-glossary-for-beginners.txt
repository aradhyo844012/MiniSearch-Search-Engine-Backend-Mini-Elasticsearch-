CSRF Glossary for Beginners

New words can make CSRF feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

CSRF: an attack that tricks a logged-in user's browser into sending unwanted requests to a site.
session cookies: a core term, most visible in protecting forms.
anti-CSRF tokens: a core term that beginners often confuse with SameSite cookies.
SameSite cookies: a core term that connects to state-changing requests.
state-changing requests: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Django CSRF middleware: a tool commonly used for securing account settings.

Come back to this CSRF list whenever documentation for Flask-WTF uses a word you do not recognize.
