The Pros and Cons of Union-Find

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Union-Find is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports detecting connected components
- Makes building minimum spanning trees easier
- Helps with grouping equivalent items

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: forgetting path compression
- Risk: merging without checking roots
- Risk: using it when deletions are required

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as detecting connected components, and you can manage the risks, Union-Find is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, implement path compression and union by rank together.
