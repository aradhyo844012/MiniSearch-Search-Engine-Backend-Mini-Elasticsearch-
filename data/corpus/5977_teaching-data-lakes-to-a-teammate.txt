Teaching Data Lakes to a Teammate

Explaining Data Lakes to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving storing logs and events, naming raw storage and schema on read as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain partitioning in Data Lakes back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, turning into a data swamp, and suggest this habit: register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.
