Debugging Managed Cloud Databases: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Managed Cloud Databases, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: surprise costs, limited configuration control, and ignoring maintenance windows.
Step 3: revisit automated backups and read replicas, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Amazon RDS or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Managed Cloud Databases in work such as launching apps quickly come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test restoring a backup before you need it. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
