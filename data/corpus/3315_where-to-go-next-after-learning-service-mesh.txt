Where to Go Next After Learning Service Mesh

You have met the basics of Service Mesh. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Log Aggregation, which belongs to the same area as Service Mesh (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sidecar proxies, mutual TLS, traffic policies or observability, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around retries and timeouts with Istio, and note every problem you meet, starting with added latency and complexity.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Service Mesh to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
