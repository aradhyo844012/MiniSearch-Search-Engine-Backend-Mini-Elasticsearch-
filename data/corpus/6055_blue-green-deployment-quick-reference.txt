Blue-Green Deployment Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Blue-Green Deployment. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one.
Key terms: blue and green environments, traffic switching, rollback, and database compatibility.
Typical uses: zero-downtime releases, quick rollbacks, and testing in production-like settings.
Risks: database schema mismatches, doubling infrastructure costs, and forgetting to drain old connections.
Tools: AWS CodeDeploy, Kubernetes services, and NGINX.

Mental picture: swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on.

Habit to build: keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.

See also: Autoscaling, which sits close to Blue-Green Deployment in the area of DevOps and Cloud.
