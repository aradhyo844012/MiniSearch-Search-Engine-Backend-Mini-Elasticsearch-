Understanding Digital Audio Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later.

In that picture, sampling rate is the first rule you follow, bit depth is the next step, and waveforms and the Nyquist limit keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second, and it involves details such as clipping from too high a level that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Audacity to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain music production to a friend.
