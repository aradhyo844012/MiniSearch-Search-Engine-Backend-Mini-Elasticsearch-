Connection Pooling in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Connection Pooling.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Connection Pooling is reusing a limited set of open database connections instead of opening a new one for every request.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn pool size, idle connections, timeouts, and connection leaks.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a rental desk that lends a few shared bikes instead of buying one per visitor. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through web backends, serverless functions that call databases, and high-traffic APIs.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for pools larger than the database allows and leaked connections, then try PgBouncer for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to always return connections in a finally block.
