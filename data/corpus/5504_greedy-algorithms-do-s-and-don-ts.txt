Greedy Algorithms Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Greedy Algorithms.

Do:
- Learn greedy choice property before anything else.
- Try pen and paper proofs on a small example.
- Make it a habit to try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it.
- Test the case of interval scheduling early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming greedy always works.
- Ignore this: missing counterexamples.
- Ignore this: skipping the correctness proof.
- Adopt Greedy Algorithms just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Greedy Algorithms, a little patience with optimal substructure pays off more than any clever shortcut.
