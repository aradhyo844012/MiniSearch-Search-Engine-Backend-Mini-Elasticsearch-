Debugging Blue-Green Deployment: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Blue-Green Deployment, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: database schema mismatches, doubling infrastructure costs, and forgetting to drain old connections.
Step 3: revisit blue and green environments and traffic switching, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use AWS CodeDeploy or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Blue-Green Deployment in work such as zero-downtime releases come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
