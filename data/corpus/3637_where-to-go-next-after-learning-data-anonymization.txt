Where to Go Next After Learning Data Anonymization

You have met the basics of Data Anonymization. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Apache Spark, which belongs to the same area as Data Anonymization (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of masking, generalization, k-anonymity or differential privacy, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around testing with production-like data with ARX, and note every problem you meet, starting with re-identification through combined fields.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Data Anonymization to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
