The Pros and Cons of Disk Partitioning and Mounting

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Disk Partitioning and Mounting is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports adding a new drive
- Makes separating data from the system easier
- Helps with encrypting volumes

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: wrong device names
- Risk: mounting without fstab entries
- Risk: formatting the wrong disk

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as adding a new drive, and you can manage the risks, Disk Partitioning and Mounting is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
