Service Mesh Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Service Mesh.

Do:
- Learn sidecar proxies before anything else.
- Try Istio on a small example.
- Make it a habit to adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
- Test the case of securing service traffic early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: added latency and complexity.
- Ignore this: adopting it too early.
- Ignore this: hard-to-debug proxy configs.
- Adopt Service Mesh just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Service Mesh, a little patience with mutual TLS pays off more than any clever shortcut.
