How to Learn CDNs in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with CDNs. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet CDNs in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to networks of servers around the world that deliver static content from a location close to the user.

Week 1: learn edge servers and cache rules, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Cloudflare on a small task such as serving images and scripts.
Week 3: build a mini project around speeding up global sites.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as caching private data, and explain CDNs to a friend.

In practice you can start with Cloudflare, then explore Akamai or Amazon CloudFront as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of CDNs pays off. Spend time on edge servers and cache rules, and the rest will follow.
