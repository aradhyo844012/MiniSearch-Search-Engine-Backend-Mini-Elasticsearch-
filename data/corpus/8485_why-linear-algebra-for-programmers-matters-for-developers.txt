Why Linear Algebra for Programmers Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Linear Algebra for Programmers make better decisions, because Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning.

Three situations show why it matters. First, 3D graphics. Second, machine learning. Third, recommendation systems. In each case, a solid grasp of vectors and matrices separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: mixing row and column conventions, shape mismatches, and forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative.

Good tools exist, including NumPy, MATLAB, and the 3Blue1Brown video series, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
