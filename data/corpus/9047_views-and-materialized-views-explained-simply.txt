Views and Materialized Views Explained Simply

If Views and Materialized Views sounds intimidating, relax. It is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Views and Materialized Views as a window cut into a wall that shows only part of the room. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: view definitions, refresh strategies, permissions, and dependencies. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with view definitions, then add refresh strategies.

You will meet Views and Materialized Views in work such as simplifying repeated queries, hiding sensitive columns, and speeding up reports.

One practical tip is to document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
