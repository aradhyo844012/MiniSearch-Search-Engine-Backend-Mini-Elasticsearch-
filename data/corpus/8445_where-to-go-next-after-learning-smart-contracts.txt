Where to Go Next After Learning Smart Contracts

You have met the basics of Smart Contracts. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Augmented and Virtual Reality, which belongs to the same area as Smart Contracts (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of contract code, transactions, gas fees or immutability, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around automated escrow with Solidity, and note every problem you meet, starting with bugs that cannot be patched.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Smart Contracts to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
