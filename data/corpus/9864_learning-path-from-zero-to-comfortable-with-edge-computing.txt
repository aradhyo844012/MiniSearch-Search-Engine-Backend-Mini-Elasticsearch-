Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Edge Computing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Edge Computing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Edge Computing is processing data close to where it is produced instead of in a distant data center) and learn edge nodes and latency.

Stage 2, hands-on: install AWS IoT Greengrass, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving factory sensors.

Stage 3, depth: study bandwidth savings and synchronization, then deliberately explore this problem: hard-to-update devices.

Stage 4, application: apply Edge Computing to video analytics, compare K3s with Cloudflare Workers, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Edge Computing to someone else, and then move on to Blockchain Fundamentals.
