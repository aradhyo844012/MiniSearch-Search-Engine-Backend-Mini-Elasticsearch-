A/B Testing in Real Projects

Reading about A/B Testing is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: testing page designs. Here, control and variant drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against stopping tests early.

Scenario two: comparing ranking algorithms. This time random assignment matters most, and tools such as Optimizely help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: evaluating new features. The key idea is metrics, and the biggest risk is testing too many variants.

Across all three scenarios, A/B Testing stays the same idea: an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better. Only the details change.
