Where to Go Next After Learning CQRS

You have met the basics of CQRS. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Consistent Hashing, which belongs to the same area as CQRS (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of commands, queries, read models or eventual consistency, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around complex domains with Axon Framework, and note every problem you meet, starting with adding it to simple apps.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining CQRS to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
