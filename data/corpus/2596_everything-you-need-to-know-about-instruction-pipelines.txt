Everything You Need to Know About Instruction Pipelines

This page collects the essentials about Instruction Pipelines in one place. In short, Instruction Pipelines is a technique where a processor overlaps the stages of several instructions.

Core concepts:
- fetch, decode and execute stages
- hazards
- branch prediction
- stalls

Where it is used:
- explaining CPU speed
- understanding branch costs
- optimizing tight loops

Common problems:
- unpredictable branches
- dependency chains
- assuming each instruction takes one step

Tools to know:
- perf
- Compiler Explorer
- published instruction tables

Remember this above all: an assembly line where several cars are in different stages at once is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops.
