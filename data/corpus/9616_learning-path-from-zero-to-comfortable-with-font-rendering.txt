Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Font Rendering

This path is for a learner who has never studied Font Rendering before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen) and learn glyphs and hinting.

Stage 2, hands-on: install FreeType, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving web pages.

Stage 3, depth: study anti-aliasing and kerning, then deliberately explore this problem: missing font fallbacks.

Stage 4, application: apply Font Rendering to operating systems, compare Google Fonts with font inspector tools, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Font Rendering to someone else, and then move on to Video Codecs.
