Debugging Vector Clocks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Vector Clocks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: clocks that grow with many nodes, confusing them with wall-clock time, and never pruning old entries.
Step 3: revisit logical time and per-process counters, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Riak or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Vector Clocks in work such as detecting concurrent edits come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
