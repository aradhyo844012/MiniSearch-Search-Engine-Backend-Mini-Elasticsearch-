Is Sprite Animation Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Sprite Animation, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Sprite Animation is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move.

It is a good fit when your work involves 2D platformers or user interface effects, and when your team understands sprite sheets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn frames, or when you cannot manage these risks: inconsistent frame timing, huge uncompressed sheets, and animation states that never end.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether frame rate and animation states are part of the answer. If they are, Sprite Animation is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with visual novels and pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
