Mistakes to Avoid with Webhooks

Everyone learns Webhooks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: not verifying signatures. It usually appears when someone skips event payloads and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow handlers causing timeouts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of payment notifications. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: processing duplicate events. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on endpoints to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
