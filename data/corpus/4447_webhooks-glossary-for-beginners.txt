Webhooks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Webhooks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Webhooks: HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens.
event payloads: a core term, most visible in payment notifications.
signature verification: a core term that beginners often confuse with retries.
retries: a core term that connects to endpoints.
endpoints: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ngrok: a tool commonly used for CI triggers.

Come back to this Webhooks list whenever documentation for the Stripe CLI uses a word you do not recognize.
