Linear Algebra for Programmers Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Linear Algebra for Programmers feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Linear Algebra for Programmers: the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning.
vectors: a core term, most visible in 3D graphics.
matrices: a core term that beginners often confuse with dot products.
dot products: a core term that connects to eigenvalues.
eigenvalues: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
NumPy: a tool commonly used for machine learning.
