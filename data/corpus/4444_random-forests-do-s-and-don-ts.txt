Random Forests Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Random Forests.

Do:
- Learn bagging before anything else.
- Try scikit-learn on a small example.
- Make it a habit to tune the number of trees only after the basics work.
- Test the case of tabular predictions early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating importance scores as causes.
- Ignore this: very large forests being slow.
- Ignore this: ignoring class imbalance.
- Adopt Random Forests just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Random Forests, a little patience with feature randomness pays off more than any clever shortcut.
