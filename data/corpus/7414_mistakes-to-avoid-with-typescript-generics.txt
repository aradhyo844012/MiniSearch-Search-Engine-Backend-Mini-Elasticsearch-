Mistakes to Avoid with TypeScript Generics

Everyone learns TypeScript Generics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overusing any. It usually appears when someone skips type parameters and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: overly complex generic types. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of reusable utility functions. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting constraints. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on generic interfaces to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: add constraints so that error messages become clearer.
