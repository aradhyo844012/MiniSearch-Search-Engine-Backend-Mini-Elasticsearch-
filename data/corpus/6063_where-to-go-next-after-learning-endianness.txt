Where to Go Next After Learning Endianness

You have met the basics of Endianness. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about System Calls, which belongs to the same area as Endianness (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of big-endian, little-endian, byte order marks or network byte order, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around binary file formats with Python struct, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing byte orders.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Endianness to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
