A Practical Checklist for Process Scheduling

Use this checklist before you rely on Process Scheduling in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: starvation of low priority tasks.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers time slices.
3. Review how Process Scheduling interacts with Virtual Memory.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on priorities.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example sharing CPUs among programs.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Process Scheduling should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: too many context switches.
8. Document your decisions in the project README.
