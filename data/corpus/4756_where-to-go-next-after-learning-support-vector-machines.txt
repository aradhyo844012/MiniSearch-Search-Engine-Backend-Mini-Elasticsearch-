Where to Go Next After Learning Support Vector Machines

You have met the basics of Support Vector Machines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Retrieval-Augmented Generation, which belongs to the same area as Support Vector Machines (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of margin, support vectors, kernels or regularization, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around small high-dimensional datasets with scikit-learn, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow training on large data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Support Vector Machines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
