Where to Go Next After Learning Star Schema Modeling

You have met the basics of Star Schema Modeling. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Jupyter Notebooks, which belongs to the same area as Star Schema Modeling (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of fact tables, dimension tables, surrogate keys or grain, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around data warehouses with dbt, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing different grains.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Star Schema Modeling to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
