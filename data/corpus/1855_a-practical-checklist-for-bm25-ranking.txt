A Practical Checklist for BM25 Ranking

Use this checklist before you rely on BM25 Ranking in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: comparing scores across different queries.
2. Review how BM25 Ranking interacts with Edit Distance.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as Elasticsearch or rank_bm25.
4. Review the core ideas: term saturation, document length normalization, k1 and b parameters, and inverse document frequency.
5. Guard against this risk: ignoring field boundaries.
6. Guard against this risk: untuned parameters.
7. Confirm that your team agrees on what BM25 Ranking should accomplish.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
