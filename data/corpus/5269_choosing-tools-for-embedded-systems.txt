Choosing Tools for Embedded Systems

There is no single best tool for Embedded Systems. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

C is a common first choice, especially for appliances. FreeRTOS suits teams that already work with similar technology, and PlatformIO is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle medical devices well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: memory leaks on tiny devices.

As a rule of thumb, plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start, no matter which tool you use.
