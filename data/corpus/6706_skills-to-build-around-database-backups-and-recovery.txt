Skills to Build Around Database Backups and Recovery

Knowing the definition of Database Backups and Recovery is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with full and incremental backups and recovery point objective, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with pg_dump on a Database Backups and Recovery task, and try at least one alternative such as mysqldump.

Judgment: learn to decide when Database Backups and Recovery fits a problem such as recovering deleted tables, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home.

Habits: schedule a restore drill on a regular basis, and write down what you learn after each project.
