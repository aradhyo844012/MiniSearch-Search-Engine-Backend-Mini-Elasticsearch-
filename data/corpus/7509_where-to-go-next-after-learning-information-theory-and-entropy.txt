Where to Go Next After Learning Information Theory and Entropy

You have met the basics of Information Theory and Entropy. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Combinatorics, which belongs to the same area as Information Theory and Entropy (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of bits, entropy, compression limits or noise, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around error-correcting codes with the Python math module, and note every problem you meet, starting with confusing information with meaning.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Information Theory and Entropy to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
