Distributed Tracing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Distributed Tracing.

Do:
- Learn spans before anything else.
- Try OpenTelemetry on a small example.
- Make it a habit to propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
- Test the case of finding slow services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: missing context propagation.
- Ignore this: sampling too aggressively.
- Ignore this: overhead from too many spans.
- Adopt Distributed Tracing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Distributed Tracing, a little patience with trace IDs pays off more than any clever shortcut.
