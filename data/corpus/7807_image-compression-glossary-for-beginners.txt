Image Compression Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Image Compression feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Image Compression: reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail.
lossy and lossless formats: a core term, most visible in web images.
quantization: a core term that beginners often confuse with transform coding.
transform coding: a core term that connects to quality settings.
quality settings: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ImageMagick: a tool commonly used for photo storage.

Come back to this Image Compression list whenever documentation for mozjpeg uses a word you do not recognize.
