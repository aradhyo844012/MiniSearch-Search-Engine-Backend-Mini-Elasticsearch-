Vector Databases Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Vector Databases regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Vector Databases so the next person understands the decision.
2. Test the case of semantic search early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit approximate nearest neighbors before anything else.
4. Reach for FAISS first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as pgvector.
5. Do not skip embeddings; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to recommendations as well as to Vector Databases in general.
