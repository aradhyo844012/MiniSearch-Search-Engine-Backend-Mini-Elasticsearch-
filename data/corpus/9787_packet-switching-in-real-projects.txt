Packet Switching in Real Projects

Reading about Packet Switching is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: internet traffic. Here, packets drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against packet loss.

Scenario two: voice over IP. This time headers matters most, and tools such as Wireshark help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: file transfers. The key idea is routing, and the biggest risk is out-of-order delivery.

Across all three scenarios, Packet Switching stays the same idea: a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network. Only the details change.
