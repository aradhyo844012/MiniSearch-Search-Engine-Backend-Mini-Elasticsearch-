Debugging Feature Stores: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Feature Stores, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: leaking future data, duplicate definitions, and stale online values.
Step 3: revisit offline store and online store, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Feast or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Feature Stores in work such as sharing features across models come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
