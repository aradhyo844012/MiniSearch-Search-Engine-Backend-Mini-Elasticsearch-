Pandas DataFrames Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Pandas DataFrames.

- Definition: table-like structures in Python for loading, cleaning and analyzing data.
- Core ideas: rows and columns; indexing; groupby; merging.
- Use it for: loading CSV files; summarizing query logs; preparing data for charts.
- Watch out for: chained assignment surprises; loading too much data into memory; forgetting to check data types.
- Tools: pandas, Jupyter, NumPy.
- Quick tip: practice rows and columns until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for indexing before building anything large.
