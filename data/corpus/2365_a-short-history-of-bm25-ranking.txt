A Short History of BM25 Ranking

Ideas behind BM25 Ranking developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. When people talk about BM25 Ranking, they usually mean a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Fast search comes from precomputing structures so that queries do very little work. Search quality depends on how text is prepared before it is ever indexed.

To understand BM25 Ranking, start with term saturation. Then move on to document length normalization, and finally look at k1 and b parameters and inverse document frequency. A good way to remember term saturation is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

BM25 Ranking and Inverted Index are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Search and Retrieval.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit BM25 Ranking whenever a new project gives you a reason.
