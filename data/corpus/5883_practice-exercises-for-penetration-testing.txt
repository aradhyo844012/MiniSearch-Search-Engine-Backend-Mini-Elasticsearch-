Practice Exercises for Penetration Testing

These exercises build real understanding of Penetration Testing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Penetration Testing and include the term scoping.
Exercise 2: Explain how exploitation and reporting differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Compare Burp Suite and Nmap for validating defenses and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: testing without written permission. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Pick OWASP ZAP and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Penetration Testing is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do.
