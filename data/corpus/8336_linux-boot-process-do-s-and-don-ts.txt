Linux Boot Process Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Linux Boot Process.

Do:
- Learn firmware before anything else.
- Try GRUB on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep a recovery option available before editing boot settings.
- Test the case of troubleshooting machines that do not start early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overwriting the bootloader.
- Ignore this: wrong kernel parameters.
- Ignore this: ignoring recovery options.
- Adopt Linux Boot Process just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Linux Boot Process, a little patience with bootloader pays off more than any clever shortcut.
