Distributed Tracing Explained Simply

Here is Distributed Tracing without the jargon: it is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Think of Distributed Tracing as a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: spans, trace IDs, context propagation, and sampling. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with spans, then add trace IDs.

You will meet Distributed Tracing in work such as finding slow services, understanding dependencies, and debugging errors across services.

One practical tip is to propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
