Where to Go Next After Learning Fine-Tuning Models

You have met the basics of Fine-Tuning Models. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Speech Recognition, which belongs to the same area as Fine-Tuning Models (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of pretrained weights, training data, the learning rate or evaluation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around style adaptation with Hugging Face, and note every problem you meet, starting with overfitting on small datasets.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Fine-Tuning Models to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
