Combining Convolutional Neural Networks with Hyperparameter Tuning in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Convolutional Neural Networks and Hyperparameter Tuning can work together in a single project.

Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Hyperparameter Tuning is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns.

Imagine a project that involves image classification and improving model accuracy. Convolutional Neural Networks brings filters, while Hyperparameter Tuning brings search spaces.

A sensible plan is to start with PyTorch for the Convolutional Neural Networks part and Optuna for the Hyperparameter Tuning part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: ignoring data augmentation on one side, and searching too many options on the other.

Picture the whole as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes, working alongside adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear.

Two habits help: first, start from a pretrained model; second, try random search before anything fancier.
