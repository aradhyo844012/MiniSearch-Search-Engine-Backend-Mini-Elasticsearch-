Where to Go Next After Learning Augmented and Virtual Reality

You have met the basics of Augmented and Virtual Reality. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Quantum Computing Basics, which belongs to the same area as Augmented and Virtual Reality (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of head tracking, rendering at high frame rates, spatial mapping or input methods, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around games with Unity XR, and note every problem you meet, starting with motion sickness from low frame rates.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Augmented and Virtual Reality to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
