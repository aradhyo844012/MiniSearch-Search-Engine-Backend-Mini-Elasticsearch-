Choosing Tools for BGP and Internet Routing

There is no single best tool for BGP and Internet Routing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

bgpq4 is a common first choice, especially for connecting ISPs. BIRD suits teams that already work with similar technology, and RIPE Stat is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for BGP and Internet Routing by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle directing traffic between large networks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for BGP and Internet Routing, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: route leaks.

As a rule of thumb, filter route announcements strictly, no matter which tool you use.
