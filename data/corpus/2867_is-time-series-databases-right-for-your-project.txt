Is Time-Series Databases Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Time-Series Databases, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.

It is a good fit when your work involves server metrics or sensor readings, and when your team understands timestamps.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn retention policies, or when you cannot manage these risks: high-cardinality tags, keeping raw data forever, and querying without time bounds.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether downsampling and tags and fields are part of the answer. If they are, Time-Series Databases is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with financial tick data and include a time range in every query.
