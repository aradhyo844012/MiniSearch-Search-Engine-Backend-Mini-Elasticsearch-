Flutter Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Flutter regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Dart first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Flutter DevTools.
2. Write down why you chose Flutter so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of cross-platform mobile apps early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can split big widgets into small reusable ones.
5. Be careful with this one: deep widget trees.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to desktop apps as well.
