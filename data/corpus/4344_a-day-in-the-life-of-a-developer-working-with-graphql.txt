A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with GraphQL

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on GraphQL.

Morning: Rahul starts by reviewing a ticket about mobile apps with limited bandwidth. Before touching any code, Rahul re-reads the notes on schema to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Rahul pairs with a teammate to explore queries and mutations with Apollo. A small test exposes the problem of the N+1 query problem, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving aggregating many services. Rahul sketches ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Rahul writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.

Days like this are typical. GraphQL is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
