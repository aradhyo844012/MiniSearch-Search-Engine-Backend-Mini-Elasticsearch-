Why Reverse Proxies Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Reverse Proxies make better decisions, because Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.

Three situations show why it matters. First, hiding backend servers. Second, terminating HTTPS. Third, routing requests to several apps. In each case, a solid grasp of upstream servers and proxy headers separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: losing the client IP address, wrong timeouts, and exposing internal paths.

You will not need to build everything yourself; NGINX, Caddy, and HAProxy cover most needs.
