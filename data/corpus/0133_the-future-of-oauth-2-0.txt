The Future of OAuth 2.0

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of OAuth 2.0 is easy to describe in general terms. Many students first meet OAuth 2.0 in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to a framework that lets applications access resources on a user's behalf without sharing passwords.
