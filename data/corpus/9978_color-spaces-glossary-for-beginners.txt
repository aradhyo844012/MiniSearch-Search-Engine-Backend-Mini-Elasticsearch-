Color Spaces Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Color Spaces feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Color Spaces: mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.
RGB: a core term, most visible in web design.
HSL: a core term that beginners often confuse with CMYK.
CMYK: a core term that connects to gamma.
gamma: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
browser color pickers: a tool commonly used for photo editing.

Come back to this Color Spaces list whenever documentation for ImageMagick uses a word you do not recognize.
