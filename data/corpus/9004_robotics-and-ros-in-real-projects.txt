Robotics and ROS in Real Projects

Reading about Robotics and ROS is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: warehouse robots. Here, sensors drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against ignoring sensor noise.

Scenario two: drones. This time actuators matters most, and tools such as ROS 2 help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: educational robots. The key idea is nodes and topics, and the biggest risk is unsafe motion commands.

Across all three scenarios, Robotics and ROS stays the same idea: building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software. Only the details change.
