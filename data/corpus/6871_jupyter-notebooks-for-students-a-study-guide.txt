Jupyter Notebooks for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Jupyter Notebooks.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Jupyter Notebooks in your own words.
2. Describe cells, kernels, markdown, and outputs.
3. Give examples from exploring data and teaching and demos.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes. Then draw a lab notebook that can run its own experiments on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is running cells out of order a problem? How does kernels relate to markdown? When would you choose JupyterLab or VS Code?

Study habit: restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.

When you feel ready, compare Jupyter Notebooks with Data Anonymization and write down three differences.
