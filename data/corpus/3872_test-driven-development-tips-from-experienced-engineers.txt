Test-Driven Development Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Test-Driven Development regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip red, green, refactor; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit small steps before anything else.
3. Test the case of designing small modules early, before the design hardens.
4. Write down why you chose Test-Driven Development so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: writing tests that are too large.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to preventing regressions as well.
