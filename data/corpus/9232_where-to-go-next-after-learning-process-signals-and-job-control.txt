Where to Go Next After Learning Process Signals and Job Control

You have met the basics of Process Signals and Job Control. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about systemd Services, which belongs to the same area as Process Signals and Job Control (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of SIGTERM and SIGKILL, foreground and background jobs, kill and trap or process groups, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around shutting down gracefully with kill, and note every problem you meet, starting with using kill -9 first.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Process Signals and Job Control to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
