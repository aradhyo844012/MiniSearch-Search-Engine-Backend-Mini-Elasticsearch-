Cloud Cost Optimization Explained Simply

Here is Cloud Cost Optimization without the jargon: it is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.

Think of Cloud Cost Optimization as turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: right-sizing, reserved capacity, tagging, and usage reports. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with right-sizing, then add reserved capacity.

You will meet Cloud Cost Optimization in work such as cutting idle resources, budgeting by team, and choosing storage tiers.

One practical tip is to tag every resource with an owner.
