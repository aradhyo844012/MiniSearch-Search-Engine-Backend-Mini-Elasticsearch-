How Monitoring and Observability Connects to Site Reliability

Monitoring and Observability and Site Reliability are closely connected parts of Cloud and DevOps. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving detecting outages may also involve running production services. In such a project, metrics and service level objectives often influence each other.

Start with Monitoring and Observability: the term refers to collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves. Then look at Site Reliability, which refers to the discipline of keeping services dependable using engineering practices.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as finding slow requests, and explain how error budgets would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Monitoring and Observability, watch for this: logs without context. For Site Reliability, watch for this: blaming people instead of systems. Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Prometheus or PagerDuty.
