Probability Basics Explained Simply

Here is Probability Basics without the jargon: it is the study of how likely events are to happen.

Think of Probability Basics as predicting how often a dice roll will land on each face. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: events, conditional probability, independence, and expected value. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with events, then add conditional probability.

You will meet Probability Basics in work such as randomized algorithms, machine learning, and risk estimates.

One practical tip is to simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.

After Probability Basics, a natural next topic is Computability and the Halting Problem.
