The Future of Pandas DataFrames

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of Pandas DataFrames is easy to describe in general terms. The term Pandas DataFrames refers to table-like structures in Python for loading, cleaning and analyzing data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.
