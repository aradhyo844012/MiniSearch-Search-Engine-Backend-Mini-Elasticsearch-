Mistakes to Avoid with Star Schema Modeling

Everyone learns Star Schema Modeling partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing different grains. It usually appears when someone skips fact tables and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: over-normalized dimensions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of business intelligence reports. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: missing date dimensions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on grain to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
