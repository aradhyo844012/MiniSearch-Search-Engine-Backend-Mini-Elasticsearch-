Endianness Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Endianness.

Do:
- Learn big-endian before anything else.
- Try Python struct on a small example.
- Make it a habit to always specify byte order in file and protocol formats.
- Test the case of network protocols early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing byte orders.
- Ignore this: assuming all machines are alike.
- Ignore this: forgetting to convert before sending.
- Adopt Endianness just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Endianness, a little patience with little-endian pays off more than any clever shortcut.
