Where to Go Next After Learning Property-Based Testing

You have met the basics of Property-Based Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Code Coverage, which belongs to the same area as Property-Based Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of properties, generators, shrinking or invariants, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around checking sorting and math functions with Hypothesis, and note every problem you meet, starting with vague properties.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Property-Based Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
