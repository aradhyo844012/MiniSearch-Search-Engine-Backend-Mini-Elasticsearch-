The Importance of Web Accessibility in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Web Accessibility until something goes wrong without it. When people talk about Web Accessibility, they usually mean designing websites so that people with different abilities can use them. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Small improvements in markup and styling often have a large effect on usability.

People use Web Accessibility for screen reader support, for keyboard-only use, and for readable forms.

Watch out for these problems: low contrast text; missing focus indicators; and images without alternative text.

If you are comfortable with Web Accessibility, explore DOM Manipulation next. The two often appear in the same project.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Web Accessibility whenever a new project gives you a reason.
