Wearable Technology Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Wearable Technology regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can design interactions that take only a few seconds.
2. Be careful with this one: battery drain.
3. Write down why you chose Wearable Technology so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip sensors; everything else builds on it.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit low-power design before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to notifications as well as to Wearable Technology in general.
