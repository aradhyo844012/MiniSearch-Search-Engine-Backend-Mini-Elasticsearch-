Where to Go Next After Learning Fenwick Trees

You have met the basics of Fenwick Trees. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Dijkstra's Algorithm, which belongs to the same area as Fenwick Trees (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of binary indexed trees, lowest set bit, prefix sums or point updates, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around maintaining running totals with C++ vectors, and note every problem you meet, starting with using zero-based indexes by mistake.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Fenwick Trees to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
