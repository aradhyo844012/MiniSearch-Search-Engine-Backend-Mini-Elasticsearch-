Push Notifications in Real Projects

Reading about Push Notifications is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: order updates. Here, device tokens drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against asking for permission too early.

Scenario two: chat alerts. This time push services matters most, and tools such as Firebase Cloud Messaging help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: re-engaging users. The key idea is payloads, and the biggest risk is sending too many messages.

Across all three scenarios, Push Notifications stays the same idea: messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed. Only the details change.
