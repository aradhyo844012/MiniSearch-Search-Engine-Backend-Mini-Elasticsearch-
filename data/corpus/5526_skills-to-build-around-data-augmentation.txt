Skills to Build Around Data Augmentation

Knowing the definition of Data Augmentation is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with transformations and label preservation, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Albumentations on a Data Augmentation task, and try at least one alternative such as torchvision.

Judgment: learn to decide when Data Augmentation fits a problem such as image models, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields.

Habits: augment only the training data, and write down what you learn after each project.
