AWS IAM Roles and Policies for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover AWS IAM Roles and Policies.

Learning goals:
1. Explain AWS IAM Roles and Policies in your own words.
2. Describe users and roles, policies, least privilege, and temporary credentials.
3. Give examples from securing deployments and granting services access.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: AWS IAM Roles and Policies is the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account. Then draw key cards that open only the doors a person needs on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is wildcard permissions a problem? How does policies relate to least privilege? When would you choose IAM Access Analyzer or the AWS CLI?

Study habit worth keeping: grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.

When you feel ready, compare AWS IAM Roles and Policies with Twelve-Factor Apps and write down three differences.
