Choosing Tools for Firewalls

There is no single best tool for Firewalls. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ufw is a common first choice, especially for protecting servers. iptables suits teams that already work with similar technology, and pfSense is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Firewalls by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle segmenting networks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Firewalls, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: rules in the wrong order.

As a rule of thumb, start with default deny and open only what you need, no matter which tool you use.
