Saga Pattern Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Saga Pattern. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions.
Key terms: local transactions, compensating actions, orchestration, and choreography.
Typical uses: order processing, travel bookings, and payment workflows.
Risks: missing compensations, steps that are not idempotent, and flows that are hard to trace.
Tools: Temporal, Camunda, and Apache Kafka.

Mental picture: booking a trip with separate hotel, flight and car reservations that can each be cancelled.

Habit to build: make every step and its compensation idempotent.

See also: Event Sourcing, which sits close to Saga Pattern in the area of Distributed Systems.
