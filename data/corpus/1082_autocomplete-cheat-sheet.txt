Autocomplete Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Autocomplete.

- Definition: suggesting likely completions while a person types a query.
- Core ideas: prefixes; ranking suggestions; latency; query logs.
- Use it for: search boxes; command palettes; address entry.
- Watch out for: slow suggestions; offensive or irrelevant completions; ignoring typos in the prefix.
- Tools: tries, Redis sorted sets, Elasticsearch suggesters.
- Related topic: Zipf's Law.
- Quick tip: practice prefixes until it feels automatic.
