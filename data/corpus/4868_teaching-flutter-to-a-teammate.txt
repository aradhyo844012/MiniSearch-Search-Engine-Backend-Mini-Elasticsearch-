Teaching Flutter to a Teammate

Explaining Flutter to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a box of customizable building blocks drawn by its own engine. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving cross-platform mobile apps, naming widgets and state as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain hot reload in Flutter back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, deep widget trees, and suggest this habit: split big widgets into small reusable ones.
