Skills to Build Around Jupyter Notebooks

Knowing the definition of Jupyter Notebooks is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with cells and kernels, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with JupyterLab on a Jupyter Notebooks task, and try at least one alternative such as VS Code.

Judgment: learn to decide when Jupyter Notebooks fits a problem such as exploring data, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a lab notebook that can run its own experiments.

Habits: restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing, and write down what you learn after each project.
