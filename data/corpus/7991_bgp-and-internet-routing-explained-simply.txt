BGP and Internet Routing Explained Simply

Here is BGP and Internet Routing without the jargon: it is the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.

Think of BGP and Internet Routing as the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: autonomous systems, route announcements, peering, and path selection. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with autonomous systems, then add route announcements.

People reach for BGP and Internet Routing when they work on connecting ISPs, directing traffic between large networks, and explaining internet outages.

One practical tip is to filter route announcements strictly.

After BGP and Internet Routing, a natural next topic is Packet Switching.
