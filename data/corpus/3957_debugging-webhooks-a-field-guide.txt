Debugging Webhooks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Webhooks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: not verifying signatures, slow handlers causing timeouts, and processing duplicate events.
Step 3: revisit event payloads and signature verification, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ngrok or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Webhooks in work such as payment notifications come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, respond quickly and process the event in a background job. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
