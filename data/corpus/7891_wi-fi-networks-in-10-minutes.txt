Wi-Fi Networks in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Wi-Fi Networks.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Wi-Fi Networks is wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn SSIDs, channels and bands, WPA encryption, and signal strength.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a conversation in a crowded room where everyone must find a quiet channel. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through home internet, office coverage, and public hotspots.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for overlapping channels and weak passwords, then try Wi-Fi analyzer apps for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
