Where to Go Next After Learning Network Troubleshooting

You have met the basics of Network Troubleshooting. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Firewalls, which belongs to the same area as Network Troubleshooting (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of protocol layers, ping and traceroute, DNS checks or packet captures, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around diagnosing slow apps with ping, and note every problem you meet, starting with changing several things at once.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Network Troubleshooting to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
