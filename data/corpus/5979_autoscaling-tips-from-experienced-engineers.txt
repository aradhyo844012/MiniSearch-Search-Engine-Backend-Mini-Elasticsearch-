Autoscaling Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Autoscaling regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of handling traffic spikes early, before the design hardens.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit cooldown periods before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Autoscaling so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: scaling too slowly.
5. Make sure you can load test the system to find realistic thresholds.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to saving money overnight as well.
