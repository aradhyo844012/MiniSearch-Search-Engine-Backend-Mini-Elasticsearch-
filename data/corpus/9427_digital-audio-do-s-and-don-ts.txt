Digital Audio Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Digital Audio.

Do:
- Learn sampling rate before anything else.
- Try Audacity on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep recordings below the clipping level.
- Test the case of music production early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: clipping from too high a level.
- Ignore this: aliasing at low sample rates.
- Ignore this: mixing sample rates.
- Adopt Digital Audio just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Digital Audio, a little patience with bit depth pays off more than any clever shortcut.
