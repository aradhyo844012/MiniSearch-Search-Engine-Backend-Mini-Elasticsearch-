Everything You Need to Know About Blockchain Fundamentals

This page collects the essentials about Blockchain Fundamentals in one place. In short, Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.

Core concepts:
- blocks
- hashes
- consensus
- decentralization

Where it is used:
- digital currencies
- supply chain tracking
- public records

Common problems:
- treating it as a database replacement
- slow throughput
- the energy use of some designs

Tools to know:
- Bitcoin Core
- Ethereum
- Hyperledger

Remember this above all: a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
