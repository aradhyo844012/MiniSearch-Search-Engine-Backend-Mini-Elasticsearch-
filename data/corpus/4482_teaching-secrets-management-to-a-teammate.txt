Teaching Secrets Management to a Teammate

Explaining Secrets Management to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving database credentials, naming vaults and rotation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain access policies in Secrets Management back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, secrets in source control, and suggest this habit: rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
