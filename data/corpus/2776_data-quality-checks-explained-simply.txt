Data Quality Checks Explained Simply

Here is Data Quality Checks without the jargon: it is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

Think of Data Quality Checks as a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: null checks, uniqueness, freshness, and schema validation. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with null checks, then add uniqueness.

You will meet Data Quality Checks in work such as protecting dashboards, validating ingestion, and catching pipeline bugs.

One practical tip is to check data as early as it enters the pipeline.

Once Data Quality Checks feels comfortable, read about Data Anonymization, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
