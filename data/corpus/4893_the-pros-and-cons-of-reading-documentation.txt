The Pros and Cons of Reading Documentation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Reading Documentation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports learning new libraries
- Makes solving errors easier
- Helps with verifying answers from other sources

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: reading the wrong version
- Risk: skipping the overview
- Risk: copying examples blindly

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as learning new libraries, and you can manage the risks, Reading Documentation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, check that the documentation version matches your installed version.
