Data Anonymization Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Data Anonymization. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.
Key terms: masking, generalization, k-anonymity, and differential privacy.
Typical uses: sharing datasets with researchers, testing with production-like data, and meeting privacy rules.
Risks: re-identification through combined fields, removing too little, and destroying usefulness by removing too much.
Tools: ARX, Faker, and Google's differential privacy library.

Mental picture: blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene.

Habit to build: test whether combining fields could identify a person.
