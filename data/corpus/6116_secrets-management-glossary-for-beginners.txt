Secrets Management Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Secrets Management feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Secrets Management: storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.
vaults: a core term, most visible in database credentials.
rotation: a core term that beginners often confuse with access policies.
access policies: a core term that connects to audit logs.
audit logs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
HashiCorp Vault: a tool commonly used for API keys.

Come back to this Secrets Management list whenever documentation for AWS Secrets Manager uses a word you do not recognize.
