Object-Relational Mappers Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Object-Relational Mappers regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can log the generated SQL during development.
2. Write down why you chose Object-Relational Mappers so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of building CRUD apps quickly early, before the design hardens.
4. Be careful with this one: N+1 queries.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit queries before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to switching databases as well.
