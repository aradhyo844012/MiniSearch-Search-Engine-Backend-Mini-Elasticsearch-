Threads and Concurrency Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Threads and Concurrency.

- Definition: ways of making progress on several tasks at once within a program.
- Core ideas: threads; locks; race conditions; thread pools.
- Use it for: handling many requests; keeping interfaces responsive; parallel computation.
- Watch out for: forgetting to protect shared data; creating too many threads; mixing up concurrency and parallelism.
- Tools: Python threading, Java ExecutorService, asyncio.
- Related topic: Containers.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for locks before building anything large.
