CPU Caches Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with CPU Caches regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for perf first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Valgrind cachegrind.
2. Do not skip cache lines; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can access arrays in the order they are stored.
4. Write down why you chose CPU Caches so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: false sharing.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to explaining performance differences as well.
