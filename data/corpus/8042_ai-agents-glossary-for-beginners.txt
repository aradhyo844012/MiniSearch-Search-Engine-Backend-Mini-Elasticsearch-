AI Agents Glossary for Beginners

New words can make AI Agents feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

AI Agents: software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.
planning: a core term, most visible in research assistants.
tool calls: a core term that beginners often confuse with memory.
memory: a core term that connects to guardrails.
guardrails: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
LangGraph: a tool commonly used for workflow automation.

Come back to this AI Agents list whenever documentation for AutoGen uses a word you do not recognize.
