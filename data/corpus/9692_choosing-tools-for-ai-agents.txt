Choosing Tools for AI Agents

There is no single best tool for AI Agents. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

LangGraph is a common first choice, especially for research assistants. AutoGen suits teams that already work with similar technology, and tool-calling APIs is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for AI Agents by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle workflow automation well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for AI Agents, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: runaway loops.

As a rule of thumb, limit what tools the agent may use and log every action, no matter which tool you use.
