Webhooks in Real Projects

Reading about Webhooks is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: payment notifications. Here, event payloads drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against not verifying signatures.

Scenario two: CI triggers. This time signature verification matters most, and tools such as ngrok help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: chat integrations. The key idea is retries, and the biggest risk is slow handlers causing timeouts.

Across all three scenarios, Webhooks stays the same idea: HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens. Only the details change.
