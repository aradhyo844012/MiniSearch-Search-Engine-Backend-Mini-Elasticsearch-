Android Basics Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Android Basics.

- Definition: the core building blocks used to create apps for Android phones.
- Core ideas: activities; layouts; intents; lifecycle.
- Use it for: building simple apps; accessing sensors; showing lists.
- Watch out for: ignoring lifecycle events; blocking the main thread; hard-coded layouts.
- Tools: Android Studio, Kotlin, Gradle.
- Related topic: Mobile App Performance.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for layouts before building anything large.
