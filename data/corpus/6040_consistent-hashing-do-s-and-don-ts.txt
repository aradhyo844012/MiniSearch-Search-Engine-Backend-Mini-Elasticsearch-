Consistent Hashing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Consistent Hashing.

Do:
- Learn hash ring before anything else.
- Try Redis Cluster on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load.
- Test the case of distributed caches early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too few virtual nodes.
- Ignore this: uneven load.
- Ignore this: hot keys.
- Adopt Consistent Hashing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Consistent Hashing, a little patience with virtual nodes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
