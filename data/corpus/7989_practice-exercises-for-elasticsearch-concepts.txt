Practice Exercises for Elasticsearch Concepts

These exercises build real understanding of Elasticsearch Concepts. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Elasticsearch Concepts and include the term indexes.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble site search or log analytics.
Exercise 3: Compare Kibana and OpenSearch for product catalogs and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Pick Elasticsearch and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: too many shards. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.
