Skills to Build Around Webhooks

Knowing the definition of Webhooks is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with event payloads and signature verification, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with ngrok on a Webhooks task, and try at least one alternative such as the Stripe CLI.

Judgment: learn to decide when Webhooks fits a problem such as payment notifications, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch.

Habits: respond quickly and process the event in a background job, and write down what you learn after each project.
