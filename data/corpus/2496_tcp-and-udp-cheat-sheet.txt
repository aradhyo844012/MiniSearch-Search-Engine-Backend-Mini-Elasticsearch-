TCP and UDP Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of TCP and UDP.

- Definition: the two main transport protocols, one reliable and ordered and the other fast and connectionless.
- Core ideas: handshakes; acknowledgements; ports; congestion control.
- Use it for: web traffic; video calls; online games.
- Watch out for: assuming UDP guarantees delivery; ignoring timeouts; opening too many connections.
- Tools: Wireshark, netcat, tcpdump.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for acknowledgements before building anything large.
- Related topic: IP Addressing and Subnets.
