Segment Trees Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Segment Trees regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit range queries before anything else.
2. Be careful with this one: off-by-one errors in ranges.
3. Make sure you can test your tree against a slow brute-force version on small inputs.
4. Reach for C++ arrays first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Python lists.
5. Test the case of range sum queries early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to finding minimums in a window as well.
