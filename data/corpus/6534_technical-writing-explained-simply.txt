Technical Writing Explained Simply

Here is Technical Writing without the jargon: it is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.

Think of Technical Writing as giving directions to a stranger. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: audience, structure, examples, and editing. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with audience, then add structure.

You will meet Technical Writing in work such as documentation, blog posts, and design proposals.

One practical tip is to state the main point in the first two sentences.
