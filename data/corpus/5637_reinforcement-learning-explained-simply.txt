Reinforcement Learning Explained Simply

Here is Reinforcement Learning without the jargon: it is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

Think of Reinforcement Learning as training a dog with treats for good behavior. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: agent, environment, reward, and policy. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with agent, then add environment.

People reach for Reinforcement Learning when they work on game playing, robot control, and recommendation strategies.

One practical tip is to start with simple environments.

After Reinforcement Learning, a natural next topic is Convolutional Neural Networks.
