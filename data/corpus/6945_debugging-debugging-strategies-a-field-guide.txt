Debugging Debugging Strategies: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Debugging Strategies, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: changing many things at once, guessing without evidence, and not writing a test afterward.
Step 3: revisit reproducing the bug and isolating the cause, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use debuggers or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Debugging Strategies in work such as fixing production issues come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, change one thing at a time and record the result. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
