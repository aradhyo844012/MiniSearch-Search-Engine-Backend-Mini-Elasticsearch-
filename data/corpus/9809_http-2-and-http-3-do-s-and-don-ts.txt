HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3.

Do:
- Learn multiplexing before anything else.
- Try curl with HTTP/3 support on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep a working HTTP/1.1 fallback.
- Test the case of faster page loads early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help.
- Ignore this: middleboxes blocking UDP.
- Ignore this: misconfigured fallbacks.
- Adopt HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3, a little patience with header compression pays off more than any clever shortcut.
