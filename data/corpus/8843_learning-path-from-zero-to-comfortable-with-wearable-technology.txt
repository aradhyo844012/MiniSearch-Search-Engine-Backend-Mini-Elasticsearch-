Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Wearable Technology

This path is for a learner who has never studied Wearable Technology before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers) and learn sensors and low-power design.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Wear OS, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving fitness tracking.

Stage 3, depth: study companion apps and data syncing, then deliberately explore this problem: battery drain.

Stage 4, application: apply Wearable Technology to notifications, compare watchOS with Bluetooth Low Energy, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Wearable Technology to someone else, and then move on to Quantum Computing Basics.
