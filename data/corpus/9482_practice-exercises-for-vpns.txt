Practice Exercises for VPNs

These exercises build real understanding of VPNs. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare OpenVPN and Tailscale for protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble remote work access or linking offices.
Exercise 3: Pick WireGuard and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: assuming it makes you anonymous. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Explain how authentication and split tunneling differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.
