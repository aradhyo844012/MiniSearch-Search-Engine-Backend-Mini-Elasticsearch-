Everything You Need to Know About Physics Engines

This page collects the essentials about Physics Engines in one place. In short, Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.

Core concepts:
- rigid bodies
- forces and impulses
- constraints
- fixed time steps

Where it is used:
- vehicles and ragdolls
- puzzle games
- training simulations

Common problems:
- variable time steps
- unstable stacks of objects
- extreme mass ratios

Tools to know:
- Box2D
- Bullet
- PhysX

Remember this above all: a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to use a fixed time step for stable results.
