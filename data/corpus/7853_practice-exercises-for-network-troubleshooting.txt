Practice Exercises for Network Troubleshooting

These exercises build real understanding of Network Troubleshooting. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Network Troubleshooting and include the term protocol layers.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: changing several things at once. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Compare traceroute and Wireshark for verifying firewall changes and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Pick ping and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how DNS checks and packet captures differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Network Troubleshooting is a step-by-step process for finding out why a network connection fails or is slow.
