Where to Go Next After Learning Form Validation

You have met the basics of Form Validation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Hooks, which belongs to the same area as Form Validation (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of client-side checks, server-side checks, error messages or constraint attributes, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around payment pages with HTML constraint validation, and note every problem you meet, starting with validating only on the client.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Form Validation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
