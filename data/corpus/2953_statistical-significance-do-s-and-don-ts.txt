Statistical Significance Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Statistical Significance.

Do:
- Learn p-values before anything else.
- Try SciPy on a small example.
- Make it a habit to always report the effect size alongside the p-value.
- Test the case of judging experiment results early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mistaking significance for importance.
- Ignore this: p-hacking.
- Ignore this: ignoring sample size.
- Adopt Statistical Significance just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Statistical Significance, a little patience with the null hypothesis pays off more than any clever shortcut.
