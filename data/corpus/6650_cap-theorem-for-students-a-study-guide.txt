CAP Theorem for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover CAP Theorem.

Learning goals:
1. Explain CAP Theorem in your own words.
2. Describe consistency, availability, partition tolerance, and trade-offs.
3. Give examples from choosing database types and designing multi-region services.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: CAP Theorem is a result stating that a distributed system cannot guarantee consistency, availability and partition tolerance all at once. Then draw a phone tree that must choose between giving old information or staying silent when a line is cut on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is treating it as a three-way menu a problem? How does availability relate to partition tolerance? When would you choose Apache Cassandra or etcd?

Study habit worth keeping: ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors.

When you feel ready, compare CAP Theorem with Consistent Hashing and write down three differences.
