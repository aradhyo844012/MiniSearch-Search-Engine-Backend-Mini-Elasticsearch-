Is Physics Engines Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Physics Engines, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.

It is a good fit when your work involves vehicles and ragdolls or puzzle games, and when your team understands rigid bodies.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn forces and impulses, or when you cannot manage these risks: variable time steps, unstable stacks of objects, and extreme mass ratios.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether constraints and fixed time steps are part of the answer. If they are, Physics Engines is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with training simulations and use a fixed time step for stable results.
