Debugging 3D Transformations: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around 3D Transformations, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: wrong multiplication order, gimbal lock, and mixing coordinate systems.
Step 3: revisit translation and rotation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use GLM or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving 3D Transformations in work such as games come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, write down the order of transformations before multiplying. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
