Wi-Fi Networks in Real Projects

Reading about Wi-Fi Networks is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: home internet. Here, SSIDs drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against overlapping channels.

Scenario two: office coverage. This time channels and bands matters most, and tools such as Wi-Fi analyzer apps help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: public hotspots. The key idea is WPA encryption, and the biggest risk is weak passwords.

Across all three scenarios, Wi-Fi Networks stays the same idea: wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals. Only the details change.
