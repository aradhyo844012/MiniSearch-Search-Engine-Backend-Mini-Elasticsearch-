Mistakes to Avoid with WebAssembly

Everyone learns WebAssembly partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: crossing the JavaScript boundary too often. It usually appears when someone skips modules and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: large binaries. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of running heavy computation in the browser. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: limited access to browser APIs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the JavaScript bridge to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: measure before moving code to WebAssembly.
