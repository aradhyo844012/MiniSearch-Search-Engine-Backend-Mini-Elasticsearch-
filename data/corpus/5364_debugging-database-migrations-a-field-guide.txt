Debugging Database Migrations: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Database Migrations, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: locking large tables, editing old migrations, and data loss on rollbacks.
Step 3: revisit up and down scripts and version tables, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Alembic or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Database Migrations in work such as adding columns safely come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
