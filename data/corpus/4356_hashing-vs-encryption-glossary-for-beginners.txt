Hashing vs Encryption Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Hashing vs Encryption feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Hashing vs Encryption: the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data.
one-way functions: a core term, most visible in storing passwords.
keys: a core term that beginners often confuse with collision resistance.
collision resistance: a core term that connects to reversibility.
reversibility: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
SHA-256: a tool commonly used for verifying file integrity.
