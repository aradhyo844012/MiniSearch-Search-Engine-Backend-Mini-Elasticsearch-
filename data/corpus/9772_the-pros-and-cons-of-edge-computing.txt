The Pros and Cons of Edge Computing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Edge Computing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports factory sensors
- Makes video analytics easier
- Helps with smart cities

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: hard-to-update devices
- Risk: inconsistent hardware
- Risk: security at many sites

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as factory sensors, and you can manage the risks, Edge Computing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, decide which data must stay local before designing the system.
