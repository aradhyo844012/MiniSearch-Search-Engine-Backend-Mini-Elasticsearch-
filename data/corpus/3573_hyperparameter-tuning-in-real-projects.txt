Hyperparameter Tuning in Real Projects

Reading about Hyperparameter Tuning is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: improving model accuracy. Here, search spaces drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against tuning on the test set.

Scenario two: comparing algorithms. This time grid search matters most, and tools such as Optuna help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: running automated experiments. The key idea is random search, and the biggest risk is searching too many options.

Across all three scenarios, Hyperparameter Tuning stays the same idea: the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns. Only the details change.
