Dependency Vulnerabilities Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Dependency Vulnerabilities regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can automate update pull requests.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit version pinning before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Dependency Vulnerabilities so the next person understands the decision.
4. Reach for Dependabot first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as pip-audit.
5. Test the case of auditing projects early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to automating updates as well.
