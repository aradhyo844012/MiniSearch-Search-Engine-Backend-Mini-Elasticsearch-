Debugging Prompt Engineering: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Prompt Engineering, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: vague requests, missing context, and trusting outputs without verification.
Step 3: revisit instructions and examples, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use model playgrounds or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Prompt Engineering in work such as drafting content come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, specify the output format and include one or two examples. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
