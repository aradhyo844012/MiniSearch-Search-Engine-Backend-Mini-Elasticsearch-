Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Saga Pattern

This path is for a learner who has never studied Saga Pattern before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Saga Pattern is a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions) and learn local transactions and compensating actions.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Temporal, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving order processing.

Stage 3, depth: study orchestration and choreography, then deliberately explore this problem: missing compensations.

Stage 4, application: apply Saga Pattern to travel bookings, compare Camunda with Apache Kafka, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Saga Pattern to someone else, and then move on to CQRS.
