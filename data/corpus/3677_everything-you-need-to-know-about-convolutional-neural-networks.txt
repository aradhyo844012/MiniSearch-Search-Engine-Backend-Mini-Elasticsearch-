Everything You Need to Know About Convolutional Neural Networks

This page collects the essentials about Convolutional Neural Networks in one place. In short, Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.

Core concepts:
- filters
- pooling
- feature maps
- stride

Where it is used:
- image classification
- medical image analysis
- object detection

Common problems:
- too little training data
- ignoring data augmentation
- overly deep models for simple tasks

Tools to know:
- PyTorch
- TensorFlow
- Keras

Remember this above all: a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to start from a pretrained model.
