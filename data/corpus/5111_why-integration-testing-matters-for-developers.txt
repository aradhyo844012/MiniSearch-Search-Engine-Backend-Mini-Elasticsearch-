Why Integration Testing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Integration Testing make better decisions, because Integration Testing is tests that check how several parts of a system work together.

Three situations show why it matters. First, verifying API and database interaction. Second, catching contract mismatches. Third, testing third-party integrations. In each case, a solid grasp of test databases and service boundaries separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: slow suites, flaky external dependencies, and unclear failures.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Testcontainers, pytest, and Postman cover most needs.
