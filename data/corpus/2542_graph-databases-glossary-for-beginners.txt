Graph Databases Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Graph Databases feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Graph Databases: databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.
nodes: a core term, most visible in social networks.
edges: a core term that beginners often confuse with properties.
properties: a core term that connects to traversal queries.
traversal queries: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Neo4j: a tool commonly used for recommendation engines.

Come back to this Graph Databases list whenever documentation for Cypher uses a word you do not recognize.
