Skills to Build Around Data Lakes

Knowing the definition of Data Lakes is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with raw storage and schema on read, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Amazon S3 on a Data Lakes task, and try at least one alternative such as Delta Lake.

Judgment: learn to decide when Data Lakes fits a problem such as storing logs and events, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment.

Habits: register every dataset in a catalog with an owner, and write down what you learn after each project.
