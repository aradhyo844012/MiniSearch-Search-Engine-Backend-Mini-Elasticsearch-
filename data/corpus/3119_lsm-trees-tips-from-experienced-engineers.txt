LSM Trees Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with LSM Trees regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for RocksDB first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as LevelDB.
2. Test the case of write-heavy databases early, before the design hardens.
3. Be careful with this one: read amplification.
4. Write down why you chose LSM Trees so the next person understands the decision.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit sorted string tables before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to time-ordered data as well.
