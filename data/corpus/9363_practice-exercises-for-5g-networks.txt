Practice Exercises for 5G Networks

These exercises build real understanding of 5G Networks. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how network slicing and small cells differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing 5G Networks and include the term spectrum bands.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: limited coverage on the fastest bands. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Compare network simulators and 3GPP documents for industrial automation and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble mobile broadband or connected vehicles.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: 5G Networks is the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency.
