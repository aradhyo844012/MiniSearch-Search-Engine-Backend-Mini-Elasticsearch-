How to Learn Database Transactions in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Database Transactions. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Database Transactions covers groups of operations that succeed or fail together. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Database Transactions and Database Normalization are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Databases.

Week 1: learn atomicity and consistency, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with PostgreSQL on a small task such as transferring money between accounts.
Week 3: build a mini project around placing orders.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as holding transactions open too long, and explain Database Transactions to a friend.

In practice you can start with PostgreSQL, then explore SQLAlchemy or JDBC as your needs grow.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Database Transactions whenever a new project gives you a reason.
