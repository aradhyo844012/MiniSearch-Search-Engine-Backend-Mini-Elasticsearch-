The Importance of Technical Debt in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Technical Debt until something goes wrong without it. When people talk about Technical Debt, they usually mean the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Small, frequent feedback loops catch problems while they are still cheap to fix.

A team working on deciding when to cut corners might adopt Technical Debt after hitting the limits of a simpler approach. Other teams turn to it for planning cleanup work or explaining risk to managers.

Watch out for these problems: never paying it down; hiding it from stakeholders; and unclear ownership.

Technical Debt and Refactoring are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Software Engineering.
