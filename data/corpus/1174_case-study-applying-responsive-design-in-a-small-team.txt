Case Study: Applying Responsive Design in a Small Team

Imagine a small student team building a project that depends on Responsive Design. Put simply, Responsive Design covers building pages that adapt smoothly to phones, tablets and large screens. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

The team first spent a week learning media queries and fluid grids. Early on they hit a familiar problem: testing only on one screen size. After fixing it, they used Chrome DevTools device mode to check their work and adopted Responsive Design for websites used on many devices. Looking back, they said the biggest lesson was to write tests earlier.

Watch out for these problems: testing only on one screen size; tiny touch targets; and horizontal scrolling.

In short, careful study of Responsive Design pays off. Spend time on media queries and fluid grids, and the rest will follow.
