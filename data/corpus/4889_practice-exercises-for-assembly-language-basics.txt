Practice Exercises for Assembly Language Basics

These exercises build real understanding of Assembly Language Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick GDB and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: wrong calling conventions. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble understanding compiled code or debugging crashes.
Exercise 4: Explain how memory addressing and the stack differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Assembly Language Basics and include the term registers.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Assembly Language Basics is low-level instructions that map closely to what a processor actually executes.
