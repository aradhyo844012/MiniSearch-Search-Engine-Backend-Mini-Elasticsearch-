Debugging Form Validation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Form Validation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: validating only on the client, vague error messages, and rejecting valid but unusual input.
Step 3: revisit client-side checks and server-side checks, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use HTML constraint validation or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Form Validation in work such as sign-up forms come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, validate on the server even when the browser checks too. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
