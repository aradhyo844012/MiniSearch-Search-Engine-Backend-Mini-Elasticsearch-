The Pros and Cons of Chaos Engineering

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Chaos Engineering is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports testing resilience
- Makes validating failover easier
- Helps with preparing teams for incidents

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: experimenting without safeguards
- Risk: having no steady-state metrics
- Risk: running first in production

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as testing resilience, and you can manage the risks, Chaos Engineering is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with small experiments in a test environment.
