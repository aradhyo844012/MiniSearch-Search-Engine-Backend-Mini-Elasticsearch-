Everything You Need to Know About Arduino Basics

This page collects the essentials about Arduino Basics in one place. In short, Arduino Basics is a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices.

Core concepts:
- sketches
- digital and analog pins
- setup and loop functions
- the serial monitor

Where it is used:
- reading sensors
- driving motors and LEDs
- learning electronics

Common problems:
- wiring errors
- blocking delays in the loop
- insufficient power for motors

Tools to know:
- Arduino IDE
- PlatformIO
- Tinkercad

Remember this above all: a tiny brain that watches inputs and flips switches is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test each sensor on its own before combining them.
