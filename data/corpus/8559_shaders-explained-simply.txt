Shaders Explained Simply

Here is Shaders without the jargon: it is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.

Think of Shaders as a tiny painter that colors each pixel following your instructions. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: vertex shaders, fragment shaders, uniforms, and GLSL. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with vertex shaders, then add fragment shaders.

You will meet Shaders in work such as lighting effects, water and fire visuals, and post-processing.

One practical tip is to start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.

Once Shaders feels comfortable, read about Entity Component Systems, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
