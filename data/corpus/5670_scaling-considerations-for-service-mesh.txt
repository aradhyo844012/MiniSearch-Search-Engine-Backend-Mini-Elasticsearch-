Scaling Considerations for Service Mesh

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Service Mesh changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding sidecar proxies and getting securing service traffic right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for added latency and complexity and adopting it too early, and measure with Istio before changing anything.

Large scale: traffic policies and observability move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around retries and timeouts at this stage.

Before you scale, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
