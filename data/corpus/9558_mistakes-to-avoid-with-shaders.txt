Mistakes to Avoid with Shaders

Everyone learns Shaders partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: branches that slow the GPU. It usually appears when someone skips vertex shaders and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: precision errors. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of lighting effects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: platform differences. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on GLSL to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
