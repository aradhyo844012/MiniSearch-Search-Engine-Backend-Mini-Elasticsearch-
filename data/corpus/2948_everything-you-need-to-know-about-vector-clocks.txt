Everything You Need to Know About Vector Clocks

This page collects the essentials about Vector Clocks in one place. In short, Vector Clocks is counters that let distributed processes track the order of events without a shared clock.

Core concepts:
- logical time
- per-process counters
- the happened-before relation
- conflict detection

Where it is used:
- detecting concurrent edits
- replicated databases
- collaborative tools

Common problems:
- clocks that grow with many nodes
- confusing them with wall-clock time
- never pruning old entries

Tools to know:
- Riak
- the Amazon Dynamo paper
- CRDT libraries

Remember this above all: each person in a group chat keeping a tally of messages seen from everyone is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
