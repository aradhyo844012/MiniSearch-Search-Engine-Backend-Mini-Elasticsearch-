Mistakes to Avoid with Mobile App Security

Everyone learns Mobile App Security partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: storing tokens in plain text. It usually appears when someone skips secure storage and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: hard-coded API keys. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of banking apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: requesting unnecessary permissions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on permissions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
