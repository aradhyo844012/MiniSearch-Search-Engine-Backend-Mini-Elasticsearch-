Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Cloud Cost Optimization

This path is for a learner who has never studied Cloud Cost Optimization before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability) and learn right-sizing and reserved capacity.

Stage 2, hands-on: install AWS Cost Explorer, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving cutting idle resources.

Stage 3, depth: study tagging and usage reports, then deliberately explore this problem: forgotten test environments.

Stage 4, application: apply Cloud Cost Optimization to budgeting by team, compare Infracost with Kubecost, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Cloud Cost Optimization to someone else, and then move on to Disaster Recovery.
