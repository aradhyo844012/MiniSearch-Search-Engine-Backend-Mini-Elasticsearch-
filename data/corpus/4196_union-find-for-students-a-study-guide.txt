Union-Find for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Union-Find.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Union-Find in your own words.
2. Describe parent pointers, union operation, find operation, and path compression.
3. Give examples from detecting connected components and building minimum spanning trees.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Union-Find is a data structure that tracks which items belong to the same group and merges groups quickly. Then draw a set of club rosters where two clubs can merge into one on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is forgetting path compression a problem? How does union operation relate to find operation? When would you choose Python lists or scipy.sparse.csgraph?

Study habit: implement path compression and union by rank together.

When you feel ready, compare Union-Find with Minimum Spanning Trees and write down three differences.
