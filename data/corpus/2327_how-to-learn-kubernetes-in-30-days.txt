How to Learn Kubernetes in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Kubernetes. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Kubernetes in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications.

Cloud resources are easy to create and just as easy to forget, which makes costs grow quietly.

Week 1: learn pods and deployments, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with kubectl on a small task such as running many containers.
Week 3: build a mini project around rolling updates.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as resource limits missing, and explain Kubernetes to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include kubectl, Helm, and minikube.

With steady practice, Kubernetes will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
