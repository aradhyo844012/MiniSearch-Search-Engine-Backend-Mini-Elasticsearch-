Data Augmentation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Data Augmentation.

Do:
- Learn transformations before anything else.
- Try Albumentations on a small example.
- Make it a habit to augment only the training data.
- Test the case of image models early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: changes that alter the label.
- Ignore this: augmenting the test set.
- Ignore this: unrealistic transformations.
- Adopt Data Augmentation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Data Augmentation, a little patience with label preservation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
