Practice Exercises for SQL Basics

These exercises build real understanding of SQL Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble reading customer records or building reports.
Exercise 2: Compare PostgreSQL and DB Browser for SQLite for changing stored data and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how ORDER BY and INSERT and UPDATE differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: forgetting the WHERE clause on updates. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Pick SQLite and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: SQL Basics is the core SQL commands for reading and changing data in tables.
