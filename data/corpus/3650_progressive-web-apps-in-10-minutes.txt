Progressive Web Apps in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Progressive Web Apps.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Progressive Web Apps is web applications that can be installed and work offline like native apps.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn web app manifest, service worker, installability, and responsive design.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a website that packs its own suitcase so it still works when the network is gone. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through offline-friendly tools, lightweight alternatives to native apps, and low-bandwidth markets.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for ignoring platform limitations and poor offline fallbacks, then try Lighthouse for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test the app in airplane mode.
