Understanding System Design Interviews Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists.

In that picture, requirements is the first rule you follow, scaling estimates is the next step, and trade-offs and components keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices, and it involves details such as jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for whiteboard sketches to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain preparing for senior roles to a friend.
