Augmented and Virtual Reality in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Augmented and Virtual Reality.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn head tracking, rendering at high frame rates, spatial mapping, and input methods.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through training simulations, games, and remote collaboration.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for motion sickness from low frame rates and heavy assets, then try Unity XR for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
