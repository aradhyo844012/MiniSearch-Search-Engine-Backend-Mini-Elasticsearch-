Biometric Authentication Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Biometric Authentication regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip enrollment; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for Android BiometricPrompt first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Face ID APIs.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit matching before anything else.
4. Make sure you can offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
5. Test the case of phone unlock early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to building access as well as to Biometric Authentication in general.
