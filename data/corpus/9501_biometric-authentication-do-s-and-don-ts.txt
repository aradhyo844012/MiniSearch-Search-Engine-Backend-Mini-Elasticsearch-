Biometric Authentication Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Biometric Authentication.

Do:
- Learn enrollment before anything else.
- Try Android BiometricPrompt on a small example.
- Make it a habit to offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
- Test the case of phone unlock early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: spoofing attacks.
- Ignore this: biased recognition.
- Ignore this: credentials that cannot be changed once leaked.
- Adopt Biometric Authentication just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Biometric Authentication, a little patience with matching pays off more than any clever shortcut.
