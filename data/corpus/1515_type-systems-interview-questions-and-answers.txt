Type Systems Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Type Systems.

Q: Explain Type Systems in simple words.
A: Type Systems refers to the rules a language uses to classify values and catch mistakes. I would add an example involving catching bugs early.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: mypy, TypeScript, and Pyright.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: static and dynamic typing, type inference, generics, and type annotations.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: treating annotations as guarantees at runtime; overusing any types; and fighting the type checker.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: treating annotations as guarantees at runtime.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as static and dynamic typing, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: How does it connect to Object-Oriented Programming?
A: Both belong to the area of Programming, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
