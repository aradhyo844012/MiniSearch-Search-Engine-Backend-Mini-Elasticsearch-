Dijkstra's Algorithm for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Dijkstra's Algorithm.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Dijkstra's Algorithm in your own words.
2. Describe priority queue, distance table, edge relaxation, and visited set.
3. Give examples from route planning and network routing.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Dijkstra's Algorithm is a method for finding the shortest path from one node to all others in a graph with non-negative weights. Then draw a GPS that keeps expanding to the nearest unvisited intersection on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is using it with negative edge weights a problem? How does distance table relate to edge relaxation? When would you choose Python heapq or NetworkX?

Study habit: stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue.

When you feel ready, compare Dijkstra's Algorithm with Topological Sorting and write down three differences.
