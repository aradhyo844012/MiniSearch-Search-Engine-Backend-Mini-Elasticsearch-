Responsible AI and Bias for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Responsible AI and Bias.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Responsible AI and Bias in your own words.
2. Describe bias in data, fairness metrics, transparency, and human oversight.
3. Give examples from hiring tools and lending models.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe. Then draw a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is assuming data is neutral a problem? How does fairness metrics relate to transparency? When would you choose Fairlearn or AI Fairness 360?

Study habit worth keeping: test performance separately for different groups.

When you feel ready, compare Responsible AI and Bias with AI Agents and write down three differences.
