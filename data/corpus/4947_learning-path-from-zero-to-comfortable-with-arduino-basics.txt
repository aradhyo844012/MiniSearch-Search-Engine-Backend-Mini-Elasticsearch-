Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Arduino Basics

This path is for a learner who has never studied Arduino Basics before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Arduino Basics is a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices) and learn sketches and digital and analog pins.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Arduino IDE, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving reading sensors.

Stage 3, depth: study setup and loop functions and the serial monitor, then deliberately explore this problem: wiring errors.

Stage 4, application: apply Arduino Basics to driving motors and LEDs, compare PlatformIO with Tinkercad, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Arduino Basics to someone else, and then move on to React Native.
