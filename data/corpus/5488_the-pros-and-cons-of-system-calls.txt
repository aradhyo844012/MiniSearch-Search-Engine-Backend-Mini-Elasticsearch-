The Pros and Cons of System Calls

No technology is free of trade-offs, and System Calls is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports reading and writing files
- Makes creating processes easier
- Helps with using network sockets

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring error codes
- Risk: making too many small calls
- Risk: unsafe assumptions about blocking

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as reading and writing files, and you can manage the risks, System Calls is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do.
