Debugging Color Spaces: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Color Spaces, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: ignoring gamma, mixing color spaces, and poor contrast choices.
Step 3: revisit RGB and HSL, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use browser color pickers or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Color Spaces in work such as web design come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, work in one color space and convert only at the end. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
