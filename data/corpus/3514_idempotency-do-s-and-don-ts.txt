Idempotency Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Idempotency.

Do:
- Learn idempotency keys before anything else.
- Try Stripe idempotency keys on a small example.
- Make it a habit to store the result with the key so retries return the same response.
- Test the case of payment APIs early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: keys that expire too soon.
- Ignore this: side effects outside the guarded step.
- Ignore this: reusing keys for different requests.
- Adopt Idempotency just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Idempotency, a little patience with safe retries pays off more than any clever shortcut.
