Where to Go Next After Learning Computability and the Halting Problem

You have met the basics of Computability and the Halting Problem. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Probability Basics, which belongs to the same area as Computability and the Halting Problem (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of Turing machines, decidability, the halting problem or reductions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around proofs of impossibility with Turing machine simulators, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming everything is solvable.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Computability and the Halting Problem to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
