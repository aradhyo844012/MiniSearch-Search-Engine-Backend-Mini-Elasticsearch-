A/B Testing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for A/B Testing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.
Key terms: control and variant, random assignment, metrics, and sample size.
Typical uses: testing page designs, comparing ranking algorithms, and evaluating new features.
Risks: stopping tests early, testing too many variants, and ignoring sample size.
Tools: Optimizely, GrowthBook, and statsmodels.

Mental picture: taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests.

Habit to build: decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
