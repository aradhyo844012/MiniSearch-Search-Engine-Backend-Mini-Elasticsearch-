Font Rendering Explained Simply

If Font Rendering sounds intimidating, relax. It is turning font outlines into readable text on screen, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Font Rendering as a calligrapher tracing letter shapes onto a grid of pixels. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: glyphs, hinting, anti-aliasing, and kerning. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with glyphs, then add hinting.

People reach for Font Rendering when they work on web pages, operating systems, and PDF viewers.

One practical tip is to subset fonts to the characters you need.
