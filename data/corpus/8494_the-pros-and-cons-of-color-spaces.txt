The Pros and Cons of Color Spaces

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Color Spaces is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports web design
- Makes photo editing easier
- Helps with printing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring gamma
- Risk: mixing color spaces
- Risk: poor contrast choices

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as web design, and you can manage the risks, Color Spaces is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, work in one color space and convert only at the end.
