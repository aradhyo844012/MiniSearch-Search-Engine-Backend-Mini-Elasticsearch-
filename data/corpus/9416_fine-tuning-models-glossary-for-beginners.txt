Fine-Tuning Models Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Fine-Tuning Models feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Fine-Tuning Models: adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data.
pretrained weights: a core term, most visible in domain-specific language tasks.
training data: a core term that beginners often confuse with the learning rate.
the learning rate: a core term that connects to evaluation.
evaluation: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Hugging Face: a tool commonly used for style adaptation.

Come back to this Fine-Tuning Models list whenever documentation for PyTorch uses a word you do not recognize.
