Why State Management Matters for Developers

Developers who understand State Management make better decisions, because State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data.

Three situations show why it matters. First, large single-page apps. Second, shared shopping carts. Third, multi-step form wizards. In each case, a solid grasp of single source of truth and actions separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: putting everything in global state, duplicated state that drifts apart, and unclear update paths.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Redux, Zustand, and Vue Pinia cover most needs.
