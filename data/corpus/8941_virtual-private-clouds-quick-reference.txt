Virtual Private Clouds Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Virtual Private Clouds. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources.
Key terms: subnets, route tables, security groups, and gateways.
Typical uses: separating public and private services, connecting to offices, and securing databases.
Risks: overlapping address ranges, databases in public subnets, and missing routes.
Tools: AWS VPC, Terraform, and VPC Flow Logs.

Mental picture: a gated neighborhood inside a big city.

Habit to build: keep databases in private subnets.
