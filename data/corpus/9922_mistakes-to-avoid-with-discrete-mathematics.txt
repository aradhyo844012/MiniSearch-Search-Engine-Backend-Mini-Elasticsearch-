Mistakes to Avoid with Discrete Mathematics

Everyone learns Discrete Mathematics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: skipping proofs. It usually appears when someone skips sets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: confusing sets with sequences. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of algorithm analysis. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: careless notation. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on proofs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: practice writing small proofs by hand.
