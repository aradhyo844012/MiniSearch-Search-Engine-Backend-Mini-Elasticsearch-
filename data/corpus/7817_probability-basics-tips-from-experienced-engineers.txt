Probability Basics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Probability Basics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Python random first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as NumPy.
2. Make sure you can simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit conditional probability before anything else.
4. Write down why you chose Probability Basics so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: confusing independent and exclusive events.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to machine learning as well as to Probability Basics in general.
