Digital Twins in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Digital Twins.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sensor data, simulation models, synchronization, and analytics.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through factory monitoring, building management, and predictive maintenance.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for stale data and over-detailed models, then try Azure Digital Twins for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to define the question the twin must answer first.
