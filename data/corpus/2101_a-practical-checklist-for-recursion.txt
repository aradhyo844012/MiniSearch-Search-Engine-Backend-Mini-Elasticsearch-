A Practical Checklist for Recursion

Use this checklist before you rely on Recursion in a project.

1. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on recursive case.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers base case.
3. Guard against this risk: missing base cases.
4. Try the approach on a small case, for example traversing trees.
5. Review how Recursion interacts with Dynamic Programming.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Pick a tool to start with, such as Python sys.setrecursionlimit or debuggers.
8. Confirm that your team agrees on what Recursion should accomplish.
