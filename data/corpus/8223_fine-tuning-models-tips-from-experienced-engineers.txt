Fine-Tuning Models Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Fine-Tuning Models regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit training data before anything else.
2. Reach for Hugging Face first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as PyTorch.
3. Do not skip pretrained weights; everything else builds on it.
4. Write down why you chose Fine-Tuning Models so the next person understands the decision.
5. Test the case of domain-specific language tasks early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to style adaptation as well as to Fine-Tuning Models in general.
