Where to Go Next After Learning Service Workers

You have met the basics of Service Workers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about State Management, which belongs to the same area as Service Workers (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of registration, install and activate events, cache storage or fetch handlers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around faster repeat visits with Workbox, and note every problem you meet, starting with caching stale files forever.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Service Workers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
