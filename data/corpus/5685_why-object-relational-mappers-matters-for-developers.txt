Why Object-Relational Mappers Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Object-Relational Mappers make better decisions, because Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code.

Three situations show why it matters. First, building CRUD apps quickly. Second, switching databases. Third, keeping schema and code aligned. In each case, a solid grasp of models and queries separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: N+1 queries, loading entire tables, and hiding slow SQL.

Good tools exist, including SQLAlchemy, the Django ORM, and Hibernate, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
