Combining Blue-Green Deployment with Log Aggregation in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Blue-Green Deployment and Log Aggregation can work together in a single project.

Blue-Green Deployment is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one. Log Aggregation is collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place.

Imagine a project that involves zero-downtime releases and troubleshooting across services. Blue-Green Deployment brings blue and green environments, while Log Aggregation brings shippers.

A sensible plan is to start with AWS CodeDeploy for the Blue-Green Deployment part and Elasticsearch for the Log Aggregation part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: doubling infrastructure costs on one side, and unstructured logs on the other.

Picture the whole as swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on, working alongside gathering notes from every desk into a single searchable binder.

Two habits help: first, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven; second, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.
