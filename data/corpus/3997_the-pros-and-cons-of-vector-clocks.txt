The Pros and Cons of Vector Clocks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Vector Clocks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports detecting concurrent edits
- Makes replicated databases easier
- Helps with collaborative tools

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: clocks that grow with many nodes
- Risk: confusing them with wall-clock time
- Risk: never pruning old entries

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as detecting concurrent edits, and you can manage the risks, Vector Clocks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
