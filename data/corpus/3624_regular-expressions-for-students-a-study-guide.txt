Regular Expressions for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Regular Expressions.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Regular Expressions in your own words.
2. Describe character classes, quantifiers, groups, and anchors.
3. Give examples from validating input and searching logs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text. Then draw a stencil that only lets certain shapes of text show through on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is catastrophic backtracking a problem? How does quantifiers relate to groups? When would you choose the Python re module or regex101?

Study habit: build patterns in small steps and test each one.

When you feel ready, compare Regular Expressions with Java Streams and write down three differences.
