A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Content Security Policy

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Content Security Policy.

Morning: Sneha starts by reviewing a ticket about reducing the impact of XSS. Before touching any code, Sneha re-reads the notes on directives to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Sneha pairs with a teammate to explore nonces with browser DevTools. A small test exposes the problem of allowing unsafe-inline, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving blocking unwanted scripts. Sneha sketches a guest list at the door of a building on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Sneha writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to start in report-only mode to learn what would break.

Days like this are typical. Content Security Policy is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
