Stored Procedures and Triggers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Stored Procedures and Triggers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Stored Procedures and Triggers in your own words.
2. Describe procedures, triggers, parameters, and side effects.
3. Give examples from enforcing rules close to the data and audit logging.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Stored Procedures and Triggers is code stored inside the database that runs on demand or automatically when data changes. Then draw a house rule that fires automatically whenever something is placed on a shelf on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is hidden logic that surprises developers a problem? How does triggers relate to parameters? When would you choose PL/pgSQL or MySQL Workbench?

Study habit: keep triggers small and well documented.

When you feel ready, compare Stored Procedures and Triggers with Database Schema Design for Beginners and write down three differences.
