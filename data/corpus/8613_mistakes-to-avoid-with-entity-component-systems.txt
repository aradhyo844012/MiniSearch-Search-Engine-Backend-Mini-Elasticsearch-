Mistakes to Avoid with Entity Component Systems

Everyone learns Entity Component Systems partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overengineering small games. It usually appears when someone skips entities and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: components that hold logic. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of large simulations. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hard debugging of system order. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on queries to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep components as plain data.
