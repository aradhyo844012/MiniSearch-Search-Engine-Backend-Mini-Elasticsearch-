Teaching Cloud Cost Optimization to a Teammate

Explaining Cloud Cost Optimization to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving cutting idle resources, naming right-sizing and reserved capacity as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain tagging in Cloud Cost Optimization back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, forgotten test environments, and suggest this habit: tag every resource with an owner.
