Skills to Build Around Data Quality Checks

Knowing the definition of Data Quality Checks is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with null checks and uniqueness, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Great Expectations on a Data Quality Checks task, and try at least one alternative such as dbt tests.

Judgment: learn to decide when Data Quality Checks fits a problem such as protecting dashboards, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line.

Habits: check data as early as it enters the pipeline, and write down what you learn after each project.
