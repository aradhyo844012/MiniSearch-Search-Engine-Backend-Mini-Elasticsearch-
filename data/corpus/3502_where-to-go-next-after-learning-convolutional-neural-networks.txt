Where to Go Next After Learning Convolutional Neural Networks

You have met the basics of Convolutional Neural Networks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Reinforcement Learning, which belongs to the same area as Convolutional Neural Networks (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of filters, pooling, feature maps or stride, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around medical image analysis with PyTorch, and note every problem you meet, starting with too little training data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Convolutional Neural Networks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
