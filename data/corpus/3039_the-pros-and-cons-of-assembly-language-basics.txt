The Pros and Cons of Assembly Language Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Assembly Language Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports understanding compiled code
- Makes debugging crashes easier
- Helps with writing tiny boot code

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: wrong calling conventions
- Risk: stack corruption
- Risk: platform differences

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as understanding compiled code, and you can manage the risks, Assembly Language Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, read the assembly of a tiny C function to see what the compiler does.
