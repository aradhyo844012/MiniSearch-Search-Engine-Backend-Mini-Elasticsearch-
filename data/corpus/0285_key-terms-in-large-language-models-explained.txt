Key Terms in Large Language Models Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Large Language Models much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Large Language Models, they usually mean very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

A model is only as good as the data used to train and test it. Reproducible experiments make it far easier to trust and improve a model.

tokens: a key term in Large Language Models. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
attention: a key term in Large Language Models. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
pretraining: a key term in Large Language Models. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
prompts: a key term in Large Language Models. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Large Language Models.
