Star Schema Modeling for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Star Schema Modeling.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Star Schema Modeling in your own words.
2. Describe fact tables, dimension tables, surrogate keys, and grain.
3. Give examples from business intelligence reports and data warehouses.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables. Then draw a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing different grains a problem? How does dimension tables relate to surrogate keys? When would you choose dbt or Snowflake?

Study habit worth keeping: state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.

When you feel ready, compare Star Schema Modeling with Data Quality Checks and write down three differences.
