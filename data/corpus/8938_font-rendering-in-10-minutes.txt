Font Rendering in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Font Rendering.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn glyphs, hinting, anti-aliasing, and kerning.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a calligrapher tracing letter shapes onto a grid of pixels. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through web pages, operating systems, and PDF viewers.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for missing font fallbacks and blurry text from scaling, then try FreeType for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to subset fonts to the characters you need.
