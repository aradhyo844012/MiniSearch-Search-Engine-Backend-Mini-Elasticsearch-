Teaching Threat Modeling to a Teammate

Explaining Threat Modeling to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: walking around a house asking where a burglar might get in. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Threat Modeling is a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving designing new features, naming assets and attackers as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain trust boundaries in Threat Modeling back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, doing it once and never again, and suggest this habit: draw the data flow diagram first.
