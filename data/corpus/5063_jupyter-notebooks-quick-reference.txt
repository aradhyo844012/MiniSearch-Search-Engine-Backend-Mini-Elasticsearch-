Jupyter Notebooks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Jupyter Notebooks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.
Key terms: cells, kernels, markdown, and outputs.
Typical uses: exploring data, teaching and demos, and sharing analysis.
Risks: running cells out of order, hidden state, and committing outputs that contain secrets.
Tools: JupyterLab, VS Code, and Google Colab.

Mental picture: a lab notebook that can run its own experiments.

Habit to build: restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
