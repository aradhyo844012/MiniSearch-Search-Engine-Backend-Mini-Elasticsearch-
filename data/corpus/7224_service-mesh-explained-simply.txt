Service Mesh Explained Simply

If Service Mesh sounds intimidating, relax. It is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Service Mesh as a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sidecar proxies, mutual TLS, traffic policies, and observability. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sidecar proxies, then add mutual TLS.

You will meet Service Mesh in work such as securing service traffic, retries and timeouts, and fine-grained routing.

One practical tip is to adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.

Once Service Mesh feels comfortable, read about Feature Flags, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
