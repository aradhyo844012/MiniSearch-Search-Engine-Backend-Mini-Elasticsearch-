Technical Writing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Technical Writing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Technical Writing in your own words.
2. Describe audience, structure, examples, and editing.
3. Give examples from documentation and blog posts.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience. Then draw giving directions to a stranger on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is assuming too much knowledge a problem? How does structure relate to examples? When would you choose Markdown or Grammarly?

Study habit: state the main point in the first two sentences.

When you feel ready, compare Technical Writing with Time Management for Developers and write down three differences.
