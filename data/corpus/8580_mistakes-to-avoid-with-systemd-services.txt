Mistakes to Avoid with systemd Services

Everyone learns systemd Services partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: editing unit files without reloading the daemon. It usually appears when someone skips unit files and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing restart policies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of starting services at boot. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring dependency ordering. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on journal logs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.
