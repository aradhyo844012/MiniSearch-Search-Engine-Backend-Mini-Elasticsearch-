Skills to Build Around Software Estimation

Knowing the definition of Software Estimation is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with story points and ranges, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with planning poker on a Software Estimation task, and try at least one alternative such as Jira.

Judgment: learn to decide when Software Estimation fits a problem such as sprint planning, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable.

Habits: give ranges instead of single numbers, and write down what you learn after each project.
