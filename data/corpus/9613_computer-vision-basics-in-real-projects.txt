Computer Vision Basics in Real Projects

Reading about Computer Vision Basics is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: face detection. Here, pixels drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against lighting changes.

Scenario two: document scanning. This time filters and edges matters most, and tools such as OpenCV help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: quality inspection. The key idea is feature detection, and the biggest risk is biased datasets.

Across all three scenarios, Computer Vision Basics stays the same idea: teaching computers to extract information from images and video. Only the details change.
