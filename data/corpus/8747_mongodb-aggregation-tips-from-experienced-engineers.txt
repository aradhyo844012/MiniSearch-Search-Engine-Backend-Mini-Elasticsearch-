MongoDB Aggregation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with MongoDB Aggregation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip pipeline stages; everything else builds on it.
2. Be careful with this one: putting $match late.
3. Write down why you chose MongoDB Aggregation so the next person understands the decision.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit $match and $group before anything else.
5. Reach for MongoDB Compass first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as mongosh.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to building analytics queries as well as to MongoDB Aggregation in general.
