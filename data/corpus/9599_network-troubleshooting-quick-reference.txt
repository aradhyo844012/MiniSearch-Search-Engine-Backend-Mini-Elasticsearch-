Network Troubleshooting Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Network Troubleshooting. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a step-by-step process for finding out why a network connection fails or is slow.
Key terms: protocol layers, ping and traceroute, DNS checks, and packet captures.
Typical uses: fixing office connectivity, diagnosing slow apps, and verifying firewall changes.
Risks: changing several things at once, blaming the application too early, and ignoring DNS.
Tools: ping, traceroute, and Wireshark.

Mental picture: a doctor who checks the heart, lungs and blood pressure in order.

Habit to build: work from the bottom layer upward.
