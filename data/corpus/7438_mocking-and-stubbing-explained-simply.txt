Mocking and Stubbing Explained Simply

If Mocking and Stubbing sounds intimidating, relax. It is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Mocking and Stubbing as a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: mocks, stubs, spies, and fakes. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with mocks, then add stubs.

You will meet Mocking and Stubbing in work such as isolating units, simulating failures, and avoiding network calls.

One practical tip is to prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.

Once Mocking and Stubbing feels comfortable, read about Property-Based Testing, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
