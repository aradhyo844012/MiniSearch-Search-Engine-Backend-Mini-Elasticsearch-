GPUs and Parallelism Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with GPUs and Parallelism regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for CUDA first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as PyTorch.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit kernels before anything else.
3. Make sure you can minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
4. Do not skip threads and blocks; everything else builds on it.
5. Be careful with this one: slow data transfers.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to rendering graphics as well.
