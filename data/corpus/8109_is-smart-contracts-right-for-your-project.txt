Is Smart Contracts Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Smart Contracts, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.

It is a good fit when your work involves token systems or automated escrow, and when your team understands contract code.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn transactions, or when you cannot manage these risks: bugs that cannot be patched, reentrancy attacks, and high gas costs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether gas fees and immutability are part of the answer. If they are, Smart Contracts is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with decentralized applications and audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
