Support Vector Machines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Support Vector Machines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for scikit-learn first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as LIBSVM.
2. Do not skip margin; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can scale features and try a linear kernel first.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit support vectors before anything else.
5. Test the case of text classification early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to small high-dimensional datasets as well.
