Hyperparameter Tuning Explained Simply

Here is Hyperparameter Tuning without the jargon: it is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns.

Think of Hyperparameter Tuning as adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: search spaces, grid search, random search, and validation scores. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with search spaces, then add grid search.

People reach for Hyperparameter Tuning when they work on improving model accuracy, comparing algorithms, and running automated experiments.

One practical tip is to try random search before anything fancier.

Once Hyperparameter Tuning feels comfortable, read about K-Means Clustering, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
