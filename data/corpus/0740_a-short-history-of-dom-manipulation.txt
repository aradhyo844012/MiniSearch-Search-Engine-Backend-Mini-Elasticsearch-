A Short History of DOM Manipulation

Ideas behind DOM Manipulation developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Put simply, DOM Manipulation covers reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Accessible design helps everyone, including people on slow connections or small screens. Web pages must work across many browsers, devices and network conditions.

To understand DOM Manipulation, start with selectors. Then move on to events, and finally look at nodes and updates. A good way to remember selectors is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

A solid grasp of DOM Manipulation also makes Responsive Design easier to understand.
