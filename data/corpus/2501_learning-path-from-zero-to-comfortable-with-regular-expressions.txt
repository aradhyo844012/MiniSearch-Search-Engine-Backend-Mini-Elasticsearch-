Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Regular Expressions

This path is for a learner who has never studied Regular Expressions before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text) and learn character classes and quantifiers.

Stage 2, hands-on: install the Python re module, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving validating input.

Stage 3, depth: study groups and anchors, then deliberately explore this problem: catastrophic backtracking.

Stage 4, application: apply Regular Expressions to searching logs, compare regex101 with grep, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Regular Expressions to someone else, and then move on to Java Streams.
