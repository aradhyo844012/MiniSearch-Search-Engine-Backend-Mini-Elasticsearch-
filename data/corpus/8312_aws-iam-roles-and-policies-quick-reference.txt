AWS IAM Roles and Policies Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for AWS IAM Roles and Policies. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account.
Key terms: users and roles, policies, least privilege, and temporary credentials.
Typical uses: securing deployments, granting services access, and auditing permissions.
Risks: wildcard permissions, long-lived access keys, and unused roles.
Tools: IAM Access Analyzer, the AWS CLI, and Terraform.

Mental picture: key cards that open only the doors a person needs.

Habit to build: grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.
