Penetration Testing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Penetration Testing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do.
Key terms: scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation, and reporting.
Typical uses: pre-launch security checks, compliance audits, and validating defenses.
Risks: testing without written permission, ignoring the findings afterward, and relying only on automated scans.
Tools: OWASP ZAP, Burp Suite, and Nmap.

Mental picture: hiring a locksmith to test your doors.

Habit to build: get written authorization before any test.
