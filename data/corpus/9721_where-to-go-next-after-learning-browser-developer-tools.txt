Where to Go Next After Learning Browser Developer Tools

You have met the basics of Browser Developer Tools. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Monorepos, which belongs to the same area as Browser Developer Tools (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of the elements panel, the console, the network panel or the performance profiler, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around inspecting API calls with Chrome DevTools, and note every problem you meet, starting with editing the page and forgetting to copy the changes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Browser Developer Tools to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
