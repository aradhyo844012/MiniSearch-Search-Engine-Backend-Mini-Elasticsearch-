The Pros and Cons of Floating-Point Arithmetic

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Floating-Point Arithmetic is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports scientific computing
- Makes graphics easier
- Helps with financial calculations that need care

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: comparing floats with equality
- Risk: accumulated rounding errors
- Risk: using floats for money

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as scientific computing, and you can manage the risks, Floating-Point Arithmetic is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, compare floats with a tolerance and use decimals for money.
