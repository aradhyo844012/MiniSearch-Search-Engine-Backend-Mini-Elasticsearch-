Packet Switching Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Packet Switching.

Do:
- Learn packets before anything else.
- Try Wireshark on a small example.
- Make it a habit to capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
- Test the case of internet traffic early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: packet loss.
- Ignore this: out-of-order delivery.
- Ignore this: large packets being fragmented.
- Adopt Packet Switching just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Packet Switching, a little patience with headers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
