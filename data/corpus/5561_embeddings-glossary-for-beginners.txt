Embeddings Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Embeddings feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Embeddings: lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.
vectors: a core term, most visible in semantic search.
cosine similarity: a core term that beginners often confuse with dimensionality.
dimensionality: a core term that connects to nearest neighbor search.
nearest neighbor search: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
sentence-transformers: a tool commonly used for recommendations.

Come back to this Embeddings list whenever documentation for FAISS uses a word you do not recognize.
