Ray Tracing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Ray Tracing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Ray Tracing: a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.
rays: a core term, most visible in movie visuals.
intersections: a core term that beginners often confuse with reflection and refraction.
reflection and refraction: a core term that connects to shadows.
shadows: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Blender Cycles: a tool commonly used for architectural visualization.

Come back to this Ray Tracing list whenever documentation for NVIDIA OptiX uses a word you do not recognize.
