Scaling Considerations for NULL Handling in SQL

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how NULL Handling in SQL changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding three-valued logic and getting optional fields right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for comparing with equals instead of IS NULL and NOT IN with NULLs, and measure with PostgreSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: COALESCE and NULL in aggregates move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around outer joins at this stage.

Before you scale, use IS NULL and COALESCE instead of comparing with equals. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
