Practice Exercises for Graph Theory Basics

These exercises build real understanding of Graph Theory Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Graphviz and Gephi for dependency analysis and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mixing directed and undirected graphs. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble social networks or maps.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Graph Theory Basics and include the term vertices and edges.
Exercise 5: Explain how paths and cycles and connectivity differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them.
