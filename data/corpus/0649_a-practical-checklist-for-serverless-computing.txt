A Practical Checklist for Serverless Computing

Use this checklist before you rely on Serverless Computing in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: vendor lock-in.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as AWS Lambda or Cloud Functions.
4. Confirm that your team agrees on what Serverless Computing should accomplish.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Guard against this risk: hard local debugging.
7. Guard against this risk: cold start delays.
8. Review how Serverless Computing interacts with Monitoring and Observability.
9. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on event triggers.
