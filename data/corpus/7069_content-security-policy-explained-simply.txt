Content Security Policy Explained Simply

If Content Security Policy sounds intimidating, relax. It is a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Content Security Policy as a guest list at the door of a building. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: directives, nonces, report-only mode, and trusted sources. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with directives, then add nonces.

You will meet Content Security Policy in work such as reducing the impact of XSS, blocking unwanted scripts, and monitoring policy violations.

One practical tip is to start in report-only mode to learn what would break.
