Configuration Management Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Configuration Management.

Do:
- Learn environment variables before anything else.
- Try dotenv on a small example.
- Make it a habit to fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing.
- Test the case of separating development and production early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: committing secrets.
- Ignore this: inconsistent defaults.
- Ignore this: undocumented settings.
- Adopt Configuration Management just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Configuration Management, a little patience with config files pays off more than any clever shortcut.
