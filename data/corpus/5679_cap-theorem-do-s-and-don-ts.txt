CAP Theorem Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with CAP Theorem.

Do:
- Learn consistency before anything else.
- Try Apache Cassandra on a small example.
- Make it a habit to ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors.
- Test the case of choosing database types early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating it as a three-way menu.
- Ignore this: ignoring latency trade-offs in normal operation.
- Ignore this: assuming partitions never happen.
- Adopt CAP Theorem just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with CAP Theorem, a little patience with availability pays off more than any clever shortcut.
