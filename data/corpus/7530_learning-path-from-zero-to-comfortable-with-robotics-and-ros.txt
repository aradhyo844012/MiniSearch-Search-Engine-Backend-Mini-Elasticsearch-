Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Robotics and ROS

This path is for a learner who has never studied Robotics and ROS before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software) and learn sensors and actuators.

Stage 2, hands-on: install ROS 2, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving warehouse robots.

Stage 3, depth: study nodes and topics and control loops, then deliberately explore this problem: ignoring sensor noise.

Stage 4, application: apply Robotics and ROS to drones, compare Gazebo with Arduino, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Robotics and ROS to someone else, and then move on to Edge Computing.
