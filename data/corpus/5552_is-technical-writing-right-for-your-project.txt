Is Technical Writing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Technical Writing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.

It is a good fit when your work involves documentation or blog posts, and when your team understands audience.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn structure, or when you cannot manage these risks: assuming too much knowledge, burying the main point, and long unbroken paragraphs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether examples and editing are part of the answer. If they are, Technical Writing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with design proposals and state the main point in the first two sentences.
