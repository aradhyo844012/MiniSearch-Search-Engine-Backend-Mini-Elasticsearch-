Time-Series Databases Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Time-Series Databases feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Time-Series Databases: databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.
timestamps: a core term, most visible in server metrics.
retention policies: a core term that beginners often confuse with downsampling.
downsampling: a core term that connects to tags and fields.
tags and fields: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
InfluxDB: a tool commonly used for sensor readings.

Come back to this Time-Series Databases list whenever documentation for TimescaleDB uses a word you do not recognize.
