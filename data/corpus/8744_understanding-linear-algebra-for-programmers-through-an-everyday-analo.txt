Understanding Linear Algebra for Programmers Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a set of arrows and stretching machines acting on space.

In that picture, vectors is the first rule you follow, matrices is the next step, and dot products and eigenvalues keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning, and it involves details such as mixing row and column conventions that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for NumPy to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain 3D graphics to a friend.
