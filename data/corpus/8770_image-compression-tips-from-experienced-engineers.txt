Image Compression Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Image Compression regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Image Compression so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for ImageMagick first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as mozjpeg.
3. Do not skip lossy and lossless formats; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
5. Be careful with this one: repeated recompression.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to photo storage as well as to Image Compression in general.
