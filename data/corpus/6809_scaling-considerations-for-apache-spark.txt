Scaling Considerations for Apache Spark

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Apache Spark changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding DataFrames and getting large ETL jobs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for data skew and too many small partitions, and measure with PySpark before changing anything.

Large scale: partitions and lazy execution move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around machine learning on big data at this stage.

Before you scale, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
