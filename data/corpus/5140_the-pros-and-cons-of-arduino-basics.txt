The Pros and Cons of Arduino Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Arduino Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports reading sensors
- Makes driving motors and LEDs easier
- Helps with learning electronics

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: wiring errors
- Risk: blocking delays in the loop
- Risk: insufficient power for motors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as reading sensors, and you can manage the risks, Arduino Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test each sensor on its own before combining them.
