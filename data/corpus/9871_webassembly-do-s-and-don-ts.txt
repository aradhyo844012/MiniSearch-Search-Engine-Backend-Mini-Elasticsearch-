WebAssembly Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with WebAssembly.

Do:
- Learn modules before anything else.
- Try Emscripten on a small example.
- Make it a habit to measure before moving code to WebAssembly.
- Test the case of running heavy computation in the browser early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: crossing the JavaScript boundary too often.
- Ignore this: large binaries.
- Ignore this: limited access to browser APIs.
- Adopt WebAssembly just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with WebAssembly, a little patience with linear memory pays off more than any clever shortcut.
