A Practical Checklist for Containers

Use this checklist before you rely on Containers in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example consistent development environments.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as Docker or Podman.
4. Write at least one automated test that covers images.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on layers.
6. Review the core ideas: images, layers, namespaces, and registries.
7. Guard against this risk: running as root.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
