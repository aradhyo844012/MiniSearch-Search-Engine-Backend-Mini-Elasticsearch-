Python Virtual Environments in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Python Virtual Environments.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Python Virtual Environments is isolated folders that hold a project's own Python packages.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn venv, pip, requirements files, and activation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a separate toolbox for each project. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through keeping projects separate, reproducing setups, and testing against different versions.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for installing packages globally and forgetting to activate, then try venv for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to create one environment per project and record its requirements.
