The Pros and Cons of Interrupts

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Interrupts is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports keyboard and mouse input
- Makes timers easier
- Helps with network packets arriving

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: long handlers
- Risk: race conditions with shared data
- Risk: lost interrupts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as keyboard and mouse input, and you can manage the risks, Interrupts is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
