Scaling Considerations for Integration Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Integration Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding test databases and getting verifying API and database interaction right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow suites and flaky external dependencies, and measure with Testcontainers before changing anything.

Large scale: fixtures and test environments move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around catching contract mismatches at this stage.

Before you scale, use disposable containers for test dependencies. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
