Mistakes to Avoid with Union-Find

Everyone learns Union-Find partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: forgetting path compression. It usually appears when someone skips parent pointers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: merging without checking roots. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of detecting connected components. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: using it when deletions are required. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on path compression to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: implement path compression and union by rank together.
