High Availability Design in Real Projects

Reading about High Availability Design is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: online stores. Here, redundancy drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against hidden single points of failure.

Scenario two: banking systems. This time failover matters most, and tools such as load balancers help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: healthcare software. The key idea is health checks, and the biggest risk is untested failover.

Across all three scenarios, High Availability Design stays the same idea: designing systems so they keep working when parts fail. Only the details change.
