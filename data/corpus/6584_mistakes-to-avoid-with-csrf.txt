Mistakes to Avoid with CSRF

Everyone learns CSRF partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using GET requests for state changes. It usually appears when someone skips session cookies and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: skipping tokens on cookie-based APIs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting forms. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: misconfigured SameSite settings. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on state-changing requests to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.
