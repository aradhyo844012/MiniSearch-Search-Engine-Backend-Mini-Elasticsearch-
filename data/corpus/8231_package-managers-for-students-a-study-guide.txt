Package Managers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Package Managers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Package Managers in your own words.
2. Describe repositories, dependency resolution, versions, and signed packages.
3. Give examples from installing system tools and keeping servers updated.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Package Managers is tools that install, update and remove software along with its dependencies. Then draw a supply store that delivers parts together with the parts they need on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing sources that conflict a problem? How does dependency resolution relate to versions? When would you choose apt or dnf?

Study habit: update the package index before installing anything.

When you feel ready, compare Package Managers with Cron Jobs and Scheduling and write down three differences.
