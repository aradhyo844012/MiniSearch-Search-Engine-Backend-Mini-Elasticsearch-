Combining Reinforcement Learning with Hyperparameter Tuning in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Reinforcement Learning and Hyperparameter Tuning can work together in a single project.

Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes. Hyperparameter Tuning is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns.

Imagine a project that involves game playing and improving model accuracy. Reinforcement Learning brings agent, while Hyperparameter Tuning brings search spaces.

A sensible plan is to start with Gymnasium for the Reinforcement Learning part and Optuna for the Hyperparameter Tuning part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: slow training on one side, and searching too many options on the other.

Picture the whole as training a dog with treats for good behavior, working alongside adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear.

Two habits help: first, start with simple environments; second, try random search before anything fancier.
