Skills to Build Around Information Theory and Entropy

Knowing the definition of Information Theory and Entropy is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with bits and entropy, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with the Python math module on a Information Theory and Entropy task, and try at least one alternative such as NumPy.

Judgment: learn to decide when Information Theory and Entropy fits a problem such as data compression, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example measuring how surprised you are by each new message.

Habits: compute the entropy of a short text by hand, and write down what you learn after each project.
