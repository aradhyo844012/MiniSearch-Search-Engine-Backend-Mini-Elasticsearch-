Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Hexagonal Architecture

This path is for a learner who has never studied Hexagonal Architecture before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Hexagonal Architecture is a design that keeps core logic separate from outside systems through ports and adapters) and learn ports and adapters.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Spring, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving testable business logic.

Stage 3, depth: study the core domain and dependency direction, then deliberately explore this problem: too many layers for small apps.

Stage 4, application: apply Hexagonal Architecture to swapping databases, compare FastAPI with pytest, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Hexagonal Architecture to someone else, and then move on to Software Estimation.
