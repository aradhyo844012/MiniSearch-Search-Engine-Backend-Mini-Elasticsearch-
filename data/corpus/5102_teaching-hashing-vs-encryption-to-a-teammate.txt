Teaching Hashing vs Encryption to a Teammate

Explaining Hashing vs Encryption to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a paper shredder compared with a locked suitcase. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Hashing vs Encryption is the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving storing passwords, naming one-way functions and keys as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain collision resistance in Hashing vs Encryption back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, encrypting passwords instead of hashing them, and suggest this habit: ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
