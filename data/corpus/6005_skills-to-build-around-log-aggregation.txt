Skills to Build Around Log Aggregation

Knowing the definition of Log Aggregation is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with shippers and indexing, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Elasticsearch on a Log Aggregation task, and try at least one alternative such as Fluent Bit.

Judgment: learn to decide when Log Aggregation fits a problem such as troubleshooting across services, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example gathering notes from every desk into a single searchable binder.

Habits: use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable, and write down what you learn after each project.
