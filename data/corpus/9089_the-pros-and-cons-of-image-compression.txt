The Pros and Cons of Image Compression

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Image Compression is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports web images
- Makes photo storage easier
- Helps with sending pictures over slow networks

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: repeated recompression
- Risk: the wrong format for graphics
- Risk: excessive quality loss

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as web images, and you can manage the risks, Image Compression is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
