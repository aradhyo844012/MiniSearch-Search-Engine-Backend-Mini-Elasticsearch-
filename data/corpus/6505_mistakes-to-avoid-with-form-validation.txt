Mistakes to Avoid with Form Validation

Everyone learns Form Validation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: validating only on the client. It usually appears when someone skips client-side checks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: vague error messages. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sign-up forms. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: rejecting valid but unusual input. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on constraint attributes to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
