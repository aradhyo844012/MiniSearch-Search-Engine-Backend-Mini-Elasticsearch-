Why Linux Boot Process Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Linux Boot Process make better decisions, because Linux Boot Process is the sequence of steps from powering on a machine to a running operating system.

Three situations show why it matters. First, troubleshooting machines that do not start. Second, configuring dual boot. Third, building custom images. In each case, a solid grasp of firmware and bootloader separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: overwriting the bootloader, wrong kernel parameters, and ignoring recovery options.

Good tools exist, including GRUB, dmesg, and journalctl -b, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
