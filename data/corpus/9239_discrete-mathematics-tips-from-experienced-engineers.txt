Discrete Mathematics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Discrete Mathematics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Discrete Mathematics so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: skipping proofs.
3. Test the case of algorithm analysis early, before the design hardens.
4. Reach for Python sets first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as proof assistants.
5. Do not skip sets; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to database theory as well as to Discrete Mathematics in general.
