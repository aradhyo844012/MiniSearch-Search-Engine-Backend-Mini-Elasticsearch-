Database Indexing Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Database Indexing.

Q: Explain Database Indexing in simple words.
A: Database Indexing refers to auxiliary structures that let a database find rows without scanning a whole table. I would add an example involving speeding up lookups.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with supporting sorted queries, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as B-tree indexes, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: How does it connect to NoSQL Databases?
A: Both belong to the area of Databases, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: B-tree indexes, selectivity, composite indexes, and covering indexes.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: indexing every column; ignoring write overhead; and wrong column order in composite indexes.
