Debugging Disk Partitioning and Mounting: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Disk Partitioning and Mounting, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: wrong device names, mounting without fstab entries, and formatting the wrong disk.
Step 3: revisit partitions and file system types, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use lsblk or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Disk Partitioning and Mounting in work such as adding a new drive come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, double-check device names with lsblk before formatting. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
