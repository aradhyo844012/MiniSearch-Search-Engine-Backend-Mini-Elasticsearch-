Practice Exercises for Blockchain Fundamentals

These exercises build real understanding of Blockchain Fundamentals. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how consensus and decentralization differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Pick Bitcoin Core and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: treating it as a database replacement. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Compare Ethereum and Hyperledger for public records and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Blockchain Fundamentals and include the term blocks.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.
