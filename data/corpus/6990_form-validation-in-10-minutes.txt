Form Validation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Form Validation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Form Validation is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn client-side checks, server-side checks, error messages, and constraint attributes.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a proofreader checking a form before it is submitted. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through sign-up forms, payment pages, and search filters.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for validating only on the client and vague error messages, then try HTML constraint validation for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
