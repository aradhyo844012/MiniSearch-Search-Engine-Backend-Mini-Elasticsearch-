Vector Clocks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Vector Clocks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Vector Clocks: counters that let distributed processes track the order of events without a shared clock.
logical time: a core term, most visible in detecting concurrent edits.
per-process counters: a core term that beginners often confuse with the happened-before relation.
the happened-before relation: a core term that connects to conflict detection.
conflict detection: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Riak: a tool commonly used for replicated databases.

Come back to this Vector Clocks list whenever documentation for the Amazon Dynamo paper uses a word you do not recognize.
