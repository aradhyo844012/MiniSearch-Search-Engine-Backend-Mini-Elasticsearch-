Service Workers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Service Workers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Service Workers in your own words.
2. Describe registration, install and activate events, cache storage, and fetch handlers.
3. Give examples from offline support and faster repeat visits.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests. Then draw a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is caching stale files forever a problem? How does install and activate events relate to cache storage? When would you choose Workbox or the Chrome DevTools Application panel?

Study habit: version your caches so old files can be removed.

When you feel ready, compare Service Workers with State Management and write down three differences.
