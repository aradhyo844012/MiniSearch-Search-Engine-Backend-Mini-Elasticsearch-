Mistakes to Avoid with Code Editors and IDEs

Everyone learns Code Editors and IDEs partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: installing too many extensions. It usually appears when someone skips syntax highlighting and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring keyboard shortcuts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of daily development. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: relying on autocomplete blindly. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on extensions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: learn the shortcuts for the actions you repeat most.
