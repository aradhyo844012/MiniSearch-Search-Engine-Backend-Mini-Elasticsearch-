Speech Recognition Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Speech Recognition.

Do:
- Learn audio features before anything else.
- Try Whisper on a small example.
- Make it a habit to measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
- Test the case of voice assistants early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: accents and background noise.
- Ignore this: domain-specific vocabulary.
- Ignore this: privacy of recordings.
- Adopt Speech Recognition just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Speech Recognition, a little patience with acoustic models pays off more than any clever shortcut.
