Choosing Tools for Reverse Proxies

There is no single best tool for Reverse Proxies. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

NGINX is a common first choice, especially for hiding backend servers. Caddy suits teams that already work with similar technology, and HAProxy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Reverse Proxies by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle terminating HTTPS well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Reverse Proxies, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: losing the client IP address.

As a rule of thumb, forward the real client IP in a trusted header, no matter which tool you use.
