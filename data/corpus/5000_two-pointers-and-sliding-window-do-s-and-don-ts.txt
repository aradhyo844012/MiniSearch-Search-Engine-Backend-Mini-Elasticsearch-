Two Pointers and Sliding Window Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Two Pointers and Sliding Window.

Do:
- Learn left and right pointers before anything else.
- Try Python lists on a small example.
- Make it a habit to write down the window invariant before coding the loop.
- Test the case of finding pairs with a given sum early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: moving the wrong pointer.
- Ignore this: forgetting empty inputs.
- Ignore this: breaking the invariant.
- Adopt Two Pointers and Sliding Window just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Two Pointers and Sliding Window, a little patience with window boundaries pays off more than any clever shortcut.
