Everything You Need to Know About K-Means Clustering

This page collects the essentials about K-Means Clustering in one place. In short, K-Means Clustering is an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points.

Core concepts:
- centroids
- distance measures
- choosing k
- iterations

Where it is used:
- customer segmentation
- image compression
- grouping documents

Common problems:
- choosing k blindly
- unscaled features
- sensitivity to starting points

Tools to know:
- scikit-learn
- NumPy
- matplotlib

Remember this above all: dividing students into study groups around the most central member is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to scale features before clustering.
