The Pros and Cons of Computability and the Halting Problem

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Computability and the Halting Problem is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports understanding the limits of tools
- Makes proofs of impossibility easier
- Helps with theory courses

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming everything is solvable
- Risk: confusing hard with undecidable
- Risk: hand-wavy proofs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as understanding the limits of tools, and you can manage the risks, Computability and the Halting Problem is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, learn the diagonal argument step by step.
