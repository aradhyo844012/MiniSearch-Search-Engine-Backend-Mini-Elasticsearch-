Vector Graphics (SVG) in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Vector Graphics (SVG).

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Vector Graphics (SVG) is images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn paths, the viewBox, fill and stroke, and transforms.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a recipe for drawing a picture rather than a photo of it. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through logos, icons, and charts.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for overly complex paths and a missing viewBox, then try Inkscape for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
