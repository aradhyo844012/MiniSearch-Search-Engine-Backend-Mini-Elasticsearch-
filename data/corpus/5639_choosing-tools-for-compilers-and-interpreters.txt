Choosing Tools for Compilers and Interpreters

There is no single best tool for Compilers and Interpreters. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GCC is a common first choice, especially for running Python and JavaScript. LLVM suits teams that already work with similar technology, and CPython is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle building native programs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: assuming interpreted means slow without measuring.

As a rule of thumb, turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously, no matter which tool you use.
