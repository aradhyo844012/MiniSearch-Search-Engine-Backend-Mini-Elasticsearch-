Where to Go Next After Learning Progressive Web Apps

You have met the basics of Progressive Web Apps. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Service Workers, which belongs to the same area as Progressive Web Apps (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of web app manifest, service worker, installability or responsive design, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around lightweight alternatives to native apps with Lighthouse, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring platform limitations.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Progressive Web Apps to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
