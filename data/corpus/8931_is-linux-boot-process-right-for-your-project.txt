Is Linux Boot Process Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Linux Boot Process, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Linux Boot Process is the sequence of steps from powering on a machine to a running operating system.

It is a good fit when your work involves troubleshooting machines that do not start or configuring dual boot, and when your team understands firmware.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn bootloader, or when you cannot manage these risks: overwriting the bootloader, wrong kernel parameters, and ignoring recovery options.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether kernel initialization and the init system are part of the answer. If they are, Linux Boot Process is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with building custom images and keep a recovery option available before editing boot settings.
