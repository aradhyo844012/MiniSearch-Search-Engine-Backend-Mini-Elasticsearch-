Monitoring and Observability: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Monitoring and Observability?
A: The term Monitoring and Observability refers to collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: noisy alerts; logs without context; and dashboards nobody reads.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with metrics and logs, then move on to traces and alerts.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Monitoring and Observability.

Q: Why do developers use Monitoring and Observability?
A: Common uses include detecting outages, finding slow requests, and capacity planning.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.
