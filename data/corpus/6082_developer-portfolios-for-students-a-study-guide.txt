Developer Portfolios for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Developer Portfolios.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Developer Portfolios in your own words.
2. Describe projects, README quality, live demos, and clear descriptions.
3. Give examples from applying for internships and showing skills to recruiters.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build. Then draw a shop window displaying your best work on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too many unfinished projects a problem? How does README quality relate to live demos? When would you choose GitHub Pages or Vercel?

Study habit worth keeping: feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.

When you feel ready, compare Developer Portfolios with Technical Writing and write down three differences.
