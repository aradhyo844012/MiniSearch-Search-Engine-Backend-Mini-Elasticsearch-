Why Monorepos Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Monorepos make better decisions, because Monorepos is single repositories that hold the code for many projects.

Three situations show why it matters. First, sharing libraries across apps. Second, coordinated refactors. Third, consistent tooling. In each case, a solid grasp of shared tooling and code ownership separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: slow checkouts, unclear ownership, and CI that rebuilds everything.

Good tools exist, including Nx, Bazel, and Turborepo, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
