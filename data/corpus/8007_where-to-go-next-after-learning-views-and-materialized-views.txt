Where to Go Next After Learning Views and Materialized Views

You have met the basics of Views and Materialized Views. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Elasticsearch Concepts, which belongs to the same area as Views and Materialized Views (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of view definitions, refresh strategies, permissions or dependencies, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around hiding sensitive columns with PostgreSQL, and note every problem you meet, starting with stale materialized data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Views and Materialized Views to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
