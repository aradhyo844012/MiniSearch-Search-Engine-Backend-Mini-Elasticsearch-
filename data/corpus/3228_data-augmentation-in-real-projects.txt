Data Augmentation in Real Projects

Reading about Data Augmentation is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: image models. Here, transformations drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against changes that alter the label.

Scenario two: text classification. This time label preservation matters most, and tools such as Albumentations help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: small datasets. The key idea is synthetic samples, and the biggest risk is augmenting the test set.

Across all three scenarios, Data Augmentation stays the same idea: creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize. Only the details change.
