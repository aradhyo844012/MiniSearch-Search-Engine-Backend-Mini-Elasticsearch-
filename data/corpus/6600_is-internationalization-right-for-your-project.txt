Is Internationalization Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Internationalization, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions.

It is a good fit when your work involves global products or multi-language websites, and when your team understands locale detection.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn translation files, or when you cannot manage these risks: hard-coded strings, text that overflows layouts, and assuming left-to-right text.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether pluralization and date and number formatting are part of the answer. If they are, Internationalization is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with regional pricing and move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
