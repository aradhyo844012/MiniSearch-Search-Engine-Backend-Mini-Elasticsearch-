Mistakes to Avoid with Flutter

Everyone learns Flutter partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: deep widget trees. It usually appears when someone skips widgets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor state organization. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of cross-platform mobile apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: large app sizes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the render tree to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: split big widgets into small reusable ones.
