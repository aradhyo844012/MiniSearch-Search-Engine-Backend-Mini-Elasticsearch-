Docker: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Docker?
A: The term Docker refers to a platform for building and running applications in containers.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Docker.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: huge images; secrets baked into images; and running as root.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Docker Compose, Docker Hub, and Podman.
