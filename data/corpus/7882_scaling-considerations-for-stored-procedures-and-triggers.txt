Scaling Considerations for Stored Procedures and Triggers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Stored Procedures and Triggers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding procedures and getting enforcing rules close to the data right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for hidden logic that surprises developers and hard-to-test code, and measure with PL/pgSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: parameters and side effects move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around audit logging at this stage.

Before you scale, keep triggers small and well documented. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
