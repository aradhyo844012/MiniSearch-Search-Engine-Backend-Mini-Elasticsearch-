Kubernetes: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Kubernetes?
A: The term Kubernetes refers to a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include kubectl, Helm, and minikube.

Q: How does it relate to Infrastructure as Code?
A: Kubernetes and Infrastructure as Code often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Cloud and DevOps.

Q: Why do developers use Kubernetes?
A: Common uses include running many containers, rolling updates, and automatic restarts.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Kubernetes.
