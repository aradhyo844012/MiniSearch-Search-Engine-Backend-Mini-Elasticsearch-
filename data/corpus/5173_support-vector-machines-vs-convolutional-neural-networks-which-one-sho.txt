Support Vector Machines vs Convolutional Neural Networks: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Support Vector Machines or Convolutional Neural Networks first. Both sit in the area of AI and Machine Learning, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Support Vector Machines is models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes, and it is closely tied to margin and support vectors. Convolutional Neural Networks, by contrast, is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns, and it is closely tied to filters and pooling.

Choose Support Vector Machines first if you care about text classification or small high-dimensional datasets. Choose Convolutional Neural Networks first if you care about image classification or medical image analysis.

Think of Support Vector Machines as drawing the widest road between two neighborhoods, and of Convolutional Neural Networks as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Support Vector Machines, avoid slow training on large data. With Convolutional Neural Networks, avoid too little training data.

A good habit for Support Vector Machines is to scale features and try a linear kernel first. A good habit for Convolutional Neural Networks is to start from a pretrained model.
