Collision Detection Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Collision Detection regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip bounding boxes; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose Collision Detection so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of platformers early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit broad and narrow phases before anything else.
5. Be careful with this one: tunneling through thin objects.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to shooters as well as to Collision Detection in general.
