Everything You Need to Know About Random Forests

This page collects the essentials about Random Forests in one place. In short, Random Forests is ensembles of many decision trees whose predictions are combined.

Core concepts:
- bagging
- feature randomness
- voting
- out-of-bag error

Where it is used:
- tabular predictions
- feature importance ranking
- strong baseline models

Common problems:
- treating importance scores as causes
- very large forests being slow
- ignoring class imbalance

Tools to know:
- scikit-learn
- XGBoost
- LightGBM

Remember this above all: asking many different experts and taking the majority view is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to tune the number of trees only after the basics work.
