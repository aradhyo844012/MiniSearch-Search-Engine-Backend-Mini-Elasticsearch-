Where to Go Next After Learning Blue-Green Deployment

You have met the basics of Blue-Green Deployment. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Log Aggregation, which belongs to the same area as Blue-Green Deployment (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of blue and green environments, traffic switching, rollback or database compatibility, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around quick rollbacks with AWS CodeDeploy, and note every problem you meet, starting with database schema mismatches.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Blue-Green Deployment to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
