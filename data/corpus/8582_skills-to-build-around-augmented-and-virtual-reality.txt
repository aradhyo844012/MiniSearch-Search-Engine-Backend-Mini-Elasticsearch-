Skills to Build Around Augmented and Virtual Reality

Knowing the definition of Augmented and Virtual Reality is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with head tracking and rendering at high frame rates, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Unity XR on a Augmented and Virtual Reality task, and try at least one alternative such as WebXR.

Judgment: learn to decide when Augmented and Virtual Reality fits a problem such as training simulations, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world.

Habits: keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users, and write down what you learn after each project.
