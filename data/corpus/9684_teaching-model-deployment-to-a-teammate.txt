Teaching Model Deployment to a Teammate

Explaining Model Deployment to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: moving a recipe from the test kitchen into the restaurant. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Model Deployment is making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving recommendation endpoints, naming serving APIs and batch versus online inference as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain containers in Model Deployment back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, training-serving mismatch, and suggest this habit: log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
