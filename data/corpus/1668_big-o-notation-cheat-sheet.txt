Big O Notation Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Big O Notation.

- Definition: a way of describing how running time or memory grows as the input size grows.
- Core ideas: upper bounds; constant, linear and logarithmic growth; worst case; amortized cost.
- Use it for: comparing algorithms; predicting scaling problems; explaining design choices in interviews.
- Watch out for: ignoring constant factors entirely; mixing up best and worst case; measuring only tiny inputs.
- Tools: Python timeit, big-O cheat sheets, profilers.
- Quick tip: practice upper bounds until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: Heaps and Priority Queues.
