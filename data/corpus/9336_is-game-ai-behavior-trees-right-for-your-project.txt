Is Game AI Behavior Trees Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Game AI Behavior Trees, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Game AI Behavior Trees is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does.

It is a good fit when your work involves enemy behavior or companion characters, and when your team understands selector nodes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn sequence nodes, or when you cannot manage these risks: trees that become tangled, expensive conditions checked every frame, and no way to debug decisions.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether conditions and actions are part of the answer. If they are, Game AI Behavior Trees is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with simulated crowds and visualize the tree while the game is running.
