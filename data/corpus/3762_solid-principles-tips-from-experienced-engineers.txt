SOLID Principles Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with SOLID Principles regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose SOLID Principles so the next person understands the decision.
2. Make sure you can apply them when a design starts to hurt, not before.
3. Reach for UML diagrams first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as SonarQube.
4. Test the case of structuring classes early, before the design hardens.
5. Be careful with this one: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to reviewing designs as well.
