Where to Go Next After Learning Dependency Injection

You have met the basics of Dependency Injection. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Logging Best Practices, which belongs to the same area as Dependency Injection (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of constructor injection, interfaces, containers or inversion of control, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around swapping implementations with Spring, and note every problem you meet, starting with hiding too much behind containers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Dependency Injection to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
