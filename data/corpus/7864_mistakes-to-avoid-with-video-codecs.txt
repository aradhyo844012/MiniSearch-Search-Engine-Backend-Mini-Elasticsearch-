Mistakes to Avoid with Video Codecs

Everyone learns Video Codecs partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mismatched codec support. It usually appears when someone skips frames and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: too low a bitrate. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of streaming services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring hardware decoding. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on containers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test playback on the weakest target device.
