Decision Trees: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Decision Trees?
A: The term Decision Trees refers to models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include scikit-learn, XGBoost, and Graphviz.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: deep trees that overfit; ignoring class imbalance; and unstable results from small data changes.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: How does it relate to Linear Regression?
A: Decision Trees and Linear Regression often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Machine Learning.
