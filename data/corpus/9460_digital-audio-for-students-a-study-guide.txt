Digital Audio for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Digital Audio.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Digital Audio in your own words.
2. Describe sampling rate, bit depth, waveforms, and the Nyquist limit.
3. Give examples from music production and voice calls.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second. Then draw taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is clipping from too high a level a problem? How does bit depth relate to waveforms? When would you choose Audacity or SoX?

Study habit worth keeping: keep recordings below the clipping level.

When you feel ready, compare Digital Audio with Color Spaces and write down three differences.
