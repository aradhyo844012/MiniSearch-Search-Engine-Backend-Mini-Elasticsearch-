Debugging Software Estimation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Software Estimation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: treating estimates as promises, ignoring unknowns, and estimating alone.
Step 3: revisit story points and ranges, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use planning poker or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Software Estimation in work such as sprint planning come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, give ranges instead of single numbers. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
