Debugging CPU Caches: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around CPU Caches, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: false sharing, poor access patterns, and assuming memory is uniformly fast.
Step 3: revisit cache lines and hit and miss rates, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use perf or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving CPU Caches in work such as speeding up loops come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, access arrays in the order they are stored. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
