Is Apache Spark Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Apache Spark, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines.

It is a good fit when your work involves large ETL jobs or machine learning on big data, and when your team understands DataFrames.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn transformations and actions, or when you cannot manage these risks: data skew, too many small partitions, and collecting huge results onto the driver.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether partitions and lazy execution are part of the answer. If they are, Apache Spark is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with log analysis and avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
