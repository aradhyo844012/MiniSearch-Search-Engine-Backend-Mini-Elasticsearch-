Mistakes to Avoid with GPUs and Parallelism

Everyone learns GPUs and Parallelism partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow data transfers. It usually appears when someone skips threads and blocks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: branch-heavy code. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of training neural networks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: launching tiny workloads. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on SIMD to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
