Key Terms in Technical Debt Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Technical Debt much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. Put simply, Technical Debt covers the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.
