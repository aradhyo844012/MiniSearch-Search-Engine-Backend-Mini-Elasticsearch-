Wearable Technology Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Wearable Technology. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.
Key terms: sensors, low-power design, companion apps, and data syncing.
Typical uses: fitness tracking, notifications, and health monitoring.
Risks: battery drain, inaccurate sensor readings, and privacy of personal data.
Tools: Wear OS, watchOS, and Bluetooth Low Energy.

Mental picture: a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist.

Habit to build: design interactions that take only a few seconds.
