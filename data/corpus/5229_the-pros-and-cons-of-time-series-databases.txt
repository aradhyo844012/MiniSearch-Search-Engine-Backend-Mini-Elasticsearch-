The Pros and Cons of Time-Series Databases

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Time-Series Databases is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports server metrics
- Makes sensor readings easier
- Helps with financial tick data

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: high-cardinality tags
- Risk: keeping raw data forever
- Risk: querying without time bounds

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as server metrics, and you can manage the risks, Time-Series Databases is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, include a time range in every query.
