Practice Exercises for Connection Pooling

These exercises build real understanding of Connection Pooling. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble web backends or serverless functions that call databases.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: pools larger than the database allows. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Connection Pooling and include the term pool size.
Exercise 4: Pick PgBouncer and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how timeouts and connection leaks differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Connection Pooling is reusing a limited set of open database connections instead of opening a new one for every request.
