SOLID Principles Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with SOLID Principles.

Do:
- Learn single responsibility before anything else.
- Try UML diagrams on a small example.
- Make it a habit to apply them when a design starts to hurt, not before.
- Test the case of structuring classes early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts.
- Ignore this: creating too many tiny classes.
- Ignore this: confusing interfaces with inheritance.
- Adopt SOLID Principles just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with SOLID Principles, a little patience with the open-closed principle pays off more than any clever shortcut.
