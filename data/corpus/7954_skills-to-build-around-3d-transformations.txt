Skills to Build Around 3D Transformations

Knowing the definition of 3D Transformations is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with translation and rotation, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with GLM on a 3D Transformations task, and try at least one alternative such as three.js.

Judgment: learn to decide when 3D Transformations fits a problem such as games, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it.

Habits: write down the order of transformations before multiplying, and write down what you learn after each project.
