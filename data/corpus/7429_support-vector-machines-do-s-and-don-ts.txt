Support Vector Machines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Support Vector Machines.

Do:
- Learn margin before anything else.
- Try scikit-learn on a small example.
- Make it a habit to scale features and try a linear kernel first.
- Test the case of text classification early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow training on large data.
- Ignore this: poor kernel choices.
- Ignore this: unscaled features.
- Adopt Support Vector Machines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Support Vector Machines, a little patience with support vectors pays off more than any clever shortcut.
