Canary Releases Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Canary Releases.

Do:
- Learn traffic percentages before anything else.
- Try Argo Rollouts on a small example.
- Make it a habit to define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.
- Test the case of safer releases early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too little traffic to reveal problems.
- Ignore this: no clear success metrics.
- Ignore this: manual promotion mistakes.
- Adopt Canary Releases just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Canary Releases, a little patience with health metrics pays off more than any clever shortcut.
