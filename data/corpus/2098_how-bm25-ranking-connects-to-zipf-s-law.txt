How BM25 Ranking Connects to Zipf's Law

BM25 Ranking and Zipf's Law are closely connected parts of Search and Retrieval. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving ranking search results may also involve analyzing text corpora. In such a project, term saturation and rank and frequency often influence each other.

Start with BM25 Ranking: the term refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. Then look at Zipf's Law, which refers to the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as enterprise search, and explain how power laws would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For BM25 Ranking, watch for this: ignoring field boundaries. For Zipf's Law, watch for this: reading too much into a perfect fit. Real query logs reveal what people actually look for, which is rarely what designers expect.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Elasticsearch or matplotlib.
