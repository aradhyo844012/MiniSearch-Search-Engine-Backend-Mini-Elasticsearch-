Teaching Saga Pattern to a Teammate

Explaining Saga Pattern to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: booking a trip with separate hotel, flight and car reservations that can each be cancelled. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Saga Pattern is a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving order processing, naming local transactions and compensating actions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain orchestration in Saga Pattern back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, missing compensations, and suggest this habit: make every step and its compensation idempotent.
