systemd Services Glossary for Beginners

New words can make systemd Services feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

systemd Services: the init system and service manager used by most modern Linux distributions.
unit files: a core term, most visible in starting services at boot.
targets: a core term that beginners often confuse with service states.
service states: a core term that connects to journal logs.
journal logs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
systemctl: a tool commonly used for restarting crashed programs.

Come back to this systemd Services list whenever documentation for journalctl uses a word you do not recognize.
