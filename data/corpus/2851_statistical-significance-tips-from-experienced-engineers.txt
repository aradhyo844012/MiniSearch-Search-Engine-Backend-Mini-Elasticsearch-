Statistical Significance Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Statistical Significance regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of judging experiment results early, before the design hardens.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit the null hypothesis before anything else.
3. Make sure you can always report the effect size alongside the p-value.
4. Be careful with this one: mistaking significance for importance.
5. Do not skip p-values; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to comparing model performance as well.
