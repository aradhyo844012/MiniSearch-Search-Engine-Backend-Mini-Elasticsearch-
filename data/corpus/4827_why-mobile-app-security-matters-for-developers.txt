Why Mobile App Security Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Mobile App Security make better decisions, because Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks.

Three situations show why it matters. First, banking apps. Second, health apps. Third, enterprise apps. In each case, a solid grasp of secure storage and certificate pinning separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: storing tokens in plain text, hard-coded API keys, and requesting unnecessary permissions.

You will not need to build everything yourself; OWASP MASVS, Android Keystore, and iOS Keychain cover most needs.
