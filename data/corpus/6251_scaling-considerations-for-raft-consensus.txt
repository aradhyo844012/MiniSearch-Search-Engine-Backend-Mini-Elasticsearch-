Scaling Considerations for Raft Consensus

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Raft Consensus changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding leader election and getting configuration stores right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for even-sized clusters and slow disks causing elections, and measure with etcd before changing anything.

Large scale: terms and majority quorum move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around distributed locks at this stage.

Before you scale, use an odd number of nodes. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
