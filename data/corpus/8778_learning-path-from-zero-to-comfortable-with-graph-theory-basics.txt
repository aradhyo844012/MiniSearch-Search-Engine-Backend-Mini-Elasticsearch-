Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Graph Theory Basics

This path is for a learner who has never studied Graph Theory Basics before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them) and learn vertices and edges and degree.

Stage 2, hands-on: install NetworkX, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving social networks.

Stage 3, depth: study paths and cycles and connectivity, then deliberately explore this problem: mixing directed and undirected graphs.

Stage 4, application: apply Graph Theory Basics to maps, compare Graphviz with Gephi, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Graph Theory Basics to someone else, and then move on to Linear Algebra for Programmers.
