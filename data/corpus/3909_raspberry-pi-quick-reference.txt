Raspberry Pi Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Raspberry Pi. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects.
Key terms: GPIO pins, Linux, SD card storage, and headless setup.
Typical uses: home automation, learning Linux, and small servers.
Risks: SD card corruption, inadequate power supplies, and exposing the device without hardening.
Tools: Raspberry Pi OS, Python GPIO libraries, and SSH.

Mental picture: a pocket-sized workshop for experiments.

Habit to build: use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
