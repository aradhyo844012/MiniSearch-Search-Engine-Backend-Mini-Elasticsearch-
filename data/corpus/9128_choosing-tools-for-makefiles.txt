Choosing Tools for Makefiles

There is no single best tool for Makefiles. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GNU Make is a common first choice, especially for compiling C projects. CMake suits teams that already work with similar technology, and a text editor is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Makefiles by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle shortcuts for common commands well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Makefiles, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: tabs versus spaces.

As a rule of thumb, use phony targets for tasks like test and clean, no matter which tool you use.
