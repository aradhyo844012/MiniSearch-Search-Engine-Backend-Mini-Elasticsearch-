How Neural Networks Connects to Overfitting

Neural Networks and Overfitting are closely connected parts of Machine Learning. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving image recognition may also involve diagnosing weak models. In such a project, layers and training and validation error often influence each other.

Start with Neural Networks: the term refers to models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. Then look at Overfitting, which refers to when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as speech recognition, and explain how model complexity would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Neural Networks, watch for this: poor learning rate choices. For Overfitting, watch for this: leaking test data. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using PyTorch or scikit-learn.
