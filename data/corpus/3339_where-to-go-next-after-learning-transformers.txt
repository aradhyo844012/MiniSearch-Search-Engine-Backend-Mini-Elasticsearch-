Where to Go Next After Learning Transformers

You have met the basics of Transformers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Convolutional Neural Networks, which belongs to the same area as Transformers (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of self-attention, positional encoding, encoder and decoder blocks or tokens, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around text generation with Hugging Face, and note every problem you meet, starting with huge compute requirements.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Transformers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
