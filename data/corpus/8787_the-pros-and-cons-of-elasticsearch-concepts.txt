The Pros and Cons of Elasticsearch Concepts

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Elasticsearch Concepts is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports site search
- Makes log analytics easier
- Helps with product catalogs

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too many shards
- Risk: mapping explosions
- Risk: treating it as a primary database

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as site search, and you can manage the risks, Elasticsearch Concepts is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
