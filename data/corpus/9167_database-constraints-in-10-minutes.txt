Database Constraints in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Database Constraints.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and check constraints.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine guard rails along a mountain road. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through preventing bad data, protecting relationships, and documenting business rules.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for skipping constraints for speed and missing indexes on foreign keys, then try PostgreSQL for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
