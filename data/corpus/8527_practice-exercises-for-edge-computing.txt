Practice Exercises for Edge Computing

These exercises build real understanding of Edge Computing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Edge Computing and include the term edge nodes.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: hard-to-update devices. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble factory sensors or video analytics.
Exercise 4: Compare K3s and Cloudflare Workers for smart cities and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how bandwidth savings and synchronization differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Edge Computing is processing data close to where it is produced instead of in a distant data center.
