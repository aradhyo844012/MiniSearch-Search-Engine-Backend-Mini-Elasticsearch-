Where to Go Next After Learning Recommendation Systems

You have met the basics of Recommendation Systems. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Prompt Engineering, which belongs to the same area as Recommendation Systems (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, ranking or the cold start problem, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around online shops with Surprise, and note every problem you meet, starting with feedback loops.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Recommendation Systems to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
