The Pros and Cons of Zero Trust Security

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Zero Trust Security is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports remote work access
- Makes protecting internal services easier
- Helps with limiting breach damage

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating it as a product to buy
- Risk: poor identity hygiene
- Risk: breaking usability

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as remote work access, and you can manage the risks, Zero Trust Security is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with strong identity and device checks.
