Union-Find Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Union-Find.

Do:
- Learn parent pointers before anything else.
- Try Python lists on a small example.
- Make it a habit to implement path compression and union by rank together.
- Test the case of detecting connected components early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: forgetting path compression.
- Ignore this: merging without checking roots.
- Ignore this: using it when deletions are required.
- Adopt Union-Find just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Union-Find, a little patience with union operation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
