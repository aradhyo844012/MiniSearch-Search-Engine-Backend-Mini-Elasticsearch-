Teaching Regression Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Regression Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: rechecking the old keys after cutting a new lock. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Regression Testing is re-running tests after changes to make sure old features still work. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving protecting releases, naming test suites and automation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain test selection in Regression Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow suites that nobody runs, and suggest this habit: add a test for every bug you fix.
