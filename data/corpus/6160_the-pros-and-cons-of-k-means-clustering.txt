The Pros and Cons of K-Means Clustering

No technology is free of trade-offs, and K-Means Clustering is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports customer segmentation
- Makes image compression easier
- Helps with grouping documents

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: choosing k blindly
- Risk: unscaled features
- Risk: sensitivity to starting points

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as customer segmentation, and you can manage the risks, K-Means Clustering is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, scale features before clustering.
