Feature Engineering vs Large Language Models: Key Differences

Feature Engineering and Large Language Models both belong to the area of Machine Learning, but they solve different problems. This article compares them side by side so you can choose with confidence.

The term Feature Engineering refers to creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily. Its core ideas are scaling, encoding categories, and interaction features.

The term Large Language Models refers to very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language. Its core ideas are tokens, attention, and pretraining.

The main difference is focus. Feature Engineering puts the emphasis on scaling and encoding categories, while Large Language Models puts the emphasis on tokens and attention.

Reach for Feature Engineering when your task involves improving model accuracy or preparing tabular data. Reach for Large Language Models when your task involves drafting text or answering questions.

Both have risks. With Feature Engineering, watch for this: data leakage. With Large Language Models, watch for this: trusting outputs without checking.

In practice you do not always have to choose. Many projects use Feature Engineering alongside Large Language Models, with tools such as pandas and Hugging Face. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.
