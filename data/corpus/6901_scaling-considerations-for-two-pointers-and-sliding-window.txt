Scaling Considerations for Two Pointers and Sliding Window

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Two Pointers and Sliding Window changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding left and right pointers and getting finding pairs with a given sum right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for moving the wrong pointer and forgetting empty inputs, and measure with Python lists before changing anything.

Large scale: invariants and shrinking and expanding move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around longest substring problems at this stage.

Before you scale, write down the window invariant before coding the loop. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
