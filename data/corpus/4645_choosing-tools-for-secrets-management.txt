Choosing Tools for Secrets Management

There is no single best tool for Secrets Management. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

HashiCorp Vault is a common first choice, especially for database credentials. AWS Secrets Manager suits teams that already work with similar technology, and SOPS is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle API keys well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: secrets in source control.

As a rule of thumb, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak, no matter which tool you use.
