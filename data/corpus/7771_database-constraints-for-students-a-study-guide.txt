Database Constraints for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Database Constraints.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Database Constraints in your own words.
2. Describe primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and check constraints.
3. Give examples from preventing bad data and protecting relationships.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid. Then draw guard rails along a mountain road on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is skipping constraints for speed a problem? How does foreign keys relate to unique constraints? When would you choose PostgreSQL or SQLite?

Study habit worth keeping: put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.

When you feel ready, compare Database Constraints with Views and Materialized Views and write down three differences.
