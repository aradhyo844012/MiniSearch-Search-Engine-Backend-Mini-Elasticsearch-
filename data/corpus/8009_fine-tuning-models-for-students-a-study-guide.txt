Fine-Tuning Models for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Fine-Tuning Models.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Fine-Tuning Models in your own words.
2. Describe pretrained weights, training data, the learning rate, and evaluation.
3. Give examples from domain-specific language tasks and style adaptation.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Fine-Tuning Models is adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data. Then draw teaching an experienced chef a new regional cuisine on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is overfitting on small datasets a problem? How does training data relate to the learning rate? When would you choose Hugging Face or PyTorch?

Study habit: start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.

When you feel ready, compare Fine-Tuning Models with Prompt Engineering and write down three differences.
