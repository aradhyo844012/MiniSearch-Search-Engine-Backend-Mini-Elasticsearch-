Information Theory and Entropy Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Information Theory and Entropy.

Do:
- Learn bits before anything else.
- Try the Python math module on a small example.
- Make it a habit to compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
- Test the case of data compression early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: confusing information with meaning.
- Ignore this: ignoring noise.
- Ignore this: misreading entropy values.
- Adopt Information Theory and Entropy just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Information Theory and Entropy, a little patience with entropy pays off more than any clever shortcut.
