Multi-Cloud Strategy in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Multi-Cloud Strategy.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn portability, vendor lock-in, abstraction layers, and cost comparison.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through avoiding single-provider risk, meeting regional rules, and using best-of-breed services.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for doubling operational complexity and lowest-common-denominator designs, then try Terraform for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
