Graph Theory Basics in Real Projects

Reading about Graph Theory Basics is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: social networks. Here, vertices and edges drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mixing directed and undirected graphs.

Scenario two: maps. This time degree matters most, and tools such as NetworkX help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: dependency analysis. The key idea is paths and cycles, and the biggest risk is ignoring disconnected parts.

Across all three scenarios, Graph Theory Basics stays the same idea: the study of nodes and the connections between them. Only the details change.
