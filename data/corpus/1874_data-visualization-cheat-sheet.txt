Data Visualization Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Data Visualization.

- Definition: presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see.
- Core ideas: chart types; axes and labels; color choices; annotations.
- Use it for: explaining results; exploring data; reporting performance.
- Watch out for: truncated axes; unreadable labels; too many colors.
- Tools: matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for axes and labels before building anything large.
- Related topic: Stream Processing.
