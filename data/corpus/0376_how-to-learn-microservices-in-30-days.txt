How to Learn Microservices in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Microservices. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Microservices covers an architecture that splits an application into small services that can be deployed independently. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn service boundaries and APIs, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Docker on a small task such as large teams working in parallel.
Week 3: build a mini project around scaling parts separately.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as splitting too early, and explain Microservices to a friend.

A backend turns requests from many clients into reliable, consistent responses.

Popular tools and libraries include Docker, Kubernetes, and API gateways.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Microservices.
