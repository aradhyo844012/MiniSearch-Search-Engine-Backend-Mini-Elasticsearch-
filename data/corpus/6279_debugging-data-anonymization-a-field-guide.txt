Debugging Data Anonymization: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Data Anonymization, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: re-identification through combined fields, removing too little, and destroying usefulness by removing too much.
Step 3: revisit masking and generalization, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ARX or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Data Anonymization in work such as sharing datasets with researchers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test whether combining fields could identify a person. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
