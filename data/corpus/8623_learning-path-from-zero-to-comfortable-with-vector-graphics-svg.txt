Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Vector Graphics (SVG)

This path is for a learner who has never studied Vector Graphics (SVG) before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Vector Graphics (SVG) is images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size) and learn paths and the viewBox.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Inkscape, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving logos.

Stage 3, depth: study fill and stroke and transforms, then deliberately explore this problem: overly complex paths.

Stage 4, application: apply Vector Graphics (SVG) to icons, compare SVGO with Figma, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Vector Graphics (SVG) to someone else, and then move on to Color Spaces.
