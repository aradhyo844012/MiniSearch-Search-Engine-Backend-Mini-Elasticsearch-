Where to Go Next After Learning Segment Trees

You have met the basics of Segment Trees. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Topological Sorting, which belongs to the same area as Segment Trees (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of tree nodes covering ranges, range queries, point updates or lazy propagation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around finding minimums in a window with C++ arrays, and note every problem you meet, starting with off-by-one errors in ranges.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Segment Trees to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
