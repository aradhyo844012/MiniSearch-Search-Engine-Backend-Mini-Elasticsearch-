Offline-First Apps Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Offline-First Apps feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Offline-First Apps: apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns.
local storage: a core term, most visible in field service tools.
sync queues: a core term that beginners often confuse with conflict resolution.
conflict resolution: a core term that connects to optimistic updates.
optimistic updates: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
SQLite: a tool commonly used for note-taking apps.

Come back to this Offline-First Apps list whenever documentation for PouchDB uses a word you do not recognize.
