Pagination Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Pagination.

Do:
- Learn offset pagination before anything else.
- Try SQL LIMIT and OFFSET on a small example.
- Make it a habit to prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
- Test the case of search results early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: offsets getting slow on huge tables.
- Ignore this: unstable sort order.
- Ignore this: missing total counts when users need them.
- Adopt Pagination just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Pagination, a little patience with cursor pagination pays off more than any clever shortcut.
