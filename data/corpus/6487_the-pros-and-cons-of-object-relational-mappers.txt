The Pros and Cons of Object-Relational Mappers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Object-Relational Mappers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports building CRUD apps quickly
- Makes switching databases easier
- Helps with keeping schema and code aligned

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: N+1 queries
- Risk: loading entire tables
- Risk: hiding slow SQL

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as building CRUD apps quickly, and you can manage the risks, Object-Relational Mappers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, log the generated SQL during development.
