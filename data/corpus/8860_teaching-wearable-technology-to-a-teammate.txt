Teaching Wearable Technology to a Teammate

Explaining Wearable Technology to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving fitness tracking, naming sensors and low-power design as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain companion apps in Wearable Technology back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, battery drain, and suggest this habit: design interactions that take only a few seconds.
