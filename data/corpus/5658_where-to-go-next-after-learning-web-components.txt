Where to Go Next After Learning Web Components

You have met the basics of Web Components. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CSS Variables, which belongs to the same area as Web Components (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of custom elements, shadow DOM, templates or slots, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around widgets reused across frameworks with Lit, and note every problem you meet, starting with styling surprises across the shadow boundary.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Web Components to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
