Mistakes to Avoid with API Design Principles

Everyone learns API Design Principles partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: leaking internal details. It usually appears when someone skips consistent naming and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: inconsistent responses. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of public platforms. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: undocumented behavior. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on documentation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: write the documentation before the implementation.
