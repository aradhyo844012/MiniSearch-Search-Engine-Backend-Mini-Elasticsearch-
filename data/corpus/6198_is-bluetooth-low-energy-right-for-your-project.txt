Is Bluetooth Low Energy Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Bluetooth Low Energy, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

It is a good fit when your work involves fitness trackers or smart locks, and when your team understands advertising.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn GATT services, or when you cannot manage these risks: ignoring connection limits, weak pairing security, and assuming long range.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether characteristics and pairing are part of the answer. If they are, Bluetooth Low Energy is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with proximity beacons and test with several phone models.
