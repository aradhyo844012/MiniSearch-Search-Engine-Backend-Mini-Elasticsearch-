Mistakes to Avoid with Blockchain Fundamentals

Everyone learns Blockchain Fundamentals partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: treating it as a database replacement. It usually appears when someone skips blocks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow throughput. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of digital currencies. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: the energy use of some designs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on decentralization to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
