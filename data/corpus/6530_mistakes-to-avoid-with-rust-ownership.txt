Mistakes to Avoid with Rust Ownership

Everyone learns Rust Ownership partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: fighting the borrow checker with needless clones. It usually appears when someone skips ownership and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: confusing moves and copies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of systems programming. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: overly complex lifetime annotations. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on moves to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: read the compiler's error messages closely because they often explain the fix.
