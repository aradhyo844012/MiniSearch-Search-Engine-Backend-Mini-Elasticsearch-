Combining Reinforcement Learning with Data Augmentation in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Reinforcement Learning and Data Augmentation can work together in a single project.

Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes. Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

Imagine a project that involves game playing and image models. Reinforcement Learning brings agent, while Data Augmentation brings transformations.

A sensible plan is to start with Gymnasium for the Reinforcement Learning part and Albumentations for the Data Augmentation part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: slow training on one side, and augmenting the test set on the other.

Picture the whole as training a dog with treats for good behavior, working alongside practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields.

Two habits help: first, start with simple environments; second, augment only the training data.
