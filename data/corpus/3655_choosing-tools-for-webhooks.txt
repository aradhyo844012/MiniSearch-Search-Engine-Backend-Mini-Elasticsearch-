Choosing Tools for Webhooks

There is no single best tool for Webhooks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ngrok is a common first choice, especially for payment notifications. the Stripe CLI suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Postman is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle CI triggers well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: not verifying signatures.

As a rule of thumb, respond quickly and process the event in a background job, no matter which tool you use.
