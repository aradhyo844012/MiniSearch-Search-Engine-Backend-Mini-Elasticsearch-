Teaching Connection Pooling to a Teammate

Explaining Connection Pooling to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a rental desk that lends a few shared bikes instead of buying one per visitor. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Connection Pooling is reusing a limited set of open database connections instead of opening a new one for every request. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving web backends, naming pool size and idle connections as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain timeouts in Connection Pooling back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, pools larger than the database allows, and suggest this habit: always return connections in a finally block.
