Practice Exercises for Database Constraints

These exercises build real understanding of Database Constraints. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble preventing bad data or protecting relationships.
Exercise 2: Explain how unique constraints and check constraints differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick PostgreSQL and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Database Constraints and include the term primary keys.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: skipping constraints for speed. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.
