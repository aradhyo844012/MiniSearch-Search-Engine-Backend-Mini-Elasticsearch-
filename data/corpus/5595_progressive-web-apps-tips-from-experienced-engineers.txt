Progressive Web Apps Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Progressive Web Apps regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can test the app in airplane mode.
2. Test the case of offline-friendly tools early, before the design hardens.
3. Do not skip web app manifest; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for Lighthouse first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Workbox.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit service worker before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to lightweight alternatives to native apps as well.
