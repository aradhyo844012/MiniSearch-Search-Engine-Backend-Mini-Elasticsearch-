Inverted Index Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Inverted Index.

- Definition: a data structure that maps each term to the documents that contain it.
- Core ideas: vocabulary; postings lists; term frequency; document frequency.
- Use it for: full-text search; log search; finding matching documents quickly.
- Watch out for: scanning very long postings lists; building the index without normalizing text; forgetting to update it after changes.
- Tools: Lucene, Elasticsearch, custom Python dictionaries.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for postings lists before building anything large.
- Related topic: TF-IDF.
