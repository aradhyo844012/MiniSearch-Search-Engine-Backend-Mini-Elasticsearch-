Everything You Need to Know About Disaster Recovery

This page collects the essentials about Disaster Recovery in one place. In short, Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Core concepts:
- recovery time objective
- recovery point objective
- failover regions
- runbooks

Where it is used:
- surviving regional outages
- meeting compliance needs
- protecting critical data

Common problems:
- untested plans
- outdated runbooks
- dependencies missed in the plan

Tools to know:
- AWS Backup
- Terraform
- Route 53 failover

Remember this above all: a fire drill for a whole system is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
