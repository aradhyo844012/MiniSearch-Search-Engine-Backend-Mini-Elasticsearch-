Mistakes to Avoid with Package Managers

Everyone learns Package Managers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing sources that conflict. It usually appears when someone skips repositories and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring signature checks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of installing system tools. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: upgrading without reading the changes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on signed packages to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: update the package index before installing anything.
