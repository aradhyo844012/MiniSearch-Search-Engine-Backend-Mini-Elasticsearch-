Skills to Build Around Two Pointers and Sliding Window

Knowing the definition of Two Pointers and Sliding Window is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with left and right pointers and window boundaries, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Python lists on a Two Pointers and Sliding Window task, and try at least one alternative such as collections.deque.

Judgment: learn to decide when Two Pointers and Sliding Window fits a problem such as finding pairs with a given sum, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a small frame that slides along a line of text.

Habits: write down the window invariant before coding the loop, and write down what you learn after each project.
