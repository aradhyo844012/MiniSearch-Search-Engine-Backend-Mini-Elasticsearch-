Debugging AI Agents: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around AI Agents, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: runaway loops, unchecked tool permissions, and hard-to-debug behavior.
Step 3: revisit planning and tool calls, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use LangGraph or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving AI Agents in work such as research assistants come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, limit what tools the agent may use and log every action. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
