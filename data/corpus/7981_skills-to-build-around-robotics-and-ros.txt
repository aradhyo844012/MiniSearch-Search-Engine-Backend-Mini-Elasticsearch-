Skills to Build Around Robotics and ROS

Knowing the definition of Robotics and ROS is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with sensors and actuators, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with ROS 2 on a Robotics and ROS task, and try at least one alternative such as Gazebo.

Judgment: learn to decide when Robotics and ROS fits a problem such as warehouse robots, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a body with senses and muscles controlled by a nervous system of messages.

Habits: test in simulation before moving real hardware, and write down what you learn after each project.
