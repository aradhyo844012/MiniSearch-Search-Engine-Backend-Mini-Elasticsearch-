Choosing Tools for Fuzz Testing

There is no single best tool for Fuzz Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

AFL++ is a common first choice, especially for testing parsers. libFuzzer suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Atheris is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle finding memory bugs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: running without sanitizers.

As a rule of thumb, keep every crashing input as a regression test, no matter which tool you use.
