A Short History of Responsive Design

Ideas behind Responsive Design developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. When people talk about Responsive Design, they usually mean building pages that adapt smoothly to phones, tablets and large screens. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

The vocabulary of Responsive Design is small. Learn media queries, fluid grids, flexible images, and mobile-first thinking, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching mobile-first thinking on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

A solid grasp of Responsive Design also makes Web Accessibility easier to understand.
