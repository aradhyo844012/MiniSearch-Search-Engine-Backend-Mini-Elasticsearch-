Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Dependency Injection

This path is for a learner who has never studied Dependency Injection before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them) and learn constructor injection and interfaces.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Spring, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving easier unit testing.

Stage 3, depth: study containers and inversion of control, then deliberately explore this problem: hiding too much behind containers.

Stage 4, application: apply Dependency Injection to swapping implementations, compare FastAPI Depends with Dagger, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Dependency Injection to someone else, and then move on to Webhooks.
