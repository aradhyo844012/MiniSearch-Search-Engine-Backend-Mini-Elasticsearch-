Debugging Feature Flags: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Feature Flags, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: never removing old flags, flags that depend on each other, and untested flag combinations.
Step 3: revisit flag evaluation and targeting rules, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use LaunchDarkly or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Feature Flags in work such as dark launches come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, give every flag an owner and a removal date. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
