Go Goroutines for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Go Goroutines.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Go Goroutines in your own words.
2. Describe goroutines, channels, select statements, and wait groups.
3. Give examples from servers handling many connections and parallel data processing.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Go Goroutines is lightweight threads managed by the Go runtime that run functions concurrently. Then draw a small crew of waiters passing notes through a serving hatch on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is goroutine leaks a problem? How does channels relate to select statements? When would you choose the go command or the race detector?

Study habit worth keeping: run your tests with the race detector turned on.

When you feel ready, compare Go Goroutines with Python Asyncio and write down three differences.
