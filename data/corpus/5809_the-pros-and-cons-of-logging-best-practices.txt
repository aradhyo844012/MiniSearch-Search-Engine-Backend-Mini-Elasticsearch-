The Pros and Cons of Logging Best Practices

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Logging Best Practices is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports debugging production issues
- Makes auditing actions easier
- Helps with measuring usage

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: logging sensitive data
- Risk: too much noise
- Risk: missing context

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as debugging production issues, and you can manage the risks, Logging Best Practices is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, include a request ID in every log line.
