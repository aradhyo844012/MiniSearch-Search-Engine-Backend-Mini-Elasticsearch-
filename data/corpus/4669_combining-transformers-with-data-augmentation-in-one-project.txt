Combining Transformers with Data Augmentation in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Transformers and Data Augmentation can work together in a single project.

Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other. Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

Imagine a project that involves language translation and image models. Transformers brings self-attention, while Data Augmentation brings transformations.

A sensible plan is to start with Hugging Face for the Transformers part and Albumentations for the Data Augmentation part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: limited context windows on one side, and augmenting the test set on the other.

Picture the whole as a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word, working alongside practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields.

Two habits help: first, begin with small pretrained models; second, augment only the training data.
