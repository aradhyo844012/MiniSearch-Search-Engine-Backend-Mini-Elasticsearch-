Teaching Game AI Behavior Trees to a Teammate

Explaining Game AI Behavior Trees to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a flowchart a character consults every moment before acting. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Game AI Behavior Trees is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving enemy behavior, naming selector nodes and sequence nodes as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain conditions in Game AI Behavior Trees back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, trees that become tangled, and suggest this habit: visualize the tree while the game is running.
