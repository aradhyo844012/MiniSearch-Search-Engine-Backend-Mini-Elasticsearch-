The Pros and Cons of Web Components

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Web Components is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports design system components
- Makes widgets reused across frameworks easier
- Helps with embeddable tools

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: styling surprises across the shadow boundary
- Risk: poor accessibility
- Risk: heavy polyfills

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as design system components, and you can manage the risks, Web Components is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, document the attributes and events like a public API.
