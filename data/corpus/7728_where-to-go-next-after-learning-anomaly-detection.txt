Where to Go Next After Learning Anomaly Detection

You have met the basics of Anomaly Detection. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Responsible AI and Bias, which belongs to the same area as Anomaly Detection (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of baselines, thresholds, isolation methods or false positives, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around server monitoring with scikit-learn, and note every problem you meet, starting with too many false alarms.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Anomaly Detection to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
