CSRF Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with CSRF regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip session cookies; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit anti-CSRF tokens before anything else.
3. Be careful with this one: using GET requests for state changes.
4. Write down why you chose CSRF so the next person understands the decision.
5. Make sure you can combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to securing account settings as well.
