Practice Exercises for Build Systems

These exercises build real understanding of Build Systems. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Build Systems and include the term targets.
Exercise 2: Pick Make and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble compiling large projects or packaging software.
Exercise 4: Explain how incremental builds and build caches differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Compare Gradle and Bazel for running repeatable pipelines and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Build Systems is tools that turn source code into runnable programs automatically.
