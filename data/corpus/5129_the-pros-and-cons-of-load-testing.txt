The Pros and Cons of Load Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Load Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports capacity planning
- Makes finding bottlenecks easier
- Helps with validating autoscaling

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: testing unrealistic traffic
- Risk: ignoring warm-up
- Risk: using average latency only

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as capacity planning, and you can manage the risks, Load Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
