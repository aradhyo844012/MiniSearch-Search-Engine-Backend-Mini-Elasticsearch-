Build Systems Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Build Systems.

Do:
- Learn targets before anything else.
- Try Make on a small example.
- Make it a habit to make builds reproducible on any machine.
- Test the case of compiling large projects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow full rebuilds.
- Ignore this: undeclared dependencies.
- Ignore this: machine-specific builds.
- Adopt Build Systems just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Build Systems, a little patience with dependencies pays off more than any clever shortcut.
