Where to Go Next After Learning Time Management for Developers

You have met the basics of Time Management for Developers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Technical Writing, which belongs to the same area as Time Management for Developers (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of prioritization, time blocking, breaking down tasks or avoiding context switches, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around meeting deadlines with calendar blocking, and note every problem you meet, starting with multitasking.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Time Management for Developers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
