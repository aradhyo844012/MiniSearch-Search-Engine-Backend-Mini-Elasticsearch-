Combining Feature Flags with Secrets Management in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Feature Flags and Secrets Management can work together in a single project.

Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code. Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.

Imagine a project that involves dark launches and database credentials. Feature Flags brings flag evaluation, while Secrets Management brings vaults.

A sensible plan is to start with LaunchDarkly for the Feature Flags part and HashiCorp Vault for the Secrets Management part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: flags that depend on each other on one side, and long-lived shared credentials on the other.

Picture the whole as light switches wired to every room of a house, working alongside a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet.

Two habits help: first, give every flag an owner and a removal date; second, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
