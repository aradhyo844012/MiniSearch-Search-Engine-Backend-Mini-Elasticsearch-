Where to Go Next After Learning API Design Principles

You have met the basics of API Design Principles. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Software Estimation, which belongs to the same area as API Design Principles (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of consistent naming, clear errors, idempotent operations or documentation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around internal service contracts with OpenAPI, and note every problem you meet, starting with leaking internal details.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining API Design Principles to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
