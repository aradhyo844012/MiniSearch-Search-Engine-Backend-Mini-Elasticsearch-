Vector Graphics (SVG) in Real Projects

Reading about Vector Graphics (SVG) is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: logos. Here, paths drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against overly complex paths.

Scenario two: icons. This time the viewBox matters most, and tools such as Inkscape help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: charts. The key idea is fill and stroke, and the biggest risk is a missing viewBox.

Across all three scenarios, Vector Graphics (SVG) stays the same idea: images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size. Only the details change.
