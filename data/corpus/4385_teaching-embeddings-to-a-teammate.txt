Teaching Embeddings to a Teammate

Explaining Embeddings to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: coordinates on a map where similar places are close together. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving semantic search, naming vectors and cosine similarity as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain dimensionality in Embeddings back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, mixing models between indexing and querying, and suggest this habit: use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
