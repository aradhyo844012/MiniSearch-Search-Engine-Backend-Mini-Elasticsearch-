Augmented and Virtual Reality for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Augmented and Virtual Reality.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Augmented and Virtual Reality in your own words.
2. Describe head tracking, rendering at high frame rates, spatial mapping, and input methods.
3. Give examples from training simulations and games.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one. Then draw wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is motion sickness from low frame rates a problem? How does rendering at high frame rates relate to spatial mapping? When would you choose Unity XR or WebXR?

Study habit worth keeping: keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.

When you feel ready, compare Augmented and Virtual Reality with Edge Computing and write down three differences.
