The Pros and Cons of Distributed Tracing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Distributed Tracing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports finding slow services
- Makes understanding dependencies easier
- Helps with debugging errors across services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing context propagation
- Risk: sampling too aggressively
- Risk: overhead from too many spans

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as finding slow services, and you can manage the risks, Distributed Tracing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
