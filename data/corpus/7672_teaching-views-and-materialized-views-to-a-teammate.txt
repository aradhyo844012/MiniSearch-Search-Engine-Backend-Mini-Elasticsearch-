Teaching Views and Materialized Views to a Teammate

Explaining Views and Materialized Views to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a window cut into a wall that shows only part of the room. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving simplifying repeated queries, naming view definitions and refresh strategies as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain permissions in Views and Materialized Views back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, stale materialized data, and suggest this habit: document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
