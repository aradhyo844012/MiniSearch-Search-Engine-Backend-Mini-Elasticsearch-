Everything You Need to Know About Packet Switching

This page collects the essentials about Packet Switching in one place. In short, Packet Switching is a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network.

Core concepts:
- packets
- headers
- routing
- reassembly

Where it is used:
- internet traffic
- voice over IP
- file transfers

Common problems:
- packet loss
- out-of-order delivery
- large packets being fragmented

Tools to know:
- Wireshark
- tcpdump
- iperf

Remember this above all: mailing a book one page per envelope and reassembling it on arrival is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
