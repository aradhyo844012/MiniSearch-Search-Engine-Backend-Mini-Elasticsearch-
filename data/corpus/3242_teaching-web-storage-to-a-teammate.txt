Teaching Web Storage to a Teammate

Explaining Web Storage to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a sticky note on the fridge that only your household can read. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Web Storage is browser features for keeping small amounts of data on the user's device. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving saving user preferences, naming localStorage and sessionStorage as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain cookies in Web Storage back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, storing sensitive data, and suggest this habit: wrap every read and write in error handling.
