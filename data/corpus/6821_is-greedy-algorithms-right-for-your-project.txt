Is Greedy Algorithms Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Greedy Algorithms, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Greedy Algorithms is algorithms that build a solution by always taking the locally best choice.

It is a good fit when your work involves interval scheduling or Huffman coding, and when your team understands greedy choice property.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn optimal substructure, or when you cannot manage these risks: assuming greedy always works, missing counterexamples, and skipping the correctness proof.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether exchange arguments and local versus global optimum are part of the answer. If they are, Greedy Algorithms is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with making change with standard coins and try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it.
