Event Sourcing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Event Sourcing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Event Sourcing in your own words.
2. Describe event log, projections, snapshots, and replay.
3. Give examples from audit trails and financial ledgers.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Event Sourcing is a design in which every change is stored as an immutable event and state is rebuilt from them. Then draw a bank statement that lists every transaction instead of only the balance on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is changing event formats carelessly a problem? How does projections relate to snapshots? When would you choose Apache Kafka or EventStoreDB?

Study habit worth keeping: version your events from the first day.

When you feel ready, compare Event Sourcing with Consistent Hashing and write down three differences.
