A* Pathfinding for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover A* Pathfinding.

Learning goals:
1. Explain A* Pathfinding in your own words.
2. Describe open and closed sets, cost so far, heuristics, and grids and graphs.
3. Give examples from game characters and robot navigation.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: A* Pathfinding is a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining. Then draw a hiker who prefers trails that seem to head toward the summit on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is overestimating the heuristic a problem? How does cost so far relate to heuristics? When would you choose Unity NavMesh or Python heapq?

Study habit: choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.

When you feel ready, compare A* Pathfinding with Procedural Generation and write down three differences.
