A* Pathfinding Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with A* Pathfinding.

Do:
- Learn open and closed sets before anything else.
- Try Unity NavMesh on a small example.
- Make it a habit to choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
- Test the case of game characters early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overestimating the heuristic.
- Ignore this: slow searches on huge maps.
- Ignore this: ignoring moving obstacles.
- Adopt A* Pathfinding just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with A* Pathfinding, a little patience with cost so far pays off more than any clever shortcut.
