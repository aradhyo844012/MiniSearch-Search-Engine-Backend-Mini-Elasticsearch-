The Pros and Cons of Data Lakes

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Data Lakes is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports storing logs and events
- Makes machine learning datasets easier
- Helps with analytics across many sources

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: turning into a data swamp
- Risk: no catalog or ownership
- Risk: poor access controls

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as storing logs and events, and you can manage the risks, Data Lakes is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.
