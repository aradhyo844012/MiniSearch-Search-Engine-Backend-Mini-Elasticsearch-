Choosing Tools for Greedy Algorithms

There is no single best tool for Greedy Algorithms. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

pen and paper proofs is a common first choice, especially for interval scheduling. Python sorted suits teams that already work with similar technology, and unit tests is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle Huffman coding well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: assuming greedy always works.

As a rule of thumb, try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it, no matter which tool you use.
