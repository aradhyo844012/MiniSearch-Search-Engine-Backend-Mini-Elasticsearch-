Encryption at Rest Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Encryption at Rest regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for LUKS first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as AWS KMS.
2. Be careful with this one: storing keys next to the data.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit key management before anything else.
4. Do not skip symmetric keys; everything else builds on it.
5. Write down why you chose Encryption at Rest so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to meeting compliance rules as well.
