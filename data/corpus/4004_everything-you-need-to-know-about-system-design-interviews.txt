Everything You Need to Know About System Design Interviews

This page collects the essentials about System Design Interviews in one place. In short, System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices.

Core concepts:
- requirements
- scaling estimates
- trade-offs
- components

Where it is used:
- preparing for senior roles
- practicing architectural thinking
- explaining real systems

Common problems:
- jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements
- ignoring trade-offs
- overcomplicating designs

Tools to know:
- whiteboard sketches
- Excalidraw
- system design primers

Remember this above all: sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
