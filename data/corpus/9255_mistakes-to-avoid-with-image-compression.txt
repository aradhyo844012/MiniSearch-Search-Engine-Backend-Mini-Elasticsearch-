Mistakes to Avoid with Image Compression

Everyone learns Image Compression partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: repeated recompression. It usually appears when someone skips lossy and lossless formats and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: the wrong format for graphics. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of web images. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: excessive quality loss. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on quality settings to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
