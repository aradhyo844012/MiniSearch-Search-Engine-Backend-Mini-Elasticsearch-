Game AI Behavior Trees in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Game AI Behavior Trees.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Game AI Behavior Trees is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn selector nodes, sequence nodes, conditions, and actions.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a flowchart a character consults every moment before acting. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through enemy behavior, companion characters, and simulated crowds.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for trees that become tangled and expensive conditions checked every frame, then try Unreal Engine behavior trees for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to visualize the tree while the game is running.
