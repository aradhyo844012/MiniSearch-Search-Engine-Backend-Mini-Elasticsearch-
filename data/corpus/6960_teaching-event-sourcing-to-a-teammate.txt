Teaching Event Sourcing to a Teammate

Explaining Event Sourcing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a bank statement that lists every transaction instead of only the balance. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Event Sourcing is a design in which every change is stored as an immutable event and state is rebuilt from them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving audit trails, naming event log and projections as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain snapshots in Event Sourcing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, changing event formats carelessly, and suggest this habit: version your events from the first day.
