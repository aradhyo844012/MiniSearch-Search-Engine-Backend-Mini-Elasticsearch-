Client-Side Routing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Client-Side Routing.

Do:
- Learn history API before anything else.
- Try React Router on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test refreshing the page on every route.
- Test the case of single-page apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: broken deep links.
- Ignore this: unhandled 404 pages.
- Ignore this: lost scroll position.
- Adopt Client-Side Routing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Client-Side Routing, a little patience with route matching pays off more than any clever shortcut.
