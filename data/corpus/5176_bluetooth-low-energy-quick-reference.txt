Bluetooth Low Energy Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Bluetooth Low Energy. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.
Key terms: advertising, GATT services, characteristics, and pairing.
Typical uses: fitness trackers, smart locks, and proximity beacons.
Risks: ignoring connection limits, weak pairing security, and assuming long range.
Tools: nRF Connect, BlueZ, and Android Bluetooth APIs.

Mental picture: a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy.

Habit to build: test with several phone models.

See also: MQTT, which sits close to Bluetooth Low Energy in the area of Mobile, Embedded and IoT.
