Choosing Tools for Log Aggregation

There is no single best tool for Log Aggregation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Elasticsearch is a common first choice, especially for troubleshooting across services. Fluent Bit suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Grafana Loki is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle security investigations well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: unbounded storage growth.

As a rule of thumb, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable, no matter which tool you use.
