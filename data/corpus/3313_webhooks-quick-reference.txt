Webhooks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Webhooks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens.
Key terms: event payloads, signature verification, retries, and endpoints.
Typical uses: payment notifications, CI triggers, and chat integrations.
Risks: not verifying signatures, slow handlers causing timeouts, and processing duplicate events.
Tools: ngrok, the Stripe CLI, and Postman.

Mental picture: a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch.

Habit to build: respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
