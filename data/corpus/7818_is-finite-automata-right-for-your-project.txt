Is Finite Automata Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Finite Automata, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Finite Automata is simple abstract machines that read input and move between a fixed set of states.

It is a good fit when your work involves tokenizers or pattern matching, and when your team understands states.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn transitions, or when you cannot manage these risks: missing transitions, confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines, and state explosion.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether accepting states and regular languages are part of the answer. If they are, Finite Automata is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with protocol design and draw the state diagram before writing any code.
