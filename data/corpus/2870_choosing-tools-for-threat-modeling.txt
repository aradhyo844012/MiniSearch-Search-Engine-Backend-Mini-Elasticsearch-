Choosing Tools for Threat Modeling

There is no single best tool for Threat Modeling. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

STRIDE is a common first choice, especially for designing new features. OWASP Threat Dragon suits teams that already work with similar technology, and data flow diagrams is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle reviewing architecture well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: doing it once and never again.

As a rule of thumb, draw the data flow diagram first, no matter which tool you use.
