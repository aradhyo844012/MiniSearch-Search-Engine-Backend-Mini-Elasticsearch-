How Linear Regression Connects to Decision Trees

Linear Regression and Decision Trees are closely connected parts of Machine Learning. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving predicting prices may also involve classifying customers. In such a project, coefficients and splits often influence each other.

Start with Linear Regression: the term refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. Then look at Decision Trees, which refers to models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as estimating demand, and explain how entropy and Gini impurity would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Linear Regression, watch for this: ignoring outliers. For Decision Trees, watch for this: ignoring class imbalance. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using scikit-learn or scikit-learn.
