Mistakes to Avoid with Jupyter Notebooks

Everyone learns Jupyter Notebooks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: running cells out of order. It usually appears when someone skips cells and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: hidden state. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of exploring data. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: committing outputs that contain secrets. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on outputs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
