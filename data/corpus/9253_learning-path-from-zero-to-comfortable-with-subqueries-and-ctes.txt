Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Subqueries and CTEs

This path is for a learner who has never studied Subqueries and CTEs before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets) and learn subqueries and WITH clauses.

Stage 2, hands-on: install PostgreSQL, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving breaking complex reports into steps.

Stage 3, depth: study correlated queries and readability, then deliberately explore this problem: correlated subqueries that run slowly.

Stage 4, application: apply Subqueries and CTEs to filtering with computed results, compare SQLite with dbt, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Subqueries and CTEs to someone else, and then move on to Database Schema Design for Beginners.
