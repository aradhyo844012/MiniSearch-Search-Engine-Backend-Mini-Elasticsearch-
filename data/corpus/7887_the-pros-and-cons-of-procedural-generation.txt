The Pros and Cons of Procedural Generation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Procedural Generation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports roguelike dungeons
- Makes infinite terrain easier
- Helps with varied item drops

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: levels that are unfair or unwinnable
- Risk: repetitive results
- Risk: bugs that cannot be reproduced without seeds

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as roguelike dungeons, and you can manage the risks, Procedural Generation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
