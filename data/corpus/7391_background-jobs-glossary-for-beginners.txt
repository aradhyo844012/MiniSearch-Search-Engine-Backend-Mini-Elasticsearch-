Background Jobs Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Background Jobs feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Background Jobs: tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them.
queues: a core term, most visible in sending emails.
workers: a core term that beginners often confuse with retries.
retries: a core term that connects to scheduling.
scheduling: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Celery: a tool commonly used for resizing images.

Come back to this Background Jobs list whenever documentation for Sidekiq uses a word you do not recognize.
