Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Single Sign-On

This path is for a learner who has never studied Single Sign-On before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications) and learn identity provider and SAML.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Okta, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving company intranets.

Stage 3, depth: study OpenID Connect and session lifetime, then deliberately explore this problem: a single point of failure.

Stage 4, application: apply Single Sign-On to reducing password fatigue, compare Keycloak with Azure AD, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Single Sign-On to someone else, and then move on to Encryption at Rest.
