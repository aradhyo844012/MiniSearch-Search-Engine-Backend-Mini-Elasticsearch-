Skills to Build Around Embeddings

Knowing the definition of Embeddings is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with vectors and cosine similarity, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with sentence-transformers on a Embeddings task, and try at least one alternative such as FAISS.

Judgment: learn to decide when Embeddings fits a problem such as semantic search, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example coordinates on a map where similar places are close together.

Habits: use the same embedding model for indexing and querying, and write down what you learn after each project.
