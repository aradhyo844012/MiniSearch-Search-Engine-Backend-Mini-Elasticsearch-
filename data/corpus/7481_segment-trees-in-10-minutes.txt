Segment Trees in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Segment Trees.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Segment Trees is a tree structure that answers range queries and handles updates in logarithmic time.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn tree nodes covering ranges, range queries, point updates, and lazy propagation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a tournament bracket that remembers the winner of every group of players. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through range sum queries, finding minimums in a window, and competitive programming problems.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for off-by-one errors in ranges and wrong tree size allocation, then try C++ arrays for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test your tree against a slow brute-force version on small inputs.
