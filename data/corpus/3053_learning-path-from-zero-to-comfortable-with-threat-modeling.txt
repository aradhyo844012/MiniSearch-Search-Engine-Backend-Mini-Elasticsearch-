Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Threat Modeling

This path is for a learner who has never studied Threat Modeling before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Threat Modeling is a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it) and learn assets and attackers.

Stage 2, hands-on: install STRIDE, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving designing new features.

Stage 3, depth: study trust boundaries and mitigations, then deliberately explore this problem: doing it once and never again.

Stage 4, application: apply Threat Modeling to reviewing architecture, compare OWASP Threat Dragon with data flow diagrams, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Threat Modeling to someone else, and then move on to Content Security Policy.
