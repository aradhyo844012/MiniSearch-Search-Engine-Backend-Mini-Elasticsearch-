Convolutional Neural Networks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Convolutional Neural Networks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: too little training data.
2. Reach for PyTorch first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as TensorFlow.
3. Write down why you chose Convolutional Neural Networks so the next person understands the decision.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit pooling before anything else.
5. Make sure you can start from a pretrained model.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to medical image analysis as well.
