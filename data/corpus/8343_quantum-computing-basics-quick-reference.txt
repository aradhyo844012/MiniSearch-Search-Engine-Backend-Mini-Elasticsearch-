Quantum Computing Basics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Quantum Computing Basics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems.
Key terms: qubits, superposition, entanglement, and quantum gates.
Typical uses: research on cryptography, simulating molecules, and optimization studies.
Risks: expecting speedups for every problem, noise and errors, and hype about current machines.
Tools: Qiskit, Cirq, and IBM Quantum.

Mental picture: a coin spinning in the air before it lands.

Habit to build: start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
