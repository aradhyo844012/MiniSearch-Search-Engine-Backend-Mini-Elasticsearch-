Embeddings Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Embeddings.

Do:
- Learn vectors before anything else.
- Try sentence-transformers on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
- Test the case of semantic search early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing models between indexing and querying.
- Ignore this: ignoring normalization.
- Ignore this: stale embeddings after data changes.
- Adopt Embeddings just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Embeddings, a little patience with cosine similarity pays off more than any clever shortcut.
