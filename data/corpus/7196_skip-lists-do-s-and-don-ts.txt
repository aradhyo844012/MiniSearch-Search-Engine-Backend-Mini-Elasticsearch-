Skip Lists Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Skip Lists.

Do:
- Learn multiple levels before anything else.
- Try Redis sorted sets on a small example.
- Make it a habit to draw several levels by hand to see how a search moves down.
- Test the case of ordered in-memory indexes early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: poor random number choices.
- Ignore this: memory overhead.
- Ignore this: harder debugging than arrays.
- Adopt Skip Lists just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Skip Lists, a little patience with random level assignment pays off more than any clever shortcut.
