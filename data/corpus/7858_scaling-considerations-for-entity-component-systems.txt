Scaling Considerations for Entity Component Systems

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Entity Component Systems changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding entities and getting large simulations right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for overengineering small games and components that hold logic, and measure with Bevy before changing anything.

Large scale: systems and queries move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around games with thousands of objects at this stage.

Before you scale, keep components as plain data. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
