Microservices Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Microservices.

- Definition: an architecture that splits an application into small services that can be deployed independently.
- Core ideas: service boundaries; APIs; independent deployment; distributed data.
- Use it for: large teams working in parallel; scaling parts separately; replacing components gradually.
- Watch out for: splitting too early; chatty services; hard debugging across services.
- Tools: Docker, Kubernetes, API gateways.
- Related topic: Caching Strategies.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for APIs before building anything large.
