Teaching gRPC to a Teammate

Explaining gRPC to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a phone line with a pre-agreed script for every call. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving service-to-service calls, naming protocol buffers and service definitions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain streaming in gRPC back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, breaking changes in message fields, and suggest this habit: never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file.
