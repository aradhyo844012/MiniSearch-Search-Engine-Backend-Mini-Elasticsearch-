Where to Go Next After Learning Build Systems

You have met the basics of Build Systems. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about WebAssembly, which belongs to the same area as Build Systems (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of targets, dependencies, incremental builds or build caches, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around packaging software with Make, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow full rebuilds.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Build Systems to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
