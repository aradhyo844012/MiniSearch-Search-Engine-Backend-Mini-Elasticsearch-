State Management in Real Projects

Reading about State Management is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: large single-page apps. Here, single source of truth drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against putting everything in global state.

Scenario two: shared shopping carts. This time actions matters most, and tools such as Redux help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: multi-step form wizards. The key idea is reducers, and the biggest risk is duplicated state that drifts apart.

Across all three scenarios, State Management stays the same idea: the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data. Only the details change.
