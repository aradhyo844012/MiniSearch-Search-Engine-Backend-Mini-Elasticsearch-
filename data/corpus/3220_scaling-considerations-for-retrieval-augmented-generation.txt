Scaling Considerations for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Retrieval-Augmented Generation changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding retrieval and getting company knowledge assistants right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for poor chunking and irrelevant retrieved text, and measure with LangChain before changing anything.

Large scale: prompts with context and grounding move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around question answering over documents at this stage.

Before you scale, evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
