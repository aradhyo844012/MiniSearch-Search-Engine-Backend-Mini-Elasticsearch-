Background Jobs Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Background Jobs. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them.
Key terms: queues, workers, retries, and scheduling.
Typical uses: sending emails, resizing images, and generating reports.
Risks: jobs that are not idempotent, unbounded retries, and losing jobs on restarts.
Tools: Celery, Sidekiq, and BullMQ.

Mental picture: dropping laundry at a service and collecting it later.

Habit to build: give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
