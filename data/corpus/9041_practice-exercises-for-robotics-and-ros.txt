Practice Exercises for Robotics and ROS

These exercises build real understanding of Robotics and ROS. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: ignoring sensor noise. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Explain how nodes and topics and control loops differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick ROS 2 and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare Gazebo and Arduino for educational robots and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Robotics and ROS and include the term sensors.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.
