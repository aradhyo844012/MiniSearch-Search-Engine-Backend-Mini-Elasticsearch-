Fenwick Trees Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Fenwick Trees.

Do:
- Learn binary indexed trees before anything else.
- Try C++ vectors on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start indexing from one to keep the bit tricks simple.
- Test the case of counting inversions early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using zero-based indexes by mistake.
- Ignore this: forgetting that nodes store partial sums.
- Ignore this: using it for operations that cannot be reversed.
- Adopt Fenwick Trees just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Fenwick Trees, a little patience with lowest set bit pays off more than any clever shortcut.
