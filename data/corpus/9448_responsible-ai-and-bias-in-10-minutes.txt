Responsible AI and Bias in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Responsible AI and Bias.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn bias in data, fairness metrics, transparency, and human oversight.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through hiring tools, lending models, and healthcare decisions.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for assuming data is neutral and testing only on average performance, then try Fairlearn for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test performance separately for different groups.
