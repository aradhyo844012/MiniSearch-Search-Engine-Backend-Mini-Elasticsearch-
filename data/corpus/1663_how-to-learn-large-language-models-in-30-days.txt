How to Learn Large Language Models in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Large Language Models. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Large Language Models. The term refers to very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language.

Week 1: learn tokens and attention, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Hugging Face on a small task such as drafting text.
Week 3: build a mini project around answering questions.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as trusting outputs without checking, and explain Large Language Models to a friend.

In practice you can start with Hugging Face, then explore API playgrounds or evaluation harnesses as your needs grow.

Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.

In short, careful study of Large Language Models pays off. Spend time on tokens and attention, and the rest will follow.
