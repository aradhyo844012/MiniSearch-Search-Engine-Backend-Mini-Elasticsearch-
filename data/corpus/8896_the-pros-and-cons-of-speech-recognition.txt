The Pros and Cons of Speech Recognition

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Speech Recognition is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports voice assistants
- Makes captioning easier
- Helps with dictation

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: accents and background noise
- Risk: domain-specific vocabulary
- Risk: privacy of recordings

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as voice assistants, and you can manage the risks, Speech Recognition is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
