Feature Stores Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Feature Stores regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of sharing features across models early, before the design hardens.
2. Write down why you chose Feature Stores so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit online store before anything else.
4. Make sure you can define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
5. Do not skip offline store; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to serving real-time predictions as well.
