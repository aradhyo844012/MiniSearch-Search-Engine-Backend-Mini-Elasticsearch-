MLOps Pipelines in Real Projects

Reading about MLOps Pipelines is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: repeatable model releases. Here, data versioning drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against untracked data changes.

Scenario two: tracking experiments. This time training pipelines matters most, and tools such as MLflow help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: rolling back models. The key idea is the model registry, and the biggest risk is manual deployment steps.

Across all three scenarios, MLOps Pipelines stays the same idea: automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models. Only the details change.
