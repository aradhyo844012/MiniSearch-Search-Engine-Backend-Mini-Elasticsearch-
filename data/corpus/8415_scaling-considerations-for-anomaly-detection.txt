Scaling Considerations for Anomaly Detection

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Anomaly Detection changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding baselines and getting fraud detection right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for too many false alarms and shifting baselines, and measure with scikit-learn before changing anything.

Large scale: isolation methods and false positives move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around server monitoring at this stage.

Before you scale, tune alerts using real examples of past incidents. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
