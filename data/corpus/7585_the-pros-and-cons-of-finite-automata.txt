The Pros and Cons of Finite Automata

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Finite Automata is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports tokenizers
- Makes pattern matching easier
- Helps with protocol design

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing transitions
- Risk: confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines
- Risk: state explosion

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as tokenizers, and you can manage the risks, Finite Automata is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, draw the state diagram before writing any code.
