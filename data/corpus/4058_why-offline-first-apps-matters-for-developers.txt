Why Offline-First Apps Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Offline-First Apps make better decisions, because Offline-First Apps is apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns.

Three situations show why it matters. First, field service tools. Second, note-taking apps. Third, apps for low-connectivity regions. In each case, a solid grasp of local storage and sync queues separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: unresolved sync conflicts, unbounded local data, and poor feedback about pending changes.

You will not need to build everything yourself; SQLite, PouchDB, and Room cover most needs.
