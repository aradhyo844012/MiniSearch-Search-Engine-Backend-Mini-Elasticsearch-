Mistakes to Avoid with Time Management for Developers

Everyone learns Time Management for Developers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: multitasking. It usually appears when someone skips prioritization and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: underestimating tasks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of managing coursework and projects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: working without breaks. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on avoiding context switches to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
