Open Source Contribution in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Open Source Contribution.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Open Source Contribution is helping improve public software projects by fixing bugs, writing documentation or adding features.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn forks, pull requests, issues, and contributing guides.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine helping repair a shared public library. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through building a portfolio, learning from experienced maintainers, and giving back to tools you use.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for opening huge pull requests and ignoring contribution rules, then try GitHub for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start with documentation fixes or small bugs.
