Practice Exercises for Disk Partitioning and Mounting

These exercises build real understanding of Disk Partitioning and Mounting. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick lsblk and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Explain how mount points and fstab differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Compare fdisk and mount for encrypting volumes and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Disk Partitioning and Mounting and include the term partitions.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble adding a new drive or separating data from the system.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Disk Partitioning and Mounting is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree.
