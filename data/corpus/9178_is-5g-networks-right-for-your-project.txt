Is 5G Networks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting 5G Networks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that 5G Networks is the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency.

It is a good fit when your work involves mobile broadband or connected vehicles, and when your team understands spectrum bands.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn latency, or when you cannot manage these risks: limited coverage on the fastest bands, battery drain, and marketing exaggeration.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether network slicing and small cells are part of the answer. If they are, 5G Networks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with industrial automation and test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds.
