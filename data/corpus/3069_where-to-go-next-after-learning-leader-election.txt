Where to Go Next After Learning Leader Election

You have met the basics of Leader Election. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Consistent Hashing, which belongs to the same area as Leader Election (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of heartbeats, terms or epochs, candidates or failover, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around job schedulers with ZooKeeper, and note every problem you meet, starting with split brain.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Leader Election to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
