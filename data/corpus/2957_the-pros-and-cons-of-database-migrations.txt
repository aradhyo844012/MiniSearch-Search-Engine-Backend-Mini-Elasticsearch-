The Pros and Cons of Database Migrations

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Database Migrations is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports adding columns safely
- Makes renaming tables easier
- Helps with keeping environments in sync

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: locking large tables
- Risk: editing old migrations
- Risk: data loss on rollbacks

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as adding columns safely, and you can manage the risks, Database Migrations is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
