Is Modular Monoliths Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Modular Monoliths, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Modular Monoliths is single deployable applications built from well-separated internal modules.

It is a good fit when your work involves small teams or early-stage products, and when your team understands module boundaries.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn public interfaces, or when you cannot manage these risks: modules reaching into each other's tables, no enforced boundaries, and treating modules as just folders.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether shared database rules and dependency checks are part of the answer. If they are, Modular Monoliths is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with preparing for later service extraction and enforce boundaries with automated checks.
