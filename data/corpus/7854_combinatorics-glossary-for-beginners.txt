Combinatorics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Combinatorics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Combinatorics: the art of counting arrangements and selections.
permutations: a core term, most visible in analyzing algorithms.
combinations: a core term that beginners often confuse with the pigeonhole principle.
the pigeonhole principle: a core term that connects to inclusion-exclusion.
inclusion-exclusion: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python itertools: a tool commonly used for probability.

Come back to this Combinatorics list whenever documentation for math.comb uses a word you do not recognize.
