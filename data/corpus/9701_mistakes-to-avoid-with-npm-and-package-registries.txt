Mistakes to Avoid with npm and Package Registries

Everyone learns npm and Package Registries partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: unpinned versions. It usually appears when someone skips package.json and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: installing untrusted packages. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of installing JavaScript libraries. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: huge dependency trees. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on registries to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: commit your lock file.
