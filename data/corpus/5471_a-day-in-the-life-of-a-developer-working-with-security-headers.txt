A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Security Headers

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Security Headers.

Morning: Imran starts by reviewing a ticket about hardening websites. Before touching any code, Imran re-reads the notes on Strict-Transport-Security to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Imran pairs with a teammate to explore X-Content-Type-Options with securityheaders.com. A small test exposes the problem of setting headers on only some routes, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving reducing clickjacking. Imran sketches safety instructions printed on the packaging of every page on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Imran writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to test the headers on every environment.

Days like this are typical. Security Headers is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
