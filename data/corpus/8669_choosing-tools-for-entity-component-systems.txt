Choosing Tools for Entity Component Systems

There is no single best tool for Entity Component Systems. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Bevy is a common first choice, especially for large simulations. Unity DOTS suits teams that already work with similar technology, and EnTT is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Entity Component Systems by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle games with thousands of objects well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Entity Component Systems, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: overengineering small games.

As a rule of thumb, keep components as plain data, no matter which tool you use.
