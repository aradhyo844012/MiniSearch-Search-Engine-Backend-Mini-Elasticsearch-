Convolutional Neural Networks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Convolutional Neural Networks.

Do:
- Learn filters before anything else.
- Try PyTorch on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start from a pretrained model.
- Test the case of image classification early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too little training data.
- Ignore this: ignoring data augmentation.
- Ignore this: overly deep models for simple tasks.
- Adopt Convolutional Neural Networks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Convolutional Neural Networks, a little patience with pooling pays off more than any clever shortcut.
