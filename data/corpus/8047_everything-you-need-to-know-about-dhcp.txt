Everything You Need to Know About DHCP

This page collects the essentials about DHCP in one place. In short, DHCP is a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings.

Core concepts:
- leases
- address pools
- reservations
- discover and offer messages

Where it is used:
- home and office networks
- guest Wi-Fi
- booting machines over a network

Common problems:
- rogue DHCP servers
- exhausted address pools
- conflicting static addresses

Tools to know:
- dnsmasq
- ISC Kea
- Wireshark

Remember this above all: a hotel desk that hands out room numbers for a limited stay is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.
