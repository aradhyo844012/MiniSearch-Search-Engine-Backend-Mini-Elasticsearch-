Mistakes to Avoid with CSS Variables

Everyone learns CSS Variables partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: missing fallbacks. It usually appears when someone skips custom properties and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: overriding at the wrong scope. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of theme switching. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: expecting preprocessor features. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fallback values to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: define theme values on the root element.
