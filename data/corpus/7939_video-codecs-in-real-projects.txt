Video Codecs in Real Projects

Reading about Video Codecs is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: streaming services. Here, frames drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mismatched codec support.

Scenario two: video calls. This time motion compensation matters most, and tools such as FFmpeg help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: archiving footage. The key idea is bitrate, and the biggest risk is too low a bitrate.

Across all three scenarios, Video Codecs stays the same idea: programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed. Only the details change.
