Everything You Need to Know About Information Theory and Entropy

This page collects the essentials about Information Theory and Entropy in one place. In short, Information Theory and Entropy is the mathematics of measuring and compressing information.

Core concepts:
- bits
- entropy
- compression limits
- noise

Where it is used:
- data compression
- error-correcting codes
- communication systems

Common problems:
- confusing information with meaning
- ignoring noise
- misreading entropy values

Tools to know:
- the Python math module
- NumPy
- zlib

Remember this above all: measuring how surprised you are by each new message is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
