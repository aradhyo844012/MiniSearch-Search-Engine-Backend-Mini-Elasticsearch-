Practice Exercises for Multi-Cloud Strategy

These exercises build real understanding of Multi-Cloud Strategy. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble avoiding single-provider risk or meeting regional rules.
Exercise 2: Explain how abstraction layers and cost comparison differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Compare Kubernetes and Crossplane for using best-of-breed services and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Multi-Cloud Strategy and include the term portability.
Exercise 5: Pick Terraform and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.
