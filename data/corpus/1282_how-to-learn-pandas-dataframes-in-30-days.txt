How to Learn Pandas DataFrames in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Pandas DataFrames. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Pandas DataFrames, they usually mean table-like structures in Python for loading, cleaning and analyzing data. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

A solid grasp of Pandas DataFrames also makes Data Cleaning easier to understand.

Week 1: learn rows and columns and indexing, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with pandas on a small task such as loading CSV files.
Week 3: build a mini project around summarizing query logs.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as chained assignment surprises, and explain Pandas DataFrames to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include pandas, Jupyter, and NumPy.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Pandas DataFrames.
