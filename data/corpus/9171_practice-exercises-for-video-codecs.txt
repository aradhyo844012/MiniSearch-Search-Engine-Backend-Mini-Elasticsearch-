Practice Exercises for Video Codecs

These exercises build real understanding of Video Codecs. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble streaming services or video calls.
Exercise 2: Explain how bitrate and containers differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Compare H.264 and AV1 for archiving footage and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mismatched codec support. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Video Codecs and include the term frames.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.
