Pair Programming in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Pair Programming.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Pair Programming is two developers working together at one computer on the same code.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn driver and navigator, rotation, communication, and shared ownership.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a pilot and co-pilot who check each other's decisions. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through teaching new teammates, solving hard bugs, and improving code quality.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for one person dominating and fatigue from long sessions, then try VS Code Live Share for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.
