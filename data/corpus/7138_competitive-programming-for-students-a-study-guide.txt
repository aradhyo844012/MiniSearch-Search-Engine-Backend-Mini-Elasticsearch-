Competitive Programming for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Competitive Programming.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Competitive Programming in your own words.
2. Describe problem patterns, time limits, contests, and upsolving.
3. Give examples from interview preparation and learning algorithms.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Competitive Programming is solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills. Then draw athletic training for problem solving on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is memorizing solutions without understanding a problem? How does time limits relate to contests? When would you choose Codeforces or LeetCode?

Study habit worth keeping: review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.

When you feel ready, compare Competitive Programming with Debugging Strategies and write down three differences.
