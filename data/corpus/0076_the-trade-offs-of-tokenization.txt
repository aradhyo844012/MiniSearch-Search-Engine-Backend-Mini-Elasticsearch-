The Trade-offs of Tokenization

Every design choice has a cost, and Tokenization is no exception. The term Tokenization refers to splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

On the positive side, Tokenization is well suited to indexing documents and preparing text for models. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: different rules for indexing and querying; dropping useful numbers; breaking on apostrophes badly. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much lowercasing and splitting on punctuation matter to your project.

Three issues come up again and again: different rules for indexing and querying; dropping useful numbers; and breaking on apostrophes badly. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.
