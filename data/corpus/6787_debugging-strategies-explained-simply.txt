Debugging Strategies Explained Simply

If Debugging Strategies sounds intimidating, relax. It is systematic ways of finding and fixing the cause of unexpected program behavior, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Debugging Strategies as a detective narrowing down suspects. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: reproducing the bug, isolating the cause, forming hypotheses, and binary search through code. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with reproducing the bug, then add isolating the cause.

People reach for Debugging Strategies when they work on fixing production issues, learning unfamiliar code, and reducing repeat bugs.

One practical tip is to change one thing at a time and record the result.
