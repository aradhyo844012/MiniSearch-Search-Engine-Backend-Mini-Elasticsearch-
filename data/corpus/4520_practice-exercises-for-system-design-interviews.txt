Practice Exercises for System Design Interviews

These exercises build real understanding of System Design Interviews. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Excalidraw and system design primers for explaining real systems and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Explain how trade-offs and components differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble preparing for senior roles or practicing architectural thinking.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing System Design Interviews and include the term requirements.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices.
