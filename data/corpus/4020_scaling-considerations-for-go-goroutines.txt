Scaling Considerations for Go Goroutines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Go Goroutines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding goroutines and getting servers handling many connections right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for goroutine leaks and data races, and measure with the go command before changing anything.

Large scale: select statements and wait groups move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around parallel data processing at this stage.

Before you scale, run your tests with the race detector turned on. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
