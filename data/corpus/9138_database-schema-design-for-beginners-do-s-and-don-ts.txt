Database Schema Design for Beginners Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Database Schema Design for Beginners.

Do:
- Learn entities before anything else.
- Try dbdiagram.io on a small example.
- Make it a habit to write down the main queries before finalizing the tables.
- Test the case of starting new projects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: storing lists in a single column.
- Ignore this: unclear names.
- Ignore this: designing without thinking about queries.
- Adopt Database Schema Design for Beginners just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Database Schema Design for Beginners, a little patience with relationships pays off more than any clever shortcut.
