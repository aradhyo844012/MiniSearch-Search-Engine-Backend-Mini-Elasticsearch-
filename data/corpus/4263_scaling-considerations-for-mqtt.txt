Scaling Considerations for MQTT

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how MQTT changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding publish and subscribe and getting sensor networks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for unsecured brokers and messy topic names, and measure with Mosquitto before changing anything.

Large scale: brokers and quality of service levels move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around smart home devices at this stage.

Before you scale, design topic names like a clear folder structure. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
