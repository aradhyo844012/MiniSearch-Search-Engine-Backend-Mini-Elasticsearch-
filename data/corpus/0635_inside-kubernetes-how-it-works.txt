Inside Kubernetes: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Kubernetes, step by step. Put simply, Kubernetes covers a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Four ideas sit at the heart of Kubernetes: pods, deployments, services, and ingress. Each one builds on the one before it. Sketching ingress on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

First, pods sets the stage for everything else. Next, deployments builds on it. After that, services comes into play, and finally ingress ties the pieces together. Seeing the sequence once, with a tiny example, makes the whole topic easier to remember.

Cloud resources are easy to create and just as easy to forget, which makes costs grow quietly. Small, frequent releases are usually safer than rare, large ones.

A solid grasp of Kubernetes also makes Git Branching easier to understand.

With steady practice, Kubernetes will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
