Scaling Considerations for Transformers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Transformers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding self-attention and getting language translation right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for huge compute requirements and limited context windows, and measure with Hugging Face before changing anything.

Large scale: encoder and decoder blocks and tokens move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around text generation at this stage.

Before you scale, begin with small pretrained models. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
