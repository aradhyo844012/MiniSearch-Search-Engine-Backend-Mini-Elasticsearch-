B-Trees on Disk vs Database Backups and Recovery: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study B-Trees on Disk or Database Backups and Recovery first. Both sit in the area of Storage Engines and Databases, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

B-Trees on Disk is balanced tree structures designed to read large blocks from disk efficiently, and it is closely tied to pages and branching factor. Database Backups and Recovery, by contrast, is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures, and it is closely tied to full and incremental backups and recovery point objective.

Choose B-Trees on Disk first if you care about database indexes or file system directories. Choose Database Backups and Recovery first if you care about recovering deleted tables or migrating servers.

Think of B-Trees on Disk as a filing cabinet where each drawer tells you which drawer to open next, and of Database Backups and Recovery as a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With B-Trees on Disk, avoid random writes on fragmented pages. With Database Backups and Recovery, avoid never testing restores.

A good habit for B-Trees on Disk is to keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page. A good habit for Database Backups and Recovery is to schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
