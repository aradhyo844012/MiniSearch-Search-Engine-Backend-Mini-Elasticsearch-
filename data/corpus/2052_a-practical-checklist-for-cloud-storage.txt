A Practical Checklist for Cloud Storage

Use this checklist before you rely on Cloud Storage in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Cloud Storage should accomplish.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers buckets.
4. Measure results before and after any change.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Review how Cloud Storage interacts with Docker.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on objects.
