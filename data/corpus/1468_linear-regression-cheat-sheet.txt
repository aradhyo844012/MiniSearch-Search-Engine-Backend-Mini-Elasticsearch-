Linear Regression Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Linear Regression.

- Definition: a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data.
- Core ideas: coefficients; residuals; least squares; assumptions.
- Use it for: predicting prices; estimating demand; finding trends.
- Watch out for: extrapolating far beyond the data; ignoring outliers; using correlated features carelessly.
- Tools: scikit-learn, NumPy, statsmodels.
- Quick tip: practice coefficients until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: Gradient Descent.
