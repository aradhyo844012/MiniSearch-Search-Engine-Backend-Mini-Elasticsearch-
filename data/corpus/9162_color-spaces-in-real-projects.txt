Color Spaces in Real Projects

Reading about Color Spaces is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: web design. Here, RGB drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against ignoring gamma.

Scenario two: photo editing. This time HSL matters most, and tools such as browser color pickers help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: printing. The key idea is CMYK, and the biggest risk is mixing color spaces.

Across all three scenarios, Color Spaces stays the same idea: mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK. Only the details change.
