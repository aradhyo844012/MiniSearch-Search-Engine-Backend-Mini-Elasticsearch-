Understanding MQTT Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a radio station with channels that devices can tune into.

In that picture, publish and subscribe is the first rule you follow, topics is the next step, and brokers and quality of service levels keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks, and it involves details such as unsecured brokers that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Mosquitto to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain sensor networks to a friend.
