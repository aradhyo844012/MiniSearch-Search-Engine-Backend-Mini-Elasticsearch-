Log Aggregation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Log Aggregation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Log Aggregation is collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn shippers, indexing, retention, and search queries.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine gathering notes from every desk into a single searchable binder. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through troubleshooting across services, security investigations, and usage analysis.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for unbounded storage growth and unstructured logs, then try Elasticsearch for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.
