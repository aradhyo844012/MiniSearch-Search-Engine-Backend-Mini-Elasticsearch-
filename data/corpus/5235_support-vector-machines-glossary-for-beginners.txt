Support Vector Machines Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Support Vector Machines feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Support Vector Machines: models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes.
margin: a core term, most visible in text classification.
support vectors: a core term that beginners often confuse with kernels.
kernels: a core term that connects to regularization.
regularization: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
scikit-learn: a tool commonly used for small high-dimensional datasets.

Come back to this Support Vector Machines list whenever documentation for LIBSVM uses a word you do not recognize.
