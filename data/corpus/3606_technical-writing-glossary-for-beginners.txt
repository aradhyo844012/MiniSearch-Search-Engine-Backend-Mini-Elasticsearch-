Technical Writing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Technical Writing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Technical Writing: explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.
audience: a core term, most visible in documentation.
structure: a core term that beginners often confuse with examples.
examples: a core term that connects to editing.
editing: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Markdown: a tool commonly used for blog posts.

Come back to this Technical Writing list whenever documentation for Grammarly uses a word you do not recognize.
