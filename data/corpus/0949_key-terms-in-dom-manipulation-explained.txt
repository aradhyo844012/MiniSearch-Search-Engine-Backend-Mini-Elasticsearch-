Key Terms in DOM Manipulation Explained

Clear vocabulary makes DOM Manipulation much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about DOM Manipulation, they usually mean reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.
