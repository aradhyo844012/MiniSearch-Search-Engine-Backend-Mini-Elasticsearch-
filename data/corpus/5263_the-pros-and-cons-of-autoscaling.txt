The Pros and Cons of Autoscaling

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Autoscaling is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports handling traffic spikes
- Makes saving money overnight easier
- Helps with keeping latency steady

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: scaling too slowly
- Risk: flapping because of short spikes
- Risk: ignoring database limits

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as handling traffic spikes, and you can manage the risks, Autoscaling is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
