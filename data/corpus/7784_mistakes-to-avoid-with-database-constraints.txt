Mistakes to Avoid with Database Constraints

Everyone learns Database Constraints partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: skipping constraints for speed. It usually appears when someone skips primary keys and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing indexes on foreign keys. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of preventing bad data. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: rules enforced only in the app. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on check constraints to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
