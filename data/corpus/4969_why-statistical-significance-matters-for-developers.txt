Why Statistical Significance Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Statistical Significance make better decisions, because Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect.

Three situations show why it matters. First, judging experiment results. Second, comparing model performance. Third, reporting research findings. In each case, a solid grasp of p-values and the null hypothesis separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: mistaking significance for importance, p-hacking, and ignoring sample size.

Good tools exist, including SciPy, statsmodels, and R, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
