Speech Recognition Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Speech Recognition feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Speech Recognition: converting spoken language into text using machine learning.
audio features: a core term, most visible in voice assistants.
acoustic models: a core term that beginners often confuse with language models.
language models: a core term that connects to word error rate.
word error rate: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Whisper: a tool commonly used for captioning.
