Why Model Deployment Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Model Deployment make better decisions, because Model Deployment is making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application.

Three situations show why it matters. First, recommendation endpoints. Second, fraud checks. Third, mobile app features. In each case, a solid grasp of serving APIs and batch versus online inference separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: training-serving mismatch, slow cold starts, and no fallback when the model fails.

Good tools exist, including FastAPI, TorchServe, and BentoML, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
