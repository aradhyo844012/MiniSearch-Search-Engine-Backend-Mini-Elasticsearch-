Debugging Greedy Algorithms: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Greedy Algorithms, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: assuming greedy always works, missing counterexamples, and skipping the correctness proof.
Step 3: revisit greedy choice property and optimal substructure, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use pen and paper proofs or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Greedy Algorithms in work such as interval scheduling come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
