Scaling Considerations for Multi-Cloud Strategy

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Multi-Cloud Strategy changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding portability and getting avoiding single-provider risk right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for doubling operational complexity and lowest-common-denominator designs, and measure with Terraform before changing anything.

Large scale: abstraction layers and cost comparison move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around meeting regional rules at this stage.

Before you scale, be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
