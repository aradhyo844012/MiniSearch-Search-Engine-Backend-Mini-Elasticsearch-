Where to Go Next After Learning Log Aggregation

You have met the basics of Log Aggregation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Service Mesh, which belongs to the same area as Log Aggregation (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of shippers, indexing, retention or search queries, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around security investigations with Elasticsearch, and note every problem you meet, starting with unbounded storage growth.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Log Aggregation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
