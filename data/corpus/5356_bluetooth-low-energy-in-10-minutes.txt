Bluetooth Low Energy in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Bluetooth Low Energy.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn advertising, GATT services, characteristics, and pairing.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through fitness trackers, smart locks, and proximity beacons.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for ignoring connection limits and weak pairing security, then try nRF Connect for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test with several phone models.
