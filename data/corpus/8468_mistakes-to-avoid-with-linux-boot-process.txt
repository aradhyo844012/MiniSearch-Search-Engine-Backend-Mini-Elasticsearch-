Mistakes to Avoid with Linux Boot Process

Everyone learns Linux Boot Process partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overwriting the bootloader. It usually appears when someone skips firmware and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: wrong kernel parameters. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of troubleshooting machines that do not start. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring recovery options. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the init system to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep a recovery option available before editing boot settings.
