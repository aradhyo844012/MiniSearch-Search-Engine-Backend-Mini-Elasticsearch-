gRPC Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with gRPC.

Do:
- Learn protocol buffers before anything else.
- Try protoc on a small example.
- Make it a habit to never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file.
- Test the case of service-to-service calls early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: breaking changes in message fields.
- Ignore this: poor browser support without a proxy.
- Ignore this: harder manual debugging.
- Adopt gRPC just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with gRPC, a little patience with service definitions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
