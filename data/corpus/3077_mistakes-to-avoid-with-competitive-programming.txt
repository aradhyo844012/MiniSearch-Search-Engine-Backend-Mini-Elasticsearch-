Mistakes to Avoid with Competitive Programming

Everyone learns Competitive Programming partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: memorizing solutions without understanding. It usually appears when someone skips problem patterns and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: skipping fundamentals. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of interview preparation. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: burning out. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on upsolving to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
