Mistakes to Avoid with Internationalization

Everyone learns Internationalization partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hard-coded strings. It usually appears when someone skips locale detection and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: text that overflows layouts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of global products. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming left-to-right text. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on date and number formatting to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
