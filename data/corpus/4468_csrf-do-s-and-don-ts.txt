CSRF Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with CSRF.

Do:
- Learn session cookies before anything else.
- Try Django CSRF middleware on a small example.
- Make it a habit to combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.
- Test the case of protecting forms early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using GET requests for state changes.
- Ignore this: skipping tokens on cookie-based APIs.
- Ignore this: misconfigured SameSite settings.
- Adopt CSRF just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with CSRF, a little patience with anti-CSRF tokens pays off more than any clever shortcut.
