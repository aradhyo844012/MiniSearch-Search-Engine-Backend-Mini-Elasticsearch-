Mistakes to Avoid with NAT

Everyone learns NAT partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: breaking peer-to-peer connections. It usually appears when someone skips private addresses and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: exhausted translation tables. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of home routers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: misconfigured port forwarding. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on port forwarding to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: document every port forward you create.
