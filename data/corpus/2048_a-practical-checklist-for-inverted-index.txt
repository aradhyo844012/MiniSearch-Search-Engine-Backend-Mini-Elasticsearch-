A Practical Checklist for Inverted Index

Use this checklist before you rely on Inverted Index in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers vocabulary.
2. Confirm that your team agrees on what Inverted Index should accomplish.
3. Review the core ideas: vocabulary, postings lists, term frequency, and document frequency.
4. Review how Inverted Index interacts with Zipf's Law.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example full-text search.
6. Guard against this risk: building the index without normalizing text.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on postings lists.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
9. Document your decisions in the project README.
