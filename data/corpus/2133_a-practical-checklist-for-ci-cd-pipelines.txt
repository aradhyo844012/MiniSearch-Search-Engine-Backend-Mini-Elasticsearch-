A Practical Checklist for CI/CD Pipelines

Use this checklist before you rely on CI/CD Pipelines in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what CI/CD Pipelines should accomplish.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on test stage.
3. Guard against this risk: flaky tests.
4. Guard against this risk: secrets in logs.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers build stage.
7. Guard against this risk: slow pipelines.
8. Pick a tool to start with, such as GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
9. Measure results before and after any change.
