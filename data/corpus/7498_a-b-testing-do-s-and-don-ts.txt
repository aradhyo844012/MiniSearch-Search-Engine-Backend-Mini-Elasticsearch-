A/B Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with A/B Testing.

Do:
- Learn control and variant before anything else.
- Try Optimizely on a small example.
- Make it a habit to decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
- Test the case of testing page designs early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: stopping tests early.
- Ignore this: testing too many variants.
- Ignore this: ignoring sample size.
- Adopt A/B Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with A/B Testing, a little patience with random assignment pays off more than any clever shortcut.
