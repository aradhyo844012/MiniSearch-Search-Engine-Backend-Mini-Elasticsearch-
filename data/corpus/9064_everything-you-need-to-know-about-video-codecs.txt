Everything You Need to Know About Video Codecs

This page collects the essentials about Video Codecs in one place. In short, Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.

Core concepts:
- frames
- motion compensation
- bitrate
- containers

Where it is used:
- streaming services
- video calls
- archiving footage

Common problems:
- mismatched codec support
- too low a bitrate
- ignoring hardware decoding

Tools to know:
- FFmpeg
- H.264
- AV1

Remember this above all: a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test playback on the weakest target device.
