CDNs: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is CDNs?
A: The term CDNs refers to networks of servers around the world that deliver static content from a location close to the user.

Q: How does it relate to TLS Handshake?
A: CDNs and TLS Handshake often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Networking.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: caching private data; forgetting to purge old files; and misconfigured cache headers.

Q: Why do developers use CDNs?
A: Common uses include serving images and scripts, speeding up global sites, and absorbing traffic spikes.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with edge servers and cache rules, then move on to origin servers and cache purging.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Cloudflare, Akamai, and Amazon CloudFront.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with CDNs.
