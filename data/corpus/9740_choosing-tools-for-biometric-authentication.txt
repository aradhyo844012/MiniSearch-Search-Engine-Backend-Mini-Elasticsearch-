Choosing Tools for Biometric Authentication

There is no single best tool for Biometric Authentication. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Android BiometricPrompt is a common first choice, especially for phone unlock. Face ID APIs suits teams that already work with similar technology, and FIDO2 is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Biometric Authentication by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle building access well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Biometric Authentication, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: spoofing attacks.

As a rule of thumb, offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates, no matter which tool you use.
