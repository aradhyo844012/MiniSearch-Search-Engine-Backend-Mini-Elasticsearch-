Game AI Behavior Trees Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Game AI Behavior Trees feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Game AI Behavior Trees: tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does.
selector nodes: a core term, most visible in enemy behavior.
sequence nodes: a core term that beginners often confuse with conditions.
conditions: a core term that connects to actions.
actions: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Unreal Engine behavior trees: a tool commonly used for companion characters.

Come back to this Game AI Behavior Trees list whenever documentation for py_trees uses a word you do not recognize.
