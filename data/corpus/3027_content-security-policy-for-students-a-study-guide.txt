Content Security Policy for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Content Security Policy.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Content Security Policy in your own words.
2. Describe directives, nonces, report-only mode, and trusted sources.
3. Give examples from reducing the impact of XSS and blocking unwanted scripts.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Content Security Policy is a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load. Then draw a guest list at the door of a building on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is allowing unsafe-inline a problem? How does nonces relate to report-only mode? When would you choose browser DevTools or CSP Evaluator?

Study habit worth keeping: start in report-only mode to learn what would break.

When you feel ready, compare Content Security Policy with CSRF and write down three differences.
