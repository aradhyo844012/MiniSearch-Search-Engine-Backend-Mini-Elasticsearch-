Physics Engines Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Physics Engines. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.
Key terms: rigid bodies, forces and impulses, constraints, and fixed time steps.
Typical uses: vehicles and ragdolls, puzzle games, and training simulations.
Risks: variable time steps, unstable stacks of objects, and extreme mass ratios.
Tools: Box2D, Bullet, and PhysX.

Mental picture: a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop.

Habit to build: use a fixed time step for stable results.
