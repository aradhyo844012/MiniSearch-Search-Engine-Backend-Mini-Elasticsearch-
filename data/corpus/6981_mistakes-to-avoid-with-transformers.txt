Mistakes to Avoid with Transformers

Everyone learns Transformers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: huge compute requirements. It usually appears when someone skips self-attention and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: limited context windows. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of language translation. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: biased training data. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on tokens to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: begin with small pretrained models.
