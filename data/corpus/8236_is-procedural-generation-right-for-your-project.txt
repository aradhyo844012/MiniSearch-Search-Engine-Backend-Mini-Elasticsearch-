Is Procedural Generation Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Procedural Generation, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Procedural Generation is creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms.

It is a good fit when your work involves roguelike dungeons or infinite terrain, and when your team understands random seeds.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn noise functions, or when you cannot manage these risks: levels that are unfair or unwinnable, repetitive results, and bugs that cannot be reproduced without seeds.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether rules and constraints and reproducibility are part of the answer. If they are, Procedural Generation is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with varied item drops and log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
