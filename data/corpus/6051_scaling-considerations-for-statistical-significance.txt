Scaling Considerations for Statistical Significance

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Statistical Significance changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding p-values and getting judging experiment results right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mistaking significance for importance and p-hacking, and measure with SciPy before changing anything.

Large scale: confidence intervals and effect size move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around comparing model performance at this stage.

Before you scale, always report the effect size alongside the p-value. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
