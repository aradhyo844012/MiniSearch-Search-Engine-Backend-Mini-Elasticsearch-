The Pros and Cons of Augmented and Virtual Reality

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Augmented and Virtual Reality is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports training simulations
- Makes games easier
- Helps with remote collaboration

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: motion sickness from low frame rates
- Risk: heavy assets
- Risk: poor interface design in 3D

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as training simulations, and you can manage the risks, Augmented and Virtual Reality is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
