Property-Based Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Property-Based Testing.

Do:
- Learn properties before anything else.
- Try Hypothesis on a small example.
- Make it a habit to save failing examples as regular tests.
- Test the case of testing parsers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: vague properties.
- Ignore this: slow generators.
- Ignore this: ignoring random seed reports.
- Adopt Property-Based Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Property-Based Testing, a little patience with generators pays off more than any clever shortcut.
