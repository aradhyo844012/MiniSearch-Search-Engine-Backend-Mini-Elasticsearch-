Inside BM25 Ranking: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside BM25 Ranking, step by step. The term BM25 Ranking refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.
