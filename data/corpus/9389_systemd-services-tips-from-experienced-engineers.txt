systemd Services Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with systemd Services regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of starting services at boot early, before the design hardens.
2. Make sure you can run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.
3. Reach for systemctl first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as journalctl.
4. Write down why you chose systemd Services so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: editing unit files without reloading the daemon.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to restarting crashed programs as well as to systemd Services in general.
