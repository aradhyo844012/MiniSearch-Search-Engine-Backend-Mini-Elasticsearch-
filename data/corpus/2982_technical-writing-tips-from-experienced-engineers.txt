Technical Writing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Technical Writing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of documentation early, before the design hardens.
2. Do not skip audience; everything else builds on it.
3. Reach for Markdown first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Grammarly.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit structure before anything else.
5. Make sure you can state the main point in the first two sentences.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to blog posts as well.
