Practice Exercises for AWS IAM Roles and Policies

These exercises build real understanding of AWS IAM Roles and Policies. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how least privilege and temporary credentials differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Compare the AWS CLI and Terraform for auditing permissions and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing AWS IAM Roles and Policies and include the term users and roles.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble securing deployments or granting services access.
Exercise 5: Pick IAM Access Analyzer and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: AWS IAM Roles and Policies is the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account.
