Practice Exercises for Reinforcement Learning

These exercises build real understanding of Reinforcement Learning. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: badly designed rewards. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare Stable-Baselines3 and PyTorch for recommendation strategies and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick Gymnasium and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Reinforcement Learning and include the term agent.
Exercise 5: Explain how reward and policy differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.
