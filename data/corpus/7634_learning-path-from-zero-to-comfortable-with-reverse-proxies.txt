Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Reverse Proxies

This path is for a learner who has never studied Reverse Proxies before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them) and learn upstream servers and proxy headers.

Stage 2, hands-on: install NGINX, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving hiding backend servers.

Stage 3, depth: study TLS termination and caching, then deliberately explore this problem: losing the client IP address.

Stage 4, application: apply Reverse Proxies to terminating HTTPS, compare Caddy with HAProxy, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Reverse Proxies to someone else, and then move on to Wi-Fi Networks.
