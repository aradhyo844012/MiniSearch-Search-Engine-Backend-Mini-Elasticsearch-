Minimum Spanning Trees Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Minimum Spanning Trees regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can sort the edges once and reuse the order when testing variations.
2. Be careful with this one: using it on directed graphs.
3. Reach for NetworkX first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as SciPy.
4. Do not skip weighted graphs; everything else builds on it.
5. Test the case of designing cable networks early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to clustering data as well.
