GraphQL Explained Simply

Here is GraphQL without the jargon: it is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.

Think of GraphQL as ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: schema, queries and mutations, resolvers, and types. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with schema, then add queries and mutations.

You will meet GraphQL in work such as mobile apps with limited bandwidth, aggregating many services, and flexible dashboards.

One practical tip is to use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.

After GraphQL, a natural next topic is gRPC.
