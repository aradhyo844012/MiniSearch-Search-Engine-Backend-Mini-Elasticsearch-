Scaling Considerations for Garbage Collection

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Garbage Collection changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding reachability and getting managed languages like Java and Python right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for creating huge numbers of short-lived objects and holding references longer than needed, and measure with JVM GC logs before changing anything.

Large scale: mark and sweep and pause times move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around reducing memory bugs at this stage.

Before you scale, measure allocation rates before tuning collector settings. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
