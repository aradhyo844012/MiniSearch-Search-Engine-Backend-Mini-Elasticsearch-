Webhooks Explained Simply

Here is Webhooks without the jargon: it is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens.

Think of Webhooks as a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: event payloads, signature verification, retries, and endpoints. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with event payloads, then add signature verification.

People reach for Webhooks when they work on payment notifications, CI triggers, and chat integrations.

One practical tip is to respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
