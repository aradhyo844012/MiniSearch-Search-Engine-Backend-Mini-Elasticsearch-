How to Learn DOM Manipulation in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with DOM Manipulation. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet DOM Manipulation in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript.

Week 1: learn selectors and events, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with querySelector on a small task such as updating search results.
Week 3: build a mini project around handling form input.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as inserting untrusted text as HTML, and explain DOM Manipulation to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include querySelector, browser console, and MDN docs.

With steady practice, DOM Manipulation will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
