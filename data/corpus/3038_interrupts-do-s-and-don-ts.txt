Interrupts Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Interrupts.

Do:
- Learn interrupt handlers before anything else.
- Try Linux /proc/interrupts on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
- Test the case of keyboard and mouse input early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: long handlers.
- Ignore this: race conditions with shared data.
- Ignore this: lost interrupts.
- Adopt Interrupts just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Interrupts, a little patience with interrupt vectors pays off more than any clever shortcut.
