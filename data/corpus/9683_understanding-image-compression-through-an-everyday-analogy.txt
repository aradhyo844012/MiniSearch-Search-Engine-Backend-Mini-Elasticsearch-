Understanding Image Compression Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly.

In that picture, lossy and lossless formats is the first rule you follow, quantization is the next step, and transform coding and quality settings keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail, and it involves details such as repeated recompression that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for ImageMagick to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain web images to a friend.
