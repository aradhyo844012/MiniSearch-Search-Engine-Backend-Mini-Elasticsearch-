Hyperparameter Tuning for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Hyperparameter Tuning.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Hyperparameter Tuning in your own words.
2. Describe search spaces, grid search, random search, and validation scores.
3. Give examples from improving model accuracy and comparing algorithms.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Hyperparameter Tuning is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns. Then draw adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is tuning on the test set a problem? How does grid search relate to random search? When would you choose Optuna or scikit-learn GridSearchCV?

Study habit: try random search before anything fancier.

When you feel ready, compare Hyperparameter Tuning with Random Forests and write down three differences.
