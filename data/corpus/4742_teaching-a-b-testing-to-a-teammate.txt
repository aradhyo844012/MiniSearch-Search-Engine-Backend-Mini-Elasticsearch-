Teaching A/B Testing to a Teammate

Explaining A/B Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving testing page designs, naming control and variant and random assignment as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain metrics in A/B Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, stopping tests early, and suggest this habit: decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
