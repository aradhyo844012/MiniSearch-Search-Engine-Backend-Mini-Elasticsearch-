K-Means Clustering Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with K-Means Clustering.

Do:
- Learn centroids before anything else.
- Try scikit-learn on a small example.
- Make it a habit to scale features before clustering.
- Test the case of customer segmentation early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: choosing k blindly.
- Ignore this: unscaled features.
- Ignore this: sensitivity to starting points.
- Adopt K-Means Clustering just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with K-Means Clustering, a little patience with distance measures pays off more than any clever shortcut.
