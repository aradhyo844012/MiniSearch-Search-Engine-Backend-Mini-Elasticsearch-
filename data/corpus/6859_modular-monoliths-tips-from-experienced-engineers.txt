Modular Monoliths Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Modular Monoliths regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit public interfaces before anything else.
2. Do not skip module boundaries; everything else builds on it.
3. Be careful with this one: modules reaching into each other's tables.
4. Make sure you can enforce boundaries with automated checks.
5. Reach for ArchUnit first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as import-linter.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to early-stage products as well.
