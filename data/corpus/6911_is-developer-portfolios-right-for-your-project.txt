Is Developer Portfolios Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Developer Portfolios, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.

It is a good fit when your work involves applying for internships or showing skills to recruiters, and when your team understands projects.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn README quality, or when you cannot manage these risks: too many unfinished projects, no explanation of decisions, and broken demo links.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether live demos and clear descriptions are part of the answer. If they are, Developer Portfolios is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with tracking personal growth and feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
