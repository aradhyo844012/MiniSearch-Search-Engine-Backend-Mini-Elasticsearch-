Mistakes to Avoid with Robotics and ROS

Everyone learns Robotics and ROS partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring sensor noise. It usually appears when someone skips sensors and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unsafe motion commands. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of warehouse robots. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: tight coupling between nodes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on control loops to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test in simulation before moving real hardware.
