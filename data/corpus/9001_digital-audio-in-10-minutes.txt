Digital Audio in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Digital Audio.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sampling rate, bit depth, waveforms, and the Nyquist limit.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through music production, voice calls, and podcasts.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for clipping from too high a level and aliasing at low sample rates, then try Audacity for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep recordings below the clipping level.
