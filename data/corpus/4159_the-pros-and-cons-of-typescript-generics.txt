The Pros and Cons of TypeScript Generics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and TypeScript Generics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports reusable utility functions
- Makes typed API clients easier
- Helps with data containers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overusing any
- Risk: overly complex generic types
- Risk: forgetting constraints

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as reusable utility functions, and you can manage the risks, TypeScript Generics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, add constraints so that error messages become clearer.
