Rust Ownership in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Rust Ownership.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Rust Ownership is a set of rules that lets Rust manage memory safely without a garbage collector.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn ownership, borrowing, lifetimes, and moves.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a single house key that you can hand over or lend out but never copy. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through systems programming, command line tools, and safe concurrent code.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for fighting the borrow checker with needless clones and confusing moves and copies, then try Cargo for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to read the compiler's error messages closely because they often explain the fix.
