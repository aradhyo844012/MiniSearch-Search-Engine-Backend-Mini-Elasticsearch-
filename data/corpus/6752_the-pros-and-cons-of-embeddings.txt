The Pros and Cons of Embeddings

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Embeddings is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports semantic search
- Makes recommendations easier
- Helps with clustering text

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing models between indexing and querying
- Risk: ignoring normalization
- Risk: stale embeddings after data changes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as semantic search, and you can manage the risks, Embeddings is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
