Practice Exercises for Quantum Computing Basics

These exercises build real understanding of Quantum Computing Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: expecting speedups for every problem. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Quantum Computing Basics and include the term qubits.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble research on cryptography or simulating molecules.
Exercise 4: Pick Qiskit and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how entanglement and quantum gates differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems.
