The Pros and Cons of Packet Switching

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Packet Switching is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports internet traffic
- Makes voice over IP easier
- Helps with file transfers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: packet loss
- Risk: out-of-order delivery
- Risk: large packets being fragmented

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as internet traffic, and you can manage the risks, Packet Switching is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
