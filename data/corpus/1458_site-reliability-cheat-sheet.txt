Site Reliability Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Site Reliability.

- Definition: the discipline of keeping services dependable using engineering practices.
- Core ideas: service level objectives; error budgets; incident response; postmortems.
- Use it for: running production services; reducing outages; planning on-call rotations.
- Watch out for: alerting on everything; blaming people instead of systems; no written postmortems.
- Tools: PagerDuty, Prometheus, runbooks.
- Related topic: CI/CD Pipelines.
- Quick tip: practice service level objectives until it feels automatic.
