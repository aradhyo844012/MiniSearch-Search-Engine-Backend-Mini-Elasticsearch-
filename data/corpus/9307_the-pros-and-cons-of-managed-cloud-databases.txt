The Pros and Cons of Managed Cloud Databases

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Managed Cloud Databases is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports launching apps quickly
- Makes reducing database administration easier
- Helps with high availability setups

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: surprise costs
- Risk: limited configuration control
- Risk: ignoring maintenance windows

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as launching apps quickly, and you can manage the risks, Managed Cloud Databases is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test restoring a backup before you need it.
