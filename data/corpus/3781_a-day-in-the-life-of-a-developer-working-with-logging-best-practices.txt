A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Logging Best Practices

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Logging Best Practices.

Morning: Divya starts by reviewing a ticket about debugging production issues. Before touching any code, Divya re-reads the notes on log levels to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Divya pairs with a teammate to explore structured logs with the Python logging module. A small test exposes the problem of logging sensitive data, and they fix it together.
