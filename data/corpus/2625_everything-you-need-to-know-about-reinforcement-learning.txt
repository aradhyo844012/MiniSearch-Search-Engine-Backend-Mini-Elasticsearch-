Everything You Need to Know About Reinforcement Learning

This page collects the essentials about Reinforcement Learning in one place. In short, Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

Core concepts:
- agent
- environment
- reward
- policy

Where it is used:
- game playing
- robot control
- recommendation strategies

Common problems:
- badly designed rewards
- slow training
- unstable results across runs

Tools to know:
- Gymnasium
- Stable-Baselines3
- PyTorch

Remember this above all: training a dog with treats for good behavior is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to start with simple environments.
