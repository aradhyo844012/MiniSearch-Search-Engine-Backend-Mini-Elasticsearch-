Practice Exercises for GPUs and Parallelism

These exercises build real understanding of GPUs and Parallelism. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: slow data transfers. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble training neural networks or rendering graphics.
Exercise 3: Compare PyTorch and OpenCL for scientific simulations and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Explain how memory transfers and SIMD differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick CUDA and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: GPUs and Parallelism is processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once.
