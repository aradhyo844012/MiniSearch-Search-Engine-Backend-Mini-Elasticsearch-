Vector Databases Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Vector Databases.

Do:
- Learn embeddings before anything else.
- Try FAISS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to measure recall on a small labeled test set.
- Test the case of semantic search early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing embedding models.
- Ignore this: ignoring recall trade-offs.
- Ignore this: oversized vectors.
- Adopt Vector Databases just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Vector Databases, a little patience with approximate nearest neighbors pays off more than any clever shortcut.
