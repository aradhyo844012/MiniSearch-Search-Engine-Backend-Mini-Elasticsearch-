Computer Vision Basics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Computer Vision Basics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn pixels, filters and edges, feature detection, and classification.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through face detection, document scanning, and quality inspection.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for lighting changes and biased datasets, then try OpenCV for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
