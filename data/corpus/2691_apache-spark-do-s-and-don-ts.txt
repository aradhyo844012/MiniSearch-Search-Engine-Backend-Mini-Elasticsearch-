Apache Spark Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Apache Spark.

Do:
- Learn DataFrames before anything else.
- Try PySpark on a small example.
- Make it a habit to avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
- Test the case of large ETL jobs early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: data skew.
- Ignore this: too many small partitions.
- Ignore this: collecting huge results onto the driver.
- Adopt Apache Spark just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Apache Spark, a little patience with transformations and actions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
