The Pros and Cons of Cloud Cost Optimization

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Cloud Cost Optimization is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports cutting idle resources
- Makes budgeting by team easier
- Helps with choosing storage tiers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: forgotten test environments
- Risk: over-provisioned databases
- Risk: surprise data transfer charges

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as cutting idle resources, and you can manage the risks, Cloud Cost Optimization is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, tag every resource with an owner.
