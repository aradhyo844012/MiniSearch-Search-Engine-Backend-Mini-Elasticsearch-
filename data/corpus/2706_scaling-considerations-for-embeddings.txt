Scaling Considerations for Embeddings

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Embeddings changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding vectors and getting semantic search right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mixing models between indexing and querying and ignoring normalization, and measure with sentence-transformers before changing anything.

Large scale: dimensionality and nearest neighbor search move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around recommendations at this stage.

Before you scale, use the same embedding model for indexing and querying. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
