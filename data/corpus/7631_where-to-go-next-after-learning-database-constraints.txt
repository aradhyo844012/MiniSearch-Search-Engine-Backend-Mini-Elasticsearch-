Where to Go Next After Learning Database Constraints

You have met the basics of Database Constraints. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Views and Materialized Views, which belongs to the same area as Database Constraints (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints or check constraints, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around protecting relationships with PostgreSQL, and note every problem you meet, starting with skipping constraints for speed.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Database Constraints to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
