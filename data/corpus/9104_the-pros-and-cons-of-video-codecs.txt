The Pros and Cons of Video Codecs

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Video Codecs is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports streaming services
- Makes video calls easier
- Helps with archiving footage

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mismatched codec support
- Risk: too low a bitrate
- Risk: ignoring hardware decoding

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as streaming services, and you can manage the risks, Video Codecs is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test playback on the weakest target device.
