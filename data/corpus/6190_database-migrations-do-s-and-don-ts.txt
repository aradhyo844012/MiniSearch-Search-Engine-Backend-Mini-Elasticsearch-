Database Migrations Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Database Migrations.

Do:
- Learn up and down scripts before anything else.
- Try Alembic on a small example.
- Make it a habit to split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
- Test the case of adding columns safely early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: locking large tables.
- Ignore this: editing old migrations.
- Ignore this: data loss on rollbacks.
- Adopt Database Migrations just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Database Migrations, a little patience with version tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
