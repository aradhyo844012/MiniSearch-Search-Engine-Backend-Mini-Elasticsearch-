Why Dependency Injection Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Dependency Injection make better decisions, because Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them.

Three situations show why it matters. First, easier unit testing. Second, swapping implementations. Third, configuring services. In each case, a solid grasp of constructor injection and interfaces separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: hiding too much behind containers, circular dependencies, and injecting too many dependencies.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Spring, FastAPI Depends, and Dagger cover most needs.
