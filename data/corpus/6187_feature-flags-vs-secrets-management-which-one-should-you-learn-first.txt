Feature Flags vs Secrets Management: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Feature Flags or Secrets Management first. Both sit in the area of DevOps and Cloud, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code, and it is closely tied to flag evaluation and targeting rules. Secrets Management, by contrast, is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely, and it is closely tied to vaults and rotation.

Choose Feature Flags first if you care about dark launches or A/B tests. Choose Secrets Management first if you care about database credentials or API keys.

Think of Feature Flags as light switches wired to every room of a house, and of Secrets Management as a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Feature Flags, avoid never removing old flags. With Secrets Management, avoid secrets in source control.

A good habit for Feature Flags is to give every flag an owner and a removal date. A good habit for Secrets Management is to rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
