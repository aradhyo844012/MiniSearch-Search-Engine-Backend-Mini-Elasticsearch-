Practice Exercises for SOLID Principles

These exercises build real understanding of SOLID Principles. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble structuring classes or reviewing designs.
Exercise 3: Explain how Liskov substitution and dependency inversion differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing SOLID Principles and include the term single responsibility.
Exercise 5: Pick UML diagrams and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: SOLID Principles is five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change.
