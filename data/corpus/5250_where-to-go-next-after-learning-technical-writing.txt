Where to Go Next After Learning Technical Writing

You have met the basics of Technical Writing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Reading Documentation, which belongs to the same area as Technical Writing (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of audience, structure, examples or editing, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around blog posts with Markdown, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming too much knowledge.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Technical Writing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
