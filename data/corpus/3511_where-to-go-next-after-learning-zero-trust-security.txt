Where to Go Next After Learning Zero Trust Security

You have met the basics of Zero Trust Security. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Threat Modeling, which belongs to the same area as Zero Trust Security (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of identity verification, least privilege, micro-segmentation or continuous checks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around protecting internal services with Okta, and note every problem you meet, starting with treating it as a product to buy.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Zero Trust Security to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
