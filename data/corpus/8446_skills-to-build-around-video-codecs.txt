Skills to Build Around Video Codecs

Knowing the definition of Video Codecs is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with frames and motion compensation, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with FFmpeg on a Video Codecs task, and try at least one alternative such as H.264.

Judgment: learn to decide when Video Codecs fits a problem such as streaming services, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages.

Habits: test playback on the weakest target device, and write down what you learn after each project.
