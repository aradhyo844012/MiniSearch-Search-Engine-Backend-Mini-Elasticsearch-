Robotics and ROS Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Robotics and ROS. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.
Key terms: sensors, actuators, nodes and topics, and control loops.
Typical uses: warehouse robots, drones, and educational robots.
Risks: ignoring sensor noise, unsafe motion commands, and tight coupling between nodes.
Tools: ROS 2, Gazebo, and Arduino.

Mental picture: a body with senses and muscles controlled by a nervous system of messages.

Habit to build: test in simulation before moving real hardware.
