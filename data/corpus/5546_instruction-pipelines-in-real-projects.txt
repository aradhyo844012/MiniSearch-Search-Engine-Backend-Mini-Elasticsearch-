Instruction Pipelines in Real Projects

Reading about Instruction Pipelines is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: explaining CPU speed. Here, fetch, decode and execute stages drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against unpredictable branches.

Scenario two: understanding branch costs. This time hazards matters most, and tools such as perf help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: optimizing tight loops. The key idea is branch prediction, and the biggest risk is dependency chains.

Across all three scenarios, Instruction Pipelines stays the same idea: a technique where a processor overlaps the stages of several instructions. Only the details change.
