Teaching State Management to a Teammate

Explaining State Management to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a shared whiteboard that everyone updates through the same rules. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving large single-page apps, naming single source of truth and actions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain reducers in State Management back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, putting everything in global state, and suggest this habit: keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
