systemd Services Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with systemd Services.

Do:
- Learn unit files before anything else.
- Try systemctl on a small example.
- Make it a habit to run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.
- Test the case of starting services at boot early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: editing unit files without reloading the daemon.
- Ignore this: missing restart policies.
- Ignore this: ignoring dependency ordering.
- Adopt systemd Services just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with systemd Services, a little patience with targets pays off more than any clever shortcut.
