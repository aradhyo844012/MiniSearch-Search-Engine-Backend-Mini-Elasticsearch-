Inside Bloom Filters: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Bloom Filters, step by step. The term Bloom Filters refers to compact probabilistic structures that quickly tell you whether an item is probably in a set. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

To understand Bloom Filters, start with bit arrays. Then move on to multiple hash functions, and finally look at false positives and no false negatives. A good way to remember bit arrays is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

First, bit arrays sets the stage for everything else. Next, multiple hash functions builds on it. After that, false positives comes into play, and finally no false negatives ties the pieces together. Seeing the sequence once, with a tiny example, makes the whole topic easier to remember.

If you are comfortable with Bloom Filters, explore Binary Search next. The two often appear in the same project.
