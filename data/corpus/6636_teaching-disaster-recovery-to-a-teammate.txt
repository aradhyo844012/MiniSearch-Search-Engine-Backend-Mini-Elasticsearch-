Teaching Disaster Recovery to a Teammate

Explaining Disaster Recovery to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a fire drill for a whole system. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving surviving regional outages, naming recovery time objective and recovery point objective as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain failover regions in Disaster Recovery back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, untested plans, and suggest this habit: run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
