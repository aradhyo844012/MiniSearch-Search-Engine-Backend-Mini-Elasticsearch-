Data Anonymization Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Data Anonymization.

Do:
- Learn masking before anything else.
- Try ARX on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test whether combining fields could identify a person.
- Test the case of sharing datasets with researchers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: re-identification through combined fields.
- Ignore this: removing too little.
- Ignore this: destroying usefulness by removing too much.
- Adopt Data Anonymization just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Data Anonymization, a little patience with generalization pays off more than any clever shortcut.
