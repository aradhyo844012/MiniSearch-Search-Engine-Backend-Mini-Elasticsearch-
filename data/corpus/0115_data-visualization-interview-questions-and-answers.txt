Data Visualization Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Data Visualization.

Q: Explain Data Visualization in simple words.
A: Data Visualization refers to presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see. I would add an example involving explaining results.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as chart types, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: truncated axes; unreadable labels; and too many colors.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with exploring data, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: chart types, axes and labels, color choices, and annotations.
