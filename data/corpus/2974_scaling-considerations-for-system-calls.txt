Scaling Considerations for System Calls

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how System Calls changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding user mode and kernel mode and getting reading and writing files right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring error codes and making too many small calls, and measure with strace before changing anything.

Large scale: file descriptors and libc wrappers move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around creating processes at this stage.

Before you scale, use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
