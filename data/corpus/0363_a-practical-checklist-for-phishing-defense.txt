A Practical Checklist for Phishing Defense

Use this checklist before you rely on Phishing Defense in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: reusing passwords.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Guard against this risk: clicking before checking.
4. Try the approach on a small case, for example protecting employees.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on sender verification.
6. Guard against this risk: ignoring reports from users.
7. Confirm that your team agrees on what Phishing Defense should accomplish.
8. Pick a tool to start with, such as SPF or DKIM.
