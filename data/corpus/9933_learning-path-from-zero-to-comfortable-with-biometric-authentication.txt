Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Biometric Authentication

This path is for a learner who has never studied Biometric Authentication before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Biometric Authentication is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices) and learn enrollment and matching.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Android BiometricPrompt, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving phone unlock.

Stage 3, depth: study false accept and false reject rates and liveness detection, then deliberately explore this problem: spoofing attacks.

Stage 4, application: apply Biometric Authentication to building access, compare Face ID APIs with FIDO2, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Biometric Authentication to someone else, and then move on to Wearable Technology.
