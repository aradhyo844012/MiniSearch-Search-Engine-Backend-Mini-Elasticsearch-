Mistakes to Avoid with Smart Contracts

Everyone learns Smart Contracts partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: bugs that cannot be patched. It usually appears when someone skips contract code and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: reentrancy attacks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of token systems. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: high gas costs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on immutability to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
