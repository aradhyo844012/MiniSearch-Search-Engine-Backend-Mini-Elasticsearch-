Debugging Anomaly Detection: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Anomaly Detection, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: too many false alarms, shifting baselines, and labeling normal data incorrectly.
Step 3: revisit baselines and thresholds, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use scikit-learn or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Anomaly Detection in work such as fraud detection come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, tune alerts using real examples of past incidents. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
