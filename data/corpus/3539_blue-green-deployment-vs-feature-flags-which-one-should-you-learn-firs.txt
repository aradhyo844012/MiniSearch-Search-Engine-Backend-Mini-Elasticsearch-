Blue-Green Deployment vs Feature Flags: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Blue-Green Deployment or Feature Flags first. Both sit in the area of DevOps and Cloud, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Blue-Green Deployment is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one, and it is closely tied to blue and green environments and traffic switching. Feature Flags, by contrast, is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code, and it is closely tied to flag evaluation and targeting rules.

Choose Blue-Green Deployment first if you care about zero-downtime releases or quick rollbacks. Choose Feature Flags first if you care about dark launches or A/B tests.

Think of Blue-Green Deployment as swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on, and of Feature Flags as light switches wired to every room of a house. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Blue-Green Deployment, avoid database schema mismatches. With Feature Flags, avoid never removing old flags.

A good habit for Blue-Green Deployment is to keep the old environment running until the new one is proven. A good habit for Feature Flags is to give every flag an owner and a removal date.
