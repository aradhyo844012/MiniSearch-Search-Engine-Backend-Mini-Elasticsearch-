Choosing Tools for Time Management for Developers

There is no single best tool for Time Management for Developers. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

calendar blocking is a common first choice, especially for managing coursework and projects. Trello suits teams that already work with similar technology, and the Pomodoro technique is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle meeting deadlines well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: multitasking.

As a rule of thumb, break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less, no matter which tool you use.
