Model Drift and Monitoring Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Model Drift and Monitoring regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Model Drift and Monitoring so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: monitoring only system health.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit concept drift before anything else.
4. Do not skip data drift; everything else builds on it.
5. Test the case of catching degrading models early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to scheduling retraining as well as to Model Drift and Monitoring in general.
