Mistakes to Avoid with Fuzz Testing

Everyone learns Fuzz Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: running without sanitizers. It usually appears when someone skips input generation and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: crashes that cannot be reproduced. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of testing parsers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring slow inputs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on corpora to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep every crashing input as a regression test.
