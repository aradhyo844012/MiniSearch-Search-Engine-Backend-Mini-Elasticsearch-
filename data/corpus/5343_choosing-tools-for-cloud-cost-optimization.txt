Choosing Tools for Cloud Cost Optimization

There is no single best tool for Cloud Cost Optimization. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

AWS Cost Explorer is a common first choice, especially for cutting idle resources. Infracost suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Kubecost is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle budgeting by team well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: forgotten test environments.

As a rule of thumb, tag every resource with an owner, no matter which tool you use.
