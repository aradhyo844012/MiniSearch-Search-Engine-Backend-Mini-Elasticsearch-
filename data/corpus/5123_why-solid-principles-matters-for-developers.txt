Why SOLID Principles Matters for Developers

Developers who understand SOLID Principles make better decisions, because SOLID Principles is five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change.

Three situations show why it matters. First, structuring classes. Second, reviewing designs. Third, reducing coupling. In each case, a solid grasp of single responsibility and the open-closed principle separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts, creating too many tiny classes, and confusing interfaces with inheritance.

You will not need to build everything yourself; UML diagrams, SonarQube, and IDE refactoring tools cover most needs.

Connect SOLID Principles with API Design Principles to see the bigger picture of Software Design.
