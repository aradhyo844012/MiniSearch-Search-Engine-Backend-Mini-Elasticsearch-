Feature Engineering vs Natural Language Processing: Key Differences

Feature Engineering and Natural Language Processing both belong to the area of Machine Learning, but they solve different problems. This article compares them side by side so you can choose with confidence.

The term Feature Engineering refers to creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily. Its core ideas are scaling, encoding categories, and interaction features.

The term Natural Language Processing refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language. Its core ideas are tokenization, embeddings, and language models.

The main difference is focus. Feature Engineering puts the emphasis on scaling and encoding categories, while Natural Language Processing puts the emphasis on tokenization and embeddings.

Reach for Feature Engineering when your task involves improving model accuracy or preparing tabular data. Reach for Natural Language Processing when your task involves search engines or chatbots.

Both have risks. With Feature Engineering, watch for this: data leakage. With Natural Language Processing, watch for this: ignoring language variety.

In practice you do not always have to choose. Many projects use Feature Engineering alongside Natural Language Processing, with tools such as pandas and spaCy. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.
