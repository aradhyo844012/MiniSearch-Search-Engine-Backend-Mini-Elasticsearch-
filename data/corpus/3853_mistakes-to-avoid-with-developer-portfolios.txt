Mistakes to Avoid with Developer Portfolios

Everyone learns Developer Portfolios partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too many unfinished projects. It usually appears when someone skips projects and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no explanation of decisions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of applying for internships. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: broken demo links. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on clear descriptions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
