BGP and Internet Routing in Real Projects

Reading about BGP and Internet Routing is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: connecting ISPs. Here, autonomous systems drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against route leaks.

Scenario two: directing traffic between large networks. This time route announcements matters most, and tools such as bgpq4 help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: explaining internet outages. The key idea is peering, and the biggest risk is hijacked prefixes.

Across all three scenarios, BGP and Internet Routing stays the same idea: the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach. Only the details change.
