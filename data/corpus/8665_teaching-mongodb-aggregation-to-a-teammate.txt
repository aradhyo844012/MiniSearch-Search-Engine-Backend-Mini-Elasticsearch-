Teaching MongoDB Aggregation to a Teammate

Explaining MongoDB Aggregation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: an assembly line where each station filters or reshapes the parts. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving summarizing orders, naming pipeline stages and $match and $group as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain projection in MongoDB Aggregation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, putting $match late, and suggest this habit: filter with $match as early as possible.
