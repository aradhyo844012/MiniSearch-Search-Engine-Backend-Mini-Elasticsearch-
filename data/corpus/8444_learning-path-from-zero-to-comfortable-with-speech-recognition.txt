Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Speech Recognition

This path is for a learner who has never studied Speech Recognition before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning) and learn audio features and acoustic models.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Whisper, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving voice assistants.

Stage 3, depth: study language models and word error rate, then deliberately explore this problem: accents and background noise.

Stage 4, application: apply Speech Recognition to captioning, compare Kaldi with cloud speech-to-text services, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Speech Recognition to someone else, and then move on to Responsible AI and Bias.
