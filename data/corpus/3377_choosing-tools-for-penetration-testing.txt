Choosing Tools for Penetration Testing

There is no single best tool for Penetration Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

OWASP ZAP is a common first choice, especially for pre-launch security checks. Burp Suite suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Nmap is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle compliance audits well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: testing without written permission.

As a rule of thumb, get written authorization before any test, no matter which tool you use.
