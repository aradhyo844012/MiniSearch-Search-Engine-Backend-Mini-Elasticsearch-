Combining Developer Portfolios with Time Management for Developers in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Developer Portfolios and Time Management for Developers can work together in a single project.

Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build. Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.

Imagine a project that involves applying for internships and managing coursework and projects. Developer Portfolios brings projects, while Time Management for Developers brings prioritization.

A sensible plan is to start with GitHub Pages for the Developer Portfolios part and calendar blocking for the Time Management for Developers part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: no explanation of decisions on one side, and underestimating tasks on the other.

Picture the whole as a shop window displaying your best work, working alongside packing a suitcase with the big items first.

Two habits help: first, feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones; second, break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
