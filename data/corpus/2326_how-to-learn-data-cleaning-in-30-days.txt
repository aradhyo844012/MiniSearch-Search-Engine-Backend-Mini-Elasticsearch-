How to Learn Data Cleaning in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Data Cleaning. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Data Cleaning in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data.

Week 1: learn missing values and duplicates, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with pandas on a small task such as preparing logs.
Week 3: build a mini project around merging data sources.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as dropping data without checking why it is missing, and explain Data Cleaning to a friend.

In practice you can start with pandas, then explore OpenRefine or regular expressions as your needs grow.

Data Cleaning and ETL Pipelines are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Data Engineering.

With steady practice, Data Cleaning will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
