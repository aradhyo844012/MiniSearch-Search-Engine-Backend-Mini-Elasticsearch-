Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Number Theory and RSA

This path is for a learner who has never studied Number Theory and RSA before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption) and learn prime numbers and modular arithmetic.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Python pow, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving public key cryptography.

Stage 3, depth: study greatest common divisor and modular exponentiation, then deliberately explore this problem: using small primes.

Stage 4, application: apply Number Theory and RSA to hash functions, compare OpenSSL with SageMath, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Number Theory and RSA to someone else, and then move on to Combinatorics.
