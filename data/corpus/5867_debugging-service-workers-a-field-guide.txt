Debugging Service Workers: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Service Workers, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: caching stale files forever, bugs surviving across deployments, and forgetting that HTTPS is required.
Step 3: revisit registration and install and activate events, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Workbox or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Service Workers in work such as offline support come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, version your caches so old files can be removed. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
