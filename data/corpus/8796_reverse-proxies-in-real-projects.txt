Reverse Proxies in Real Projects

Reading about Reverse Proxies is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: hiding backend servers. Here, upstream servers drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against losing the client IP address.

Scenario two: terminating HTTPS. This time proxy headers matters most, and tools such as NGINX help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: routing requests to several apps. The key idea is TLS termination, and the biggest risk is wrong timeouts.

Across all three scenarios, Reverse Proxies stays the same idea: servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them. Only the details change.
