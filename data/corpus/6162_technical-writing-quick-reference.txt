Technical Writing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Technical Writing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.
Key terms: audience, structure, examples, and editing.
Typical uses: documentation, blog posts, and design proposals.
Risks: assuming too much knowledge, burying the main point, and long unbroken paragraphs.
Tools: Markdown, Grammarly, and Vale.

Mental picture: giving directions to a stranger.

Habit to build: state the main point in the first two sentences.
