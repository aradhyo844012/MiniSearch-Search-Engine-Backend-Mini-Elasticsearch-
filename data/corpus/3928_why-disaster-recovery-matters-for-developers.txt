Why Disaster Recovery Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Disaster Recovery make better decisions, because Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Three situations show why it matters. First, surviving regional outages. Second, meeting compliance needs. Third, protecting critical data. In each case, a solid grasp of recovery time objective and recovery point objective separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: untested plans, outdated runbooks, and dependencies missed in the plan.

You will not need to build everything yourself; AWS Backup, Terraform, and Route 53 failover cover most needs.
