Scaling Considerations for SQL Basics

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how SQL Basics changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding SELECT and getting reading customer records right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for forgetting the WHERE clause on updates and selecting every column, and measure with SQLite before changing anything.

Large scale: ORDER BY and INSERT and UPDATE move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around building reports at this stage.

Before you scale, run a SELECT with the same WHERE clause before any UPDATE or DELETE. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
