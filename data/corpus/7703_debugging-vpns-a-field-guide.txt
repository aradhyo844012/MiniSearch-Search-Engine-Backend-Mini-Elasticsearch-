Debugging VPNs: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around VPNs, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: assuming it makes you anonymous, slow routing through distant servers, and leaking DNS requests.
Step 3: revisit tunneling and encryption, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use WireGuard or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving VPNs in work such as remote work access come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test for DNS leaks after connecting. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
