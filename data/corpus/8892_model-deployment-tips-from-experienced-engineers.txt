Model Deployment Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Model Deployment regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of recommendation endpoints early, before the design hardens.
2. Reach for FastAPI first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as TorchServe.
3. Do not skip serving APIs; everything else builds on it.
4. Write down why you chose Model Deployment so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: training-serving mismatch.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to fraud checks as well as to Model Deployment in general.
