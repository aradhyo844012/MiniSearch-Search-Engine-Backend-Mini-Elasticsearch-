Scaling Considerations for React Native

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how React Native changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding components and getting cross-platform apps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for heavy re-renders and poorly tuned lists, and measure with Expo before changing anything.

Large scale: styling with flexbox and navigation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around rapid prototypes at this stage.

Before you scale, test on real devices for both platforms. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
