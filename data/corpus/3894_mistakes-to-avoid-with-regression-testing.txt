Mistakes to Avoid with Regression Testing

Everyone learns Regression Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow suites that nobody runs. It usually appears when someone skips test suites and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing tests for fixed bugs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting releases. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring flaky failures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on bug reproduction tests to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: add a test for every bug you fix.
