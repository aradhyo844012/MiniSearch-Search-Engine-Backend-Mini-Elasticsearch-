A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Single Sign-On

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Single Sign-On.

Morning: Karthik starts by reviewing a ticket about company intranets. Before touching any code, Karthik re-reads the notes on identity provider to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Karthik pairs with a teammate to explore SAML with Okta. A small test exposes the problem of a single point of failure, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving reducing password fatigue. Karthik sketches a festival wristband that opens every gate on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Karthik writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to plan for identity provider outages.

Days like this are typical. Single Sign-On is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
