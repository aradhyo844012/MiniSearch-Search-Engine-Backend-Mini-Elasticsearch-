Where to Go Next After Learning Security Headers

You have met the basics of Security Headers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Dependency Vulnerabilities, which belongs to the same area as Security Headers (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of Strict-Transport-Security, X-Content-Type-Options, frame protections or referrer policy, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reducing clickjacking with securityheaders.com, and note every problem you meet, starting with setting headers on only some routes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Security Headers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
