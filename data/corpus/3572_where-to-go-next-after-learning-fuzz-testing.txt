Where to Go Next After Learning Fuzz Testing

You have met the basics of Fuzz Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Regression Testing, which belongs to the same area as Fuzz Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of input generation, coverage guidance, crash triage or corpora, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around finding memory bugs with AFL++, and note every problem you meet, starting with running without sanitizers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Fuzz Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
