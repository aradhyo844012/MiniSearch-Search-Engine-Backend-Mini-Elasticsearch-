Transformers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Transformers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Transformers in your own words.
2. Describe self-attention, positional encoding, encoder and decoder blocks, and tokens.
3. Give examples from language translation and text generation.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other. Then draw a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is huge compute requirements a problem? How does positional encoding relate to encoder and decoder blocks? When would you choose Hugging Face or PyTorch?

Study habit: begin with small pretrained models.

When you feel ready, compare Transformers with Reinforcement Learning and write down three differences.
