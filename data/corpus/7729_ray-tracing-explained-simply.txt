Ray Tracing Explained Simply

Here is Ray Tracing without the jargon: it is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.

Think of Ray Tracing as tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: rays, intersections, reflection and refraction, and shadows. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with rays, then add intersections.

People reach for Ray Tracing when they work on movie visuals, architectural visualization, and realistic game lighting.

One practical tip is to start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
