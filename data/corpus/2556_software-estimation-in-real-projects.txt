Software Estimation in Real Projects

Reading about Software Estimation is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: sprint planning. Here, story points drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against treating estimates as promises.

Scenario two: setting deadlines. This time ranges matters most, and tools such as planning poker help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: negotiating scope. The key idea is uncertainty, and the biggest risk is ignoring unknowns.

Across all three scenarios, Software Estimation stays the same idea: predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take. Only the details change.
