Column-Oriented Storage in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Column-Oriented Storage.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Column-Oriented Storage is a way of storing table data by column instead of by row to speed up analytics.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn column chunks, compression, vectorized scans, and predicate pushdown.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a spreadsheet kept as separate lists per column so you can read just one. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through analytics dashboards, large aggregations, and data warehouses.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for slow single-row lookups and many small writes, then try Apache Parquet for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to select only the columns you really need.
