GPUs and Parallelism Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for GPUs and Parallelism. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once.
Key terms: threads and blocks, kernels, memory transfers, and SIMD.
Typical uses: training neural networks, rendering graphics, and scientific simulations.
Risks: slow data transfers, branch-heavy code, and launching tiny workloads.
Tools: CUDA, PyTorch, and OpenCL.

Mental picture: a stadium of workers each doing one small identical task.

Habit to build: minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
