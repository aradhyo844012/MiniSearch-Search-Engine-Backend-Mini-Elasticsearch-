Where to Go Next After Learning A/B Testing

You have met the basics of A/B Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about SQL Window Functions, which belongs to the same area as A/B Testing (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of control and variant, random assignment, metrics or sample size, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around comparing ranking algorithms with Optimizely, and note every problem you meet, starting with stopping tests early.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining A/B Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
