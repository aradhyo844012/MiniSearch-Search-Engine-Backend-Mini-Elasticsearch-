Scaling Considerations for Collision Detection

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Collision Detection changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding bounding boxes and getting platformers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for tunneling through thin objects and checking every pair of objects, and measure with Box2D before changing anything.

Large scale: spatial partitioning and collision response move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around shooters at this stage.

Before you scale, use simple shapes first and refine only when needed. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
