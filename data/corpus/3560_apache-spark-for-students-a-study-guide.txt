Apache Spark for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Apache Spark.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Apache Spark in your own words.
2. Describe DataFrames, transformations and actions, partitions, and lazy execution.
3. Give examples from large ETL jobs and machine learning on big data.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines. Then draw a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is data skew a problem? How does transformations and actions relate to partitions? When would you choose PySpark or Databricks?

Study habit worth keeping: avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.

When you feel ready, compare Apache Spark with Data Lakes and write down three differences.
