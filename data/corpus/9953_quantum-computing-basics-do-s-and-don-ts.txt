Quantum Computing Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Quantum Computing Basics.

Do:
- Learn qubits before anything else.
- Try Qiskit on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
- Test the case of research on cryptography early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: expecting speedups for every problem.
- Ignore this: noise and errors.
- Ignore this: hype about current machines.
- Adopt Quantum Computing Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Quantum Computing Basics, a little patience with superposition pays off more than any clever shortcut.
