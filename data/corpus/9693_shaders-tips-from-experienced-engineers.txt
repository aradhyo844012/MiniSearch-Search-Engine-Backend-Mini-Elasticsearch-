Shaders Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Shaders regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Shaders so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: branches that slow the GPU.
3. Make sure you can start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
4. Test the case of lighting effects early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for GLSL first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Shadertoy.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to water and fire visuals as well as to Shaders in general.
