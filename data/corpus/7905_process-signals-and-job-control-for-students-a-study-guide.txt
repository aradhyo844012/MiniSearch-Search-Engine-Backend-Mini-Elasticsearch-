Process Signals and Job Control for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Process Signals and Job Control.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Process Signals and Job Control in your own words.
2. Describe SIGTERM and SIGKILL, foreground and background jobs, kill and trap, and process groups.
3. Give examples from stopping runaway programs and shutting down gracefully.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Process Signals and Job Control is messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs. Then draw a traffic officer's hand signals for running programs on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is using kill -9 first a problem? How does foreground and background jobs relate to kill and trap? When would you choose kill or jobs and fg?

Study habit: try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.

When you feel ready, compare Process Signals and Job Control with systemd Services and write down three differences.
