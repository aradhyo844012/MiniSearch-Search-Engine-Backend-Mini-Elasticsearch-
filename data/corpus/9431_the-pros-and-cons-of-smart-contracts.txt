The Pros and Cons of Smart Contracts

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Smart Contracts is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports token systems
- Makes automated escrow easier
- Helps with decentralized applications

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: bugs that cannot be patched
- Risk: reentrancy attacks
- Risk: high gas costs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as token systems, and you can manage the risks, Smart Contracts is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
