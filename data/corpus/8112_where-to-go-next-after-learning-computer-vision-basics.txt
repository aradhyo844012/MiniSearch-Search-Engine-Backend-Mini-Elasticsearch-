Where to Go Next After Learning Computer Vision Basics

You have met the basics of Computer Vision Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about 3D Transformations, which belongs to the same area as Computer Vision Basics (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of pixels, filters and edges, feature detection or classification, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around document scanning with OpenCV, and note every problem you meet, starting with lighting changes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Computer Vision Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
