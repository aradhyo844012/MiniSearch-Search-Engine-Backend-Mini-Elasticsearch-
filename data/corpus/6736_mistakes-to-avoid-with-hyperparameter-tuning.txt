Mistakes to Avoid with Hyperparameter Tuning

Everyone learns Hyperparameter Tuning partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: tuning on the test set. It usually appears when someone skips search spaces and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: searching too many options. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of improving model accuracy. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring training cost. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on validation scores to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: try random search before anything fancier.
