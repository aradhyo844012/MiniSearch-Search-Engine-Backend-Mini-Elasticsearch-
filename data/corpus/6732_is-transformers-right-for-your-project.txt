Is Transformers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Transformers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other.

It is a good fit when your work involves language translation or text generation, and when your team understands self-attention.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn positional encoding, or when you cannot manage these risks: huge compute requirements, limited context windows, and biased training data.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether encoder and decoder blocks and tokens are part of the answer. If they are, Transformers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with code assistance and begin with small pretrained models.
