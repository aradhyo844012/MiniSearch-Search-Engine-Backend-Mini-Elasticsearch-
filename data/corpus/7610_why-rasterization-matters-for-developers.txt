Why Rasterization Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Rasterization make better decisions, because Rasterization is converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.

Three situations show why it matters. First, real-time games. Second, user interfaces. Third, CAD previews. In each case, a solid grasp of triangles and pixel coverage separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: aliasing along edges, overdraw, and depth fighting.

You will not need to build everything yourself; OpenGL, Vulkan, and WebGL cover most needs.
