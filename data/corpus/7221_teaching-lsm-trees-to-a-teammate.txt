Teaching LSM Trees to a Teammate

Explaining LSM Trees to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a mail room that sorts incoming letters in batches rather than one by one. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: LSM Trees is storage structures that buffer writes in memory and merge them into sorted files on disk. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving write-heavy databases, naming memtable and sorted string tables as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain compaction in LSM Trees back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, read amplification, and suggest this habit: watch compaction metrics before blaming the application.
