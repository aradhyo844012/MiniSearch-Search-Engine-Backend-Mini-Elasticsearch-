Natural Language Processing Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Natural Language Processing.

Q: Explain Natural Language Processing in simple words.
A: Natural Language Processing refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language. I would add an example involving search engines.

Q: How does it connect to Linear Regression?
A: Both belong to the area of Machine Learning, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: ignoring language variety.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with chatbots, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: tokenization, embeddings, language models, and evaluation metrics.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: spaCy, NLTK, and Hugging Face.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: ignoring language variety; messy text preprocessing; and trusting metrics on tiny samples.
