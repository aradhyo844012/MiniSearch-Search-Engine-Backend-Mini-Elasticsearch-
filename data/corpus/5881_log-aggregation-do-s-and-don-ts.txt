Log Aggregation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Log Aggregation.

Do:
- Learn shippers before anything else.
- Try Elasticsearch on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.
- Test the case of troubleshooting across services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: unbounded storage growth.
- Ignore this: unstructured logs.
- Ignore this: missing timestamps.
- Adopt Log Aggregation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Log Aggregation, a little patience with indexing pays off more than any clever shortcut.
