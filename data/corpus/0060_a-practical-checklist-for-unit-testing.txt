A Practical Checklist for Unit Testing

Use this checklist before you rely on Unit Testing in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Unit Testing should accomplish.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on assertions.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as pytest or unittest.
4. Write at least one automated test that covers test cases.
5. Guard against this risk: slow tests.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Review the core ideas: test cases, assertions, fixtures, and mocking.
8. Guard against this risk: tests that depend on each other.
9. Try the approach on a small case, for example catching regressions.
