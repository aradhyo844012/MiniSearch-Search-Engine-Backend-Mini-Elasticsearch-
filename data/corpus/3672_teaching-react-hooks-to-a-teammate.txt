Teaching React Hooks to a Teammate

Explaining React Hooks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: sticky notes a component keeps between redraws. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: React Hooks is functions that let components use state and lifecycle features without writing classes. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving managing form input, naming useState and useEffect as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain dependency arrays in React Hooks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, missing dependencies in effects, and suggest this habit: enable the hooks lint rules from the start.
