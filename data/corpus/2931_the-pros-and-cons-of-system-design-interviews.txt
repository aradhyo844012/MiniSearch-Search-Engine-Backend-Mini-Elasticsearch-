The Pros and Cons of System Design Interviews

No technology is free of trade-offs, and System Design Interviews is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports preparing for senior roles
- Makes practicing architectural thinking easier
- Helps with explaining real systems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements
- Risk: ignoring trade-offs
- Risk: overcomplicating designs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as preparing for senior roles, and you can manage the risks, System Design Interviews is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
