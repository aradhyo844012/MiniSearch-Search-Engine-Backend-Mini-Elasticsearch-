Teaching Zero Trust Security to a Teammate

Explaining Zero Trust Security to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a building where every door checks your badge, not just the front entrance. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving remote work access, naming identity verification and least privilege as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain micro-segmentation in Zero Trust Security back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, treating it as a product to buy, and suggest this habit: start with strong identity and device checks.
