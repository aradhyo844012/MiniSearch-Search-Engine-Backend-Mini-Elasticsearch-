Where to Go Next After Learning AI Agents

You have met the basics of AI Agents. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Model Deployment, which belongs to the same area as AI Agents (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of planning, tool calls, memory or guardrails, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around workflow automation with LangGraph, and note every problem you meet, starting with runaway loops.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining AI Agents to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
