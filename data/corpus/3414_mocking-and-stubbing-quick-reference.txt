Mocking and Stubbing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Mocking and Stubbing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests.
Key terms: mocks, stubs, spies, and fakes.
Typical uses: isolating units, simulating failures, and avoiding network calls.
Risks: over-mocking, tests that mirror the implementation, and mocks that drift from reality.
Tools: unittest.mock, Mockito, and Sinon.js.

Mental picture: a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger.

Habit to build: prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
