Feature Engineering Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Feature Engineering.

- Definition: creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily.
- Core ideas: scaling; encoding categories; interaction features; missing values.
- Use it for: improving model accuracy; preparing tabular data; handling text and dates.
- Watch out for: data leakage; inconsistent transformations between training and serving; too many redundant features.
- Tools: pandas, scikit-learn pipelines, Featuretools.
- Related topic: Model Evaluation.
- Quick tip: practice scaling until it feels automatic.
