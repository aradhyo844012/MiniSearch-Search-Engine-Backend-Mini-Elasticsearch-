Skills to Build Around Collision Detection

Knowing the definition of Collision Detection is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with bounding boxes and broad and narrow phases, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Box2D on a Collision Detection task, and try at least one alternative such as Unity colliders.

Judgment: learn to decide when Collision Detection fits a problem such as platformers, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example two people checking whether their personal space bubbles overlap.

Habits: use simple shapes first and refine only when needed, and write down what you learn after each project.
