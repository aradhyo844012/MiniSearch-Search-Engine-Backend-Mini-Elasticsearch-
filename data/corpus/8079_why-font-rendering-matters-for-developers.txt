Why Font Rendering Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Font Rendering make better decisions, because Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen.

Three situations show why it matters. First, web pages. Second, operating systems. Third, PDF viewers. In each case, a solid grasp of glyphs and hinting separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: missing font fallbacks, blurry text from scaling, and large font files.

You will not need to build everything yourself; FreeType, Google Fonts, and font inspector tools cover most needs.
