Inside Database Transactions: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Database Transactions, step by step. Put simply, Database Transactions covers groups of operations that succeed or fail together. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

To understand Database Transactions, start with atomicity. Then move on to consistency, and finally look at isolation and durability. Sketching durability on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

First, atomicity sets the stage for everything else. Next, consistency builds on it. After that, isolation comes into play, and finally durability ties the pieces together. Seeing the sequence once, with a tiny example, makes the whole topic easier to remember.
