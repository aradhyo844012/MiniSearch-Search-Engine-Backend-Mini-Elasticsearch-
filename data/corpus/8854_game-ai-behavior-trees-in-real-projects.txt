Game AI Behavior Trees in Real Projects

Reading about Game AI Behavior Trees is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: enemy behavior. Here, selector nodes drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against trees that become tangled.

Scenario two: companion characters. This time sequence nodes matters most, and tools such as Unreal Engine behavior trees help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: simulated crowds. The key idea is conditions, and the biggest risk is expensive conditions checked every frame.

Across all three scenarios, Game AI Behavior Trees stays the same idea: tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does. Only the details change.
