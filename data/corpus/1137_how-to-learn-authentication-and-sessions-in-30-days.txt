How to Learn Authentication and Sessions in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Authentication and Sessions. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Authentication and Sessions covers the process of proving who a user is and remembering them between requests. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

If you are comfortable with Authentication and Sessions, explore REST APIs next. The two often appear in the same project.

Week 1: learn credentials and session cookies, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Flask-Login on a small task such as logging in to web apps.
Week 3: build a mini project around protecting admin pages.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as storing session IDs insecurely, and explain Authentication and Sessions to a friend.

In practice you can start with Flask-Login, then explore Django auth or Passport.js as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Authentication and Sessions.
