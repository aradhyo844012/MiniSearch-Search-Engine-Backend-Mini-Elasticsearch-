The Pros and Cons of Event Sourcing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Event Sourcing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports audit trails
- Makes financial ledgers easier
- Helps with rebuilding read models

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: changing event formats carelessly
- Risk: unbounded replay times
- Risk: using it for simple CRUD apps

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as audit trails, and you can manage the risks, Event Sourcing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, version your events from the first day.
