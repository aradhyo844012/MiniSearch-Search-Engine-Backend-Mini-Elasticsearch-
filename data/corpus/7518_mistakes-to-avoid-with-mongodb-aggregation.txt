Mistakes to Avoid with MongoDB Aggregation

Everyone learns MongoDB Aggregation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: putting $match late. It usually appears when someone skips pipeline stages and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: huge intermediate results. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of summarizing orders. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hitting memory limits. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on indexes to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: filter with $match as early as possible.
