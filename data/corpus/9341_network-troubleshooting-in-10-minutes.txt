Network Troubleshooting in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Network Troubleshooting.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Network Troubleshooting is a step-by-step process for finding out why a network connection fails or is slow.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn protocol layers, ping and traceroute, DNS checks, and packet captures.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a doctor who checks the heart, lungs and blood pressure in order. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through fixing office connectivity, diagnosing slow apps, and verifying firewall changes.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for changing several things at once and blaming the application too early, then try ping for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to work from the bottom layer upward.
