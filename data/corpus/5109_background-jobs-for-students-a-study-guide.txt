Background Jobs for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Background Jobs.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Background Jobs in your own words.
2. Describe queues, workers, retries, and scheduling.
3. Give examples from sending emails and resizing images.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Background Jobs is tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them. Then draw dropping laundry at a service and collecting it later on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is jobs that are not idempotent a problem? How does workers relate to retries? When would you choose Celery or Sidekiq?

Study habit: give every job a timeout and a retry limit.

When you feel ready, compare Background Jobs with Logging Best Practices and write down three differences.
