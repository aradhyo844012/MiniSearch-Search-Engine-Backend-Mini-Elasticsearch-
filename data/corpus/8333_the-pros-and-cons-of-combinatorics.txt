The Pros and Cons of Combinatorics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Combinatorics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports analyzing algorithms
- Makes probability easier
- Helps with estimating password strength

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: double counting
- Risk: mixing order and no-order cases
- Risk: ignoring repetition

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as analyzing algorithms, and you can manage the risks, Combinatorics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
