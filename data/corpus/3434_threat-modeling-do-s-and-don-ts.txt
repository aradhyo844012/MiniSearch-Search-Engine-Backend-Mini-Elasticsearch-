Threat Modeling Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Threat Modeling.

Do:
- Learn assets before anything else.
- Try STRIDE on a small example.
- Make it a habit to draw the data flow diagram first.
- Test the case of designing new features early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: doing it once and never again.
- Ignore this: ignoring data flows.
- Ignore this: focusing only on technical threats.
- Adopt Threat Modeling just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Threat Modeling, a little patience with attackers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
