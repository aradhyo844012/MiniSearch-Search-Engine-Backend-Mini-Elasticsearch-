Views and Materialized Views Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Views and Materialized Views regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit refresh strategies before anything else.
2. Test the case of simplifying repeated queries early, before the design hardens.
3. Write down why you chose Views and Materialized Views so the next person understands the decision.
4. Reach for PostgreSQL first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as MySQL.
5. Do not skip view definitions; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to hiding sensitive columns as well as to Views and Materialized Views in general.
