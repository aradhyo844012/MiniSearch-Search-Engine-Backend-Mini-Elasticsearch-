Mistakes to Avoid with Technical Writing

Everyone learns Technical Writing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming too much knowledge. It usually appears when someone skips audience and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: burying the main point. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of documentation. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: long unbroken paragraphs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on editing to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: state the main point in the first two sentences.
