Scaling Considerations for Consistent Hashing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Consistent Hashing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding hash ring and getting distributed caches right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for too few virtual nodes and uneven load, and measure with Redis Cluster before changing anything.

Large scale: key placement and rebalancing move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around sharded databases at this stage.

Before you scale, use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
