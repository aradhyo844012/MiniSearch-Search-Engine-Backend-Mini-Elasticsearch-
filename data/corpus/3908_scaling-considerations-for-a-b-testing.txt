Scaling Considerations for A/B Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how A/B Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding control and variant and getting testing page designs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for stopping tests early and testing too many variants, and measure with Optimizely before changing anything.

Large scale: metrics and sample size move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around comparing ranking algorithms at this stage.

Before you scale, decide the success metric and sample size before you begin. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
