Everything You Need to Know About Single Sign-On

This page collects the essentials about Single Sign-On in one place. In short, Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications.

Core concepts:
- identity provider
- SAML
- OpenID Connect
- session lifetime

Where it is used:
- company intranets
- reducing password fatigue
- centralized access control

Common problems:
- a single point of failure
- misconfigured redirects
- long sessions on shared devices

Tools to know:
- Okta
- Keycloak
- Azure AD

Remember this above all: a festival wristband that opens every gate is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to plan for identity provider outages.
