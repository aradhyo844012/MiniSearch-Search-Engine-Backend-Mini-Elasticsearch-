A Mini Project Idea Using Process Signals and Job Control

A small project is the fastest way to make Process Signals and Job Control stick. This one takes a weekend and needs only a laptop.

The goal is to build a tiny program or demo that shows Process Signals and Job Control at work on stopping runaway programs. Keep the scope small enough to finish.

Milestones:
1. Read about SIGTERM and SIGKILL and foreground and background jobs.
2. Set up kill and run a hello-world example.
3. Add the core feature.
4. Handle one failure case, such as using kill -9 first.
5. Write a short README explaining what you learned.

Stretch goals: extend the demo to cover shutting down gracefully, or compare jobs and fg with nohup.

Finish by recording what surprised you. Writing it down is how knowledge about Process Signals and Job Control turns into something you can show others.
