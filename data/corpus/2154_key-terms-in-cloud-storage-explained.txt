Key Terms in Cloud Storage Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Cloud Storage much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. The term Cloud Storage refers to storing files and objects on remote servers that scale on demand. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

buckets: a key term in Cloud Storage. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
objects: a key term in Cloud Storage. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
access policies: a key term in Cloud Storage. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
storage classes: a key term in Cloud Storage. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
