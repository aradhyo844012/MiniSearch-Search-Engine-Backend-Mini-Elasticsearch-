Practice Exercises for Transformers

These exercises build real understanding of Transformers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble language translation or text generation.
Exercise 2: Pick Hugging Face and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: huge compute requirements. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Transformers and include the term self-attention.
Exercise 5: Compare PyTorch and TensorFlow for code assistance and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other.
