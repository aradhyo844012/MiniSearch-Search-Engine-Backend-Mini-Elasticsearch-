Is Python Decorators Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Python Decorators, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Python Decorators is functions that wrap other functions to add behavior without changing their code.

It is a good fit when your work involves logging calls or caching results, and when your team understands higher-order functions.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn wrapper functions, or when you cannot manage these risks: losing function metadata, stacking decorators in the wrong order, and hiding side effects.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether functools.wraps and decorator arguments are part of the answer. If they are, Python Decorators is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with checking permissions and always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator.
