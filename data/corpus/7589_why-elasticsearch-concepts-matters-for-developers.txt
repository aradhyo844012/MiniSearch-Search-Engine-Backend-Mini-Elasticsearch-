Why Elasticsearch Concepts Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Elasticsearch Concepts make better decisions, because Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.

Three situations show why it matters. First, site search. Second, log analytics. Third, product catalogs. In each case, a solid grasp of indexes and shards separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: too many shards, mapping explosions, and treating it as a primary database.

Good tools exist, including Elasticsearch, Kibana, and OpenSearch, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
