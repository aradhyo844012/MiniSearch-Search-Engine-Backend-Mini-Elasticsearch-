A Practical Checklist for HTML Semantics

Use this checklist before you rely on HTML Semantics in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: missing labels on form inputs.
2. Document your decisions in the project README.
3. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on landmarks.
4. Guard against this risk: using div for everything.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example accessible pages.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what HTML Semantics should accomplish.
7. Review how HTML Semantics interacts with Single Page Applications.
