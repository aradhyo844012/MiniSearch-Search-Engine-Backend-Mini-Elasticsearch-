How Tokenization Connects to Autocomplete

Tokenization and Autocomplete are closely connected parts of Search and Retrieval. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving indexing documents may also involve search boxes. In such a project, lowercasing and prefixes often influence each other.

Start with Tokenization: the term refers to splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare. Then look at Autocomplete, which refers to suggesting likely completions while a person types a query.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as preparing text for models, and explain how ranking suggestions would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Tokenization, watch for this: dropping useful numbers. For Autocomplete, watch for this: offensive or irrelevant completions. Users judge a search engine by whether the right result appears near the top.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Python re module or tries.
