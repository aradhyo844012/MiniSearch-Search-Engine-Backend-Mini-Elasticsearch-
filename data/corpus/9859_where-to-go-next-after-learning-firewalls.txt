Where to Go Next After Learning Firewalls

You have met the basics of Firewalls. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about BGP and Internet Routing, which belongs to the same area as Firewalls (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of rules, stateful inspection, default deny or zones, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around segmenting networks with ufw, and note every problem you meet, starting with rules in the wrong order.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Firewalls to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
