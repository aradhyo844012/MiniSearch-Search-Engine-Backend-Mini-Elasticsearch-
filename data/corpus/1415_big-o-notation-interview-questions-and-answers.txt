Big O Notation Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Big O Notation.

Q: Explain Big O Notation in simple words.
A: Big O Notation refers to a way of describing how running time or memory grows as the input size grows. I would add an example involving comparing algorithms.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Python timeit, big-O cheat sheets, and profilers.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with predicting scaling problems, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: ignoring constant factors entirely.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as upper bounds, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: upper bounds, constant, linear and logarithmic growth, worst case, and amortized cost.
