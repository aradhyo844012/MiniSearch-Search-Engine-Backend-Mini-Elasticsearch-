Raft Consensus Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Raft Consensus regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for etcd first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Consul.
2. Write down why you chose Raft Consensus so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of configuration stores early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit log replication before anything else.
5. Do not skip leader election; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to distributed locks as well.
