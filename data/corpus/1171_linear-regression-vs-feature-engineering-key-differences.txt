Linear Regression vs Feature Engineering: Key Differences

Linear Regression and Feature Engineering both belong to the area of Machine Learning, but they solve different problems. This article compares them side by side so you can choose with confidence.

The term Linear Regression refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. Its core ideas are coefficients, residuals, and least squares.

The term Feature Engineering refers to creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily. Its core ideas are scaling, encoding categories, and interaction features.

The main difference is focus. Linear Regression puts the emphasis on coefficients and residuals, while Feature Engineering puts the emphasis on scaling and encoding categories.

Reach for Linear Regression when your task involves predicting prices or estimating demand. Reach for Feature Engineering when your task involves improving model accuracy or preparing tabular data.

Both have risks. With Linear Regression, watch for this: extrapolating far beyond the data. With Feature Engineering, watch for this: data leakage.

In practice you do not always have to choose. Many projects use Linear Regression alongside Feature Engineering, with tools such as scikit-learn and pandas. Reproducible experiments make it far easier to trust and improve a model.
