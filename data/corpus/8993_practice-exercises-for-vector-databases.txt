Practice Exercises for Vector Databases

These exercises build real understanding of Vector Databases. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Vector Databases and include the term embeddings.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mixing embedding models. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Pick FAISS and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare pgvector and Pinecone for retrieval for AI assistants and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble semantic search or recommendations.
