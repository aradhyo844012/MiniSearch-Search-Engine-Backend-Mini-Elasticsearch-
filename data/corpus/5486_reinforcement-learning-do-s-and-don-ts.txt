Reinforcement Learning Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Reinforcement Learning.

Do:
- Learn agent before anything else.
- Try Gymnasium on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with simple environments.
- Test the case of game playing early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: badly designed rewards.
- Ignore this: slow training.
- Ignore this: unstable results across runs.
- Adopt Reinforcement Learning just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Reinforcement Learning, a little patience with environment pays off more than any clever shortcut.
