Is Views and Materialized Views Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Views and Materialized Views, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results.

It is a good fit when your work involves simplifying repeated queries or hiding sensitive columns, and when your team understands view definitions.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn refresh strategies, or when you cannot manage these risks: stale materialized data, views stacked on views, and surprising performance costs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether permissions and dependencies are part of the answer. If they are, Views and Materialized Views is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with speeding up reports and document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
