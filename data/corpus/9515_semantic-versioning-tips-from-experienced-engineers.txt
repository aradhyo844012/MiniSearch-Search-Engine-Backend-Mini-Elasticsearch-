Semantic Versioning Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Semantic Versioning regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip major, minor and patch numbers; everything else builds on it.
2. Be careful with this one: breaking changes in minor releases.
3. Write down why you chose Semantic Versioning so the next person understands the decision.
4. Test the case of publishing libraries early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for semver.org first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as npm semver.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to declaring dependencies as well as to Semantic Versioning in general.
