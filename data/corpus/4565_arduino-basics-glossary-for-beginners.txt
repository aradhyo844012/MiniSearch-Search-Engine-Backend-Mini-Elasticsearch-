Arduino Basics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Arduino Basics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Arduino Basics: a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices.
sketches: a core term, most visible in reading sensors.
digital and analog pins: a core term that beginners often confuse with setup and loop functions.
setup and loop functions: a core term that connects to the serial monitor.
the serial monitor: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Arduino IDE: a tool commonly used for driving motors and LEDs.

Come back to this Arduino Basics list whenever documentation for PlatformIO uses a word you do not recognize.
