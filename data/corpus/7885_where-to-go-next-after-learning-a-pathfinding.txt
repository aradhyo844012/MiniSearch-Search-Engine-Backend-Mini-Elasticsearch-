Where to Go Next After Learning A* Pathfinding

You have met the basics of A* Pathfinding. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Game AI Behavior Trees, which belongs to the same area as A* Pathfinding (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of open and closed sets, cost so far, heuristics or grids and graphs, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around robot navigation with Unity NavMesh, and note every problem you meet, starting with overestimating the heuristic.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining A* Pathfinding to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
