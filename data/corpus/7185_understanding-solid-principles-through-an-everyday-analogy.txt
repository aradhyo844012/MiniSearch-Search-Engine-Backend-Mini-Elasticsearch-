Understanding SOLID Principles Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture house rules that keep a shared kitchen tidy.

In that picture, single responsibility is the first rule you follow, the open-closed principle is the next step, and Liskov substitution and dependency inversion keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real SOLID Principles is five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change, and it involves details such as applying them rigidly to tiny scripts that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for UML diagrams to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain structuring classes to a friend.
