Teaching MLOps Pipelines to a Teammate

Explaining MLOps Pipelines to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a factory line that turns data into a tested, packaged model. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: MLOps Pipelines is automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving repeatable model releases, naming data versioning and training pipelines as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain the model registry in MLOps Pipelines back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, untracked data changes, and suggest this habit: version both the code and the data.
