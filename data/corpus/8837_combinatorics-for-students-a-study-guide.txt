Combinatorics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Combinatorics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Combinatorics in your own words.
2. Describe permutations, combinations, the pigeonhole principle, and inclusion-exclusion.
3. Give examples from analyzing algorithms and probability.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Combinatorics is the art of counting arrangements and selections. Then draw counting the ways to seat guests at a table on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is double counting a problem? How does combinations relate to the pigeonhole principle? When would you choose Python itertools or math.comb?

Study habit worth keeping: test counting formulas on tiny cases first.

When you feel ready, compare Combinatorics with Discrete Mathematics and write down three differences.
