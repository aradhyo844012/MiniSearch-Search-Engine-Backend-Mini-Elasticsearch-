A Practical Checklist for Single Page Applications

Use this checklist before you rely on Single Page Applications in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers client-side routing.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example dashboards.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Single Page Applications should accomplish.
4. Measure results before and after any change.
5. Guard against this risk: slow first load.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on state management.
