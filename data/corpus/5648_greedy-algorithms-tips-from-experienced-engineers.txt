Greedy Algorithms Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Greedy Algorithms regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip greedy choice property; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for pen and paper proofs first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Python sorted.
3. Test the case of interval scheduling early, before the design hardens.
4. Write down why you chose Greedy Algorithms so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: assuming greedy always works.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to Huffman coding as well.
