Where to Go Next After Learning Developer Portfolios

You have met the basics of Developer Portfolios. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Competitive Programming, which belongs to the same area as Developer Portfolios (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of projects, README quality, live demos or clear descriptions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around showing skills to recruiters with GitHub Pages, and note every problem you meet, starting with too many unfinished projects.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Developer Portfolios to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
