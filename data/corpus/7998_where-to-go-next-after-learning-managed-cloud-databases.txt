Where to Go Next After Learning Managed Cloud Databases

You have met the basics of Managed Cloud Databases. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Regions and Availability Zones, which belongs to the same area as Managed Cloud Databases (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of automated backups, read replicas, maintenance windows or scaling options, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reducing database administration with Amazon RDS, and note every problem you meet, starting with surprise costs.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Managed Cloud Databases to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
