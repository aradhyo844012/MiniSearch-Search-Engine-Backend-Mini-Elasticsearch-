How to Learn REST APIs in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with REST APIs. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term REST APIs refers to an approach to designing web APIs around resources, URLs and standard HTTP methods. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn resources and HTTP verbs, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Flask on a small task such as mobile app backends.
Week 3: build a mini project around integrating services.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as inconsistent naming, and explain REST APIs to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include Flask, FastAPI, and Postman.

Good APIs are boring, predictable and well documented. A backend turns requests from many clients into reliable, consistent responses.

In short, careful study of REST APIs pays off. Spend time on resources and HTTP verbs, and the rest will follow.
