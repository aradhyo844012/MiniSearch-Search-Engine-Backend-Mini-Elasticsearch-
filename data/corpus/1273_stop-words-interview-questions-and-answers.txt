Stop Words Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Stop Words.

Q: Explain Stop Words in simple words.
A: Stop Words refers to very common words such as the and of that are often ignored by search systems. I would add an example involving shrinking indexes.

Q: How does it connect to Tokenization?
A: Both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: NLTK stopwords, scikit-learn, and custom word lists.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: removing words that matter in phrases.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with speeding up queries, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: frequency lists, index size, query relevance, and language-specific lists.
