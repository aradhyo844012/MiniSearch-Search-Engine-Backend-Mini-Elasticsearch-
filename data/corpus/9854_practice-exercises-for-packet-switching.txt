Practice Exercises for Packet Switching

These exercises build real understanding of Packet Switching. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: packet loss. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick Wireshark and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Packet Switching and include the term packets.
Exercise 4: Compare tcpdump and iperf for file transfers and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how routing and reassembly differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Packet Switching is a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network.
