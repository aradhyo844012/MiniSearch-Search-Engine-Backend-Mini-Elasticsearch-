Practice Exercises for Competitive Programming

These exercises build real understanding of Competitive Programming. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how contests and upsolving differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Compare LeetCode and AtCoder for practicing under pressure and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: memorizing solutions without understanding. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Pick Codeforces and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble interview preparation or learning algorithms.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Competitive Programming is solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills.
