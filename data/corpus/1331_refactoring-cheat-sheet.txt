Refactoring Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Refactoring.

- Definition: improving the structure of existing code without changing its behavior.
- Core ideas: small steps; tests as a safety net; extracting functions; renaming.
- Use it for: reducing duplication; preparing for new features; simplifying complex code.
- Watch out for: refactoring without tests; mixing refactoring with new features; rewriting instead of improving.
- Tools: IDE refactoring tools, pytest, linters.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for tests as a safety net before building anything large.
- Related topic: Agile and Scrum.
