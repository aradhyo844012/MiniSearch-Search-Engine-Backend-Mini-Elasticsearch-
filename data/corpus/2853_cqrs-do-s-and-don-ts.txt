CQRS Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with CQRS.

Do:
- Learn commands before anything else.
- Try Axon Framework on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
- Test the case of high-read dashboards early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: adding it to simple apps.
- Ignore this: read models drifting out of sync.
- Ignore this: duplicated logic.
- Adopt CQRS just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with CQRS, a little patience with queries pays off more than any clever shortcut.
