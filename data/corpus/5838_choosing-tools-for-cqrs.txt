Choosing Tools for CQRS

There is no single best tool for CQRS. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Axon Framework is a common first choice, especially for high-read dashboards. MediatR suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Apache Kafka is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle complex domains well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: adding it to simple apps.

As a rule of thumb, start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge, no matter which tool you use.
