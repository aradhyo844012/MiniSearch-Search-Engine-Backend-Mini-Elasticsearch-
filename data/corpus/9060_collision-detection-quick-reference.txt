Collision Detection Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Collision Detection. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap.
Key terms: bounding boxes, broad and narrow phases, spatial partitioning, and collision response.
Typical uses: platformers, shooters, and physics puzzles.
Risks: tunneling through thin objects, checking every pair of objects, and inconsistent hitboxes.
Tools: Box2D, Unity colliders, and quadtrees.

Mental picture: two people checking whether their personal space bubbles overlap.

Habit to build: use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
