Debugging Regular Expressions: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Regular Expressions, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: catastrophic backtracking, patterns that are too greedy, and using them to parse HTML.
Step 3: revisit character classes and quantifiers, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use the Python re module or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Regular Expressions in work such as validating input come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, build patterns in small steps and test each one. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
