Load Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Load Testing.

Do:
- Learn virtual users before anything else.
- Try k6 on a small example.
- Make it a habit to report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
- Test the case of capacity planning early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: testing unrealistic traffic.
- Ignore this: ignoring warm-up.
- Ignore this: using average latency only.
- Adopt Load Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Load Testing, a little patience with throughput pays off more than any clever shortcut.
