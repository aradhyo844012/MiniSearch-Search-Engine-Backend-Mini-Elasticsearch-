Stream Processing Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Stream Processing.

- Definition: handling data continuously as it arrives rather than in large batches.
- Core ideas: events; windows; offsets; state.
- Use it for: live dashboards; fraud detection; real-time alerts.
- Watch out for: late events; losing state after crashes; duplicate processing.
- Tools: Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, Spark Structured Streaming.
- Quick tip: practice events until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for windows before building anything large.
