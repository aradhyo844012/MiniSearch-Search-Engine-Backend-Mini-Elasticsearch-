The Pros and Cons of Boolean Logic and Gates

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Boolean Logic and Gates is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports writing conditions
- Makes designing circuits easier
- Helps with simplifying expressions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: operator precedence mistakes
- Risk: forgetting short-circuit behavior
- Risk: negating complex conditions incorrectly

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as writing conditions, and you can manage the risks, Boolean Logic and Gates is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
