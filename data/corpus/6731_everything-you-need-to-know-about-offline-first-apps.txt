Everything You Need to Know About Offline-First Apps

This page collects the essentials about Offline-First Apps in one place. In short, Offline-First Apps is apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns.

Core concepts:
- local storage
- sync queues
- conflict resolution
- optimistic updates

Where it is used:
- field service tools
- note-taking apps
- apps for low-connectivity regions

Common problems:
- unresolved sync conflicts
- unbounded local data
- poor feedback about pending changes

Tools to know:
- SQLite
- PouchDB
- Room

Remember this above all: writing in a notebook and mailing the pages later is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to show users which changes have not synced yet.
