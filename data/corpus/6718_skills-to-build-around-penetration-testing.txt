Skills to Build Around Penetration Testing

Knowing the definition of Penetration Testing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with scoping and reconnaissance, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with OWASP ZAP on a Penetration Testing task, and try at least one alternative such as Burp Suite.

Judgment: learn to decide when Penetration Testing fits a problem such as pre-launch security checks, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example hiring a locksmith to test your doors.

Habits: get written authorization before any test, and write down what you learn after each project.
