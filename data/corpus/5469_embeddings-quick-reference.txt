Embeddings Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Embeddings. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.
Key terms: vectors, cosine similarity, dimensionality, and nearest neighbor search.
Typical uses: semantic search, recommendations, and clustering text.
Risks: mixing models between indexing and querying, ignoring normalization, and stale embeddings after data changes.
Tools: sentence-transformers, FAISS, and Word2Vec.

Mental picture: coordinates on a map where similar places are close together.

Habit to build: use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
