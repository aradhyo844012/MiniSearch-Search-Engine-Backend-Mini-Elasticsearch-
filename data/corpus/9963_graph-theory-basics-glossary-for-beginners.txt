Graph Theory Basics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Graph Theory Basics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Graph Theory Basics: the study of nodes and the connections between them.
vertices and edges: a core term, most visible in social networks.
degree: a core term that beginners often confuse with paths and cycles.
paths and cycles: a core term that connects to connectivity.
connectivity: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
NetworkX: a tool commonly used for maps.

Come back to this Graph Theory Basics list whenever documentation for Graphviz uses a word you do not recognize.
