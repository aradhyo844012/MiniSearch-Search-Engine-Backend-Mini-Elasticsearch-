Where to Go Next After Learning Two Pointers and Sliding Window

You have met the basics of Two Pointers and Sliding Window. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Fenwick Trees, which belongs to the same area as Two Pointers and Sliding Window (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of left and right pointers, window boundaries, invariants or shrinking and expanding, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around longest substring problems with Python lists, and note every problem you meet, starting with moving the wrong pointer.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Two Pointers and Sliding Window to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
