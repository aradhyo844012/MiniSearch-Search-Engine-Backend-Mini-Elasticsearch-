Is Jupyter Notebooks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Jupyter Notebooks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.

It is a good fit when your work involves exploring data or teaching and demos, and when your team understands cells.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn kernels, or when you cannot manage these risks: running cells out of order, hidden state, and committing outputs that contain secrets.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether markdown and outputs are part of the answer. If they are, Jupyter Notebooks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with sharing analysis and restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
