Regions and Availability Zones Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Regions and Availability Zones.

Do:
- Learn regions before anything else.
- Try AWS Regions on a small example.
- Make it a habit to spread critical services across at least two zones.
- Test the case of placing services near users early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: putting everything in one zone.
- Ignore this: cross-region transfer fees.
- Ignore this: forgetting data residency rules.
- Adopt Regions and Availability Zones just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Regions and Availability Zones, a little patience with availability zones pays off more than any clever shortcut.
