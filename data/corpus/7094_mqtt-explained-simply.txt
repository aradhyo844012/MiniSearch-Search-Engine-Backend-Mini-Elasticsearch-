MQTT Explained Simply

If MQTT sounds intimidating, relax. It is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of MQTT as a radio station with channels that devices can tune into. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: publish and subscribe, topics, brokers, and quality of service levels. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with publish and subscribe, then add topics.

You will meet MQTT in work such as sensor networks, smart home devices, and telemetry.

One practical tip is to design topic names like a clear folder structure.
