Where to Go Next After Learning Finite Automata

You have met the basics of Finite Automata. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Information Theory and Entropy, which belongs to the same area as Finite Automata (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of states, transitions, accepting states or regular languages, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around pattern matching with JFLAP, and note every problem you meet, starting with missing transitions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Finite Automata to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
