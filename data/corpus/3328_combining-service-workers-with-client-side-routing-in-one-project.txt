Combining Service Workers with Client-Side Routing in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Service Workers and Client-Side Routing can work together in a single project.

Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests. Client-Side Routing is changing the visible page in the browser without a full page reload.

Imagine a project that involves offline support and single-page apps. Service Workers brings registration, while Client-Side Routing brings history API.

A sensible plan is to start with Workbox for the Service Workers part and React Router for the Client-Side Routing part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: bugs surviving across deployments on one side, and unhandled 404 pages on the other.

Picture the whole as a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager, working alongside swapping the picture in a frame without replacing the frame.

Two habits help: first, version your caches so old files can be removed; second, test refreshing the page on every route.
