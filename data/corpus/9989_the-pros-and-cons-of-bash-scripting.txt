The Pros and Cons of Bash Scripting

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Bash Scripting is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports automating deployments
- Makes processing text files easier
- Helps with setting up developer machines

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: unquoted variables
- Risk: ignoring errors in pipelines
- Risk: scripts that are hard to read

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as automating deployments, and you can manage the risks, Bash Scripting is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start scripts with set -euo pipefail and quote every variable.
