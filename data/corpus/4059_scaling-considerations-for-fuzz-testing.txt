Scaling Considerations for Fuzz Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Fuzz Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding input generation and getting testing parsers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for running without sanitizers and crashes that cannot be reproduced, and measure with AFL++ before changing anything.

Large scale: crash triage and corpora move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around finding memory bugs at this stage.

Before you scale, keep every crashing input as a regression test. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
