DOM Manipulation Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about DOM Manipulation.

Q: Explain DOM Manipulation in simple words.
A: DOM Manipulation refers to reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript. I would add an example involving updating search results.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: inserting untrusted text as HTML.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: inserting untrusted text as HTML; querying the DOM inside tight loops; and forgetting to remove event listeners.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: querySelector, browser console, and MDN docs.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with handling form input, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to Responsive Design?
A: Both belong to the area of Web Development, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: selectors, events, nodes, and updates.
