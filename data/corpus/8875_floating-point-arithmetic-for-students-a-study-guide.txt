Floating-Point Arithmetic for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Floating-Point Arithmetic.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Floating-Point Arithmetic in your own words.
2. Describe mantissa and exponent, rounding, precision, and special values.
3. Give examples from scientific computing and graphics.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Floating-Point Arithmetic is the way computers approximate real numbers with a fixed number of bits. Then draw writing numbers in scientific notation with a limited number of digits on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is comparing floats with equality a problem? How does rounding relate to precision? When would you choose Python decimal or NumPy?

Study habit: compare floats with a tolerance and use decimals for money.

When you feel ready, compare Floating-Point Arithmetic with Discrete Mathematics and write down three differences.
