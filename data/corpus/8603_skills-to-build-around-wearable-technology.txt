Skills to Build Around Wearable Technology

Knowing the definition of Wearable Technology is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with sensors and low-power design, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Wear OS on a Wearable Technology task, and try at least one alternative such as watchOS.

Judgment: learn to decide when Wearable Technology fits a problem such as fitness tracking, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist.

Habits: design interactions that take only a few seconds, and write down what you learn after each project.
