Where to Go Next After Learning Responsible AI and Bias

You have met the basics of Responsible AI and Bias. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Model Deployment, which belongs to the same area as Responsible AI and Bias (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of bias in data, fairness metrics, transparency or human oversight, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around lending models with Fairlearn, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming data is neutral.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Responsible AI and Bias to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
