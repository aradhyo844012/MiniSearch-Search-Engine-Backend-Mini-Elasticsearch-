Configuration Management Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Configuration Management regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for dotenv first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Pydantic settings.
2. Do not skip environment variables; everything else builds on it.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit config files before anything else.
4. Be careful with this one: committing secrets.
5. Write down why you chose Configuration Management so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to toggling features as well.
