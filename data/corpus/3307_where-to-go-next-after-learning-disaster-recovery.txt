Where to Go Next After Learning Disaster Recovery

You have met the basics of Disaster Recovery. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Blue-Green Deployment, which belongs to the same area as Disaster Recovery (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of recovery time objective, recovery point objective, failover regions or runbooks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around meeting compliance needs with AWS Backup, and note every problem you meet, starting with untested plans.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Disaster Recovery to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
