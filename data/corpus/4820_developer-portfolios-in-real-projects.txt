Developer Portfolios in Real Projects

Reading about Developer Portfolios is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: applying for internships. Here, projects drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against too many unfinished projects.

Scenario two: showing skills to recruiters. This time README quality matters most, and tools such as GitHub Pages help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: tracking personal growth. The key idea is live demos, and the biggest risk is no explanation of decisions.

Across all three scenarios, Developer Portfolios stays the same idea: a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build. Only the details change.
