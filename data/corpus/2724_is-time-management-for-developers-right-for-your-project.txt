Is Time Management for Developers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Time Management for Developers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.

It is a good fit when your work involves managing coursework and projects or meeting deadlines, and when your team understands prioritization.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn time blocking, or when you cannot manage these risks: multitasking, underestimating tasks, and working without breaks.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether breaking down tasks and avoiding context switches are part of the answer. If they are, Time Management for Developers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with reducing stress and break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
