Mistakes to Avoid with Color Spaces

Everyone learns Color Spaces partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring gamma. It usually appears when someone skips RGB and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: mixing color spaces. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of web design. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor contrast choices. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on gamma to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: work in one color space and convert only at the end.
