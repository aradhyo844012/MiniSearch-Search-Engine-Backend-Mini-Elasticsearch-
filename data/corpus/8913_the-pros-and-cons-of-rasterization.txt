The Pros and Cons of Rasterization

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Rasterization is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports real-time games
- Makes user interfaces easier
- Helps with CAD previews

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: aliasing along edges
- Risk: overdraw
- Risk: depth fighting

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as real-time games, and you can manage the risks, Rasterization is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.
