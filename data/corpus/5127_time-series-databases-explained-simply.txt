Time-Series Databases Explained Simply

Here is Time-Series Databases without the jargon: it is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.

Think of Time-Series Databases as a long logbook where every line starts with the time. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: timestamps, retention policies, downsampling, and tags and fields. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with timestamps, then add retention policies.

You will meet Time-Series Databases in work such as server metrics, sensor readings, and financial tick data.

One practical tip is to include a time range in every query.

Once Time-Series Databases feels comfortable, read about Write-Ahead Logging, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
