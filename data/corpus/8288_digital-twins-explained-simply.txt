Digital Twins Explained Simply

Here is Digital Twins without the jargon: it is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.

Think of Digital Twins as a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sensor data, simulation models, synchronization, and analytics. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sensor data, then add simulation models.

You will meet Digital Twins in work such as factory monitoring, building management, and predictive maintenance.

One practical tip is to define the question the twin must answer first.

Once Digital Twins feels comfortable, read about Blockchain Fundamentals, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
