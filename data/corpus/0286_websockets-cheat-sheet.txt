WebSockets Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of WebSockets.

- Definition: a protocol that keeps a two-way connection open between a browser and a server.
- Core ideas: persistent connections; messages; heartbeats; reconnection.
- Use it for: chat applications; live dashboards; collaborative editing.
- Watch out for: forgetting reconnection logic; unbounded message queues; proxies closing idle connections.
- Tools: Socket.IO, ws library, Python websockets.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for messages before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice persistent connections until it feels automatic.
