A* Pathfinding Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with A* Pathfinding regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip open and closed sets; everything else builds on it.
2. Make sure you can choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
3. Be careful with this one: overestimating the heuristic.
4. Reach for Unity NavMesh first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Python heapq.
5. Test the case of game characters early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to robot navigation as well as to A* Pathfinding in general.
