How Natural Language Processing Connects to Gradient Descent

Natural Language Processing and Gradient Descent are closely connected parts of Machine Learning. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving search engines may also involve training neural networks. In such a project, tokenization and learning rate often influence each other.

Start with Natural Language Processing: the term refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language. Then look at Gradient Descent, which refers to an optimization method that improves a model by repeatedly stepping in the direction that reduces error.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as chatbots, and explain how gradients would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Natural Language Processing, watch for this: messy text preprocessing. For Gradient Descent, watch for this: getting stuck on plateaus. Reproducible experiments make it far easier to trust and improve a model.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using spaCy or PyTorch.
