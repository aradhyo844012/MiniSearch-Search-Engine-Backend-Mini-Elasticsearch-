Support Vector Machines in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Support Vector Machines.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Support Vector Machines is models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn margin, support vectors, kernels, and regularization.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine drawing the widest road between two neighborhoods. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through text classification, small high-dimensional datasets, and image recognition baselines.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for slow training on large data and poor kernel choices, then try scikit-learn for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to scale features and try a linear kernel first.
