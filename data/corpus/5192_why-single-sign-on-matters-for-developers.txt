Why Single Sign-On Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Single Sign-On make better decisions, because Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications.

Three situations show why it matters. First, company intranets. Second, reducing password fatigue. Third, centralized access control. In each case, a solid grasp of identity provider and SAML separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: a single point of failure, misconfigured redirects, and long sessions on shared devices.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Okta, Keycloak, and Azure AD cover most needs.
