Transformers Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Transformers. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other.
Key terms: self-attention, positional encoding, encoder and decoder blocks, and tokens.
Typical uses: language translation, text generation, and code assistance.
Risks: huge compute requirements, limited context windows, and biased training data.
Tools: Hugging Face, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.

Mental picture: a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word.

Habit to build: begin with small pretrained models.
