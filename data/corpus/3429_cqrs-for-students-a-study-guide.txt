CQRS for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover CQRS.

Learning goals:
1. Explain CQRS in your own words.
2. Describe commands, queries, read models, and eventual consistency.
3. Give examples from high-read dashboards and complex domains.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it. Then draw a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is adding it to simple apps a problem? How does queries relate to read models? When would you choose Axon Framework or MediatR?

Study habit worth keeping: start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.

When you feel ready, compare CQRS with Vector Clocks and write down three differences.
