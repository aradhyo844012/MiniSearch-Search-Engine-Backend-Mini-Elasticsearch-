Where to Go Next After Learning Logging Best Practices

You have met the basics of Logging Best Practices. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about API Versioning, which belongs to the same area as Logging Best Practices (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of log levels, structured logs, correlation IDs or log rotation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around auditing actions with the Python logging module, and note every problem you meet, starting with logging sensitive data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Logging Best Practices to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
