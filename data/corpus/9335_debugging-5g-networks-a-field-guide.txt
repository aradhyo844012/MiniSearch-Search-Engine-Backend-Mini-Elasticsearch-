Debugging 5G Networks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around 5G Networks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: limited coverage on the fastest bands, battery drain, and marketing exaggeration.
Step 3: revisit spectrum bands and latency, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use speed test tools or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving 5G Networks in work such as mobile broadband come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
