Scaling Considerations for AWS IAM Roles and Policies

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how AWS IAM Roles and Policies changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding users and roles and getting securing deployments right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for wildcard permissions and long-lived access keys, and measure with IAM Access Analyzer before changing anything.

Large scale: least privilege and temporary credentials move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around granting services access at this stage.

Before you scale, grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
