Scaling Considerations for Digital Audio

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Digital Audio changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding sampling rate and getting music production right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for clipping from too high a level and aliasing at low sample rates, and measure with Audacity before changing anything.

Large scale: waveforms and the Nyquist limit move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around voice calls at this stage.

Before you scale, keep recordings below the clipping level. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
