Choosing Tools for Managed Kubernetes Services

There is no single best tool for Managed Kubernetes Services. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Amazon EKS is a common first choice, especially for running containerized apps. Google GKE suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Azure AKS is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Managed Kubernetes Services by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle scaling services well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Managed Kubernetes Services, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: neglecting version upgrades.

As a rule of thumb, plan regular cluster upgrades, no matter which tool you use.
