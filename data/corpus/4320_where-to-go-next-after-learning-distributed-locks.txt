Where to Go Next After Learning Distributed Locks

You have met the basics of Distributed Locks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Idempotency, which belongs to the same area as Distributed Locks (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of lease times, fencing tokens, lock owners or timeouts, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around protecting shared resources with Redis, and note every problem you meet, starting with locks that never expire.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Distributed Locks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
