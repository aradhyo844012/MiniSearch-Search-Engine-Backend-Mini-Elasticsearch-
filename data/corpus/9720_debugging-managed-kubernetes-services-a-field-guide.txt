Debugging Managed Kubernetes Services: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Managed Kubernetes Services, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: neglecting version upgrades, oversized node pools, and costly idle clusters.
Step 3: revisit the control plane and node pools, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Amazon EKS or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Managed Kubernetes Services in work such as running containerized apps come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, plan regular cluster upgrades. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
