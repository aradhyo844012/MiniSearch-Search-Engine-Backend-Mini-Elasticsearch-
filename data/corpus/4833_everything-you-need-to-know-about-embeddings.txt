Everything You Need to Know About Embeddings

This page collects the essentials about Embeddings in one place. In short, Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.

Core concepts:
- vectors
- cosine similarity
- dimensionality
- nearest neighbor search

Where it is used:
- semantic search
- recommendations
- clustering text

Common problems:
- mixing models between indexing and querying
- ignoring normalization
- stale embeddings after data changes

Tools to know:
- sentence-transformers
- FAISS
- Word2Vec

Remember this above all: coordinates on a map where similar places are close together is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
