SQL Window Functions Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with SQL Window Functions.

Do:
- Learn the OVER clause before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with ROW_NUMBER and LAG on a small table.
- Test the case of running totals early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: forgetting ORDER BY inside the window.
- Ignore this: confusing them with GROUP BY.
- Ignore this: slow sorts on huge partitions.
- Adopt SQL Window Functions just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with SQL Window Functions, a little patience with partitions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
