Database Normalization Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Database Normalization.

- Definition: the practice of organizing tables to reduce duplicated data and update anomalies.
- Core ideas: first normal form; second normal form; third normal form; functional dependencies.
- Use it for: designing clean schemas; avoiding update anomalies; keeping data consistent.
- Watch out for: over-normalizing reporting tables; ignoring query patterns; duplicating data by accident.
- Tools: ER diagrams, dbdiagram.io, MySQL Workbench.
- Quick tip: practice first normal form until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for second normal form before building anything large.
