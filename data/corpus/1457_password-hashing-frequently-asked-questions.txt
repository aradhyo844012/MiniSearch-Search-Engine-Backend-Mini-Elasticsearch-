Password Hashing: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Password Hashing?
A: The term Password Hashing refers to storing passwords as one-way hashes so the originals are never kept.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include bcrypt, Argon2, and scrypt.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: using fast hashes like plain SHA-256; reusing salts; and storing passwords in plain text.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with salts and slow hash functions, then move on to work factors and verification.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: How does it relate to Cross-Site Scripting?
A: Password Hashing and Cross-Site Scripting often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Security.
