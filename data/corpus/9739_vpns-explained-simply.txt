VPNs Explained Simply

Here is VPNs without the jargon: it is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.

Think of VPNs as a private tunnel under a public road. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: tunneling, encryption, authentication, and split tunneling. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with tunneling, then add encryption.

People reach for VPNs when they work on remote work access, linking offices, and protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi.

One practical tip is to test for DNS leaks after connecting.

Once VPNs feels comfortable, read about BGP and Internet Routing, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
