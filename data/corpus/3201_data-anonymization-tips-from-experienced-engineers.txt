Data Anonymization Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Data Anonymization regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can test whether combining fields could identify a person.
2. Do not skip masking; everything else builds on it.
3. Reach for ARX first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Faker.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit generalization before anything else.
5. Test the case of sharing datasets with researchers early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to testing with production-like data as well.
