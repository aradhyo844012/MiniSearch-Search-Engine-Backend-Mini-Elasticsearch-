How Unit Testing Connects to Agile and Scrum

Unit Testing and Agile and Scrum are closely connected parts of Software Engineering. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving catching regressions may also involve student team projects. In such a project, test cases and sprints often influence each other.

Start with Unit Testing: the term refers to writing small automated tests that check one piece of code at a time. Then look at Agile and Scrum, which refers to iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as documenting behavior, and explain how backlogs would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Unit Testing, watch for this: slow tests. For Agile and Scrum, watch for this: overloading sprints. Small, frequent feedback loops catch problems while they are still cheap to fix.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using pytest or Jira.
