BGP and Internet Routing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make BGP and Internet Routing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

BGP and Internet Routing: the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.
autonomous systems: a core term, most visible in connecting ISPs.
route announcements: a core term that beginners often confuse with peering.
peering: a core term that connects to path selection.
path selection: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
bgpq4: a tool commonly used for directing traffic between large networks.
