DHCP Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with DHCP.

Do:
- Learn leases before anything else.
- Try dnsmasq on a small example.
- Make it a habit to reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.
- Test the case of home and office networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: rogue DHCP servers.
- Ignore this: exhausted address pools.
- Ignore this: conflicting static addresses.
- Adopt DHCP just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with DHCP, a little patience with address pools pays off more than any clever shortcut.
