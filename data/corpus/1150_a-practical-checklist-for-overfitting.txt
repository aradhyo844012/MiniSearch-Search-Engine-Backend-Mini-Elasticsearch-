A Practical Checklist for Overfitting

Use this checklist before you rely on Overfitting in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers training and validation error.
2. Guard against this risk: leaking test data.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Try the approach on a small case, for example diagnosing weak models.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Overfitting should accomplish.
6. Guard against this risk: evaluating only on training data.
7. Pick a tool to start with, such as scikit-learn or early stopping.
