Choosing Tools for Single Sign-On

There is no single best tool for Single Sign-On. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Okta is a common first choice, especially for company intranets. Keycloak suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Azure AD is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle reducing password fatigue well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: a single point of failure.

As a rule of thumb, plan for identity provider outages, no matter which tool you use.
