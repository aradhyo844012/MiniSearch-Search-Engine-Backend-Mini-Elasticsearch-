Offline-First Apps Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Offline-First Apps. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns.
Key terms: local storage, sync queues, conflict resolution, and optimistic updates.
Typical uses: field service tools, note-taking apps, and apps for low-connectivity regions.
Risks: unresolved sync conflicts, unbounded local data, and poor feedback about pending changes.
Tools: SQLite, PouchDB, and Room.

Mental picture: writing in a notebook and mailing the pages later.

Habit to build: show users which changes have not synced yet.

See also: Push Notifications, which sits close to Offline-First Apps in the area of Mobile, Embedded and IoT.
