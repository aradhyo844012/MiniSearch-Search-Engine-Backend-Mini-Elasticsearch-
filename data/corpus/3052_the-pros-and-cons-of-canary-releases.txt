The Pros and Cons of Canary Releases

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Canary Releases is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports safer releases
- Makes testing with real traffic easier
- Helps with reducing the blast radius of bugs

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too little traffic to reveal problems
- Risk: no clear success metrics
- Risk: manual promotion mistakes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as safer releases, and you can manage the risks, Canary Releases is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.
