Views and Materialized Views for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Views and Materialized Views.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Views and Materialized Views in your own words.
2. Describe view definitions, refresh strategies, permissions, and dependencies.
3. Give examples from simplifying repeated queries and hiding sensitive columns.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results. Then draw a window cut into a wall that shows only part of the room on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is stale materialized data a problem? How does refresh strategies relate to permissions? When would you choose PostgreSQL or MySQL?

Study habit worth keeping: document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.

When you feel ready, compare Views and Materialized Views with NULL Handling in SQL and write down three differences.
