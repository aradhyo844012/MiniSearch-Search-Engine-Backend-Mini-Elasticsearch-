Two Pointers and Sliding Window for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Two Pointers and Sliding Window.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Two Pointers and Sliding Window in your own words.
2. Describe left and right pointers, window boundaries, invariants, and shrinking and expanding.
3. Give examples from finding pairs with a given sum and longest substring problems.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Two Pointers and Sliding Window is techniques that scan arrays with two moving positions to avoid nested loops. Then draw a small frame that slides along a line of text on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is moving the wrong pointer a problem? How does window boundaries relate to invariants? When would you choose Python lists or collections.deque?

Study habit worth keeping: write down the window invariant before coding the loop.

When you feel ready, compare Two Pointers and Sliding Window with Fenwick Trees and write down three differences.
