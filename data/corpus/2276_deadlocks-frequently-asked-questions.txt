Deadlocks: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Deadlocks?
A: The term Deadlocks refers to situations where two or more tasks wait for each other forever.

Q: Why do developers use Deadlocks?
A: Common uses include database lock conflicts, multi-threaded programs, and resource allocation.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Deadlocks.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include thread dumps, database deadlock logs, and static analyzers.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: acquiring locks in inconsistent order; holding locks while waiting on I/O; and no timeouts on locks.
