Why Test-Driven Development Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Test-Driven Development make better decisions, because Test-Driven Development is a workflow where you write a failing test first and then write code to make it pass.

Three situations show why it matters. First, designing small modules. Second, preventing regressions. Third, gaining confidence for refactoring. In each case, a solid grasp of red, green, refactor and small steps separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: writing tests that are too large, skipping the refactor step, and testing implementation details.

Good tools exist, including pytest, JUnit, and Jest, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
