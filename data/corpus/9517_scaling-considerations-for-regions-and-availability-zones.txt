Scaling Considerations for Regions and Availability Zones

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Regions and Availability Zones changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding regions and getting placing services near users right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for putting everything in one zone and cross-region transfer fees, and measure with AWS Regions before changing anything.

Large scale: latency and data residency move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around surviving data center failures at this stage.

Before you scale, spread critical services across at least two zones. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
