Is Hyperparameter Tuning Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Hyperparameter Tuning, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Hyperparameter Tuning is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns.

It is a good fit when your work involves improving model accuracy or comparing algorithms, and when your team understands search spaces.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn grid search, or when you cannot manage these risks: tuning on the test set, searching too many options, and ignoring training cost.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether random search and validation scores are part of the answer. If they are, Hyperparameter Tuning is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with running automated experiments and try random search before anything fancier.
