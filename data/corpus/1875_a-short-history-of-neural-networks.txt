A Short History of Neural Networks

Ideas behind Neural Networks developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. When people talk about Neural Networks, they usually mean models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Simple baselines are worth building first because they show what improvement is realistic.

To understand Neural Networks, start with layers. Then move on to weights, and finally look at activation functions and backpropagation. Sketching backpropagation on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

Neural Networks and Large Language Models are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

In short, careful study of Neural Networks pays off. Spend time on layers and weights, and the rest will follow.
