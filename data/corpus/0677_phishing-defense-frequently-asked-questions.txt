Phishing Defense: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Phishing Defense?
A: The term Phishing Defense refers to recognizing and reducing attacks that trick people into revealing information.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: clicking before checking; reusing passwords; and ignoring reports from users.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with suspicious links and sender verification, then move on to email filtering and user training.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Phishing Defense.

Q: How does it relate to Secure Coding?
A: Phishing Defense and Secure Coding often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Security.

Q: Why do developers use Phishing Defense?
A: Common uses include protecting employees, securing shared mailboxes, and incident response drills.
