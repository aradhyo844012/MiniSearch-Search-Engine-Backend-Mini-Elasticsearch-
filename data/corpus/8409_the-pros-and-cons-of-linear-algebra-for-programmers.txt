The Pros and Cons of Linear Algebra for Programmers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Linear Algebra for Programmers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports 3D graphics
- Makes machine learning easier
- Helps with recommendation systems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing row and column conventions
- Risk: shape mismatches
- Risk: forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as 3D graphics, and you can manage the risks, Linear Algebra for Programmers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, check shapes before multiplying.
