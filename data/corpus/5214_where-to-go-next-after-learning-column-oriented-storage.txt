Where to Go Next After Learning Column-Oriented Storage

You have met the basics of Column-Oriented Storage. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about B-Trees on Disk, which belongs to the same area as Column-Oriented Storage (Storage Engines and Databases) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of column chunks, compression, vectorized scans or predicate pushdown, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around large aggregations with Apache Parquet, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow single-row lookups.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Column-Oriented Storage to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
