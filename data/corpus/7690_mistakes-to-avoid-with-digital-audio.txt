Mistakes to Avoid with Digital Audio

Everyone learns Digital Audio partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: clipping from too high a level. It usually appears when someone skips sampling rate and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: aliasing at low sample rates. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of music production. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: mixing sample rates. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the Nyquist limit to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep recordings below the clipping level.
