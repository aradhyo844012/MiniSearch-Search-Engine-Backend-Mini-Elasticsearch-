Understanding Internationalization Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a stage set where the backdrop can be swapped for each audience.

In that picture, locale detection is the first rule you follow, translation files is the next step, and pluralization and date and number formatting keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions, and it involves details such as hard-coded strings that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for i18next to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain global products to a friend.
