CSRF for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover CSRF.

Learning goals:
1. Explain CSRF in your own words.
2. Describe session cookies, anti-CSRF tokens, SameSite cookies, and state-changing requests.
3. Give examples from protecting forms and securing account settings.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: CSRF is an attack that tricks a logged-in user's browser into sending unwanted requests to a site. Then draw a forged letter that a bank clerk stamps because your ID is already on file on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is using GET requests for state changes a problem? How does anti-CSRF tokens relate to SameSite cookies? When would you choose Django CSRF middleware or Flask-WTF?

Study habit: combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.

When you feel ready, compare CSRF with Single Sign-On and write down three differences.
