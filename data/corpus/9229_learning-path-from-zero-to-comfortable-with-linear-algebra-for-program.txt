Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Linear Algebra for Programmers

This path is for a learner who has never studied Linear Algebra for Programmers before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning) and learn vectors and matrices.

Stage 2, hands-on: install NumPy, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving 3D graphics.

Stage 3, depth: study dot products and eigenvalues, then deliberately explore this problem: mixing row and column conventions.

Stage 4, application: apply Linear Algebra for Programmers to machine learning, compare MATLAB with the 3Blue1Brown video series, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Linear Algebra for Programmers to someone else, and then move on to Discrete Mathematics.
