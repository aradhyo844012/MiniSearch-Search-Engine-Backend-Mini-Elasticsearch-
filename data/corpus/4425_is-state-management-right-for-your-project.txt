Is State Management Right for Your Project?

Before adopting State Management, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data.

It is a good fit when your work involves large single-page apps or shared shopping carts, and when your team understands single source of truth.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn actions, or when you cannot manage these risks: putting everything in global state, duplicated state that drifts apart, and unclear update paths.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether reducers and derived state are part of the answer. If they are, State Management is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with multi-step form wizards and keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
