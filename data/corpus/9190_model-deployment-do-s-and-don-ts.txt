Model Deployment Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Model Deployment.

Do:
- Learn serving APIs before anything else.
- Try FastAPI on a small example.
- Make it a habit to log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
- Test the case of recommendation endpoints early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: training-serving mismatch.
- Ignore this: slow cold starts.
- Ignore this: no fallback when the model fails.
- Adopt Model Deployment just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Model Deployment, a little patience with batch versus online inference pays off more than any clever shortcut.
