Choosing Tools for Service Mesh

There is no single best tool for Service Mesh. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Istio is a common first choice, especially for securing service traffic. Linkerd suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Envoy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle retries and timeouts well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: added latency and complexity.

As a rule of thumb, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful, no matter which tool you use.
