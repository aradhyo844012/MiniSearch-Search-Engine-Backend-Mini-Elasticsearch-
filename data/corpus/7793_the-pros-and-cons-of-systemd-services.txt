The Pros and Cons of systemd Services

No technology is free of trade-offs, and systemd Services is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports starting services at boot
- Makes restarting crashed programs easier
- Helps with managing dependencies between services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: editing unit files without reloading the daemon
- Risk: missing restart policies
- Risk: ignoring dependency ordering

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as starting services at boot, and you can manage the risks, systemd Services is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.
