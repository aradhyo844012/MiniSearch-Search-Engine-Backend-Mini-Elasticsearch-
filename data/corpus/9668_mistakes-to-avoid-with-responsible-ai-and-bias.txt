Mistakes to Avoid with Responsible AI and Bias

Everyone learns Responsible AI and Bias partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming data is neutral. It usually appears when someone skips bias in data and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: testing only on average performance. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of hiring tools. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: giving people no way to appeal. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on human oversight to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test performance separately for different groups.
