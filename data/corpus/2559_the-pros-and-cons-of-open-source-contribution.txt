The Pros and Cons of Open Source Contribution

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Open Source Contribution is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports building a portfolio
- Makes learning from experienced maintainers easier
- Helps with giving back to tools you use

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: opening huge pull requests
- Risk: ignoring contribution rules
- Risk: expecting instant replies

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as building a portfolio, and you can manage the risks, Open Source Contribution is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with documentation fixes or small bugs.
