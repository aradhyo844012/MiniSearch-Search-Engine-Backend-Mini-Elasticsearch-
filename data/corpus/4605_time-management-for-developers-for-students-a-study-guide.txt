Time Management for Developers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Time Management for Developers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Time Management for Developers in your own words.
2. Describe prioritization, time blocking, breaking down tasks, and avoiding context switches.
3. Give examples from managing coursework and projects and meeting deadlines.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule. Then draw packing a suitcase with the big items first on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is multitasking a problem? How does time blocking relate to breaking down tasks? When would you choose calendar blocking or Trello?

Study habit worth keeping: break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.

When you feel ready, compare Time Management for Developers with Developer Portfolios and write down three differences.
