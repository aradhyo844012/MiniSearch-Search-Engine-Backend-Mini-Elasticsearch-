The Pros and Cons of Event-Driven Architecture

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Event-Driven Architecture is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports order pipelines
- Makes real-time notifications easier
- Helps with integrating many services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: hard-to-trace flows
- Risk: duplicate events
- Risk: unclear event ownership

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as order pipelines, and you can manage the risks, Event-Driven Architecture is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, document every event name and schema.
