Key Terms in NoSQL Databases Explained

Clear vocabulary makes NoSQL Databases much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about NoSQL Databases, they usually mean a broad family of databases that do not use the traditional relational table model. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

document stores: a key term in NoSQL Databases. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
key-value stores: a key term in NoSQL Databases. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
