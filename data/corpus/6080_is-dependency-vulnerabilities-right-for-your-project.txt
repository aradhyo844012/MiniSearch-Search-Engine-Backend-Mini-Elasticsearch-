Is Dependency Vulnerabilities Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Dependency Vulnerabilities, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Dependency Vulnerabilities is security weaknesses that come from third-party libraries a project uses.

It is a good fit when your work involves auditing projects or automating updates, and when your team understands CVE identifiers.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn version pinning, or when you cannot manage these risks: ignoring transitive dependencies, never updating, and trusting packages without checking them.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether transitive dependencies and software bills of materials are part of the answer. If they are, Dependency Vulnerabilities is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with meeting supply chain requirements and automate update pull requests.
