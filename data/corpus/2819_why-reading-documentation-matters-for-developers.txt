Why Reading Documentation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Reading Documentation make better decisions, because Reading Documentation is the skill of finding accurate answers in official guides and references.

Three situations show why it matters. First, learning new libraries. Second, solving errors. Third, verifying answers from other sources. In each case, a solid grasp of search and tutorials versus references separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: reading the wrong version, skipping the overview, and copying examples blindly.

You will not need to build everything yourself; MDN Web Docs, the Python docs, and man pages cover most needs.
