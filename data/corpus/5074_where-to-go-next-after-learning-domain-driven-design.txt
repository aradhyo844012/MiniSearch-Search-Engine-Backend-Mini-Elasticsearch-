Where to Go Next After Learning Domain-Driven Design

You have met the basics of Domain-Driven Design. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Code Documentation, which belongs to the same area as Domain-Driven Design (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of ubiquitous language, bounded contexts, aggregates or entities and value objects, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around aligning developers with domain experts with event storming, and note every problem you meet, starting with using it for simple CRUD apps.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Domain-Driven Design to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
