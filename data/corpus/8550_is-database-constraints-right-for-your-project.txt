Is Database Constraints Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Database Constraints, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.

It is a good fit when your work involves preventing bad data or protecting relationships, and when your team understands primary keys.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn foreign keys, or when you cannot manage these risks: skipping constraints for speed, missing indexes on foreign keys, and rules enforced only in the app.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether unique constraints and check constraints are part of the answer. If they are, Database Constraints is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with documenting business rules and put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
