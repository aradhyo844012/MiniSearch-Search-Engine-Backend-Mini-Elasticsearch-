Choosing Tools for MongoDB Aggregation

There is no single best tool for MongoDB Aggregation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

MongoDB Compass is a common first choice, especially for summarizing orders. mongosh suits teams that already work with similar technology, and PyMongo is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for MongoDB Aggregation by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle building analytics queries well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for MongoDB Aggregation, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: putting $match late.

As a rule of thumb, filter with $match as early as possible, no matter which tool you use.
