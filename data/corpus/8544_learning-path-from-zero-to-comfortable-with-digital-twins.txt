Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Digital Twins

This path is for a learner who has never studied Digital Twins before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data) and learn sensor data and simulation models.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Azure Digital Twins, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving factory monitoring.

Stage 3, depth: study synchronization and analytics, then deliberately explore this problem: stale data.

Stage 4, application: apply Digital Twins to building management, compare Unity with MQTT, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Digital Twins to someone else, and then move on to Blockchain Fundamentals.
