The Future of TLS Handshake

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of TLS Handshake is easy to describe in general terms. The term TLS Handshake refers to the exchange that lets a client and server agree on keys and set up an encrypted connection. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Every web request crosses several layers of protocols before it reaches an application.

Expect tools such as OpenSSL and Let's Encrypt to keep improving, and expect newer ideas to keep building on certificates. The basics, though, tend to stay the same, so time spent on cipher suites and session keys is rarely wasted. Anyone working on securing websites will keep meeting TLS Handshake for years to come.
