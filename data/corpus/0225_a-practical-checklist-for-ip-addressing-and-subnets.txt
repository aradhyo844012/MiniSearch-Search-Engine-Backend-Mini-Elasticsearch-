A Practical Checklist for IP Addressing and Subnets

Use this checklist before you rely on IP Addressing and Subnets in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: exhausting small subnets.
2. Review how IP Addressing and Subnets interacts with WebSockets.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what IP Addressing and Subnets should accomplish.
4. Write at least one automated test that covers IPv4 and IPv6.
5. Pick a tool to start with, such as ipcalc or ping.
6. Guard against this risk: overlapping address ranges.
7. Review the core ideas: IPv4 and IPv6, subnet masks, CIDR notation, and private ranges.
8. Guard against this risk: miscounting host bits.
