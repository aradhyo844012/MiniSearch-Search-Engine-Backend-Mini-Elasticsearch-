Firewalls Explained Simply

If Firewalls sounds intimidating, relax. It is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Firewalls as a security gate that checks each visitor against a list. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: rules, stateful inspection, default deny, and zones. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with rules, then add stateful inspection.

People reach for Firewalls when they work on protecting servers, segmenting networks, and logging suspicious traffic.

One practical tip is to start with default deny and open only what you need.

After Firewalls, a natural next topic is BGP and Internet Routing.
