Choosing Tools for systemd Services

There is no single best tool for systemd Services. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

systemctl is a common first choice, especially for starting services at boot. journalctl suits teams that already work with similar technology, and systemd-analyze is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for systemd Services by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle restarting crashed programs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for systemd Services, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: editing unit files without reloading the daemon.

As a rule of thumb, run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change, no matter which tool you use.
