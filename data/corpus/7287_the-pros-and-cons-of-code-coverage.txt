The Pros and Cons of Code Coverage

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Code Coverage is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports finding untested code
- Makes setting quality gates easier
- Helps with tracking test progress

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: chasing 100 percent
- Risk: treating coverage as quality
- Risk: ignoring branch coverage

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as finding untested code, and you can manage the risks, Code Coverage is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use coverage to find gaps, not to judge quality.
