REST APIs vs Microservices: Key Differences

REST APIs and Microservices both belong to the area of Backend and APIs, but they solve different problems. This article compares them side by side so you can choose with confidence.

The term REST APIs refers to an approach to designing web APIs around resources, URLs and standard HTTP methods. Its core ideas are resources, HTTP verbs, and status codes.

The term Microservices refers to an architecture that splits an application into small services that can be deployed independently. Its core ideas are service boundaries, APIs, and independent deployment.

The main difference is focus. REST APIs puts the emphasis on resources and HTTP verbs, while Microservices puts the emphasis on service boundaries and APIs.

Reach for REST APIs when your task involves mobile app backends or integrating services. Reach for Microservices when your task involves large teams working in parallel or scaling parts separately.

Both have risks. With REST APIs, watch for this: inconsistent naming. With Microservices, watch for this: splitting too early.

In practice you do not always have to choose. Many projects use REST APIs alongside Microservices, with tools such as Flask and Docker. Good APIs are boring, predictable and well documented.
