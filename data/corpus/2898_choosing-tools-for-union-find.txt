Choosing Tools for Union-Find

There is no single best tool for Union-Find. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python lists is a common first choice, especially for detecting connected components. scipy.sparse.csgraph suits teams that already work with similar technology, and NetworkX is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle building minimum spanning trees well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: forgetting path compression.

As a rule of thumb, implement path compression and union by rank together, no matter which tool you use.
