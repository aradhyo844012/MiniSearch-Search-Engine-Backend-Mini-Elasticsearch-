State Management Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with State Management.

Do:
- Learn single source of truth before anything else.
- Try Redux on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
- Test the case of large single-page apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: putting everything in global state.
- Ignore this: duplicated state that drifts apart.
- Ignore this: unclear update paths.
- Adopt State Management just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with State Management, a little patience with actions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
