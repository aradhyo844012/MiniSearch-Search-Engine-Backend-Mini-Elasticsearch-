Is Convolutional Neural Networks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Convolutional Neural Networks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.

It is a good fit when your work involves image classification or medical image analysis, and when your team understands filters.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn pooling, or when you cannot manage these risks: too little training data, ignoring data augmentation, and overly deep models for simple tasks.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether feature maps and stride are part of the answer. If they are, Convolutional Neural Networks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with object detection and start from a pretrained model.
