Skills to Build Around Modular Monoliths

Knowing the definition of Modular Monoliths is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with module boundaries and public interfaces, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with ArchUnit on a Modular Monoliths task, and try at least one alternative such as import-linter.

Judgment: learn to decide when Modular Monoliths fits a problem such as small teams, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example an apartment building with separate flats under one roof.

Habits: enforce boundaries with automated checks, and write down what you learn after each project.
