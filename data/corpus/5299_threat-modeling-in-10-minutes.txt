Threat Modeling in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Threat Modeling.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Threat Modeling is a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn assets, attackers, trust boundaries, and mitigations.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine walking around a house asking where a burglar might get in. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through designing new features, reviewing architecture, and prioritizing security work.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for doing it once and never again and ignoring data flows, then try STRIDE for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to draw the data flow diagram first.
