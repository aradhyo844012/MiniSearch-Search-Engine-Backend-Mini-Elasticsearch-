Skills to Build Around GraphQL

Knowing the definition of GraphQL is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with schema and queries and mutations, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Apollo on a GraphQL task, and try at least one alternative such as GraphQL Playground.

Judgment: learn to decide when GraphQL fits a problem such as mobile apps with limited bandwidth, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu.

Habits: use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries, and write down what you learn after each project.
