Game AI Behavior Trees Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Game AI Behavior Trees. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does.
Key terms: selector nodes, sequence nodes, conditions, and actions.
Typical uses: enemy behavior, companion characters, and simulated crowds.
Risks: trees that become tangled, expensive conditions checked every frame, and no way to debug decisions.
Tools: Unreal Engine behavior trees, py_trees, and Behavior Designer.

Mental picture: a flowchart a character consults every moment before acting.

Habit to build: visualize the tree while the game is running.
