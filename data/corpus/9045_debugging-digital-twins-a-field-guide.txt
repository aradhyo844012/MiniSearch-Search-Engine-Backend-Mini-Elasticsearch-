Debugging Digital Twins: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Digital Twins, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: stale data, over-detailed models, and unclear goals.
Step 3: revisit sensor data and simulation models, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Azure Digital Twins or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Digital Twins in work such as factory monitoring come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, define the question the twin must answer first. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
