Disaster Recovery Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Disaster Recovery feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Disaster Recovery: the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.
recovery time objective: a core term, most visible in surviving regional outages.
recovery point objective: a core term that beginners often confuse with failover regions.
failover regions: a core term that connects to runbooks.
runbooks: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
AWS Backup: a tool commonly used for meeting compliance needs.

Come back to this Disaster Recovery list whenever documentation for Terraform uses a word you do not recognize.
