Key Terms in Natural Language Processing Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Natural Language Processing much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. Many students first meet Natural Language Processing in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language.

tokenization: a key term in Natural Language Processing. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
embeddings: a key term in Natural Language Processing. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
language models: a key term in Natural Language Processing. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
evaluation metrics: a key term in Natural Language Processing. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
