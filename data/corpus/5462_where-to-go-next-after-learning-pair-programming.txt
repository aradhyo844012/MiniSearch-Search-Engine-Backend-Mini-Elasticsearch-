Where to Go Next After Learning Pair Programming

You have met the basics of Pair Programming. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Event-Driven Architecture, which belongs to the same area as Pair Programming (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of driver and navigator, rotation, communication or shared ownership, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around solving hard bugs with VS Code Live Share, and note every problem you meet, starting with one person dominating.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Pair Programming to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
