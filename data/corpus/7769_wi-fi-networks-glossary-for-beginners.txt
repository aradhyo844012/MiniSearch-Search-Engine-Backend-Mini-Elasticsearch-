Wi-Fi Networks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Wi-Fi Networks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Wi-Fi Networks: wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.
SSIDs: a core term, most visible in home internet.
channels and bands: a core term that beginners often confuse with WPA encryption.
WPA encryption: a core term that connects to signal strength.
signal strength: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Wi-Fi analyzer apps: a tool commonly used for office coverage.

Come back to this Wi-Fi Networks list whenever documentation for Wireshark uses a word you do not recognize.
