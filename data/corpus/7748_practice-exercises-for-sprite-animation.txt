Practice Exercises for Sprite Animation

These exercises build real understanding of Sprite Animation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: inconsistent frame timing. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare Unity Animator and Phaser for visual novels and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick Aseprite and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Sprite Animation and include the term sprite sheets.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble 2D platformers or user interface effects.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Sprite Animation is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move.
