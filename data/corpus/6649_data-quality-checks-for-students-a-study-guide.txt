Data Quality Checks for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Data Quality Checks.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Data Quality Checks in your own words.
2. Describe null checks, uniqueness, freshness, and schema validation.
3. Give examples from protecting dashboards and validating ingestion.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent. Then draw a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is alerts nobody reads a problem? How does uniqueness relate to freshness? When would you choose Great Expectations or dbt tests?

Study habit: check data as early as it enters the pipeline.

When you feel ready, compare Data Quality Checks with Feature Stores and write down three differences.
