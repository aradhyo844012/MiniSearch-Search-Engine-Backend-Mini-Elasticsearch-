Mistakes to Avoid with Disk Partitioning and Mounting

Everyone learns Disk Partitioning and Mounting partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: wrong device names. It usually appears when someone skips partitions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: mounting without fstab entries. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of adding a new drive. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: formatting the wrong disk. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fstab to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
