Practice Exercises for Form Validation

These exercises build real understanding of Form Validation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick HTML constraint validation and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble sign-up forms or payment pages.
Exercise 3: Explain how error messages and constraint attributes differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Form Validation and include the term client-side checks.
Exercise 5: Compare Zod and Yup for search filters and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Form Validation is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted.
