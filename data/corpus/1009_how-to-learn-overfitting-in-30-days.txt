How to Learn Overfitting in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Overfitting. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term Overfitting refers to when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn training and validation error and model complexity, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with scikit-learn on a small task such as diagnosing weak models.
Week 3: build a mini project around choosing simpler models.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as evaluating only on training data, and explain Overfitting to a friend.

In practice you can start with scikit-learn, then explore early stopping or learning curves as your needs grow.

A solid grasp of Overfitting also makes Gradient Descent easier to understand.

In short, careful study of Overfitting pays off. Spend time on training and validation error and model complexity, and the rest will follow.
