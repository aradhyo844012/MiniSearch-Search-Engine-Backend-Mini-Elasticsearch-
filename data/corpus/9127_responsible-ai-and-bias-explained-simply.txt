Responsible AI and Bias Explained Simply

If Responsible AI and Bias sounds intimidating, relax. It is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Responsible AI and Bias as a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: bias in data, fairness metrics, transparency, and human oversight. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with bias in data, then add fairness metrics.

You will meet Responsible AI and Bias in work such as hiring tools, lending models, and healthcare decisions.

One practical tip is to test performance separately for different groups.
