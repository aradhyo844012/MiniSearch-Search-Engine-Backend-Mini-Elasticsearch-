Choosing Tools for Technical Writing

There is no single best tool for Technical Writing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Markdown is a common first choice, especially for documentation. Grammarly suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Vale is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle blog posts well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: assuming too much knowledge.

As a rule of thumb, state the main point in the first two sentences, no matter which tool you use.
