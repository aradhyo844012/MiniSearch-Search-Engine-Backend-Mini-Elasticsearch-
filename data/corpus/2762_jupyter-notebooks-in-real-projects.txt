Jupyter Notebooks in Real Projects

Reading about Jupyter Notebooks is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: exploring data. Here, cells drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against running cells out of order.

Scenario two: teaching and demos. This time kernels matters most, and tools such as JupyterLab help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: sharing analysis. The key idea is markdown, and the biggest risk is hidden state.

Across all three scenarios, Jupyter Notebooks stays the same idea: interactive documents that combine code, results and notes. Only the details change.
