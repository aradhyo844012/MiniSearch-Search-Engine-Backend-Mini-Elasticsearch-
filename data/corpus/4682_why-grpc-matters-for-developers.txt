Why gRPC Matters for Developers

Developers who understand gRPC make better decisions, because gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts.

Three situations show why it matters. First, service-to-service calls. Second, low-latency APIs. Third, streaming data. In each case, a solid grasp of protocol buffers and service definitions separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: breaking changes in message fields, poor browser support without a proxy, and harder manual debugging.

You will not need to build everything yourself; protoc, grpcurl, and Envoy cover most needs.
