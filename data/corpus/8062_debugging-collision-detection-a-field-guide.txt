Debugging Collision Detection: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Collision Detection, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: tunneling through thin objects, checking every pair of objects, and inconsistent hitboxes.
Step 3: revisit bounding boxes and broad and narrow phases, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Box2D or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Collision Detection in work such as platformers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use simple shapes first and refine only when needed. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
