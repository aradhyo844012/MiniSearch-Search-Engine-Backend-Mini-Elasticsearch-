A Practical Checklist for Authentication and Sessions

Use this checklist before you rely on Authentication and Sessions in a project.

1. Pick a tool to start with, such as Flask-Login or Django auth.
2. Review how Authentication and Sessions interacts with Caching Strategies.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example logging in to web apps.
4. Guard against this risk: trusting client-side checks.
5. Guard against this risk: storing session IDs insecurely.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on session cookies.
7. Document your decisions in the project README.
8. Confirm that your team agrees on what Authentication and Sessions should accomplish.
