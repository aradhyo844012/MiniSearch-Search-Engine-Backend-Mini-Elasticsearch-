Where to Go Next After Learning CSRF

You have met the basics of CSRF. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Hashing vs Encryption, which belongs to the same area as CSRF (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of session cookies, anti-CSRF tokens, SameSite cookies or state-changing requests, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around securing account settings with Django CSRF middleware, and note every problem you meet, starting with using GET requests for state changes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining CSRF to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
