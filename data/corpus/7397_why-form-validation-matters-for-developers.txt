Why Form Validation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Form Validation make better decisions, because Form Validation is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted.

Three situations show why it matters. First, sign-up forms. Second, payment pages. Third, search filters. In each case, a solid grasp of client-side checks and server-side checks separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: validating only on the client, vague error messages, and rejecting valid but unusual input.

You will not need to build everything yourself; HTML constraint validation, Zod, and Yup cover most needs.
