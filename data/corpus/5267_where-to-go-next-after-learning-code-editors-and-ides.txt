Where to Go Next After Learning Code Editors and IDEs

You have met the basics of Code Editors and IDEs. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Time Management for Developers, which belongs to the same area as Code Editors and IDEs (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of syntax highlighting, autocomplete, debugger integration or extensions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around navigating large codebases with VS Code, and note every problem you meet, starting with installing too many extensions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Code Editors and IDEs to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
