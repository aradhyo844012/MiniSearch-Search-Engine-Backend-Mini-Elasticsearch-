Blockchain Fundamentals in Real Projects

Reading about Blockchain Fundamentals is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: digital currencies. Here, blocks drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against treating it as a database replacement.

Scenario two: supply chain tracking. This time hashes matters most, and tools such as Bitcoin Core help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: public records. The key idea is consensus, and the biggest risk is slow throughput.

Across all three scenarios, Blockchain Fundamentals stays the same idea: a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes. Only the details change.
