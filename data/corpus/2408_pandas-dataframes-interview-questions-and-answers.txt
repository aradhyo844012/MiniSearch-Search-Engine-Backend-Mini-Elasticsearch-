Pandas DataFrames Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Pandas DataFrames.

Q: Explain Pandas DataFrames in simple words.
A: Pandas DataFrames refers to table-like structures in Python for loading, cleaning and analyzing data. I would add an example involving loading CSV files.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with summarizing query logs, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: chained assignment surprises.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: pandas, Jupyter, and NumPy.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: chained assignment surprises; loading too much data into memory; and forgetting to check data types.

Q: How does it connect to ETL Pipelines?
A: Both belong to the area of Data Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
