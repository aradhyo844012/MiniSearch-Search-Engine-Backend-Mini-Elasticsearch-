Go Goroutines Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Go Goroutines. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: lightweight threads managed by the Go runtime that run functions concurrently.
Key terms: goroutines, channels, select statements, and wait groups.
Typical uses: servers handling many connections, parallel data processing, and background workers.
Risks: goroutine leaks, data races, and unbuffered channels causing deadlocks.
Tools: the go command, the race detector, and pprof.

Mental picture: a small crew of waiters passing notes through a serving hatch.

Habit to build: run your tests with the race detector turned on.
