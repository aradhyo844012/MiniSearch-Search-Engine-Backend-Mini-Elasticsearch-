Event Sourcing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Event Sourcing.

Do:
- Learn event log before anything else.
- Try Apache Kafka on a small example.
- Make it a habit to version your events from the first day.
- Test the case of audit trails early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: changing event formats carelessly.
- Ignore this: unbounded replay times.
- Ignore this: using it for simple CRUD apps.
- Adopt Event Sourcing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Event Sourcing, a little patience with projections pays off more than any clever shortcut.
