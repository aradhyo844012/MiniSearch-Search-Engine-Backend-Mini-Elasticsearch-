Where to Go Next After Learning Combinatorics

You have met the basics of Combinatorics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Information Theory and Entropy, which belongs to the same area as Combinatorics (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of permutations, combinations, the pigeonhole principle or inclusion-exclusion, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around probability with Python itertools, and note every problem you meet, starting with double counting.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Combinatorics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
