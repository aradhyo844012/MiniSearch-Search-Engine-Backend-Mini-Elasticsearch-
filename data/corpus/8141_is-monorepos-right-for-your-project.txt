Is Monorepos Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Monorepos, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Monorepos is single repositories that hold the code for many projects.

It is a good fit when your work involves sharing libraries across apps or coordinated refactors, and when your team understands shared tooling.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn code ownership, or when you cannot manage these risks: slow checkouts, unclear ownership, and CI that rebuilds everything.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether selective builds and atomic changes are part of the answer. If they are, Monorepos is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with consistent tooling and set up selective builds and tests early.
