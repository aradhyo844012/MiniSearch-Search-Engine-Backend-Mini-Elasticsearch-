Where to Go Next After Learning Linear Algebra for Programmers

You have met the basics of Linear Algebra for Programmers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Combinatorics, which belongs to the same area as Linear Algebra for Programmers (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of vectors, matrices, dot products or eigenvalues, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around machine learning with NumPy, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing row and column conventions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Linear Algebra for Programmers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
