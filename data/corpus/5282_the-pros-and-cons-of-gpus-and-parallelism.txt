The Pros and Cons of GPUs and Parallelism

No technology is free of trade-offs, and GPUs and Parallelism is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports training neural networks
- Makes rendering graphics easier
- Helps with scientific simulations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow data transfers
- Risk: branch-heavy code
- Risk: launching tiny workloads

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as training neural networks, and you can manage the risks, GPUs and Parallelism is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
