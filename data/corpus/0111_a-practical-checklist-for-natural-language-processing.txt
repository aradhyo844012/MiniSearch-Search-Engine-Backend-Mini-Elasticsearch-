A Practical Checklist for Natural Language Processing

Use this checklist before you rely on Natural Language Processing in a project.

1. Review how Natural Language Processing interacts with Gradient Descent.
2. Guard against this risk: ignoring language variety.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as spaCy or NLTK.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on embeddings.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Natural Language Processing should accomplish.
6. Guard against this risk: messy text preprocessing.
7. Measure results before and after any change.
8. Try the approach on a small case, for example search engines.
