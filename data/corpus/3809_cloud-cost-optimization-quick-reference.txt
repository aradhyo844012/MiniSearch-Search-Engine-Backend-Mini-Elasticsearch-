Cloud Cost Optimization Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Cloud Cost Optimization. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.
Key terms: right-sizing, reserved capacity, tagging, and usage reports.
Typical uses: cutting idle resources, budgeting by team, and choosing storage tiers.
Risks: forgotten test environments, over-provisioned databases, and surprise data transfer charges.
Tools: AWS Cost Explorer, Infracost, and Kubecost.

Mental picture: turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using.

Habit to build: tag every resource with an owner.
