Practice Exercises for Wi-Fi Networks

These exercises build real understanding of Wi-Fi Networks. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Wi-Fi Networks and include the term SSIDs.
Exercise 2: Pick Wi-Fi analyzer apps and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: overlapping channels. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Explain how WPA encryption and signal strength differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Compare Wireshark and router admin pages for public hotspots and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Wi-Fi Networks is wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.
