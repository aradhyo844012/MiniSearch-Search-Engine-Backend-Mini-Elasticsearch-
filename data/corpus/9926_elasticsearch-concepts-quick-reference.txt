Elasticsearch Concepts Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Elasticsearch Concepts. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.
Key terms: indexes, shards, analyzers, and queries.
Typical uses: site search, log analytics, and product catalogs.
Risks: too many shards, mapping explosions, and treating it as a primary database.
Tools: Elasticsearch, Kibana, and OpenSearch.

Mental picture: a giant library with an index card for every word.

Habit to build: define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
