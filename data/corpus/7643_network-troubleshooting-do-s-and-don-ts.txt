Network Troubleshooting Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Network Troubleshooting.

Do:
- Learn protocol layers before anything else.
- Try ping on a small example.
- Make it a habit to work from the bottom layer upward.
- Test the case of fixing office connectivity early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: changing several things at once.
- Ignore this: blaming the application too early.
- Ignore this: ignoring DNS.
- Adopt Network Troubleshooting just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Network Troubleshooting, a little patience with ping and traceroute pays off more than any clever shortcut.
