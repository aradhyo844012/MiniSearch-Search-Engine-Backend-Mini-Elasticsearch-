GPUs and Parallelism Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with GPUs and Parallelism.

Do:
- Learn threads and blocks before anything else.
- Try CUDA on a small example.
- Make it a habit to minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
- Test the case of training neural networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow data transfers.
- Ignore this: branch-heavy code.
- Ignore this: launching tiny workloads.
- Adopt GPUs and Parallelism just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with GPUs and Parallelism, a little patience with kernels pays off more than any clever shortcut.
