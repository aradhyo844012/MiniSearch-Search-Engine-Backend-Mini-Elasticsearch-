Hashing vs Encryption Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Hashing vs Encryption.

Do:
- Learn one-way functions before anything else.
- Try SHA-256 on a small example.
- Make it a habit to ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
- Test the case of storing passwords early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them.
- Ignore this: using broken hash functions.
- Ignore this: confusing encoding with encryption.
- Adopt Hashing vs Encryption just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Hashing vs Encryption, a little patience with keys pays off more than any clever shortcut.
