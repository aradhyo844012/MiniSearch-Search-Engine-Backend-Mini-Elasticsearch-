Choosing Tools for Python Decorators

There is no single best tool for Python Decorators. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

functools is a common first choice, especially for logging calls. Flask routes suits teams that already work with similar technology, and pytest fixtures is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle caching results well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: losing function metadata.

As a rule of thumb, always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator, no matter which tool you use.
