The Pros and Cons of SQL Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and SQL Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports reading customer records
- Makes building reports easier
- Helps with changing stored data

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: forgetting the WHERE clause on updates
- Risk: selecting every column
- Risk: assuming row order without ORDER BY

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as reading customer records, and you can manage the risks, SQL Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, run a SELECT with the same WHERE clause before any UPDATE or DELETE.
