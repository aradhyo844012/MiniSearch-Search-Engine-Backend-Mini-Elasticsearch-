Teaching AI Agents to a Teammate

Explaining AI Agents to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving research assistants, naming planning and tool calls as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain memory in AI Agents back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, runaway loops, and suggest this habit: limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
