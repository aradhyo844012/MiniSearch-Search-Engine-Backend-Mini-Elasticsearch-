Debugging Bluetooth Low Energy: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Bluetooth Low Energy, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: ignoring connection limits, weak pairing security, and assuming long range.
Step 3: revisit advertising and GATT services, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use nRF Connect or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Bluetooth Low Energy in work such as fitness trackers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test with several phone models. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
