The Pros and Cons of Dependency Vulnerabilities

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Dependency Vulnerabilities is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports auditing projects
- Makes automating updates easier
- Helps with meeting supply chain requirements

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring transitive dependencies
- Risk: never updating
- Risk: trusting packages without checking them

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as auditing projects, and you can manage the risks, Dependency Vulnerabilities is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, automate update pull requests.
