Skills to Build Around Recommendation Systems

Knowing the definition of Recommendation Systems is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with collaborative filtering and content-based filtering, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Surprise on a Recommendation Systems task, and try at least one alternative such as implicit.

Judgment: learn to decide when Recommendation Systems fits a problem such as streaming services, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked.

Habits: evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data, and write down what you learn after each project.
