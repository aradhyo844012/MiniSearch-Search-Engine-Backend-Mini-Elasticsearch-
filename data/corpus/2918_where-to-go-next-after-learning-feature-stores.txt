Where to Go Next After Learning Feature Stores

You have met the basics of Feature Stores. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Statistical Significance, which belongs to the same area as Feature Stores (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of offline store, online store, point-in-time correctness or feature definitions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around serving real-time predictions with Feast, and note every problem you meet, starting with leaking future data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Feature Stores to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
