Configuration Management in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Configuration Management.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Configuration Management is keeping application settings separate from code so they can change per environment.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn environment variables, config files, defaults, and secrets handling.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine the settings dial on a machine that stays the same on every site. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through separating development and production, toggling features, and deploying the same build everywhere.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for committing secrets and inconsistent defaults, then try dotenv for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing.
