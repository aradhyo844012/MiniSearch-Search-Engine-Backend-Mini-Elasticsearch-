Choosing Tools for Model Deployment

There is no single best tool for Model Deployment. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

FastAPI is a common first choice, especially for recommendation endpoints. TorchServe suits teams that already work with similar technology, and BentoML is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Model Deployment by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle fraud checks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Model Deployment, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: training-serving mismatch.

As a rule of thumb, log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited, no matter which tool you use.
