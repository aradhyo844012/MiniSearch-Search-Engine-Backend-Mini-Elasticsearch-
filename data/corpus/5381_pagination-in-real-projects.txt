Pagination in Real Projects

Reading about Pagination is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: search results. Here, offset pagination drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against offsets getting slow on huge tables.

Scenario two: activity feeds. This time cursor pagination matters most, and tools such as SQL LIMIT and OFFSET help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: admin tables. The key idea is page size, and the biggest risk is unstable sort order.

Across all three scenarios, Pagination stays the same idea: splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time. Only the details change.
