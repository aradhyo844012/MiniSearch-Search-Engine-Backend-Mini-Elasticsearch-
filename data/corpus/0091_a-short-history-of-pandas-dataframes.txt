A Short History of Pandas DataFrames

Ideas behind Pandas DataFrames developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Put simply, Pandas DataFrames covers table-like structures in Python for loading, cleaning and analyzing data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.
