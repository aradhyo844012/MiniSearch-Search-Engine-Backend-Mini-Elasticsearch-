The Pros and Cons of Regression Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Regression Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports protecting releases
- Makes verifying bug fixes easier
- Helps with refactoring safely

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow suites that nobody runs
- Risk: missing tests for fixed bugs
- Risk: ignoring flaky failures

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as protecting releases, and you can manage the risks, Regression Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, add a test for every bug you fix.
