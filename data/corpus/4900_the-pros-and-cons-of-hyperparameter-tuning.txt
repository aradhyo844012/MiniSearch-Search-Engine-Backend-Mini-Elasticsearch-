The Pros and Cons of Hyperparameter Tuning

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Hyperparameter Tuning is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports improving model accuracy
- Makes comparing algorithms easier
- Helps with running automated experiments

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: tuning on the test set
- Risk: searching too many options
- Risk: ignoring training cost

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as improving model accuracy, and you can manage the risks, Hyperparameter Tuning is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, try random search before anything fancier.
