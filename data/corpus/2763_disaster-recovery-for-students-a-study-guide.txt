Disaster Recovery for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Disaster Recovery.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Disaster Recovery in your own words.
2. Describe recovery time objective, recovery point objective, failover regions, and runbooks.
3. Give examples from surviving regional outages and meeting compliance needs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures. Then draw a fire drill for a whole system on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is untested plans a problem? How does recovery point objective relate to failover regions? When would you choose AWS Backup or Terraform?

Study habit: run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.

When you feel ready, compare Disaster Recovery with Cloud Cost Optimization and write down three differences.
