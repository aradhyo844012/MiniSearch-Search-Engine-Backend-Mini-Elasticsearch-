Best Practices for Feature Engineering

The practices below come from patterns that tend to work well across many teams and projects. The term Feature Engineering refers to creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

1. Practice scaling with small exercises until it feels natural.
2. Measure before you optimize. Use pandas or a simple timer to see where time is actually spent.
3. Keep an eye on this risk during code review: data leakage.
4. Ask a teammate to explain interaction features back to you; teaching is a quick way to find gaps.
5. Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce.
