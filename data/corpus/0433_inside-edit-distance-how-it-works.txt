Inside Edit Distance: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Edit Distance, step by step. Many students first meet Edit Distance in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to a measure of how many single-character changes turn one word into another.

The vocabulary of Edit Distance is small. Learn insertions, deletions, substitutions, and transpositions, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching transpositions on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

First, insertions sets the stage for everything else. Next, deletions builds on it. After that, substitutions comes into play, and finally transpositions ties the pieces together. Seeing the sequence once, with a tiny example, makes the whole topic easier to remember.
