Image Compression for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Image Compression.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Image Compression in your own words.
2. Describe lossy and lossless formats, quantization, transform coding, and quality settings.
3. Give examples from web images and photo storage.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail. Then draw packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is repeated recompression a problem? How does quantization relate to transform coding? When would you choose ImageMagick or mozjpeg?

Study habit worth keeping: pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.

When you feel ready, compare Image Compression with Computer Vision Basics and write down three differences.
