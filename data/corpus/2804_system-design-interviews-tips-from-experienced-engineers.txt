System Design Interviews Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with System Design Interviews regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for whiteboard sketches first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Excalidraw.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit scaling estimates before anything else.
3. Do not skip requirements; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of preparing for senior roles early, before the design hardens.
5. Write down why you chose System Design Interviews so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to practicing architectural thinking as well.
