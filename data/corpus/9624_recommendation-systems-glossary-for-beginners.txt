Recommendation Systems Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Recommendation Systems feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Recommendation Systems: systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.
collaborative filtering: a core term, most visible in streaming services.
content-based filtering: a core term that beginners often confuse with ranking.
ranking: a core term that connects to the cold start problem.
the cold start problem: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Surprise: a tool commonly used for online shops.

Come back to this Recommendation Systems list whenever documentation for implicit uses a word you do not recognize.
