Wi-Fi Networks for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Wi-Fi Networks.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Wi-Fi Networks in your own words.
2. Describe SSIDs, channels and bands, WPA encryption, and signal strength.
3. Give examples from home internet and office coverage.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Wi-Fi Networks is wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals. Then draw a conversation in a crowded room where everyone must find a quiet channel on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is overlapping channels a problem? How does channels and bands relate to WPA encryption? When would you choose Wi-Fi analyzer apps or Wireshark?

Study habit worth keeping: use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.

When you feel ready, compare Wi-Fi Networks with Network Troubleshooting and write down three differences.
