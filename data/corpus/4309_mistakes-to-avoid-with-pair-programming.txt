Mistakes to Avoid with Pair Programming

Everyone learns Pair Programming partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: one person dominating. It usually appears when someone skips driver and navigator and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: fatigue from long sessions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of teaching new teammates. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: pairing on every task. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on shared ownership to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.
