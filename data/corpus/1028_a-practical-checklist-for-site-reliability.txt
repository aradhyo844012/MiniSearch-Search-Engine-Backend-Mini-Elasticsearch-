A Practical Checklist for Site Reliability

Use this checklist before you rely on Site Reliability in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers service level objectives.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as PagerDuty or Prometheus.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example running production services.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Guard against this risk: alerting on everything.
6. Guard against this risk: no written postmortems.
7. Review how Site Reliability interacts with Serverless Computing.
8. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on error budgets.
