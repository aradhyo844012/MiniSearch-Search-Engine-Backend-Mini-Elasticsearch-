API Gateways in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through API Gateways.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: API Gateways is services that sit in front of APIs to handle routing, authentication and limits.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn routing, authentication, rate limits, and request transformation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a reception desk that checks visitors and sends them to the right office. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through exposing microservices, securing public APIs, and collecting usage metrics.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for a single point of failure and business logic leaking into the gateway, then try Kong for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep the gateway thin and put business logic in services.
