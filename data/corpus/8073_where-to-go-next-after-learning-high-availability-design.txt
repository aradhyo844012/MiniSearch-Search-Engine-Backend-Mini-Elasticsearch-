Where to Go Next After Learning High Availability Design

You have met the basics of High Availability Design. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Virtual Private Clouds, which belongs to the same area as High Availability Design (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of redundancy, failover, health checks or no single point of failure, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around banking systems with load balancers, and note every problem you meet, starting with hidden single points of failure.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining High Availability Design to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
