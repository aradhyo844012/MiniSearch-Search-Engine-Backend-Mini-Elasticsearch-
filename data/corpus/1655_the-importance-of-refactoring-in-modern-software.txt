The Importance of Refactoring in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Refactoring until something goes wrong without it. When people talk about Refactoring, they usually mean improving the structure of existing code without changing its behavior. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Readable code saves more time than clever code over the long run.

You will see Refactoring whenever people work on reducing duplication or preparing for new features. It also helps with simplifying complex code.

Watch out for these problems: refactoring without tests; mixing refactoring with new features; and rewriting instead of improving.

A solid grasp of Refactoring also makes Technical Debt easier to understand.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Refactoring.
