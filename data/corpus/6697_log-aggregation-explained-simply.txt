Log Aggregation Explained Simply

Here is Log Aggregation without the jargon: it is collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place.

Think of Log Aggregation as gathering notes from every desk into a single searchable binder. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: shippers, indexing, retention, and search queries. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with shippers, then add indexing.

You will meet Log Aggregation in work such as troubleshooting across services, security investigations, and usage analysis.

One practical tip is to use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.

Once Log Aggregation feels comfortable, read about Service Mesh, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
