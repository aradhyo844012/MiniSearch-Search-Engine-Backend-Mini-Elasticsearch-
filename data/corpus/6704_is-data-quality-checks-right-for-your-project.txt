Is Data Quality Checks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Data Quality Checks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

It is a good fit when your work involves protecting dashboards or validating ingestion, and when your team understands null checks.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn uniqueness, or when you cannot manage these risks: alerts nobody reads, checks only at the end of pipelines, and no ownership of failures.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether freshness and schema validation are part of the answer. If they are, Data Quality Checks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with catching pipeline bugs and check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
