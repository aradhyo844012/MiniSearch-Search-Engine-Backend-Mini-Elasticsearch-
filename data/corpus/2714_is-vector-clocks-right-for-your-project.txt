Is Vector Clocks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Vector Clocks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Vector Clocks is counters that let distributed processes track the order of events without a shared clock.

It is a good fit when your work involves detecting concurrent edits or replicated databases, and when your team understands logical time.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn per-process counters, or when you cannot manage these risks: clocks that grow with many nodes, confusing them with wall-clock time, and never pruning old entries.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether the happened-before relation and conflict detection are part of the answer. If they are, Vector Clocks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with collaborative tools and compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
