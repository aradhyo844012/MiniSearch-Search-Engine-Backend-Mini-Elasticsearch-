A/B Testing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make A/B Testing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

A/B Testing: an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.
control and variant: a core term, most visible in testing page designs.
random assignment: a core term that beginners often confuse with metrics.
metrics: a core term that connects to sample size.
sample size: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Optimizely: a tool commonly used for comparing ranking algorithms.

Come back to this A/B Testing list whenever documentation for GrowthBook uses a word you do not recognize.
