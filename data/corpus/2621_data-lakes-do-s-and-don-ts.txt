Data Lakes Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Data Lakes.

Do:
- Learn raw storage before anything else.
- Try Amazon S3 on a small example.
- Make it a habit to register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.
- Test the case of storing logs and events early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: turning into a data swamp.
- Ignore this: no catalog or ownership.
- Ignore this: poor access controls.
- Adopt Data Lakes just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Data Lakes, a little patience with schema on read pays off more than any clever shortcut.
