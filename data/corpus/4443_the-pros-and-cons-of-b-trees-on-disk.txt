The Pros and Cons of B-Trees on Disk

No technology is free of trade-offs, and B-Trees on Disk is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports database indexes
- Makes file system directories easier
- Helps with range scans

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: random writes on fragmented pages
- Risk: oversized keys
- Risk: ignoring page fill factor

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as database indexes, and you can manage the risks, B-Trees on Disk is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page.
