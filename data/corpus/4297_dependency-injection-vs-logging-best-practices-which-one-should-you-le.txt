Dependency Injection vs Logging Best Practices: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Dependency Injection or Logging Best Practices first. Both sit in the area of Backend Engineering, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them, and it is closely tied to constructor injection and interfaces. Logging Best Practices, by contrast, is recording useful, structured information about what an application is doing, and it is closely tied to log levels and structured logs.

Choose Dependency Injection first if you care about easier unit testing or swapping implementations. Choose Logging Best Practices first if you care about debugging production issues or auditing actions.

Think of Dependency Injection as a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them, and of Logging Best Practices as a ship's logbook that explains what happened and when. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Dependency Injection, avoid hiding too much behind containers. With Logging Best Practices, avoid logging sensitive data.

A good habit for Dependency Injection is to inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes. A good habit for Logging Best Practices is to include a request ID in every log line.
