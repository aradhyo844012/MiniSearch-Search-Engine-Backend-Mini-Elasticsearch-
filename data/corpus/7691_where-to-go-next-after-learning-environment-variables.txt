Where to Go Next After Learning Environment Variables

You have met the basics of Environment Variables. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Disk Partitioning and Mounting, which belongs to the same area as Environment Variables (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of variable names, scope, export or shell startup files, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around selecting program paths with env, and note every problem you meet, starting with committing secrets in dotfiles.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Environment Variables to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
