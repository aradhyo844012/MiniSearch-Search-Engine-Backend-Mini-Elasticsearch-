Scaling Considerations for API Gateways

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how API Gateways changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding routing and getting exposing microservices right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for a single point of failure and business logic leaking into the gateway, and measure with Kong before changing anything.

Large scale: rate limits and request transformation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around securing public APIs at this stage.

Before you scale, keep the gateway thin and put business logic in services. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
