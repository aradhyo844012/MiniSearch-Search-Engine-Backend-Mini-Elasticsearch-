Regression Testing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Regression Testing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Regression Testing is re-running tests after changes to make sure old features still work.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn test suites, automation, test selection, and bug reproduction tests.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine rechecking the old keys after cutting a new lock. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through protecting releases, verifying bug fixes, and refactoring safely.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for slow suites that nobody runs and missing tests for fixed bugs, then try pytest for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to add a test for every bug you fix.
