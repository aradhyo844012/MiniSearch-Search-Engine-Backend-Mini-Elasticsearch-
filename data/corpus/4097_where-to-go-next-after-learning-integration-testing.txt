Where to Go Next After Learning Integration Testing

You have met the basics of Integration Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Code Coverage, which belongs to the same area as Integration Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of test databases, service boundaries, fixtures or test environments, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around catching contract mismatches with Testcontainers, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow suites.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Integration Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
