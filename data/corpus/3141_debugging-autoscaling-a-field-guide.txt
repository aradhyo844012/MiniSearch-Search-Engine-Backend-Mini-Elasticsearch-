Debugging Autoscaling: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Autoscaling, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: scaling too slowly, flapping because of short spikes, and ignoring database limits.
Step 3: revisit scaling metrics and cooldown periods, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Kubernetes HPA or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Autoscaling in work such as handling traffic spikes come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, load test the system to find realistic thresholds. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
