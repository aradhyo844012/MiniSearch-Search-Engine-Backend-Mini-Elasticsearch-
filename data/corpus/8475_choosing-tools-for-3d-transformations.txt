Choosing Tools for 3D Transformations

There is no single best tool for 3D Transformations. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GLM is a common first choice, especially for games. three.js suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Blender is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for 3D Transformations by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle modeling tools well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for 3D Transformations, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: wrong multiplication order.

As a rule of thumb, write down the order of transformations before multiplying, no matter which tool you use.
