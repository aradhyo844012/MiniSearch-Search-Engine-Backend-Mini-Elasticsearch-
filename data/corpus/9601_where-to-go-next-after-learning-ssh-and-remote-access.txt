Where to Go Next After Learning SSH and Remote Access

You have met the basics of SSH and Remote Access. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Process Signals and Job Control, which belongs to the same area as SSH and Remote Access (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of key pairs, host keys, port forwarding or config files, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around copying files securely with OpenSSH, and note every problem you meet, starting with password logins left enabled.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining SSH and Remote Access to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
