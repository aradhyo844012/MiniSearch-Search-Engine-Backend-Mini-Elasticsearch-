Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Flutter

This path is for a learner who has never studied Flutter before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase) and learn widgets and state.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Dart, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving cross-platform mobile apps.

Stage 3, depth: study hot reload and the render tree, then deliberately explore this problem: deep widget trees.

Stage 4, application: apply Flutter to desktop apps, compare Flutter DevTools with Android Studio, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Flutter to someone else, and then move on to Bluetooth Low Energy.
