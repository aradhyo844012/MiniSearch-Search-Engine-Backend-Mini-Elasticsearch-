Skills to Build Around End-to-End Testing

Knowing the definition of End-to-End Testing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with browser automation and user flows, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Playwright on a End-to-End Testing task, and try at least one alternative such as Cypress.

Judgment: learn to decide when End-to-End Testing fits a problem such as checking login and checkout flows, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a test drive of the whole car on a real road.

Habits: cover only the most important user journeys, and write down what you learn after each project.
