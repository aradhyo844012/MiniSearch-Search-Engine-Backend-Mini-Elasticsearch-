The Pros and Cons of Test-Driven Development

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Test-Driven Development is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports designing small modules
- Makes preventing regressions easier
- Helps with gaining confidence for refactoring

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: writing tests that are too large
- Risk: skipping the refactor step
- Risk: testing implementation details

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as designing small modules, and you can manage the risks, Test-Driven Development is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep each red-green cycle to a few minutes.
