Skills to Build Around Anomaly Detection

Knowing the definition of Anomaly Detection is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with baselines and thresholds, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with scikit-learn on a Anomaly Detection task, and try at least one alternative such as PyOD.

Judgment: learn to decide when Anomaly Detection fits a problem such as fraud detection, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a security guard who notices the one person acting differently.

Habits: tune alerts using real examples of past incidents, and write down what you learn after each project.
