The Pros and Cons of Model Drift and Monitoring

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Model Drift and Monitoring is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports catching degrading models
- Makes scheduling retraining easier
- Helps with keeping predictions trustworthy

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: monitoring only system health
- Risk: having no ground truth labels
- Risk: alert fatigue

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as catching degrading models, and you can manage the risks, Model Drift and Monitoring is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, monitor input data as well as accuracy.
