Debugging Regression Testing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Regression Testing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: slow suites that nobody runs, missing tests for fixed bugs, and ignoring flaky failures.
Step 3: revisit test suites and automation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use pytest or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Regression Testing in work such as protecting releases come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, add a test for every bug you fix. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
