Debugging Arduino Basics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Arduino Basics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: wiring errors, blocking delays in the loop, and insufficient power for motors.
Step 3: revisit sketches and digital and analog pins, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Arduino IDE or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Arduino Basics in work such as reading sensors come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test each sensor on its own before combining them. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
