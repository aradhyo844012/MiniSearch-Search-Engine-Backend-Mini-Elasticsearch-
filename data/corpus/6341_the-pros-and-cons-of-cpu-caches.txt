The Pros and Cons of CPU Caches

No technology is free of trade-offs, and CPU Caches is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports speeding up loops
- Makes explaining performance differences easier
- Helps with designing data layouts

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: false sharing
- Risk: poor access patterns
- Risk: assuming memory is uniformly fast

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as speeding up loops, and you can manage the risks, CPU Caches is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, access arrays in the order they are stored.
