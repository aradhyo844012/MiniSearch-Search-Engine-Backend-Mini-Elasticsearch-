Rasterization Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Rasterization. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.
Key terms: triangles, pixel coverage, the depth buffer, and interpolation.
Typical uses: real-time games, user interfaces, and CAD previews.
Risks: aliasing along edges, overdraw, and depth fighting.
Tools: OpenGL, Vulkan, and WebGL.

Mental picture: coloring squares on graph paper to match a drawn outline.

Habit to build: draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.

See also: Image Compression, which sits close to Rasterization in the area of Graphics and Multimedia.
