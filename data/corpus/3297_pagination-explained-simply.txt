Pagination Explained Simply

If Pagination sounds intimidating, relax. It is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Pagination as reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: offset pagination, cursor pagination, page size, and stable ordering. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with offset pagination, then add cursor pagination.

People reach for Pagination when they work on search results, activity feeds, and admin tables.

One practical tip is to prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.

Once Pagination feels comfortable, read about API Versioning, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
