Choosing Tools for Skip Lists

There is no single best tool for Skip Lists. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Redis sorted sets is a common first choice, especially for ordered in-memory indexes. Java ConcurrentSkipListMap suits teams that already work with similar technology, and LevelDB is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle sorted sets in caches well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: poor random number choices.

As a rule of thumb, draw several levels by hand to see how a search moves down, no matter which tool you use.
