Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Digital Audio

This path is for a learner who has never studied Digital Audio before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second) and learn sampling rate and bit depth.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Audacity, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving music production.

Stage 3, depth: study waveforms and the Nyquist limit, then deliberately explore this problem: clipping from too high a level.

Stage 4, application: apply Digital Audio to voice calls, compare SoX with the Web Audio API, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Digital Audio to someone else, and then move on to Color Spaces.
