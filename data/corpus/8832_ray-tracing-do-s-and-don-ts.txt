Ray Tracing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Ray Tracing.

Do:
- Learn rays before anything else.
- Try Blender Cycles on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
- Test the case of movie visuals early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow render times.
- Ignore this: noise from too few samples.
- Ignore this: numerical precision errors.
- Adopt Ray Tracing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Ray Tracing, a little patience with intersections pays off more than any clever shortcut.
