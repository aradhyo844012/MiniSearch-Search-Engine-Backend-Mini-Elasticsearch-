The Pros and Cons of Quantum Computing Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Quantum Computing Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports research on cryptography
- Makes simulating molecules easier
- Helps with optimization studies

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: expecting speedups for every problem
- Risk: noise and errors
- Risk: hype about current machines

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as research on cryptography, and you can manage the risks, Quantum Computing Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
