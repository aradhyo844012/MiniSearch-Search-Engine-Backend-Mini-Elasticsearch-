Sprite Animation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Sprite Animation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: inconsistent frame timing.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit frames before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Sprite Animation so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip sprite sheets; everything else builds on it.
5. Make sure you can pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to user interface effects as well as to Sprite Animation in general.
