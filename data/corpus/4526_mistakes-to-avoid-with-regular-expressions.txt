Mistakes to Avoid with Regular Expressions

Everyone learns Regular Expressions partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: catastrophic backtracking. It usually appears when someone skips character classes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: patterns that are too greedy. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of validating input. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: using them to parse HTML. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on anchors to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: build patterns in small steps and test each one.
