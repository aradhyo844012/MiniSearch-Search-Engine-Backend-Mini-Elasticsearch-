Software Estimation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Software Estimation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn story points, ranges, uncertainty, and velocity.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through sprint planning, setting deadlines, and negotiating scope.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for treating estimates as promises and ignoring unknowns, then try planning poker for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to give ranges instead of single numbers.
