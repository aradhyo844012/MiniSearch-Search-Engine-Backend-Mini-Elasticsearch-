Is Graph Theory Basics Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Graph Theory Basics, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them.

It is a good fit when your work involves social networks or maps, and when your team understands vertices and edges.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn degree, or when you cannot manage these risks: mixing directed and undirected graphs, ignoring disconnected parts, and choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether paths and cycles and connectivity are part of the answer. If they are, Graph Theory Basics is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with dependency analysis and draw a small example before writing code.
