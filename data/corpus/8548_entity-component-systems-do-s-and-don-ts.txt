Entity Component Systems Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Entity Component Systems.

Do:
- Learn entities before anything else.
- Try Bevy on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep components as plain data.
- Test the case of large simulations early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overengineering small games.
- Ignore this: components that hold logic.
- Ignore this: hard debugging of system order.
- Adopt Entity Component Systems just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Entity Component Systems, a little patience with components pays off more than any clever shortcut.
