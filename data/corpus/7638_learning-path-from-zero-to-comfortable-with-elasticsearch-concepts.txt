Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Elasticsearch Concepts

This path is for a learner who has never studied Elasticsearch Concepts before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents) and learn indexes and shards.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Elasticsearch, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving site search.

Stage 3, depth: study analyzers and queries, then deliberately explore this problem: too many shards.

Stage 4, application: apply Elasticsearch Concepts to log analytics, compare Kibana with OpenSearch, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Elasticsearch Concepts to someone else, and then move on to Database Constraints.
