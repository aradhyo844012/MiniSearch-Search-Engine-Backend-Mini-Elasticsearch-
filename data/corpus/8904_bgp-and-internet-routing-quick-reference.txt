BGP and Internet Routing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for BGP and Internet Routing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.
Key terms: autonomous systems, route announcements, peering, and path selection.
Typical uses: connecting ISPs, directing traffic between large networks, and explaining internet outages.
Risks: route leaks, hijacked prefixes, and misconfigured filters.
Tools: bgpq4, BIRD, and RIPE Stat.

Mental picture: the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region.

Habit to build: filter route announcements strictly.
