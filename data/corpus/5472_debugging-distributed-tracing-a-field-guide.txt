Debugging Distributed Tracing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Distributed Tracing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: missing context propagation, sampling too aggressively, and overhead from too many spans.
Step 3: revisit spans and trace IDs, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use OpenTelemetry or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Distributed Tracing in work such as finding slow services come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
