Smart Contracts for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Smart Contracts.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Smart Contracts in your own words.
2. Describe contract code, transactions, gas fees, and immutability.
3. Give examples from token systems and automated escrow.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met. Then draw a vending machine that follows its rules with no clerk on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is bugs that cannot be patched a problem? How does transactions relate to gas fees? When would you choose Solidity or Hardhat?

Study habit worth keeping: audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.

When you feel ready, compare Smart Contracts with 5G Networks and write down three differences.
