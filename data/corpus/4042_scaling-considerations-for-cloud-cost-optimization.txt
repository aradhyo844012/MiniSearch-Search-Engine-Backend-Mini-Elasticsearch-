Scaling Considerations for Cloud Cost Optimization

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Cloud Cost Optimization changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding right-sizing and getting cutting idle resources right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for forgotten test environments and over-provisioned databases, and measure with AWS Cost Explorer before changing anything.

Large scale: tagging and usage reports move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around budgeting by team at this stage.

Before you scale, tag every resource with an owner. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
