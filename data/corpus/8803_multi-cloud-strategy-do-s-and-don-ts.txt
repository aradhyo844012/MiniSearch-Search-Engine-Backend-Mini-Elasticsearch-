Multi-Cloud Strategy Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Multi-Cloud Strategy.

Do:
- Learn portability before anything else.
- Try Terraform on a small example.
- Make it a habit to be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
- Test the case of avoiding single-provider risk early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: doubling operational complexity.
- Ignore this: lowest-common-denominator designs.
- Ignore this: hidden data transfer costs.
- Adopt Multi-Cloud Strategy just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Multi-Cloud Strategy, a little patience with vendor lock-in pays off more than any clever shortcut.
