Is Rasterization Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Rasterization, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Rasterization is converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.

It is a good fit when your work involves real-time games or user interfaces, and when your team understands triangles.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn pixel coverage, or when you cannot manage these risks: aliasing along edges, overdraw, and depth fighting.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether the depth buffer and interpolation are part of the answer. If they are, Rasterization is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with CAD previews and draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.
