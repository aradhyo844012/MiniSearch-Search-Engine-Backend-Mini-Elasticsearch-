Choosing Tools for Ray Tracing

There is no single best tool for Ray Tracing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Blender Cycles is a common first choice, especially for movie visuals. NVIDIA OptiX suits teams that already work with similar technology, and POV-Ray is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Ray Tracing by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle architectural visualization well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Ray Tracing, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow render times.

As a rule of thumb, start with spheres and a single light before adding features, no matter which tool you use.
