The Pros and Cons of Database Backups and Recovery

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Database Backups and Recovery is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports recovering deleted tables
- Makes migrating servers easier
- Helps with meeting compliance rules

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: never testing restores
- Risk: storing backups on the same disk
- Risk: keeping credentials inside backup scripts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as recovering deleted tables, and you can manage the risks, Database Backups and Recovery is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
