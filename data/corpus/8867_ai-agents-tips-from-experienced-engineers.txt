AI Agents Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with AI Agents regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip planning; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose AI Agents so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: runaway loops.
4. Reach for LangGraph first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as AutoGen.
5. Test the case of research assistants early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to workflow automation as well as to AI Agents in general.
