Interrupts Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Interrupts regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: long handlers.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit interrupt vectors before anything else.
3. Do not skip interrupt handlers; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of keyboard and mouse input early, before the design hardens.
5. Write down why you chose Interrupts so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to timers as well.
