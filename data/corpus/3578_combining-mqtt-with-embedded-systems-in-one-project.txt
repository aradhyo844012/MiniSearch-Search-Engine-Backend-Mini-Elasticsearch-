Combining MQTT with Embedded Systems in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how MQTT and Embedded Systems can work together in a single project.

MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks. Embedded Systems is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits.

Imagine a project that involves sensor networks and appliances. MQTT brings publish and subscribe, while Embedded Systems brings microcontrollers.

A sensible plan is to start with Mosquitto for the MQTT part and C for the Embedded Systems part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: messy topic names on one side, and no plan for updates on the other.

Picture the whole as a radio station with channels that devices can tune into, working alongside a small brain inside an appliance that only knows one job.

Two habits help: first, design topic names like a clear folder structure; second, plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
