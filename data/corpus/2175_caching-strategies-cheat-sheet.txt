Caching Strategies Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Caching Strategies.

- Definition: approaches for storing computed results so they can be reused instead of recalculated.
- Core ideas: cache-aside; write-through; time to live; invalidation.
- Use it for: speeding up read-heavy APIs; reducing database load; serving repeated searches.
- Watch out for: stale data; cache stampedes; unbounded memory use.
- Tools: Redis, Memcached, HTTP cache headers.
- Related topic: Rate Limiting.
- Quick tip: practice cache-aside until it feels automatic.
