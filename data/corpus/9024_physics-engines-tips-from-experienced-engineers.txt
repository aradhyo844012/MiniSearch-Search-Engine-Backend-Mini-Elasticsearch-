Physics Engines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Physics Engines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Physics Engines so the next person understands the decision.
2. Do not skip rigid bodies; everything else builds on it.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit forces and impulses before anything else.
4. Test the case of vehicles and ragdolls early, before the design hardens.
5. Be careful with this one: variable time steps.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to puzzle games as well as to Physics Engines in general.
