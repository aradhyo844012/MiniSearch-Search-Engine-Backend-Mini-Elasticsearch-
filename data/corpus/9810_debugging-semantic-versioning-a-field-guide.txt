Debugging Semantic Versioning: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Semantic Versioning, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: breaking changes in minor releases, overly loose ranges, and forgetting to document changes.
Step 3: revisit major, minor and patch numbers and breaking changes, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use semver.org or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Semantic Versioning in work such as publishing libraries come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, write a changelog entry with every release. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
