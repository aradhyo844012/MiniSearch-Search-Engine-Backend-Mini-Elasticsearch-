The Trade-offs of Autocomplete

Every design choice has a cost, and Autocomplete is no exception. Many students first meet Autocomplete in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to suggesting likely completions while a person types a query.

On the positive side, Autocomplete is well suited to search boxes and command palettes. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: slow suggestions; offensive or irrelevant completions; ignoring typos in the prefix. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much prefixes and ranking suggestions matter to your project.

Three issues come up again and again: slow suggestions; offensive or irrelevant completions; and ignoring typos in the prefix. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.
