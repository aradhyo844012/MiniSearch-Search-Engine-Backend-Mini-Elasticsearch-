Where to Go Next After Learning Cloud Cost Optimization

You have met the basics of Cloud Cost Optimization. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Disaster Recovery, which belongs to the same area as Cloud Cost Optimization (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of right-sizing, reserved capacity, tagging or usage reports, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around budgeting by team with AWS Cost Explorer, and note every problem you meet, starting with forgotten test environments.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Cloud Cost Optimization to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
