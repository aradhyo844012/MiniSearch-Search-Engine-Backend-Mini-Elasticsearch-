Is MLOps Pipelines Right for Your Project?

Before adopting MLOps Pipelines, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that MLOps Pipelines is automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models.

It is a good fit when your work involves repeatable model releases or tracking experiments, and when your team understands data versioning.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn training pipelines, or when you cannot manage these risks: untracked data changes, manual deployment steps, and no testing of data.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether the model registry and continuous delivery are part of the answer. If they are, MLOps Pipelines is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with rolling back models and version both the code and the data.
