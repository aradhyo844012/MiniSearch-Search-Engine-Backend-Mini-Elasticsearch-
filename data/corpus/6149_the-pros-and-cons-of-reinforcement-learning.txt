The Pros and Cons of Reinforcement Learning

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Reinforcement Learning is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports game playing
- Makes robot control easier
- Helps with recommendation strategies

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: badly designed rewards
- Risk: slow training
- Risk: unstable results across runs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as game playing, and you can manage the risks, Reinforcement Learning is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with simple environments.
