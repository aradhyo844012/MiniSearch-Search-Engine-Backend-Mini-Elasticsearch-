Choosing Tools for Edge Computing

There is no single best tool for Edge Computing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

AWS IoT Greengrass is a common first choice, especially for factory sensors. K3s suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Cloudflare Workers is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Edge Computing by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle video analytics well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Edge Computing, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: hard-to-update devices.

As a rule of thumb, decide which data must stay local before designing the system, no matter which tool you use.
