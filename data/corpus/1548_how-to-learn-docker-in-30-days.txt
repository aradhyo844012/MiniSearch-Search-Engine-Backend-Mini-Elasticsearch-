How to Learn Docker in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Docker. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Docker covers a platform for building and running applications in containers. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn Dockerfile and image layers, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Docker Compose on a small task such as reproducible development setups.
Week 3: build a mini project around shipping applications.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as huge images, and explain Docker to a friend.

Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes. Cloud resources are easy to create and just as easy to forget, which makes costs grow quietly.

In practice you can start with Docker Compose, then explore Docker Hub or Podman as your needs grow.

With steady practice, Docker will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
