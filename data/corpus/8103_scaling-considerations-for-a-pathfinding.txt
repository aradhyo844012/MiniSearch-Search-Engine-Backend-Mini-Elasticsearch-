Scaling Considerations for A* Pathfinding

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how A* Pathfinding changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding open and closed sets and getting game characters right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for overestimating the heuristic and slow searches on huge maps, and measure with Unity NavMesh before changing anything.

Large scale: heuristics and grids and graphs move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around robot navigation at this stage.

Before you scale, choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
