Unit Testing: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Unit Testing?
A: The term Unit Testing refers to writing small automated tests that check one piece of code at a time.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include pytest, unittest, and coverage.py.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: testing implementation details; slow tests; and tests that depend on each other.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with test cases and assertions, then move on to fixtures and mocking.

Q: Why do developers use Unit Testing?
A: Common uses include catching regressions, documenting behavior, and refactoring safely.
