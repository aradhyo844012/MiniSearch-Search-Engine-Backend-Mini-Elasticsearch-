How to Learn Natural Language Processing in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Natural Language Processing. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Natural Language Processing, they usually mean the field of teaching computers to work with human language. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn tokenization and embeddings, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with spaCy on a small task such as search engines.
Week 3: build a mini project around chatbots.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as ignoring language variety, and explain Natural Language Processing to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include spaCy, NLTK, and Hugging Face.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Natural Language Processing.
