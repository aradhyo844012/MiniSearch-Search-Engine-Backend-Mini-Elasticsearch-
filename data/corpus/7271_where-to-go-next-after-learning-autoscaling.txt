Where to Go Next After Learning Autoscaling

You have met the basics of Autoscaling. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Log Aggregation, which belongs to the same area as Autoscaling (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of scaling metrics, cooldown periods, minimum and maximum limits or horizontal scaling, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around saving money overnight with Kubernetes HPA, and note every problem you meet, starting with scaling too slowly.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Autoscaling to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
