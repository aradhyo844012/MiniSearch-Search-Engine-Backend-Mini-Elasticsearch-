Mistakes to Avoid with Convolutional Neural Networks

Everyone learns Convolutional Neural Networks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too little training data. It usually appears when someone skips filters and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring data augmentation. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of image classification. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: overly deep models for simple tasks. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on stride to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start from a pretrained model.
