Error Handling Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Error Handling.

Q: Explain Error Handling in simple words.
A: Error Handling refers to planning for failures so that programs respond clearly instead of crashing. I would add an example involving reading files.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: swallowing exceptions silently; catching overly broad errors; and unhelpful error messages.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: swallowing exceptions silently.

Q: How does it connect to Type Systems?
A: Both belong to the area of Programming, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with calling networks, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Python try and except, logging module, and Sentry.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: exceptions, validation, logging, and retries.
