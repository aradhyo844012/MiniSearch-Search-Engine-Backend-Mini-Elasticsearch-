The Pros and Cons of Two Pointers and Sliding Window

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Two Pointers and Sliding Window is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports finding pairs with a given sum
- Makes longest substring problems easier
- Helps with merging sorted arrays

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: moving the wrong pointer
- Risk: forgetting empty inputs
- Risk: breaking the invariant

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as finding pairs with a given sum, and you can manage the risks, Two Pointers and Sliding Window is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, write down the window invariant before coding the loop.
