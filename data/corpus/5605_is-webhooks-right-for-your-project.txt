Is Webhooks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Webhooks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens.

It is a good fit when your work involves payment notifications or CI triggers, and when your team understands event payloads.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn signature verification, or when you cannot manage these risks: not verifying signatures, slow handlers causing timeouts, and processing duplicate events.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether retries and endpoints are part of the answer. If they are, Webhooks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with chat integrations and respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
