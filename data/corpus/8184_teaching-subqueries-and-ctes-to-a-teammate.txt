Teaching Subqueries and CTEs to a Teammate

Explaining Subqueries and CTEs to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving breaking complex reports into steps, naming subqueries and WITH clauses as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain correlated queries in Subqueries and CTEs back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, correlated subqueries that run slowly, and suggest this habit: name each CTE after what it returns.
