Teaching Discrete Mathematics to a Teammate

Explaining Discrete Mathematics to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: the grammar underneath every program. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving algorithm analysis, naming sets and logic as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain relations in Discrete Mathematics back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, skipping proofs, and suggest this habit: practice writing small proofs by hand.
