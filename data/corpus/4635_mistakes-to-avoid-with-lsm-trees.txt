Mistakes to Avoid with LSM Trees

Everyone learns LSM Trees partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: read amplification. It usually appears when someone skips memtable and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: compaction stalls. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of write-heavy databases. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor bloom filter tuning. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on write amplification to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: watch compaction metrics before blaming the application.
