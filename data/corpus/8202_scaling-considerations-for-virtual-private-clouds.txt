Scaling Considerations for Virtual Private Clouds

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Virtual Private Clouds changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding subnets and getting separating public and private services right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for overlapping address ranges and databases in public subnets, and measure with AWS VPC before changing anything.

Large scale: security groups and gateways move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around connecting to offices at this stage.

Before you scale, keep databases in private subnets. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
