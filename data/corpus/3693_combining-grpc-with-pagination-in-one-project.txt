Combining gRPC with Pagination in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how gRPC and Pagination can work together in a single project.

gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts. Pagination is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time.

Imagine a project that involves service-to-service calls and search results. gRPC brings protocol buffers, while Pagination brings offset pagination.

A sensible plan is to start with protoc for the gRPC part and SQL LIMIT and OFFSET for the Pagination part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: poor browser support without a proxy on one side, and unstable sort order on the other.

Picture the whole as a phone line with a pre-agreed script for every call, working alongside reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark.

Two habits help: first, never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file; second, prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
