Practice Exercises for DHCP

These exercises build real understanding of DHCP. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how reservations and discover and offer messages differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Compare ISC Kea and Wireshark for booting machines over a network and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing DHCP and include the term leases.
Exercise 4: Pick dnsmasq and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble home and office networks or guest Wi-Fi.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: DHCP is a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings.
