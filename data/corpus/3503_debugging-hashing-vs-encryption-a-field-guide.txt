Debugging Hashing vs Encryption: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Hashing vs Encryption, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them, using broken hash functions, and confusing encoding with encryption.
Step 3: revisit one-way functions and keys, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use SHA-256 or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Hashing vs Encryption in work such as storing passwords come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, ask whether you will ever need the original value back. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
