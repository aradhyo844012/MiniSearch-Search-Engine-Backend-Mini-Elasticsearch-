Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Procedural Generation

This path is for a learner who has never studied Procedural Generation before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Procedural Generation is creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms) and learn random seeds and noise functions.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Perlin noise, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving roguelike dungeons.

Stage 3, depth: study rules and constraints and reproducibility, then deliberately explore this problem: levels that are unfair or unwinnable.

Stage 4, application: apply Procedural Generation to infinite terrain, compare Wave Function Collapse with Unity, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Procedural Generation to someone else, and then move on to Collision Detection.
