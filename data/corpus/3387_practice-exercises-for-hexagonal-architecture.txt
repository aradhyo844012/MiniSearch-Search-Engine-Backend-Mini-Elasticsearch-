Practice Exercises for Hexagonal Architecture

These exercises build real understanding of Hexagonal Architecture. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble testable business logic or swapping databases.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: too many layers for small apps. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Hexagonal Architecture and include the term ports.
Exercise 4: Compare FastAPI and pytest for supporting several interfaces and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how the core domain and dependency direction differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Hexagonal Architecture is a design that keeps core logic separate from outside systems through ports and adapters.
