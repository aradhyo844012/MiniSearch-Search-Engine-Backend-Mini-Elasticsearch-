Practice Exercises for Data Anonymization

These exercises build real understanding of Data Anonymization. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Faker and Google's differential privacy library for meeting privacy rules and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble sharing datasets with researchers or testing with production-like data.
Exercise 3: Pick ARX and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: re-identification through combined fields. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Explain how k-anonymity and differential privacy differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.
