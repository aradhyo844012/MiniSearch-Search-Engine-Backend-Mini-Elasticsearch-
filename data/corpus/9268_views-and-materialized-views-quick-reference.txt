Views and Materialized Views Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Views and Materialized Views. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results.
Key terms: view definitions, refresh strategies, permissions, and dependencies.
Typical uses: simplifying repeated queries, hiding sensitive columns, and speeding up reports.
Risks: stale materialized data, views stacked on views, and surprising performance costs.
Tools: PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Snowflake.

Mental picture: a window cut into a wall that shows only part of the room.

Habit to build: document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
