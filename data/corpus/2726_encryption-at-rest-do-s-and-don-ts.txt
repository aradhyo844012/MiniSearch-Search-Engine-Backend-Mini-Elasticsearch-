Encryption at Rest Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Encryption at Rest.

Do:
- Learn symmetric keys before anything else.
- Try LUKS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep keys in a separate service from the data.
- Test the case of protecting backups early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: storing keys next to the data.
- Ignore this: no key rotation.
- Ignore this: assuming it stops every kind of attack.
- Adopt Encryption at Rest just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Encryption at Rest, a little patience with key management pays off more than any clever shortcut.
