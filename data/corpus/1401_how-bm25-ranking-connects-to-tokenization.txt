How BM25 Ranking Connects to Tokenization

BM25 Ranking and Tokenization are closely connected parts of Search and Retrieval. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving ranking search results may also involve indexing documents. In such a project, term saturation and lowercasing often influence each other.

Start with BM25 Ranking: the term refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. Then look at Tokenization, which refers to splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as enterprise search, and explain how splitting on punctuation would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For BM25 Ranking, watch for this: ignoring field boundaries. For Tokenization, watch for this: dropping useful numbers. Users judge a search engine by whether the right result appears near the top.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Elasticsearch or Python re module.
