Key Terms in Tokenization Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Tokenization much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. Put simply, Tokenization covers splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

lowercasing: a key term in Tokenization. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
splitting on punctuation: a key term in Tokenization. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
stop words: a key term in Tokenization. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
normalization: a key term in Tokenization. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.

In short, careful study of Tokenization pays off. Spend time on lowercasing and splitting on punctuation, and the rest will follow.
