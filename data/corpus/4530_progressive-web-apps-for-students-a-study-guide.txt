Progressive Web Apps for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Progressive Web Apps.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Progressive Web Apps in your own words.
2. Describe web app manifest, service worker, installability, and responsive design.
3. Give examples from offline-friendly tools and lightweight alternatives to native apps.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Progressive Web Apps is web applications that can be installed and work offline like native apps. Then draw a website that packs its own suitcase so it still works when the network is gone on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring platform limitations a problem? How does service worker relate to installability? When would you choose Lighthouse or Workbox?

Study habit worth keeping: test the app in airplane mode.

When you feel ready, compare Progressive Web Apps with Internationalization and write down three differences.
