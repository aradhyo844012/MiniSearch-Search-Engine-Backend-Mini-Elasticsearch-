The Pros and Cons of Environment Variables

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Environment Variables is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports storing configuration
- Makes selecting program paths easier
- Helps with passing settings to containers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: committing secrets in dotfiles
- Risk: variables not visible to child processes
- Risk: typos in names

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as storing configuration, and you can manage the risks, Environment Variables is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, print a variable to confirm its value before blaming the code.
