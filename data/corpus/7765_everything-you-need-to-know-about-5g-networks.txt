Everything You Need to Know About 5G Networks

This page collects the essentials about 5G Networks in one place. In short, 5G Networks is the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency.

Core concepts:
- spectrum bands
- latency
- network slicing
- small cells

Where it is used:
- mobile broadband
- connected vehicles
- industrial automation

Common problems:
- limited coverage on the fastest bands
- battery drain
- marketing exaggeration

Tools to know:
- speed test tools
- network simulators
- 3GPP documents

Remember this above all: a wider highway with more lanes and faster exits is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds.
