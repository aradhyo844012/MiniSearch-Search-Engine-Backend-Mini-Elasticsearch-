A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Twelve-Factor Apps

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Twelve-Factor Apps.

Morning: Imran starts by reviewing a ticket about building web services. Before touching any code, Imran re-reads the notes on config in the environment to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Imran pairs with a teammate to explore stateless processes with Heroku. A small test exposes the problem of storing state on local disks, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving deploying to platforms. Imran sketches a checklist for packing a suitcase that fits any airline on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Imran writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to keep config out of the code.

Days like this are typical. Twelve-Factor Apps is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
