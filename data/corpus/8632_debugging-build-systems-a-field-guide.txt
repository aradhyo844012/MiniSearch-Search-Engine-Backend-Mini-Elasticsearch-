Debugging Build Systems: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Build Systems, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: slow full rebuilds, undeclared dependencies, and machine-specific builds.
Step 3: revisit targets and dependencies, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Make or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Build Systems in work such as compiling large projects come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, make builds reproducible on any machine. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
