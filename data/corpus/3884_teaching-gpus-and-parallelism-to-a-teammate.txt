Teaching GPUs and Parallelism to a Teammate

Explaining GPUs and Parallelism to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stadium of workers each doing one small identical task. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: GPUs and Parallelism is processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving training neural networks, naming threads and blocks and kernels as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain memory transfers in GPUs and Parallelism back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow data transfers, and suggest this habit: minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.
