Skills to Build Around Computer Vision Basics

Knowing the definition of Computer Vision Basics is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with pixels and filters and edges, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with OpenCV on a Computer Vision Basics task, and try at least one alternative such as Pillow.

Judgment: learn to decide when Computer Vision Basics fits a problem such as face detection, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do.

Habits: visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks, and write down what you learn after each project.
