Scaling Considerations for Database Migrations

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Database Migrations changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding up and down scripts and getting adding columns safely right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for locking large tables and editing old migrations, and measure with Alembic before changing anything.

Large scale: backward compatibility and zero-downtime changes move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around renaming tables at this stage.

Before you scale, split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
