Bluetooth Low Energy Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Bluetooth Low Energy.

Do:
- Learn advertising before anything else.
- Try nRF Connect on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test with several phone models.
- Test the case of fitness trackers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring connection limits.
- Ignore this: weak pairing security.
- Ignore this: assuming long range.
- Adopt Bluetooth Low Energy just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Bluetooth Low Energy, a little patience with GATT services pays off more than any clever shortcut.
