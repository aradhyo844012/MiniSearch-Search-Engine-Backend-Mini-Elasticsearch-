Choosing Tools for Mobile App Security

There is no single best tool for Mobile App Security. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

OWASP MASVS is a common first choice, especially for banking apps. Android Keystore suits teams that already work with similar technology, and iOS Keychain is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle health apps well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: storing tokens in plain text.

As a rule of thumb, store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore, no matter which tool you use.
