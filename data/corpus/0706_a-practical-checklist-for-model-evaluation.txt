A Practical Checklist for Model Evaluation

Use this checklist before you rely on Model Evaluation in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: ignoring variance between runs.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example comparing models.
3. Review how Model Evaluation interacts with Natural Language Processing.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as scikit-learn metrics or MLflow.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Model Evaluation should accomplish.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers accuracy.
8. Guard against this risk: tuning on the test set.
9. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on precision and recall.
