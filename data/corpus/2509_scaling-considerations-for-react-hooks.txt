Scaling Considerations for React Hooks

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how React Hooks changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding useState and getting managing form input right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for missing dependencies in effects and calling hooks conditionally, and measure with React DevTools before changing anything.

Large scale: dependency arrays and custom hooks move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around fetching data on load at this stage.

Before you scale, enable the hooks lint rules from the start. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
