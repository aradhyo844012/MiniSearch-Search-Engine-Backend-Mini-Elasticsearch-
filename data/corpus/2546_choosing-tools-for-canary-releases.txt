Choosing Tools for Canary Releases

There is no single best tool for Canary Releases. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Argo Rollouts is a common first choice, especially for safer releases. Istio suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Flagger is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle testing with real traffic well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too little traffic to reveal problems.

As a rule of thumb, define rollback conditions before starting the rollout, no matter which tool you use.
