Reinforcement Learning Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Reinforcement Learning. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.
Key terms: agent, environment, reward, and policy.
Typical uses: game playing, robot control, and recommendation strategies.
Risks: badly designed rewards, slow training, and unstable results across runs.
Tools: Gymnasium, Stable-Baselines3, and PyTorch.

Mental picture: training a dog with treats for good behavior.

Habit to build: start with simple environments.
