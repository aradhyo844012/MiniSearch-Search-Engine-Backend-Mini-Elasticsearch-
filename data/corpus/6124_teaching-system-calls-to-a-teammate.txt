Teaching System Calls to a Teammate

Explaining System Calls to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a service window where a customer asks staff to do things in the back office. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: System Calls is the controlled doorway through which programs ask the operating system to do privileged work. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving reading and writing files, naming user mode and kernel mode and syscall numbers as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain file descriptors in System Calls back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, ignoring error codes, and suggest this habit: use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do.
