Scaling Considerations for Bash Scripting

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Bash Scripting changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding variables and getting automating deployments right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for unquoted variables and ignoring errors in pipelines, and measure with Bash before changing anything.

Large scale: exit codes and functions move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around processing text files at this stage.

Before you scale, start scripts with set -euo pipefail and quote every variable. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
