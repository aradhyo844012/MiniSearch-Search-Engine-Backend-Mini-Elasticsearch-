The Pros and Cons of API Design Principles

No technology is free of trade-offs, and API Design Principles is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports public platforms
- Makes internal service contracts easier
- Helps with mobile backends

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: leaking internal details
- Risk: inconsistent responses
- Risk: undocumented behavior

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as public platforms, and you can manage the risks, API Design Principles is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, write the documentation before the implementation.
