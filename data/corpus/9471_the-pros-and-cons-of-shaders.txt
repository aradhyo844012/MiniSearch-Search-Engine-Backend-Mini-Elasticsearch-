The Pros and Cons of Shaders

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Shaders is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports lighting effects
- Makes water and fire visuals easier
- Helps with post-processing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: branches that slow the GPU
- Risk: precision errors
- Risk: platform differences

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as lighting effects, and you can manage the risks, Shaders is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
