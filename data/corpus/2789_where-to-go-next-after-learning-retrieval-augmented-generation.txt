Where to Go Next After Learning Retrieval-Augmented Generation

You have met the basics of Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Convolutional Neural Networks, which belongs to the same area as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of retrieval, chunking, prompts with context or grounding, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around question answering over documents with LangChain, and note every problem you meet, starting with poor chunking.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Retrieval-Augmented Generation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
