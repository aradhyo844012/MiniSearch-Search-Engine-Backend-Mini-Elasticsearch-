Choosing Tools for Probability Basics

There is no single best tool for Probability Basics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python random is a common first choice, especially for randomized algorithms. NumPy suits teams that already work with similar technology, and simulation notebooks is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Probability Basics by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle machine learning well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Probability Basics, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: confusing independent and exclusive events.

As a rule of thumb, simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning, no matter which tool you use.
