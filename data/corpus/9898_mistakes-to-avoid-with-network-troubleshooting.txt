Mistakes to Avoid with Network Troubleshooting

Everyone learns Network Troubleshooting partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: changing several things at once. It usually appears when someone skips protocol layers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: blaming the application too early. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of fixing office connectivity. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring DNS. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on packet captures to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: work from the bottom layer upward.
