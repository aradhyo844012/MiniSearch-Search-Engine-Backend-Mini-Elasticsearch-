Topological Sorting Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Topological Sorting.

Do:
- Learn directed acyclic graphs before anything else.
- Try Python graphlib on a small example.
- Make it a habit to check for cycles first so you can report a clear error.
- Test the case of build systems early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring cycles.
- Ignore this: assuming the order is unique.
- Ignore this: mixing up edge directions.
- Adopt Topological Sorting just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Topological Sorting, a little patience with in-degree counts pays off more than any clever shortcut.
