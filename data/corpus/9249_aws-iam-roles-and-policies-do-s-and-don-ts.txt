AWS IAM Roles and Policies Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with AWS IAM Roles and Policies.

Do:
- Learn users and roles before anything else.
- Try IAM Access Analyzer on a small example.
- Make it a habit to grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.
- Test the case of securing deployments early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: wildcard permissions.
- Ignore this: long-lived access keys.
- Ignore this: unused roles.
- Adopt AWS IAM Roles and Policies just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with AWS IAM Roles and Policies, a little patience with policies pays off more than any clever shortcut.
