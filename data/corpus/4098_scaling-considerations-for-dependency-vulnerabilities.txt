Scaling Considerations for Dependency Vulnerabilities

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Dependency Vulnerabilities changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding CVE identifiers and getting auditing projects right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring transitive dependencies and never updating, and measure with Dependabot before changing anything.

Large scale: transitive dependencies and software bills of materials move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around automating updates at this stage.

Before you scale, automate update pull requests. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
