Speech Recognition in Real Projects

Reading about Speech Recognition is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: voice assistants. Here, audio features drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against accents and background noise.

Scenario two: captioning. This time acoustic models matters most, and tools such as Whisper help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: dictation. The key idea is language models, and the biggest risk is domain-specific vocabulary.

Across all three scenarios, Speech Recognition stays the same idea: converting spoken language into text using machine learning. Only the details change.
