Scaling Considerations for Robotics and ROS

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Robotics and ROS changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding sensors and getting warehouse robots right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring sensor noise and unsafe motion commands, and measure with ROS 2 before changing anything.

Large scale: nodes and topics and control loops move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around drones at this stage.

Before you scale, test in simulation before moving real hardware. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
