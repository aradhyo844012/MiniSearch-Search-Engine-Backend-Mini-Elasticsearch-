CQRS Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with CQRS regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit queries before anything else.
2. Write down why you chose CQRS so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: adding it to simple apps.
4. Test the case of high-read dashboards early, before the design hardens.
5. Do not skip commands; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to complex domains as well.
