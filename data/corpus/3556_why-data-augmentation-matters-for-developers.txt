Why Data Augmentation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Data Augmentation make better decisions, because Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

Three situations show why it matters. First, image models. Second, text classification. Third, small datasets. In each case, a solid grasp of transformations and label preservation separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: changes that alter the label, augmenting the test set, and unrealistic transformations.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Albumentations, torchvision, and nlpaug cover most needs.
