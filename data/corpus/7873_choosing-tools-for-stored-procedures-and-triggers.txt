Choosing Tools for Stored Procedures and Triggers

There is no single best tool for Stored Procedures and Triggers. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PL/pgSQL is a common first choice, especially for enforcing rules close to the data. MySQL Workbench suits teams that already work with similar technology, and pgTAP is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Stored Procedures and Triggers by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle audit logging well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Stored Procedures and Triggers, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: hidden logic that surprises developers.

As a rule of thumb, keep triggers small and well documented, no matter which tool you use.
