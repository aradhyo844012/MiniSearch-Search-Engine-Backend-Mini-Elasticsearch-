Mistakes to Avoid with Java Streams

Everyone learns Java Streams partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: reusing a consumed stream. It usually appears when someone skips map and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: side effects inside lambdas. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of transforming lists. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: using parallel streams without measuring. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on lazy evaluation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep each stream step small and readable.
