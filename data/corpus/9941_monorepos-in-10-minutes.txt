Monorepos in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Monorepos.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Monorepos is single repositories that hold the code for many projects.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn shared tooling, code ownership, selective builds, and atomic changes.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine one big toolbox for the whole workshop. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through sharing libraries across apps, coordinated refactors, and consistent tooling.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for slow checkouts and unclear ownership, then try Nx for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to set up selective builds and tests early.
