TF-IDF Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of TF-IDF.

- Definition: a scoring method that weights terms by how often they appear in a document and how rare they are overall.
- Core ideas: term frequency; inverse document frequency; weighting; normalization.
- Use it for: ranking search results; finding keywords; comparing documents.
- Watch out for: over-weighting common words; ignoring document length; using it on tiny corpora.
- Tools: scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer, Lucene, Gensim.
- Related topic: Stop Words.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for inverse document frequency before building anything large.
