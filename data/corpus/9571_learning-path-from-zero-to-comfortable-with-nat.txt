Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with NAT

This path is for a learner who has never studied NAT before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (NAT is a technique that lets many devices share one public IP address by rewriting addresses) and learn private addresses and translation tables.

Stage 2, hands-on: install iptables, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving home routers.

Stage 3, depth: study port mapping and port forwarding, then deliberately explore this problem: breaking peer-to-peer connections.

Stage 4, application: apply NAT to sharing limited IPv4 addresses, compare router admin pages with conntrack, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach NAT to someone else, and then move on to Firewalls.
