Jupyter Notebooks Explained Simply

If Jupyter Notebooks sounds intimidating, relax. It is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Jupyter Notebooks as a lab notebook that can run its own experiments. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: cells, kernels, markdown, and outputs. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with cells, then add kernels.

You will meet Jupyter Notebooks in work such as exploring data, teaching and demos, and sharing analysis.

One practical tip is to restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.

After Jupyter Notebooks, a natural next topic is Apache Spark.
