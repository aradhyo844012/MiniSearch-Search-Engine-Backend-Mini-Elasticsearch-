Understanding Webhooks Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch.

In that picture, event payloads is the first rule you follow, signature verification is the next step, and retries and endpoints keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens, and it involves details such as not verifying signatures that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for ngrok to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain payment notifications to a friend.
