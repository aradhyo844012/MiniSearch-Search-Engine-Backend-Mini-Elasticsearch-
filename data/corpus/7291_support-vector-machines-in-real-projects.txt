Support Vector Machines in Real Projects

Reading about Support Vector Machines is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: text classification. Here, margin drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against slow training on large data.

Scenario two: small high-dimensional datasets. This time support vectors matters most, and tools such as scikit-learn help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: image recognition baselines. The key idea is kernels, and the biggest risk is poor kernel choices.

Across all three scenarios, Support Vector Machines stays the same idea: models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes. Only the details change.
