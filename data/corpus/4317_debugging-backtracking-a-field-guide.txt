Debugging Backtracking: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Backtracking, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: forgetting to undo changes, missing pruning rules, and copying large state at every step.
Step 3: revisit decision tree and pruning, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python recursion or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Backtracking in work such as solving Sudoku come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, prune as early as possible because the savings multiply. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
