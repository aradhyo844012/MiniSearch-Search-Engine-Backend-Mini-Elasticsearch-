Load Testing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Load Testing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Load Testing so the next person understands the decision.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit throughput before anything else.
3. Do not skip virtual users; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of capacity planning early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to finding bottlenecks as well.
