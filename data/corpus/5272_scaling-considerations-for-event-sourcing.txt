Scaling Considerations for Event Sourcing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Event Sourcing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding event log and getting audit trails right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for changing event formats carelessly and unbounded replay times, and measure with Apache Kafka before changing anything.

Large scale: snapshots and replay move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around financial ledgers at this stage.

Before you scale, version your events from the first day. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
