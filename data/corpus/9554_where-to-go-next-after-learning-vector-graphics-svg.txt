Where to Go Next After Learning Vector Graphics (SVG)

You have met the basics of Vector Graphics (SVG). Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Ray Tracing, which belongs to the same area as Vector Graphics (SVG) (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of paths, the viewBox, fill and stroke or transforms, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around icons with Inkscape, and note every problem you meet, starting with overly complex paths.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Vector Graphics (SVG) to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
