Where to Go Next After Learning CSS Variables

You have met the basics of CSS Variables. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Hooks, which belongs to the same area as CSS Variables (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of custom properties, the var function, inheritance or fallback values, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around design systems with browser DevTools, and note every problem you meet, starting with missing fallbacks.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining CSS Variables to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
