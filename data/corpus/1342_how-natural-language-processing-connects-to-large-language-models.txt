How Natural Language Processing Connects to Large Language Models

Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models are closely connected parts of Machine Learning. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving search engines may also involve drafting text. In such a project, tokenization and tokens often influence each other.

Start with Natural Language Processing: the term refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language. Then look at Large Language Models, which refers to very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as chatbots, and explain how attention would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Natural Language Processing, watch for this: messy text preprocessing. For Large Language Models, watch for this: vague prompts. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using spaCy or Hugging Face.
