Eventual Consistency Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Eventual Consistency regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip replication delay; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose Eventual Consistency so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: assuming reads are always fresh.
4. Reach for Amazon DynamoDB first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Apache Cassandra.
5. Test the case of global social feeds early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to shopping cart services as well.
