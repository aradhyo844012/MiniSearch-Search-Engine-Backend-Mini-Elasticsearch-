Teaching VPNs to a Teammate

Explaining VPNs to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a private tunnel under a public road. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving remote work access, naming tunneling and encryption as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain authentication in VPNs back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, assuming it makes you anonymous, and suggest this habit: test for DNS leaks after connecting.
