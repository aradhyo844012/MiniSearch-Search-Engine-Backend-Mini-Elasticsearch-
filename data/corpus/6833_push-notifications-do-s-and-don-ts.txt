Push Notifications Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Push Notifications.

Do:
- Learn device tokens before anything else.
- Try Firebase Cloud Messaging on a small example.
- Make it a habit to ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
- Test the case of order updates early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: asking for permission too early.
- Ignore this: sending too many messages.
- Ignore this: ignoring token refresh.
- Adopt Push Notifications just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Push Notifications, a little patience with push services pays off more than any clever shortcut.
