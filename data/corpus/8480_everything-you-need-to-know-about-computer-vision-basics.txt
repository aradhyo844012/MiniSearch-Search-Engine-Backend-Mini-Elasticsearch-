Everything You Need to Know About Computer Vision Basics

This page collects the essentials about Computer Vision Basics in one place. In short, Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video.

Core concepts:
- pixels
- filters and edges
- feature detection
- classification

Where it is used:
- face detection
- document scanning
- quality inspection

Common problems:
- lighting changes
- biased datasets
- overfitting to a small set of photos

Tools to know:
- OpenCV
- Pillow
- scikit-image

Remember this above all: teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
