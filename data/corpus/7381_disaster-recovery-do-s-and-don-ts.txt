Disaster Recovery Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Disaster Recovery.

Do:
- Learn recovery time objective before anything else.
- Try AWS Backup on a small example.
- Make it a habit to run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
- Test the case of surviving regional outages early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: untested plans.
- Ignore this: outdated runbooks.
- Ignore this: dependencies missed in the plan.
- Adopt Disaster Recovery just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Disaster Recovery, a little patience with recovery point objective pays off more than any clever shortcut.
