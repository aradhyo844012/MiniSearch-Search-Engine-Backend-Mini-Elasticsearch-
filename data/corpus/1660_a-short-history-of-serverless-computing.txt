A Short History of Serverless Computing

Ideas behind Serverless Computing developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. The term Serverless Computing refers to running code in managed functions without maintaining servers yourself. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes.

The vocabulary of Serverless Computing is small. Learn functions, event triggers, cold starts, and pay per use, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching pay per use on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.
