Code Editors and IDEs in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Code Editors and IDEs.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Code Editors and IDEs is tools for writing, navigating and debugging code.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn syntax highlighting, autocomplete, debugger integration, and extensions.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a well-organized workbench. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through daily development, navigating large codebases, and refactoring.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for installing too many extensions and ignoring keyboard shortcuts, then try VS Code for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to learn the shortcuts for the actions you repeat most.
