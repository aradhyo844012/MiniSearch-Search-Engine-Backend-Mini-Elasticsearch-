Finite Automata in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Finite Automata.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Finite Automata is simple abstract machines that read input and move between a fixed set of states.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn states, transitions, accepting states, and regular languages.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a turnstile that moves between locked and unlocked. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through tokenizers, pattern matching, and protocol design.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for missing transitions and confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines, then try JFLAP for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to draw the state diagram before writing any code.
