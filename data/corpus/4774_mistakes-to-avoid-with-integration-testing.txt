Mistakes to Avoid with Integration Testing

Everyone learns Integration Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow suites. It usually appears when someone skips test databases and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: flaky external dependencies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of verifying API and database interaction. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unclear failures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on test environments to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use disposable containers for test dependencies.
