Property-Based Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Property-Based Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Property-Based Testing in your own words.
2. Describe properties, generators, shrinking, and invariants.
3. Give examples from testing parsers and checking sorting and math functions.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold. Then draw a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is vague properties a problem? How does generators relate to shrinking? When would you choose Hypothesis or QuickCheck?

Study habit worth keeping: save failing examples as regular tests.

When you feel ready, compare Property-Based Testing with Code Coverage and write down three differences.
