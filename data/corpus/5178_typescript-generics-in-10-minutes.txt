TypeScript Generics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through TypeScript Generics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: TypeScript Generics is a feature that lets functions and types work with many types while keeping type safety.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn type parameters, constraints, type inference, and generic interfaces.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a labeled box whose label type is filled in when you use it. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through reusable utility functions, typed API clients, and data containers.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for overusing any and overly complex generic types, then try tsc for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to add constraints so that error messages become clearer.
