Practice Exercises for Prompt Engineering

These exercises build real understanding of Prompt Engineering. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: vague requests. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick model playgrounds and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Prompt Engineering and include the term instructions.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble drafting content or extracting data from text.
Exercise 5: Compare LangChain and evaluation sets for building assistants and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models.
