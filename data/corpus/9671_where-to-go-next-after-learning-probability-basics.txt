Where to Go Next After Learning Probability Basics

You have met the basics of Probability Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Number Theory and RSA, which belongs to the same area as Probability Basics (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of events, conditional probability, independence or expected value, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around machine learning with Python random, and note every problem you meet, starting with confusing independent and exclusive events.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Probability Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
