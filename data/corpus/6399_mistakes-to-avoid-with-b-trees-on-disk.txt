Mistakes to Avoid with B-Trees on Disk

Everyone learns B-Trees on Disk partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: random writes on fragmented pages. It usually appears when someone skips pages and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: oversized keys. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of database indexes. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring page fill factor. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on sorted keys to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page.
