Graph Theory Basics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Graph Theory Basics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Graph Theory Basics in your own words.
2. Describe vertices and edges, degree, paths and cycles, and connectivity.
3. Give examples from social networks and maps.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them. Then draw a map of towns and the roads between them on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing directed and undirected graphs a problem? How does degree relate to paths and cycles? When would you choose NetworkX or Graphviz?

Study habit: draw a small example before writing code.

When you feel ready, compare Graph Theory Basics with Finite Automata and write down three differences.
