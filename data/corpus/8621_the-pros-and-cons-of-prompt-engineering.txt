The Pros and Cons of Prompt Engineering

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Prompt Engineering is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports drafting content
- Makes extracting data from text easier
- Helps with building assistants

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: vague requests
- Risk: missing context
- Risk: trusting outputs without verification

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as drafting content, and you can manage the risks, Prompt Engineering is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, specify the output format and include one or two examples.
