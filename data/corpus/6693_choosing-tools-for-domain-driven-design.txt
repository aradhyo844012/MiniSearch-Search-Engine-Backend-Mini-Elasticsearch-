Choosing Tools for Domain-Driven Design

There is no single best tool for Domain-Driven Design. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

event storming is a common first choice, especially for complex business systems. UML suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Miro is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle aligning developers with domain experts well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: using it for simple CRUD apps.

As a rule of thumb, agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes, no matter which tool you use.
