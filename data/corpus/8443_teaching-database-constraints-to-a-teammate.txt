Teaching Database Constraints to a Teammate

Explaining Database Constraints to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: guard rails along a mountain road. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving preventing bad data, naming primary keys and foreign keys as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain unique constraints in Database Constraints back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, skipping constraints for speed, and suggest this habit: put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
