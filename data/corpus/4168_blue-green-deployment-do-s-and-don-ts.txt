Blue-Green Deployment Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Blue-Green Deployment.

Do:
- Learn blue and green environments before anything else.
- Try AWS CodeDeploy on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
- Test the case of zero-downtime releases early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: database schema mismatches.
- Ignore this: doubling infrastructure costs.
- Ignore this: forgetting to drain old connections.
- Adopt Blue-Green Deployment just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Blue-Green Deployment, a little patience with traffic switching pays off more than any clever shortcut.
