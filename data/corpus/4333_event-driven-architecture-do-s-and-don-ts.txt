Event-Driven Architecture Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Event-Driven Architecture.

Do:
- Learn producers and consumers before anything else.
- Try Apache Kafka on a small example.
- Make it a habit to document every event name and schema.
- Test the case of order pipelines early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hard-to-trace flows.
- Ignore this: duplicate events.
- Ignore this: unclear event ownership.
- Adopt Event-Driven Architecture just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Event-Driven Architecture, a little patience with event brokers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
