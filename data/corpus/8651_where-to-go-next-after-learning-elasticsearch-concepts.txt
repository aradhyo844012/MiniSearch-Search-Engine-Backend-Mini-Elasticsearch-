Where to Go Next After Learning Elasticsearch Concepts

You have met the basics of Elasticsearch Concepts. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Views and Materialized Views, which belongs to the same area as Elasticsearch Concepts (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of indexes, shards, analyzers or queries, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around log analytics with Elasticsearch, and note every problem you meet, starting with too many shards.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Elasticsearch Concepts to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
