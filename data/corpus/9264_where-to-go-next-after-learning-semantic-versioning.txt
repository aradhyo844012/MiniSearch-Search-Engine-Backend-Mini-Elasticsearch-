Where to Go Next After Learning Semantic Versioning

You have met the basics of Semantic Versioning. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Makefiles, which belongs to the same area as Semantic Versioning (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of major, minor and patch numbers, breaking changes, pre-release tags or version ranges, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around declaring dependencies with semver.org, and note every problem you meet, starting with breaking changes in minor releases.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Semantic Versioning to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
