How to Learn Technical Debt in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Technical Debt. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Technical Debt, they usually mean the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn shortcuts and interest, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with SonarQube on a small task such as deciding when to cut corners.
Week 3: build a mini project around planning cleanup work.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as never paying it down, and explain Technical Debt to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include SonarQube, issue trackers, and code metrics.

In short, careful study of Technical Debt pays off. Spend time on shortcuts and interest, and the rest will follow.
