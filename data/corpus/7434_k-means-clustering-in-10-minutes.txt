K-Means Clustering in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through K-Means Clustering.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: K-Means Clustering is an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn centroids, distance measures, choosing k, and iterations.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine dividing students into study groups around the most central member. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through customer segmentation, image compression, and grouping documents.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for choosing k blindly and unscaled features, then try scikit-learn for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to scale features before clustering.
