DOM Manipulation Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of DOM Manipulation.

- Definition: reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript.
- Core ideas: selectors; events; nodes; updates.
- Use it for: updating search results; handling form input; showing messages.
- Watch out for: inserting untrusted text as HTML; querying the DOM inside tight loops; forgetting to remove event listeners.
- Tools: querySelector, browser console, MDN docs.
- Quick tip: practice selectors until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for events before building anything large.
