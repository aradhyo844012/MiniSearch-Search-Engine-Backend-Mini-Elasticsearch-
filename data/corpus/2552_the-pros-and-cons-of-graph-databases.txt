The Pros and Cons of Graph Databases

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Graph Databases is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports social networks
- Makes recommendation engines easier
- Helps with fraud detection

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using them for simple tabular data
- Risk: unbounded traversals
- Risk: supernodes with millions of edges

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as social networks, and you can manage the risks, Graph Databases is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
