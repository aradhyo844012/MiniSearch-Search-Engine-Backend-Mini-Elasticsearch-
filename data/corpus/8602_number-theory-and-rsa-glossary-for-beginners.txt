Number Theory and RSA Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Number Theory and RSA feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Number Theory and RSA: the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.
prime numbers: a core term, most visible in public key cryptography.
modular arithmetic: a core term that beginners often confuse with greatest common divisor.
greatest common divisor: a core term that connects to modular exponentiation.
modular exponentiation: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python pow: a tool commonly used for hash functions.
