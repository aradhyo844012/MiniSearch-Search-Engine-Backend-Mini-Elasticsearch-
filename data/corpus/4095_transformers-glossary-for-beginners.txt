Transformers Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Transformers feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Transformers: neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other.
self-attention: a core term, most visible in language translation.
positional encoding: a core term that beginners often confuse with encoder and decoder blocks.
encoder and decoder blocks: a core term that connects to tokens.
tokens: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Hugging Face: a tool commonly used for text generation.

Come back to this Transformers list whenever documentation for PyTorch uses a word you do not recognize.
