Teaching Transformers to a Teammate

Explaining Transformers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving language translation, naming self-attention and positional encoding as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain encoder and decoder blocks in Transformers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, huge compute requirements, and suggest this habit: begin with small pretrained models.
