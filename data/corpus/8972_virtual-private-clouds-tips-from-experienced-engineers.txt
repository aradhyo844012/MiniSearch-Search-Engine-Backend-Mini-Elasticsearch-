Virtual Private Clouds Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Virtual Private Clouds regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can keep databases in private subnets.
2. Be careful with this one: overlapping address ranges.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit route tables before anything else.
4. Do not skip subnets; everything else builds on it.
5. Test the case of separating public and private services early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to connecting to offices as well as to Virtual Private Clouds in general.
