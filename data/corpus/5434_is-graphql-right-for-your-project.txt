Is GraphQL Right for Your Project?

Before adopting GraphQL, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.

It is a good fit when your work involves mobile apps with limited bandwidth or aggregating many services, and when your team understands schema.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn queries and mutations, or when you cannot manage these risks: the N+1 query problem, unbounded query depth, and weak caching.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether resolvers and types are part of the answer. If they are, GraphQL is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with flexible dashboards and use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
