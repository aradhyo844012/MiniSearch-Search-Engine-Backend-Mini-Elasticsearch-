Troubleshooting Neural Networks Problems

When something breaks around Neural Networks, a calm and systematic approach saves the most time. The term Neural Networks refers to models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Neural Networks and Decision Trees are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

Start by reproducing the problem with the smallest possible example. Then change one thing at a time and note what happens. Logs, tests and PyTorch usually reveal the cause faster than guessing.

Common culprits: too little training data; poor learning rate choices; not monitoring validation loss. If none of these explains the symptom, re-read the basics of layers and weights.

In practice you can start with PyTorch, then explore TensorFlow or Keras as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Neural Networks.
