Domain-Driven Design Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Domain-Driven Design feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Domain-Driven Design: an approach to software that models the code around the real business domain.
ubiquitous language: a core term, most visible in complex business systems.
bounded contexts: a core term that beginners often confuse with aggregates.
aggregates: a core term that connects to entities and value objects.
entities and value objects: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
event storming: a tool commonly used for aligning developers with domain experts.

Come back to this Domain-Driven Design list whenever documentation for UML uses a word you do not recognize.
