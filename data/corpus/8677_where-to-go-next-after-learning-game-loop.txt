Where to Go Next After Learning Game Loop

You have met the basics of Game Loop. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Sprite Animation, which belongs to the same area as Game Loop (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of input handling, the update step, rendering or frame timing, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around keeping animation smooth with Unity, and note every problem you meet, starting with tying logic to frame rate.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Game Loop to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
