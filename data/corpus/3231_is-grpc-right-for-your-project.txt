Is gRPC Right for Your Project?

Before adopting gRPC, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts.

It is a good fit when your work involves service-to-service calls or low-latency APIs, and when your team understands protocol buffers.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn service definitions, or when you cannot manage these risks: breaking changes in message fields, poor browser support without a proxy, and harder manual debugging.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether streaming and HTTP/2 are part of the answer. If they are, gRPC is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with streaming data and never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file.
