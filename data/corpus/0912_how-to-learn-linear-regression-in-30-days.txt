How to Learn Linear Regression in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Linear Regression. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term Linear Regression refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn coefficients and residuals, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with scikit-learn on a small task such as predicting prices.
Week 3: build a mini project around estimating demand.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as extrapolating far beyond the data, and explain Linear Regression to a friend.

Linear Regression and Natural Language Processing are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

In practice you can start with scikit-learn, then explore NumPy or statsmodels as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Linear Regression.
