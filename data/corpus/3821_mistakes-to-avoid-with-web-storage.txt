Mistakes to Avoid with Web Storage

Everyone learns Web Storage partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: storing sensitive data. It usually appears when someone skips localStorage and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: assuming storage is always available. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of saving user preferences. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hitting size limits. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on IndexedDB to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: wrap every read and write in error handling.
