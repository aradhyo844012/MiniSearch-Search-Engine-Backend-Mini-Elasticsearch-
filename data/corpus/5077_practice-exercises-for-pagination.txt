Practice Exercises for Pagination

These exercises build real understanding of Pagination. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Pagination and include the term offset pagination.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble search results or activity feeds.
Exercise 3: Explain how page size and stable ordering differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Compare keyset queries and Django REST Framework for admin tables and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Pick SQL LIMIT and OFFSET and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Pagination is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time.
