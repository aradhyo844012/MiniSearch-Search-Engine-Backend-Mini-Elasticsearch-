CSRF in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through CSRF.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: CSRF is an attack that tricks a logged-in user's browser into sending unwanted requests to a site.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn session cookies, anti-CSRF tokens, SameSite cookies, and state-changing requests.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a forged letter that a bank clerk stamps because your ID is already on file. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through protecting forms, securing account settings, and hardening admin panels.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for using GET requests for state changes and skipping tokens on cookie-based APIs, then try Django CSRF middleware for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.
