Understanding Encryption at Rest Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a locked safe inside a locked room.

In that picture, symmetric keys is the first rule you follow, key management is the next step, and disk encryption and envelope encryption keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database, and it involves details such as storing keys next to the data that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for LUKS to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain protecting backups to a friend.
