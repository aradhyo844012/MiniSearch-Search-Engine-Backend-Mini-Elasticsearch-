Vector Graphics (SVG) Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Vector Graphics (SVG).

Do:
- Learn paths before anything else.
- Try Inkscape on a small example.
- Make it a habit to set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
- Test the case of logos early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overly complex paths.
- Ignore this: a missing viewBox.
- Ignore this: inconsistent stroke widths.
- Adopt Vector Graphics (SVG) just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Vector Graphics (SVG), a little patience with the viewBox pays off more than any clever shortcut.
