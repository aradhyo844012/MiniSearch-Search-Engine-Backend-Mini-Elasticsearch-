Teaching Database Migrations to a Teammate

Explaining Database Migrations to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a numbered set of renovation steps applied to a building in order. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Database Migrations is versioned scripts that change a database schema in a controlled and repeatable way. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving adding columns safely, naming up and down scripts and version tables as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain backward compatibility in Database Migrations back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, locking large tables, and suggest this habit: split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
