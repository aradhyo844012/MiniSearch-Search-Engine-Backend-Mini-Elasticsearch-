Practice Exercises for Event-Driven Architecture

These exercises build real understanding of Event-Driven Architecture. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: hard-to-trace flows. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare RabbitMQ and AWS EventBridge for integrating many services and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick Apache Kafka and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble order pipelines or real-time notifications.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Event-Driven Architecture and include the term producers and consumers.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Event-Driven Architecture is a style in which components communicate by publishing events and reacting to them.
