Model Evaluation Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Model Evaluation.

Q: Explain Model Evaluation in simple words.
A: Model Evaluation refers to measuring how well a machine learning model works on data it has not seen. I would add an example involving comparing models.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: accuracy, precision and recall, confusion matrices, and validation sets.

Q: How does it connect to Gradient Descent?
A: Both belong to the area of Machine Learning, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: using accuracy on imbalanced data.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with choosing thresholds, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: scikit-learn metrics, MLflow, and pandas.
