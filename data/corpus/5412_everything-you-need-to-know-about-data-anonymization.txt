Everything You Need to Know About Data Anonymization

This page collects the essentials about Data Anonymization in one place. In short, Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.

Core concepts:
- masking
- generalization
- k-anonymity
- differential privacy

Where it is used:
- sharing datasets with researchers
- testing with production-like data
- meeting privacy rules

Common problems:
- re-identification through combined fields
- removing too little
- destroying usefulness by removing too much

Tools to know:
- ARX
- Faker
- Google's differential privacy library

Remember this above all: blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test whether combining fields could identify a person.
