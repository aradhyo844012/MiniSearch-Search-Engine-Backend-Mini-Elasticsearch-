Choosing Tools for CSRF

There is no single best tool for CSRF. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Django CSRF middleware is a common first choice, especially for protecting forms. Flask-WTF suits teams that already work with similar technology, and OWASP guides is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle securing account settings well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: using GET requests for state changes.

As a rule of thumb, combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens, no matter which tool you use.
