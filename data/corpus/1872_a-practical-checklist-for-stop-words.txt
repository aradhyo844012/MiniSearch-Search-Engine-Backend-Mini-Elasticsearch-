A Practical Checklist for Stop Words

Use this checklist before you rely on Stop Words in a project.

1. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on index size.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers frequency lists.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Guard against this risk: using the wrong language list.
5. Measure results before and after any change.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Stop Words should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: removing words that matter in phrases.
