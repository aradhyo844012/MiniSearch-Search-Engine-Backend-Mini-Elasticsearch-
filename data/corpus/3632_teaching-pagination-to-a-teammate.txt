Teaching Pagination to a Teammate

Explaining Pagination to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Pagination is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving search results, naming offset pagination and cursor pagination as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain page size in Pagination back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, offsets getting slow on huge tables, and suggest this habit: prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
