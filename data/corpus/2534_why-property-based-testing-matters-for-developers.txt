Why Property-Based Testing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Property-Based Testing make better decisions, because Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold.

Three situations show why it matters. First, testing parsers. Second, checking sorting and math functions. Third, finding edge cases. In each case, a solid grasp of properties and generators separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: vague properties, slow generators, and ignoring random seed reports.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Hypothesis, QuickCheck, and fast-check cover most needs.
