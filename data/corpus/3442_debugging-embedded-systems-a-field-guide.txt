Debugging Embedded Systems: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Embedded Systems, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: memory leaks on tiny devices, no plan for updates, and ignoring power use.
Step 3: revisit microcontrollers and firmware, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use C or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Embedded Systems in work such as appliances come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
