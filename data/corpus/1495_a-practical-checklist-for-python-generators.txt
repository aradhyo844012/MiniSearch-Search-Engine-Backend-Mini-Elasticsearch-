A Practical Checklist for Python Generators

Use this checklist before you rely on Python Generators in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: consuming a generator twice.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example reading large files line by line.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Review the core ideas: yield, lazy evaluation, iterators, and generator expressions.
5. Measure results before and after any change.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Python Generators should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: hiding errors until iteration.
8. Guard against this risk: forgetting generators are lazy.
