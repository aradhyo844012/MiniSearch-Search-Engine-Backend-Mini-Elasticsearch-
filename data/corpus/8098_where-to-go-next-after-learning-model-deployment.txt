Where to Go Next After Learning Model Deployment

You have met the basics of Model Deployment. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Speech Recognition, which belongs to the same area as Model Deployment (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of serving APIs, batch versus online inference, containers or latency budgets, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around fraud checks with FastAPI, and note every problem you meet, starting with training-serving mismatch.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Model Deployment to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
