Where to Go Next After Learning Static Analysis

You have met the basics of Static Analysis. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Mocking and Stubbing, which belongs to the same area as Static Analysis (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of linters, type checkers, rule sets or false positives, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around enforcing team style with ESLint, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring warnings.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Static Analysis to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
