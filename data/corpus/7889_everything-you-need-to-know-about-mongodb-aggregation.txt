Everything You Need to Know About MongoDB Aggregation

This page collects the essentials about MongoDB Aggregation in one place. In short, MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.

Core concepts:
- pipeline stages
- $match and $group
- projection
- indexes

Where it is used:
- summarizing orders
- building analytics queries
- reshaping documents

Common problems:
- putting $match late
- huge intermediate results
- hitting memory limits

Tools to know:
- MongoDB Compass
- mongosh
- PyMongo

Remember this above all: an assembly line where each station filters or reshapes the parts is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to filter with $match as early as possible.
