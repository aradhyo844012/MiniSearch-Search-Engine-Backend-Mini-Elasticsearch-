Feature Flags vs Service Mesh: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Feature Flags or Service Mesh first. Both sit in the area of DevOps and Cloud, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code, and it is closely tied to flag evaluation and targeting rules. Service Mesh, by contrast, is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies, and it is closely tied to sidecar proxies and mutual TLS.

Choose Feature Flags first if you care about dark launches or A/B tests. Choose Service Mesh first if you care about securing service traffic or retries and timeouts.

Think of Feature Flags as light switches wired to every room of a house, and of Service Mesh as a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Feature Flags, avoid never removing old flags. With Service Mesh, avoid added latency and complexity.

A good habit for Feature Flags is to give every flag an owner and a removal date. A good habit for Service Mesh is to adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
