The Pros and Cons of Distributed Locks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Distributed Locks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running scheduled jobs once
- Makes protecting shared resources easier
- Helps with selecting a leader

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: locks that never expire
- Risk: clock drift
- Risk: assuming the lock holder is still alive

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running scheduled jobs once, and you can manage the risks, Distributed Locks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
