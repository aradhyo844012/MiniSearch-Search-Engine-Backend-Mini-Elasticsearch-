Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Background Jobs

This path is for a learner who has never studied Background Jobs before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Background Jobs is tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them) and learn queues and workers.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Celery, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving sending emails.

Stage 3, depth: study retries and scheduling, then deliberately explore this problem: jobs that are not idempotent.

Stage 4, application: apply Background Jobs to resizing images, compare Sidekiq with BullMQ, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Background Jobs to someone else, and then move on to Webhooks.
