The Pros and Cons of Multi-Cloud Strategy

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Multi-Cloud Strategy is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports avoiding single-provider risk
- Makes meeting regional rules easier
- Helps with using best-of-breed services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: doubling operational complexity
- Risk: lowest-common-denominator designs
- Risk: hidden data transfer costs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as avoiding single-provider risk, and you can manage the risks, Multi-Cloud Strategy is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
