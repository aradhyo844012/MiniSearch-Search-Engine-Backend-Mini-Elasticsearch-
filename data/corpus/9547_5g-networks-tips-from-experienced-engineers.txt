5G Networks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with 5G Networks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: limited coverage on the fastest bands.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit latency before anything else.
3. Do not skip spectrum bands; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for speed test tools first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as network simulators.
5. Test the case of mobile broadband early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to connected vehicles as well as to 5G Networks in general.
