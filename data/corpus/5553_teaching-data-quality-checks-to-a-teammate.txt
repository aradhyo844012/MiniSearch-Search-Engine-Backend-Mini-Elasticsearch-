Teaching Data Quality Checks to a Teammate

Explaining Data Quality Checks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving protecting dashboards, naming null checks and uniqueness as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain freshness in Data Quality Checks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, alerts nobody reads, and suggest this habit: check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
