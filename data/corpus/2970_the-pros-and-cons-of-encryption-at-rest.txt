The Pros and Cons of Encryption at Rest

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Encryption at Rest is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports protecting backups
- Makes meeting compliance rules easier
- Helps with securing laptops

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: storing keys next to the data
- Risk: no key rotation
- Risk: assuming it stops every kind of attack

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as protecting backups, and you can manage the risks, Encryption at Rest is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep keys in a separate service from the data.
