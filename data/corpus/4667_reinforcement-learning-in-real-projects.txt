Reinforcement Learning in Real Projects

Reading about Reinforcement Learning is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: game playing. Here, agent drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against badly designed rewards.

Scenario two: robot control. This time environment matters most, and tools such as Gymnasium help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: recommendation strategies. The key idea is reward, and the biggest risk is slow training.

Across all three scenarios, Reinforcement Learning stays the same idea: a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes. Only the details change.
