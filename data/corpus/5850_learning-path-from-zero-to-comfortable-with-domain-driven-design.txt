Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Domain-Driven Design

This path is for a learner who has never studied Domain-Driven Design before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Domain-Driven Design is an approach to software that models the code around the real business domain) and learn ubiquitous language and bounded contexts.

Stage 2, hands-on: install event storming, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving complex business systems.

Stage 3, depth: study aggregates and entities and value objects, then deliberately explore this problem: using it for simple CRUD apps.

Stage 4, application: apply Domain-Driven Design to aligning developers with domain experts, compare UML with Miro, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Domain-Driven Design to someone else, and then move on to SOLID Principles.
