Why Physics Engines Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Physics Engines make better decisions, because Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.

Three situations show why it matters. First, vehicles and ragdolls. Second, puzzle games. Third, training simulations. In each case, a solid grasp of rigid bodies and forces and impulses separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: variable time steps, unstable stacks of objects, and extreme mass ratios.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Box2D, Bullet, and PhysX cover most needs.
