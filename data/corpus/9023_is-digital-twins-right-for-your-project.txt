Is Digital Twins Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Digital Twins, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.

It is a good fit when your work involves factory monitoring or building management, and when your team understands sensor data.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn simulation models, or when you cannot manage these risks: stale data, over-detailed models, and unclear goals.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether synchronization and analytics are part of the answer. If they are, Digital Twins is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with predictive maintenance and define the question the twin must answer first.
