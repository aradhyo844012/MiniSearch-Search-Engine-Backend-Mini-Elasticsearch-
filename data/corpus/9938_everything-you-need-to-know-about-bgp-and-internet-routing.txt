Everything You Need to Know About BGP and Internet Routing

This page collects the essentials about BGP and Internet Routing in one place. In short, BGP and Internet Routing is the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.

Core concepts:
- autonomous systems
- route announcements
- peering
- path selection

Where it is used:
- connecting ISPs
- directing traffic between large networks
- explaining internet outages

Common problems:
- route leaks
- hijacked prefixes
- misconfigured filters

Tools to know:
- bgpq4
- BIRD
- RIPE Stat

Remember this above all: the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to filter route announcements strictly.
