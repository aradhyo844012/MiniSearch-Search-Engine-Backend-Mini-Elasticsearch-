Graph Databases Explained Simply

If Graph Databases sounds intimidating, relax. It is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Graph Databases as a city map where intersections are records and roads are relationships. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: nodes, edges, properties, and traversal queries. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with nodes, then add edges.

You will meet Graph Databases in work such as social networks, recommendation engines, and fraud detection.

One practical tip is to limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.

Once Graph Databases feels comfortable, read about Eventual Consistency, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
