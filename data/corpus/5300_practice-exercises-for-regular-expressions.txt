Practice Exercises for Regular Expressions

These exercises build real understanding of Regular Expressions. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare regex101 and grep for extracting fields from text and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: catastrophic backtracking. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble validating input or searching logs.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Regular Expressions and include the term character classes.
Exercise 5: Explain how groups and anchors differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text.
