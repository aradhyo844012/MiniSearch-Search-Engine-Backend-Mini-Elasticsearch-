Where to Go Next After Learning Code Coverage

You have met the basics of Code Coverage. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Static Analysis, which belongs to the same area as Code Coverage (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of line coverage, branch coverage, coverage reports or thresholds, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around setting quality gates with coverage.py, and note every problem you meet, starting with chasing 100 percent.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Code Coverage to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
