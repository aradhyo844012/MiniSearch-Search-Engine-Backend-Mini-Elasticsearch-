The Pros and Cons of Instruction Pipelines

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Instruction Pipelines is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports explaining CPU speed
- Makes understanding branch costs easier
- Helps with optimizing tight loops

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: unpredictable branches
- Risk: dependency chains
- Risk: assuming each instruction takes one step

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as explaining CPU speed, and you can manage the risks, Instruction Pipelines is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops.
