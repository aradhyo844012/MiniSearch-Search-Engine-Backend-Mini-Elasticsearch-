Message Queues Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Message Queues.

- Definition: systems that let services send work to each other asynchronously through a buffer.
- Core ideas: producers; consumers; acknowledgements; retries.
- Use it for: sending emails; processing uploads; smoothing traffic spikes.
- Watch out for: losing messages without acknowledgements; poison messages that retry forever; duplicate processing.
- Tools: RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka, Amazon SQS.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for consumers before building anything large.
- Related topic: Microservices.
