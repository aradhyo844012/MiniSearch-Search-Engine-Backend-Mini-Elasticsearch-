Scaling Considerations for Secrets Management

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Secrets Management changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding vaults and getting database credentials right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for secrets in source control and long-lived shared credentials, and measure with HashiCorp Vault before changing anything.

Large scale: access policies and audit logs move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around API keys at this stage.

Before you scale, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
