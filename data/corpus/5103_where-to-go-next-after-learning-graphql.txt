Where to Go Next After Learning GraphQL

You have met the basics of GraphQL. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Background Jobs, which belongs to the same area as GraphQL (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of schema, queries and mutations, resolvers or types, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around aggregating many services with Apollo, and note every problem you meet, starting with the N+1 query problem.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining GraphQL to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
