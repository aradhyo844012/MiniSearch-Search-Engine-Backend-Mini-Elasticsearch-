Everything You Need to Know About 3D Transformations

This page collects the essentials about 3D Transformations in one place. In short, 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.

Core concepts:
- translation
- rotation
- scaling
- matrices

Where it is used:
- games
- modeling tools
- robotics

Common problems:
- wrong multiplication order
- gimbal lock
- mixing coordinate systems

Tools to know:
- GLM
- three.js
- Blender

Remember this above all: moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
