Choosing Tools for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

There is no single best tool for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

LangChain is a common first choice, especially for company knowledge assistants. FAISS suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Elasticsearch is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle question answering over documents well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: poor chunking.

As a rule of thumb, evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality, no matter which tool you use.
