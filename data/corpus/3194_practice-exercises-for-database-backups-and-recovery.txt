Practice Exercises for Database Backups and Recovery

These exercises build real understanding of Database Backups and Recovery. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick pg_dump and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble recovering deleted tables or migrating servers.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: never testing restores. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Database Backups and Recovery and include the term full and incremental backups.
Exercise 5: Compare mysqldump and AWS Backup for meeting compliance rules and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.
