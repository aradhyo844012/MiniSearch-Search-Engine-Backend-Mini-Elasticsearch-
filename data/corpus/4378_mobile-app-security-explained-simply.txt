Mobile App Security Explained Simply

Here is Mobile App Security without the jargon: it is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks.

Think of Mobile App Security as locking an app's valuables in a hotel safe. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: secure storage, certificate pinning, obfuscation, and permissions. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with secure storage, then add certificate pinning.

People reach for Mobile App Security when they work on banking apps, health apps, and enterprise apps.

One practical tip is to store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
