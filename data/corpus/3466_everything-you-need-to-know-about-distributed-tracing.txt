Everything You Need to Know About Distributed Tracing

This page collects the essentials about Distributed Tracing in one place. In short, Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Core concepts:
- spans
- trace IDs
- context propagation
- sampling

Where it is used:
- finding slow services
- understanding dependencies
- debugging errors across services

Common problems:
- missing context propagation
- sampling too aggressively
- overhead from too many spans

Tools to know:
- OpenTelemetry
- Jaeger
- Zipkin

Remember this above all: a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
