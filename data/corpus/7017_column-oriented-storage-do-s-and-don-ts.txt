Column-Oriented Storage Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Column-Oriented Storage.

Do:
- Learn column chunks before anything else.
- Try Apache Parquet on a small example.
- Make it a habit to select only the columns you really need.
- Test the case of analytics dashboards early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow single-row lookups.
- Ignore this: many small writes.
- Ignore this: wide tables with unused columns.
- Adopt Column-Oriented Storage just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Column-Oriented Storage, a little patience with compression pays off more than any clever shortcut.
