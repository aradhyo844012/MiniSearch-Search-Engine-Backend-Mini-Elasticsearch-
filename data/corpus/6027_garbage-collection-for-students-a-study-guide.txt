Garbage Collection for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Garbage Collection.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Garbage Collection in your own words.
2. Describe reachability, generations, mark and sweep, and pause times.
3. Give examples from managed languages like Java and Python and reducing memory bugs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Garbage Collection is automatic memory management that finds and frees objects a program no longer uses. Then draw a cleaner who removes items nobody has touched in a while on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is creating huge numbers of short-lived objects a problem? How does generations relate to mark and sweep? When would you choose JVM GC logs or the Python gc module?

Study habit: measure allocation rates before tuning collector settings.

When you feel ready, compare Garbage Collection with Rust Ownership and write down three differences.
