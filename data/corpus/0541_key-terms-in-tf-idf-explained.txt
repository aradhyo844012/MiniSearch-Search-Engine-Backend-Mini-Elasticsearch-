Key Terms in TF-IDF Explained

Clear vocabulary makes TF-IDF much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about TF-IDF, they usually mean a scoring method that weights terms by how often they appear in a document and how rare they are overall. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.
