Caching Strategies Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Caching Strategies.

Q: Explain Caching Strategies in simple words.
A: Caching Strategies refers to approaches for storing computed results so they can be reused instead of recalculated. I would add an example involving speeding up read-heavy APIs.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: stale data; cache stampedes; and unbounded memory use.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: cache-aside, write-through, time to live, and invalidation.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: stale data.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Redis, Memcached, and HTTP cache headers.
