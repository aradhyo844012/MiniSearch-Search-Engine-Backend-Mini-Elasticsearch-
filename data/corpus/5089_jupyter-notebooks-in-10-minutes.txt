Jupyter Notebooks in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Jupyter Notebooks.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn cells, kernels, markdown, and outputs.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a lab notebook that can run its own experiments. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through exploring data, teaching and demos, and sharing analysis.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for running cells out of order and hidden state, then try JupyterLab for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
