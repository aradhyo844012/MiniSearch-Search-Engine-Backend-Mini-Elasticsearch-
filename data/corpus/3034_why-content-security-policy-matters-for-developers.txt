Why Content Security Policy Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Content Security Policy make better decisions, because Content Security Policy is a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load.

Three situations show why it matters. First, reducing the impact of XSS. Second, blocking unwanted scripts. Third, monitoring policy violations. In each case, a solid grasp of directives and nonces separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: allowing unsafe-inline, policies too broad to help, and breaking third-party widgets.

You will not need to build everything yourself; browser DevTools, CSP Evaluator, and Report URI cover most needs.
