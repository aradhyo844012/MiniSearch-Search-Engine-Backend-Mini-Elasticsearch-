BGP and Internet Routing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with BGP and Internet Routing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for bgpq4 first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as BIRD.
2. Make sure you can filter route announcements strictly.
3. Write down why you chose BGP and Internet Routing so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip autonomous systems; everything else builds on it.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit route announcements before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to directing traffic between large networks as well as to BGP and Internet Routing in general.
