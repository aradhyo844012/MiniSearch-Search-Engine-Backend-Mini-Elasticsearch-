Smart Contracts Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Smart Contracts. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.
Key terms: contract code, transactions, gas fees, and immutability.
Typical uses: token systems, automated escrow, and decentralized applications.
Risks: bugs that cannot be patched, reentrancy attacks, and high gas costs.
Tools: Solidity, Hardhat, and Remix.

Mental picture: a vending machine that follows its rules with no clerk.

Habit to build: audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
