Mistakes to Avoid with CPU Caches

Everyone learns CPU Caches partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: false sharing. It usually appears when someone skips cache lines and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor access patterns. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of speeding up loops. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming memory is uniformly fast. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on locality to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: access arrays in the order they are stored.
