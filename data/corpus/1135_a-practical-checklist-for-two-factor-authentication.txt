A Practical Checklist for Two-Factor Authentication

Use this checklist before you rely on Two-Factor Authentication in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: no recovery plan.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on authenticator apps.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as Google Authenticator or YubiKey.
4. Measure results before and after any change.
5. Guard against this risk: relying only on SMS.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers one-time codes.
7. Review how Two-Factor Authentication interacts with SQL Injection.
8. Review the core ideas: one-time codes, authenticator apps, hardware keys, and backup codes.
9. Document your decisions in the project README.
