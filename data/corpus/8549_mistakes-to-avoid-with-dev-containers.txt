Mistakes to Avoid with Dev Containers

Everyone learns Dev Containers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow image builds. It usually appears when someone skips devcontainer.json and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: files owned by the wrong user. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of onboarding new developers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting to pin tool versions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on extensions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: pin tool versions inside the container definition.
