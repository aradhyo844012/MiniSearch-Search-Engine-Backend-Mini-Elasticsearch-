The Pros and Cons of Reverse Proxies

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Reverse Proxies is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports hiding backend servers
- Makes terminating HTTPS easier
- Helps with routing requests to several apps

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: losing the client IP address
- Risk: wrong timeouts
- Risk: exposing internal paths

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as hiding backend servers, and you can manage the risks, Reverse Proxies is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
