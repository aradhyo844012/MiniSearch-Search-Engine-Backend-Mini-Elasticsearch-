Key Terms in Design Patterns Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Design Patterns much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. The term Design Patterns refers to reusable solutions to common problems in software design. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

singleton: a key term in Design Patterns. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
factory: a key term in Design Patterns. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
observer: a key term in Design Patterns. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
strategy: a key term in Design Patterns. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.

With steady practice, Design Patterns will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
