Where to Go Next After Learning NULL Handling in SQL

You have met the basics of NULL Handling in SQL. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Views and Materialized Views, which belongs to the same area as NULL Handling in SQL (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of three-valued logic, IS NULL, COALESCE or NULL in aggregates, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around outer joins with PostgreSQL, and note every problem you meet, starting with comparing with equals instead of IS NULL.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining NULL Handling in SQL to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
