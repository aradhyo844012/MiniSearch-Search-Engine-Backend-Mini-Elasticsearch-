Mistakes to Avoid with Interrupts

Everyone learns Interrupts partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: long handlers. It usually appears when someone skips interrupt handlers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: race conditions with shared data. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of keyboard and mouse input. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: lost interrupts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on context saving to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
