Responsible AI and Bias Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Responsible AI and Bias feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Responsible AI and Bias: practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.
bias in data: a core term, most visible in hiring tools.
fairness metrics: a core term that beginners often confuse with transparency.
transparency: a core term that connects to human oversight.
human oversight: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Fairlearn: a tool commonly used for lending models.
