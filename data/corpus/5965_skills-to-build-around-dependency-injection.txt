Skills to Build Around Dependency Injection

Knowing the definition of Dependency Injection is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with constructor injection and interfaces, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Spring on a Dependency Injection task, and try at least one alternative such as FastAPI Depends.

Judgment: learn to decide when Dependency Injection fits a problem such as easier unit testing, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them.

Habits: inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes, and write down what you learn after each project.
