REST APIs: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is REST APIs?
A: The term REST APIs refers to an approach to designing web APIs around resources, URLs and standard HTTP methods.

Q: Why do developers use REST APIs?
A: Common uses include mobile app backends, integrating services, and public developer platforms.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with REST APIs.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Flask, FastAPI, and Postman.

Q: How does it relate to Authentication and Sessions?
A: REST APIs and Authentication and Sessions often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Backend and APIs.
