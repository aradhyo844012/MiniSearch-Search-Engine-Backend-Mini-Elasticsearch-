Where to Go Next After Learning Video Codecs

You have met the basics of Video Codecs. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Rasterization, which belongs to the same area as Video Codecs (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of frames, motion compensation, bitrate or containers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around video calls with FFmpeg, and note every problem you meet, starting with mismatched codec support.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Video Codecs to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
