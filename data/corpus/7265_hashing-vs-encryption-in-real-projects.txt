Hashing vs Encryption in Real Projects

Reading about Hashing vs Encryption is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: storing passwords. Here, one-way functions drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against encrypting passwords instead of hashing them.

Scenario two: verifying file integrity. This time keys matters most, and tools such as SHA-256 help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: protecting data in transit. The key idea is collision resistance, and the biggest risk is using broken hash functions.

Across all three scenarios, Hashing vs Encryption stays the same idea: the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data. Only the details change.
