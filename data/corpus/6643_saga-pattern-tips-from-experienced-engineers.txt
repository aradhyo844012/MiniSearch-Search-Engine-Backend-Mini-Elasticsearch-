Saga Pattern Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Saga Pattern regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Saga Pattern so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for Temporal first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Camunda.
3. Test the case of order processing early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit compensating actions before anything else.
5. Do not skip local transactions; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to travel bookings as well.
