Responsible AI and Bias Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Responsible AI and Bias. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.
Key terms: bias in data, fairness metrics, transparency, and human oversight.
Typical uses: hiring tools, lending models, and healthcare decisions.
Risks: assuming data is neutral, testing only on average performance, and giving people no way to appeal.
Tools: Fairlearn, AI Fairness 360, and model cards.

Mental picture: a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team.

Habit to build: test performance separately for different groups.
