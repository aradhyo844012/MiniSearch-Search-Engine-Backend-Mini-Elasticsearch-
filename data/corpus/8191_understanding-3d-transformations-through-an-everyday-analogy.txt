Understanding 3D Transformations Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it.

In that picture, translation is the first rule you follow, rotation is the next step, and scaling and matrices keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space, and it involves details such as wrong multiplication order that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for GLM to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain games to a friend.
