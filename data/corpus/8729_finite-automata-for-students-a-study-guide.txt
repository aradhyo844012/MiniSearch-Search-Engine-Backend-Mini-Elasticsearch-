Finite Automata for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Finite Automata.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Finite Automata in your own words.
2. Describe states, transitions, accepting states, and regular languages.
3. Give examples from tokenizers and pattern matching.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Finite Automata is simple abstract machines that read input and move between a fixed set of states. Then draw a turnstile that moves between locked and unlocked on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is missing transitions a problem? How does transitions relate to accepting states? When would you choose JFLAP or Python dictionaries?

Study habit: draw the state diagram before writing any code.

When you feel ready, compare Finite Automata with Graph Theory Basics and write down three differences.
