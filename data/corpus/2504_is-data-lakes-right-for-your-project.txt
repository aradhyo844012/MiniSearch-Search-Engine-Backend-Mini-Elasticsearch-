Is Data Lakes Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Data Lakes, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed.

It is a good fit when your work involves storing logs and events or machine learning datasets, and when your team understands raw storage.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn schema on read, or when you cannot manage these risks: turning into a data swamp, no catalog or ownership, and poor access controls.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether partitioning and metadata catalogs are part of the answer. If they are, Data Lakes is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with analytics across many sources and register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.
