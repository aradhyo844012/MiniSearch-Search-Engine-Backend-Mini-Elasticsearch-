Mobile App Performance Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Mobile App Performance.

- Definition: making mobile apps start quickly, run smoothly and use little battery.
- Core ideas: startup time; frame rate; memory use; network calls.
- Use it for: smoother scrolling; longer battery life; faster screens.
- Watch out for: loading everything at startup; large images; too many background tasks.
- Tools: Android Profiler, Xcode Instruments, Firebase Performance Monitoring.
- Quick tip: practice startup time until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: Android Basics.
