Choosing Tools for Views and Materialized Views

There is no single best tool for Views and Materialized Views. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PostgreSQL is a common first choice, especially for simplifying repeated queries. MySQL suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Snowflake is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Views and Materialized Views by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle hiding sensitive columns well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Views and Materialized Views, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: stale materialized data.

As a rule of thumb, document how and when each materialized view is refreshed, no matter which tool you use.
