Why Blockchain Fundamentals Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Blockchain Fundamentals make better decisions, because Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.

Three situations show why it matters. First, digital currencies. Second, supply chain tracking. Third, public records. In each case, a solid grasp of blocks and hashes separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: treating it as a database replacement, slow throughput, and the energy use of some designs.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Bitcoin Core, Ethereum, and Hyperledger cover most needs.
