3D Transformations Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with 3D Transformations regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose 3D Transformations so the next person understands the decision.
2. Do not skip translation; everything else builds on it.
3. Test the case of games early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit rotation before anything else.
5. Make sure you can write down the order of transformations before multiplying.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to modeling tools as well as to 3D Transformations in general.
