A Practical Checklist for Bloom Filters

Use this checklist before you rely on Bloom Filters in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Review the core ideas: bit arrays, multiple hash functions, false positives, and no false negatives.
3. Guard against this risk: forgetting that false positives exist.
4. Guard against this risk: choosing too small a bit array.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example skipping disk lookups for missing keys.
6. Review how Bloom Filters interacts with Sorting Algorithms.
7. Measure results before and after any change.
