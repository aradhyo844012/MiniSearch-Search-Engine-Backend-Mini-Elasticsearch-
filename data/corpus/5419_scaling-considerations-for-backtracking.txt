Scaling Considerations for Backtracking

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Backtracking changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding decision tree and getting solving Sudoku right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for forgetting to undo changes and missing pruning rules, and measure with Python recursion before changing anything.

Large scale: state restoration and constraint checking move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around generating permutations at this stage.

Before you scale, prune as early as possible because the savings multiply. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
