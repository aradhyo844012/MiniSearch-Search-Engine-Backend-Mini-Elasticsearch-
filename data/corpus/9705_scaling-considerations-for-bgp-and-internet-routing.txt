Scaling Considerations for BGP and Internet Routing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how BGP and Internet Routing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding autonomous systems and getting connecting ISPs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for route leaks and hijacked prefixes, and measure with bgpq4 before changing anything.

Large scale: peering and path selection move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around directing traffic between large networks at this stage.

Before you scale, filter route announcements strictly. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
