Everything You Need to Know About Encryption at Rest

This page collects the essentials about Encryption at Rest in one place. In short, Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database.

Core concepts:
- symmetric keys
- key management
- disk encryption
- envelope encryption

Where it is used:
- protecting backups
- meeting compliance rules
- securing laptops

Common problems:
- storing keys next to the data
- no key rotation
- assuming it stops every kind of attack

Tools to know:
- LUKS
- AWS KMS
- BitLocker

Remember this above all: a locked safe inside a locked room is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to keep keys in a separate service from the data.
