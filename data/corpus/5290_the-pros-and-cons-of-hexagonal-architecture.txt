The Pros and Cons of Hexagonal Architecture

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Hexagonal Architecture is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports testable business logic
- Makes swapping databases easier
- Helps with supporting several interfaces

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too many layers for small apps
- Risk: leaking framework types into the core
- Risk: confusing ports with adapters

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as testable business logic, and you can manage the risks, Hexagonal Architecture is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep framework imports out of the core domain.
