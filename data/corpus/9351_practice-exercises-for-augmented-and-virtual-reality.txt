Practice Exercises for Augmented and Virtual Reality

These exercises build real understanding of Augmented and Virtual Reality. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare WebXR and ARKit for remote collaboration and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Augmented and Virtual Reality and include the term head tracking.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble training simulations or games.
Exercise 4: Pick Unity XR and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: motion sickness from low frame rates. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.
