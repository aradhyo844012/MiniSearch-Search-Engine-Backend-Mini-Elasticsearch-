The Pros and Cons of A/B Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and A/B Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports testing page designs
- Makes comparing ranking algorithms easier
- Helps with evaluating new features

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: stopping tests early
- Risk: testing too many variants
- Risk: ignoring sample size

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as testing page designs, and you can manage the risks, A/B Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
