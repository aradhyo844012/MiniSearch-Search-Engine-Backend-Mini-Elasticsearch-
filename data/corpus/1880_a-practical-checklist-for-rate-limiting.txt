A Practical Checklist for Rate Limiting

Use this checklist before you rely on Rate Limiting in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Rate Limiting should accomplish.
2. Guard against this risk: limits that are too strict.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers token bucket.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Measure results before and after any change.
6. Review the core ideas: token bucket, fixed window, sliding window, and 429 responses.
7. Review how Rate Limiting interacts with Caching Strategies.
8. Try the approach on a small case, for example protecting APIs.
