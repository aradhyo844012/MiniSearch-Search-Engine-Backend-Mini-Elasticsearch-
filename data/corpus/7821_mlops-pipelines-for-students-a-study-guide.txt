MLOps Pipelines for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover MLOps Pipelines.

Learning goals:
1. Explain MLOps Pipelines in your own words.
2. Describe data versioning, training pipelines, the model registry, and continuous delivery.
3. Give examples from repeatable model releases and tracking experiments.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: MLOps Pipelines is automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models. Then draw a factory line that turns data into a tested, packaged model on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is untracked data changes a problem? How does training pipelines relate to the model registry? When would you choose MLflow or Kubeflow?

Study habit: version both the code and the data.

When you feel ready, compare MLOps Pipelines with Speech Recognition and write down three differences.
