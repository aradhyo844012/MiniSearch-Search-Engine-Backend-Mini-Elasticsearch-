Everything You Need to Know About Biometric Authentication

This page collects the essentials about Biometric Authentication in one place. In short, Biometric Authentication is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices.

Core concepts:
- enrollment
- matching
- false accept and false reject rates
- liveness detection

Where it is used:
- phone unlock
- building access
- payment approval

Common problems:
- spoofing attacks
- biased recognition
- credentials that cannot be changed once leaked

Tools to know:
- Android BiometricPrompt
- Face ID APIs
- FIDO2

Remember this above all: a door that recognizes you by your face instead of a key is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
