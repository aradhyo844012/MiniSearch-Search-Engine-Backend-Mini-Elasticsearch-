Secrets Management Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Secrets Management.

Do:
- Learn vaults before anything else.
- Try HashiCorp Vault on a small example.
- Make it a habit to rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
- Test the case of database credentials early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: secrets in source control.
- Ignore this: long-lived shared credentials.
- Ignore this: no rotation plan.
- Adopt Secrets Management just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Secrets Management, a little patience with rotation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
