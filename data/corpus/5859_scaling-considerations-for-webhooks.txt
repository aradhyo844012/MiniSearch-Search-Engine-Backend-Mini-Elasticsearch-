Scaling Considerations for Webhooks

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Webhooks changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding event payloads and getting payment notifications right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for not verifying signatures and slow handlers causing timeouts, and measure with ngrok before changing anything.

Large scale: retries and endpoints move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around CI triggers at this stage.

Before you scale, respond quickly and process the event in a background job. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
