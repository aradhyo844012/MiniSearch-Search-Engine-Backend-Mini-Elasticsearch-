MQTT Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with MQTT regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip publish and subscribe; everything else builds on it.
2. Be careful with this one: unsecured brokers.
3. Write down why you chose MQTT so the next person understands the decision.
4. Reach for Mosquitto first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as paho-mqtt.
5. Make sure you can design topic names like a clear folder structure.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to smart home devices as well.
