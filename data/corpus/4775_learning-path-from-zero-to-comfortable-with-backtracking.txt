Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Backtracking

This path is for a learner who has never studied Backtracking before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Backtracking is a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends) and learn decision tree and pruning.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Python recursion, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving solving Sudoku.

Stage 3, depth: study state restoration and constraint checking, then deliberately explore this problem: forgetting to undo changes.

Stage 4, application: apply Backtracking to generating permutations, compare itertools with debuggers, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Backtracking to someone else, and then move on to Greedy Algorithms.
