Convolutional Neural Networks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Convolutional Neural Networks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Convolutional Neural Networks: neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.
filters: a core term, most visible in image classification.
pooling: a core term that beginners often confuse with feature maps.
feature maps: a core term that connects to stride.
stride: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
PyTorch: a tool commonly used for medical image analysis.

Come back to this Convolutional Neural Networks list whenever documentation for TensorFlow uses a word you do not recognize.
