Everything You Need to Know About Time Management for Developers

This page collects the essentials about Time Management for Developers in one place. In short, Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.

Core concepts:
- prioritization
- time blocking
- breaking down tasks
- avoiding context switches

Where it is used:
- managing coursework and projects
- meeting deadlines
- reducing stress

Common problems:
- multitasking
- underestimating tasks
- working without breaks

Tools to know:
- calendar blocking
- Trello
- the Pomodoro technique

Remember this above all: packing a suitcase with the big items first is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
