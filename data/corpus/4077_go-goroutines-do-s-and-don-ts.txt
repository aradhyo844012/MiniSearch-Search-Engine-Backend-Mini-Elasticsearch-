Go Goroutines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Go Goroutines.

Do:
- Learn goroutines before anything else.
- Try the go command on a small example.
- Make it a habit to run your tests with the race detector turned on.
- Test the case of servers handling many connections early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: goroutine leaks.
- Ignore this: data races.
- Ignore this: unbuffered channels causing deadlocks.
- Adopt Go Goroutines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Go Goroutines, a little patience with channels pays off more than any clever shortcut.
