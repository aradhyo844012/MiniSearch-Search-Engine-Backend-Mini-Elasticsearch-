Teaching Monorepos to a Teammate

Explaining Monorepos to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: one big toolbox for the whole workshop. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Monorepos is single repositories that hold the code for many projects. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sharing libraries across apps, naming shared tooling and code ownership as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain selective builds in Monorepos back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow checkouts, and suggest this habit: set up selective builds and tests early.
