Is Model Deployment Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Model Deployment, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Model Deployment is making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application.

It is a good fit when your work involves recommendation endpoints or fraud checks, and when your team understands serving APIs.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn batch versus online inference, or when you cannot manage these risks: training-serving mismatch, slow cold starts, and no fallback when the model fails.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether containers and latency budgets are part of the answer. If they are, Model Deployment is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with mobile app features and log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
