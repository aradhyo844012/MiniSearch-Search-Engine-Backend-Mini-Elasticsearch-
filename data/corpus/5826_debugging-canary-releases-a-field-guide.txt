Debugging Canary Releases: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Canary Releases, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: too little traffic to reveal problems, no clear success metrics, and manual promotion mistakes.
Step 3: revisit traffic percentages and health metrics, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Argo Rollouts or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Canary Releases in work such as safer releases come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, define rollback conditions before starting the rollout. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
