Autoscaling Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Autoscaling. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.
Key terms: scaling metrics, cooldown periods, minimum and maximum limits, and horizontal scaling.
Typical uses: handling traffic spikes, saving money overnight, and keeping latency steady.
Risks: scaling too slowly, flapping because of short spikes, and ignoring database limits.
Tools: Kubernetes HPA, AWS Auto Scaling, and KEDA.

Mental picture: a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives.

Habit to build: load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
