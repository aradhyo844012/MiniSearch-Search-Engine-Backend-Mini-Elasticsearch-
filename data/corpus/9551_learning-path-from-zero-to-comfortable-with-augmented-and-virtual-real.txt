Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Augmented and Virtual Reality

This path is for a learner who has never studied Augmented and Virtual Reality before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one) and learn head tracking and rendering at high frame rates.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Unity XR, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving training simulations.

Stage 3, depth: study spatial mapping and input methods, then deliberately explore this problem: motion sickness from low frame rates.

Stage 4, application: apply Augmented and Virtual Reality to games, compare WebXR with ARKit, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Augmented and Virtual Reality to someone else, and then move on to Blockchain Fundamentals.
