Procedural Generation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Procedural Generation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Procedural Generation: creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms.
random seeds: a core term, most visible in roguelike dungeons.
noise functions: a core term that beginners often confuse with rules and constraints.
rules and constraints: a core term that connects to reproducibility.
reproducibility: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Perlin noise: a tool commonly used for infinite terrain.
