Font Rendering Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Font Rendering regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip glyphs; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for FreeType first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Google Fonts.
3. Make sure you can subset fonts to the characters you need.
4. Write down why you chose Font Rendering so the next person understands the decision.
5. Test the case of web pages early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to operating systems as well as to Font Rendering in general.
