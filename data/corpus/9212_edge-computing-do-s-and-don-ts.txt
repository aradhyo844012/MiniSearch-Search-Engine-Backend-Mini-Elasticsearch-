Edge Computing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Edge Computing.

Do:
- Learn edge nodes before anything else.
- Try AWS IoT Greengrass on a small example.
- Make it a habit to decide which data must stay local before designing the system.
- Test the case of factory sensors early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hard-to-update devices.
- Ignore this: inconsistent hardware.
- Ignore this: security at many sites.
- Adopt Edge Computing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Edge Computing, a little patience with latency pays off more than any clever shortcut.
