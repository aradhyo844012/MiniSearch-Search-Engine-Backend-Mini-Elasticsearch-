Load Testing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Load Testing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Load Testing: measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic.
virtual users: a core term, most visible in capacity planning.
throughput: a core term that beginners often confuse with latency percentiles.
latency percentiles: a core term that connects to ramp-up.
ramp-up: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
k6: a tool commonly used for finding bottlenecks.

Come back to this Load Testing list whenever documentation for Locust uses a word you do not recognize.
