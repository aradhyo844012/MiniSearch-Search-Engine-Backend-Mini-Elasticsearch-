Technical Debt Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Technical Debt.

- Definition: the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation.
- Core ideas: shortcuts; interest; refactoring backlog; code smells.
- Use it for: deciding when to cut corners; planning cleanup work; explaining risk to managers.
- Watch out for: never paying it down; hiding it from stakeholders; unclear ownership.
- Tools: SonarQube, issue trackers, code metrics.
- Related topic: Agile and Scrum.
- Quick tip: practice shortcuts until it feels automatic.
