Game Engines in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Game Engines.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Game Engines is software frameworks that provide graphics, physics, input and tools for building games.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn scenes, assets, scripting, and build pipelines.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a fully equipped workshop with the tools for building a game. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through indie games, simulations, and interactive demos.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for choosing an engine by hype and loading everything at start-up, then try Unity for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
