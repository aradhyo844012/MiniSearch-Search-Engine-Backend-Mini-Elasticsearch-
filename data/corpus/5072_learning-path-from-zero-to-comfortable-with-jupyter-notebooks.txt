Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Jupyter Notebooks

This path is for a learner who has never studied Jupyter Notebooks before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes) and learn cells and kernels.

Stage 2, hands-on: install JupyterLab, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving exploring data.

Stage 3, depth: study markdown and outputs, then deliberately explore this problem: running cells out of order.

Stage 4, application: apply Jupyter Notebooks to teaching and demos, compare VS Code with Google Colab, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Jupyter Notebooks to someone else, and then move on to SQL Window Functions.
