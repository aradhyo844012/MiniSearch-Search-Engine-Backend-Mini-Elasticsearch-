Teaching Disk Partitioning and Mounting to a Teammate

Explaining Disk Partitioning and Mounting to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: building rooms inside a warehouse and putting door numbers on them. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Disk Partitioning and Mounting is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving adding a new drive, naming partitions and file system types as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain mount points in Disk Partitioning and Mounting back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, wrong device names, and suggest this habit: double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
