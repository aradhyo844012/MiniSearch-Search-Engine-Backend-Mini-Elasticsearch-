Distributed Tracing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Distributed Tracing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: following a single request as it passes through many services.
Key terms: spans, trace IDs, context propagation, and sampling.
Typical uses: finding slow services, understanding dependencies, and debugging errors across services.
Risks: missing context propagation, sampling too aggressively, and overhead from too many spans.
Tools: OpenTelemetry, Jaeger, and Zipkin.

Mental picture: a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route.

Habit to build: propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.

See also: Autoscaling, which sits close to Distributed Tracing in the area of DevOps and Cloud.
