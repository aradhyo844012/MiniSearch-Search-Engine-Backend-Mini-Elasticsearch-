Why Robotics and ROS Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Robotics and ROS make better decisions, because Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.

Three situations show why it matters. First, warehouse robots. Second, drones. Third, educational robots. In each case, a solid grasp of sensors and actuators separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: ignoring sensor noise, unsafe motion commands, and tight coupling between nodes.

You will not need to build everything yourself; ROS 2, Gazebo, and Arduino cover most needs.
