Graph Traversal: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Graph Traversal?
A: The term Graph Traversal refers to systematic ways of visiting every node in a graph, usually breadth-first or depth-first.

Q: Why do developers use Graph Traversal?
A: Common uses include finding shortest paths in unweighted graphs, detecting cycles, and crawling linked pages.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include NetworkX, Python collections.deque, and Graphviz.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with adjacency lists and breadth-first search, then move on to depth-first search and visited sets.
