Is Software Estimation Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Software Estimation, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.

It is a good fit when your work involves sprint planning or setting deadlines, and when your team understands story points.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn ranges, or when you cannot manage these risks: treating estimates as promises, ignoring unknowns, and estimating alone.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether uncertainty and velocity are part of the answer. If they are, Software Estimation is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with negotiating scope and give ranges instead of single numbers.
