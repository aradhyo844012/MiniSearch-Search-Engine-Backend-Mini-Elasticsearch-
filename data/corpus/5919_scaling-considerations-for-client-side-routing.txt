Scaling Considerations for Client-Side Routing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Client-Side Routing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding history API and getting single-page apps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for broken deep links and unhandled 404 pages, and measure with React Router before changing anything.

Large scale: nested routes and route guards move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around dashboards at this stage.

Before you scale, test refreshing the page on every route. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
