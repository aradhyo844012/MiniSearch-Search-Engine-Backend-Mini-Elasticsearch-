Statistical Significance in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Statistical Significance.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn p-values, the null hypothesis, confidence intervals, and effect size.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine asking how surprised you should be if a coin were actually fair. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through judging experiment results, comparing model performance, and reporting research findings.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for mistaking significance for importance and p-hacking, then try SciPy for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to always report the effect size alongside the p-value.
