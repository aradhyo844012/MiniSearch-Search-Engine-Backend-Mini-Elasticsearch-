The Pros and Cons of Idempotency

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Idempotency is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports payment APIs
- Makes message processing easier
- Helps with retrying failed requests

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: keys that expire too soon
- Risk: side effects outside the guarded step
- Risk: reusing keys for different requests

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as payment APIs, and you can manage the risks, Idempotency is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, store the result with the key so retries return the same response.
