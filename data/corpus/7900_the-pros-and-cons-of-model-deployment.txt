The Pros and Cons of Model Deployment

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Model Deployment is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports recommendation endpoints
- Makes fraud checks easier
- Helps with mobile app features

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: training-serving mismatch
- Risk: slow cold starts
- Risk: no fallback when the model fails

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as recommendation endpoints, and you can manage the risks, Model Deployment is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
