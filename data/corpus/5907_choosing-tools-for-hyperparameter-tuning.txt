Choosing Tools for Hyperparameter Tuning

There is no single best tool for Hyperparameter Tuning. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Optuna is a common first choice, especially for improving model accuracy. scikit-learn GridSearchCV suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Ray Tune is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle comparing algorithms well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: tuning on the test set.

As a rule of thumb, try random search before anything fancier, no matter which tool you use.
