Understanding Software Estimation Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable.

In that picture, story points is the first rule you follow, ranges is the next step, and uncertainty and velocity keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take, and it involves details such as treating estimates as promises that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for planning poker to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain sprint planning to a friend.
