Autoscaling in Real Projects

Reading about Autoscaling is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: handling traffic spikes. Here, scaling metrics drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against scaling too slowly.

Scenario two: saving money overnight. This time cooldown periods matters most, and tools such as Kubernetes HPA help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: keeping latency steady. The key idea is minimum and maximum limits, and the biggest risk is flapping because of short spikes.

Across all three scenarios, Autoscaling stays the same idea: automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes. Only the details change.
