C Pointers in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through C Pointers.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: C Pointers is variables that store the memory address of another value.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn addresses, dereferencing, pointer arithmetic, and null pointers.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a street address written on a card instead of the house itself. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through dynamic memory allocation, passing large data efficiently, and building linked structures.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for dereferencing null pointers and memory leaks, then try GCC for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to set pointers to NULL after freeing them.
