The Pros and Cons of Regular Expressions

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Regular Expressions is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports validating input
- Makes searching logs easier
- Helps with extracting fields from text

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: catastrophic backtracking
- Risk: patterns that are too greedy
- Risk: using them to parse HTML

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as validating input, and you can manage the risks, Regular Expressions is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, build patterns in small steps and test each one.
