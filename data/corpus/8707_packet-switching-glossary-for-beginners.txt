Packet Switching Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Packet Switching feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Packet Switching: a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network.
packets: a core term, most visible in internet traffic.
headers: a core term that beginners often confuse with routing.
routing: a core term that connects to reassembly.
reassembly: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Wireshark: a tool commonly used for voice over IP.

Come back to this Packet Switching list whenever documentation for tcpdump uses a word you do not recognize.
