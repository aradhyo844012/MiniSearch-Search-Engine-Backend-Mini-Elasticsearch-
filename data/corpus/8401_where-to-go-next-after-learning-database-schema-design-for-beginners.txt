Where to Go Next After Learning Database Schema Design for Beginners

You have met the basics of Database Schema Design for Beginners. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Elasticsearch Concepts, which belongs to the same area as Database Schema Design for Beginners (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of entities, relationships, keys or naming conventions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reviewing existing schemas with dbdiagram.io, and note every problem you meet, starting with storing lists in a single column.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Database Schema Design for Beginners to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
