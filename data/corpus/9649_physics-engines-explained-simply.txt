Physics Engines Explained Simply

If Physics Engines sounds intimidating, relax. It is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Physics Engines as a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: rigid bodies, forces and impulses, constraints, and fixed time steps. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with rigid bodies, then add forces and impulses.

You will meet Physics Engines in work such as vehicles and ragdolls, puzzle games, and training simulations.

One practical tip is to use a fixed time step for stable results.

After Physics Engines, a natural next topic is Sprite Animation.
