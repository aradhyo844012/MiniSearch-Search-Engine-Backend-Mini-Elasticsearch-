Speech Recognition Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Speech Recognition. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: converting spoken language into text using machine learning.
Key terms: audio features, acoustic models, language models, and word error rate.
Typical uses: voice assistants, captioning, and dictation.
Risks: accents and background noise, domain-specific vocabulary, and privacy of recordings.
Tools: Whisper, Kaldi, and cloud speech-to-text services.

Mental picture: a stenographer who types as people talk.

Habit to build: measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
