CQRS Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for CQRS. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.
Key terms: commands, queries, read models, and eventual consistency.
Typical uses: high-read dashboards, complex domains, and scaling reads independently.
Risks: adding it to simple apps, read models drifting out of sync, and duplicated logic.
Tools: Axon Framework, MediatR, and Apache Kafka.

Mental picture: a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup.

Habit to build: start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.

See also: Leader Election, which sits close to CQRS in the area of Distributed Systems.
