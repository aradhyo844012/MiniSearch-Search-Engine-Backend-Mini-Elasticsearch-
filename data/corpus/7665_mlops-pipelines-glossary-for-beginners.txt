MLOps Pipelines Glossary for Beginners

New words can make MLOps Pipelines feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

MLOps Pipelines: automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models.
data versioning: a core term, most visible in repeatable model releases.
training pipelines: a core term that beginners often confuse with the model registry.
the model registry: a core term that connects to continuous delivery.
continuous delivery: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
MLflow: a tool commonly used for tracking experiments.

Come back to this MLOps Pipelines list whenever documentation for Kubeflow uses a word you do not recognize.
