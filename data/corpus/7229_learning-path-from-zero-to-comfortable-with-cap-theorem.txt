Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with CAP Theorem

This path is for a learner who has never studied CAP Theorem before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (CAP Theorem is a result stating that a distributed system cannot guarantee consistency, availability and partition tolerance all at once) and learn consistency and availability.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Apache Cassandra, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving choosing database types.

Stage 3, depth: study partition tolerance and trade-offs, then deliberately explore this problem: treating it as a three-way menu.

Stage 4, application: apply CAP Theorem to designing multi-region services, compare etcd with ZooKeeper, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach CAP Theorem to someone else, and then move on to Leader Election.
