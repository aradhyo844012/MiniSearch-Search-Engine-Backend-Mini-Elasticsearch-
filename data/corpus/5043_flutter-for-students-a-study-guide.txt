Flutter for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Flutter.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Flutter in your own words.
2. Describe widgets, state, hot reload, and the render tree.
3. Give examples from cross-platform mobile apps and desktop apps.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase. Then draw a box of customizable building blocks drawn by its own engine on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is deep widget trees a problem? How does state relate to hot reload? When would you choose Dart or Flutter DevTools?

Study habit: split big widgets into small reusable ones.

When you feel ready, compare Flutter with Push Notifications and write down three differences.
