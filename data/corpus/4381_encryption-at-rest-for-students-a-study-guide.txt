Encryption at Rest for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Encryption at Rest.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Encryption at Rest in your own words.
2. Describe symmetric keys, key management, disk encryption, and envelope encryption.
3. Give examples from protecting backups and meeting compliance rules.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database. Then draw a locked safe inside a locked room on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is storing keys next to the data a problem? How does key management relate to disk encryption? When would you choose LUKS or AWS KMS?

Study habit worth keeping: keep keys in a separate service from the data.

When you feel ready, compare Encryption at Rest with Security Headers and write down three differences.
