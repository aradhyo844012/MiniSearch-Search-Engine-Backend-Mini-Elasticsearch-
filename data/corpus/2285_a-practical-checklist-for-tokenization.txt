A Practical Checklist for Tokenization

Use this checklist before you rely on Tokenization in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Tokenization should accomplish.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on splitting on punctuation.
3. Guard against this risk: dropping useful numbers.
4. Try the approach on a small case, for example indexing documents.
5. Guard against this risk: breaking on apostrophes badly.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers lowercasing.
7. Document your decisions in the project README.
