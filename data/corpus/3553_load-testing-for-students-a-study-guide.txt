Load Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Load Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Load Testing in your own words.
2. Describe virtual users, throughput, latency percentiles, and ramp-up.
3. Give examples from capacity planning and finding bottlenecks.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic. Then draw a stress test that fills a stadium to see how the exits cope on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is testing unrealistic traffic a problem? How does throughput relate to latency percentiles? When would you choose k6 or Locust?

Study habit worth keeping: report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.

When you feel ready, compare Load Testing with Chaos Engineering and write down three differences.
