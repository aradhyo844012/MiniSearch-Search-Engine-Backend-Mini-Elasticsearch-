Teaching Regions and Availability Zones to a Teammate

Explaining Regions and Availability Zones to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Regions and Availability Zones is the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving placing services near users, naming regions and availability zones as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain latency in Regions and Availability Zones back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, putting everything in one zone, and suggest this habit: spread critical services across at least two zones.
