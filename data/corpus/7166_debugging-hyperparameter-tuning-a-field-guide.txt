Debugging Hyperparameter Tuning: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Hyperparameter Tuning, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: tuning on the test set, searching too many options, and ignoring training cost.
Step 3: revisit search spaces and grid search, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Optuna or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Hyperparameter Tuning in work such as improving model accuracy come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, try random search before anything fancier. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
