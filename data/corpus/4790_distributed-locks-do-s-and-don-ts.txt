Distributed Locks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Distributed Locks.

Do:
- Learn lease times before anything else.
- Try Redis on a small example.
- Make it a habit to always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
- Test the case of running scheduled jobs once early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: locks that never expire.
- Ignore this: clock drift.
- Ignore this: assuming the lock holder is still alive.
- Adopt Distributed Locks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Distributed Locks, a little patience with fencing tokens pays off more than any clever shortcut.
