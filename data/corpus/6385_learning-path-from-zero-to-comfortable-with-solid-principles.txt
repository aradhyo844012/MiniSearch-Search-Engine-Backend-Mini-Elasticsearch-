Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with SOLID Principles

This path is for a learner who has never studied SOLID Principles before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (SOLID Principles is five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change) and learn single responsibility and the open-closed principle.

Stage 2, hands-on: install UML diagrams, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving structuring classes.

Stage 3, depth: study Liskov substitution and dependency inversion, then deliberately explore this problem: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts.

Stage 4, application: apply SOLID Principles to reviewing designs, compare SonarQube with IDE refactoring tools, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach SOLID Principles to someone else, and then move on to Pair Programming.
