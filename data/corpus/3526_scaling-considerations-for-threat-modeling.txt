Scaling Considerations for Threat Modeling

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Threat Modeling changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding assets and getting designing new features right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for doing it once and never again and ignoring data flows, and measure with STRIDE before changing anything.

Large scale: trust boundaries and mitigations move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around reviewing architecture at this stage.

Before you scale, draw the data flow diagram first. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
