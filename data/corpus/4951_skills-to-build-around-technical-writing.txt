Skills to Build Around Technical Writing

Knowing the definition of Technical Writing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with audience and structure, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Markdown on a Technical Writing task, and try at least one alternative such as Grammarly.

Judgment: learn to decide when Technical Writing fits a problem such as documentation, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example giving directions to a stranger.

Habits: state the main point in the first two sentences, and write down what you learn after each project.
