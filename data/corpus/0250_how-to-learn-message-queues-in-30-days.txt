How to Learn Message Queues in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Message Queues. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Message Queues in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to systems that let services send work to each other asynchronously through a buffer.

Week 1: learn producers and consumers, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with RabbitMQ on a small task such as sending emails.
Week 3: build a mini project around processing uploads.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as losing messages without acknowledgements, and explain Message Queues to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka, and Amazon SQS.

Clear contracts between services make systems easier to change and test.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Message Queues.
