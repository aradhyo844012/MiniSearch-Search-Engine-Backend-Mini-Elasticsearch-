Everything You Need to Know About Speech Recognition

This page collects the essentials about Speech Recognition in one place. In short, Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning.

Core concepts:
- audio features
- acoustic models
- language models
- word error rate

Where it is used:
- voice assistants
- captioning
- dictation

Common problems:
- accents and background noise
- domain-specific vocabulary
- privacy of recordings

Tools to know:
- Whisper
- Kaldi
- cloud speech-to-text services

Remember this above all: a stenographer who types as people talk is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
