Mobile App Performance Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Mobile App Performance.

Q: Explain Mobile App Performance in simple words.
A: Mobile App Performance refers to making mobile apps start quickly, run smoothly and use little battery. I would add an example involving smoother scrolling.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: loading everything at startup.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as startup time, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: startup time, frame rate, memory use, and network calls.

Q: How does it connect to Android Basics?
A: Both belong to the area of Mobile Development, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: loading everything at startup; large images; and too many background tasks.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with longer battery life, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
