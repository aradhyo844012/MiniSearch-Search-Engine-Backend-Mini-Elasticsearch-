Skills to Build Around Number Theory and RSA

Knowing the definition of Number Theory and RSA is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with prime numbers and modular arithmetic, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Python pow on a Number Theory and RSA task, and try at least one alternative such as OpenSSL.

Judgment: learn to decide when Number Theory and RSA fits a problem such as public key cryptography, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a clock whose numbers wrap around.

Habits: use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own, and write down what you learn after each project.
