The Pros and Cons of Network Troubleshooting

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Network Troubleshooting is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports fixing office connectivity
- Makes diagnosing slow apps easier
- Helps with verifying firewall changes

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: changing several things at once
- Risk: blaming the application too early
- Risk: ignoring DNS

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as fixing office connectivity, and you can manage the risks, Network Troubleshooting is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, work from the bottom layer upward.
