MQTT vs Embedded Systems: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study MQTT or Embedded Systems first. Both sit in the area of Mobile, Embedded and IoT, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks, and it is closely tied to publish and subscribe and topics. Embedded Systems, by contrast, is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits, and it is closely tied to microcontrollers and firmware.

Choose MQTT first if you care about sensor networks or smart home devices. Choose Embedded Systems first if you care about appliances or medical devices.

Think of MQTT as a radio station with channels that devices can tune into, and of Embedded Systems as a small brain inside an appliance that only knows one job. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With MQTT, avoid unsecured brokers. With Embedded Systems, avoid memory leaks on tiny devices.

A good habit for MQTT is to design topic names like a clear folder structure. A good habit for Embedded Systems is to plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
