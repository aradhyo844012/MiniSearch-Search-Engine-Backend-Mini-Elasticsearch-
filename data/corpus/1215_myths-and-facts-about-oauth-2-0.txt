Myths and Facts About OAuth 2.0

A lot of folklore has built up around OAuth 2.0. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. Many students first meet OAuth 2.0 in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to a framework that lets applications access resources on a user's behalf without sharing passwords.

Myth: OAuth 2.0 is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand authorization server and access tokens.

Myth: OAuth 2.0 is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as sign in with another account benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: Auth0, Keycloak, and authlib make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with OAuth 2.0 are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example loose redirect URI checks.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit OAuth 2.0 whenever a new project gives you a reason.
