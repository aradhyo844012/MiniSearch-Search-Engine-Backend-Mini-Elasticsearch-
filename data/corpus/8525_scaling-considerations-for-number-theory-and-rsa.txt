Scaling Considerations for Number Theory and RSA

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Number Theory and RSA changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding prime numbers and getting public key cryptography right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using small primes and implementing crypto by hand in production, and measure with Python pow before changing anything.

Large scale: greatest common divisor and modular exponentiation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around hash functions at this stage.

Before you scale, use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
