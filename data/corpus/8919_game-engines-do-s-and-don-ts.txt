Game Engines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Game Engines.

Do:
- Learn scenes before anything else.
- Try Unity on a small example.
- Make it a habit to build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
- Test the case of indie games early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: choosing an engine by hype.
- Ignore this: loading everything at start-up.
- Ignore this: ignoring platform limits.
- Adopt Game Engines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Game Engines, a little patience with assets pays off more than any clever shortcut.
