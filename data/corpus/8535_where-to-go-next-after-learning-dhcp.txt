Where to Go Next After Learning DHCP

You have met the basics of DHCP. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about VPNs, which belongs to the same area as DHCP (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of leases, address pools, reservations or discover and offer messages, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around guest Wi-Fi with dnsmasq, and note every problem you meet, starting with rogue DHCP servers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining DHCP to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
