Backtracking Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Backtracking. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends.
Key terms: decision tree, pruning, state restoration, and constraint checking.
Typical uses: solving Sudoku, generating permutations, and N-Queens puzzles.
Risks: forgetting to undo changes, missing pruning rules, and copying large state at every step.
Tools: Python recursion, itertools, and debuggers.

Mental picture: walking a maze and returning to the last fork whenever you hit a wall.

Habit to build: prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
