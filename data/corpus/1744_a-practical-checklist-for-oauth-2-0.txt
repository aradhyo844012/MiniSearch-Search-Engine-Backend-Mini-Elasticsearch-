A Practical Checklist for OAuth 2.0

Use this checklist before you rely on OAuth 2.0 in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what OAuth 2.0 should accomplish.
2. Guard against this risk: requesting excessive scopes.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example sign in with another account.
4. Guard against this risk: loose redirect URI checks.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Review how OAuth 2.0 interacts with Secure Coding.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on access tokens.
