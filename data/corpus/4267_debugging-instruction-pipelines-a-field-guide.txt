Debugging Instruction Pipelines: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Instruction Pipelines, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: unpredictable branches, dependency chains, and assuming each instruction takes one step.
Step 3: revisit fetch, decode and execute stages and hazards, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use perf or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Instruction Pipelines in work such as explaining CPU speed come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
