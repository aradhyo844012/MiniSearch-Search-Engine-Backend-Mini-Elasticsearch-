MongoDB Aggregation Explained Simply

If MongoDB Aggregation sounds intimidating, relax. It is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of MongoDB Aggregation as an assembly line where each station filters or reshapes the parts. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: pipeline stages, $match and $group, projection, and indexes. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with pipeline stages, then add $match and $group.

People reach for MongoDB Aggregation when they work on summarizing orders, building analytics queries, and reshaping documents.

One practical tip is to filter with $match as early as possible.

Once MongoDB Aggregation feels comfortable, read about Subqueries and CTEs, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
