Is Property-Based Testing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Property-Based Testing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold.

It is a good fit when your work involves testing parsers or checking sorting and math functions, and when your team understands properties.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn generators, or when you cannot manage these risks: vague properties, slow generators, and ignoring random seed reports.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether shrinking and invariants are part of the answer. If they are, Property-Based Testing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with finding edge cases and save failing examples as regular tests.
