Combining Star Schema Modeling with Data Quality Checks in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Star Schema Modeling and Data Quality Checks can work together in a single project.

Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables. Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

Imagine a project that involves business intelligence reports and protecting dashboards. Star Schema Modeling brings fact tables, while Data Quality Checks brings null checks.

A sensible plan is to start with dbt for the Star Schema Modeling part and Great Expectations for the Data Quality Checks part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: over-normalized dimensions on one side, and checks only at the end of pipelines on the other.

Picture the whole as a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions, working alongside a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line.

Two habits help: first, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else; second, check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
