Convolutional Neural Networks in Real Projects

Reading about Convolutional Neural Networks is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: image classification. Here, filters drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against too little training data.

Scenario two: medical image analysis. This time pooling matters most, and tools such as PyTorch help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: object detection. The key idea is feature maps, and the biggest risk is ignoring data augmentation.

Across all three scenarios, Convolutional Neural Networks stays the same idea: neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Only the details change.
