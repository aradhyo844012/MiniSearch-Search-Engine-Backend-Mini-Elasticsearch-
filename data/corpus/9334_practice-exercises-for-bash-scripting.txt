Practice Exercises for Bash Scripting

These exercises build real understanding of Bash Scripting. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Bash and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Bash Scripting and include the term variables.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble automating deployments or processing text files.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: unquoted variables. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Explain how exit codes and functions differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Bash Scripting is writing shell scripts that automate tasks by chaining commands, variables and control flow.
