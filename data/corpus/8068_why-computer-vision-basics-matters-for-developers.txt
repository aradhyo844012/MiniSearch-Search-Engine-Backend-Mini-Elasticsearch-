Why Computer Vision Basics Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Computer Vision Basics make better decisions, because Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video.

Three situations show why it matters. First, face detection. Second, document scanning. Third, quality inspection. In each case, a solid grasp of pixels and filters and edges separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: lighting changes, biased datasets, and overfitting to a small set of photos.

Good tools exist, including OpenCV, Pillow, and scikit-image, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
