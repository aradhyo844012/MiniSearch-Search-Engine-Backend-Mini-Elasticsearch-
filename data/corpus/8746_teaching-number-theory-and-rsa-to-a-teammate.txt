Teaching Number Theory and RSA to a Teammate

Explaining Number Theory and RSA to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a clock whose numbers wrap around. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving public key cryptography, naming prime numbers and modular arithmetic as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain greatest common divisor in Number Theory and RSA back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, using small primes, and suggest this habit: use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
