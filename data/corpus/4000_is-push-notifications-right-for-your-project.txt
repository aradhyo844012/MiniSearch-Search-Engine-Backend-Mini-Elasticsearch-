Is Push Notifications Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Push Notifications, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed.

It is a good fit when your work involves order updates or chat alerts, and when your team understands device tokens.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn push services, or when you cannot manage these risks: asking for permission too early, sending too many messages, and ignoring token refresh.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether payloads and permissions are part of the answer. If they are, Push Notifications is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with re-engaging users and ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
