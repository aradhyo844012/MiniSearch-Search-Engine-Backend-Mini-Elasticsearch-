DHCP for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover DHCP.

Learning goals:
1. Explain DHCP in your own words.
2. Describe leases, address pools, reservations, and discover and offer messages.
3. Give examples from home and office networks and guest Wi-Fi.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: DHCP is a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings. Then draw a hotel desk that hands out room numbers for a limited stay on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is rogue DHCP servers a problem? How does address pools relate to reservations? When would you choose dnsmasq or ISC Kea?

Study habit worth keeping: reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.

When you feel ready, compare DHCP with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 and write down three differences.
