Everything You Need to Know About Digital Twins

This page collects the essentials about Digital Twins in one place. In short, Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.

Core concepts:
- sensor data
- simulation models
- synchronization
- analytics

Where it is used:
- factory monitoring
- building management
- predictive maintenance

Common problems:
- stale data
- over-detailed models
- unclear goals

Tools to know:
- Azure Digital Twins
- Unity
- MQTT

Remember this above all: a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to define the question the twin must answer first.
