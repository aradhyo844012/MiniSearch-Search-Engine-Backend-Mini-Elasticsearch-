Skills to Build Around Physics Engines

Knowing the definition of Physics Engines is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with rigid bodies and forces and impulses, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Box2D on a Physics Engines task, and try at least one alternative such as Bullet.

Judgment: learn to decide when Physics Engines fits a problem such as vehicles and ragdolls, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop.

Habits: use a fixed time step for stable results, and write down what you learn after each project.
