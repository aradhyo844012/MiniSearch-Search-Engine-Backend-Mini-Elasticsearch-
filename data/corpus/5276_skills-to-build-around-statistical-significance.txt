Skills to Build Around Statistical Significance

Knowing the definition of Statistical Significance is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with p-values and the null hypothesis, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with SciPy on a Statistical Significance task, and try at least one alternative such as statsmodels.

Judgment: learn to decide when Statistical Significance fits a problem such as judging experiment results, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example asking how surprised you should be if a coin were actually fair.

Habits: always report the effect size alongside the p-value, and write down what you learn after each project.
