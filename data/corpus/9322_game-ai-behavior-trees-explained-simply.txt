Game AI Behavior Trees Explained Simply

Here is Game AI Behavior Trees without the jargon: it is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does.

Think of Game AI Behavior Trees as a flowchart a character consults every moment before acting. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: selector nodes, sequence nodes, conditions, and actions. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with selector nodes, then add sequence nodes.

People reach for Game AI Behavior Trees when they work on enemy behavior, companion characters, and simulated crowds.

One practical tip is to visualize the tree while the game is running.
