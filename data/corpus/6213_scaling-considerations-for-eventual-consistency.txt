Scaling Considerations for Eventual Consistency

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Eventual Consistency changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding replication delay and getting global social feeds right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for assuming reads are always fresh and unresolved write conflicts, and measure with Amazon DynamoDB before changing anything.

Large scale: read-your-writes and convergence move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around shopping cart services at this stage.

Before you scale, decide up front how your app handles stale reads. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
