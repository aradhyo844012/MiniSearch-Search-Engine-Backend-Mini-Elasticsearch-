Hexagonal Architecture Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Hexagonal Architecture.

Do:
- Learn ports before anything else.
- Try Spring on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep framework imports out of the core domain.
- Test the case of testable business logic early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too many layers for small apps.
- Ignore this: leaking framework types into the core.
- Ignore this: confusing ports with adapters.
- Adopt Hexagonal Architecture just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Hexagonal Architecture, a little patience with adapters pays off more than any clever shortcut.
