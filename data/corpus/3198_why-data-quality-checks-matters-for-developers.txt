Why Data Quality Checks Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Data Quality Checks make better decisions, because Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

Three situations show why it matters. First, protecting dashboards. Second, validating ingestion. Third, catching pipeline bugs. In each case, a solid grasp of null checks and uniqueness separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: alerts nobody reads, checks only at the end of pipelines, and no ownership of failures.

Good tools exist, including Great Expectations, dbt tests, and Deequ, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
