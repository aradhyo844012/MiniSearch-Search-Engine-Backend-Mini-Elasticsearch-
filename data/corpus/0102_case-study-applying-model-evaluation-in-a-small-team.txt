Case Study: Applying Model Evaluation in a Small Team

Imagine a small student team building a project that depends on Model Evaluation. The term Model Evaluation refers to measuring how well a machine learning model works on data it has not seen. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

The team first spent a week learning accuracy and precision and recall. Early on they hit a familiar problem: using accuracy on imbalanced data. After fixing it, they used scikit-learn metrics to check their work and adopted Model Evaluation for comparing models. Looking back, they said the biggest lesson was to measure before optimizing.

Three issues come up again and again: using accuracy on imbalanced data; tuning on the test set; and ignoring variance between runs. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Model Evaluation.
