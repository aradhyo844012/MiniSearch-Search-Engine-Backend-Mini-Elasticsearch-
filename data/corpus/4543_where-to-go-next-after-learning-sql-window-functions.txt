Where to Go Next After Learning SQL Window Functions

You have met the basics of SQL Window Functions. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Anonymization, which belongs to the same area as SQL Window Functions (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of the OVER clause, partitions, ordering or frames, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around ranking rows with PostgreSQL, and note every problem you meet, starting with forgetting ORDER BY inside the window.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining SQL Window Functions to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
