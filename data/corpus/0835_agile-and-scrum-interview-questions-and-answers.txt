Agile and Scrum Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Agile and Scrum.

Q: Explain Agile and Scrum in simple words.
A: Agile and Scrum refers to iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles. I would add an example involving student team projects.

Q: How does it connect to Design Patterns?
A: Both belong to the area of Software Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: skipping retrospectives.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as sprints, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Jira, Trello, and GitHub Projects.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: sprints, backlogs, stand-ups, and retrospectives.
