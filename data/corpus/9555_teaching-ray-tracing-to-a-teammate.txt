Teaching Ray Tracing to a Teammate

Explaining Ray Tracing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving movie visuals, naming rays and intersections as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain reflection and refraction in Ray Tracing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow render times, and suggest this habit: start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
