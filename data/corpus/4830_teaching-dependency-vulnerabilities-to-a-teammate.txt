Teaching Dependency Vulnerabilities to a Teammate

Explaining Dependency Vulnerabilities to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: checking every ingredient supplier for recalls. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Dependency Vulnerabilities is security weaknesses that come from third-party libraries a project uses. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving auditing projects, naming CVE identifiers and version pinning as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain transitive dependencies in Dependency Vulnerabilities back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, ignoring transitive dependencies, and suggest this habit: automate update pull requests.
