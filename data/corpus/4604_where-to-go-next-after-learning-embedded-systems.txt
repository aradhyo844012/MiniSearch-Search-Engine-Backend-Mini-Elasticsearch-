Where to Go Next After Learning Embedded Systems

You have met the basics of Embedded Systems. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Flutter, which belongs to the same area as Embedded Systems (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of microcontrollers, firmware, real-time constraints or memory limits, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around medical devices with C, and note every problem you meet, starting with memory leaks on tiny devices.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Embedded Systems to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
