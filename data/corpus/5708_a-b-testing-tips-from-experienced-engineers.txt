A/B Testing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with A/B Testing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: stopping tests early.
2. Test the case of testing page designs early, before the design hardens.
3. Make sure you can decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit random assignment before anything else.
5. Write down why you chose A/B Testing so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to comparing ranking algorithms as well.
