Disk Partitioning and Mounting for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Disk Partitioning and Mounting.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Disk Partitioning and Mounting in your own words.
2. Describe partitions, file system types, mount points, and fstab.
3. Give examples from adding a new drive and separating data from the system.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Disk Partitioning and Mounting is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree. Then draw building rooms inside a warehouse and putting door numbers on them on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is wrong device names a problem? How does file system types relate to mount points? When would you choose lsblk or fdisk?

Study habit: double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.

When you feel ready, compare Disk Partitioning and Mounting with SSH and Remote Access and write down three differences.
