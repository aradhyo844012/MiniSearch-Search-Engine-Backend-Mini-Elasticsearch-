Choosing Tools for Anomaly Detection

There is no single best tool for Anomaly Detection. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

scikit-learn is a common first choice, especially for fraud detection. PyOD suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Prometheus is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Anomaly Detection by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle server monitoring well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Anomaly Detection, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too many false alarms.

As a rule of thumb, tune alerts using real examples of past incidents, no matter which tool you use.
