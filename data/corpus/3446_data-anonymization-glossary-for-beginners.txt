Data Anonymization Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Data Anonymization feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Data Anonymization: removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.
masking: a core term, most visible in sharing datasets with researchers.
generalization: a core term that beginners often confuse with k-anonymity.
k-anonymity: a core term that connects to differential privacy.
differential privacy: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ARX: a tool commonly used for testing with production-like data.

Come back to this Data Anonymization list whenever documentation for Faker uses a word you do not recognize.
