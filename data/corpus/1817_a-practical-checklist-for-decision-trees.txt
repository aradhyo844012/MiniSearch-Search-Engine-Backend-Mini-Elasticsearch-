A Practical Checklist for Decision Trees

Use this checklist before you rely on Decision Trees in a project.

1. Review the core ideas: splits, entropy and Gini impurity, leaf nodes, and pruning.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as scikit-learn or XGBoost.
3. Guard against this risk: ignoring class imbalance.
4. Guard against this risk: unstable results from small data changes.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Decision Trees should accomplish.
6. Guard against this risk: deep trees that overfit.
7. Review how Decision Trees interacts with Linear Regression.
8. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on entropy and Gini impurity.
9. Write at least one automated test that covers splits.
