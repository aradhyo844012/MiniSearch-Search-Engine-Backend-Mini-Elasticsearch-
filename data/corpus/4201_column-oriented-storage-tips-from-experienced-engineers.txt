Column-Oriented Storage Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Column-Oriented Storage regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: slow single-row lookups.
2. Write down why you chose Column-Oriented Storage so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit compression before anything else.
4. Test the case of analytics dashboards early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can select only the columns you really need.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to large aggregations as well.
