Why Views and Materialized Views Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Views and Materialized Views make better decisions, because Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results.

Three situations show why it matters. First, simplifying repeated queries. Second, hiding sensitive columns. Third, speeding up reports. In each case, a solid grasp of view definitions and refresh strategies separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: stale materialized data, views stacked on views, and surprising performance costs.

You will not need to build everything yourself; PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Snowflake cover most needs.
