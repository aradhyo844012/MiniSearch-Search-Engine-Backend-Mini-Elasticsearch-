Choosing Tools for Sprite Animation

There is no single best tool for Sprite Animation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Aseprite is a common first choice, especially for 2D platformers. Unity Animator suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Phaser is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Sprite Animation by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle user interface effects well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Sprite Animation, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: inconsistent frame timing.

As a rule of thumb, pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls, no matter which tool you use.
