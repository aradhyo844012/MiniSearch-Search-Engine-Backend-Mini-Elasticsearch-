Feature Flags Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Feature Flags feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Feature Flags: switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code.
flag evaluation: a core term, most visible in dark launches.
targeting rules: a core term that beginners often confuse with gradual rollout.
gradual rollout: a core term that connects to flag cleanup.
flag cleanup: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
LaunchDarkly: a tool commonly used for A/B tests.

Come back to this Feature Flags list whenever documentation for Unleash uses a word you do not recognize.
