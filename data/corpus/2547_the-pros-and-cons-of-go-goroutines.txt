The Pros and Cons of Go Goroutines

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Go Goroutines is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports servers handling many connections
- Makes parallel data processing easier
- Helps with background workers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: goroutine leaks
- Risk: data races
- Risk: unbuffered channels causing deadlocks

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as servers handling many connections, and you can manage the risks, Go Goroutines is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, run your tests with the race detector turned on.
