The Trade-offs of Natural Language Processing

Every design choice has a cost, and Natural Language Processing is no exception. The term Natural Language Processing refers to the field of teaching computers to work with human language. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

On the positive side, Natural Language Processing is well suited to search engines and chatbots. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: ignoring language variety; messy text preprocessing; trusting metrics on tiny samples. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much tokenization and embeddings matter to your project.
