Scaling Considerations for Chaos Engineering

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Chaos Engineering changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding steady state and getting testing resilience right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for experimenting without safeguards and having no steady-state metrics, and measure with Chaos Monkey before changing anything.

Large scale: blast radius and fault injection move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around validating failover at this stage.

Before you scale, start with small experiments in a test environment. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
