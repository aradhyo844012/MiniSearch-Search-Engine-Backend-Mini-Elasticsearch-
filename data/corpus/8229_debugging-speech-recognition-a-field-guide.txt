Debugging Speech Recognition: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Speech Recognition, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: accents and background noise, domain-specific vocabulary, and privacy of recordings.
Step 3: revisit audio features and acoustic models, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Whisper or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Speech Recognition in work such as voice assistants come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, measure word error rate on audio like your real users'. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
