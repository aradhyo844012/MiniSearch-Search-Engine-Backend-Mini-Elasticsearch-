Scaling Considerations for Greedy Algorithms

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Greedy Algorithms changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding greedy choice property and getting interval scheduling right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for assuming greedy always works and missing counterexamples, and measure with pen and paper proofs before changing anything.

Large scale: exchange arguments and local versus global optimum move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around Huffman coding at this stage.

Before you scale, try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
