Skills to Build Around LSM Trees

Knowing the definition of LSM Trees is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with memtable and sorted string tables, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with RocksDB on a LSM Trees task, and try at least one alternative such as LevelDB.

Judgment: learn to decide when LSM Trees fits a problem such as write-heavy databases, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a mail room that sorts incoming letters in batches rather than one by one.

Habits: watch compaction metrics before blaming the application, and write down what you learn after each project.
