Color Spaces Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Color Spaces. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.
Key terms: RGB, HSL, CMYK, and gamma.
Typical uses: web design, photo editing, and printing.
Risks: ignoring gamma, mixing color spaces, and poor contrast choices.
Tools: browser color pickers, ImageMagick, and Coolors.

Mental picture: different ways of giving directions to the same destination.

Habit to build: work in one color space and convert only at the end.

See also: Vector Graphics (SVG), which sits close to Color Spaces in the area of Graphics and Multimedia.
