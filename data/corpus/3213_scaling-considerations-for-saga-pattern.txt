Scaling Considerations for Saga Pattern

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Saga Pattern changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding local transactions and getting order processing right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for missing compensations and steps that are not idempotent, and measure with Temporal before changing anything.

Large scale: orchestration and choreography move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around travel bookings at this stage.

Before you scale, make every step and its compensation idempotent. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
