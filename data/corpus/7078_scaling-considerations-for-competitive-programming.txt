Scaling Considerations for Competitive Programming

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Competitive Programming changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding problem patterns and getting interview preparation right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for memorizing solutions without understanding and skipping fundamentals, and measure with Codeforces before changing anything.

Large scale: contests and upsolving move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around learning algorithms at this stage.

Before you scale, review problems you could not solve and study the editorial. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
