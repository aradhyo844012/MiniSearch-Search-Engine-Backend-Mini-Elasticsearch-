The Pros and Cons of High Availability Design

No technology is free of trade-offs, and High Availability Design is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports online stores
- Makes banking systems easier
- Helps with healthcare software

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: hidden single points of failure
- Risk: untested failover
- Risk: correlated failures

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as online stores, and you can manage the risks, High Availability Design is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
