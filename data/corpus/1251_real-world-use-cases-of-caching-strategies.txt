Real-World Use Cases of Caching Strategies

The best way to appreciate Caching Strategies is to see where it is actually used. The term Caching Strategies refers to approaches for storing computed results so they can be reused instead of recalculated. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

A team working on speeding up read-heavy APIs might adopt Caching Strategies after hitting the limits of a simpler approach. Other teams turn to it for reducing database load or serving repeated searches.
