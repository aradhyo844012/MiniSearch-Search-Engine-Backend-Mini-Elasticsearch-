High Availability Design Glossary for Beginners

New words can make High Availability Design feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

High Availability Design: designing systems so they keep working when parts fail.
redundancy: a core term, most visible in online stores.
failover: a core term that beginners often confuse with health checks.
health checks: a core term that connects to no single point of failure.
no single point of failure: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
load balancers: a tool commonly used for banking systems.

Come back to this High Availability Design list whenever documentation for Kubernetes uses a word you do not recognize.
