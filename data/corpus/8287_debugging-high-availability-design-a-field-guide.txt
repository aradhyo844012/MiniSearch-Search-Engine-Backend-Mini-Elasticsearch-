Debugging High Availability Design: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around High Availability Design, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: hidden single points of failure, untested failover, and correlated failures.
Step 3: revisit redundancy and failover, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use load balancers or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving High Availability Design in work such as online stores come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, list every component and ask what happens if it dies. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
