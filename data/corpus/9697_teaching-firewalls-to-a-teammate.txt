Teaching Firewalls to a Teammate

Explaining Firewalls to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a security gate that checks each visitor against a list. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Firewalls is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving protecting servers, naming rules and stateful inspection as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain default deny in Firewalls back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, rules in the wrong order, and suggest this habit: start with default deny and open only what you need.
