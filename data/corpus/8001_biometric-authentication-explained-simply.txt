Biometric Authentication Explained Simply

If Biometric Authentication sounds intimidating, relax. It is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Biometric Authentication as a door that recognizes you by your face instead of a key. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: enrollment, matching, false accept and false reject rates, and liveness detection. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with enrollment, then add matching.

People reach for Biometric Authentication when they work on phone unlock, building access, and payment approval.

One practical tip is to offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
