Graph Traversal Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Graph Traversal.

Q: Explain Graph Traversal in simple words.
A: Graph Traversal refers to systematic ways of visiting every node in a graph, usually breadth-first or depth-first. I would add an example involving finding shortest paths in unweighted graphs.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with detecting cycles, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to Heaps and Priority Queues?
A: Both belong to the area of Data Structures and Algorithms, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as adjacency lists, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: NetworkX, Python collections.deque, and Graphviz.
