End-to-End Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with End-to-End Testing.

Do:
- Learn browser automation before anything else.
- Try Playwright on a small example.
- Make it a habit to cover only the most important user journeys.
- Test the case of checking login and checkout flows early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: flaky tests.
- Ignore this: slow runs.
- Ignore this: brittle selectors.
- Adopt End-to-End Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with End-to-End Testing, a little patience with user flows pays off more than any clever shortcut.
