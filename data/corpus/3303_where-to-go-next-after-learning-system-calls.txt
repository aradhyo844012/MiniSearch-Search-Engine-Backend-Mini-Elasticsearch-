Where to Go Next After Learning System Calls

You have met the basics of System Calls. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Memory Hierarchy, which belongs to the same area as System Calls (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of user mode and kernel mode, syscall numbers, file descriptors or libc wrappers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around creating processes with strace, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring error codes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining System Calls to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
