Understanding Augmented and Virtual Reality Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world.

In that picture, head tracking is the first rule you follow, rendering at high frame rates is the next step, and spatial mapping and input methods keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one, and it involves details such as motion sickness from low frame rates that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Unity XR to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain training simulations to a friend.
