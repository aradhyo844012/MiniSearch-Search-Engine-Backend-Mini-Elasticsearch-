Key Terms in Public Key Cryptography Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Public Key Cryptography much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. Put simply, Public Key Cryptography covers encryption that uses a pair of keys, one public and one private. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Attackers usually choose the easiest weakness, not the most interesting one. Never trust input that comes from outside your own code.
