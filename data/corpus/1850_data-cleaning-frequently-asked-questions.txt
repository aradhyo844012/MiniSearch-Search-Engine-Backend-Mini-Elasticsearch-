Data Cleaning: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Data Cleaning?
A: The term Data Cleaning refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include pandas, OpenRefine, and regular expressions.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Data Cleaning.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with missing values and duplicates, then move on to inconsistent formats and outliers.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: dropping data without checking why it is missing; cleaning by hand without a record; and changing the original files.
