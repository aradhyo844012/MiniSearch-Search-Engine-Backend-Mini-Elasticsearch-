GraphQL Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with GraphQL.

Do:
- Learn schema before anything else.
- Try Apollo on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
- Test the case of mobile apps with limited bandwidth early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: the N+1 query problem.
- Ignore this: unbounded query depth.
- Ignore this: weak caching.
- Adopt GraphQL just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with GraphQL, a little patience with queries and mutations pays off more than any clever shortcut.
