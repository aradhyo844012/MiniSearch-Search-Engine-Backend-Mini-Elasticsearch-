Graph Theory Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Graph Theory Basics.

Do:
- Learn vertices and edges before anything else.
- Try NetworkX on a small example.
- Make it a habit to draw a small example before writing code.
- Test the case of social networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing directed and undirected graphs.
- Ignore this: ignoring disconnected parts.
- Ignore this: choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs.
- Adopt Graph Theory Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Graph Theory Basics, a little patience with degree pays off more than any clever shortcut.
