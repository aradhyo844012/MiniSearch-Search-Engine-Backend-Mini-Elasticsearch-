A Practical Checklist for Browser Rendering

Use this checklist before you rely on Browser Rendering in a project.

1. Pick a tool to start with, such as Chrome DevTools Performance panel or Lighthouse.
2. Document your decisions in the project README.
3. Guard against this risk: blocking scripts in the head.
4. Confirm that your team agrees on what Browser Rendering should accomplish.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example explaining slow pages.
6. Review how Browser Rendering interacts with HTML Semantics.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on style calculation.
