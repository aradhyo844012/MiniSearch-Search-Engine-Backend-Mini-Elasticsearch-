Is Blockchain Fundamentals Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Blockchain Fundamentals, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.

It is a good fit when your work involves digital currencies or supply chain tracking, and when your team understands blocks.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn hashes, or when you cannot manage these risks: treating it as a database replacement, slow throughput, and the energy use of some designs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether consensus and decentralization are part of the answer. If they are, Blockchain Fundamentals is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with public records and ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
