The Importance of Natural Language Processing in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Natural Language Processing until something goes wrong without it. When people talk about Natural Language Processing, they usually mean the field of teaching computers to work with human language. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen. A model is only as good as the data used to train and test it.

A solid grasp of Natural Language Processing also makes Model Evaluation easier to understand.

Typical applications include search engines, chatbots, and sentiment analysis. Small projects and large systems both benefit.

Three issues come up again and again: ignoring language variety; messy text preprocessing; and trusting metrics on tiny samples. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

Natural Language Processing and Model Evaluation are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Natural Language Processing whenever a new project gives you a reason.
