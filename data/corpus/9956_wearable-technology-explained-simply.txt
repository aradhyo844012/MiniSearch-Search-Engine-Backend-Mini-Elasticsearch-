Wearable Technology Explained Simply

If Wearable Technology sounds intimidating, relax. It is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Wearable Technology as a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sensors, low-power design, companion apps, and data syncing. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sensors, then add low-power design.

People reach for Wearable Technology when they work on fitness tracking, notifications, and health monitoring.

One practical tip is to design interactions that take only a few seconds.

After Wearable Technology, a natural next topic is 5G Networks.
