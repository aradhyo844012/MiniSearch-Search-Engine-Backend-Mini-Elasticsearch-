Kubernetes Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Kubernetes.

- Definition: a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications.
- Core ideas: pods; deployments; services; ingress.
- Use it for: running many containers; rolling updates; automatic restarts.
- Watch out for: resource limits missing; over-complicated setups; ignoring health probes.
- Tools: kubectl, Helm, minikube.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for deployments before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice pods until it feels automatic.
