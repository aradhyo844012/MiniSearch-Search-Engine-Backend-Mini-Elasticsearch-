3D Transformations Glossary for Beginners

New words can make 3D Transformations feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

3D Transformations: the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.
translation: a core term, most visible in games.
rotation: a core term that beginners often confuse with scaling.
scaling: a core term that connects to matrices.
matrices: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
GLM: a tool commonly used for modeling tools.

Come back to this 3D Transformations list whenever documentation for three.js uses a word you do not recognize.
