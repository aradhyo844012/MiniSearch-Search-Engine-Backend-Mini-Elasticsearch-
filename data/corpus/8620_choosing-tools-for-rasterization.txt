Choosing Tools for Rasterization

There is no single best tool for Rasterization. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

OpenGL is a common first choice, especially for real-time games. Vulkan suits teams that already work with similar technology, and WebGL is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Rasterization by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle user interfaces well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Rasterization, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: aliasing along edges.

As a rule of thumb, draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline, no matter which tool you use.
