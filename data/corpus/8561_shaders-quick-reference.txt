Shaders Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Shaders. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.
Key terms: vertex shaders, fragment shaders, uniforms, and GLSL.
Typical uses: lighting effects, water and fire visuals, and post-processing.
Risks: branches that slow the GPU, precision errors, and platform differences.
Tools: GLSL, Shadertoy, and RenderDoc.

Mental picture: a tiny painter that colors each pixel following your instructions.

Habit to build: start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.

See also: Procedural Generation, which sits close to Shaders in the area of Game Development.
