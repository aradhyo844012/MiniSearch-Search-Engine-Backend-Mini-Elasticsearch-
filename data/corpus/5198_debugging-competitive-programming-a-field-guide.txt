Debugging Competitive Programming: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Competitive Programming, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: memorizing solutions without understanding, skipping fundamentals, and burning out.
Step 3: revisit problem patterns and time limits, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Codeforces or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Competitive Programming in work such as interview preparation come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, review problems you could not solve and study the editorial. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
