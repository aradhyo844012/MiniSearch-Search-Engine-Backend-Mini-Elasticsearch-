Font Rendering in Real Projects

Reading about Font Rendering is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: web pages. Here, glyphs drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against missing font fallbacks.

Scenario two: operating systems. This time hinting matters most, and tools such as FreeType help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: PDF viewers. The key idea is anti-aliasing, and the biggest risk is blurry text from scaling.

Across all three scenarios, Font Rendering stays the same idea: turning font outlines into readable text on screen. Only the details change.
