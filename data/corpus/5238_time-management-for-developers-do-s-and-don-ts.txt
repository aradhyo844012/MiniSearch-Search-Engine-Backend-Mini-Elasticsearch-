Time Management for Developers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Time Management for Developers.

Do:
- Learn prioritization before anything else.
- Try calendar blocking on a small example.
- Make it a habit to break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
- Test the case of managing coursework and projects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: multitasking.
- Ignore this: underestimating tasks.
- Ignore this: working without breaks.
- Adopt Time Management for Developers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Time Management for Developers, a little patience with time blocking pays off more than any clever shortcut.
