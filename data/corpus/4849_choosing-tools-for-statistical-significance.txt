Choosing Tools for Statistical Significance

There is no single best tool for Statistical Significance. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

SciPy is a common first choice, especially for judging experiment results. statsmodels suits teams that already work with similar technology, and R is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle comparing model performance well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: mistaking significance for importance.

As a rule of thumb, always report the effect size alongside the p-value, no matter which tool you use.
