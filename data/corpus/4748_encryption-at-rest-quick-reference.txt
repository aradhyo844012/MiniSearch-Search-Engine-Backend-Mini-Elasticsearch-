Encryption at Rest Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Encryption at Rest. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database.
Key terms: symmetric keys, key management, disk encryption, and envelope encryption.
Typical uses: protecting backups, meeting compliance rules, and securing laptops.
Risks: storing keys next to the data, no key rotation, and assuming it stops every kind of attack.
Tools: LUKS, AWS KMS, and BitLocker.

Mental picture: a locked safe inside a locked room.

Habit to build: keep keys in a separate service from the data.

See also: Single Sign-On, which sits close to Encryption at Rest in the area of Application Security.
