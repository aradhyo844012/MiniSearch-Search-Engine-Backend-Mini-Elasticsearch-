Gradient Descent Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Gradient Descent.

- Definition: an optimization method that improves a model by repeatedly stepping in the direction that reduces error.
- Core ideas: learning rate; gradients; loss function; batches.
- Use it for: training neural networks; fitting regression models; tuning parameters.
- Watch out for: learning rates that are too large; getting stuck on plateaus; unscaled features.
- Tools: PyTorch, NumPy, TensorFlow.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for gradients before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice learning rate until it feels automatic.
