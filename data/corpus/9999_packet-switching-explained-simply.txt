Packet Switching Explained Simply

Here is Packet Switching without the jargon: it is a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network.

Think of Packet Switching as mailing a book one page per envelope and reassembling it on arrival. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: packets, headers, routing, and reassembly. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with packets, then add headers.

You will meet Packet Switching in work such as internet traffic, voice over IP, and file transfers.

One practical tip is to capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.

Once Packet Switching feels comfortable, read about Network Troubleshooting, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
