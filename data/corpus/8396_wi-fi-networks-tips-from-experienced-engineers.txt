Wi-Fi Networks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Wi-Fi Networks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of home internet early, before the design hardens.
2. Write down why you chose Wi-Fi Networks so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: overlapping channels.
4. Reach for Wi-Fi analyzer apps first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Wireshark.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit channels and bands before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to office coverage as well as to Wi-Fi Networks in general.
