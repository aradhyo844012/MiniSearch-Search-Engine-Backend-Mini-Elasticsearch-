Regular Expressions Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Regular Expressions feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Regular Expressions: compact patterns for matching and extracting text.
character classes: a core term, most visible in validating input.
quantifiers: a core term that beginners often confuse with groups.
groups: a core term that connects to anchors.
anchors: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
the Python re module: a tool commonly used for searching logs.

Come back to this Regular Expressions list whenever documentation for regex101 uses a word you do not recognize.
