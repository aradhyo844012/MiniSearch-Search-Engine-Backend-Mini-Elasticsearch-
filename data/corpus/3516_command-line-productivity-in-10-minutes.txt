Command Line Productivity in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Command Line Productivity.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Command Line Productivity is using shell tools and shortcuts to complete tasks faster.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn aliases, pipes, history search, and scripts.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine learning the shortcuts on a well-used road. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through automating repetitive tasks, exploring files and logs, and managing servers.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for copying commands without understanding them and dangerous wildcard use, then try bash for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to turn commands you repeat into small scripts.
