Reverse Proxies for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Reverse Proxies.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Reverse Proxies in your own words.
2. Describe upstream servers, proxy headers, TLS termination, and caching.
3. Give examples from hiding backend servers and terminating HTTPS.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them. Then draw a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is losing the client IP address a problem? How does proxy headers relate to TLS termination? When would you choose NGINX or Caddy?

Study habit: forward the real client IP in a trusted header.

When you feel ready, compare Reverse Proxies with Wi-Fi Networks and write down three differences.
