Teaching Union-Find to a Teammate

Explaining Union-Find to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a set of club rosters where two clubs can merge into one. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Union-Find is a data structure that tracks which items belong to the same group and merges groups quickly. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving detecting connected components, naming parent pointers and union operation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain find operation in Union-Find back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, forgetting path compression, and suggest this habit: implement path compression and union by rank together.
