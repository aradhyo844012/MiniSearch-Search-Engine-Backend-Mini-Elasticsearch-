Teaching Modular Monoliths to a Teammate

Explaining Modular Monoliths to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: an apartment building with separate flats under one roof. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Modular Monoliths is single deployable applications built from well-separated internal modules. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving small teams, naming module boundaries and public interfaces as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain shared database rules in Modular Monoliths back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, modules reaching into each other's tables, and suggest this habit: enforce boundaries with automated checks.
