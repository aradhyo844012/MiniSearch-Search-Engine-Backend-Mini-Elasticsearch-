Developer Portfolios in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Developer Portfolios.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn projects, README quality, live demos, and clear descriptions.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a shop window displaying your best work. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through applying for internships, showing skills to recruiters, and tracking personal growth.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for too many unfinished projects and no explanation of decisions, then try GitHub Pages for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
