Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Distributed Tracing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Distributed Tracing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services) and learn spans and trace IDs.

Stage 2, hands-on: install OpenTelemetry, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving finding slow services.

Stage 3, depth: study context propagation and sampling, then deliberately explore this problem: missing context propagation.

Stage 4, application: apply Distributed Tracing to understanding dependencies, compare Jaeger with Zipkin, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Distributed Tracing to someone else, and then move on to Service Mesh.
