A Practical Checklist for Data Cleaning

Use this checklist before you rely on Data Cleaning in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Review the core ideas: missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and outliers.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example preparing logs.
4. Guard against this risk: changing the original files.
5. Write at least one automated test that covers missing values.
6. Review how Data Cleaning interacts with Stream Processing.
7. Pick a tool to start with, such as pandas or OpenRefine.
8. Confirm that your team agrees on what Data Cleaning should accomplish.
9. Guard against this risk: dropping data without checking why it is missing.
