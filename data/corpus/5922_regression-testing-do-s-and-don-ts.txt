Regression Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Regression Testing.

Do:
- Learn test suites before anything else.
- Try pytest on a small example.
- Make it a habit to add a test for every bug you fix.
- Test the case of protecting releases early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow suites that nobody runs.
- Ignore this: missing tests for fixed bugs.
- Ignore this: ignoring flaky failures.
- Adopt Regression Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Regression Testing, a little patience with automation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
