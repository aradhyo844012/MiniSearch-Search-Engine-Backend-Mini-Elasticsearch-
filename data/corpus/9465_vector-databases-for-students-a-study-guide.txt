Vector Databases for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Vector Databases.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Vector Databases in your own words.
2. Describe embeddings, approximate nearest neighbors, distance metrics, and metadata filters.
3. Give examples from semantic search and recommendations.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Vector Databases is databases built to store embeddings and find the most similar ones quickly. Then draw a map where similar ideas sit near each other on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing embedding models a problem? How does approximate nearest neighbors relate to distance metrics? When would you choose FAISS or pgvector?

Study habit worth keeping: measure recall on a small labeled test set.

When you feel ready, compare Vector Databases with NULL Handling in SQL and write down three differences.
