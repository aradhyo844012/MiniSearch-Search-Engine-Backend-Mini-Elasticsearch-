Digital Audio in Real Projects

Reading about Digital Audio is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: music production. Here, sampling rate drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against clipping from too high a level.

Scenario two: voice calls. This time bit depth matters most, and tools such as Audacity help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: podcasts. The key idea is waveforms, and the biggest risk is aliasing at low sample rates.

Across all three scenarios, Digital Audio stays the same idea: representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second. Only the details change.
