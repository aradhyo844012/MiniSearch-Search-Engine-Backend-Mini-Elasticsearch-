Physics Engines Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Physics Engines feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Physics Engines: software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.
rigid bodies: a core term, most visible in vehicles and ragdolls.
forces and impulses: a core term that beginners often confuse with constraints.
constraints: a core term that connects to fixed time steps.
fixed time steps: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Box2D: a tool commonly used for puzzle games.

Come back to this Physics Engines list whenever documentation for Bullet uses a word you do not recognize.
