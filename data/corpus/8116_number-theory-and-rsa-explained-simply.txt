Number Theory and RSA Explained Simply

Here is Number Theory and RSA without the jargon: it is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.

Think of Number Theory and RSA as a clock whose numbers wrap around. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: prime numbers, modular arithmetic, greatest common divisor, and modular exponentiation. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with prime numbers, then add modular arithmetic.

People reach for Number Theory and RSA when they work on public key cryptography, hash functions, and digital signatures.

One practical tip is to use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.

After Number Theory and RSA, a natural next topic is Probability Basics.
