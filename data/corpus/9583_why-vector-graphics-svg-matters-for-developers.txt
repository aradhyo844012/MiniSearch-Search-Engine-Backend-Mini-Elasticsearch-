Why Vector Graphics (SVG) Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Vector Graphics (SVG) make better decisions, because Vector Graphics (SVG) is images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size.

Three situations show why it matters. First, logos. Second, icons. Third, charts. In each case, a solid grasp of paths and the viewBox separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: overly complex paths, a missing viewBox, and inconsistent stroke widths.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Inkscape, SVGO, and Figma cover most needs.
