The Pros and Cons of Data Quality Checks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Data Quality Checks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports protecting dashboards
- Makes validating ingestion easier
- Helps with catching pipeline bugs

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: alerts nobody reads
- Risk: checks only at the end of pipelines
- Risk: no ownership of failures

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as protecting dashboards, and you can manage the risks, Data Quality Checks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
