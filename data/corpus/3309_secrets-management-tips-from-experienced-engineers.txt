Secrets Management Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Secrets Management regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip vaults; everything else builds on it.
2. Test the case of database credentials early, before the design hardens.
3. Make sure you can rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
4. Reach for HashiCorp Vault first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as AWS Secrets Manager.
5. Be careful with this one: secrets in source control.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to API keys as well.
