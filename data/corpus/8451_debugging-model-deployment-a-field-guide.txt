Debugging Model Deployment: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Model Deployment, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: training-serving mismatch, slow cold starts, and no fallback when the model fails.
Step 3: revisit serving APIs and batch versus online inference, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use FastAPI or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Model Deployment in work such as recommendation endpoints come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
