Mistakes to Avoid with Hexagonal Architecture

Everyone learns Hexagonal Architecture partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too many layers for small apps. It usually appears when someone skips ports and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: leaking framework types into the core. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of testable business logic. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: confusing ports with adapters. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on dependency direction to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep framework imports out of the core domain.
