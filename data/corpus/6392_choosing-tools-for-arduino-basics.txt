Choosing Tools for Arduino Basics

There is no single best tool for Arduino Basics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Arduino IDE is a common first choice, especially for reading sensors. PlatformIO suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Tinkercad is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle driving motors and LEDs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: wiring errors.

As a rule of thumb, test each sensor on its own before combining them, no matter which tool you use.
