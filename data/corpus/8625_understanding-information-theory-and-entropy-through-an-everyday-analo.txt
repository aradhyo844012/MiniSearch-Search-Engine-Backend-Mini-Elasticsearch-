Understanding Information Theory and Entropy Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture measuring how surprised you are by each new message.

In that picture, bits is the first rule you follow, entropy is the next step, and compression limits and noise keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Information Theory and Entropy is the mathematics of measuring and compressing information, and it involves details such as confusing information with meaning that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for the Python math module to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain data compression to a friend.
