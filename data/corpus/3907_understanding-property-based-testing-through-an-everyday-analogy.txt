Understanding Property-Based Testing Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs.

In that picture, properties is the first rule you follow, generators is the next step, and shrinking and invariants keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold, and it involves details such as vague properties that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Hypothesis to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain testing parsers to a friend.
