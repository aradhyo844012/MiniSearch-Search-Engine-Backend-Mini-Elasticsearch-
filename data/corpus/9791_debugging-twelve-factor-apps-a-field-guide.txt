Debugging Twelve-Factor Apps: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Twelve-Factor Apps, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: storing state on local disks, hard-coded config, and long start-up times.
Step 3: revisit config in the environment and stateless processes, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Heroku or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Twelve-Factor Apps in work such as building web services come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, keep config out of the code. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
