A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with SOLID Principles

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on SOLID Principles.

Morning: Imran starts by reviewing a ticket about structuring classes. Before touching any code, Imran re-reads the notes on single responsibility to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Imran pairs with a teammate to explore the open-closed principle with UML diagrams. A small test exposes the problem of applying them rigidly to tiny scripts, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving reviewing designs. Imran sketches house rules that keep a shared kitchen tidy on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Imran writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to apply them when a design starts to hurt, not before.

Days like this are typical. SOLID Principles is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
