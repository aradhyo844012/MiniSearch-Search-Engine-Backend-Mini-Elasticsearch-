Floating-Point Arithmetic Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Floating-Point Arithmetic regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit rounding before anything else.
2. Test the case of scientific computing early, before the design hardens.
3. Reach for Python decimal first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as NumPy.
4. Make sure you can compare floats with a tolerance and use decimals for money.
5. Do not skip mantissa and exponent; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to graphics as well as to Floating-Point Arithmetic in general.
