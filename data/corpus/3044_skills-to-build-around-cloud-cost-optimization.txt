Skills to Build Around Cloud Cost Optimization

Knowing the definition of Cloud Cost Optimization is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with right-sizing and reserved capacity, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with AWS Cost Explorer on a Cloud Cost Optimization task, and try at least one alternative such as Infracost.

Judgment: learn to decide when Cloud Cost Optimization fits a problem such as cutting idle resources, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using.

Habits: tag every resource with an owner, and write down what you learn after each project.
