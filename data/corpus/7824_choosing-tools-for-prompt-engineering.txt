Choosing Tools for Prompt Engineering

There is no single best tool for Prompt Engineering. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

model playgrounds is a common first choice, especially for drafting content. LangChain suits teams that already work with similar technology, and evaluation sets is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Prompt Engineering by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle extracting data from text well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Prompt Engineering, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: vague requests.

As a rule of thumb, specify the output format and include one or two examples, no matter which tool you use.
