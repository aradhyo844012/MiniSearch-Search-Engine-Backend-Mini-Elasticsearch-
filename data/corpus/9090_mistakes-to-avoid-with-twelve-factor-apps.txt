Mistakes to Avoid with Twelve-Factor Apps

Everyone learns Twelve-Factor Apps partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: storing state on local disks. It usually appears when someone skips config in the environment and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: hard-coded config. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of building web services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: long start-up times. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on disposability to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep config out of the code.
