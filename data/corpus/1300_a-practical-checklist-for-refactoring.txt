A Practical Checklist for Refactoring

Use this checklist before you rely on Refactoring in a project.

1. Review the core ideas: small steps, tests as a safety net, extracting functions, and renaming.
2. Document your decisions in the project README.
3. Guard against this risk: refactoring without tests.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as IDE refactoring tools or pytest.
5. Guard against this risk: mixing refactoring with new features.
6. Measure results before and after any change.
7. Try the approach on a small case, for example reducing duplication.
