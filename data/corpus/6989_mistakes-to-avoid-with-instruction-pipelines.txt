Mistakes to Avoid with Instruction Pipelines

Everyone learns Instruction Pipelines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: unpredictable branches. It usually appears when someone skips fetch, decode and execute stages and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: dependency chains. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of explaining CPU speed. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming each instruction takes one step. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on stalls to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops.
