Everything You Need to Know About Combinatorics

This page collects the essentials about Combinatorics in one place. In short, Combinatorics is the art of counting arrangements and selections.

Core concepts:
- permutations
- combinations
- the pigeonhole principle
- inclusion-exclusion

Where it is used:
- analyzing algorithms
- probability
- estimating password strength

Common problems:
- double counting
- mixing order and no-order cases
- ignoring repetition

Tools to know:
- Python itertools
- math.comb
- SymPy

Remember this above all: counting the ways to seat guests at a table is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
