Packet Switching Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Packet Switching. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network.
Key terms: packets, headers, routing, and reassembly.
Typical uses: internet traffic, voice over IP, and file transfers.
Risks: packet loss, out-of-order delivery, and large packets being fragmented.
Tools: Wireshark, tcpdump, and iperf.

Mental picture: mailing a book one page per envelope and reassembling it on arrival.

Habit to build: capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
