Ray Tracing: Core Concepts and Terminology

This page defines the vocabulary you need to read about Ray Tracing with confidence. The subject itself is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.

rays: one of the building blocks of Ray Tracing. Documentation for Blender Cycles usually mentions it early.
intersections: one of the building blocks of Ray Tracing. Documentation for Blender Cycles usually mentions it early.
reflection and refraction: one of the building blocks of Ray Tracing. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
shadows: one of the building blocks of Ray Tracing. You should be able to explain it in a single sentence before moving on.
