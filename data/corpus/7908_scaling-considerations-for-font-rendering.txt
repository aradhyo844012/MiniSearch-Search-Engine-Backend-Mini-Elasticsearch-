Scaling Considerations for Font Rendering

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Font Rendering changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding glyphs and getting web pages right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for missing font fallbacks and blurry text from scaling, and measure with FreeType before changing anything.

Large scale: anti-aliasing and kerning move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around operating systems at this stage.

Before you scale, subset fonts to the characters you need. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
