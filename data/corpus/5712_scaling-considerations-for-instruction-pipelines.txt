Scaling Considerations for Instruction Pipelines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Instruction Pipelines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding fetch, decode and execute stages and getting explaining CPU speed right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for unpredictable branches and dependency chains, and measure with perf before changing anything.

Large scale: branch prediction and stalls move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around understanding branch costs at this stage.

Before you scale, avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
