Why A/B Testing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand A/B Testing make better decisions, because A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.

Three situations show why it matters. First, testing page designs. Second, comparing ranking algorithms. Third, evaluating new features. In each case, a solid grasp of control and variant and random assignment separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: stopping tests early, testing too many variants, and ignoring sample size.

Good tools exist, including Optimizely, GrowthBook, and statsmodels, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
