Neural Networks Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Neural Networks.

- Definition: models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data.
- Core ideas: layers; weights; activation functions; backpropagation.
- Use it for: image recognition; speech recognition; language tasks.
- Watch out for: too little training data; poor learning rate choices; not monitoring validation loss.
- Tools: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for weights before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice layers until it feels automatic.
