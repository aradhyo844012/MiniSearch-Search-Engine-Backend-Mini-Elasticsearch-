Mistakes to Avoid with Property-Based Testing

Everyone learns Property-Based Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: vague properties. It usually appears when someone skips properties and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow generators. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of testing parsers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring random seed reports. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on invariants to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: save failing examples as regular tests.
