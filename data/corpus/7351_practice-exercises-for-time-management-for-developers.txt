Practice Exercises for Time Management for Developers

These exercises build real understanding of Time Management for Developers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Time Management for Developers and include the term prioritization.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble managing coursework and projects or meeting deadlines.
Exercise 3: Pick calendar blocking and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare Trello and the Pomodoro technique for reducing stress and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: multitasking. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.
