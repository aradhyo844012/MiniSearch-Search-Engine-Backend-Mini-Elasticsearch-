API Design Principles in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through API Design Principles.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: API Design Principles is guidelines for making APIs consistent, predictable and easy to use.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn consistent naming, clear errors, idempotent operations, and documentation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine the controls of a car that behave the same in every model. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through public platforms, internal service contracts, and mobile backends.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for leaking internal details and inconsistent responses, then try OpenAPI for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to write the documentation before the implementation.
