The Pros and Cons of Configuration Management

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Configuration Management is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports separating development and production
- Makes toggling features easier
- Helps with deploying the same build everywhere

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: committing secrets
- Risk: inconsistent defaults
- Risk: undocumented settings

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as separating development and production, and you can manage the risks, Configuration Management is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing.
