Is Digital Audio Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Digital Audio, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

It is a good fit when your work involves music production or voice calls, and when your team understands sampling rate.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn bit depth, or when you cannot manage these risks: clipping from too high a level, aliasing at low sample rates, and mixing sample rates.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether waveforms and the Nyquist limit are part of the answer. If they are, Digital Audio is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with podcasts and keep recordings below the clipping level.
