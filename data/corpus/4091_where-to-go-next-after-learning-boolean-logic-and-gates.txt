Where to Go Next After Learning Boolean Logic and Gates

You have met the basics of Boolean Logic and Gates. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Memory Hierarchy, which belongs to the same area as Boolean Logic and Gates (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of AND, OR and NOT, truth tables, De Morgan's laws or logic gates, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around designing circuits with Logisim, and note every problem you meet, starting with operator precedence mistakes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Boolean Logic and Gates to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
