Teaching Jupyter Notebooks to a Teammate

Explaining Jupyter Notebooks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a lab notebook that can run its own experiments. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving exploring data, naming cells and kernels as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain markdown in Jupyter Notebooks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, running cells out of order, and suggest this habit: restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
