Monorepos Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Monorepos.

Do:
- Learn shared tooling before anything else.
- Try Nx on a small example.
- Make it a habit to set up selective builds and tests early.
- Test the case of sharing libraries across apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: slow checkouts.
- Ignore this: unclear ownership.
- Ignore this: CI that rebuilds everything.
- Adopt Monorepos just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Monorepos, a little patience with code ownership pays off more than any clever shortcut.
