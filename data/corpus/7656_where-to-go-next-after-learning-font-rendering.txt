Where to Go Next After Learning Font Rendering

You have met the basics of Font Rendering. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Image Compression, which belongs to the same area as Font Rendering (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of glyphs, hinting, anti-aliasing or kerning, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around operating systems with FreeType, and note every problem you meet, starting with missing font fallbacks.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Font Rendering to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
