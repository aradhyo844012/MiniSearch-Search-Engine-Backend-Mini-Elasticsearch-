How to Learn Mobile App Performance in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Mobile App Performance. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Mobile App Performance covers making mobile apps start quickly, run smoothly and use little battery. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn startup time and frame rate, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Android Profiler on a small task such as smoother scrolling.
Week 3: build a mini project around longer battery life.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as loading everything at startup, and explain Mobile App Performance to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include Android Profiler, Xcode Instruments, and Firebase Performance Monitoring.

With steady practice, Mobile App Performance will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
