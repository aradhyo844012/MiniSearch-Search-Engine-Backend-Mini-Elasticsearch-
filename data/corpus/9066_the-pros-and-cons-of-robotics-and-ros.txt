The Pros and Cons of Robotics and ROS

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Robotics and ROS is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports warehouse robots
- Makes drones easier
- Helps with educational robots

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring sensor noise
- Risk: unsafe motion commands
- Risk: tight coupling between nodes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as warehouse robots, and you can manage the risks, Robotics and ROS is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test in simulation before moving real hardware.
