Apache Spark in Real Projects

Reading about Apache Spark is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: large ETL jobs. Here, DataFrames drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against data skew.

Scenario two: machine learning on big data. This time transformations and actions matters most, and tools such as PySpark help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: log analysis. The key idea is partitions, and the biggest risk is too many small partitions.

Across all three scenarios, Apache Spark stays the same idea: a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines. Only the details change.
