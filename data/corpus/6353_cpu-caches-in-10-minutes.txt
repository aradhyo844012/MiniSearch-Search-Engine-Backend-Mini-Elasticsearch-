CPU Caches in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through CPU Caches.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: CPU Caches is small, fast memory close to the processor that stores recently used data.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn cache lines, hit and miss rates, levels L1 to L3, and locality.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine keeping your most-used books on your desk instead of in the library. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through speeding up loops, explaining performance differences, and designing data layouts.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for false sharing and poor access patterns, then try perf for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to access arrays in the order they are stored.
