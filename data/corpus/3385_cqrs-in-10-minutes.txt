CQRS in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through CQRS.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn commands, queries, read models, and eventual consistency.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through high-read dashboards, complex domains, and scaling reads independently.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for adding it to simple apps and read models drifting out of sync, then try Axon Framework for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
