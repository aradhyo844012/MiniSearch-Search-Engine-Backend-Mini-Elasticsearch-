Where to Go Next After Learning Instruction Pipelines

You have met the basics of Instruction Pipelines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about System Calls, which belongs to the same area as Instruction Pipelines (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of fetch, decode and execute stages, hazards, branch prediction or stalls, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around understanding branch costs with perf, and note every problem you meet, starting with unpredictable branches.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Instruction Pipelines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
