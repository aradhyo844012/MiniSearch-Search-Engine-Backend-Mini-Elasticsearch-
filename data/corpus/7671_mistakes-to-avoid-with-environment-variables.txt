Mistakes to Avoid with Environment Variables

Everyone learns Environment Variables partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: committing secrets in dotfiles. It usually appears when someone skips variable names and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: variables not visible to child processes. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of storing configuration. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: typos in names. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on shell startup files to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: print a variable to confirm its value before blaming the code.
