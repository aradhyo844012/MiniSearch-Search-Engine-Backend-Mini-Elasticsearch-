Prompt Engineering for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Prompt Engineering.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Prompt Engineering in your own words.
2. Describe instructions, examples, context, and output format.
3. Give examples from drafting content and extracting data from text.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models. Then draw giving clear directions to a very literal helper on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is vague requests a problem? How does examples relate to context? When would you choose model playgrounds or LangChain?

Study habit worth keeping: specify the output format and include one or two examples.

When you feel ready, compare Prompt Engineering with Anomaly Detection and write down three differences.
