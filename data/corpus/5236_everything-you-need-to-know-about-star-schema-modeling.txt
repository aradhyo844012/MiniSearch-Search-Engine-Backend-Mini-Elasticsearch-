Everything You Need to Know About Star Schema Modeling

This page collects the essentials about Star Schema Modeling in one place. In short, Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.

Core concepts:
- fact tables
- dimension tables
- surrogate keys
- grain

Where it is used:
- business intelligence reports
- data warehouses
- fast aggregations

Common problems:
- mixing different grains
- over-normalized dimensions
- missing date dimensions

Tools to know:
- dbt
- Snowflake
- Kimball techniques

Remember this above all: a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
