End-to-End Testing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through End-to-End Testing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: End-to-End Testing is tests that drive an application through a real user journey from start to finish.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn browser automation, user flows, test data, and selectors.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a test drive of the whole car on a real road. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through checking login and checkout flows, release verification, and cross-browser checks.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for flaky tests and slow runs, then try Playwright for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to cover only the most important user journeys.
