Twelve-Factor Apps Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Twelve-Factor Apps.

Do:
- Learn config in the environment before anything else.
- Try Heroku on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep config out of the code.
- Test the case of building web services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: storing state on local disks.
- Ignore this: hard-coded config.
- Ignore this: long start-up times.
- Adopt Twelve-Factor Apps just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Twelve-Factor Apps, a little patience with stateless processes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
