Scaling Considerations for Dependency Injection

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Dependency Injection changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding constructor injection and getting easier unit testing right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for hiding too much behind containers and circular dependencies, and measure with Spring before changing anything.

Large scale: containers and inversion of control move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around swapping implementations at this stage.

Before you scale, inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
