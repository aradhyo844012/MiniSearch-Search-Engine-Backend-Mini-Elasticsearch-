The Pros and Cons of Command Line Productivity

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Command Line Productivity is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports automating repetitive tasks
- Makes exploring files and logs easier
- Helps with managing servers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: copying commands without understanding them
- Risk: dangerous wildcard use
- Risk: undocumented scripts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as automating repetitive tasks, and you can manage the risks, Command Line Productivity is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, turn commands you repeat into small scripts.
