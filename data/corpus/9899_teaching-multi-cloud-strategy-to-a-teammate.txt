Teaching Multi-Cloud Strategy to a Teammate

Explaining Multi-Cloud Strategy to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving avoiding single-provider risk, naming portability and vendor lock-in as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain abstraction layers in Multi-Cloud Strategy back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, doubling operational complexity, and suggest this habit: be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
