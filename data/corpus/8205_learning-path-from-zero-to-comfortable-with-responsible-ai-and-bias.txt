Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Responsible AI and Bias

This path is for a learner who has never studied Responsible AI and Bias before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe) and learn bias in data and fairness metrics.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Fairlearn, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving hiring tools.

Stage 3, depth: study transparency and human oversight, then deliberately explore this problem: assuming data is neutral.

Stage 4, application: apply Responsible AI and Bias to lending models, compare AI Fairness 360 with model cards, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Responsible AI and Bias to someone else, and then move on to Model Deployment.
