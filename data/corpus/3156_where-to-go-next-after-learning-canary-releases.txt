Where to Go Next After Learning Canary Releases

You have met the basics of Canary Releases. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Log Aggregation, which belongs to the same area as Canary Releases (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of traffic percentages, health metrics, automatic rollback or progressive rollout, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around testing with real traffic with Argo Rollouts, and note every problem you meet, starting with too little traffic to reveal problems.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Canary Releases to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
