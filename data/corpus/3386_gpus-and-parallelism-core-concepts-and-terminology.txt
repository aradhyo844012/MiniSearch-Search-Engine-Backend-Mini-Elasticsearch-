GPUs and Parallelism: Core Concepts and Terminology

This page defines the vocabulary you need to read about GPUs and Parallelism with confidence. The subject itself is processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once.

threads and blocks: one of the building blocks of GPUs and Parallelism. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.

memory transfers: one of the building blocks of GPUs and Parallelism. It shows up whenever you work on training neural networks.
