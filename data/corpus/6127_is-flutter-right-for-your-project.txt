Is Flutter Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Flutter, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.

It is a good fit when your work involves cross-platform mobile apps or desktop apps, and when your team understands widgets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn state, or when you cannot manage these risks: deep widget trees, poor state organization, and large app sizes.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether hot reload and the render tree are part of the answer. If they are, Flutter is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with custom user interfaces and split big widgets into small reusable ones.
