Dependency Injection Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Dependency Injection. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them.
Key terms: constructor injection, interfaces, containers, and inversion of control.
Typical uses: easier unit testing, swapping implementations, and configuring services.
Risks: hiding too much behind containers, circular dependencies, and injecting too many dependencies.
Tools: Spring, FastAPI Depends, and Dagger.

Mental picture: a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them.

Habit to build: inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.

See also: Webhooks, which sits close to Dependency Injection in the area of Backend Engineering.
