Backtracking Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Backtracking regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
2. Be careful with this one: forgetting to undo changes.
3. Do not skip decision tree; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for Python recursion first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as itertools.
5. Write down why you chose Backtracking so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to generating permutations as well.
