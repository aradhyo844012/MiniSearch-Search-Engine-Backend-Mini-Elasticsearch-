Why BGP and Internet Routing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand BGP and Internet Routing make better decisions, because BGP and Internet Routing is the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.

Three situations show why it matters. First, connecting ISPs. Second, directing traffic between large networks. Third, explaining internet outages. In each case, a solid grasp of autonomous systems and route announcements separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: route leaks, hijacked prefixes, and misconfigured filters.

You will not need to build everything yourself; bgpq4, BIRD, and RIPE Stat cover most needs.
