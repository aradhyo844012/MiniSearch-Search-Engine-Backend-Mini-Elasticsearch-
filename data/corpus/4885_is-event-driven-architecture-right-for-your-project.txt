Is Event-Driven Architecture Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Event-Driven Architecture, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Event-Driven Architecture is a style in which components communicate by publishing events and reacting to them.

It is a good fit when your work involves order pipelines or real-time notifications, and when your team understands producers and consumers.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn event brokers, or when you cannot manage these risks: hard-to-trace flows, duplicate events, and unclear event ownership.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether loose coupling and asynchronous handling are part of the answer. If they are, Event-Driven Architecture is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with integrating many services and document every event name and schema.
