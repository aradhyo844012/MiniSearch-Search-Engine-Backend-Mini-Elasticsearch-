Choosing Tools for Data Lakes

There is no single best tool for Data Lakes. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Amazon S3 is a common first choice, especially for storing logs and events. Delta Lake suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Apache Iceberg is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle machine learning datasets well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: turning into a data swamp.

As a rule of thumb, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner, no matter which tool you use.
