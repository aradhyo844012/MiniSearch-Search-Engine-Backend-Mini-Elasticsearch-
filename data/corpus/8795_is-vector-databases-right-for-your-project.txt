Is Vector Databases Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Vector Databases, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Vector Databases is databases built to store embeddings and find the most similar ones quickly.

It is a good fit when your work involves semantic search or recommendations, and when your team understands embeddings.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn approximate nearest neighbors, or when you cannot manage these risks: mixing embedding models, ignoring recall trade-offs, and oversized vectors.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether distance metrics and metadata filters are part of the answer. If they are, Vector Databases is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with retrieval for AI assistants and measure recall on a small labeled test set.
