The Pros and Cons of Fuzz Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Fuzz Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports testing parsers
- Makes finding memory bugs easier
- Helps with security research

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: running without sanitizers
- Risk: crashes that cannot be reproduced
- Risk: ignoring slow inputs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as testing parsers, and you can manage the risks, Fuzz Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep every crashing input as a regression test.
