Mistakes to Avoid with Build Systems

Everyone learns Build Systems partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow full rebuilds. It usually appears when someone skips targets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: undeclared dependencies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of compiling large projects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: machine-specific builds. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on build caches to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: make builds reproducible on any machine.
