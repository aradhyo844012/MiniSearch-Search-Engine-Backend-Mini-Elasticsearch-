Teaching Graph Theory Basics to a Teammate

Explaining Graph Theory Basics to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a map of towns and the roads between them. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving social networks, naming vertices and edges and degree as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain paths and cycles in Graph Theory Basics back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, mixing directed and undirected graphs, and suggest this habit: draw a small example before writing code.
