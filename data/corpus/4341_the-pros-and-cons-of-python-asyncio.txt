The Pros and Cons of Python Asyncio

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Python Asyncio is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports calling many APIs at once
- Makes web servers easier
- Helps with network clients

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: blocking calls inside coroutines
- Risk: forgetting to await
- Risk: mixing threads and event loops carelessly

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as calling many APIs at once, and you can manage the risks, Python Asyncio is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, never call blocking code directly inside a coroutine.
