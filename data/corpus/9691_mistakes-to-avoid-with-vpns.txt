Mistakes to Avoid with VPNs

Everyone learns VPNs partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming it makes you anonymous. It usually appears when someone skips tunneling and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow routing through distant servers. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of remote work access. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: leaking DNS requests. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on split tunneling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test for DNS leaks after connecting.
