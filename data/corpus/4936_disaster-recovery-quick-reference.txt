Disaster Recovery Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Disaster Recovery. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.
Key terms: recovery time objective, recovery point objective, failover regions, and runbooks.
Typical uses: surviving regional outages, meeting compliance needs, and protecting critical data.
Risks: untested plans, outdated runbooks, and dependencies missed in the plan.
Tools: AWS Backup, Terraform, and Route 53 failover.

Mental picture: a fire drill for a whole system.

Habit to build: run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
