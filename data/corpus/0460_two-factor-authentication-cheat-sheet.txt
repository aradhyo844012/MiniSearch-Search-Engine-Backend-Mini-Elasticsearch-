Two-Factor Authentication Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Two-Factor Authentication.

- Definition: requiring a second proof of identity in addition to a password.
- Core ideas: one-time codes; authenticator apps; hardware keys; backup codes.
- Use it for: protecting email accounts; securing admin panels; reducing account takeovers.
- Watch out for: relying only on SMS; no recovery plan; weak backup code storage.
- Tools: Google Authenticator, YubiKey, TOTP libraries.
- Related topic: SQL Injection.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for authenticator apps before building anything large.
