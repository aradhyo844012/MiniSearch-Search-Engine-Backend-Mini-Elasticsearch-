Where to Go Next After Learning Packet Switching

You have met the basics of Packet Switching. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about BGP and Internet Routing, which belongs to the same area as Packet Switching (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of packets, headers, routing or reassembly, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around voice over IP with Wireshark, and note every problem you meet, starting with packet loss.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Packet Switching to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
