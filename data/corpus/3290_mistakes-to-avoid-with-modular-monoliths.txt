Mistakes to Avoid with Modular Monoliths

Everyone learns Modular Monoliths partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: modules reaching into each other's tables. It usually appears when someone skips module boundaries and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no enforced boundaries. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of small teams. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: treating modules as just folders. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on dependency checks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: enforce boundaries with automated checks.
