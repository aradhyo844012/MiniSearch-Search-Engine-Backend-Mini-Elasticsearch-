Wearable Technology Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Wearable Technology feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Wearable Technology: small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.
sensors: a core term, most visible in fitness tracking.
low-power design: a core term that beginners often confuse with companion apps.
companion apps: a core term that connects to data syncing.
data syncing: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Wear OS: a tool commonly used for notifications.

Come back to this Wearable Technology list whenever documentation for watchOS uses a word you do not recognize.
