Prompt Engineering in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Prompt Engineering.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn instructions, examples, context, and output format.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine giving clear directions to a very literal helper. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through drafting content, extracting data from text, and building assistants.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for vague requests and missing context, then try model playgrounds for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to specify the output format and include one or two examples.
