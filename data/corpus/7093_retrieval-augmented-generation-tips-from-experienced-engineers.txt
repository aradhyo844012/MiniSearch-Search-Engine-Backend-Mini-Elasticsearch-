Retrieval-Augmented Generation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Retrieval-Augmented Generation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of company knowledge assistants early, before the design hardens.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit chunking before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Retrieval-Augmented Generation so the next person understands the decision.
4. Reach for LangChain first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as FAISS.
5. Do not skip retrieval; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to question answering over documents as well.
