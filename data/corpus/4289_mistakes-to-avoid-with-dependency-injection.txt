Mistakes to Avoid with Dependency Injection

Everyone learns Dependency Injection partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hiding too much behind containers. It usually appears when someone skips constructor injection and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: circular dependencies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of easier unit testing. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: injecting too many dependencies. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on inversion of control to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
