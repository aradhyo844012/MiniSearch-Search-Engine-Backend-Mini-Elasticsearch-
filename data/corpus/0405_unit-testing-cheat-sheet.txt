Unit Testing Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Unit Testing.

- Definition: writing small automated tests that check one piece of code at a time.
- Core ideas: test cases; assertions; fixtures; mocking.
- Use it for: catching regressions; documenting behavior; refactoring safely.
- Watch out for: testing implementation details; slow tests; tests that depend on each other.
- Tools: pytest, unittest, coverage.py.
- Related topic: Agile and Scrum.
- Quick tip: practice test cases until it feels automatic.
