Bluetooth Low Energy Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Bluetooth Low Energy feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Bluetooth Low Energy: a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.
advertising: a core term, most visible in fitness trackers.
GATT services: a core term that beginners often confuse with characteristics.
characteristics: a core term that connects to pairing.
pairing: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
nRF Connect: a tool commonly used for smart locks.
