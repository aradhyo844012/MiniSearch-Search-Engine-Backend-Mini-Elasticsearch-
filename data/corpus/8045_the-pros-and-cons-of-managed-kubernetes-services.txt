The Pros and Cons of Managed Kubernetes Services

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Managed Kubernetes Services is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running containerized apps
- Makes scaling services easier
- Helps with reducing operations work

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: neglecting version upgrades
- Risk: oversized node pools
- Risk: costly idle clusters

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running containerized apps, and you can manage the risks, Managed Kubernetes Services is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, plan regular cluster upgrades.
