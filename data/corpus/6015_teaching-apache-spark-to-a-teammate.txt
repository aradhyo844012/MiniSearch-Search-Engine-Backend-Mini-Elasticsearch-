Teaching Apache Spark to a Teammate

Explaining Apache Spark to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving large ETL jobs, naming DataFrames and transformations and actions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain partitions in Apache Spark back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, data skew, and suggest this habit: avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
