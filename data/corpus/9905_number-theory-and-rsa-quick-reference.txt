Number Theory and RSA Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Number Theory and RSA. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.
Key terms: prime numbers, modular arithmetic, greatest common divisor, and modular exponentiation.
Typical uses: public key cryptography, hash functions, and digital signatures.
Risks: using small primes, implementing crypto by hand in production, and ignoring padding.
Tools: Python pow, OpenSSL, and SageMath.

Mental picture: a clock whose numbers wrap around.

Habit to build: use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
