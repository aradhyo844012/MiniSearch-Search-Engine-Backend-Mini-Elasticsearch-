Software Estimation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Software Estimation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Software Estimation: predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.
story points: a core term, most visible in sprint planning.
ranges: a core term that beginners often confuse with uncertainty.
uncertainty: a core term that connects to velocity.
velocity: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
planning poker: a tool commonly used for setting deadlines.

Come back to this Software Estimation list whenever documentation for Jira uses a word you do not recognize.
