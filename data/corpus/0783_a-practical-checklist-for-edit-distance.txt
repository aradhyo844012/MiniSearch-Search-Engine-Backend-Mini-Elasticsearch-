A Practical Checklist for Edit Distance

Use this checklist before you rely on Edit Distance in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Edit Distance should accomplish.
2. Review how Edit Distance interacts with TF-IDF.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Guard against this risk: ignoring word length.
5. Write at least one automated test that covers insertions.
6. Try the approach on a small case, for example spell checking.
7. Guard against this risk: forgetting transpositions.
