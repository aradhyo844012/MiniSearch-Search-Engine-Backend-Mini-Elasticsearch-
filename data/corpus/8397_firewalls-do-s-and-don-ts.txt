Firewalls Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Firewalls.

Do:
- Learn rules before anything else.
- Try ufw on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with default deny and open only what you need.
- Test the case of protecting servers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: rules in the wrong order.
- Ignore this: overly broad allow rules.
- Ignore this: locking yourself out remotely.
- Adopt Firewalls just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Firewalls, a little patience with stateful inspection pays off more than any clever shortcut.
