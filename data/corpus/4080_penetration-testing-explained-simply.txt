Penetration Testing Explained Simply

If Penetration Testing sounds intimidating, relax. It is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Penetration Testing as hiring a locksmith to test your doors. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation, and reporting. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with scoping, then add reconnaissance.

People reach for Penetration Testing when they work on pre-launch security checks, compliance audits, and validating defenses.

One practical tip is to get written authorization before any test.
