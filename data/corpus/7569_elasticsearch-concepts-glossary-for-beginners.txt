Elasticsearch Concepts Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Elasticsearch Concepts feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Elasticsearch Concepts: the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.
indexes: a core term, most visible in site search.
shards: a core term that beginners often confuse with analyzers.
analyzers: a core term that connects to queries.
queries: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Elasticsearch: a tool commonly used for log analytics.

Come back to this Elasticsearch Concepts list whenever documentation for Kibana uses a word you do not recognize.
