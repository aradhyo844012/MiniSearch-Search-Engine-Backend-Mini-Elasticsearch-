Understanding Single Sign-On Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a festival wristband that opens every gate.

In that picture, identity provider is the first rule you follow, SAML is the next step, and OpenID Connect and session lifetime keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications, and it involves details such as a single point of failure that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Okta to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain company intranets to a friend.
