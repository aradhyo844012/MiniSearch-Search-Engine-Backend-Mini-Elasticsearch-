The Pros and Cons of Ray Tracing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Ray Tracing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports movie visuals
- Makes architectural visualization easier
- Helps with realistic game lighting

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow render times
- Risk: noise from too few samples
- Risk: numerical precision errors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as movie visuals, and you can manage the risks, Ray Tracing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
