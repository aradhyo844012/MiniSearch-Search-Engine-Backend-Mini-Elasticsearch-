Combining Star Schema Modeling with Data Anonymization in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Star Schema Modeling and Data Anonymization can work together in a single project.

Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables. Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.

Imagine a project that involves business intelligence reports and sharing datasets with researchers. Star Schema Modeling brings fact tables, while Data Anonymization brings masking.

A sensible plan is to start with dbt for the Star Schema Modeling part and ARX for the Data Anonymization part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: over-normalized dimensions on one side, and removing too little on the other.

Picture the whole as a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions, working alongside blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene.

Two habits help: first, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else; second, test whether combining fields could identify a person.
