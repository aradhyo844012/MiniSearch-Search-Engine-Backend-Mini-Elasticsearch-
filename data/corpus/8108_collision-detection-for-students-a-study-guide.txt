Collision Detection for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Collision Detection.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Collision Detection in your own words.
2. Describe bounding boxes, broad and narrow phases, spatial partitioning, and collision response.
3. Give examples from platformers and shooters.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Collision Detection is the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap. Then draw two people checking whether their personal space bubbles overlap on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is tunneling through thin objects a problem? How does broad and narrow phases relate to spatial partitioning? When would you choose Box2D or Unity colliders?

Study habit worth keeping: use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.

When you feel ready, compare Collision Detection with Game Loop and write down three differences.
