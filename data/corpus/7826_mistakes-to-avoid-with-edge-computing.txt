Mistakes to Avoid with Edge Computing

Everyone learns Edge Computing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hard-to-update devices. It usually appears when someone skips edge nodes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: inconsistent hardware. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of factory sensors. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: security at many sites. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on synchronization to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: decide which data must stay local before designing the system.
