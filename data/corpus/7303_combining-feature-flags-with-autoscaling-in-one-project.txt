Combining Feature Flags with Autoscaling in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Feature Flags and Autoscaling can work together in a single project.

Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code. Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Imagine a project that involves dark launches and handling traffic spikes. Feature Flags brings flag evaluation, while Autoscaling brings scaling metrics.

A sensible plan is to start with LaunchDarkly for the Feature Flags part and Kubernetes HPA for the Autoscaling part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: flags that depend on each other on one side, and flapping because of short spikes on the other.

Picture the whole as light switches wired to every room of a house, working alongside a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives.

Two habits help: first, give every flag an owner and a removal date; second, load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
