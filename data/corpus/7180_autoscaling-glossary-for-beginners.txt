Autoscaling Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Autoscaling feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Autoscaling: automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.
scaling metrics: a core term, most visible in handling traffic spikes.
cooldown periods: a core term that beginners often confuse with minimum and maximum limits.
minimum and maximum limits: a core term that connects to horizontal scaling.
horizontal scaling: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Kubernetes HPA: a tool commonly used for saving money overnight.

Come back to this Autoscaling list whenever documentation for AWS Auto Scaling uses a word you do not recognize.
