Skills to Build Around Feature Flags

Knowing the definition of Feature Flags is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with flag evaluation and targeting rules, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with LaunchDarkly on a Feature Flags task, and try at least one alternative such as Unleash.

Judgment: learn to decide when Feature Flags fits a problem such as dark launches, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example light switches wired to every room of a house.

Habits: give every flag an owner and a removal date, and write down what you learn after each project.
