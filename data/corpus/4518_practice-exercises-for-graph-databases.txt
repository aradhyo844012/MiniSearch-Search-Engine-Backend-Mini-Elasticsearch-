Practice Exercises for Graph Databases

These exercises build real understanding of Graph Databases. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: using them for simple tabular data. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick Neo4j and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Compare Cypher and Amazon Neptune for fraud detection and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Graph Databases and include the term nodes.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble social networks or recommendation engines.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.
