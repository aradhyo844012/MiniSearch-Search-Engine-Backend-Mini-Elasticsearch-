Distributed Tracing in Real Projects

Reading about Distributed Tracing is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: finding slow services. Here, spans drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against missing context propagation.

Scenario two: understanding dependencies. This time trace IDs matters most, and tools such as OpenTelemetry help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: debugging errors across services. The key idea is context propagation, and the biggest risk is sampling too aggressively.

Across all three scenarios, Distributed Tracing stays the same idea: following a single request as it passes through many services. Only the details change.
