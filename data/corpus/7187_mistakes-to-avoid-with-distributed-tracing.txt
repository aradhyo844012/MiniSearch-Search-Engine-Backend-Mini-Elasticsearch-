Mistakes to Avoid with Distributed Tracing

Everyone learns Distributed Tracing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: missing context propagation. It usually appears when someone skips spans and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: sampling too aggressively. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of finding slow services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: overhead from too many spans. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on sampling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
