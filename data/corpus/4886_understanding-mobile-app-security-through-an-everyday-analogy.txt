Understanding Mobile App Security Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture locking an app's valuables in a hotel safe.

In that picture, secure storage is the first rule you follow, certificate pinning is the next step, and obfuscation and permissions keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks, and it involves details such as storing tokens in plain text that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for OWASP MASVS to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain banking apps to a friend.
