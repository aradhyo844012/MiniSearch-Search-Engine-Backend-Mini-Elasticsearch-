Probability Basics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Probability Basics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the study of how likely events are to happen.
Key terms: events, conditional probability, independence, and expected value.
Typical uses: randomized algorithms, machine learning, and risk estimates.
Risks: confusing independent and exclusive events, ignoring base rates, and falling for the gambler's fallacy.
Tools: Python random, NumPy, and simulation notebooks.

Mental picture: predicting how often a dice roll will land on each face.

Habit to build: simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.

See also: Discrete Mathematics, which sits close to Probability Basics in the area of Mathematics for Computing.
