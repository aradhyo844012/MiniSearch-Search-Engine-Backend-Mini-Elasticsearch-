Everything You Need to Know About Regions and Availability Zones

This page collects the essentials about Regions and Availability Zones in one place. In short, Regions and Availability Zones is the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources.

Core concepts:
- regions
- availability zones
- latency
- data residency

Where it is used:
- placing services near users
- surviving data center failures
- meeting data laws

Common problems:
- putting everything in one zone
- cross-region transfer fees
- forgetting data residency rules

Tools to know:
- AWS Regions
- Azure regions
- Google Cloud regions

Remember this above all: branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to spread critical services across at least two zones.
