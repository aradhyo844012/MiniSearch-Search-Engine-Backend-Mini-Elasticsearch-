The Pros and Cons of Star Schema Modeling

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Star Schema Modeling is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports business intelligence reports
- Makes data warehouses easier
- Helps with fast aggregations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing different grains
- Risk: over-normalized dimensions
- Risk: missing date dimensions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as business intelligence reports, and you can manage the risks, Star Schema Modeling is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
