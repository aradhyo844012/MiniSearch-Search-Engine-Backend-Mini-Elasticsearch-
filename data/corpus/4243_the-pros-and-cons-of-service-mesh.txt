The Pros and Cons of Service Mesh

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Service Mesh is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports securing service traffic
- Makes retries and timeouts easier
- Helps with fine-grained routing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: added latency and complexity
- Risk: adopting it too early
- Risk: hard-to-debug proxy configs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as securing service traffic, and you can manage the risks, Service Mesh is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
