Teaching Distributed Tracing to a Teammate

Explaining Distributed Tracing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving finding slow services, naming spans and trace IDs as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain context propagation in Distributed Tracing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, missing context propagation, and suggest this habit: propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
