A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with VPNs

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on VPNs.

Morning: Karthik starts by reviewing a ticket about remote work access. Before touching any code, Karthik re-reads the notes on tunneling to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Karthik pairs with a teammate to explore encryption with WireGuard. A small test exposes the problem of assuming it makes you anonymous, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving linking offices. Karthik sketches a private tunnel under a public road on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Karthik writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to test for DNS leaks after connecting.

Days like this are typical. VPNs is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
