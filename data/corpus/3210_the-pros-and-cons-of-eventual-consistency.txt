The Pros and Cons of Eventual Consistency

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Eventual Consistency is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports global social feeds
- Makes shopping cart services easier
- Helps with DNS records

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming reads are always fresh
- Risk: unresolved write conflicts
- Risk: no user feedback during delays

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as global social feeds, and you can manage the risks, Eventual Consistency is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
