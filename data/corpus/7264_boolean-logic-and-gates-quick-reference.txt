Boolean Logic and Gates Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Boolean Logic and Gates. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from.
Key terms: AND, OR and NOT, truth tables, De Morgan's laws, and logic gates.
Typical uses: writing conditions, designing circuits, and simplifying expressions.
Risks: operator precedence mistakes, forgetting short-circuit behavior, and negating complex conditions incorrectly.
Tools: Logisim, Python bitwise operators, and Karnaugh maps.

Mental picture: light switches wired in series or in parallel.

Habit to build: write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
