Why Developer Portfolios Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Developer Portfolios make better decisions, because Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.

Three situations show why it matters. First, applying for internships. Second, showing skills to recruiters. Third, tracking personal growth. In each case, a solid grasp of projects and README quality separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: too many unfinished projects, no explanation of decisions, and broken demo links.

You will not need to build everything yourself; GitHub Pages, Vercel, and Netlify cover most needs.
