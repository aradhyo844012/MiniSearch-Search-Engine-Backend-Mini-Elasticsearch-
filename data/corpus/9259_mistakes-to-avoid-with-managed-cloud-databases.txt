Mistakes to Avoid with Managed Cloud Databases

Everyone learns Managed Cloud Databases partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: surprise costs. It usually appears when someone skips automated backups and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: limited configuration control. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of launching apps quickly. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring maintenance windows. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on scaling options to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test restoring a backup before you need it.
