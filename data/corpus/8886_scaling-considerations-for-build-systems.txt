Scaling Considerations for Build Systems

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Build Systems changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding targets and getting compiling large projects right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow full rebuilds and undeclared dependencies, and measure with Make before changing anything.

Large scale: incremental builds and build caches move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around packaging software at this stage.

Before you scale, make builds reproducible on any machine. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
