Scaling Considerations for Physics Engines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Physics Engines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding rigid bodies and getting vehicles and ragdolls right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for variable time steps and unstable stacks of objects, and measure with Box2D before changing anything.

Large scale: constraints and fixed time steps move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around puzzle games at this stage.

Before you scale, use a fixed time step for stable results. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
