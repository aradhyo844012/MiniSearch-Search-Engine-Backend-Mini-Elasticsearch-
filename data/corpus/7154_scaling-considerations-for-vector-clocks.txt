Scaling Considerations for Vector Clocks

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Vector Clocks changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding logical time and getting detecting concurrent edits right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for clocks that grow with many nodes and confusing them with wall-clock time, and measure with Riak before changing anything.

Large scale: the happened-before relation and conflict detection move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around replicated databases at this stage.

Before you scale, compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
