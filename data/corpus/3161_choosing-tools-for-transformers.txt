Choosing Tools for Transformers

There is no single best tool for Transformers. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Hugging Face is a common first choice, especially for language translation. PyTorch suits teams that already work with similar technology, and TensorFlow is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle text generation well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: huge compute requirements.

As a rule of thumb, begin with small pretrained models, no matter which tool you use.
