Scaling Considerations for Zero Trust Security

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Zero Trust Security changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding identity verification and getting remote work access right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for treating it as a product to buy and poor identity hygiene, and measure with Okta before changing anything.

Large scale: micro-segmentation and continuous checks move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around protecting internal services at this stage.

Before you scale, start with strong identity and device checks. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
