The Pros and Cons of Offline-First Apps

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Offline-First Apps is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports field service tools
- Makes note-taking apps easier
- Helps with apps for low-connectivity regions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: unresolved sync conflicts
- Risk: unbounded local data
- Risk: poor feedback about pending changes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as field service tools, and you can manage the risks, Offline-First Apps is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, show users which changes have not synced yet.
