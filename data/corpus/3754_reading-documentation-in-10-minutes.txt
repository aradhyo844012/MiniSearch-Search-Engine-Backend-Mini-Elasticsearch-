Reading Documentation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Reading Documentation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Reading Documentation is the skill of finding accurate answers in official guides and references.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn search, tutorials versus references, examples, and version numbers.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine reading the manual before assembling the furniture. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through learning new libraries, solving errors, and verifying answers from other sources.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for reading the wrong version and skipping the overview, then try MDN Web Docs for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to check that the documentation version matches your installed version.
