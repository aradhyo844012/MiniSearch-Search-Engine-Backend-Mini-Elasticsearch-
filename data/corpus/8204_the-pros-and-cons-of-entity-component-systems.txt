The Pros and Cons of Entity Component Systems

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Entity Component Systems is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports large simulations
- Makes games with thousands of objects easier
- Helps with flexible game design

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overengineering small games
- Risk: components that hold logic
- Risk: hard debugging of system order

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as large simulations, and you can manage the risks, Entity Component Systems is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep components as plain data.
