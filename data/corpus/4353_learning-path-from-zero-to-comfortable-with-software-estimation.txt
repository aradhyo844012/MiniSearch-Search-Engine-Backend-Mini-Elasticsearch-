Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Software Estimation

This path is for a learner who has never studied Software Estimation before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take) and learn story points and ranges.

Stage 2, hands-on: install planning poker, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving sprint planning.

Stage 3, depth: study uncertainty and velocity, then deliberately explore this problem: treating estimates as promises.

Stage 4, application: apply Software Estimation to setting deadlines, compare Jira with spreadsheets, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Software Estimation to someone else, and then move on to API Design Principles.
