Information Theory and Entropy Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Information Theory and Entropy. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the mathematics of measuring and compressing information.
Key terms: bits, entropy, compression limits, and noise.
Typical uses: data compression, error-correcting codes, and communication systems.
Risks: confusing information with meaning, ignoring noise, and misreading entropy values.
Tools: the Python math module, NumPy, and zlib.

Mental picture: measuring how surprised you are by each new message.

Habit to build: compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
