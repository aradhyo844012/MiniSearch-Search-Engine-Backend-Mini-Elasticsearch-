Database Constraints Explained Simply

Here is Database Constraints without the jargon: it is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.

Think of Database Constraints as guard rails along a mountain road. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and check constraints. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with primary keys, then add foreign keys.

You will meet Database Constraints in work such as preventing bad data, protecting relationships, and documenting business rules.

One practical tip is to put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
