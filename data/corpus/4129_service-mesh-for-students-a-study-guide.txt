Service Mesh for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Service Mesh.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Service Mesh in your own words.
2. Describe sidecar proxies, mutual TLS, traffic policies, and observability.
3. Give examples from securing service traffic and retries and timeouts.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Then draw a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is added latency and complexity a problem? How does mutual TLS relate to traffic policies? When would you choose Istio or Linkerd?

Study habit: adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.

When you feel ready, compare Service Mesh with Disaster Recovery and write down three differences.
