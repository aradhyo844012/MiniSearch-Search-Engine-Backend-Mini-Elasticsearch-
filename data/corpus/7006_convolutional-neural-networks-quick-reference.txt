Convolutional Neural Networks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Convolutional Neural Networks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.
Key terms: filters, pooling, feature maps, and stride.
Typical uses: image classification, medical image analysis, and object detection.
Risks: too little training data, ignoring data augmentation, and overly deep models for simple tasks.
Tools: PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Keras.

Mental picture: a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes.

Habit to build: start from a pretrained model.
