Where to Go Next After Learning Interrupts

You have met the basics of Interrupts. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Endianness, which belongs to the same area as Interrupts (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of interrupt handlers, interrupt vectors, priorities or context saving, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around timers with Linux /proc/interrupts, and note every problem you meet, starting with long handlers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Interrupts to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
