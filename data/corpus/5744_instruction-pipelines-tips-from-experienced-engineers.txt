Instruction Pipelines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Instruction Pipelines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: unpredictable branches.
2. Test the case of explaining CPU speed early, before the design hardens.
3. Write down why you chose Instruction Pipelines so the next person understands the decision.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit hazards before anything else.
5. Reach for perf first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Compiler Explorer.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to understanding branch costs as well.
