Interrupts Explained Simply

Here is Interrupts without the jargon: it is signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event.

Think of Interrupts as a phone call interrupting your reading. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: interrupt handlers, interrupt vectors, priorities, and context saving. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with interrupt handlers, then add interrupt vectors.

You will meet Interrupts in work such as keyboard and mouse input, timers, and network packets arriving.

One practical tip is to keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.

Once Interrupts feels comfortable, read about Compilers and Interpreters, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
