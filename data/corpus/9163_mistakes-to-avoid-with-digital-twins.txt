Mistakes to Avoid with Digital Twins

Everyone learns Digital Twins partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: stale data. It usually appears when someone skips sensor data and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: over-detailed models. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of factory monitoring. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unclear goals. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on analytics to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: define the question the twin must answer first.
