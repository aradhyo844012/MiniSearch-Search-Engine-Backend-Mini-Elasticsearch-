Skills to Build Around Model Drift and Monitoring

Knowing the definition of Model Drift and Monitoring is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with data drift and concept drift, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Evidently on a Model Drift and Monitoring task, and try at least one alternative such as Prometheus.

Judgment: learn to decide when Model Drift and Monitoring fits a problem such as catching degrading models, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example checking a car's gauges on a long trip.

Habits: monitor input data as well as accuracy, and write down what you learn after each project.
