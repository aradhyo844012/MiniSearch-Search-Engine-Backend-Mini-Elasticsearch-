Software Estimation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Software Estimation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can give ranges instead of single numbers.
2. Be careful with this one: treating estimates as promises.
3. Do not skip story points; everything else builds on it.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit ranges before anything else.
5. Reach for planning poker first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Jira.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to setting deadlines as well.
