How to Learn Cloud Storage in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Cloud Storage. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Cloud Storage in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to storing files and objects on remote servers that scale on demand.

Week 1: learn buckets and objects, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Amazon S3 on a small task such as backups.
Week 3: build a mini project around serving images.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as public buckets by mistake, and explain Cloud Storage to a friend.

In practice you can start with Amazon S3, then explore Google Cloud Storage or Azure Blob Storage as your needs grow.

A system that cannot be observed cannot be reliably operated. Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Cloud Storage.
