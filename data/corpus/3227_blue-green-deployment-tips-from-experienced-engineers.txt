Blue-Green Deployment Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Blue-Green Deployment regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of zero-downtime releases early, before the design hardens.
2. Do not skip blue and green environments; everything else builds on it.
3. Write down why you chose Blue-Green Deployment so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: database schema mismatches.
5. Make sure you can keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to quick rollbacks as well.
