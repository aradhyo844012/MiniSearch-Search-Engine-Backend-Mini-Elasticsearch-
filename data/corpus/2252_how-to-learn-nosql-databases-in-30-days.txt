How to Learn NoSQL Databases in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with NoSQL Databases. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term NoSQL Databases refers to a broad family of databases that do not use the traditional relational table model. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn document stores and key-value stores, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with MongoDB on a small task such as flexible schemas.
Week 3: build a mini project around high write throughput.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as assuming no schema means no design, and explain NoSQL Databases to a friend.

In practice you can start with MongoDB, then explore Cassandra or Redis as your needs grow.

NoSQL Databases and Query Optimization are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Databases.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with NoSQL Databases.
