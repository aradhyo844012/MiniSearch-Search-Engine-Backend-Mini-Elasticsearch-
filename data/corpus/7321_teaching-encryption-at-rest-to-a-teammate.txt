Teaching Encryption at Rest to a Teammate

Explaining Encryption at Rest to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a locked safe inside a locked room. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving protecting backups, naming symmetric keys and key management as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain disk encryption in Encryption at Rest back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, storing keys next to the data, and suggest this habit: keep keys in a separate service from the data.
