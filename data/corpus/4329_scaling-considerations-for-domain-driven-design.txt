Scaling Considerations for Domain-Driven Design

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Domain-Driven Design changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding ubiquitous language and getting complex business systems right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using it for simple CRUD apps and models that never match the business, and measure with event storming before changing anything.

Large scale: aggregates and entities and value objects move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around aligning developers with domain experts at this stage.

Before you scale, agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
