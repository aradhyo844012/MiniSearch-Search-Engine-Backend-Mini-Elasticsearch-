Where to Go Next After Learning Write-Ahead Logging

You have met the basics of Write-Ahead Logging. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Database Backups and Recovery, which belongs to the same area as Write-Ahead Logging (Storage Engines and Databases) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of log records, checkpoints, crash recovery or fsync, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around feeding replication streams with PostgreSQL WAL, and note every problem you meet, starting with disabling fsync for speed.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Write-Ahead Logging to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
