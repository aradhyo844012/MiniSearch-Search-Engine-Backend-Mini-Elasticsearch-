Zipf's Law Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Zipf's Law.

- Definition: the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language.
- Core ideas: rank and frequency; power laws; log-log plots; long tails.
- Use it for: analyzing text corpora; sizing indexes; designing caches.
- Watch out for: testing it on random text; reading too much into a perfect fit; ignoring small samples.
- Tools: matplotlib, NumPy, pandas.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for power laws before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice rank and frequency until it feels automatic.
