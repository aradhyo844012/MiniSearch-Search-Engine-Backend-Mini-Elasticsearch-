Understanding Time-Series Databases Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a long logbook where every line starts with the time.

In that picture, timestamps is the first rule you follow, retention policies is the next step, and downsampling and tags and fields keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time, and it involves details such as high-cardinality tags that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for InfluxDB to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain server metrics to a friend.
