Interrupts Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Interrupts feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Interrupts: signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event.
interrupt handlers: a core term, most visible in keyboard and mouse input.
interrupt vectors: a core term that beginners often confuse with priorities.
priorities: a core term that connects to context saving.
context saving: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Linux /proc/interrupts: a tool commonly used for timers.

Come back to this Interrupts list whenever documentation for logic analyzers uses a word you do not recognize.
