A Practical Checklist for HTTP and HTTPS

Use this checklist before you rely on HTTP and HTTPS in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what HTTP and HTTPS should accomplish.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on status codes.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers methods.
4. Guard against this risk: mixing up status codes.
5. Guard against this risk: sending secrets in URLs.
6. Review the core ideas: methods, status codes, headers, and cookies.
7. Guard against this risk: ignoring caching headers.
8. Document your decisions in the project README.
9. Review how HTTP and HTTPS interacts with Load Balancing.
