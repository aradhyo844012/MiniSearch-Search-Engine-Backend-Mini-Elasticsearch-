Debugging Smart Contracts: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Smart Contracts, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: bugs that cannot be patched, reentrancy attacks, and high gas costs.
Step 3: revisit contract code and transactions, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Solidity or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Smart Contracts in work such as token systems come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
