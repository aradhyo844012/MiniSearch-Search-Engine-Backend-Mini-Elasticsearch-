Practice Exercises for Push Notifications

These exercises build real understanding of Push Notifications. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble order updates or chat alerts.
Exercise 2: Pick Firebase Cloud Messaging and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Explain how payloads and permissions differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Compare Apple Push Notification service and OneSignal for re-engaging users and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: asking for permission too early. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed.
