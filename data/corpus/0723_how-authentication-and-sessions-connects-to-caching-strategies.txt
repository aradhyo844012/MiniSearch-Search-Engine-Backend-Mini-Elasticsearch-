How Authentication and Sessions Connects to Caching Strategies

Authentication and Sessions and Caching Strategies are closely connected parts of Backend and APIs. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving logging in to web apps may also involve speeding up read-heavy APIs. In such a project, credentials and cache-aside often influence each other.

Start with Authentication and Sessions: the term refers to the process of proving who a user is and remembering them between requests. Then look at Caching Strategies, which refers to approaches for storing computed results so they can be reused instead of recalculated.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as protecting admin pages, and explain how write-through would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Authentication and Sessions, watch for this: never expiring sessions. For Caching Strategies, watch for this: cache stampedes. Clear contracts between services make systems easier to change and test.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Flask-Login or Redis.
