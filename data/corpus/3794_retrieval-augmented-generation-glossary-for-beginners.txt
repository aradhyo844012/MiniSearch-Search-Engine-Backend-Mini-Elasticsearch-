Retrieval-Augmented Generation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Retrieval-Augmented Generation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation: a technique that gives a language model relevant documents to read before it answers.
retrieval: a core term, most visible in company knowledge assistants.
chunking: a core term that beginners often confuse with prompts with context.
prompts with context: a core term that connects to grounding.
grounding: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
LangChain: a tool commonly used for question answering over documents.

Come back to this Retrieval-Augmented Generation list whenever documentation for FAISS uses a word you do not recognize.
