Skills to Build Around MQTT

Knowing the definition of MQTT is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with publish and subscribe and topics, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Mosquitto on a MQTT task, and try at least one alternative such as paho-mqtt.

Judgment: learn to decide when MQTT fits a problem such as sensor networks, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a radio station with channels that devices can tune into.

Habits: design topic names like a clear folder structure, and write down what you learn after each project.
