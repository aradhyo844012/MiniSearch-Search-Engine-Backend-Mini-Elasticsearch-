Everything You Need to Know About Multi-Cloud Strategy

This page collects the essentials about Multi-Cloud Strategy in one place. In short, Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.

Core concepts:
- portability
- vendor lock-in
- abstraction layers
- cost comparison

Where it is used:
- avoiding single-provider risk
- meeting regional rules
- using best-of-breed services

Common problems:
- doubling operational complexity
- lowest-common-denominator designs
- hidden data transfer costs

Tools to know:
- Terraform
- Kubernetes
- Crossplane

Remember this above all: shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
