Prompt Engineering Explained Simply

Here is Prompt Engineering without the jargon: it is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models.

Think of Prompt Engineering as giving clear directions to a very literal helper. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: instructions, examples, context, and output format. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with instructions, then add examples.

People reach for Prompt Engineering when they work on drafting content, extracting data from text, and building assistants.

One practical tip is to specify the output format and include one or two examples.
