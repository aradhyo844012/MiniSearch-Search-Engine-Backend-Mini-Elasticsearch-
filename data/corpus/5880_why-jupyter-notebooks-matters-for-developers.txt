Why Jupyter Notebooks Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Jupyter Notebooks make better decisions, because Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.

Three situations show why it matters. First, exploring data. Second, teaching and demos. Third, sharing analysis. In each case, a solid grasp of cells and kernels separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: running cells out of order, hidden state, and committing outputs that contain secrets.

Good tools exist, including JupyterLab, VS Code, and Google Colab, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
