Where to Go Next After Learning Twelve-Factor Apps

You have met the basics of Twelve-Factor Apps. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Cloud-Native Applications, which belongs to the same area as Twelve-Factor Apps (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of config in the environment, stateless processes, backing services or disposability, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around deploying to platforms with Heroku, and note every problem you meet, starting with storing state on local disks.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Twelve-Factor Apps to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
