Mistakes to Avoid with Disaster Recovery

Everyone learns Disaster Recovery partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: untested plans. It usually appears when someone skips recovery time objective and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: outdated runbooks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of surviving regional outages. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: dependencies missed in the plan. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on runbooks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
