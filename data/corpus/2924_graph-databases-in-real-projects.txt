Graph Databases in Real Projects

Reading about Graph Databases is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: social networks. Here, nodes drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against using them for simple tabular data.

Scenario two: recommendation engines. This time edges matters most, and tools such as Neo4j help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: fraud detection. The key idea is properties, and the biggest risk is unbounded traversals.

Across all three scenarios, Graph Databases stays the same idea: databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly. Only the details change.
