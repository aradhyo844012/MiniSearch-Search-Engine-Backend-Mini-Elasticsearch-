Mistakes to Avoid with Subqueries and CTEs

Everyone learns Subqueries and CTEs partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: correlated subqueries that run slowly. It usually appears when someone skips subqueries and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: deep nesting. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of breaking complex reports into steps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming CTEs are always materialized. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on readability to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: name each CTE after what it returns.
