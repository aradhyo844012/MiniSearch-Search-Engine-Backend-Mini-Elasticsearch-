Subqueries and CTEs in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Subqueries and CTEs.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn subqueries, WITH clauses, correlated queries, and readability.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through breaking complex reports into steps, filtering with computed results, and walking recursive hierarchies.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for correlated subqueries that run slowly and deep nesting, then try PostgreSQL for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to name each CTE after what it returns.
