Endianness Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Endianness regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Endianness so the next person understands the decision.
2. Test the case of network protocols early, before the design hardens.
3. Be careful with this one: mixing byte orders.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit little-endian before anything else.
5. Do not skip big-endian; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to binary file formats as well.
