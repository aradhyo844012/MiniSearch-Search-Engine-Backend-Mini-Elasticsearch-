State Management for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover State Management.

Learning goals:
1. Explain State Management in your own words.
2. Describe single source of truth, actions, reducers, and derived state.
3. Give examples from large single-page apps and shared shopping carts.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data. Then draw a shared whiteboard that everyone updates through the same rules on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is putting everything in global state a problem? How does actions relate to reducers? When would you choose Redux or Zustand?

Study habit worth keeping: keep state as close as possible to where it is used.

When you feel ready, compare State Management with Internationalization and write down three differences.
