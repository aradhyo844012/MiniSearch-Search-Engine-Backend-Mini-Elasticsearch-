Regular Expressions in Real Projects

Reading about Regular Expressions is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: validating input. Here, character classes drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against catastrophic backtracking.

Scenario two: searching logs. This time quantifiers matters most, and tools such as the Python re module help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: extracting fields from text. The key idea is groups, and the biggest risk is patterns that are too greedy.

Across all three scenarios, Regular Expressions stays the same idea: compact patterns for matching and extracting text. Only the details change.
