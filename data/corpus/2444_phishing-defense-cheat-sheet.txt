Phishing Defense Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Phishing Defense.

- Definition: recognizing and reducing attacks that trick people into revealing information.
- Core ideas: suspicious links; sender verification; email filtering; user training.
- Use it for: protecting employees; securing shared mailboxes; incident response drills.
- Watch out for: clicking before checking; reusing passwords; ignoring reports from users.
- Tools: SPF, DKIM, DMARC.
- Related topic: Cross-Site Scripting.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for sender verification before building anything large.
