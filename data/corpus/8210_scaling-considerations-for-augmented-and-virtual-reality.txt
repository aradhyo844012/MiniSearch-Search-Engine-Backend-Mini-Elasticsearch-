Scaling Considerations for Augmented and Virtual Reality

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Augmented and Virtual Reality changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding head tracking and getting training simulations right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for motion sickness from low frame rates and heavy assets, and measure with Unity XR before changing anything.

Large scale: spatial mapping and input methods move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around games at this stage.

Before you scale, keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
