Practice Exercises for Autoscaling

These exercises build real understanding of Autoscaling. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare AWS Auto Scaling and KEDA for keeping latency steady and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble handling traffic spikes or saving money overnight.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Autoscaling and include the term scaling metrics.
Exercise 4: Explain how minimum and maximum limits and horizontal scaling differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: scaling too slowly. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.
