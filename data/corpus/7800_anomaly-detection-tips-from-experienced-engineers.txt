Anomaly Detection Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Anomaly Detection regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
2. Reach for scikit-learn first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as PyOD.
3. Do not skip baselines; everything else builds on it.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit thresholds before anything else.
5. Be careful with this one: too many false alarms.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to server monitoring as well as to Anomaly Detection in general.
