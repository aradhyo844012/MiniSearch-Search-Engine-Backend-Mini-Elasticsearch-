Everything You Need to Know About Autoscaling

This page collects the essentials about Autoscaling in one place. In short, Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Core concepts:
- scaling metrics
- cooldown periods
- minimum and maximum limits
- horizontal scaling

Where it is used:
- handling traffic spikes
- saving money overnight
- keeping latency steady

Common problems:
- scaling too slowly
- flapping because of short spikes
- ignoring database limits

Tools to know:
- Kubernetes HPA
- AWS Auto Scaling
- KEDA

Remember this above all: a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
