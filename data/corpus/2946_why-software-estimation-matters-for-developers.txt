Why Software Estimation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Software Estimation make better decisions, because Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.

Three situations show why it matters. First, sprint planning. Second, setting deadlines. Third, negotiating scope. In each case, a solid grasp of story points and ranges separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: treating estimates as promises, ignoring unknowns, and estimating alone.

You will not need to build everything yourself; planning poker, Jira, and spreadsheets cover most needs.
