Why Image Compression Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Image Compression make better decisions, because Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail.

Three situations show why it matters. First, web images. Second, photo storage. Third, sending pictures over slow networks. In each case, a solid grasp of lossy and lossless formats and quantization separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: repeated recompression, the wrong format for graphics, and excessive quality loss.

You will not need to build everything yourself; ImageMagick, mozjpeg, and Squoosh cover most needs.
