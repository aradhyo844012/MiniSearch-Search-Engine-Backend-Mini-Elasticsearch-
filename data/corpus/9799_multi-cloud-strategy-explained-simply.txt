Multi-Cloud Strategy Explained Simply

If Multi-Cloud Strategy sounds intimidating, relax. It is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Multi-Cloud Strategy as shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: portability, vendor lock-in, abstraction layers, and cost comparison. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with portability, then add vendor lock-in.

You will meet Multi-Cloud Strategy in work such as avoiding single-provider risk, meeting regional rules, and using best-of-breed services.

One practical tip is to be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
