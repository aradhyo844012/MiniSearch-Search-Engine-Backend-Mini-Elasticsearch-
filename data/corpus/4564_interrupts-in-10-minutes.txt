Interrupts in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Interrupts.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Interrupts is signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn interrupt handlers, interrupt vectors, priorities, and context saving.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a phone call interrupting your reading. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through keyboard and mouse input, timers, and network packets arriving.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for long handlers and race conditions with shared data, then try Linux /proc/interrupts for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
