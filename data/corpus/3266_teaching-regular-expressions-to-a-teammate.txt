Teaching Regular Expressions to a Teammate

Explaining Regular Expressions to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stencil that only lets certain shapes of text show through. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving validating input, naming character classes and quantifiers as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain groups in Regular Expressions back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, catastrophic backtracking, and suggest this habit: build patterns in small steps and test each one.
