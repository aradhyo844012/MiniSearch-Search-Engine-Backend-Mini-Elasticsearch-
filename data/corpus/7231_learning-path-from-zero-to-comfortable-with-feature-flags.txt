Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Feature Flags

This path is for a learner who has never studied Feature Flags before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code) and learn flag evaluation and targeting rules.

Stage 2, hands-on: install LaunchDarkly, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving dark launches.

Stage 3, depth: study gradual rollout and flag cleanup, then deliberately explore this problem: never removing old flags.

Stage 4, application: apply Feature Flags to A/B tests, compare Unleash with OpenFeature, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Feature Flags to someone else, and then move on to Autoscaling.
