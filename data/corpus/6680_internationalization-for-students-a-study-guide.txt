Internationalization for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Internationalization.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Internationalization in your own words.
2. Describe locale detection, translation files, pluralization, and date and number formatting.
3. Give examples from global products and multi-language websites.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions. Then draw a stage set where the backdrop can be swapped for each audience on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is hard-coded strings a problem? How does translation files relate to pluralization? When would you choose i18next or the Intl API?

Study habit: move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.

When you feel ready, compare Internationalization with Web Components and write down three differences.
