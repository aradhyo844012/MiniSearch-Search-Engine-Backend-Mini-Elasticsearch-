Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Multi-Cloud Strategy

This path is for a learner who has never studied Multi-Cloud Strategy before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose) and learn portability and vendor lock-in.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Terraform, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving avoiding single-provider risk.

Stage 3, depth: study abstraction layers and cost comparison, then deliberately explore this problem: doubling operational complexity.

Stage 4, application: apply Multi-Cloud Strategy to meeting regional rules, compare Kubernetes with Crossplane, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Multi-Cloud Strategy to someone else, and then move on to AWS IAM Roles and Policies.
