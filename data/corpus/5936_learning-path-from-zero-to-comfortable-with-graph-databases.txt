Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Graph Databases

This path is for a learner who has never studied Graph Databases before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly) and learn nodes and edges.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Neo4j, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving social networks.

Stage 3, depth: study properties and traversal queries, then deliberately explore this problem: using them for simple tabular data.

Stage 4, application: apply Graph Databases to recommendation engines, compare Cypher with Amazon Neptune, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Graph Databases to someone else, and then move on to LSM Trees.
