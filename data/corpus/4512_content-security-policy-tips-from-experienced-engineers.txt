Content Security Policy Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Content Security Policy regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: allowing unsafe-inline.
2. Make sure you can start in report-only mode to learn what would break.
3. Write down why you chose Content Security Policy so the next person understands the decision.
4. Test the case of reducing the impact of XSS early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for browser DevTools first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as CSP Evaluator.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to blocking unwanted scripts as well.
