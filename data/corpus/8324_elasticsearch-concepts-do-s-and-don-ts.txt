Elasticsearch Concepts Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Elasticsearch Concepts.

Do:
- Learn indexes before anything else.
- Try Elasticsearch on a small example.
- Make it a habit to define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
- Test the case of site search early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too many shards.
- Ignore this: mapping explosions.
- Ignore this: treating it as a primary database.
- Adopt Elasticsearch Concepts just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Elasticsearch Concepts, a little patience with shards pays off more than any clever shortcut.
