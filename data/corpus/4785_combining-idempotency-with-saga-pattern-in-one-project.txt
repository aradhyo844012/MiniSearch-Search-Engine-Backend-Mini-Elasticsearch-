Combining Idempotency with Saga Pattern in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Idempotency and Saga Pattern can work together in a single project.

Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once. Saga Pattern is a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions.

Imagine a project that involves payment APIs and order processing. Idempotency brings idempotency keys, while Saga Pattern brings local transactions.

A sensible plan is to start with Stripe idempotency keys for the Idempotency part and Temporal for the Saga Pattern part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: side effects outside the guarded step on one side, and steps that are not idempotent on the other.

Picture the whole as pressing an elevator call button many times and still getting one elevator, working alongside booking a trip with separate hotel, flight and car reservations that can each be cancelled.

Two habits help: first, store the result with the key so retries return the same response; second, make every step and its compensation idempotent.
