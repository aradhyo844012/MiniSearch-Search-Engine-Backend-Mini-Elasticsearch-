Bluetooth Low Energy Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Bluetooth Low Energy regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: ignoring connection limits.
2. Do not skip advertising; everything else builds on it.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit GATT services before anything else.
4. Reach for nRF Connect first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as BlueZ.
5. Test the case of fitness trackers early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to smart locks as well.
