A Practical Checklist for Stream Processing

Use this checklist before you rely on Stream Processing in a project.

1. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on windows.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Review the core ideas: events, windows, offsets, and state.
4. Write at least one automated test that covers events.
5. Guard against this risk: late events.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Stream Processing should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: duplicate processing.
