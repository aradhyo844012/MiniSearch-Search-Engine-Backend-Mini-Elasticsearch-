Internationalization Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Internationalization regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: hard-coded strings.
2. Test the case of global products early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit translation files before anything else.
4. Write down why you chose Internationalization so the next person understands the decision.
5. Make sure you can move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to multi-language websites as well.
