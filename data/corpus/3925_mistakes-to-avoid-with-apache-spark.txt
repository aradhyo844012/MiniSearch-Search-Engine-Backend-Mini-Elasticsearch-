Mistakes to Avoid with Apache Spark

Everyone learns Apache Spark partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: data skew. It usually appears when someone skips DataFrames and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: too many small partitions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of large ETL jobs. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: collecting huge results onto the driver. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on lazy execution to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
