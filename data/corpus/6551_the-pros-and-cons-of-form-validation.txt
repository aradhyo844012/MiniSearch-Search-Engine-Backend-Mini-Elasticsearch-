The Pros and Cons of Form Validation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Form Validation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sign-up forms
- Makes payment pages easier
- Helps with search filters

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: validating only on the client
- Risk: vague error messages
- Risk: rejecting valid but unusual input

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sign-up forms, and you can manage the risks, Form Validation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
