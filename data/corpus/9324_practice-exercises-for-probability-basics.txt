Practice Exercises for Probability Basics

These exercises build real understanding of Probability Basics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Python random and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Explain how independence and expected value differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Compare NumPy and simulation notebooks for risk estimates and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble randomized algorithms or machine learning.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: confusing independent and exclusive events. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Probability Basics is the study of how likely events are to happen.
