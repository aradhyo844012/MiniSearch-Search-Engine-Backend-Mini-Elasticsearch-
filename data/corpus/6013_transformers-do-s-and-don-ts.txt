Transformers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Transformers.

Do:
- Learn self-attention before anything else.
- Try Hugging Face on a small example.
- Make it a habit to begin with small pretrained models.
- Test the case of language translation early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: huge compute requirements.
- Ignore this: limited context windows.
- Ignore this: biased training data.
- Adopt Transformers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Transformers, a little patience with positional encoding pays off more than any clever shortcut.
