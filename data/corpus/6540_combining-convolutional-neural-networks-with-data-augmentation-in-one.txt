Combining Convolutional Neural Networks with Data Augmentation in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Convolutional Neural Networks and Data Augmentation can work together in a single project.

Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

Imagine a project that involves image classification and image models. Convolutional Neural Networks brings filters, while Data Augmentation brings transformations.

A sensible plan is to start with PyTorch for the Convolutional Neural Networks part and Albumentations for the Data Augmentation part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: ignoring data augmentation on one side, and augmenting the test set on the other.

Picture the whole as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes, working alongside practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields.

Two habits help: first, start from a pretrained model; second, augment only the training data.
