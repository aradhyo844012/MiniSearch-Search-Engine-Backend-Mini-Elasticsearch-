Wearable Technology Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Wearable Technology.

Do:
- Learn sensors before anything else.
- Try Wear OS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to design interactions that take only a few seconds.
- Test the case of fitness tracking early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: battery drain.
- Ignore this: inaccurate sensor readings.
- Ignore this: privacy of personal data.
- Adopt Wearable Technology just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Wearable Technology, a little patience with low-power design pays off more than any clever shortcut.
