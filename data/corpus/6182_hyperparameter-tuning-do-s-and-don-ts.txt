Hyperparameter Tuning Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Hyperparameter Tuning.

Do:
- Learn search spaces before anything else.
- Try Optuna on a small example.
- Make it a habit to try random search before anything fancier.
- Test the case of improving model accuracy early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: tuning on the test set.
- Ignore this: searching too many options.
- Ignore this: ignoring training cost.
- Adopt Hyperparameter Tuning just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Hyperparameter Tuning, a little patience with grid search pays off more than any clever shortcut.
