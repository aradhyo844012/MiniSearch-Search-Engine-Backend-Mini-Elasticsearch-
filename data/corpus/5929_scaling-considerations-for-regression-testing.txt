Scaling Considerations for Regression Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Regression Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding test suites and getting protecting releases right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow suites that nobody runs and missing tests for fixed bugs, and measure with pytest before changing anything.

Large scale: test selection and bug reproduction tests move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around verifying bug fixes at this stage.

Before you scale, add a test for every bug you fix. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
