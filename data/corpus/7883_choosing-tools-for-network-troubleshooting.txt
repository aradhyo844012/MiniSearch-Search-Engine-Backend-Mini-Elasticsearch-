Choosing Tools for Network Troubleshooting

There is no single best tool for Network Troubleshooting. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ping is a common first choice, especially for fixing office connectivity. traceroute suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Wireshark is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Network Troubleshooting by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle diagnosing slow apps well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Network Troubleshooting, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: changing several things at once.

As a rule of thumb, work from the bottom layer upward, no matter which tool you use.
