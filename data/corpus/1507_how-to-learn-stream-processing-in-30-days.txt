How to Learn Stream Processing in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Stream Processing. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Stream Processing covers handling data continuously as it arrives rather than in large batches. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn events and windows, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Apache Kafka on a small task such as live dashboards.
Week 3: build a mini project around fraud detection.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as late events, and explain Stream Processing to a friend.

In practice you can start with Apache Kafka, then explore Apache Flink or Spark Structured Streaming as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Stream Processing.
