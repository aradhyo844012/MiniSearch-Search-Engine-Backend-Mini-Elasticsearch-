Performance Tips for DOM Manipulation

Fast software rarely happens by accident, and DOM Manipulation offers several places to gain speed. The term DOM Manipulation refers to reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Start by measuring. Use querySelector or a simple timer to find out where time is really spent, rather than guessing. Pay special attention to selectors and events, because they usually decide how well things scale. For workloads such as updating search results, even small improvements add up quickly.

The vocabulary of DOM Manipulation is small. Learn selectors, events, nodes, and updates, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching updates on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.
