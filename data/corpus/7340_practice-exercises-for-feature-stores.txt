Practice Exercises for Feature Stores

These exercises build real understanding of Feature Stores. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Tecton and Databricks Feature Store for avoiding training-serving skew and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Pick Feast and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble sharing features across models or serving real-time predictions.
Exercise 4: Explain how point-in-time correctness and feature definitions differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Feature Stores and include the term offline store.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Feature Stores is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models.
