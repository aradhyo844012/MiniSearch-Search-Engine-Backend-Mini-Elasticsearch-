Union-Find in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Union-Find.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Union-Find is a data structure that tracks which items belong to the same group and merges groups quickly.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn parent pointers, union operation, find operation, and path compression.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a set of club rosters where two clubs can merge into one. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through detecting connected components, building minimum spanning trees, and grouping equivalent items.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for forgetting path compression and merging without checking roots, then try Python lists for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to implement path compression and union by rank together.
