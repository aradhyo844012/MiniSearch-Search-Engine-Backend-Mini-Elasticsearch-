Blue-Green Deployment Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Blue-Green Deployment feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Blue-Green Deployment: a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one.
blue and green environments: a core term, most visible in zero-downtime releases.
traffic switching: a core term that beginners often confuse with rollback.
rollback: a core term that connects to database compatibility.
database compatibility: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
AWS CodeDeploy: a tool commonly used for quick rollbacks.

Come back to this Blue-Green Deployment list whenever documentation for Kubernetes services uses a word you do not recognize.
