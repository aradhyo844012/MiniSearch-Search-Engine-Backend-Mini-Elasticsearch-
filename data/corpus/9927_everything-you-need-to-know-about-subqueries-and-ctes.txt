Everything You Need to Know About Subqueries and CTEs

This page collects the essentials about Subqueries and CTEs in one place. In short, Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.

Core concepts:
- subqueries
- WITH clauses
- correlated queries
- readability

Where it is used:
- breaking complex reports into steps
- filtering with computed results
- walking recursive hierarchies

Common problems:
- correlated subqueries that run slowly
- deep nesting
- assuming CTEs are always materialized

Tools to know:
- PostgreSQL
- SQLite
- dbt

Remember this above all: solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to name each CTE after what it returns.
