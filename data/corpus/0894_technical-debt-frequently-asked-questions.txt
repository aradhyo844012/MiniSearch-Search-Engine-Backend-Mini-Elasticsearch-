Technical Debt: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Technical Debt?
A: The term Technical Debt refers to the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with shortcuts and interest, then move on to refactoring backlog and code smells.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: never paying it down; hiding it from stakeholders; and unclear ownership.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Technical Debt.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: How does it relate to Design Patterns?
A: Technical Debt and Design Patterns often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Software Engineering.
