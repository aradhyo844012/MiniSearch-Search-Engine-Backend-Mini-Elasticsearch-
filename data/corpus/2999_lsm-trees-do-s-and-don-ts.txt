LSM Trees Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with LSM Trees.

Do:
- Learn memtable before anything else.
- Try RocksDB on a small example.
- Make it a habit to watch compaction metrics before blaming the application.
- Test the case of write-heavy databases early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: read amplification.
- Ignore this: compaction stalls.
- Ignore this: poor bloom filter tuning.
- Adopt LSM Trees just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with LSM Trees, a little patience with sorted string tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
