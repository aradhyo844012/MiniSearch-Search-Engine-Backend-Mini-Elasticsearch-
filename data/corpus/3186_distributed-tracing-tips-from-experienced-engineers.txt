Distributed Tracing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Distributed Tracing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for OpenTelemetry first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Jaeger.
2. Do not skip spans; everything else builds on it.
3. Be careful with this one: missing context propagation.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit trace IDs before anything else.
5. Write down why you chose Distributed Tracing so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to understanding dependencies as well.
