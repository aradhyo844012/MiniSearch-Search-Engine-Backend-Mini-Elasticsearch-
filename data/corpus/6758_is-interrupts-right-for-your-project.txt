Is Interrupts Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Interrupts, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Interrupts is signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event.

It is a good fit when your work involves keyboard and mouse input or timers, and when your team understands interrupt handlers.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn interrupt vectors, or when you cannot manage these risks: long handlers, race conditions with shared data, and lost interrupts.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether priorities and context saving are part of the answer. If they are, Interrupts is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with network packets arriving and keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
