Scaling Considerations for Data Quality Checks

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Data Quality Checks changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding null checks and getting protecting dashboards right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for alerts nobody reads and checks only at the end of pipelines, and measure with Great Expectations before changing anything.

Large scale: freshness and schema validation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around validating ingestion at this stage.

Before you scale, check data as early as it enters the pipeline. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
