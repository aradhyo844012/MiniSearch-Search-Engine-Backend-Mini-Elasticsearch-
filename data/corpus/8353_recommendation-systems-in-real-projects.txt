Recommendation Systems in Real Projects

Reading about Recommendation Systems is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: streaming services. Here, collaborative filtering drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against feedback loops.

Scenario two: online shops. This time content-based filtering matters most, and tools such as Surprise help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: news feeds. The key idea is ranking, and the biggest risk is popularity bias.

Across all three scenarios, Recommendation Systems stays the same idea: systems that suggest items a user is likely to want. Only the details change.
