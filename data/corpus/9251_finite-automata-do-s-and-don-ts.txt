Finite Automata Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Finite Automata.

Do:
- Learn states before anything else.
- Try JFLAP on a small example.
- Make it a habit to draw the state diagram before writing any code.
- Test the case of tokenizers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: missing transitions.
- Ignore this: confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines.
- Ignore this: state explosion.
- Adopt Finite Automata just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Finite Automata, a little patience with transitions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
