Is System Design Interviews Right for Your Project?

Before adopting System Design Interviews, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices.

It is a good fit when your work involves preparing for senior roles or practicing architectural thinking, and when your team understands requirements.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn scaling estimates, or when you cannot manage these risks: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements, ignoring trade-offs, and overcomplicating designs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether trade-offs and components are part of the answer. If they are, System Design Interviews is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with explaining real systems and clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
