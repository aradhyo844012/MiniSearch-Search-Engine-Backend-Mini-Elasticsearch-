Where to Go Next After Learning Ray Tracing

You have met the basics of Ray Tracing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Font Rendering, which belongs to the same area as Ray Tracing (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of rays, intersections, reflection and refraction or shadows, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around architectural visualization with Blender Cycles, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow render times.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Ray Tracing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
