Process Signals and Job Control Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Process Signals and Job Control feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Process Signals and Job Control: messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs.
SIGTERM and SIGKILL: a core term, most visible in stopping runaway programs.
foreground and background jobs: a core term that beginners often confuse with kill and trap.
kill and trap: a core term that connects to process groups.
process groups: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
kill: a tool commonly used for shutting down gracefully.

Come back to this Process Signals and Job Control list whenever documentation for jobs and fg uses a word you do not recognize.
