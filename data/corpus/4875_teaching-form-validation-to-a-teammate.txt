Teaching Form Validation to a Teammate

Explaining Form Validation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a proofreader checking a form before it is submitted. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Form Validation is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sign-up forms, naming client-side checks and server-side checks as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain error messages in Form Validation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, validating only on the client, and suggest this habit: validate on the server even when the browser checks too.
