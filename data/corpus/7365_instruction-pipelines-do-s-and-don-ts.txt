Instruction Pipelines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Instruction Pipelines.

Do:
- Learn fetch, decode and execute stages before anything else.
- Try perf on a small example.
- Make it a habit to avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops.
- Test the case of explaining CPU speed early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: unpredictable branches.
- Ignore this: dependency chains.
- Ignore this: assuming each instruction takes one step.
- Adopt Instruction Pipelines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Instruction Pipelines, a little patience with hazards pays off more than any clever shortcut.
