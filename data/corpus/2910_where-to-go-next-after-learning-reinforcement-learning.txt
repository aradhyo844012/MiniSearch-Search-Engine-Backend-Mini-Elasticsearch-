Where to Go Next After Learning Reinforcement Learning

You have met the basics of Reinforcement Learning. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Transformers, which belongs to the same area as Reinforcement Learning (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of agent, environment, reward or policy, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around robot control with Gymnasium, and note every problem you meet, starting with badly designed rewards.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Reinforcement Learning to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
