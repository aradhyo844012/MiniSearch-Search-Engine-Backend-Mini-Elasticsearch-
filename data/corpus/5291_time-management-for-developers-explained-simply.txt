Time Management for Developers Explained Simply

If Time Management for Developers sounds intimidating, relax. It is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Time Management for Developers as packing a suitcase with the big items first. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: prioritization, time blocking, breaking down tasks, and avoiding context switches. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with prioritization, then add time blocking.

You will meet Time Management for Developers in work such as managing coursework and projects, meeting deadlines, and reducing stress.

One practical tip is to break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
