Scaling Considerations for CQRS

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how CQRS changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding commands and getting high-read dashboards right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for adding it to simple apps and read models drifting out of sync, and measure with Axon Framework before changing anything.

Large scale: read models and eventual consistency move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around complex domains at this stage.

Before you scale, start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
