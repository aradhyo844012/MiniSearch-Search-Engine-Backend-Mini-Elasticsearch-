Multi-Cloud Strategy for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Multi-Cloud Strategy.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Multi-Cloud Strategy in your own words.
2. Describe portability, vendor lock-in, abstraction layers, and cost comparison.
3. Give examples from avoiding single-provider risk and meeting regional rules.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose. Then draw shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is doubling operational complexity a problem? How does vendor lock-in relate to abstraction layers? When would you choose Terraform or Kubernetes?

Study habit: be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.

When you feel ready, compare Multi-Cloud Strategy with AWS IAM Roles and Policies and write down three differences.
