Why Pagination Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Pagination make better decisions, because Pagination is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time.

Three situations show why it matters. First, search results. Second, activity feeds. Third, admin tables. In each case, a solid grasp of offset pagination and cursor pagination separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: offsets getting slow on huge tables, unstable sort order, and missing total counts when users need them.

You will not need to build everything yourself; SQL LIMIT and OFFSET, keyset queries, and Django REST Framework cover most needs.
