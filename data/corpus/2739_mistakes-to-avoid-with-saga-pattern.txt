Mistakes to Avoid with Saga Pattern

Everyone learns Saga Pattern partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: missing compensations. It usually appears when someone skips local transactions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: steps that are not idempotent. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of order processing. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: flows that are hard to trace. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on choreography to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: make every step and its compensation idempotent.
