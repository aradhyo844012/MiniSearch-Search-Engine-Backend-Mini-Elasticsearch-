Arduino Basics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Arduino Basics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Arduino Basics is a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sketches, digital and analog pins, setup and loop functions, and the serial monitor.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a tiny brain that watches inputs and flips switches. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through reading sensors, driving motors and LEDs, and learning electronics.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for wiring errors and blocking delays in the loop, then try Arduino IDE for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test each sensor on its own before combining them.
