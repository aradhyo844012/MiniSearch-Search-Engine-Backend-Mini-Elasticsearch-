Debugging Column-Oriented Storage: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Column-Oriented Storage, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: slow single-row lookups, many small writes, and wide tables with unused columns.
Step 3: revisit column chunks and compression, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Apache Parquet or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Column-Oriented Storage in work such as analytics dashboards come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, select only the columns you really need. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
