Segment Trees Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Segment Trees.

Do:
- Learn tree nodes covering ranges before anything else.
- Try C++ arrays on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test your tree against a slow brute-force version on small inputs.
- Test the case of range sum queries early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: off-by-one errors in ranges.
- Ignore this: wrong tree size allocation.
- Ignore this: forgetting to push lazy updates.
- Adopt Segment Trees just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Segment Trees, a little patience with range queries pays off more than any clever shortcut.
