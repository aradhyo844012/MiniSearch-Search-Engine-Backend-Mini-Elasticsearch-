Why 3D Transformations Matters for Developers

Developers who understand 3D Transformations make better decisions, because 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.

Three situations show why it matters. First, games. Second, modeling tools. Third, robotics. In each case, a solid grasp of translation and rotation separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: wrong multiplication order, gimbal lock, and mixing coordinate systems.

Good tools exist, including GLM, three.js, and Blender, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
