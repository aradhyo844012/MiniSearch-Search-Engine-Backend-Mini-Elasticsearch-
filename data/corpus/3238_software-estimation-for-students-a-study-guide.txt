Software Estimation for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Software Estimation.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Software Estimation in your own words.
2. Describe story points, ranges, uncertainty, and velocity.
3. Give examples from sprint planning and setting deadlines.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take. Then draw forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is treating estimates as promises a problem? How does ranges relate to uncertainty? When would you choose planning poker or Jira?

Study habit: give ranges instead of single numbers.

When you feel ready, compare Software Estimation with Event-Driven Architecture and write down three differences.
