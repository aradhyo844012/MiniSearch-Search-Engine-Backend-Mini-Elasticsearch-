Teaching Reinforcement Learning to a Teammate

Explaining Reinforcement Learning to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: training a dog with treats for good behavior. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving game playing, naming agent and environment as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain reward in Reinforcement Learning back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, badly designed rewards, and suggest this habit: start with simple environments.
