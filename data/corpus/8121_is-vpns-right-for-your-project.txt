Is VPNs Right for Your Project?

Before adopting VPNs, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.

It is a good fit when your work involves remote work access or linking offices, and when your team understands tunneling.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn encryption, or when you cannot manage these risks: assuming it makes you anonymous, slow routing through distant servers, and leaking DNS requests.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether authentication and split tunneling are part of the answer. If they are, VPNs is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi and test for DNS leaks after connecting.
