Skills to Build Around Blockchain Fundamentals

Knowing the definition of Blockchain Fundamentals is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with blocks and hashes, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Bitcoin Core on a Blockchain Fundamentals task, and try at least one alternative such as Ethereum.

Judgment: learn to decide when Blockchain Fundamentals fits a problem such as digital currencies, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page.

Habits: ask whether a normal database would solve the problem, and write down what you learn after each project.
