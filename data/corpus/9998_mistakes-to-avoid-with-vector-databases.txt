Mistakes to Avoid with Vector Databases

Everyone learns Vector Databases partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing embedding models. It usually appears when someone skips embeddings and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring recall trade-offs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of semantic search. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: oversized vectors. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on metadata filters to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: measure recall on a small labeled test set.
