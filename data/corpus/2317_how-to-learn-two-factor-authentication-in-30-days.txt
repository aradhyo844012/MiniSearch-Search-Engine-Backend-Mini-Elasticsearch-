How to Learn Two-Factor Authentication in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Two-Factor Authentication. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Two-Factor Authentication covers requiring a second proof of identity in addition to a password. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn one-time codes and authenticator apps, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Google Authenticator on a small task such as protecting email accounts.
Week 3: build a mini project around securing admin panels.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as relying only on SMS, and explain Two-Factor Authentication to a friend.

Keeping dependencies updated removes a large share of known risks. Attackers usually choose the easiest weakness, not the most interesting one.

In practice you can start with Google Authenticator, then explore YubiKey or TOTP libraries as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of Two-Factor Authentication pays off. Spend time on one-time codes and authenticator apps, and the rest will follow.
