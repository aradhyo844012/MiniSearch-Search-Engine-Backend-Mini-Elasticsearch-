Why Progressive Web Apps Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Progressive Web Apps make better decisions, because Progressive Web Apps is web applications that can be installed and work offline like native apps.

Three situations show why it matters. First, offline-friendly tools. Second, lightweight alternatives to native apps. Third, low-bandwidth markets. In each case, a solid grasp of web app manifest and service worker separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: ignoring platform limitations, poor offline fallbacks, and heavy first loads.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Lighthouse, Workbox, and PWABuilder cover most needs.
