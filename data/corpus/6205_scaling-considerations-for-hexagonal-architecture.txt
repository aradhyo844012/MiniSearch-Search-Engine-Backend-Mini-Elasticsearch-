Scaling Considerations for Hexagonal Architecture

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Hexagonal Architecture changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding ports and getting testable business logic right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for too many layers for small apps and leaking framework types into the core, and measure with Spring before changing anything.

Large scale: the core domain and dependency direction move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around swapping databases at this stage.

Before you scale, keep framework imports out of the core domain. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
