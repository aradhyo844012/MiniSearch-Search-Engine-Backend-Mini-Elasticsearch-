The Pros and Cons of Flutter

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Flutter is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports cross-platform mobile apps
- Makes desktop apps easier
- Helps with custom user interfaces

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: deep widget trees
- Risk: poor state organization
- Risk: large app sizes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as cross-platform mobile apps, and you can manage the risks, Flutter is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, split big widgets into small reusable ones.
