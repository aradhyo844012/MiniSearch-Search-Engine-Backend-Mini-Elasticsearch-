Where to Go Next After Learning Push Notifications

You have met the basics of Push Notifications. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Arduino Basics, which belongs to the same area as Push Notifications (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of device tokens, push services, payloads or permissions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around chat alerts with Firebase Cloud Messaging, and note every problem you meet, starting with asking for permission too early.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Push Notifications to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
