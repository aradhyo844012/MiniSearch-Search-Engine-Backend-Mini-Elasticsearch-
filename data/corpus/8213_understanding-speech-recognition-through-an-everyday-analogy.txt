Understanding Speech Recognition Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a stenographer who types as people talk.

In that picture, audio features is the first rule you follow, acoustic models is the next step, and language models and word error rate keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning, and it involves details such as accents and background noise that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Whisper to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain voice assistants to a friend.
