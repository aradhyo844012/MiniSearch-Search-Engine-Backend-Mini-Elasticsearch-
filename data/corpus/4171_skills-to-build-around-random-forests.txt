Skills to Build Around Random Forests

Knowing the definition of Random Forests is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with bagging and feature randomness, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with scikit-learn on a Random Forests task, and try at least one alternative such as XGBoost.

Judgment: learn to decide when Random Forests fits a problem such as tabular predictions, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example asking many different experts and taking the majority view.

Habits: tune the number of trees only after the basics work, and write down what you learn after each project.
