Debugging Client-Side Routing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Client-Side Routing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: broken deep links, unhandled 404 pages, and lost scroll position.
Step 3: revisit history API and route matching, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use React Router or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Client-Side Routing in work such as single-page apps come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test refreshing the page on every route. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
