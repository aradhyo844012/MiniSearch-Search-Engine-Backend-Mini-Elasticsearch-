Where to Go Next After Learning Wi-Fi Networks

You have met the basics of Wi-Fi Networks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Firewalls, which belongs to the same area as Wi-Fi Networks (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of SSIDs, channels and bands, WPA encryption or signal strength, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around office coverage with Wi-Fi analyzer apps, and note every problem you meet, starting with overlapping channels.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Wi-Fi Networks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
