Authentication and Sessions Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Authentication and Sessions.

- Definition: the process of proving who a user is and remembering them between requests.
- Core ideas: credentials; session cookies; tokens; expiration.
- Use it for: logging in to web apps; protecting admin pages; tracking signed-in users.
- Watch out for: storing session IDs insecurely; never expiring sessions; trusting client-side checks.
- Tools: Flask-Login, Django auth, Passport.js.
- Related topic: JSON.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for session cookies before building anything large.
