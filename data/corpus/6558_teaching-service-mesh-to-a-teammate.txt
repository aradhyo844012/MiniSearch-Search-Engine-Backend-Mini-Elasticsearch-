Teaching Service Mesh to a Teammate

Explaining Service Mesh to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving securing service traffic, naming sidecar proxies and mutual TLS as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain traffic policies in Service Mesh back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, added latency and complexity, and suggest this habit: adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
