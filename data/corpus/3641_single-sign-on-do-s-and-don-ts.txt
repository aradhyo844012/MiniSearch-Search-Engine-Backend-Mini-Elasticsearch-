Single Sign-On Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Single Sign-On.

Do:
- Learn identity provider before anything else.
- Try Okta on a small example.
- Make it a habit to plan for identity provider outages.
- Test the case of company intranets early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: a single point of failure.
- Ignore this: misconfigured redirects.
- Ignore this: long sessions on shared devices.
- Adopt Single Sign-On just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Single Sign-On, a little patience with SAML pays off more than any clever shortcut.
