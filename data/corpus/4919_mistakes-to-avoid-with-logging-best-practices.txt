Mistakes to Avoid with Logging Best Practices

Everyone learns Logging Best Practices partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: logging sensitive data. It usually appears when someone skips log levels and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: too much noise. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of debugging production issues. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: missing context. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on log rotation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: include a request ID in every log line.
