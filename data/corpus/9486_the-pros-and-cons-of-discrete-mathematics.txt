The Pros and Cons of Discrete Mathematics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Discrete Mathematics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports algorithm analysis
- Makes database theory easier
- Helps with cryptography

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: skipping proofs
- Risk: confusing sets with sequences
- Risk: careless notation

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as algorithm analysis, and you can manage the risks, Discrete Mathematics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, practice writing small proofs by hand.
