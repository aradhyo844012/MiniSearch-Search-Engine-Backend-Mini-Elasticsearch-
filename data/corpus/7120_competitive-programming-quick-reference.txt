Competitive Programming Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Competitive Programming. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills.
Key terms: problem patterns, time limits, contests, and upsolving.
Typical uses: interview preparation, learning algorithms, and practicing under pressure.
Risks: memorizing solutions without understanding, skipping fundamentals, and burning out.
Tools: Codeforces, LeetCode, and AtCoder.

Mental picture: athletic training for problem solving.

Habit to build: review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
