Mistakes to Avoid with Reading Documentation

Everyone learns Reading Documentation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: reading the wrong version. It usually appears when someone skips search and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: skipping the overview. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of learning new libraries. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: copying examples blindly. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on version numbers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: check that the documentation version matches your installed version.
