Practice Exercises for Statistical Significance

These exercises build real understanding of Statistical Significance. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick SciPy and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Statistical Significance and include the term p-values.
Exercise 3: Explain how confidence intervals and effect size differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble judging experiment results or comparing model performance.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mistaking significance for importance. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect.
