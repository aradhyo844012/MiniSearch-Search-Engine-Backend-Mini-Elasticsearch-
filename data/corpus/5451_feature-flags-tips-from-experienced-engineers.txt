Feature Flags Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Feature Flags regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for LaunchDarkly first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Unleash.
2. Be careful with this one: never removing old flags.
3. Write down why you chose Feature Flags so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip flag evaluation; everything else builds on it.
5. Test the case of dark launches early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to A/B tests as well.
