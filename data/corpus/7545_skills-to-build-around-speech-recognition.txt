Skills to Build Around Speech Recognition

Knowing the definition of Speech Recognition is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with audio features and acoustic models, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Whisper on a Speech Recognition task, and try at least one alternative such as Kaldi.

Judgment: learn to decide when Speech Recognition fits a problem such as voice assistants, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a stenographer who types as people talk.

Habits: measure word error rate on audio like your real users', and write down what you learn after each project.
