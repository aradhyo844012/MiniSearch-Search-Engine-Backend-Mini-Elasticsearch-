Database Schema Design for Beginners in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Database Schema Design for Beginners.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Database Schema Design for Beginners is planning tables, columns and relationships before building an application.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn entities, relationships, keys, and naming conventions.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine drawing the floor plan before building the house. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through starting new projects, reviewing existing schemas, and preparing for migrations.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for storing lists in a single column and unclear names, then try dbdiagram.io for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to write down the main queries before finalizing the tables.
