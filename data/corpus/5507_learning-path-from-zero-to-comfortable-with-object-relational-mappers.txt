Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Object-Relational Mappers

This path is for a learner who has never studied Object-Relational Mappers before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code) and learn models and queries.

Stage 2, hands-on: install SQLAlchemy, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving building CRUD apps quickly.

Stage 3, depth: study relationships and migrations, then deliberately explore this problem: N+1 queries.

Stage 4, application: apply Object-Relational Mappers to switching databases, compare the Django ORM with Hibernate, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Object-Relational Mappers to someone else, and then move on to GraphQL.
