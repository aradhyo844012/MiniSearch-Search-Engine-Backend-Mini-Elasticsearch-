Why Threat Modeling Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Threat Modeling make better decisions, because Threat Modeling is a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it.

Three situations show why it matters. First, designing new features. Second, reviewing architecture. Third, prioritizing security work. In each case, a solid grasp of assets and attackers separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: doing it once and never again, ignoring data flows, and focusing only on technical threats.

You will not need to build everything yourself; STRIDE, OWASP Threat Dragon, and data flow diagrams cover most needs.
