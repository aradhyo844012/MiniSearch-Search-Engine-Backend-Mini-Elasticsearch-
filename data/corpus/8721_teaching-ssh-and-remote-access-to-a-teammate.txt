Teaching SSH and Remote Access to a Teammate

Explaining SSH and Remote Access to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a private phone line with a secret handshake to a faraway office. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: SSH and Remote Access is a secure protocol for logging in to and controlling remote machines. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving administering servers, naming key pairs and host keys as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain port forwarding in SSH and Remote Access back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, password logins left enabled, and suggest this habit: use key-based login and disable password authentication.
