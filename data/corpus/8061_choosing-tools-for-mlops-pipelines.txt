Choosing Tools for MLOps Pipelines

There is no single best tool for MLOps Pipelines. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

MLflow is a common first choice, especially for repeatable model releases. Kubeflow suits teams that already work with similar technology, and DVC is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for MLOps Pipelines by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle tracking experiments well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for MLOps Pipelines, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: untracked data changes.

As a rule of thumb, version both the code and the data, no matter which tool you use.
