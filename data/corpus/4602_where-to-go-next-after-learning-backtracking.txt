Where to Go Next After Learning Backtracking

You have met the basics of Backtracking. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Skip Lists, which belongs to the same area as Backtracking (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of decision tree, pruning, state restoration or constraint checking, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around generating permutations with Python recursion, and note every problem you meet, starting with forgetting to undo changes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Backtracking to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
