The Pros and Cons of Saga Pattern

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Saga Pattern is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports order processing
- Makes travel bookings easier
- Helps with payment workflows

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing compensations
- Risk: steps that are not idempotent
- Risk: flows that are hard to trace

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as order processing, and you can manage the risks, Saga Pattern is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, make every step and its compensation idempotent.
