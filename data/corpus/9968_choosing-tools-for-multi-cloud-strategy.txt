Choosing Tools for Multi-Cloud Strategy

There is no single best tool for Multi-Cloud Strategy. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Terraform is a common first choice, especially for avoiding single-provider risk. Kubernetes suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Crossplane is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Multi-Cloud Strategy by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle meeting regional rules well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Multi-Cloud Strategy, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: doubling operational complexity.

As a rule of thumb, be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve, no matter which tool you use.
