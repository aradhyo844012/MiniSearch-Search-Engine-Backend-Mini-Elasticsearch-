Scaling Considerations for Combinatorics

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Combinatorics changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding permutations and getting analyzing algorithms right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for double counting and mixing order and no-order cases, and measure with Python itertools before changing anything.

Large scale: the pigeonhole principle and inclusion-exclusion move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around probability at this stage.

Before you scale, test counting formulas on tiny cases first. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
