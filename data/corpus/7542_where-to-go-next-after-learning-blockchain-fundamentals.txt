Where to Go Next After Learning Blockchain Fundamentals

You have met the basics of Blockchain Fundamentals. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Biometric Authentication, which belongs to the same area as Blockchain Fundamentals (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of blocks, hashes, consensus or decentralization, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around supply chain tracking with Bitcoin Core, and note every problem you meet, starting with treating it as a database replacement.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Blockchain Fundamentals to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
