Understanding Number Theory and RSA Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a clock whose numbers wrap around.

In that picture, prime numbers is the first rule you follow, modular arithmetic is the next step, and greatest common divisor and modular exponentiation keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption, and it involves details such as using small primes that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Python pow to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain public key cryptography to a friend.
