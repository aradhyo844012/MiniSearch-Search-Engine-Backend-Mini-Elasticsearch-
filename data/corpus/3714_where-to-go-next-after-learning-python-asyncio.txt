Where to Go Next After Learning Python Asyncio

You have met the basics of Python Asyncio. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Garbage Collection, which belongs to the same area as Python Asyncio (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of event loop, coroutines, await or tasks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around web servers with asyncio, and note every problem you meet, starting with blocking calls inside coroutines.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Python Asyncio to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
