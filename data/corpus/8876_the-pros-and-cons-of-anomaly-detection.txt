The Pros and Cons of Anomaly Detection

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Anomaly Detection is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports fraud detection
- Makes server monitoring easier
- Helps with manufacturing quality

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too many false alarms
- Risk: shifting baselines
- Risk: labeling normal data incorrectly

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as fraud detection, and you can manage the risks, Anomaly Detection is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
