Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Bluetooth Low Energy

This path is for a learner who has never studied Bluetooth Low Energy before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use) and learn advertising and GATT services.

Stage 2, hands-on: install nRF Connect, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving fitness trackers.

Stage 3, depth: study characteristics and pairing, then deliberately explore this problem: ignoring connection limits.

Stage 4, application: apply Bluetooth Low Energy to smart locks, compare BlueZ with Android Bluetooth APIs, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Bluetooth Low Energy to someone else, and then move on to Flutter.
