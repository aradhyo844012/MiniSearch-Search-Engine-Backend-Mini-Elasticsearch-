Entity Component Systems in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Entity Component Systems.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Entity Component Systems is a game architecture that builds objects from data components processed by systems.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn entities, components, systems, and queries.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine building characters from interchangeable parts snapped onto an ID tag. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through large simulations, games with thousands of objects, and flexible game design.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for overengineering small games and components that hold logic, then try Bevy for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep components as plain data.
