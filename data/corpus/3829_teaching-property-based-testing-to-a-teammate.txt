Teaching Property-Based Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Property-Based Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving testing parsers, naming properties and generators as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain shrinking in Property-Based Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, vague properties, and suggest this habit: save failing examples as regular tests.
