VPNs Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with VPNs.

Do:
- Learn tunneling before anything else.
- Try WireGuard on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test for DNS leaks after connecting.
- Test the case of remote work access early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming it makes you anonymous.
- Ignore this: slow routing through distant servers.
- Ignore this: leaking DNS requests.
- Adopt VPNs just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with VPNs, a little patience with encryption pays off more than any clever shortcut.
