Linear Algebra for Programmers in Real Projects

Reading about Linear Algebra for Programmers is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: 3D graphics. Here, vectors drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mixing row and column conventions.

Scenario two: machine learning. This time matrices matters most, and tools such as NumPy help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: recommendation systems. The key idea is dot products, and the biggest risk is shape mismatches.

Across all three scenarios, Linear Algebra for Programmers stays the same idea: the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning. Only the details change.
