Why Smart Contracts Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Smart Contracts make better decisions, because Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.

Three situations show why it matters. First, token systems. Second, automated escrow. Third, decentralized applications. In each case, a solid grasp of contract code and transactions separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: bugs that cannot be patched, reentrancy attacks, and high gas costs.

Good tools exist, including Solidity, Hardhat, and Remix, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
