Mistakes to Avoid with Raspberry Pi

Everyone learns Raspberry Pi partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: SD card corruption. It usually appears when someone skips GPIO pins and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: inadequate power supplies. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of home automation. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: exposing the device without hardening. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on headless setup to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
