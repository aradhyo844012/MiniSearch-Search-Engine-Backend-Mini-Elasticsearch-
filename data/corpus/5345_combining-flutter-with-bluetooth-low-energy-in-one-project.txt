Combining Flutter with Bluetooth Low Energy in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Flutter and Bluetooth Low Energy can work together in a single project.

Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase. Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

Imagine a project that involves cross-platform mobile apps and fitness trackers. Flutter brings widgets, while Bluetooth Low Energy brings advertising.

A sensible plan is to start with Dart for the Flutter part and nRF Connect for the Bluetooth Low Energy part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: poor state organization on one side, and weak pairing security on the other.

Picture the whole as a box of customizable building blocks drawn by its own engine, working alongside a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy.

Two habits help: first, split big widgets into small reusable ones; second, test with several phone models.
