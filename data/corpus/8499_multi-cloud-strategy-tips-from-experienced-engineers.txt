Multi-Cloud Strategy Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Multi-Cloud Strategy regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Terraform first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Kubernetes.
2. Write down why you chose Multi-Cloud Strategy so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of avoiding single-provider risk early, before the design hardens.
4. Be careful with this one: doubling operational complexity.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit vendor lock-in before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to meeting regional rules as well as to Multi-Cloud Strategy in general.
