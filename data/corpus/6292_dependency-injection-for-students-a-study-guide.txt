Dependency Injection for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Dependency Injection.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Dependency Injection in your own words.
2. Describe constructor injection, interfaces, containers, and inversion of control.
3. Give examples from easier unit testing and swapping implementations.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them. Then draw a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is hiding too much behind containers a problem? How does interfaces relate to containers? When would you choose Spring or FastAPI Depends?

Study habit worth keeping: inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.

When you feel ready, compare Dependency Injection with Configuration Management and write down three differences.
