Compilers and Interpreters Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Compilers and Interpreters.

Do:
- Learn lexing before anything else.
- Try GCC on a small example.
- Make it a habit to turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously.
- Test the case of running Python and JavaScript early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming interpreted means slow without measuring.
- Ignore this: ignoring compiler warnings.
- Ignore this: confusing compile-time and run-time errors.
- Adopt Compilers and Interpreters just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Compilers and Interpreters, a little patience with parsing pays off more than any clever shortcut.
