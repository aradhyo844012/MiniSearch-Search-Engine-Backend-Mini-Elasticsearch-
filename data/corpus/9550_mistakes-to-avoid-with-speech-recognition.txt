Mistakes to Avoid with Speech Recognition

Everyone learns Speech Recognition partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: accents and background noise. It usually appears when someone skips audio features and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: domain-specific vocabulary. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of voice assistants. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: privacy of recordings. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on word error rate to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
