GraphQL Glossary for Beginners

New words can make GraphQL feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

GraphQL: a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.
schema: a core term, most visible in mobile apps with limited bandwidth.
queries and mutations: a core term that beginners often confuse with resolvers.
resolvers: a core term that connects to types.
types: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Apollo: a tool commonly used for aggregating many services.

Come back to this GraphQL list whenever documentation for GraphQL Playground uses a word you do not recognize.
