Practice Exercises for Entity Component Systems

These exercises build real understanding of Entity Component Systems. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Unity DOTS and EnTT for flexible game design and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble large simulations or games with thousands of objects.
Exercise 3: Explain how systems and queries differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Bevy and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Entity Component Systems and include the term entities.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Entity Component Systems is a game architecture that builds objects from data components processed by systems.
