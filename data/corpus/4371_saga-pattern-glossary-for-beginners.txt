Saga Pattern Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Saga Pattern feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Saga Pattern: a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions.
local transactions: a core term, most visible in order processing.
compensating actions: a core term that beginners often confuse with orchestration.
orchestration: a core term that connects to choreography.
choreography: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Temporal: a tool commonly used for travel bookings.

Come back to this Saga Pattern list whenever documentation for Camunda uses a word you do not recognize.
