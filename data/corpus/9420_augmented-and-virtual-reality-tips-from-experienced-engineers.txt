Augmented and Virtual Reality Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Augmented and Virtual Reality regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip head tracking; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit rendering at high frame rates before anything else.
3. Be careful with this one: motion sickness from low frame rates.
4. Make sure you can keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
5. Test the case of training simulations early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to games as well as to Augmented and Virtual Reality in general.
