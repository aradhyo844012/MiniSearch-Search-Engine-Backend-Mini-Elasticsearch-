Myths and Facts About Overfitting

A lot of folklore has built up around Overfitting. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. Put simply, Overfitting covers when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Myth: Overfitting is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand training and validation error and model complexity.

Myth: Overfitting is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as diagnosing weak models benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: scikit-learn, early stopping, and learning curves make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Overfitting are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example evaluating only on training data.

A model is only as good as the data used to train and test it.

With steady practice, Overfitting will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
