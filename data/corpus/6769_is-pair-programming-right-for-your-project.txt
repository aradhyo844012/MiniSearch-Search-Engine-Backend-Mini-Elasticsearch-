Is Pair Programming Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Pair Programming, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Pair Programming is two developers working together at one computer on the same code.

It is a good fit when your work involves teaching new teammates or solving hard bugs, and when your team understands driver and navigator.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn rotation, or when you cannot manage these risks: one person dominating, fatigue from long sessions, and pairing on every task.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether communication and shared ownership are part of the answer. If they are, Pair Programming is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with improving code quality and swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.
