A Short History of Linear Regression

Ideas behind Linear Regression developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Put simply, Linear Regression covers a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

A solid grasp of Linear Regression also makes Overfitting easier to understand.

Simple baselines are worth building first because they show what improvement is realistic.

The vocabulary of Linear Regression is small. Learn coefficients, residuals, least squares, and assumptions, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching assumptions on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

A solid grasp of Linear Regression also makes Overfitting easier to understand.

In short, careful study of Linear Regression pays off. Spend time on coefficients and residuals, and the rest will follow.
