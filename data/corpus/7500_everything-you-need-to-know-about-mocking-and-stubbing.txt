Everything You Need to Know About Mocking and Stubbing

This page collects the essentials about Mocking and Stubbing in one place. In short, Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests.

Core concepts:
- mocks
- stubs
- spies
- fakes

Where it is used:
- isolating units
- simulating failures
- avoiding network calls

Common problems:
- over-mocking
- tests that mirror the implementation
- mocks that drift from reality

Tools to know:
- unittest.mock
- Mockito
- Sinon.js

Remember this above all: a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
