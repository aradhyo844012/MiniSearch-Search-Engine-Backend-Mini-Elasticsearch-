Debugging Data Lakes: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Data Lakes, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: turning into a data swamp, no catalog or ownership, and poor access controls.
Step 3: revisit raw storage and schema on read, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Amazon S3 or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Data Lakes in work such as storing logs and events come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
