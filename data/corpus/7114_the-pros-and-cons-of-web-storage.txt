The Pros and Cons of Web Storage

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Web Storage is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports saving user preferences
- Makes remembering form drafts easier
- Helps with caching small API results

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: storing sensitive data
- Risk: assuming storage is always available
- Risk: hitting size limits

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as saving user preferences, and you can manage the risks, Web Storage is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, wrap every read and write in error handling.
