Where to Go Next After Learning Compilers and Interpreters

You have met the basics of Compilers and Interpreters. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Instruction Pipelines, which belongs to the same area as Compilers and Interpreters (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of lexing, parsing, code generation or bytecode, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around building native programs with GCC, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming interpreted means slow without measuring.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Compilers and Interpreters to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
