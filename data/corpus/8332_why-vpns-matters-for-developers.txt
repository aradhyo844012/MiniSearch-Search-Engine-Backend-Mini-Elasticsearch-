Why VPNs Matters for Developers

Developers who understand VPNs make better decisions, because VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.

Three situations show why it matters. First, remote work access. Second, linking offices. Third, protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi. In each case, a solid grasp of tunneling and encryption separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: assuming it makes you anonymous, slow routing through distant servers, and leaking DNS requests.

Good tools exist, including WireGuard, OpenVPN, and Tailscale, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
