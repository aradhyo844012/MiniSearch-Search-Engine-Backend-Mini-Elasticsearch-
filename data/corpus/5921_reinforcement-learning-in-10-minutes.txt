Reinforcement Learning in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Reinforcement Learning.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn agent, environment, reward, and policy.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine training a dog with treats for good behavior. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through game playing, robot control, and recommendation strategies.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for badly designed rewards and slow training, then try Gymnasium for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start with simple environments.
