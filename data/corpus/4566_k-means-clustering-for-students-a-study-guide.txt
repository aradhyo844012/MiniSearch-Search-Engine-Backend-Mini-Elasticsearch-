K-Means Clustering for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover K-Means Clustering.

Learning goals:
1. Explain K-Means Clustering in your own words.
2. Describe centroids, distance measures, choosing k, and iterations.
3. Give examples from customer segmentation and image compression.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: K-Means Clustering is an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points. Then draw dividing students into study groups around the most central member on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is choosing k blindly a problem? How does distance measures relate to choosing k? When would you choose scikit-learn or NumPy?

Study habit worth keeping: scale features before clustering.

When you feel ready, compare K-Means Clustering with Transformers and write down three differences.
