Pagination Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Pagination regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Pagination so the next person understands the decision.
2. Test the case of search results early, before the design hardens.
3. Reach for SQL LIMIT and OFFSET first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as keyset queries.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit cursor pagination before anything else.
5. Be careful with this one: offsets getting slow on huge tables.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to activity feeds as well.
