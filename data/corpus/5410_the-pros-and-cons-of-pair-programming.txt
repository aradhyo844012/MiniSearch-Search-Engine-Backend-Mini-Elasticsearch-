The Pros and Cons of Pair Programming

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Pair Programming is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports teaching new teammates
- Makes solving hard bugs easier
- Helps with improving code quality

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: one person dominating
- Risk: fatigue from long sessions
- Risk: pairing on every task

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as teaching new teammates, and you can manage the risks, Pair Programming is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.
