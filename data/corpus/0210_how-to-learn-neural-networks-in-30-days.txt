How to Learn Neural Networks in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Neural Networks. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Neural Networks covers models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn layers and weights, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with PyTorch on a small task such as image recognition.
Week 3: build a mini project around speech recognition.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as too little training data, and explain Neural Networks to a friend.

In practice you can start with PyTorch, then explore TensorFlow or Keras as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Neural Networks.
