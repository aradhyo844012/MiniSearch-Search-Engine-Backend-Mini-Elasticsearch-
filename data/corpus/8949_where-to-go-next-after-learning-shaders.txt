Where to Go Next After Learning Shaders

You have met the basics of Shaders. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Procedural Generation, which belongs to the same area as Shaders (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of vertex shaders, fragment shaders, uniforms or GLSL, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around water and fire visuals with GLSL, and note every problem you meet, starting with branches that slow the GPU.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Shaders to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
