Zero Trust Security in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Zero Trust Security.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn identity verification, least privilege, micro-segmentation, and continuous checks.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a building where every door checks your badge, not just the front entrance. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through remote work access, protecting internal services, and limiting breach damage.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for treating it as a product to buy and poor identity hygiene, then try Okta for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start with strong identity and device checks.
