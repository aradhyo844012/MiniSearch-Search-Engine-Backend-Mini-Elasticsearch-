Key Terms in Overfitting Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Overfitting much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Overfitting, they usually mean when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

training and validation error: a key term in Overfitting. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
model complexity: a key term in Overfitting. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
regularization: a key term in Overfitting. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
cross-validation: a key term in Overfitting. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.

With steady practice, Overfitting will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
