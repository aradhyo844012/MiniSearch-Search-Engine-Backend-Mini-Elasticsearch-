The Pros and Cons of Data Anonymization

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Data Anonymization is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sharing datasets with researchers
- Makes testing with production-like data easier
- Helps with meeting privacy rules

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: re-identification through combined fields
- Risk: removing too little
- Risk: destroying usefulness by removing too much

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sharing datasets with researchers, and you can manage the risks, Data Anonymization is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test whether combining fields could identify a person.
