Two-Factor Authentication: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Two-Factor Authentication?
A: The term Two-Factor Authentication refers to requiring a second proof of identity in addition to a password.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Google Authenticator, YubiKey, and TOTP libraries.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Two-Factor Authentication.

Q: How does it relate to Password Hashing?
A: Two-Factor Authentication and Password Hashing often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Security.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: relying only on SMS; no recovery plan; and weak backup code storage.

Q: Why do developers use Two-Factor Authentication?
A: Common uses include protecting email accounts, securing admin panels, and reducing account takeovers.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.
