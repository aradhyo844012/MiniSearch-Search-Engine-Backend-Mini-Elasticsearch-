The Importance of ETL Pipelines in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate ETL Pipelines until something goes wrong without it. If you build software for long enough, you will run into ETL Pipelines. The term refers to processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination.

Data work is mostly preparation, and cleaning often takes longer than analysis. Pipelines should be repeatable so that results can be reproduced later.

People use ETL Pipelines for building reports, for moving data into warehouses, and for combining systems.

Three issues come up again and again: silent data loss; no retry logic; and poor error reporting. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.
