Is Object-Relational Mappers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Object-Relational Mappers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code.

It is a good fit when your work involves building CRUD apps quickly or switching databases, and when your team understands models.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn queries, or when you cannot manage these risks: N+1 queries, loading entire tables, and hiding slow SQL.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether relationships and migrations are part of the answer. If they are, Object-Relational Mappers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with keeping schema and code aligned and log the generated SQL during development.
