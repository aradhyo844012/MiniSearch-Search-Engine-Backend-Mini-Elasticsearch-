Procedural Generation Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Procedural Generation. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms.
Key terms: random seeds, noise functions, rules and constraints, and reproducibility.
Typical uses: roguelike dungeons, infinite terrain, and varied item drops.
Risks: levels that are unfair or unwinnable, repetitive results, and bugs that cannot be reproduced without seeds.
Tools: Perlin noise, Wave Function Collapse, and Unity.

Mental picture: a chef inventing endless dishes from a fixed set of ingredients.

Habit to build: log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
