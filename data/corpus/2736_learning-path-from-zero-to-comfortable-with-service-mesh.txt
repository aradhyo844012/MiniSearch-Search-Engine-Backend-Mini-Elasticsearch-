Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Service Mesh

This path is for a learner who has never studied Service Mesh before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies) and learn sidecar proxies and mutual TLS.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Istio, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving securing service traffic.

Stage 3, depth: study traffic policies and observability, then deliberately explore this problem: added latency and complexity.

Stage 4, application: apply Service Mesh to retries and timeouts, compare Linkerd with Envoy, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Service Mesh to someone else, and then move on to Disaster Recovery.
