Everything You Need to Know About Background Jobs

This page collects the essentials about Background Jobs in one place. In short, Background Jobs is tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them.

Core concepts:
- queues
- workers
- retries
- scheduling

Where it is used:
- sending emails
- resizing images
- generating reports

Common problems:
- jobs that are not idempotent
- unbounded retries
- losing jobs on restarts

Tools to know:
- Celery
- Sidekiq
- BullMQ

Remember this above all: dropping laundry at a service and collecting it later is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
