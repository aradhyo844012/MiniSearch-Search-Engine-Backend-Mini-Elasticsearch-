Leader Election Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Leader Election. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work.
Key terms: heartbeats, terms or epochs, candidates, and failover.
Typical uses: database primaries, job schedulers, and cluster managers.
Risks: split brain, flapping leaders, and slow failure detection.
Tools: ZooKeeper, etcd, and Kubernetes leases.

Mental picture: a classroom picking a monitor when the teacher leaves.

Habit to build: tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
