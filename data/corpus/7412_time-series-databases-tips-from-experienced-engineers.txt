Time-Series Databases Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Time-Series Databases regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip timestamps; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit retention policies before anything else.
3. Reach for InfluxDB first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as TimescaleDB.
4. Test the case of server metrics early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can include a time range in every query.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to sensor readings as well.
