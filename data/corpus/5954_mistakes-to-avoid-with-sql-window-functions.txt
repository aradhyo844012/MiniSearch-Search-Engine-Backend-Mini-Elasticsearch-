Mistakes to Avoid with SQL Window Functions

Everyone learns SQL Window Functions partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: forgetting ORDER BY inside the window. It usually appears when someone skips the OVER clause and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: confusing them with GROUP BY. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of running totals. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: slow sorts on huge partitions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on frames to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with ROW_NUMBER and LAG on a small table.
