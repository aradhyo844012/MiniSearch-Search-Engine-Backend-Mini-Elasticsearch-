End-to-End Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover End-to-End Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain End-to-End Testing in your own words.
2. Describe browser automation, user flows, test data, and selectors.
3. Give examples from checking login and checkout flows and release verification.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: End-to-End Testing is tests that drive an application through a real user journey from start to finish. Then draw a test drive of the whole car on a real road on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is flaky tests a problem? How does user flows relate to test data? When would you choose Playwright or Cypress?

Study habit worth keeping: cover only the most important user journeys.

When you feel ready, compare End-to-End Testing with Chaos Engineering and write down three differences.
