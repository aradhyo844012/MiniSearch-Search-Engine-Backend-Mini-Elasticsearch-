Where to Go Next After Learning Floating-Point Arithmetic

You have met the basics of Floating-Point Arithmetic. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Number Theory and RSA, which belongs to the same area as Floating-Point Arithmetic (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of mantissa and exponent, rounding, precision or special values, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around graphics with Python decimal, and note every problem you meet, starting with comparing floats with equality.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Floating-Point Arithmetic to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
