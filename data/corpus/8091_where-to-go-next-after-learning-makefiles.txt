Where to Go Next After Learning Makefiles

You have met the basics of Makefiles. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Dev Containers, which belongs to the same area as Makefiles (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of targets, prerequisites, recipes or phony targets, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around shortcuts for common commands with GNU Make, and note every problem you meet, starting with tabs versus spaces.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Makefiles to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
