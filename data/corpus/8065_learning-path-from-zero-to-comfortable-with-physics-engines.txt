Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Physics Engines

This path is for a learner who has never studied Physics Engines before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects) and learn rigid bodies and forces and impulses.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Box2D, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving vehicles and ragdolls.

Stage 3, depth: study constraints and fixed time steps, then deliberately explore this problem: variable time steps.

Stage 4, application: apply Physics Engines to puzzle games, compare Bullet with PhysX, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Physics Engines to someone else, and then move on to Game AI Behavior Trees.
