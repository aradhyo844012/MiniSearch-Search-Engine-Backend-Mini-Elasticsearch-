Vector Databases in Real Projects

Reading about Vector Databases is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: semantic search. Here, embeddings drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mixing embedding models.

Scenario two: recommendations. This time approximate nearest neighbors matters most, and tools such as FAISS help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: retrieval for AI assistants. The key idea is distance metrics, and the biggest risk is ignoring recall trade-offs.

Across all three scenarios, Vector Databases stays the same idea: databases built to store embeddings and find the most similar ones quickly. Only the details change.
