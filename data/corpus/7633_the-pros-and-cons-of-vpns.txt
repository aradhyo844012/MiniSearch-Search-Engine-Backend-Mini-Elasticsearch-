The Pros and Cons of VPNs

No technology is free of trade-offs, and VPNs is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports remote work access
- Makes linking offices easier
- Helps with protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming it makes you anonymous
- Risk: slow routing through distant servers
- Risk: leaking DNS requests

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as remote work access, and you can manage the risks, VPNs is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test for DNS leaks after connecting.
