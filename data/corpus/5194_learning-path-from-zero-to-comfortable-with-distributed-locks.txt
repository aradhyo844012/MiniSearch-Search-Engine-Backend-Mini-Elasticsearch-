Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Distributed Locks

This path is for a learner who has never studied Distributed Locks before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Distributed Locks is mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines) and learn lease times and fencing tokens.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Redis, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving running scheduled jobs once.

Stage 3, depth: study lock owners and timeouts, then deliberately explore this problem: locks that never expire.

Stage 4, application: apply Distributed Locks to protecting shared resources, compare ZooKeeper with etcd, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Distributed Locks to someone else, and then move on to Idempotency.
