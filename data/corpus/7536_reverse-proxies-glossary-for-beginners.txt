Reverse Proxies Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Reverse Proxies feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Reverse Proxies: servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.
upstream servers: a core term, most visible in hiding backend servers.
proxy headers: a core term that beginners often confuse with TLS termination.
TLS termination: a core term that connects to caching.
caching: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
NGINX: a tool commonly used for terminating HTTPS.

Come back to this Reverse Proxies list whenever documentation for Caddy uses a word you do not recognize.
