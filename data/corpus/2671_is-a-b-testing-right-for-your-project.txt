Is A/B Testing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting A/B Testing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.

It is a good fit when your work involves testing page designs or comparing ranking algorithms, and when your team understands control and variant.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn random assignment, or when you cannot manage these risks: stopping tests early, testing too many variants, and ignoring sample size.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether metrics and sample size are part of the answer. If they are, A/B Testing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with evaluating new features and decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
