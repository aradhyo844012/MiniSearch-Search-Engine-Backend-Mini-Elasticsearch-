MongoDB Aggregation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through MongoDB Aggregation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn pipeline stages, $match and $group, projection, and indexes.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine an assembly line where each station filters or reshapes the parts. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through summarizing orders, building analytics queries, and reshaping documents.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for putting $match late and huge intermediate results, then try MongoDB Compass for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to filter with $match as early as possible.
