Understanding Multi-Cloud Strategy Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one.

In that picture, portability is the first rule you follow, vendor lock-in is the next step, and abstraction layers and cost comparison keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose, and it involves details such as doubling operational complexity that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Terraform to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain avoiding single-provider risk to a friend.
