Everything You Need to Know About Fine-Tuning Models

This page collects the essentials about Fine-Tuning Models in one place. In short, Fine-Tuning Models is adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data.

Core concepts:
- pretrained weights
- training data
- the learning rate
- evaluation

Where it is used:
- domain-specific language tasks
- style adaptation
- improving accuracy on narrow problems

Common problems:
- overfitting on small datasets
- forgetting general abilities
- low-quality training data

Tools to know:
- Hugging Face
- PyTorch
- LoRA libraries

Remember this above all: teaching an experienced chef a new regional cuisine is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
