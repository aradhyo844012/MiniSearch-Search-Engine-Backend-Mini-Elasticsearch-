Why Regions and Availability Zones Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Regions and Availability Zones make better decisions, because Regions and Availability Zones is the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources.

Three situations show why it matters. First, placing services near users. Second, surviving data center failures. Third, meeting data laws. In each case, a solid grasp of regions and availability zones separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: putting everything in one zone, cross-region transfer fees, and forgetting data residency rules.

Good tools exist, including AWS Regions, Azure regions, and Google Cloud regions, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
