Assembly Language Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Assembly Language Basics.

Do:
- Learn registers before anything else.
- Try GDB on a small example.
- Make it a habit to read the assembly of a tiny C function to see what the compiler does.
- Test the case of understanding compiled code early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: wrong calling conventions.
- Ignore this: stack corruption.
- Ignore this: platform differences.
- Adopt Assembly Language Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Assembly Language Basics, a little patience with instructions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
