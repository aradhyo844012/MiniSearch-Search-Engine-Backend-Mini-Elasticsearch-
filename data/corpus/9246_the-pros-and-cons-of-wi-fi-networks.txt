The Pros and Cons of Wi-Fi Networks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Wi-Fi Networks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports home internet
- Makes office coverage easier
- Helps with public hotspots

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overlapping channels
- Risk: weak passwords
- Risk: poor access point placement

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as home internet, and you can manage the risks, Wi-Fi Networks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
