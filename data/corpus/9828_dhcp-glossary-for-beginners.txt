DHCP Glossary for Beginners

New words can make DHCP feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

DHCP: a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings.
leases: a core term, most visible in home and office networks.
address pools: a core term that beginners often confuse with reservations.
reservations: a core term that connects to discover and offer messages.
discover and offer messages: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
dnsmasq: a tool commonly used for guest Wi-Fi.

Come back to this DHCP list whenever documentation for ISC Kea uses a word you do not recognize.
