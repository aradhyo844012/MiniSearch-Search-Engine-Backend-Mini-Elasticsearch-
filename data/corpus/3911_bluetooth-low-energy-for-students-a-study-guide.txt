Bluetooth Low Energy for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Bluetooth Low Energy.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Bluetooth Low Energy in your own words.
2. Describe advertising, GATT services, characteristics, and pairing.
3. Give examples from fitness trackers and smart locks.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use. Then draw a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring connection limits a problem? How does GATT services relate to characteristics? When would you choose nRF Connect or BlueZ?

Study habit worth keeping: test with several phone models.

When you feel ready, compare Bluetooth Low Energy with MQTT and write down three differences.
