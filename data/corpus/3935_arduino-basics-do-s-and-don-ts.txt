Arduino Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Arduino Basics.

Do:
- Learn sketches before anything else.
- Try Arduino IDE on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test each sensor on its own before combining them.
- Test the case of reading sensors early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: wiring errors.
- Ignore this: blocking delays in the loop.
- Ignore this: insufficient power for motors.
- Adopt Arduino Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Arduino Basics, a little patience with digital and analog pins pays off more than any clever shortcut.
