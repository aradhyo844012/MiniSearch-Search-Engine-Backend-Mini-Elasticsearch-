Managed Kubernetes Services Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Managed Kubernetes Services regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Managed Kubernetes Services so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: neglecting version upgrades.
3. Test the case of running containerized apps early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit node pools before anything else.
5. Reach for Amazon EKS first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Google GKE.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to scaling services as well as to Managed Kubernetes Services in general.
