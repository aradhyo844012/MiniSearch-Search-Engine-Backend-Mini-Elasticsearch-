WebSockets: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is WebSockets?
A: The term WebSockets refers to a protocol that keeps a two-way connection open between a browser and a server.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: forgetting reconnection logic; unbounded message queues; and proxies closing idle connections.

Q: Why do developers use WebSockets?
A: Common uses include chat applications, live dashboards, and collaborative editing.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Socket.IO, ws library, and Python websockets.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with persistent connections and messages, then move on to heartbeats and reconnection.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with WebSockets.
