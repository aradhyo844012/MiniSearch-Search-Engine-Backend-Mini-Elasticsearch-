Teaching Convolutional Neural Networks to a Teammate

Explaining Convolutional Neural Networks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving image classification, naming filters and pooling as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain feature maps in Convolutional Neural Networks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, too little training data, and suggest this habit: start from a pretrained model.
