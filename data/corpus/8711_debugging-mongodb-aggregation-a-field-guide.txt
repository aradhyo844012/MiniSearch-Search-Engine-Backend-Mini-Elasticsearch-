Debugging MongoDB Aggregation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around MongoDB Aggregation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: putting $match late, huge intermediate results, and hitting memory limits.
Step 3: revisit pipeline stages and $match and $group, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use MongoDB Compass or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving MongoDB Aggregation in work such as summarizing orders come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, filter with $match as early as possible. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
