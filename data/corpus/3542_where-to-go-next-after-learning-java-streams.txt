Where to Go Next After Learning Java Streams

You have met the basics of Java Streams. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Python Asyncio, which belongs to the same area as Java Streams (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of map, filter, reduce or lazy evaluation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around aggregating records with OpenJDK, and note every problem you meet, starting with reusing a consumed stream.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Java Streams to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
