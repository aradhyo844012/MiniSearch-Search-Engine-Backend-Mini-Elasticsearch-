Everything You Need to Know About Competitive Programming

This page collects the essentials about Competitive Programming in one place. In short, Competitive Programming is solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills.

Core concepts:
- problem patterns
- time limits
- contests
- upsolving

Where it is used:
- interview preparation
- learning algorithms
- practicing under pressure

Common problems:
- memorizing solutions without understanding
- skipping fundamentals
- burning out

Tools to know:
- Codeforces
- LeetCode
- AtCoder

Remember this above all: athletic training for problem solving is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
