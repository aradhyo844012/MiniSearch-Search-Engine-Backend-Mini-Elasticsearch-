Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Statistical Significance

This path is for a learner who has never studied Statistical Significance before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect) and learn p-values and the null hypothesis.

Stage 2, hands-on: install SciPy, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving judging experiment results.

Stage 3, depth: study confidence intervals and effect size, then deliberately explore this problem: mistaking significance for importance.

Stage 4, application: apply Statistical Significance to comparing model performance, compare statsmodels with R, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Statistical Significance to someone else, and then move on to Data Anonymization.
