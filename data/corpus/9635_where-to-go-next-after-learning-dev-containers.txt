Where to Go Next After Learning Dev Containers

You have met the basics of Dev Containers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Python Virtual Environments, which belongs to the same area as Dev Containers (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of devcontainer.json, images, mounted folders or extensions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around matching production tools with VS Code Dev Containers, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow image builds.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Dev Containers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
