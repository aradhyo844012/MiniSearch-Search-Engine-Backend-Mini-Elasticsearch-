Skills to Build Around Single Sign-On

Knowing the definition of Single Sign-On is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with identity provider and SAML, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Okta on a Single Sign-On task, and try at least one alternative such as Keycloak.

Judgment: learn to decide when Single Sign-On fits a problem such as company intranets, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a festival wristband that opens every gate.

Habits: plan for identity provider outages, and write down what you learn after each project.
