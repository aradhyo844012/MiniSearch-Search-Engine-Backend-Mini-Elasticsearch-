A/B Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover A/B Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain A/B Testing in your own words.
2. Describe control and variant, random assignment, metrics, and sample size.
3. Give examples from testing page designs and comparing ranking algorithms.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better. Then draw taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is stopping tests early a problem? How does random assignment relate to metrics? When would you choose Optimizely or GrowthBook?

Study habit: decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.

When you feel ready, compare A/B Testing with Star Schema Modeling and write down three differences.
