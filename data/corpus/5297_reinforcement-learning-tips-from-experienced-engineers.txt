Reinforcement Learning Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Reinforcement Learning regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Gymnasium first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Stable-Baselines3.
2. Be careful with this one: badly designed rewards.
3. Write down why you chose Reinforcement Learning so the next person understands the decision.
4. Make sure you can start with simple environments.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit environment before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to robot control as well.
