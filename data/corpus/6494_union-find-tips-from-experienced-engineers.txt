Union-Find Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Union-Find regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of detecting connected components early, before the design hardens.
2. Reach for Python lists first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as scipy.sparse.csgraph.
3. Be careful with this one: forgetting path compression.
4. Write down why you chose Union-Find so the next person understands the decision.
5. Do not skip parent pointers; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to building minimum spanning trees as well.
