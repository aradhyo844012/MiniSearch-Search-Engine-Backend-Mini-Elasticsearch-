Scaling Considerations for Python Decorators

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Python Decorators changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding higher-order functions and getting logging calls right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for losing function metadata and stacking decorators in the wrong order, and measure with functools before changing anything.

Large scale: functools.wraps and decorator arguments move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around caching results at this stage.

Before you scale, always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
