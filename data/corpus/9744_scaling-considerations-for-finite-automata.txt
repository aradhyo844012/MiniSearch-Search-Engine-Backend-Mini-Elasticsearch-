Scaling Considerations for Finite Automata

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Finite Automata changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding states and getting tokenizers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for missing transitions and confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines, and measure with JFLAP before changing anything.

Large scale: accepting states and regular languages move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around pattern matching at this stage.

Before you scale, draw the state diagram before writing any code. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
