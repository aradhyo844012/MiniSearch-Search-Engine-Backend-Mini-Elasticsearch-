Single Sign-On for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Single Sign-On.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Single Sign-On in your own words.
2. Describe identity provider, SAML, OpenID Connect, and session lifetime.
3. Give examples from company intranets and reducing password fatigue.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications. Then draw a festival wristband that opens every gate on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is a single point of failure a problem? How does SAML relate to OpenID Connect? When would you choose Okta or Keycloak?

Study habit worth keeping: plan for identity provider outages.

When you feel ready, compare Single Sign-On with Security Headers and write down three differences.
