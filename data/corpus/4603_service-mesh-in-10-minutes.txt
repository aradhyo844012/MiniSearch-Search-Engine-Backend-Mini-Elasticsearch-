Service Mesh in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Service Mesh.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sidecar proxies, mutual TLS, traffic policies, and observability.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through securing service traffic, retries and timeouts, and fine-grained routing.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for added latency and complexity and adopting it too early, then try Istio for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
