Retrieval-Augmented Generation Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a technique that gives a language model relevant documents to read before it answers.
Key terms: retrieval, chunking, prompts with context, and grounding.
Typical uses: company knowledge assistants, question answering over documents, and support bots.
Risks: poor chunking, irrelevant retrieved text, and no evaluation of the answers.
Tools: LangChain, FAISS, and Elasticsearch.

Mental picture: an open-book exam where the student first looks up the right pages.

Habit to build: evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality.
