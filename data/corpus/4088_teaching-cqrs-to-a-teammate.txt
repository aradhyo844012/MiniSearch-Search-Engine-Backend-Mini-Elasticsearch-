Teaching CQRS to a Teammate

Explaining CQRS to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving high-read dashboards, naming commands and queries as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain read models in CQRS back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, adding it to simple apps, and suggest this habit: start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
