Cloud Cost Optimization in Real Projects

Reading about Cloud Cost Optimization is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: cutting idle resources. Here, right-sizing drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against forgotten test environments.

Scenario two: budgeting by team. This time reserved capacity matters most, and tools such as AWS Cost Explorer help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: choosing storage tiers. The key idea is tagging, and the biggest risk is over-provisioned databases.

Across all three scenarios, Cloud Cost Optimization stays the same idea: the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability. Only the details change.
