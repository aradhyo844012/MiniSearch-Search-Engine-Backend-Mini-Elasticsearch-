Choosing Tools for Wearable Technology

There is no single best tool for Wearable Technology. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Wear OS is a common first choice, especially for fitness tracking. watchOS suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Bluetooth Low Energy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Wearable Technology by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle notifications well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Wearable Technology, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: battery drain.

As a rule of thumb, design interactions that take only a few seconds, no matter which tool you use.
