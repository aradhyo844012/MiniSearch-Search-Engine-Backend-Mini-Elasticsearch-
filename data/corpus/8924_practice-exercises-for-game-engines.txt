Practice Exercises for Game Engines

These exercises build real understanding of Game Engines. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Game Engines and include the term scenes.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble indie games or simulations.
Exercise 3: Compare Godot and Unreal Engine for interactive demos and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Explain how scripting and build pipelines differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick Unity and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Game Engines is software frameworks that provide graphics, physics, input and tools for building games.
