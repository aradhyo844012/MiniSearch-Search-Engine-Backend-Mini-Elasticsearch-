Tokenization Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Tokenization.

- Definition: splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare.
- Core ideas: lowercasing; splitting on punctuation; stop words; normalization.
- Use it for: indexing documents; preparing text for models; analyzing queries.
- Watch out for: different rules for indexing and querying; dropping useful numbers; breaking on apostrophes badly.
- Tools: Python re module, NLTK, spaCy.
- Related topic: TF-IDF.
- Quick tip: practice lowercasing until it feels automatic.
