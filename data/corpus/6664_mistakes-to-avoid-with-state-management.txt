Mistakes to Avoid with State Management

Everyone learns State Management partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: putting everything in global state. It usually appears when someone skips single source of truth and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: duplicated state that drifts apart. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of large single-page apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unclear update paths. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on derived state to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
