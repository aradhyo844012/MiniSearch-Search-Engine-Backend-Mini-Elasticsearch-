Inside Tokenization: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Tokenization, step by step. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Tokenization. The term refers to splitting text into smaller units such as words that a program can count and compare.

To understand Tokenization, start with lowercasing. Then move on to splitting on punctuation, and finally look at stop words and normalization. A good way to remember lowercasing is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

First, lowercasing sets the stage for everything else. Next, splitting on punctuation builds on it. After that, stop words comes into play, and finally normalization ties the pieces together. Seeing the sequence once, with a tiny example, makes the whole topic easier to remember.
