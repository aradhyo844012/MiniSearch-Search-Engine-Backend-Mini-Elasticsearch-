Teaching Mobile App Security to a Teammate

Explaining Mobile App Security to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: locking an app's valuables in a hotel safe. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving banking apps, naming secure storage and certificate pinning as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain obfuscation in Mobile App Security back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, storing tokens in plain text, and suggest this habit: store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
