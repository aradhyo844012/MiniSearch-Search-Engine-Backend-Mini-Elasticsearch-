Convolutional Neural Networks Explained Simply

Here is Convolutional Neural Networks without the jargon: it is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.

Think of Convolutional Neural Networks as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: filters, pooling, feature maps, and stride. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with filters, then add pooling.

You will meet Convolutional Neural Networks in work such as image classification, medical image analysis, and object detection.

One practical tip is to start from a pretrained model.

Once Convolutional Neural Networks feels comfortable, read about Retrieval-Augmented Generation, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
