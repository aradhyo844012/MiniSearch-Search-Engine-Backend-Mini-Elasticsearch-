Shaders Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Shaders.

Do:
- Learn vertex shaders before anything else.
- Try GLSL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
- Test the case of lighting effects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: branches that slow the GPU.
- Ignore this: precision errors.
- Ignore this: platform differences.
- Adopt Shaders just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Shaders, a little patience with fragment shaders pays off more than any clever shortcut.
