Skills to Build Around Bluetooth Low Energy

Knowing the definition of Bluetooth Low Energy is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with advertising and GATT services, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with nRF Connect on a Bluetooth Low Energy task, and try at least one alternative such as BlueZ.

Judgment: learn to decide when Bluetooth Low Energy fits a problem such as fitness trackers, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy.

Habits: test with several phone models, and write down what you learn after each project.
