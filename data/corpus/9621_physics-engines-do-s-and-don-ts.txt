Physics Engines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Physics Engines.

Do:
- Learn rigid bodies before anything else.
- Try Box2D on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use a fixed time step for stable results.
- Test the case of vehicles and ragdolls early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: variable time steps.
- Ignore this: unstable stacks of objects.
- Ignore this: extreme mass ratios.
- Adopt Physics Engines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Physics Engines, a little patience with forces and impulses pays off more than any clever shortcut.
