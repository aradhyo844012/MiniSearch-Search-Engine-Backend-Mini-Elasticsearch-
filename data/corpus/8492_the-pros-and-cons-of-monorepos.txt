The Pros and Cons of Monorepos

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Monorepos is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sharing libraries across apps
- Makes coordinated refactors easier
- Helps with consistent tooling

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow checkouts
- Risk: unclear ownership
- Risk: CI that rebuilds everything

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sharing libraries across apps, and you can manage the risks, Monorepos is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, set up selective builds and tests early.
