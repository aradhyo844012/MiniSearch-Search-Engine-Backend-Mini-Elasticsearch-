Technical Writing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Technical Writing.

Do:
- Learn audience before anything else.
- Try Markdown on a small example.
- Make it a habit to state the main point in the first two sentences.
- Test the case of documentation early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming too much knowledge.
- Ignore this: burying the main point.
- Ignore this: long unbroken paragraphs.
- Adopt Technical Writing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Technical Writing, a little patience with structure pays off more than any clever shortcut.
