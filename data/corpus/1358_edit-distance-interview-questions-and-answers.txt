Edit Distance Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Edit Distance.

Q: Explain Edit Distance in simple words.
A: Edit Distance refers to a measure of how many single-character changes turn one word into another. I would add an example involving spell checking.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as insertions, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: comparing against every word in a large vocabulary.

Q: How does it connect to TF-IDF?
A: Both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: comparing against every word in a large vocabulary; ignoring word length; and forgetting transpositions.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with fuzzy matching, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
