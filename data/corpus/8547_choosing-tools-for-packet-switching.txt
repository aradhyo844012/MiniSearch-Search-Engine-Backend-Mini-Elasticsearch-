Choosing Tools for Packet Switching

There is no single best tool for Packet Switching. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Wireshark is a common first choice, especially for internet traffic. tcpdump suits teams that already work with similar technology, and iperf is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Packet Switching by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle voice over IP well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Packet Switching, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: packet loss.

As a rule of thumb, capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself, no matter which tool you use.
