A Short History of Phishing Defense

Ideas behind Phishing Defense developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Many students first meet Phishing Defense in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to recognizing and reducing attacks that trick people into revealing information.

Security is a habit applied throughout development, not a feature added at the end. Never trust input that comes from outside your own code.

The vocabulary of Phishing Defense is small. Learn suspicious links, sender verification, email filtering, and user training, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching user training on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

A solid grasp of Phishing Defense also makes Secure Coding easier to understand.

With steady practice, Phishing Defense will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
