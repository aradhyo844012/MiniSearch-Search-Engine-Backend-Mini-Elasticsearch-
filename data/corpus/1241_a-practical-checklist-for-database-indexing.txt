A Practical Checklist for Database Indexing

Use this checklist before you rely on Database Indexing in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Database Indexing should accomplish.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example speeding up lookups.
3. Measure results before and after any change.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on selectivity.
5. Write at least one automated test that covers B-tree indexes.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Review how Database Indexing interacts with Database Replication.
8. Guard against this risk: indexing every column.
