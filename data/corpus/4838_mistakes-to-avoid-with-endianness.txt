Mistakes to Avoid with Endianness

Everyone learns Endianness partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing byte orders. It usually appears when someone skips big-endian and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: assuming all machines are alike. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of network protocols. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting to convert before sending. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on network byte order to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: always specify byte order in file and protocol formats.
