Is Color Spaces Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Color Spaces, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.

It is a good fit when your work involves web design or photo editing, and when your team understands RGB.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn HSL, or when you cannot manage these risks: ignoring gamma, mixing color spaces, and poor contrast choices.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether CMYK and gamma are part of the answer. If they are, Color Spaces is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with printing and work in one color space and convert only at the end.
