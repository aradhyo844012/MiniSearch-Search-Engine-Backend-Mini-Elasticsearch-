Debugging Recommendation Systems: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Recommendation Systems, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: feedback loops, popularity bias, and ignoring new users.
Step 3: revisit collaborative filtering and content-based filtering, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Surprise or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Recommendation Systems in work such as streaming services come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
