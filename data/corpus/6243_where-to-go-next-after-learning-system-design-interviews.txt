Where to Go Next After Learning System Design Interviews

You have met the basics of System Design Interviews. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Debugging Strategies, which belongs to the same area as System Design Interviews (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of requirements, scaling estimates, trade-offs or components, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around practicing architectural thinking with whiteboard sketches, and note every problem you meet, starting with jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining System Design Interviews to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
