Video Codecs Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Video Codecs.

Do:
- Learn frames before anything else.
- Try FFmpeg on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test playback on the weakest target device.
- Test the case of streaming services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mismatched codec support.
- Ignore this: too low a bitrate.
- Ignore this: ignoring hardware decoding.
- Adopt Video Codecs just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Video Codecs, a little patience with motion compensation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
