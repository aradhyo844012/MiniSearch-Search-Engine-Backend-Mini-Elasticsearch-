CDNs Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about CDNs.

Q: Explain CDNs in simple words.
A: CDNs refers to networks of servers around the world that deliver static content from a location close to the user. I would add an example involving serving images and scripts.

Q: How does it connect to TLS Handshake?
A: Both belong to the area of Networking, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Cloudflare, Akamai, and Amazon CloudFront.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: caching private data.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as edge servers, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: edge servers, cache rules, origin servers, and cache purging.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with speeding up global sites, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
