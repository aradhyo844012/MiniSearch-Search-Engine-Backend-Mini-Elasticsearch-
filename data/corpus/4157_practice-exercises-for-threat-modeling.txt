Practice Exercises for Threat Modeling

These exercises build real understanding of Threat Modeling. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare OWASP Threat Dragon and data flow diagrams for prioritizing security work and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Pick STRIDE and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Explain how trust boundaries and mitigations differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: doing it once and never again. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble designing new features or reviewing architecture.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Threat Modeling is a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it.
