Teaching Single Sign-On to a Teammate

Explaining Single Sign-On to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a festival wristband that opens every gate. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving company intranets, naming identity provider and SAML as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain OpenID Connect in Single Sign-On back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, a single point of failure, and suggest this habit: plan for identity provider outages.
