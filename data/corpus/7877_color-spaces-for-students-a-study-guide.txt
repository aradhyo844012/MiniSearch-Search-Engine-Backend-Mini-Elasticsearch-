Color Spaces for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Color Spaces.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Color Spaces in your own words.
2. Describe RGB, HSL, CMYK, and gamma.
3. Give examples from web design and photo editing.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK. Then draw different ways of giving directions to the same destination on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring gamma a problem? How does HSL relate to CMYK? When would you choose browser color pickers or ImageMagick?

Study habit: work in one color space and convert only at the end.

When you feel ready, compare Color Spaces with Ray Tracing and write down three differences.
