Mistakes to Avoid with Assembly Language Basics

Everyone learns Assembly Language Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: wrong calling conventions. It usually appears when someone skips registers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: stack corruption. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of understanding compiled code. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: platform differences. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the stack to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: read the assembly of a tiny C function to see what the compiler does.
