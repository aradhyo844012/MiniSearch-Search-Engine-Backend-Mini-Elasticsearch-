Practice Exercises for Rasterization

These exercises build real understanding of Rasterization. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Vulkan and WebGL for CAD previews and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: aliasing along edges. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Rasterization and include the term triangles.
Exercise 4: Pick OpenGL and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble real-time games or user interfaces.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Rasterization is converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.
