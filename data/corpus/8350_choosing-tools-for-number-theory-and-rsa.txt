Choosing Tools for Number Theory and RSA

There is no single best tool for Number Theory and RSA. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python pow is a common first choice, especially for public key cryptography. OpenSSL suits teams that already work with similar technology, and SageMath is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Number Theory and RSA by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle hash functions well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Number Theory and RSA, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: using small primes.

As a rule of thumb, use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own, no matter which tool you use.
