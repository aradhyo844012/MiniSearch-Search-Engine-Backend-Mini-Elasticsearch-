Where to Go Next After Learning Consistent Hashing

You have met the basics of Consistent Hashing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Vector Clocks, which belongs to the same area as Consistent Hashing (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of hash ring, virtual nodes, key placement or rebalancing, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around sharded databases with Redis Cluster, and note every problem you meet, starting with too few virtual nodes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Consistent Hashing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
