Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Random Forests

This path is for a learner who has never studied Random Forests before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Random Forests is ensembles of many decision trees whose predictions are combined) and learn bagging and feature randomness.

Stage 2, hands-on: install scikit-learn, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving tabular predictions.

Stage 3, depth: study voting and out-of-bag error, then deliberately explore this problem: treating importance scores as causes.

Stage 4, application: apply Random Forests to feature importance ranking, compare XGBoost with LightGBM, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Random Forests to someone else, and then move on to Hyperparameter Tuning.
