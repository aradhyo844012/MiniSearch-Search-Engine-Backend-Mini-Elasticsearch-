Choosing Tools for gRPC

There is no single best tool for gRPC. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

protoc is a common first choice, especially for service-to-service calls. grpcurl suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Envoy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle low-latency APIs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: breaking changes in message fields.

As a rule of thumb, never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file, no matter which tool you use.
