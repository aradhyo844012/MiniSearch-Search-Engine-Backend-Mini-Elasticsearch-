Why Shaders Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Shaders make better decisions, because Shaders is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.

Three situations show why it matters. First, lighting effects. Second, water and fire visuals. Third, post-processing. In each case, a solid grasp of vertex shaders and fragment shaders separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: branches that slow the GPU, precision errors, and platform differences.

You will not need to build everything yourself; GLSL, Shadertoy, and RenderDoc cover most needs.
