Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with 3D Transformations

This path is for a learner who has never studied 3D Transformations before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space) and learn translation and rotation.

Stage 2, hands-on: install GLM, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving games.

Stage 3, depth: study scaling and matrices, then deliberately explore this problem: wrong multiplication order.

Stage 4, application: apply 3D Transformations to modeling tools, compare three.js with Blender, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach 3D Transformations to someone else, and then move on to Rasterization.
