Practice Exercises for Discrete Mathematics

These exercises build real understanding of Discrete Mathematics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Discrete Mathematics and include the term sets.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble algorithm analysis or database theory.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: skipping proofs. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Compare proof assistants and textbooks and problem sets for cryptography and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Pick Python sets and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.
