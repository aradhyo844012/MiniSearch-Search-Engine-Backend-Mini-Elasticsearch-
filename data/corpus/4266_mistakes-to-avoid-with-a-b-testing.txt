Mistakes to Avoid with A/B Testing

Everyone learns A/B Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: stopping tests early. It usually appears when someone skips control and variant and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: testing too many variants. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of testing page designs. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring sample size. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on sample size to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
