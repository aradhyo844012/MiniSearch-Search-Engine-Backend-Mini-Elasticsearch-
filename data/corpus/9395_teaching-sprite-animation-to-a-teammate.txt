Teaching Sprite Animation to a Teammate

Explaining Sprite Animation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a flip book drawn one page at a time. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Sprite Animation is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving 2D platformers, naming sprite sheets and frames as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain frame rate in Sprite Animation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, inconsistent frame timing, and suggest this habit: pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
