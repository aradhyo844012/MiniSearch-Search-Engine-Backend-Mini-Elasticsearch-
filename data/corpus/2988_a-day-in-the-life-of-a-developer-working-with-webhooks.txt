A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Webhooks

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Webhooks.

Morning: Arjun starts by reviewing a ticket about payment notifications. Before touching any code, Arjun re-reads the notes on event payloads to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Arjun pairs with a teammate to explore signature verification with ngrok. A small test exposes the problem of not verifying signatures, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving CI triggers. Arjun sketches a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Arjun writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to respond quickly and process the event in a background job.

Days like this are typical. Webhooks is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
