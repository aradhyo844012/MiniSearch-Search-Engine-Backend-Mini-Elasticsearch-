Mistakes to Avoid with Wi-Fi Networks

Everyone learns Wi-Fi Networks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overlapping channels. It usually appears when someone skips SSIDs and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: weak passwords. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of home internet. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor access point placement. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on signal strength to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
