Inside Zipf's Law: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Zipf's Law, step by step. The term Zipf's Law refers to the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

The vocabulary of Zipf's Law is small. Learn rank and frequency, power laws, log-log plots, and long tails, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching long tails on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.
