GraphQL in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through GraphQL.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn schema, queries and mutations, resolvers, and types.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through mobile apps with limited bandwidth, aggregating many services, and flexible dashboards.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for the N+1 query problem and unbounded query depth, then try Apollo for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
