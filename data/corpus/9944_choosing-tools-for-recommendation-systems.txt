Choosing Tools for Recommendation Systems

There is no single best tool for Recommendation Systems. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Surprise is a common first choice, especially for streaming services. implicit suits teams that already work with similar technology, and TensorFlow Recommenders is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Recommendation Systems by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle online shops well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Recommendation Systems, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: feedback loops.

As a rule of thumb, evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data, no matter which tool you use.
