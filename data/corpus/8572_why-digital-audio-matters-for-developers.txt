Why Digital Audio Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Digital Audio make better decisions, because Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

Three situations show why it matters. First, music production. Second, voice calls. Third, podcasts. In each case, a solid grasp of sampling rate and bit depth separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: clipping from too high a level, aliasing at low sample rates, and mixing sample rates.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Audacity, SoX, and the Web Audio API cover most needs.
