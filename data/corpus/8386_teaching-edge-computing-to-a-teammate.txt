Teaching Edge Computing to a Teammate

Explaining Edge Computing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a local branch office that handles tasks without calling headquarters. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Edge Computing is processing data close to where it is produced instead of in a distant data center. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving factory sensors, naming edge nodes and latency as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain bandwidth savings in Edge Computing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, hard-to-update devices, and suggest this habit: decide which data must stay local before designing the system.
