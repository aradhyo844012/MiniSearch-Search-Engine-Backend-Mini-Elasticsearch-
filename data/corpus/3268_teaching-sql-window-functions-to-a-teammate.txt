Teaching SQL Window Functions to a Teammate

Explaining SQL Window Functions to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: looking at the neighbors in a line without leaving your place. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: SQL Window Functions is SQL features that calculate values across related rows without collapsing them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving running totals, naming the OVER clause and partitions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain ordering in SQL Window Functions back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, forgetting ORDER BY inside the window, and suggest this habit: start with ROW_NUMBER and LAG on a small table.
