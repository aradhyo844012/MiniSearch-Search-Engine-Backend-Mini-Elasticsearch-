Where to Go Next After Learning Union-Find

You have met the basics of Union-Find. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Topological Sorting, which belongs to the same area as Union-Find (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of parent pointers, union operation, find operation or path compression, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around building minimum spanning trees with Python lists, and note every problem you meet, starting with forgetting path compression.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Union-Find to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
