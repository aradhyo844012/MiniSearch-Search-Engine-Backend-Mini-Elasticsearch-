Regions and Availability Zones Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Regions and Availability Zones feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Regions and Availability Zones: the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources.
regions: a core term, most visible in placing services near users.
availability zones: a core term that beginners often confuse with latency.
latency: a core term that connects to data residency.
data residency: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
AWS Regions: a tool commonly used for surviving data center failures.
