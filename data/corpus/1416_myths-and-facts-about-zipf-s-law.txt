Myths and Facts About Zipf's Law

A lot of folklore has built up around Zipf's Law. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. The term Zipf's Law refers to the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Myth: Zipf's Law is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand rank and frequency and power laws.

Myth: Zipf's Law is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as analyzing text corpora benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: matplotlib, NumPy, and pandas make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Zipf's Law are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example testing it on random text.

In short, careful study of Zipf's Law pays off. Spend time on rank and frequency and power laws, and the rest will follow.
