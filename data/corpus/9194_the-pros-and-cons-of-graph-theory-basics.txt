The Pros and Cons of Graph Theory Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Graph Theory Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports social networks
- Makes maps easier
- Helps with dependency analysis

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing directed and undirected graphs
- Risk: ignoring disconnected parts
- Risk: choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as social networks, and you can manage the risks, Graph Theory Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, draw a small example before writing code.
