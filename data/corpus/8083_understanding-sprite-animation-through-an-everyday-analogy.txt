Understanding Sprite Animation Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a flip book drawn one page at a time.

In that picture, sprite sheets is the first rule you follow, frames is the next step, and frame rate and animation states keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Sprite Animation is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move, and it involves details such as inconsistent frame timing that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Aseprite to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain 2D platformers to a friend.
