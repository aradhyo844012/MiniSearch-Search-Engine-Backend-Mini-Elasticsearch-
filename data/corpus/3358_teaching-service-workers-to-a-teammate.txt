Teaching Service Workers to a Teammate

Explaining Service Workers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving offline support, naming registration and install and activate events as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain cache storage in Service Workers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, caching stale files forever, and suggest this habit: version your caches so old files can be removed.
