Mistakes to Avoid with Event Sourcing

Everyone learns Event Sourcing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: changing event formats carelessly. It usually appears when someone skips event log and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unbounded replay times. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of audit trails. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: using it for simple CRUD apps. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on replay to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: version your events from the first day.
