Prompt Engineering Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Prompt Engineering regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of drafting content early, before the design hardens.
2. Be careful with this one: vague requests.
3. Write down why you chose Prompt Engineering so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip instructions; everything else builds on it.
5. Reach for model playgrounds first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as LangChain.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to extracting data from text as well as to Prompt Engineering in general.
