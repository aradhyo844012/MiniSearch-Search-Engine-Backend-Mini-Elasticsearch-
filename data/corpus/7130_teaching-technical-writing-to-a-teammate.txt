Teaching Technical Writing to a Teammate

Explaining Technical Writing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: giving directions to a stranger. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving documentation, naming audience and structure as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain examples in Technical Writing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, assuming too much knowledge, and suggest this habit: state the main point in the first two sentences.
