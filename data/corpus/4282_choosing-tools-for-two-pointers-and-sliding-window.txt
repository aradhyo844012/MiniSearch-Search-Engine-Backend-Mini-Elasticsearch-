Choosing Tools for Two Pointers and Sliding Window

There is no single best tool for Two Pointers and Sliding Window. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python lists is a common first choice, especially for finding pairs with a given sum. collections.deque suits teams that already work with similar technology, and LeetCode is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle longest substring problems well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: moving the wrong pointer.

As a rule of thumb, write down the window invariant before coding the loop, no matter which tool you use.
