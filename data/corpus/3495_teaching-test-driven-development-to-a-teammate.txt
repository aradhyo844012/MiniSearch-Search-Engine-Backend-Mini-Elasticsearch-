Teaching Test-Driven Development to a Teammate

Explaining Test-Driven Development to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: writing the exam questions before teaching the lesson. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Test-Driven Development is a workflow where you write a failing test first and then write code to make it pass. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving designing small modules, naming red, green, refactor and small steps as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain test first in Test-Driven Development back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, writing tests that are too large, and suggest this habit: keep each red-green cycle to a few minutes.
