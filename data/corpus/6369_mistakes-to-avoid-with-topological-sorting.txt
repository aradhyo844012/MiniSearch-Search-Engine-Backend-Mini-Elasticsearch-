Mistakes to Avoid with Topological Sorting

Everyone learns Topological Sorting partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring cycles. It usually appears when someone skips directed acyclic graphs and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: assuming the order is unique. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of build systems. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: mixing up edge directions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on cycle detection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: check for cycles first so you can report a clear error.
