The Importance of Big O Notation in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Big O Notation until something goes wrong without it. The term Big O Notation refers to a way of describing how running time or memory grows as the input size grows. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Algorithms are easiest to learn by tracing small examples on paper before writing any code.

Typical applications include comparing algorithms, predicting scaling problems, and explaining design choices in interviews. Small projects and large systems both benefit.

Three issues come up again and again: ignoring constant factors entirely; mixing up best and worst case; and measuring only tiny inputs. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

A solid grasp of Big O Notation also makes Binary Search Trees easier to understand.

In short, careful study of Big O Notation pays off. Spend time on upper bounds and constant, linear and logarithmic growth, and the rest will follow.
