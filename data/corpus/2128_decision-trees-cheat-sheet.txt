Decision Trees Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Decision Trees.

- Definition: models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data.
- Core ideas: splits; entropy and Gini impurity; leaf nodes; pruning.
- Use it for: classifying customers; explaining predictions; baseline models.
- Watch out for: deep trees that overfit; ignoring class imbalance; unstable results from small data changes.
- Tools: scikit-learn, XGBoost, Graphviz.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for entropy and Gini impurity before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice splits until it feels automatic.
