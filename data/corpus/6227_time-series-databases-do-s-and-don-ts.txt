Time-Series Databases Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Time-Series Databases.

Do:
- Learn timestamps before anything else.
- Try InfluxDB on a small example.
- Make it a habit to include a time range in every query.
- Test the case of server metrics early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: high-cardinality tags.
- Ignore this: keeping raw data forever.
- Ignore this: querying without time bounds.
- Adopt Time-Series Databases just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Time-Series Databases, a little patience with retention policies pays off more than any clever shortcut.
