Practice Exercises for End-to-End Testing

These exercises build real understanding of End-to-End Testing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing End-to-End Testing and include the term browser automation.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: flaky tests. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Pick Playwright and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble checking login and checkout flows or release verification.
Exercise 5: Explain how test data and selectors differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: End-to-End Testing is tests that drive an application through a real user journey from start to finish.
