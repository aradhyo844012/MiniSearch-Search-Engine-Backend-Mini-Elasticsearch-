Teaching Virtual Private Clouds to a Teammate

Explaining Virtual Private Clouds to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a gated neighborhood inside a big city. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Virtual Private Clouds is isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving separating public and private services, naming subnets and route tables as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain security groups in Virtual Private Clouds back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, overlapping address ranges, and suggest this habit: keep databases in private subnets.
