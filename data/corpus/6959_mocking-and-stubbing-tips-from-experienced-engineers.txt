Mocking and Stubbing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Mocking and Stubbing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for unittest.mock first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Mockito.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit stubs before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Mocking and Stubbing so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: over-mocking.
5. Make sure you can prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to simulating failures as well.
