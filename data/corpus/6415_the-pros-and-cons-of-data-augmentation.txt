The Pros and Cons of Data Augmentation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Data Augmentation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports image models
- Makes text classification easier
- Helps with small datasets

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: changes that alter the label
- Risk: augmenting the test set
- Risk: unrealistic transformations

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as image models, and you can manage the risks, Data Augmentation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, augment only the training data.
