Teaching Twelve-Factor Apps to a Teammate

Explaining Twelve-Factor Apps to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a checklist for packing a suitcase that fits any airline. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Twelve-Factor Apps is a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving building web services, naming config in the environment and stateless processes as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain backing services in Twelve-Factor Apps back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, storing state on local disks, and suggest this habit: keep config out of the code.
