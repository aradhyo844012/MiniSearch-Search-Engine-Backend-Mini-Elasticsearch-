Debugging Physics Engines: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Physics Engines, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: variable time steps, unstable stacks of objects, and extreme mass ratios.
Step 3: revisit rigid bodies and forces and impulses, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Box2D or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Physics Engines in work such as vehicles and ragdolls come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use a fixed time step for stable results. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
