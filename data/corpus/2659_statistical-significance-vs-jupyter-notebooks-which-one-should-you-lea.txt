Statistical Significance vs Jupyter Notebooks: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Statistical Significance or Jupyter Notebooks first. Both sit in the area of Data and Analytics, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect, and it is closely tied to p-values and the null hypothesis. Jupyter Notebooks, by contrast, is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes, and it is closely tied to cells and kernels.

Choose Statistical Significance first if you care about judging experiment results or comparing model performance. Choose Jupyter Notebooks first if you care about exploring data or teaching and demos.

Think of Statistical Significance as asking how surprised you should be if a coin were actually fair, and of Jupyter Notebooks as a lab notebook that can run its own experiments. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Statistical Significance, avoid mistaking significance for importance. With Jupyter Notebooks, avoid running cells out of order.

A good habit for Statistical Significance is to always report the effect size alongside the p-value. A good habit for Jupyter Notebooks is to restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
