Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Convolutional Neural Networks

This path is for a learner who has never studied Convolutional Neural Networks before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns) and learn filters and pooling.

Stage 2, hands-on: install PyTorch, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving image classification.

Stage 3, depth: study feature maps and stride, then deliberately explore this problem: too little training data.

Stage 4, application: apply Convolutional Neural Networks to medical image analysis, compare TensorFlow with Keras, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Convolutional Neural Networks to someone else, and then move on to Support Vector Machines.
