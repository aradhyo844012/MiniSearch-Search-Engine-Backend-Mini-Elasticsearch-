Skills to Build Around Regular Expressions

Knowing the definition of Regular Expressions is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with character classes and quantifiers, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with the Python re module on a Regular Expressions task, and try at least one alternative such as regex101.

Judgment: learn to decide when Regular Expressions fits a problem such as validating input, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a stencil that only lets certain shapes of text show through.

Habits: build patterns in small steps and test each one, and write down what you learn after each project.
