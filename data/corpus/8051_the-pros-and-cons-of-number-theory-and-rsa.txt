The Pros and Cons of Number Theory and RSA

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Number Theory and RSA is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports public key cryptography
- Makes hash functions easier
- Helps with digital signatures

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using small primes
- Risk: implementing crypto by hand in production
- Risk: ignoring padding

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as public key cryptography, and you can manage the risks, Number Theory and RSA is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
