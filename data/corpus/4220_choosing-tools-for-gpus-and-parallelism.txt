Choosing Tools for GPUs and Parallelism

There is no single best tool for GPUs and Parallelism. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

CUDA is a common first choice, especially for training neural networks. PyTorch suits teams that already work with similar technology, and OpenCL is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle rendering graphics well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow data transfers.

As a rule of thumb, minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory, no matter which tool you use.
