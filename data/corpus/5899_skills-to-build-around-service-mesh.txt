Skills to Build Around Service Mesh

Knowing the definition of Service Mesh is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with sidecar proxies and mutual TLS, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Istio on a Service Mesh task, and try at least one alternative such as Linkerd.

Judgment: learn to decide when Service Mesh fits a problem such as securing service traffic, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings.

Habits: adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful, and write down what you learn after each project.
