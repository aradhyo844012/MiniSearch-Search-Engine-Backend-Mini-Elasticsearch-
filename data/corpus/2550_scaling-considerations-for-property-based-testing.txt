Scaling Considerations for Property-Based Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Property-Based Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding properties and getting testing parsers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for vague properties and slow generators, and measure with Hypothesis before changing anything.

Large scale: shrinking and invariants move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around checking sorting and math functions at this stage.

Before you scale, save failing examples as regular tests. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
