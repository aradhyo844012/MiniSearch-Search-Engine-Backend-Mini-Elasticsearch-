Debugging Single Sign-On: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Single Sign-On, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: a single point of failure, misconfigured redirects, and long sessions on shared devices.
Step 3: revisit identity provider and SAML, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Okta or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Single Sign-On in work such as company intranets come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, plan for identity provider outages. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
