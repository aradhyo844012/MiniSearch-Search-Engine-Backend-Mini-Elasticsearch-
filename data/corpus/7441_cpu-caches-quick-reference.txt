CPU Caches Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for CPU Caches. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: small, fast memory close to the processor that stores recently used data.
Key terms: cache lines, hit and miss rates, levels L1 to L3, and locality.
Typical uses: speeding up loops, explaining performance differences, and designing data layouts.
Risks: false sharing, poor access patterns, and assuming memory is uniformly fast.
Tools: perf, Valgrind cachegrind, and Intel VTune.

Mental picture: keeping your most-used books on your desk instead of in the library.

Habit to build: access arrays in the order they are stored.
