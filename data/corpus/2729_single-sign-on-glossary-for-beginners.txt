Single Sign-On Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Single Sign-On feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Single Sign-On: letting users log in once and access several applications.
identity provider: a core term, most visible in company intranets.
SAML: a core term that beginners often confuse with OpenID Connect.
OpenID Connect: a core term that connects to session lifetime.
session lifetime: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Okta: a tool commonly used for reducing password fatigue.

Come back to this Single Sign-On list whenever documentation for Keycloak uses a word you do not recognize.
