Combining Webhooks with Background Jobs in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Webhooks and Background Jobs can work together in a single project.

Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens. Background Jobs is tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them.

Imagine a project that involves payment notifications and sending emails. Webhooks brings event payloads, while Background Jobs brings queues.

A sensible plan is to start with ngrok for the Webhooks part and Celery for the Background Jobs part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: slow handlers causing timeouts on one side, and unbounded retries on the other.

Picture the whole as a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch, working alongside dropping laundry at a service and collecting it later.

Two habits help: first, respond quickly and process the event in a background job; second, give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
