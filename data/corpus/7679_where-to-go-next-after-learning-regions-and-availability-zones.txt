Where to Go Next After Learning Regions and Availability Zones

You have met the basics of Regions and Availability Zones. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Cloud-Native Applications, which belongs to the same area as Regions and Availability Zones (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of regions, availability zones, latency or data residency, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around surviving data center failures with AWS Regions, and note every problem you meet, starting with putting everything in one zone.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Regions and Availability Zones to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
