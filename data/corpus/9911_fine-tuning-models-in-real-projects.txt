Fine-Tuning Models in Real Projects

Reading about Fine-Tuning Models is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: domain-specific language tasks. Here, pretrained weights drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against overfitting on small datasets.

Scenario two: style adaptation. This time training data matters most, and tools such as Hugging Face help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: improving accuracy on narrow problems. The key idea is the learning rate, and the biggest risk is forgetting general abilities.

Across all three scenarios, Fine-Tuning Models stays the same idea: adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data. Only the details change.
