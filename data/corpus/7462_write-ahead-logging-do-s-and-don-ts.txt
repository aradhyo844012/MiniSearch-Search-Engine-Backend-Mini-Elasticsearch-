Write-Ahead Logging Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Write-Ahead Logging.

Do:
- Learn log records before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL WAL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose.
- Test the case of surviving crashes early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: disabling fsync for speed.
- Ignore this: log files filling the disk.
- Ignore this: forgetting to archive logs.
- Adopt Write-Ahead Logging just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Write-Ahead Logging, a little patience with checkpoints pays off more than any clever shortcut.
