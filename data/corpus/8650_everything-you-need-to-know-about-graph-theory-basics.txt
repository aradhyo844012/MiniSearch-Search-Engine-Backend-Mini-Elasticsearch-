Everything You Need to Know About Graph Theory Basics

This page collects the essentials about Graph Theory Basics in one place. In short, Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them.

Core concepts:
- vertices and edges
- degree
- paths and cycles
- connectivity

Where it is used:
- social networks
- maps
- dependency analysis

Common problems:
- mixing directed and undirected graphs
- ignoring disconnected parts
- choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs

Tools to know:
- NetworkX
- Graphviz
- Gephi

Remember this above all: a map of towns and the roads between them is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to draw a small example before writing code.
