Computer Vision Basics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Computer Vision Basics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: teaching computers to extract information from images and video.
Key terms: pixels, filters and edges, feature detection, and classification.
Typical uses: face detection, document scanning, and quality inspection.
Risks: lighting changes, biased datasets, and overfitting to a small set of photos.
Tools: OpenCV, Pillow, and scikit-image.

Mental picture: teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do.

Habit to build: visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
