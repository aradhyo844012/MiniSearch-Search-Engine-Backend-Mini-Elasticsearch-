The Pros and Cons of Convolutional Neural Networks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Convolutional Neural Networks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports image classification
- Makes medical image analysis easier
- Helps with object detection

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too little training data
- Risk: ignoring data augmentation
- Risk: overly deep models for simple tasks

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as image classification, and you can manage the risks, Convolutional Neural Networks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start from a pretrained model.
