Scaling Considerations for Single Sign-On

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Single Sign-On changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding identity provider and getting company intranets right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for a single point of failure and misconfigured redirects, and measure with Okta before changing anything.

Large scale: OpenID Connect and session lifetime move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around reducing password fatigue at this stage.

Before you scale, plan for identity provider outages. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
