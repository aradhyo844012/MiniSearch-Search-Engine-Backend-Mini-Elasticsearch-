Mistakes to Avoid with System Calls

Everyone learns System Calls partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring error codes. It usually appears when someone skips user mode and kernel mode and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: making too many small calls. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of reading and writing files. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unsafe assumptions about blocking. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on libc wrappers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do.
