The Pros and Cons of Progressive Web Apps

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Progressive Web Apps is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports offline-friendly tools
- Makes lightweight alternatives to native apps easier
- Helps with low-bandwidth markets

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring platform limitations
- Risk: poor offline fallbacks
- Risk: heavy first loads

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as offline-friendly tools, and you can manage the risks, Progressive Web Apps is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test the app in airplane mode.
