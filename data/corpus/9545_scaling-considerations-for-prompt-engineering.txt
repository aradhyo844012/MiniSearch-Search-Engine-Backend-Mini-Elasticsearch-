Scaling Considerations for Prompt Engineering

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Prompt Engineering changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding instructions and getting drafting content right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for vague requests and missing context, and measure with model playgrounds before changing anything.

Large scale: context and output format move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around extracting data from text at this stage.

Before you scale, specify the output format and include one or two examples. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
