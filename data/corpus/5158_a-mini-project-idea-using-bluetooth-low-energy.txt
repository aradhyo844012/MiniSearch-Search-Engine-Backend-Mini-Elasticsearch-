A Mini Project Idea Using Bluetooth Low Energy

A small project is the fastest way to make Bluetooth Low Energy stick. This one takes a weekend and needs only a laptop.

The goal is to build a tiny program or demo that shows Bluetooth Low Energy at work on fitness trackers. Keep the scope small enough to finish.

Milestones:
1. Read about advertising and GATT services.
2. Set up nRF Connect and run a hello-world example.
3. Add the core feature.
4. Handle one failure case, such as ignoring connection limits.
5. Write a short README explaining what you learned.

Stretch goals: extend the demo to cover smart locks, or compare BlueZ with Android Bluetooth APIs.

Finish by recording what surprised you. Writing it down is how knowledge about Bluetooth Low Energy turns into something you can show others.
