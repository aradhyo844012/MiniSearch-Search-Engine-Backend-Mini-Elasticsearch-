Mistakes to Avoid with Web Components

Everyone learns Web Components partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: styling surprises across the shadow boundary. It usually appears when someone skips custom elements and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor accessibility. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of design system components. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: heavy polyfills. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on slots to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: document the attributes and events like a public API.
