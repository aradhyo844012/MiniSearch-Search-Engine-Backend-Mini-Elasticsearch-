Mistakes to Avoid with Dijkstra's Algorithm

Everyone learns Dijkstra's Algorithm partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using it with negative edge weights. It usually appears when someone skips priority queue and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: skipping the priority queue and getting slow code. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of route planning. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: not stopping early when only one target is needed. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on visited set to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue.
