Where to Go Next After Learning Quantum Computing Basics

You have met the basics of Quantum Computing Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Digital Twins, which belongs to the same area as Quantum Computing Basics (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of qubits, superposition, entanglement or quantum gates, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around simulating molecules with Qiskit, and note every problem you meet, starting with expecting speedups for every problem.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Quantum Computing Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
