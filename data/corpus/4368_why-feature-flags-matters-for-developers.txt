Why Feature Flags Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Feature Flags make better decisions, because Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code.

Three situations show why it matters. First, dark launches. Second, A/B tests. Third, emergency kill switches. In each case, a solid grasp of flag evaluation and targeting rules separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: never removing old flags, flags that depend on each other, and untested flag combinations.

You will not need to build everything yourself; LaunchDarkly, Unleash, and OpenFeature cover most needs.
