Views and Materialized Views Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Views and Materialized Views feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Views and Materialized Views: saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results.
view definitions: a core term, most visible in simplifying repeated queries.
refresh strategies: a core term that beginners often confuse with permissions.
permissions: a core term that connects to dependencies.
dependencies: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
PostgreSQL: a tool commonly used for hiding sensitive columns.
