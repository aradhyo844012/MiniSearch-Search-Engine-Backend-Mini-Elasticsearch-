Teaching System Design Interviews to a Teammate

Explaining System Design Interviews to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving preparing for senior roles, naming requirements and scaling estimates as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain trade-offs in System Design Interviews back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements, and suggest this habit: clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
