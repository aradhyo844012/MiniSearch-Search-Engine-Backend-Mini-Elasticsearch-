Skills to Build Around Secrets Management

Knowing the definition of Secrets Management is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with vaults and rotation, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with HashiCorp Vault on a Secrets Management task, and try at least one alternative such as AWS Secrets Manager.

Judgment: learn to decide when Secrets Management fits a problem such as database credentials, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet.

Habits: rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak, and write down what you learn after each project.
