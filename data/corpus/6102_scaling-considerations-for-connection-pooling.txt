Scaling Considerations for Connection Pooling

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Connection Pooling changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding pool size and getting web backends right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for pools larger than the database allows and leaked connections, and measure with PgBouncer before changing anything.

Large scale: timeouts and connection leaks move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around serverless functions that call databases at this stage.

Before you scale, always return connections in a finally block. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
