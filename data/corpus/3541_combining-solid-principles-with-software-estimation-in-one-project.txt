Combining SOLID Principles with Software Estimation in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how SOLID Principles and Software Estimation can work together in a single project.

SOLID Principles is five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change. Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.

Imagine a project that involves structuring classes and sprint planning. SOLID Principles brings single responsibility, while Software Estimation brings story points.

A sensible plan is to start with UML diagrams for the SOLID Principles part and planning poker for the Software Estimation part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: creating too many tiny classes on one side, and ignoring unknowns on the other.

Picture the whole as house rules that keep a shared kitchen tidy, working alongside forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable.

Two habits help: first, apply them when a design starts to hurt, not before; second, give ranges instead of single numbers.
