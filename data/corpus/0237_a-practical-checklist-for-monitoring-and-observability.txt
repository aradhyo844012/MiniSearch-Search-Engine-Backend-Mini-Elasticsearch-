A Practical Checklist for Monitoring and Observability

Use this checklist before you rely on Monitoring and Observability in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: logs without context.
2. Guard against this risk: noisy alerts.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers metrics.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Review the core ideas: metrics, logs, traces, and alerts.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on logs.
7. Review how Monitoring and Observability interacts with Site Reliability.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
