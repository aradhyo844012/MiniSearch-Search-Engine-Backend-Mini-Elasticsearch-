Practice Exercises for Smart Contracts

These exercises build real understanding of Smart Contracts. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: bugs that cannot be patched. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Smart Contracts and include the term contract code.
Exercise 3: Compare Hardhat and Remix for decentralized applications and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Pick Solidity and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how gas fees and immutability differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.
