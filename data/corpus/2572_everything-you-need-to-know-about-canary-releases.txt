Everything You Need to Know About Canary Releases

This page collects the essentials about Canary Releases in one place. In short, Canary Releases is a rollout strategy that sends a small share of traffic to a new version before everyone gets it.

Core concepts:
- traffic percentages
- health metrics
- automatic rollback
- progressive rollout

Where it is used:
- safer releases
- testing with real traffic
- reducing the blast radius of bugs

Common problems:
- too little traffic to reveal problems
- no clear success metrics
- manual promotion mistakes

Tools to know:
- Argo Rollouts
- Istio
- Flagger

Remember this above all: a canary in a coal mine warning miners before the danger spreads is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.
