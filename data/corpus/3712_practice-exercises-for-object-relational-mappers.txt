Practice Exercises for Object-Relational Mappers

These exercises build real understanding of Object-Relational Mappers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Object-Relational Mappers and include the term models.
Exercise 2: Compare the Django ORM and Hibernate for keeping schema and code aligned and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how relationships and migrations differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble building CRUD apps quickly or switching databases.
Exercise 5: Pick SQLAlchemy and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code.
