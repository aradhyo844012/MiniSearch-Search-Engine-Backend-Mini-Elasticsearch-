Mistakes to Avoid with Distributed Locks

Everyone learns Distributed Locks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: locks that never expire. It usually appears when someone skips lease times and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: clock drift. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of running scheduled jobs once. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming the lock holder is still alive. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on timeouts to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
