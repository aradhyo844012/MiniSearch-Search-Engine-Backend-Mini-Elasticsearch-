Choosing Tools for Combinatorics

There is no single best tool for Combinatorics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python itertools is a common first choice, especially for analyzing algorithms. math.comb suits teams that already work with similar technology, and SymPy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Combinatorics by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle probability well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Combinatorics, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: double counting.

As a rule of thumb, test counting formulas on tiny cases first, no matter which tool you use.
