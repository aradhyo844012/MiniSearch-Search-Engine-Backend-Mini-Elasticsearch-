Wi-Fi Networks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Wi-Fi Networks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.
Key terms: SSIDs, channels and bands, WPA encryption, and signal strength.
Typical uses: home internet, office coverage, and public hotspots.
Risks: overlapping channels, weak passwords, and poor access point placement.
Tools: Wi-Fi analyzer apps, Wireshark, and router admin pages.

Mental picture: a conversation in a crowded room where everyone must find a quiet channel.

Habit to build: use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
