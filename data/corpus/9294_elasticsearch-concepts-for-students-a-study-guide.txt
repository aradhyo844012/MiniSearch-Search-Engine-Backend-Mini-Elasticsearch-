Elasticsearch Concepts for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Elasticsearch Concepts.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Elasticsearch Concepts in your own words.
2. Describe indexes, shards, analyzers, and queries.
3. Give examples from site search and log analytics.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents. Then draw a giant library with an index card for every word on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too many shards a problem? How does shards relate to analyzers? When would you choose Elasticsearch or Kibana?

Study habit worth keeping: define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.

When you feel ready, compare Elasticsearch Concepts with Subqueries and CTEs and write down three differences.
