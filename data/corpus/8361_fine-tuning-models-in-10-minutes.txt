Fine-Tuning Models in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Fine-Tuning Models.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Fine-Tuning Models is adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn pretrained weights, training data, the learning rate, and evaluation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine teaching an experienced chef a new regional cuisine. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through domain-specific language tasks, style adaptation, and improving accuracy on narrow problems.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for overfitting on small datasets and forgetting general abilities, then try Hugging Face for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
