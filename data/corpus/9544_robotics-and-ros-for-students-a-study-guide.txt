Robotics and ROS for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Robotics and ROS.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Robotics and ROS in your own words.
2. Describe sensors, actuators, nodes and topics, and control loops.
3. Give examples from warehouse robots and drones.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software. Then draw a body with senses and muscles controlled by a nervous system of messages on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring sensor noise a problem? How does actuators relate to nodes and topics? When would you choose ROS 2 or Gazebo?

Study habit worth keeping: test in simulation before moving real hardware.

When you feel ready, compare Robotics and ROS with Wearable Technology and write down three differences.
