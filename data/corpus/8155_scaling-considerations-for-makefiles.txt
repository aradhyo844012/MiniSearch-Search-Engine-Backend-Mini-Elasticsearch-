Scaling Considerations for Makefiles

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Makefiles changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding targets and getting compiling C projects right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for tabs versus spaces and missing phony declarations, and measure with GNU Make before changing anything.

Large scale: recipes and phony targets move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around shortcuts for common commands at this stage.

Before you scale, use phony targets for tasks like test and clean. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
