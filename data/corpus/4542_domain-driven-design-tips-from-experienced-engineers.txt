Domain-Driven Design Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Domain-Driven Design regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip ubiquitous language; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for event storming first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as UML.
3. Test the case of complex business systems early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes.
5. Write down why you chose Domain-Driven Design so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to aligning developers with domain experts as well.
