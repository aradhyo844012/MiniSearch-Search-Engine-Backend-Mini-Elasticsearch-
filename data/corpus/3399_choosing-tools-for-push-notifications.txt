Choosing Tools for Push Notifications

There is no single best tool for Push Notifications. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Firebase Cloud Messaging is a common first choice, especially for order updates. Apple Push Notification service suits teams that already work with similar technology, and OneSignal is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle chat alerts well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: asking for permission too early.

As a rule of thumb, ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value, no matter which tool you use.
