VPNs Glossary for Beginners

New words can make VPNs feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

VPNs: encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.
tunneling: a core term, most visible in remote work access.
encryption: a core term that beginners often confuse with authentication.
authentication: a core term that connects to split tunneling.
split tunneling: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
WireGuard: a tool commonly used for linking offices.

Come back to this VPNs list whenever documentation for OpenVPN uses a word you do not recognize.
