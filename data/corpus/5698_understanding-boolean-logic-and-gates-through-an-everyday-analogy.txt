Understanding Boolean Logic and Gates Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture light switches wired in series or in parallel.

In that picture, AND, OR and NOT is the first rule you follow, truth tables is the next step, and De Morgan's laws and logic gates keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from, and it involves details such as operator precedence mistakes that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Logisim to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain writing conditions to a friend.
