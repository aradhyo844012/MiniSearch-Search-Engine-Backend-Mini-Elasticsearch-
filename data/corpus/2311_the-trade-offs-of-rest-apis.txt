The Trade-offs of REST APIs

Every design choice has a cost, and REST APIs is no exception. Many students first meet REST APIs in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to an approach to designing web APIs around resources, URLs and standard HTTP methods.

On the positive side, REST APIs is well suited to mobile app backends and integrating services. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: inconsistent naming; returning 200 for errors; no versioning plan. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much resources and HTTP verbs matter to your project.
