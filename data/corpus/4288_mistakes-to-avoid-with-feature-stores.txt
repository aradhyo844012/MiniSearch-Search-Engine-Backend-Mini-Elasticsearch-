Mistakes to Avoid with Feature Stores

Everyone learns Feature Stores partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: leaking future data. It usually appears when someone skips offline store and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: duplicate definitions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sharing features across models. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: stale online values. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on feature definitions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
