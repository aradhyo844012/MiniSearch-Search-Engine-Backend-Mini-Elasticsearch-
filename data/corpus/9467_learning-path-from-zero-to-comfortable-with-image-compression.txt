Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Image Compression

This path is for a learner who has never studied Image Compression before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail) and learn lossy and lossless formats and quantization.

Stage 2, hands-on: install ImageMagick, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving web images.

Stage 3, depth: study transform coding and quality settings, then deliberately explore this problem: repeated recompression.

Stage 4, application: apply Image Compression to photo storage, compare mozjpeg with Squoosh, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Image Compression to someone else, and then move on to Video Codecs.
