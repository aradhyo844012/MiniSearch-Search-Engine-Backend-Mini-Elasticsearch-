Choosing Tools for GraphQL

There is no single best tool for GraphQL. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Apollo is a common first choice, especially for mobile apps with limited bandwidth. GraphQL Playground suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Hasura is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle aggregating many services well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: the N+1 query problem.

As a rule of thumb, use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries, no matter which tool you use.
