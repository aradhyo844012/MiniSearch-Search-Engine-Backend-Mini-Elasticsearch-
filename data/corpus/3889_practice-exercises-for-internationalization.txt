Practice Exercises for Internationalization

These exercises build real understanding of Internationalization. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Internationalization and include the term locale detection.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: hard-coded strings. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Compare the Intl API and Crowdin for regional pricing and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble global products or multi-language websites.
Exercise 5: Explain how pluralization and date and number formatting differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions.
