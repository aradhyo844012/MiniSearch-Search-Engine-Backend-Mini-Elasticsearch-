Scaling Considerations for Content Security Policy

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Content Security Policy changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding directives and getting reducing the impact of XSS right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for allowing unsafe-inline and policies too broad to help, and measure with browser DevTools before changing anything.

Large scale: report-only mode and trusted sources move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around blocking unwanted scripts at this stage.

Before you scale, start in report-only mode to learn what would break. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
