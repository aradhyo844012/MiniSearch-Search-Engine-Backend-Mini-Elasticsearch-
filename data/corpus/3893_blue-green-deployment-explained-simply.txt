Blue-Green Deployment Explained Simply

If Blue-Green Deployment sounds intimidating, relax. It is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Blue-Green Deployment as swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: blue and green environments, traffic switching, rollback, and database compatibility. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with blue and green environments, then add traffic switching.

People reach for Blue-Green Deployment when they work on zero-downtime releases, quick rollbacks, and testing in production-like settings.

One practical tip is to keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
