Combining Log Aggregation with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Log Aggregation and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Log Aggregation is collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves troubleshooting across services and surviving regional outages. Log Aggregation brings shippers, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with Elasticsearch for the Log Aggregation part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: unstructured logs on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as gathering notes from every desk into a single searchable binder, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
