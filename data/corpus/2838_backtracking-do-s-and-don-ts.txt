Backtracking Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Backtracking.

Do:
- Learn decision tree before anything else.
- Try Python recursion on a small example.
- Make it a habit to prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
- Test the case of solving Sudoku early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: forgetting to undo changes.
- Ignore this: missing pruning rules.
- Ignore this: copying large state at every step.
- Adopt Backtracking just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Backtracking, a little patience with pruning pays off more than any clever shortcut.
