Service Workers Explained Simply

Here is Service Workers without the jargon: it is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests.

Think of Service Workers as a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: registration, install and activate events, cache storage, and fetch handlers. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with registration, then add install and activate events.

People reach for Service Workers when they work on offline support, faster repeat visits, and push notifications.

One practical tip is to version your caches so old files can be removed.

Once Service Workers feels comfortable, read about Client-Side Routing, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
