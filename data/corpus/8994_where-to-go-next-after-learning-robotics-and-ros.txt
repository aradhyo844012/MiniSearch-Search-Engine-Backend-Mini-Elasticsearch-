Where to Go Next After Learning Robotics and ROS

You have met the basics of Robotics and ROS. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Wearable Technology, which belongs to the same area as Robotics and ROS (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sensors, actuators, nodes and topics or control loops, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around drones with ROS 2, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring sensor noise.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Robotics and ROS to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
