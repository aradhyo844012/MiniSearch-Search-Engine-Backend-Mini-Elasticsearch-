Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Canary Releases

This path is for a learner who has never studied Canary Releases before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Canary Releases is a rollout strategy that sends a small share of traffic to a new version before everyone gets it) and learn traffic percentages and health metrics.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Argo Rollouts, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving safer releases.

Stage 3, depth: study automatic rollback and progressive rollout, then deliberately explore this problem: too little traffic to reveal problems.

Stage 4, application: apply Canary Releases to testing with real traffic, compare Istio with Flagger, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Canary Releases to someone else, and then move on to Autoscaling.
