Is Leader Election Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Leader Election, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Leader Election is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work.

It is a good fit when your work involves database primaries or job schedulers, and when your team understands heartbeats.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn terms or epochs, or when you cannot manage these risks: split brain, flapping leaders, and slow failure detection.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether candidates and failover are part of the answer. If they are, Leader Election is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with cluster managers and tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
