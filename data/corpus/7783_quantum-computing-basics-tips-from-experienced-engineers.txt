Quantum Computing Basics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Quantum Computing Basics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
2. Write down why you chose Quantum Computing Basics so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit superposition before anything else.
4. Reach for Qiskit first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Cirq.
5. Be careful with this one: expecting speedups for every problem.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to simulating molecules as well as to Quantum Computing Basics in general.
