Why Raspberry Pi Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Raspberry Pi make better decisions, because Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects.

Three situations show why it matters. First, home automation. Second, learning Linux. Third, small servers. In each case, a solid grasp of GPIO pins and Linux separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: SD card corruption, inadequate power supplies, and exposing the device without hardening.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Raspberry Pi OS, Python GPIO libraries, and SSH cover most needs.

Connect Raspberry Pi with Flutter to see the bigger picture of Mobile, Embedded and IoT.
