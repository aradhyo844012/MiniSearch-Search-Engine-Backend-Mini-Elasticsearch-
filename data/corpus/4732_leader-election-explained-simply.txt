Leader Election Explained Simply

If Leader Election sounds intimidating, relax. It is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Leader Election as a classroom picking a monitor when the teacher leaves. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: heartbeats, terms or epochs, candidates, and failover. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with heartbeats, then add terms or epochs.

You will meet Leader Election in work such as database primaries, job schedulers, and cluster managers.

One practical tip is to tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.

Once Leader Election feels comfortable, read about Event Sourcing, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
