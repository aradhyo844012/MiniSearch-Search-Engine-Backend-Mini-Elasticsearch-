Debugging Combinatorics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Combinatorics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: double counting, mixing order and no-order cases, and ignoring repetition.
Step 3: revisit permutations and combinations, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python itertools or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Combinatorics in work such as analyzing algorithms come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test counting formulas on tiny cases first. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
