Disk Partitioning and Mounting Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Disk Partitioning and Mounting. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree.
Key terms: partitions, file system types, mount points, and fstab.
Typical uses: adding a new drive, separating data from the system, and encrypting volumes.
Risks: wrong device names, mounting without fstab entries, and formatting the wrong disk.
Tools: lsblk, fdisk, and mount.

Mental picture: building rooms inside a warehouse and putting door numbers on them.

Habit to build: double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
