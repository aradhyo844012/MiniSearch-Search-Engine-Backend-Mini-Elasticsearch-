Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Embedded Systems

This path is for a learner who has never studied Embedded Systems before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Embedded Systems is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits) and learn microcontrollers and firmware.

Stage 2, hands-on: install C, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving appliances.

Stage 3, depth: study real-time constraints and memory limits, then deliberately explore this problem: memory leaks on tiny devices.

Stage 4, application: apply Embedded Systems to medical devices, compare FreeRTOS with PlatformIO, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Embedded Systems to someone else, and then move on to Bluetooth Low Energy.
