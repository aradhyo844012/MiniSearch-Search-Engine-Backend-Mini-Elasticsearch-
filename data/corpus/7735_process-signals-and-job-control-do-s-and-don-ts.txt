Process Signals and Job Control Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Process Signals and Job Control.

Do:
- Learn SIGTERM and SIGKILL before anything else.
- Try kill on a small example.
- Make it a habit to try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.
- Test the case of stopping runaway programs early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using kill -9 first.
- Ignore this: orphaned child processes.
- Ignore this: ignoring cleanup in handlers.
- Adopt Process Signals and Job Control just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Process Signals and Job Control, a little patience with foreground and background jobs pays off more than any clever shortcut.
