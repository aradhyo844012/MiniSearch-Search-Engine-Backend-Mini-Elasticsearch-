A Practical Checklist for Database Sharding

Use this checklist before you rely on Database Sharding in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: hot shards.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example scaling beyond one server.
3. Measure results before and after any change.
4. Review the core ideas: shard keys, routing, rebalancing, and cross-shard queries.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Database Sharding should accomplish.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on routing.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers shard keys.
