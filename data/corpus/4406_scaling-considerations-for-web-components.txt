Scaling Considerations for Web Components

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Web Components changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding custom elements and getting design system components right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for styling surprises across the shadow boundary and poor accessibility, and measure with Lit before changing anything.

Large scale: templates and slots move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around widgets reused across frameworks at this stage.

Before you scale, document the attributes and events like a public API. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
