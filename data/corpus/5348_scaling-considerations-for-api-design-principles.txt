Scaling Considerations for API Design Principles

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how API Design Principles changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding consistent naming and getting public platforms right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for leaking internal details and inconsistent responses, and measure with OpenAPI before changing anything.

Large scale: idempotent operations and documentation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around internal service contracts at this stage.

Before you scale, write the documentation before the implementation. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
