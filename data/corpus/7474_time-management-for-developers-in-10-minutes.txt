Time Management for Developers in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Time Management for Developers.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn prioritization, time blocking, breaking down tasks, and avoiding context switches.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine packing a suitcase with the big items first. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through managing coursework and projects, meeting deadlines, and reducing stress.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for multitasking and underestimating tasks, then try calendar blocking for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
