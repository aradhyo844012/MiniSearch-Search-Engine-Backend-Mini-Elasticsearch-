A Practical Checklist for Database Transactions

Use this checklist before you rely on Database Transactions in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers atomicity.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example transferring money between accounts.
3. Guard against this risk: forgetting to roll back on errors.
4. Review how Database Transactions interacts with SQL Joins.
5. Guard against this risk: ignoring isolation levels.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on consistency.
7. Confirm that your team agrees on what Database Transactions should accomplish.
