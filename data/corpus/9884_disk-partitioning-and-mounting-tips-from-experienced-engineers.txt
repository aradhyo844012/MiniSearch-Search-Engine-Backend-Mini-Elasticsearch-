Disk Partitioning and Mounting Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Disk Partitioning and Mounting regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: wrong device names.
2. Write down why you chose Disk Partitioning and Mounting so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit file system types before anything else.
4. Reach for lsblk first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as fdisk.
5. Do not skip partitions; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to separating data from the system as well as to Disk Partitioning and Mounting in general.
