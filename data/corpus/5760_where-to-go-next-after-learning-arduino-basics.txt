Where to Go Next After Learning Arduino Basics

You have met the basics of Arduino Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Native, which belongs to the same area as Arduino Basics (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sketches, digital and analog pins, setup and loop functions or the serial monitor, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around driving motors and LEDs with Arduino IDE, and note every problem you meet, starting with wiring errors.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Arduino Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
