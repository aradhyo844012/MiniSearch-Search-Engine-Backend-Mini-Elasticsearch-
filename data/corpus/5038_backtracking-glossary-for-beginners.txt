Backtracking Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Backtracking feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Backtracking: a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends.
decision tree: a core term, most visible in solving Sudoku.
pruning: a core term that beginners often confuse with state restoration.
state restoration: a core term that connects to constraint checking.
constraint checking: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python recursion: a tool commonly used for generating permutations.

Come back to this Backtracking list whenever documentation for itertools uses a word you do not recognize.
