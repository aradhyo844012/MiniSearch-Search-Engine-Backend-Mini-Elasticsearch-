Model Evaluation Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Model Evaluation.

- Definition: measuring how well a machine learning model works on data it has not seen.
- Core ideas: accuracy; precision and recall; confusion matrices; validation sets.
- Use it for: comparing models; choosing thresholds; reporting results honestly.
- Watch out for: using accuracy on imbalanced data; tuning on the test set; ignoring variance between runs.
- Tools: scikit-learn metrics, MLflow, pandas.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for precision and recall before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice accuracy until it feels automatic.
