The Pros and Cons of Internationalization

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Internationalization is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports global products
- Makes multi-language websites easier
- Helps with regional pricing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: hard-coded strings
- Risk: text that overflows layouts
- Risk: assuming left-to-right text

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as global products, and you can manage the risks, Internationalization is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
