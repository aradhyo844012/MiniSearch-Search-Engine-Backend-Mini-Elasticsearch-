Is Encryption at Rest Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Encryption at Rest, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database.

It is a good fit when your work involves protecting backups or meeting compliance rules, and when your team understands symmetric keys.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn key management, or when you cannot manage these risks: storing keys next to the data, no key rotation, and assuming it stops every kind of attack.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether disk encryption and envelope encryption are part of the answer. If they are, Encryption at Rest is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with securing laptops and keep keys in a separate service from the data.
