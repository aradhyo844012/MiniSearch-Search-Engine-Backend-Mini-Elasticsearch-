Teaching Fenwick Trees to a Teammate

Explaining Fenwick Trees to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a set of running subtotals where each one covers a block of entries. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Fenwick Trees is a compact structure for prefix sums that supports fast updates. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving counting inversions, naming binary indexed trees and lowest set bit as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain prefix sums in Fenwick Trees back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, using zero-based indexes by mistake, and suggest this habit: start indexing from one to keep the bit tricks simple.
