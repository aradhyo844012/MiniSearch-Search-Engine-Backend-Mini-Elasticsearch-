Combining Transformers with Embeddings in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Transformers and Embeddings can work together in a single project.

Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other. Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.

Imagine a project that involves language translation and semantic search. Transformers brings self-attention, while Embeddings brings vectors.

A sensible plan is to start with Hugging Face for the Transformers part and sentence-transformers for the Embeddings part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: limited context windows on one side, and ignoring normalization on the other.

Picture the whole as a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word, working alongside coordinates on a map where similar places are close together.

Two habits help: first, begin with small pretrained models; second, use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
