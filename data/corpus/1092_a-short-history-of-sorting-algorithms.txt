A Short History of Sorting Algorithms

Ideas behind Sorting Algorithms developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Many students first meet Sorting Algorithms in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to methods for arranging items in a defined order.

Algorithms are easiest to learn by tracing small examples on paper before writing any code.

Four ideas sit at the heart of Sorting Algorithms: comparison sorts, stability, divide and conquer, and in-place sorting. Each one builds on the one before it. A good way to remember comparison sorts is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

A solid grasp of Sorting Algorithms also makes Hash Tables easier to understand.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Sorting Algorithms whenever a new project gives you a reason.
