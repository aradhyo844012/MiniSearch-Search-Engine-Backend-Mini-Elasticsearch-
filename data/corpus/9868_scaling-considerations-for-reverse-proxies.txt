Scaling Considerations for Reverse Proxies

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Reverse Proxies changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding upstream servers and getting hiding backend servers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for losing the client IP address and wrong timeouts, and measure with NGINX before changing anything.

Large scale: TLS termination and caching move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around terminating HTTPS at this stage.

Before you scale, forward the real client IP in a trusted header. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
