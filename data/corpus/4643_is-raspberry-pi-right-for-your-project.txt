Is Raspberry Pi Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Raspberry Pi, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects.

It is a good fit when your work involves home automation or learning Linux, and when your team understands GPIO pins.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn Linux, or when you cannot manage these risks: SD card corruption, inadequate power supplies, and exposing the device without hardening.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether SD card storage and headless setup are part of the answer. If they are, Raspberry Pi is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with small servers and use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
