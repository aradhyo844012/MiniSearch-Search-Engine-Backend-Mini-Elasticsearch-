A Practical Checklist for TLS Handshake

Use this checklist before you rely on TLS Handshake in a project.

1. Review how TLS Handshake interacts with DNS.
2. Review the core ideas: certificates, cipher suites, session keys, and certificate authorities.
3. Measure results before and after any change.
4. Guard against this risk: disabling certificate validation.
5. Guard against this risk: expired certificates.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers certificates.
7. Guard against this risk: using outdated protocol versions.
8. Try the approach on a small case, for example securing websites.
