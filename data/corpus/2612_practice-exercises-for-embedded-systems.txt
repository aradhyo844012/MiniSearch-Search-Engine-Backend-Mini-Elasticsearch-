Practice Exercises for Embedded Systems

These exercises build real understanding of Embedded Systems. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare FreeRTOS and PlatformIO for vehicles and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Embedded Systems and include the term microcontrollers.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: memory leaks on tiny devices. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Pick C and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how real-time constraints and memory limits differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Embedded Systems is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits.
