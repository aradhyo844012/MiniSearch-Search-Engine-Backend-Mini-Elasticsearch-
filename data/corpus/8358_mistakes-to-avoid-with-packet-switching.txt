Mistakes to Avoid with Packet Switching

Everyone learns Packet Switching partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: packet loss. It usually appears when someone skips packets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: out-of-order delivery. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of internet traffic. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: large packets being fragmented. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on reassembly to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.
