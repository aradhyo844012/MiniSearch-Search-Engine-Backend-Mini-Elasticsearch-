Mistakes to Avoid with Firewalls

Everyone learns Firewalls partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: rules in the wrong order. It usually appears when someone skips rules and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: overly broad allow rules. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting servers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: locking yourself out remotely. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on zones to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with default deny and open only what you need.
