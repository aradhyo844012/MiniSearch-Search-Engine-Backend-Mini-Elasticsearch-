Video Codecs for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Video Codecs.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Video Codecs in your own words.
2. Describe frames, motion compensation, bitrate, and containers.
3. Give examples from streaming services and video calls.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed. Then draw a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mismatched codec support a problem? How does motion compensation relate to bitrate? When would you choose FFmpeg or H.264?

Study habit worth keeping: test playback on the weakest target device.

When you feel ready, compare Video Codecs with Image Compression and write down three differences.
