Hashing vs Encryption Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Hashing vs Encryption regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them.
2. Reach for SHA-256 first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as AES.
3. Do not skip one-way functions; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
5. Write down why you chose Hashing vs Encryption so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to verifying file integrity as well.
