Debugging Star Schema Modeling: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Star Schema Modeling, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: mixing different grains, over-normalized dimensions, and missing date dimensions.
Step 3: revisit fact tables and dimension tables, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use dbt or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Star Schema Modeling in work such as business intelligence reports come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
