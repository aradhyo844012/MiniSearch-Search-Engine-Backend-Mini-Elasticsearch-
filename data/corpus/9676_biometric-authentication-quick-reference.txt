Biometric Authentication Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Biometric Authentication. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices.
Key terms: enrollment, matching, false accept and false reject rates, and liveness detection.
Typical uses: phone unlock, building access, and payment approval.
Risks: spoofing attacks, biased recognition, and credentials that cannot be changed once leaked.
Tools: Android BiometricPrompt, Face ID APIs, and FIDO2.

Mental picture: a door that recognizes you by your face instead of a key.

Habit to build: offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
