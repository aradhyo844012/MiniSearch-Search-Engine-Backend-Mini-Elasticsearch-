Log Aggregation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Log Aggregation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Log Aggregation: collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place.
shippers: a core term, most visible in troubleshooting across services.
indexing: a core term that beginners often confuse with retention.
retention: a core term that connects to search queries.
search queries: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Elasticsearch: a tool commonly used for security investigations.

Come back to this Log Aggregation list whenever documentation for Fluent Bit uses a word you do not recognize.
