Mistakes to Avoid with Database Schema Design for Beginners

Everyone learns Database Schema Design for Beginners partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: storing lists in a single column. It usually appears when someone skips entities and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unclear names. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of starting new projects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: designing without thinking about queries. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on naming conventions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: write down the main queries before finalizing the tables.
