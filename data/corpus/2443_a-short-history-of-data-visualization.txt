A Short History of Data Visualization

Ideas behind Data Visualization developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. When people talk about Data Visualization, they usually mean presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

A chart is only useful if the reader can see the point within a few seconds.

To understand Data Visualization, start with chart types. Then move on to axes and labels, and finally look at color choices and annotations. Sketching annotations on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

A solid grasp of Data Visualization also makes Data Cleaning easier to understand.

Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce. Practice chart types with small exercises until it feels natural.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Data Visualization whenever a new project gives you a reason.
