The Pros and Cons of A* Pathfinding

No technology is free of trade-offs, and A* Pathfinding is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports game characters
- Makes robot navigation easier
- Helps with map routing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overestimating the heuristic
- Risk: slow searches on huge maps
- Risk: ignoring moving obstacles

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as game characters, and you can manage the risks, A* Pathfinding is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
