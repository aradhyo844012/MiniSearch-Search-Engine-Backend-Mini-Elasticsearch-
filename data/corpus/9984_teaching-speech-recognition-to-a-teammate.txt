Teaching Speech Recognition to a Teammate

Explaining Speech Recognition to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stenographer who types as people talk. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving voice assistants, naming audio features and acoustic models as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain language models in Speech Recognition back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, accents and background noise, and suggest this habit: measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
