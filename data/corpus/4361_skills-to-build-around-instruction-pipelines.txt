Skills to Build Around Instruction Pipelines

Knowing the definition of Instruction Pipelines is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with fetch, decode and execute stages and hazards, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with perf on a Instruction Pipelines task, and try at least one alternative such as Compiler Explorer.

Judgment: learn to decide when Instruction Pipelines fits a problem such as explaining CPU speed, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example an assembly line where several cars are in different stages at once.

Habits: avoid unpredictable branches in hot loops, and write down what you learn after each project.
