Choosing Tools for Data Quality Checks

There is no single best tool for Data Quality Checks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Great Expectations is a common first choice, especially for protecting dashboards. dbt tests suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Deequ is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle validating ingestion well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: alerts nobody reads.

As a rule of thumb, check data as early as it enters the pipeline, no matter which tool you use.
