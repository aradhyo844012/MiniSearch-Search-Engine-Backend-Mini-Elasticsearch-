Support Vector Machines Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Support Vector Machines. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes.
Key terms: margin, support vectors, kernels, and regularization.
Typical uses: text classification, small high-dimensional datasets, and image recognition baselines.
Risks: slow training on large data, poor kernel choices, and unscaled features.
Tools: scikit-learn, LIBSVM, and NumPy.

Mental picture: drawing the widest road between two neighborhoods.

Habit to build: scale features and try a linear kernel first.

See also: Reinforcement Learning, which sits close to Support Vector Machines in the area of AI and Machine Learning.
