Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with AI Agents

This path is for a learner who has never studied AI Agents before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal) and learn planning and tool calls.

Stage 2, hands-on: install LangGraph, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving research assistants.

Stage 3, depth: study memory and guardrails, then deliberately explore this problem: runaway loops.

Stage 4, application: apply AI Agents to workflow automation, compare AutoGen with tool-calling APIs, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach AI Agents to someone else, and then move on to Prompt Engineering.
