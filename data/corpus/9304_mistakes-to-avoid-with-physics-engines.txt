Mistakes to Avoid with Physics Engines

Everyone learns Physics Engines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: variable time steps. It usually appears when someone skips rigid bodies and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unstable stacks of objects. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of vehicles and ragdolls. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: extreme mass ratios. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fixed time steps to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use a fixed time step for stable results.
