Twelve-Factor Apps Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Twelve-Factor Apps regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit stateless processes before anything else.
2. Reach for Heroku first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Docker.
3. Make sure you can keep config out of the code.
4. Write down why you chose Twelve-Factor Apps so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: storing state on local disks.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to deploying to platforms as well as to Twelve-Factor Apps in general.
