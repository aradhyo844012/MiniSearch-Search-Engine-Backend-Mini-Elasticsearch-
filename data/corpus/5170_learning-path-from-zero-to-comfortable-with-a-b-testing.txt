Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with A/B Testing

This path is for a learner who has never studied A/B Testing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better) and learn control and variant and random assignment.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Optimizely, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving testing page designs.

Stage 3, depth: study metrics and sample size, then deliberately explore this problem: stopping tests early.

Stage 4, application: apply A/B Testing to comparing ranking algorithms, compare GrowthBook with statsmodels, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach A/B Testing to someone else, and then move on to Data Anonymization.
