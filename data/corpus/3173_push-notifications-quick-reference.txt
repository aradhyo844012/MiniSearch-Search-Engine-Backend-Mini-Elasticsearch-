Push Notifications Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Push Notifications. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed.
Key terms: device tokens, push services, payloads, and permissions.
Typical uses: order updates, chat alerts, and re-engaging users.
Risks: asking for permission too early, sending too many messages, and ignoring token refresh.
Tools: Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service, and OneSignal.

Mental picture: a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives.

Habit to build: ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
