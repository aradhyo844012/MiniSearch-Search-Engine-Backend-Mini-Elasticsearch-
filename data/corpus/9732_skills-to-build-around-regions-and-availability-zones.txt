Skills to Build Around Regions and Availability Zones

Knowing the definition of Regions and Availability Zones is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with regions and availability zones, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with AWS Regions on a Regions and Availability Zones task, and try at least one alternative such as Azure regions.

Judgment: learn to decide when Regions and Availability Zones fits a problem such as placing services near users, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings.

Habits: spread critical services across at least two zones, and write down what you learn after each project.
