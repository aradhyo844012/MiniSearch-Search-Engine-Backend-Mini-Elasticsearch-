Speech Recognition Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Speech Recognition regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: accents and background noise.
2. Reach for Whisper first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Kaldi.
3. Make sure you can measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
4. Test the case of voice assistants early, before the design hardens.
5. Do not skip audio features; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to captioning as well as to Speech Recognition in general.
