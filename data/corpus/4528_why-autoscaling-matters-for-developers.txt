Why Autoscaling Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Autoscaling make better decisions, because Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Three situations show why it matters. First, handling traffic spikes. Second, saving money overnight. Third, keeping latency steady. In each case, a solid grasp of scaling metrics and cooldown periods separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: scaling too slowly, flapping because of short spikes, and ignoring database limits.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Kubernetes HPA, AWS Auto Scaling, and KEDA cover most needs.
