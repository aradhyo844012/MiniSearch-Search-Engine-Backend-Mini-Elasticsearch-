Scaling Considerations for Canary Releases

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Canary Releases changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding traffic percentages and getting safer releases right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for too little traffic to reveal problems and no clear success metrics, and measure with Argo Rollouts before changing anything.

Large scale: automatic rollback and progressive rollout move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around testing with real traffic at this stage.

Before you scale, define rollback conditions before starting the rollout. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
