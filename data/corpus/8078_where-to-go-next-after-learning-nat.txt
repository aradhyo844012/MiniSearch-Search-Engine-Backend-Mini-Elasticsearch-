Where to Go Next After Learning NAT

You have met the basics of NAT. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about DHCP, which belongs to the same area as NAT (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of private addresses, translation tables, port mapping or port forwarding, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around sharing limited IPv4 addresses with iptables, and note every problem you meet, starting with breaking peer-to-peer connections.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining NAT to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
