Mistakes to Avoid with Procedural Generation

Everyone learns Procedural Generation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: levels that are unfair or unwinnable. It usually appears when someone skips random seeds and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: repetitive results. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of roguelike dungeons. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: bugs that cannot be reproduced without seeds. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on reproducibility to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
