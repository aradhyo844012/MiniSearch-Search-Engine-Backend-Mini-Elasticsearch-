Case Study: Applying Bootstrap in a Small Team

Imagine a small student team building a project that depends on Bootstrap. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Bootstrap. The term refers to a popular front-end toolkit with ready-made layout, components and utilities.

The team first spent a week learning grid system and components. Early on they hit a familiar problem: overriding styles with heavy custom CSS. After fixing it, they used Bootstrap CDN to check their work and adopted Bootstrap for prototypes and dashboards. Looking back, they said the biggest lesson was to document decisions as they went.

Watch out for these problems: overriding styles with heavy custom CSS; loading unused components; and copying markup without understanding it.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Bootstrap.
