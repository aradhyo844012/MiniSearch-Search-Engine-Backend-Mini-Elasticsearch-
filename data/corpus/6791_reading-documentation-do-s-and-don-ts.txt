Reading Documentation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Reading Documentation.

Do:
- Learn search before anything else.
- Try MDN Web Docs on a small example.
- Make it a habit to check that the documentation version matches your installed version.
- Test the case of learning new libraries early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: reading the wrong version.
- Ignore this: skipping the overview.
- Ignore this: copying examples blindly.
- Adopt Reading Documentation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Reading Documentation, a little patience with tutorials versus references pays off more than any clever shortcut.
