Where to Go Next After Learning Raspberry Pi

You have met the basics of Raspberry Pi. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Mobile App Security, which belongs to the same area as Raspberry Pi (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of GPIO pins, Linux, SD card storage or headless setup, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around learning Linux with Raspberry Pi OS, and note every problem you meet, starting with SD card corruption.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Raspberry Pi to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
