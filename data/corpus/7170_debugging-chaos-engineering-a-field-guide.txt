Debugging Chaos Engineering: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Chaos Engineering, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: experimenting without safeguards, having no steady-state metrics, and running first in production.
Step 3: revisit steady state and hypotheses, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Chaos Monkey or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Chaos Engineering in work such as testing resilience come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start with small experiments in a test environment. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
