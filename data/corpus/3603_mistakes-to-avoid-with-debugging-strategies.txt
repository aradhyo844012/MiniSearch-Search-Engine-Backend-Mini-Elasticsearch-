Mistakes to Avoid with Debugging Strategies

Everyone learns Debugging Strategies partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: changing many things at once. It usually appears when someone skips reproducing the bug and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: guessing without evidence. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of fixing production issues. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: not writing a test afterward. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on binary search through code to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: change one thing at a time and record the result.
