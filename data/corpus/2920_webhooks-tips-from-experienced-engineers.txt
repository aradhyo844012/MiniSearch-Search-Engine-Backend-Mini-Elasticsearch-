Webhooks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Webhooks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
2. Test the case of payment notifications early, before the design hardens.
3. Be careful with this one: not verifying signatures.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit signature verification before anything else.
5. Write down why you chose Webhooks so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to CI triggers as well.
