BM25 Ranking Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of BM25 Ranking.

- Definition: a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization.
- Core ideas: term saturation; document length normalization; k1 and b parameters; inverse document frequency.
- Use it for: ranking search results; enterprise search; question answering baselines.
- Watch out for: untuned parameters; ignoring field boundaries; comparing scores across different queries.
- Tools: Elasticsearch, rank_bm25, Lucene.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for document length normalization before building anything large.
- Related topic: Zipf's Law.
