Why B-Trees on Disk Matters for Developers

Developers who understand B-Trees on Disk make better decisions, because B-Trees on Disk is balanced tree structures designed to read large blocks from disk efficiently.

Three situations show why it matters. First, database indexes. Second, file system directories. Third, range scans. In each case, a solid grasp of pages and branching factor separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: random writes on fragmented pages, oversized keys, and ignoring page fill factor.

Good tools exist, including PostgreSQL, SQLite, and MySQL InnoDB, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
