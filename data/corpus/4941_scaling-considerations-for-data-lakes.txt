Scaling Considerations for Data Lakes

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Data Lakes changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding raw storage and getting storing logs and events right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for turning into a data swamp and no catalog or ownership, and measure with Amazon S3 before changing anything.

Large scale: partitioning and metadata catalogs move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around machine learning datasets at this stage.

Before you scale, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
