The Pros and Cons of Biometric Authentication

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Biometric Authentication is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports phone unlock
- Makes building access easier
- Helps with payment approval

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: spoofing attacks
- Risk: biased recognition
- Risk: credentials that cannot be changed once leaked

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as phone unlock, and you can manage the risks, Biometric Authentication is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
