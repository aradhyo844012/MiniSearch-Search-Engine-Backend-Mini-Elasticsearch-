Scaling Considerations for Topological Sorting

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Topological Sorting changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding directed acyclic graphs and getting build systems right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring cycles and assuming the order is unique, and measure with Python graphlib before changing anything.

Large scale: Kahn's algorithm and cycle detection move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around course prerequisite planning at this stage.

Before you scale, check for cycles first so you can report a clear error. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
