Blockchain Fundamentals Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Blockchain Fundamentals regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
2. Reach for Bitcoin Core first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Ethereum.
3. Be careful with this one: treating it as a database replacement.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit hashes before anything else.
5. Do not skip blocks; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to supply chain tracking as well as to Blockchain Fundamentals in general.
