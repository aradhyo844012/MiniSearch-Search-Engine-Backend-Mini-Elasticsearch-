Everything You Need to Know About Software Estimation

This page collects the essentials about Software Estimation in one place. In short, Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.

Core concepts:
- story points
- ranges
- uncertainty
- velocity

Where it is used:
- sprint planning
- setting deadlines
- negotiating scope

Common problems:
- treating estimates as promises
- ignoring unknowns
- estimating alone

Tools to know:
- planning poker
- Jira
- spreadsheets

Remember this above all: forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to give ranges instead of single numbers.
