Is Game Engines Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Game Engines, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Game Engines is software frameworks that provide graphics, physics, input and tools for building games.

It is a good fit when your work involves indie games or simulations, and when your team understands scenes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn assets, or when you cannot manage these risks: choosing an engine by hype, loading everything at start-up, and ignoring platform limits.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether scripting and build pipelines are part of the answer. If they are, Game Engines is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with interactive demos and build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
