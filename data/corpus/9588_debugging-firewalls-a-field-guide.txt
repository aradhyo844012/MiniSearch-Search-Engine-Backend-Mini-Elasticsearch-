Debugging Firewalls: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Firewalls, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: rules in the wrong order, overly broad allow rules, and locking yourself out remotely.
Step 3: revisit rules and stateful inspection, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ufw or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Firewalls in work such as protecting servers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start with default deny and open only what you need. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
