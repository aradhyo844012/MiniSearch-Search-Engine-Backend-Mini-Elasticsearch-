Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Process Signals and Job Control

This path is for a learner who has never studied Process Signals and Job Control before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Process Signals and Job Control is messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs) and learn SIGTERM and SIGKILL and foreground and background jobs.

Stage 2, hands-on: install kill, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving stopping runaway programs.

Stage 3, depth: study kill and trap and process groups, then deliberately explore this problem: using kill -9 first.

Stage 4, application: apply Process Signals and Job Control to shutting down gracefully, compare jobs and fg with nohup, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Process Signals and Job Control to someone else, and then move on to systemd Services.
