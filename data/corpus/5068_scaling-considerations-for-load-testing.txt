Scaling Considerations for Load Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Load Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding virtual users and getting capacity planning right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for testing unrealistic traffic and ignoring warm-up, and measure with k6 before changing anything.

Large scale: latency percentiles and ramp-up move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around finding bottlenecks at this stage.

Before you scale, report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
