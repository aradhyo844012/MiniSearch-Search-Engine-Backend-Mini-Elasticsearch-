Push Notifications Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Push Notifications regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Firebase Cloud Messaging first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Apple Push Notification service.
2. Write down why you chose Push Notifications so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: asking for permission too early.
4. Make sure you can ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
5. Test the case of order updates early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to chat alerts as well.
