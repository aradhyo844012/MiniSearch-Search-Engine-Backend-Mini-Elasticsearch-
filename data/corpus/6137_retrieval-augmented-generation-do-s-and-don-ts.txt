Retrieval-Augmented Generation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Do:
- Learn retrieval before anything else.
- Try LangChain on a small example.
- Make it a habit to evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality.
- Test the case of company knowledge assistants early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: poor chunking.
- Ignore this: irrelevant retrieved text.
- Ignore this: no evaluation of the answers.
- Adopt Retrieval-Augmented Generation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Retrieval-Augmented Generation, a little patience with chunking pays off more than any clever shortcut.
