Eventual Consistency Explained Simply

Here is Eventual Consistency without the jargon: it is a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough.

Think of Eventual Consistency as news spreading through a village until everyone has heard it. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: replication delay, conflict resolution, read-your-writes, and convergence. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with replication delay, then add conflict resolution.

You will meet Eventual Consistency in work such as global social feeds, shopping cart services, and DNS records.

One practical tip is to decide up front how your app handles stale reads.

Once Eventual Consistency feels comfortable, read about Graph Databases, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
