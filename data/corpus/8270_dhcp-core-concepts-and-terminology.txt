DHCP: Core Concepts and Terminology

This page defines the vocabulary you need to read about DHCP with confidence. The subject itself is a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings.

leases: one of the building blocks of DHCP. Documentation for dnsmasq usually mentions it early.
address pools: one of the building blocks of DHCP. Documentation for dnsmasq usually mentions it early.
reservations: one of the building blocks of DHCP. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
discover and offer messages: one of the building blocks of DHCP. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
