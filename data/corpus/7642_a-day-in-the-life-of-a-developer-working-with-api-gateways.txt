A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with API Gateways

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on API Gateways.

Morning: Divya starts by reviewing a ticket about exposing microservices. Before touching any code, Divya re-reads the notes on routing to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Divya pairs with a teammate to explore authentication with Kong. A small test exposes the problem of a single point of failure, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving securing public APIs. Divya sketches a reception desk that checks visitors and sends them to the right office on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Divya writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to keep the gateway thin and put business logic in services.

Days like this are typical. API Gateways is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
