Where to Go Next After Learning Load Testing

You have met the basics of Load Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Static Analysis, which belongs to the same area as Load Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of virtual users, throughput, latency percentiles or ramp-up, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around finding bottlenecks with k6, and note every problem you meet, starting with testing unrealistic traffic.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Load Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
