Mistakes to Avoid with Autoscaling

Everyone learns Autoscaling partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: scaling too slowly. It usually appears when someone skips scaling metrics and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: flapping because of short spikes. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of handling traffic spikes. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring database limits. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on horizontal scaling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
