Relational Databases Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Relational Databases.

- Definition: systems that store data in tables with rows, columns and defined relationships.
- Core ideas: tables and rows; primary keys; foreign keys; schemas.
- Use it for: storing orders and customers; reporting and analytics; content management.
- Watch out for: skipping constraints; storing lists in a single column; missing indexes on filtered columns.
- Tools: PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite.
- Related topic: NoSQL Databases.
- Quick tip: practice tables and rows until it feels automatic.
