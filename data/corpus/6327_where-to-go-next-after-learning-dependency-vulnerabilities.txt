Where to Go Next After Learning Dependency Vulnerabilities

You have met the basics of Dependency Vulnerabilities. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Single Sign-On, which belongs to the same area as Dependency Vulnerabilities (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of CVE identifiers, version pinning, transitive dependencies or software bills of materials, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around automating updates with Dependabot, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring transitive dependencies.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Dependency Vulnerabilities to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
