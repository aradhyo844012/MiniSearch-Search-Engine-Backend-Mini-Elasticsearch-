How BM25 Ranking Connects to Autocomplete

BM25 Ranking and Autocomplete are closely connected parts of Search and Retrieval. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving ranking search results may also involve search boxes. In such a project, term saturation and prefixes often influence each other.

Start with BM25 Ranking: the term refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. Then look at Autocomplete, which refers to suggesting likely completions while a person types a query.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as enterprise search, and explain how ranking suggestions would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For BM25 Ranking, watch for this: ignoring field boundaries. For Autocomplete, watch for this: offensive or irrelevant completions. Real query logs reveal what people actually look for, which is rarely what designers expect.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Elasticsearch or tries.
