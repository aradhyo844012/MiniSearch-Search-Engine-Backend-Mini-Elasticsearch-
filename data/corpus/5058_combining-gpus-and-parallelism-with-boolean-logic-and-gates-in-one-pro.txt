Combining GPUs and Parallelism with Boolean Logic and Gates in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how GPUs and Parallelism and Boolean Logic and Gates can work together in a single project.

GPUs and Parallelism is processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once. Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from.

Imagine a project that involves training neural networks and writing conditions. GPUs and Parallelism brings threads and blocks, while Boolean Logic and Gates brings AND, OR and NOT.

A sensible plan is to start with CUDA for the GPUs and Parallelism part and Logisim for the Boolean Logic and Gates part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: branch-heavy code on one side, and forgetting short-circuit behavior on the other.

Picture the whole as a stadium of workers each doing one small identical task, working alongside light switches wired in series or in parallel.

Two habits help: first, minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory; second, write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
