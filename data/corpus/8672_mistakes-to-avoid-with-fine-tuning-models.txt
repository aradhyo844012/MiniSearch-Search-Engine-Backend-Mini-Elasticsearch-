Mistakes to Avoid with Fine-Tuning Models

Everyone learns Fine-Tuning Models partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overfitting on small datasets. It usually appears when someone skips pretrained weights and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: forgetting general abilities. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of domain-specific language tasks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: low-quality training data. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on evaluation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
