Jupyter Notebooks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Jupyter Notebooks.

Do:
- Learn cells before anything else.
- Try JupyterLab on a small example.
- Make it a habit to restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
- Test the case of exploring data early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: running cells out of order.
- Ignore this: hidden state.
- Ignore this: committing outputs that contain secrets.
- Adopt Jupyter Notebooks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Jupyter Notebooks, a little patience with kernels pays off more than any clever shortcut.
