Why Database Constraints Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Database Constraints make better decisions, because Database Constraints is rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.

Three situations show why it matters. First, preventing bad data. Second, protecting relationships. Third, documenting business rules. In each case, a solid grasp of primary keys and foreign keys separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: skipping constraints for speed, missing indexes on foreign keys, and rules enforced only in the app.

Good tools exist, including PostgreSQL, SQLite, and ER diagram tools, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
