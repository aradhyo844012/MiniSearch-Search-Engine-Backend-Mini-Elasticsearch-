Choosing Tools for Jupyter Notebooks

There is no single best tool for Jupyter Notebooks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

JupyterLab is a common first choice, especially for exploring data. VS Code suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Google Colab is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle teaching and demos well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: running cells out of order.

As a rule of thumb, restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing, no matter which tool you use.
