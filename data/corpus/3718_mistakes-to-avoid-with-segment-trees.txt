Mistakes to Avoid with Segment Trees

Everyone learns Segment Trees partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: off-by-one errors in ranges. It usually appears when someone skips tree nodes covering ranges and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: wrong tree size allocation. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of range sum queries. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting to push lazy updates. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on lazy propagation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test your tree against a slow brute-force version on small inputs.
