The Pros and Cons of HTTP/2 and HTTP/3

No technology is free of trade-offs, and HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports faster page loads
- Makes improving mobile performance easier
- Helps with reducing connection overhead

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help
- Risk: middleboxes blocking UDP
- Risk: misconfigured fallbacks

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as faster page loads, and you can manage the risks, HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep a working HTTP/1.1 fallback.
