Mistakes to Avoid with Graph Theory Basics

Everyone learns Graph Theory Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing directed and undirected graphs. It usually appears when someone skips vertices and edges and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring disconnected parts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of social networks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on connectivity to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: draw a small example before writing code.
