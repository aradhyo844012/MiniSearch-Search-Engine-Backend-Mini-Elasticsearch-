The Pros and Cons of Apache Spark

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Apache Spark is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports large ETL jobs
- Makes machine learning on big data easier
- Helps with log analysis

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: data skew
- Risk: too many small partitions
- Risk: collecting huge results onto the driver

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as large ETL jobs, and you can manage the risks, Apache Spark is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
