Where to Go Next After Learning LSM Trees

You have met the basics of LSM Trees. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about B-Trees on Disk, which belongs to the same area as LSM Trees (Storage Engines and Databases) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of memtable, sorted string tables, compaction or write amplification, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around time-ordered data with RocksDB, and note every problem you meet, starting with read amplification.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining LSM Trees to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
