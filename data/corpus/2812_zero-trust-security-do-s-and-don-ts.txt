Zero Trust Security Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Zero Trust Security.

Do:
- Learn identity verification before anything else.
- Try Okta on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with strong identity and device checks.
- Test the case of remote work access early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating it as a product to buy.
- Ignore this: poor identity hygiene.
- Ignore this: breaking usability.
- Adopt Zero Trust Security just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Zero Trust Security, a little patience with least privilege pays off more than any clever shortcut.
