Sprite Animation Explained Simply

If Sprite Animation sounds intimidating, relax. It is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Sprite Animation as a flip book drawn one page at a time. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sprite sheets, frames, frame rate, and animation states. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sprite sheets, then add frames.

People reach for Sprite Animation when they work on 2D platformers, user interface effects, and visual novels.

One practical tip is to pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
