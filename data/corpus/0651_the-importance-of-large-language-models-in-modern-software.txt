The Importance of Large Language Models in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Large Language Models until something goes wrong without it. Many students first meet Large Language Models in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language.

Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen. Reproducible experiments make it far easier to trust and improve a model.

A model is only as good as the data used to train and test it.

You will see Large Language Models whenever people work on drafting text or answering questions. It also helps with assisting with code.

Beginners often stumble on three things. First: trusting outputs without checking. Second: vague prompts. Third: sending sensitive data carelessly.

A solid grasp of Large Language Models also makes Feature Engineering easier to understand.

In short, careful study of Large Language Models pays off. Spend time on tokens and attention, and the rest will follow.
