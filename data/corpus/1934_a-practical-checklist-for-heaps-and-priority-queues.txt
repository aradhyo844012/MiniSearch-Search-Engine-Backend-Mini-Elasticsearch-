A Practical Checklist for Heaps and Priority Queues

Use this checklist before you rely on Heaps and Priority Queues in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: using a full sort when only the top k are needed.
2. Guard against this risk: mixing up min-heaps and max-heaps.
3. Review the core ideas: heap property, sift up and sift down, array representation, and top-k selection.
4. Guard against this risk: assuming a heap is fully sorted.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Heaps and Priority Queues should accomplish.
6. Review how Heaps and Priority Queues interacts with Sorting Algorithms.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on sift up and sift down.
