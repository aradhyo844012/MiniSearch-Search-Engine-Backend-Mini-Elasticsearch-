Mistakes to Avoid with Finite Automata

Everyone learns Finite Automata partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: missing transitions. It usually appears when someone skips states and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of tokenizers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: state explosion. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on regular languages to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: draw the state diagram before writing any code.
