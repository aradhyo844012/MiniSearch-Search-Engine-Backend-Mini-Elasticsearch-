Content Security Policy Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Content Security Policy feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Content Security Policy: a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load.
directives: a core term, most visible in reducing the impact of XSS.
nonces: a core term that beginners often confuse with report-only mode.
report-only mode: a core term that connects to trusted sources.
trusted sources: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
browser DevTools: a tool commonly used for blocking unwanted scripts.

Come back to this Content Security Policy list whenever documentation for CSP Evaluator uses a word you do not recognize.
