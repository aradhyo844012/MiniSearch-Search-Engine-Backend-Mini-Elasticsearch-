Mistakes to Avoid with Hashing vs Encryption

Everyone learns Hashing vs Encryption partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them. It usually appears when someone skips one-way functions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: using broken hash functions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of storing passwords. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: confusing encoding with encryption. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on reversibility to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
