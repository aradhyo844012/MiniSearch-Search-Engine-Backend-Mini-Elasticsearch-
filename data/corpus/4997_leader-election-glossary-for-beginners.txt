Leader Election Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Leader Election feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Leader Election: a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work.
heartbeats: a core term, most visible in database primaries.
terms or epochs: a core term that beginners often confuse with candidates.
candidates: a core term that connects to failover.
failover: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ZooKeeper: a tool commonly used for job schedulers.

Come back to this Leader Election list whenever documentation for etcd uses a word you do not recognize.
