Skip Lists Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Skip Lists feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Skip Lists: layered linked lists that allow fast searching by skipping over many elements.
multiple levels: a core term, most visible in ordered in-memory indexes.
random level assignment: a core term that beginners often confuse with forward pointers.
forward pointers: a core term that connects to expected logarithmic time.
expected logarithmic time: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Redis sorted sets: a tool commonly used for sorted sets in caches.
