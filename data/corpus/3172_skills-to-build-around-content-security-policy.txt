Skills to Build Around Content Security Policy

Knowing the definition of Content Security Policy is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with directives and nonces, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with browser DevTools on a Content Security Policy task, and try at least one alternative such as CSP Evaluator.

Judgment: learn to decide when Content Security Policy fits a problem such as reducing the impact of XSS, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a guest list at the door of a building.

Habits: start in report-only mode to learn what would break, and write down what you learn after each project.
