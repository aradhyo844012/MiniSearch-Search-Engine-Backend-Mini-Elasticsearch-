Where to Go Next After Learning Graph Databases

You have met the basics of Graph Databases. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about B-Trees on Disk, which belongs to the same area as Graph Databases (Storage Engines and Databases) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of nodes, edges, properties or traversal queries, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around recommendation engines with Neo4j, and note every problem you meet, starting with using them for simple tabular data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Graph Databases to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
