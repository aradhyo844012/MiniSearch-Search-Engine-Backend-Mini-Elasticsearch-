Choosing Tools for Model Drift and Monitoring

There is no single best tool for Model Drift and Monitoring. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Evidently is a common first choice, especially for catching degrading models. Prometheus suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Grafana is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Model Drift and Monitoring by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle scheduling retraining well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Model Drift and Monitoring, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: monitoring only system health.

As a rule of thumb, monitor input data as well as accuracy, no matter which tool you use.
