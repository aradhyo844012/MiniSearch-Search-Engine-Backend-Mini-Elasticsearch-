Rasterization Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Rasterization.

Do:
- Learn triangles before anything else.
- Try OpenGL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.
- Test the case of real-time games early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: aliasing along edges.
- Ignore this: overdraw.
- Ignore this: depth fighting.
- Adopt Rasterization just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Rasterization, a little patience with pixel coverage pays off more than any clever shortcut.
