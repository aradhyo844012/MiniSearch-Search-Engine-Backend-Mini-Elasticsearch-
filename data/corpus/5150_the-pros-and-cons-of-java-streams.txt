The Pros and Cons of Java Streams

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Java Streams is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports transforming lists
- Makes aggregating records easier
- Helps with processing data in parallel

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: reusing a consumed stream
- Risk: side effects inside lambdas
- Risk: using parallel streams without measuring

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as transforming lists, and you can manage the risks, Java Streams is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep each stream step small and readable.
