Single Sign-On Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Single Sign-On. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: letting users log in once and access several applications.
Key terms: identity provider, SAML, OpenID Connect, and session lifetime.
Typical uses: company intranets, reducing password fatigue, and centralized access control.
Risks: a single point of failure, misconfigured redirects, and long sessions on shared devices.
Tools: Okta, Keycloak, and Azure AD.

Mental picture: a festival wristband that opens every gate.

Habit to build: plan for identity provider outages.
