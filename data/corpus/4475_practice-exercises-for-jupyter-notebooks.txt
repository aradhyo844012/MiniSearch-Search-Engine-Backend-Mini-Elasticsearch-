Practice Exercises for Jupyter Notebooks

These exercises build real understanding of Jupyter Notebooks. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble exploring data or teaching and demos.
Exercise 2: Compare VS Code and Google Colab for sharing analysis and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: running cells out of order. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Explain how markdown and outputs differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Jupyter Notebooks and include the term cells.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.
