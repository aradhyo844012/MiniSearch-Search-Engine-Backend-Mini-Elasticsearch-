Is Virtual Private Clouds Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Virtual Private Clouds, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Virtual Private Clouds is isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources.

It is a good fit when your work involves separating public and private services or connecting to offices, and when your team understands subnets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn route tables, or when you cannot manage these risks: overlapping address ranges, databases in public subnets, and missing routes.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether security groups and gateways are part of the answer. If they are, Virtual Private Clouds is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with securing databases and keep databases in private subnets.
