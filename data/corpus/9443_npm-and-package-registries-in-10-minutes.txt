npm and Package Registries in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through npm and Package Registries.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: npm and Package Registries is services and tools for publishing and installing reusable code packages.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn package.json, semantic versions, lock files, and registries.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a shared parts warehouse with a catalog. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through installing JavaScript libraries, sharing internal packages, and reproducible builds.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for unpinned versions and installing untrusted packages, then try npm for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to commit your lock file.
