Is Event Sourcing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Event Sourcing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Event Sourcing is a design in which every change is stored as an immutable event and state is rebuilt from them.

It is a good fit when your work involves audit trails or financial ledgers, and when your team understands event log.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn projections, or when you cannot manage these risks: changing event formats carelessly, unbounded replay times, and using it for simple CRUD apps.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether snapshots and replay are part of the answer. If they are, Event Sourcing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with rebuilding read models and version your events from the first day.
