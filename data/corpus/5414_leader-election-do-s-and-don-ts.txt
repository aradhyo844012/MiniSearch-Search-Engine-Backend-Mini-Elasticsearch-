Leader Election Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Leader Election.

Do:
- Learn heartbeats before anything else.
- Try ZooKeeper on a small example.
- Make it a habit to tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
- Test the case of database primaries early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: split brain.
- Ignore this: flapping leaders.
- Ignore this: slow failure detection.
- Adopt Leader Election just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Leader Election, a little patience with terms or epochs pays off more than any clever shortcut.
