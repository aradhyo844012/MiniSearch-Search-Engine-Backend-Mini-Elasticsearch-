Edit Distance Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Edit Distance.

- Definition: a measure of how many single-character changes turn one word into another.
- Core ideas: insertions; deletions; substitutions; transpositions.
- Use it for: spell checking; fuzzy matching; correcting search queries.
- Watch out for: comparing against every word in a large vocabulary; ignoring word length; forgetting transpositions.
- Tools: Levenshtein libraries, deletion-neighbour indexes, difflib.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for deletions before building anything large.
- Related topic: Tokenization.
