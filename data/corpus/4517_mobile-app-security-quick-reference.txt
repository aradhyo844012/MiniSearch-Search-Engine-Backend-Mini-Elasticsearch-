Mobile App Security Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Mobile App Security. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks.
Key terms: secure storage, certificate pinning, obfuscation, and permissions.
Typical uses: banking apps, health apps, and enterprise apps.
Risks: storing tokens in plain text, hard-coded API keys, and requesting unnecessary permissions.
Tools: OWASP MASVS, Android Keystore, and iOS Keychain.

Mental picture: locking an app's valuables in a hotel safe.

Habit to build: store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
