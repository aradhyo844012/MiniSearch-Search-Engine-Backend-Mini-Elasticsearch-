Firewalls for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Firewalls.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Firewalls in your own words.
2. Describe rules, stateful inspection, default deny, and zones.
3. Give examples from protecting servers and segmenting networks.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Firewalls is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules. Then draw a security gate that checks each visitor against a list on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is rules in the wrong order a problem? How does stateful inspection relate to default deny? When would you choose ufw or iptables?

Study habit worth keeping: start with default deny and open only what you need.

When you feel ready, compare Firewalls with Reverse Proxies and write down three differences.
