NAT Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with NAT.

Do:
- Learn private addresses before anything else.
- Try iptables on a small example.
- Make it a habit to document every port forward you create.
- Test the case of home routers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: breaking peer-to-peer connections.
- Ignore this: exhausted translation tables.
- Ignore this: misconfigured port forwarding.
- Adopt NAT just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with NAT, a little patience with translation tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
