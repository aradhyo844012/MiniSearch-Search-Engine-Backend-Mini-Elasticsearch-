Mistakes to Avoid with Zero Trust Security

Everyone learns Zero Trust Security partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: treating it as a product to buy. It usually appears when someone skips identity verification and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor identity hygiene. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of remote work access. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: breaking usability. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on continuous checks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with strong identity and device checks.
