Everything You Need to Know About Rasterization

This page collects the essentials about Rasterization in one place. In short, Rasterization is converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.

Core concepts:
- triangles
- pixel coverage
- the depth buffer
- interpolation

Where it is used:
- real-time games
- user interfaces
- CAD previews

Common problems:
- aliasing along edges
- overdraw
- depth fighting

Tools to know:
- OpenGL
- Vulkan
- WebGL

Remember this above all: coloring squares on graph paper to match a drawn outline is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.
