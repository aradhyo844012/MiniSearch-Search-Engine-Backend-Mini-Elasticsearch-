Where to Go Next After Learning Python Virtual Environments

You have met the basics of Python Virtual Environments. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Monorepos, which belongs to the same area as Python Virtual Environments (Developer Tooling and Build Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of venv, pip, requirements files or activation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reproducing setups with venv, and note every problem you meet, starting with installing packages globally.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Python Virtual Environments to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
