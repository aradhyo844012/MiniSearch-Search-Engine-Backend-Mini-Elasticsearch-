Hands-On Tutorial: Getting Started with Overfitting

This hands-on tutorial takes you from zero to a small working experiment with Overfitting. When people talk about Overfitting, they usually mean when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

If you are comfortable with Overfitting, explore Neural Networks next. The two often appear in the same project.

Step 1: Install or open scikit-learn and create a new empty project folder.
Step 2: Read about training and validation error and write down a definition in your own words.
Step 3: Build the smallest possible example that uses model complexity.
Step 4: Add one test or print statement that proves it works.
Step 5: Extend the example to handle a case such as diagnosing weak models.
Step 6: Break your example on purpose and see what happens. A good experiment: evaluating only on training data.

Popular tools and libraries include scikit-learn, early stopping, and learning curves.

Watch out for these problems: evaluating only on training data; leaking test data; and adding complexity without evidence.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Overfitting.
