BM25 Ranking Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about BM25 Ranking.

Q: Explain BM25 Ranking in simple words.
A: BM25 Ranking refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. I would add an example involving ranking search results.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: untuned parameters; ignoring field boundaries; and comparing scores across different queries.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with enterprise search, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Elasticsearch, rank_bm25, and Lucene.

Q: How does it connect to Stop Words?
A: Both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: untuned parameters.
