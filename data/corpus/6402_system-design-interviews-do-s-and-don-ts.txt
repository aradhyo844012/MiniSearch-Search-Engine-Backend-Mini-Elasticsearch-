System Design Interviews Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with System Design Interviews.

Do:
- Learn requirements before anything else.
- Try whiteboard sketches on a small example.
- Make it a habit to clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
- Test the case of preparing for senior roles early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements.
- Ignore this: ignoring trade-offs.
- Ignore this: overcomplicating designs.
- Adopt System Design Interviews just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with System Design Interviews, a little patience with scaling estimates pays off more than any clever shortcut.
