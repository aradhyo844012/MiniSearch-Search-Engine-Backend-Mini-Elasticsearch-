The Pros and Cons of Static Analysis

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Static Analysis is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports catching bugs early
- Makes enforcing team style easier
- Helps with improving security

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring warnings
- Risk: too many noisy rules
- Risk: disabling checks permanently

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as catching bugs early, and you can manage the risks, Static Analysis is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, run it in CI so problems never reach the main branch.
