Ray Tracing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Ray Tracing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: slow render times.
2. Reach for Blender Cycles first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as NVIDIA OptiX.
3. Do not skip rays; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
5. Test the case of movie visuals early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to architectural visualization as well as to Ray Tracing in general.
