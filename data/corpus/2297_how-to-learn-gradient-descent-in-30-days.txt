How to Learn Gradient Descent in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Gradient Descent. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Gradient Descent covers an optimization method that improves a model by repeatedly stepping in the direction that reduces error. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn learning rate and gradients, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with PyTorch on a small task such as training neural networks.
Week 3: build a mini project around fitting regression models.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as learning rates that are too large, and explain Gradient Descent to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include PyTorch, NumPy, and TensorFlow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Gradient Descent.
