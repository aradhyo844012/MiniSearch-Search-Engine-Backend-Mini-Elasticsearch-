How to Learn ETL Pipelines in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with ETL Pipelines. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, ETL Pipelines covers processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

ETL Pipelines and Stream Processing are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Data Engineering.

Week 1: learn extraction and transformation, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Apache Airflow on a small task such as building reports.
Week 3: build a mini project around moving data into warehouses.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as silent data loss, and explain ETL Pipelines to a friend.

In practice you can start with Apache Airflow, then explore dbt or Python scripts as your needs grow.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit ETL Pipelines whenever a new project gives you a reason.
