Everything You Need to Know About Augmented and Virtual Reality

This page collects the essentials about Augmented and Virtual Reality in one place. In short, Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.

Core concepts:
- head tracking
- rendering at high frame rates
- spatial mapping
- input methods

Where it is used:
- training simulations
- games
- remote collaboration

Common problems:
- motion sickness from low frame rates
- heavy assets
- poor interface design in 3D

Tools to know:
- Unity XR
- WebXR
- ARKit

Remember this above all: wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
