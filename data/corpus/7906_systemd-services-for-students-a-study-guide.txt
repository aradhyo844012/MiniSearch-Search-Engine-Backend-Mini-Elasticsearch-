systemd Services for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover systemd Services.

Learning goals:
1. Explain systemd Services in your own words.
2. Describe unit files, targets, service states, and journal logs.
3. Give examples from starting services at boot and restarting crashed programs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: systemd Services is the init system and service manager used by most modern Linux distributions. Then draw a building manager who opens each office in the right order every morning on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is editing unit files without reloading the daemon a problem? How does targets relate to service states? When would you choose systemctl or journalctl?

Study habit worth keeping: run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.

When you feel ready, compare systemd Services with Disk Partitioning and Mounting and write down three differences.
