Why Cloud Cost Optimization Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Cloud Cost Optimization make better decisions, because Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.

Three situations show why it matters. First, cutting idle resources. Second, budgeting by team. Third, choosing storage tiers. In each case, a solid grasp of right-sizing and reserved capacity separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: forgotten test environments, over-provisioned databases, and surprise data transfer charges.

Good tools exist, including AWS Cost Explorer, Infracost, and Kubecost, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
