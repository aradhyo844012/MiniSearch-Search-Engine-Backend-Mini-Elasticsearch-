Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Color Spaces

This path is for a learner who has never studied Color Spaces before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK) and learn RGB and HSL.

Stage 2, hands-on: install browser color pickers, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving web design.

Stage 3, depth: study CMYK and gamma, then deliberately explore this problem: ignoring gamma.

Stage 4, application: apply Color Spaces to photo editing, compare ImageMagick with Coolors, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Color Spaces to someone else, and then move on to 3D Transformations.
