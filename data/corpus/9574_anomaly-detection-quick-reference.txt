Anomaly Detection Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Anomaly Detection. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: finding data points or events that differ sharply from normal behavior.
Key terms: baselines, thresholds, isolation methods, and false positives.
Typical uses: fraud detection, server monitoring, and manufacturing quality.
Risks: too many false alarms, shifting baselines, and labeling normal data incorrectly.
Tools: scikit-learn, PyOD, and Prometheus.

Mental picture: a security guard who notices the one person acting differently.

Habit to build: tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
