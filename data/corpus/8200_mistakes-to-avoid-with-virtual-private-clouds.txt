Mistakes to Avoid with Virtual Private Clouds

Everyone learns Virtual Private Clouds partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overlapping address ranges. It usually appears when someone skips subnets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: databases in public subnets. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of separating public and private services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: missing routes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on gateways to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep databases in private subnets.
