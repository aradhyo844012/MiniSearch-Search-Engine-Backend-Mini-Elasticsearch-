Why Database Migrations Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Database Migrations make better decisions, because Database Migrations is versioned scripts that change a database schema in a controlled and repeatable way.

Three situations show why it matters. First, adding columns safely. Second, renaming tables. Third, keeping environments in sync. In each case, a solid grasp of up and down scripts and version tables separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: locking large tables, editing old migrations, and data loss on rollbacks.

Good tools exist, including Alembic, Flyway, and Django migrations, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
