Load Balancing Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Load Balancing.

Q: Explain Load Balancing in simple words.
A: Load Balancing refers to spreading incoming requests across several servers so no single server is overwhelmed. I would add an example involving scaling web applications.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: round robin, health checks, sticky sessions, and layer 4 and layer 7.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: the balancer becoming a single point of failure; ignoring health checks; and session data stored only on one server.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: NGINX, HAProxy, and AWS Elastic Load Balancing.

Q: How does it connect to TCP and UDP?
A: Both belong to the area of Networking, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: the balancer becoming a single point of failure.
