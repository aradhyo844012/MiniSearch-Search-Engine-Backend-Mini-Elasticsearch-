Where to Go Next After Learning Client-Side Routing

You have met the basics of Client-Side Routing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Progressive Web Apps, which belongs to the same area as Client-Side Routing (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of history API, route matching, nested routes or route guards, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around dashboards with React Router, and note every problem you meet, starting with broken deep links.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Client-Side Routing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
