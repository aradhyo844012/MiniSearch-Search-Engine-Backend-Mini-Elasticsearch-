Teaching Leader Election to a Teammate

Explaining Leader Election to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a classroom picking a monitor when the teacher leaves. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Leader Election is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving database primaries, naming heartbeats and terms or epochs as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain candidates in Leader Election back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, split brain, and suggest this habit: tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
