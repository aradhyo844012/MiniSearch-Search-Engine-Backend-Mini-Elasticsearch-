Debugging Apache Spark: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Apache Spark, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: data skew, too many small partitions, and collecting huge results onto the driver.
Step 3: revisit DataFrames and transformations and actions, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use PySpark or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Apache Spark in work such as large ETL jobs come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
