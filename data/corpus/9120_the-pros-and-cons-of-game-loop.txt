The Pros and Cons of Game Loop

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Game Loop is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running every real-time game
- Makes keeping animation smooth easier
- Helps with simulating physics steadily

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: tying logic to frame rate
- Risk: slow update steps
- Risk: ignoring delta time

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running every real-time game, and you can manage the risks, Game Loop is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use delta time so speed does not depend on frame rate.
