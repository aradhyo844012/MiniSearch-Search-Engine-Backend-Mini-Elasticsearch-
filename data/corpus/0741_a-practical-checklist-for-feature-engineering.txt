A Practical Checklist for Feature Engineering

Use this checklist before you rely on Feature Engineering in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: data leakage.
2. Review the core ideas: scaling, encoding categories, interaction features, and missing values.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as pandas or scikit-learn pipelines.
4. Measure results before and after any change.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on encoding categories.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Feature Engineering should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: inconsistent transformations between training and serving.
8. Review how Feature Engineering interacts with Large Language Models.
9. Try the approach on a small case, for example improving model accuracy.
