Debugging Fuzz Testing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Fuzz Testing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: running without sanitizers, crashes that cannot be reproduced, and ignoring slow inputs.
Step 3: revisit input generation and coverage guidance, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use AFL++ or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Fuzz Testing in work such as testing parsers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, keep every crashing input as a regression test. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
