Scaling Considerations for Penetration Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Penetration Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding scoping and getting pre-launch security checks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for testing without written permission and ignoring the findings afterward, and measure with OWASP ZAP before changing anything.

Large scale: exploitation and reporting move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around compliance audits at this stage.

Before you scale, get written authorization before any test. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
