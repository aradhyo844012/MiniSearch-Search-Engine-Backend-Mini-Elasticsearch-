Where to Go Next After Learning Wearable Technology

You have met the basics of Wearable Technology. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about 5G Networks, which belongs to the same area as Wearable Technology (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sensors, low-power design, companion apps or data syncing, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around notifications with Wear OS, and note every problem you meet, starting with battery drain.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Wearable Technology to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
