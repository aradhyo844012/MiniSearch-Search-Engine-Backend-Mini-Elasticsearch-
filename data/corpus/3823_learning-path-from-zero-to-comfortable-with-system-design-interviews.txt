Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with System Design Interviews

This path is for a learner who has never studied System Design Interviews before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices) and learn requirements and scaling estimates.

Stage 2, hands-on: install whiteboard sketches, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving preparing for senior roles.

Stage 3, depth: study trade-offs and components, then deliberately explore this problem: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements.

Stage 4, application: apply System Design Interviews to practicing architectural thinking, compare Excalidraw with system design primers, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach System Design Interviews to someone else, and then move on to Time Management for Developers.
