Data Quality Checks in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Data Quality Checks.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn null checks, uniqueness, freshness, and schema validation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through protecting dashboards, validating ingestion, and catching pipeline bugs.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for alerts nobody reads and checks only at the end of pipelines, then try Great Expectations for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
