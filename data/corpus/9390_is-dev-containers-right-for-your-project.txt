Is Dev Containers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Dev Containers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Dev Containers is containerized development environments that every teammate can start in the same way.

It is a good fit when your work involves onboarding new developers or matching production tools, and when your team understands devcontainer.json.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn images, or when you cannot manage these risks: slow image builds, files owned by the wrong user, and forgetting to pin tool versions.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether mounted folders and extensions are part of the answer. If they are, Dev Containers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with trying projects safely and pin tool versions inside the container definition.
