Practice Exercises for Ray Tracing

These exercises build real understanding of Ray Tracing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble movie visuals or architectural visualization.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: slow render times. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Pick Blender Cycles and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Explain how reflection and refraction and shadows differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Compare NVIDIA OptiX and POV-Ray for realistic game lighting and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.
