Mistakes to Avoid with MLOps Pipelines

Everyone learns MLOps Pipelines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: untracked data changes. It usually appears when someone skips data versioning and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: manual deployment steps. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of repeatable model releases. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no testing of data. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on continuous delivery to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: version both the code and the data.
