Single Sign-On Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Single Sign-On regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of company intranets early, before the design hardens.
2. Make sure you can plan for identity provider outages.
3. Do not skip identity provider; everything else builds on it.
4. Write down why you chose Single Sign-On so the next person understands the decision.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit SAML before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to reducing password fatigue as well.
