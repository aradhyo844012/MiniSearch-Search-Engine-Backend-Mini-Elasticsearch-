Mistakes to Avoid with Anomaly Detection

Everyone learns Anomaly Detection partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too many false alarms. It usually appears when someone skips baselines and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: shifting baselines. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of fraud detection. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: labeling normal data incorrectly. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on false positives to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
