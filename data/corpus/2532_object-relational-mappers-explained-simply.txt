Object-Relational Mappers Explained Simply

If Object-Relational Mappers sounds intimidating, relax. It is libraries that map database tables to objects in code, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Object-Relational Mappers as a translator between the language of tables and the language of objects. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: models, queries, relationships, and migrations. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with models, then add queries.

You will meet Object-Relational Mappers in work such as building CRUD apps quickly, switching databases, and keeping schema and code aligned.

One practical tip is to log the generated SQL during development.

Once Object-Relational Mappers feels comfortable, read about Dependency Injection, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
