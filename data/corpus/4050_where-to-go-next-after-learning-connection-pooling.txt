Where to Go Next After Learning Connection Pooling

You have met the basics of Connection Pooling. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about LSM Trees, which belongs to the same area as Connection Pooling (Storage Engines and Databases) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of pool size, idle connections, timeouts or connection leaks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around serverless functions that call databases with PgBouncer, and note every problem you meet, starting with pools larger than the database allows.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Connection Pooling to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
