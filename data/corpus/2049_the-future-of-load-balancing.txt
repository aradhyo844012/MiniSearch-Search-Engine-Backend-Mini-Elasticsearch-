The Future of Load Balancing

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of Load Balancing is easy to describe in general terms. Put simply, Load Balancing covers spreading incoming requests across several servers so no single server is overwhelmed. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Network failures are normal, so well-designed software expects timeouts and retries.

Expect tools such as NGINX and HAProxy to keep improving, and expect newer ideas to keep building on round robin. The basics, though, tend to stay the same, so time spent on health checks and sticky sessions is rarely wasted. Anyone working on scaling web applications will keep meeting Load Balancing for years to come.
