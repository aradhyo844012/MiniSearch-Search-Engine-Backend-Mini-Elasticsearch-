Choosing Tools for Responsible AI and Bias

There is no single best tool for Responsible AI and Bias. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Fairlearn is a common first choice, especially for hiring tools. AI Fairness 360 suits teams that already work with similar technology, and model cards is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Responsible AI and Bias by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle lending models well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Responsible AI and Bias, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: assuming data is neutral.

As a rule of thumb, test performance separately for different groups, no matter which tool you use.
