Where to Go Next After Learning Garbage Collection

You have met the basics of Garbage Collection. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Python Asyncio, which belongs to the same area as Garbage Collection (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of reachability, generations, mark and sweep or pause times, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reducing memory bugs with JVM GC logs, and note every problem you meet, starting with creating huge numbers of short-lived objects.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Garbage Collection to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
