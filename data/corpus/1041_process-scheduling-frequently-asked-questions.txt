Process Scheduling: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Process Scheduling?
A: The term Process Scheduling refers to the way an operating system decides which process runs on the CPU and for how long.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Process Scheduling.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include top, htop, and perf.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with time slices and priorities, then move on to context switches and run queues.
