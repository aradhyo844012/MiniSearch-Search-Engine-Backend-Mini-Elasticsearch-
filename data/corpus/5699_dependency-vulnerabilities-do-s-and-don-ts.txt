Dependency Vulnerabilities Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Dependency Vulnerabilities.

Do:
- Learn CVE identifiers before anything else.
- Try Dependabot on a small example.
- Make it a habit to automate update pull requests.
- Test the case of auditing projects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring transitive dependencies.
- Ignore this: never updating.
- Ignore this: trusting packages without checking them.
- Adopt Dependency Vulnerabilities just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Dependency Vulnerabilities, a little patience with version pinning pays off more than any clever shortcut.
