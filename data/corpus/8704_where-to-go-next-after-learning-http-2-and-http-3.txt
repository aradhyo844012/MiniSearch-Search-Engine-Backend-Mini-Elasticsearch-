Where to Go Next After Learning HTTP/2 and HTTP/3

You have met the basics of HTTP/2 and HTTP/3. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Firewalls, which belongs to the same area as HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of multiplexing, header compression, QUIC or server push, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around improving mobile performance with curl with HTTP/3 support, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
