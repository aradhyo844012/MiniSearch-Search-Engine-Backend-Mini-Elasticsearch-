Is Load Testing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Load Testing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic.

It is a good fit when your work involves capacity planning or finding bottlenecks, and when your team understands virtual users.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn throughput, or when you cannot manage these risks: testing unrealistic traffic, ignoring warm-up, and using average latency only.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether latency percentiles and ramp-up are part of the answer. If they are, Load Testing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with validating autoscaling and report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
