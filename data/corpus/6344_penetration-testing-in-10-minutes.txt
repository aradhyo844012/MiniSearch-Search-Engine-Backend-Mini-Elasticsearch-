Penetration Testing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Penetration Testing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Penetration Testing is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation, and reporting.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine hiring a locksmith to test your doors. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through pre-launch security checks, compliance audits, and validating defenses.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for testing without written permission and ignoring the findings afterward, then try OWASP ZAP for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to get written authorization before any test.
