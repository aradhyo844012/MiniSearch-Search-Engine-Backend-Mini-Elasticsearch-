The Pros and Cons of Consistent Hashing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Consistent Hashing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports distributed caches
- Makes sharded databases easier
- Helps with load balancing

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too few virtual nodes
- Risk: uneven load
- Risk: hot keys

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as distributed caches, and you can manage the risks, Consistent Hashing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load.
