Mobile App Security Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Mobile App Security.

Do:
- Learn secure storage before anything else.
- Try OWASP MASVS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
- Test the case of banking apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: storing tokens in plain text.
- Ignore this: hard-coded API keys.
- Ignore this: requesting unnecessary permissions.
- Adopt Mobile App Security just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Mobile App Security, a little patience with certificate pinning pays off more than any clever shortcut.
