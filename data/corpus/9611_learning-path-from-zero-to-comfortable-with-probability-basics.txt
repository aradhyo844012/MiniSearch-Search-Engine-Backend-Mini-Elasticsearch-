Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Probability Basics

This path is for a learner who has never studied Probability Basics before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Probability Basics is the study of how likely events are to happen) and learn events and conditional probability.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Python random, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving randomized algorithms.

Stage 3, depth: study independence and expected value, then deliberately explore this problem: confusing independent and exclusive events.

Stage 4, application: apply Probability Basics to machine learning, compare NumPy with simulation notebooks, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Probability Basics to someone else, and then move on to Graph Theory Basics.
