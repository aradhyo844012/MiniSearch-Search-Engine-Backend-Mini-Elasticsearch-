Troubleshooting Data Cleaning Problems

When something breaks around Data Cleaning, a calm and systematic approach saves the most time. The term Data Cleaning refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Start by reproducing the problem with the smallest possible example. Then change one thing at a time and note what happens. Logs, tests and pandas usually reveal the cause faster than guessing.

Common culprits: dropping data without checking why it is missing; cleaning by hand without a record; changing the original files. If none of these explains the symptom, re-read the basics of missing values and duplicates.

In practice you can start with pandas, then explore OpenRefine or regular expressions as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of Data Cleaning pays off. Spend time on missing values and duplicates, and the rest will follow.
