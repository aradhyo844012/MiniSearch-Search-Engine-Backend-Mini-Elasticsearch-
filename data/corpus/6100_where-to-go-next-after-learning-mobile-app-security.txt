Where to Go Next After Learning Mobile App Security

You have met the basics of Mobile App Security. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Native, which belongs to the same area as Mobile App Security (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of secure storage, certificate pinning, obfuscation or permissions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around health apps with OWASP MASVS, and note every problem you meet, starting with storing tokens in plain text.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Mobile App Security to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
