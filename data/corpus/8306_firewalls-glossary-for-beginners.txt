Firewalls Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Firewalls feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Firewalls: systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules.
rules: a core term, most visible in protecting servers.
stateful inspection: a core term that beginners often confuse with default deny.
default deny: a core term that connects to zones.
zones: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
ufw: a tool commonly used for segmenting networks.

Come back to this Firewalls list whenever documentation for iptables uses a word you do not recognize.
