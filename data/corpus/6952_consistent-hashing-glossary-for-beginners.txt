Consistent Hashing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Consistent Hashing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Consistent Hashing: a way of mapping keys to servers so that adding or removing servers moves very few keys.
hash ring: a core term, most visible in distributed caches.
virtual nodes: a core term that beginners often confuse with key placement.
key placement: a core term that connects to rebalancing.
rebalancing: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Redis Cluster: a tool commonly used for sharded databases.

Come back to this Consistent Hashing list whenever documentation for Memcached clients uses a word you do not recognize.
