Mistakes to Avoid with Connection Pooling

Everyone learns Connection Pooling partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: pools larger than the database allows. It usually appears when someone skips pool size and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: leaked connections. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of web backends. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: long transactions that hold connections. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on connection leaks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: always return connections in a finally block.
