State Management Explained Simply

Here is State Management without the jargon: it is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data.

Think of State Management as a shared whiteboard that everyone updates through the same rules. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: single source of truth, actions, reducers, and derived state. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with single source of truth, then add actions.

People reach for State Management when they work on large single-page apps, shared shopping carts, and multi-step form wizards.

One practical tip is to keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
