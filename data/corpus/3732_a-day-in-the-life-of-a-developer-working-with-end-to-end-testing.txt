A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with End-to-End Testing

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on End-to-End Testing.

Morning: Sneha starts by reviewing a ticket about checking login and checkout flows. Before touching any code, Sneha re-reads the notes on browser automation to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Sneha pairs with a teammate to explore user flows with Playwright. A small test exposes the problem of flaky tests, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving release verification. Sneha sketches a test drive of the whole car on a real road on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Sneha writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to cover only the most important user journeys.

Days like this are typical. End-to-End Testing is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
