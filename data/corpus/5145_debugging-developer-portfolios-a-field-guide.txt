Debugging Developer Portfolios: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Developer Portfolios, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: too many unfinished projects, no explanation of decisions, and broken demo links.
Step 3: revisit projects and README quality, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use GitHub Pages or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Developer Portfolios in work such as applying for internships come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
