The Pros and Cons of Dijkstra's Algorithm

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Dijkstra's Algorithm is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports route planning
- Makes network routing easier
- Helps with game pathfinding

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using it with negative edge weights
- Risk: skipping the priority queue and getting slow code
- Risk: not stopping early when only one target is needed

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as route planning, and you can manage the risks, Dijkstra's Algorithm is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue.
