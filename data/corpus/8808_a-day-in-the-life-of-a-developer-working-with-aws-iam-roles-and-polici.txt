A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with AWS IAM Roles and Policies

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on AWS IAM Roles and Policies.

Morning: Imran starts by reviewing a ticket about securing deployments. Before touching any code, Imran re-reads the notes on users and roles to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Imran pairs with a teammate to explore policies with IAM Access Analyzer. A small test exposes the problem of wildcard permissions, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving granting services access. Imran sketches key cards that open only the doors a person needs on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Imran writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.

Days like this are typical. AWS IAM Roles and Policies is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
