Practice Exercises for Bluetooth Low Energy

These exercises build real understanding of Bluetooth Low Energy. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: ignoring connection limits. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble fitness trackers or smart locks.
Exercise 3: Pick nRF Connect and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Bluetooth Low Energy and include the term advertising.
Exercise 5: Explain how characteristics and pairing differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.
