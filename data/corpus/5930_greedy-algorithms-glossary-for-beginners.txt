Greedy Algorithms Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Greedy Algorithms feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Greedy Algorithms: algorithms that build a solution by always taking the locally best choice.
greedy choice property: a core term, most visible in interval scheduling.
optimal substructure: a core term that beginners often confuse with exchange arguments.
exchange arguments: a core term that connects to local versus global optimum.
local versus global optimum: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
pen and paper proofs: a tool commonly used for Huffman coding.
