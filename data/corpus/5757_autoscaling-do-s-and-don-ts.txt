Autoscaling Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Autoscaling.

Do:
- Learn scaling metrics before anything else.
- Try Kubernetes HPA on a small example.
- Make it a habit to load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
- Test the case of handling traffic spikes early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: scaling too slowly.
- Ignore this: flapping because of short spikes.
- Ignore this: ignoring database limits.
- Adopt Autoscaling just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Autoscaling, a little patience with cooldown periods pays off more than any clever shortcut.
