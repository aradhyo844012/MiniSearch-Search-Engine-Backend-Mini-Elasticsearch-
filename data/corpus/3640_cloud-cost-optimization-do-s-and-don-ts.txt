Cloud Cost Optimization Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Cloud Cost Optimization.

Do:
- Learn right-sizing before anything else.
- Try AWS Cost Explorer on a small example.
- Make it a habit to tag every resource with an owner.
- Test the case of cutting idle resources early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: forgotten test environments.
- Ignore this: over-provisioned databases.
- Ignore this: surprise data transfer charges.
- Adopt Cloud Cost Optimization just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Cloud Cost Optimization, a little patience with reserved capacity pays off more than any clever shortcut.
