Reverse Proxies in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Reverse Proxies.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn upstream servers, proxy headers, TLS termination, and caching.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through hiding backend servers, terminating HTTPS, and routing requests to several apps.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for losing the client IP address and wrong timeouts, then try NGINX for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
