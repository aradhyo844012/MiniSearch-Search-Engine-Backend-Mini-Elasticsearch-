Everything You Need to Know About MQTT

This page collects the essentials about MQTT in one place. In short, MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.

Core concepts:
- publish and subscribe
- topics
- brokers
- quality of service levels

Where it is used:
- sensor networks
- smart home devices
- telemetry

Common problems:
- unsecured brokers
- messy topic names
- wrong quality of service choices

Tools to know:
- Mosquitto
- paho-mqtt
- MQTT Explorer

Remember this above all: a radio station with channels that devices can tune into is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to design topic names like a clear folder structure.
