Choosing Tools for Internationalization

There is no single best tool for Internationalization. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

i18next is a common first choice, especially for global products. the Intl API suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Crowdin is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle multi-language websites well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: hard-coded strings.

As a rule of thumb, move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day, no matter which tool you use.
