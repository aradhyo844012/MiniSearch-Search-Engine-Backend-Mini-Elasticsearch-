GPUs and Parallelism for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover GPUs and Parallelism.

Learning goals:
1. Explain GPUs and Parallelism in your own words.
2. Describe threads and blocks, kernels, memory transfers, and SIMD.
3. Give examples from training neural networks and rendering graphics.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: GPUs and Parallelism is processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once. Then draw a stadium of workers each doing one small identical task on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is slow data transfers a problem? How does kernels relate to memory transfers? When would you choose CUDA or PyTorch?

Study habit: minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory.

When you feel ready, compare GPUs and Parallelism with Endianness and write down three differences.
