gRPC Glossary for Beginners

New words can make gRPC feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

gRPC: a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts.
protocol buffers: a core term, most visible in service-to-service calls.
service definitions: a core term that beginners often confuse with streaming.
streaming: a core term that connects to HTTP/2.
HTTP/2: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
protoc: a tool commonly used for low-latency APIs.

Come back to this gRPC list whenever documentation for grpcurl uses a word you do not recognize.
