Mistakes to Avoid with Monorepos

Everyone learns Monorepos partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow checkouts. It usually appears when someone skips shared tooling and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unclear ownership. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sharing libraries across apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: CI that rebuilds everything. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on atomic changes to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: set up selective builds and tests early.
