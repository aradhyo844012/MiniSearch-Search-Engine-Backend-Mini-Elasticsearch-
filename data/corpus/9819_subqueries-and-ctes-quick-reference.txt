Subqueries and CTEs Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Subqueries and CTEs. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.
Key terms: subqueries, WITH clauses, correlated queries, and readability.
Typical uses: breaking complex reports into steps, filtering with computed results, and walking recursive hierarchies.
Risks: correlated subqueries that run slowly, deep nesting, and assuming CTEs are always materialized.
Tools: PostgreSQL, SQLite, and dbt.

Mental picture: solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers.

Habit to build: name each CTE after what it returns.
