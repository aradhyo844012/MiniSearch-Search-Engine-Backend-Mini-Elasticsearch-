Graph Databases Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Graph Databases. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.
Key terms: nodes, edges, properties, and traversal queries.
Typical uses: social networks, recommendation engines, and fraud detection.
Risks: using them for simple tabular data, unbounded traversals, and supernodes with millions of edges.
Tools: Neo4j, Cypher, and Amazon Neptune.

Mental picture: a city map where intersections are records and roads are relationships.

Habit to build: limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
