The Pros and Cons of Probability Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Probability Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports randomized algorithms
- Makes machine learning easier
- Helps with risk estimates

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: confusing independent and exclusive events
- Risk: ignoring base rates
- Risk: falling for the gambler's fallacy

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as randomized algorithms, and you can manage the risks, Probability Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.
