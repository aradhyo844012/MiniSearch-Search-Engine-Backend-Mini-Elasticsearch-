Internationalization in Real Projects

Reading about Internationalization is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: global products. Here, locale detection drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against hard-coded strings.

Scenario two: multi-language websites. This time translation files matters most, and tools such as i18next help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: regional pricing. The key idea is pluralization, and the biggest risk is text that overflows layouts.

Across all three scenarios, Internationalization stays the same idea: designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions. Only the details change.
