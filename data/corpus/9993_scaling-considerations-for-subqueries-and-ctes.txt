Scaling Considerations for Subqueries and CTEs

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Subqueries and CTEs changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding subqueries and getting breaking complex reports into steps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for correlated subqueries that run slowly and deep nesting, and measure with PostgreSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: correlated queries and readability move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around filtering with computed results at this stage.

Before you scale, name each CTE after what it returns. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
