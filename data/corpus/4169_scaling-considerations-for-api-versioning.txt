Scaling Considerations for API Versioning

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how API Versioning changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding version in the URL and getting public APIs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for versioning too often and removing fields without notice, and measure with OpenAPI before changing anything.

Large scale: deprecation and backward compatibility move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around mobile apps that cannot update instantly at this stage.

Before you scale, announce deprecations with a clear sunset date. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
