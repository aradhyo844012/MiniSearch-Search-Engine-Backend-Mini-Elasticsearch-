System Calls Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with System Calls.

Do:
- Learn user mode and kernel mode before anything else.
- Try strace on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do.
- Test the case of reading and writing files early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring error codes.
- Ignore this: making too many small calls.
- Ignore this: unsafe assumptions about blocking.
- Adopt System Calls just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with System Calls, a little patience with syscall numbers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
