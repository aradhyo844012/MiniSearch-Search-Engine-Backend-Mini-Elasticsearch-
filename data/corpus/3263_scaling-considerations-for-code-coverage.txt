Scaling Considerations for Code Coverage

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Code Coverage changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding line coverage and getting finding untested code right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for chasing 100 percent and treating coverage as quality, and measure with coverage.py before changing anything.

Large scale: coverage reports and thresholds move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around setting quality gates at this stage.

Before you scale, use coverage to find gaps, not to judge quality. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
