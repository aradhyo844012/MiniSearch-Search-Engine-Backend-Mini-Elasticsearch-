Combining Canary Releases with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Canary Releases and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Canary Releases is a rollout strategy that sends a small share of traffic to a new version before everyone gets it. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves safer releases and surviving regional outages. Canary Releases brings traffic percentages, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with Argo Rollouts for the Canary Releases part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: no clear success metrics on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as a canary in a coal mine warning miners before the danger spreads, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, define rollback conditions before starting the rollout; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
