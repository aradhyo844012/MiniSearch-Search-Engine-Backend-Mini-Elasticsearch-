gRPC Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with gRPC regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose gRPC so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: breaking changes in message fields.
3. Test the case of service-to-service calls early, before the design hardens.
4. Reach for protoc first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as grpcurl.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit service definitions before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to low-latency APIs as well.
