Mistakes to Avoid with Model Deployment

Everyone learns Model Deployment partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: training-serving mismatch. It usually appears when someone skips serving APIs and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow cold starts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of recommendation endpoints. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no fallback when the model fails. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on latency budgets to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
