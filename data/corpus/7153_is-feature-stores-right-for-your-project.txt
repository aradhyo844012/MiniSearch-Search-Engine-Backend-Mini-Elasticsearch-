Is Feature Stores Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Feature Stores, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Feature Stores is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models.

It is a good fit when your work involves sharing features across models or serving real-time predictions, and when your team understands offline store.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn online store, or when you cannot manage these risks: leaking future data, duplicate definitions, and stale online values.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether point-in-time correctness and feature definitions are part of the answer. If they are, Feature Stores is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with avoiding training-serving skew and define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
