Debugging Data Quality Checks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Data Quality Checks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: alerts nobody reads, checks only at the end of pipelines, and no ownership of failures.
Step 3: revisit null checks and uniqueness, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Great Expectations or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Data Quality Checks in work such as protecting dashboards come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, check data as early as it enters the pipeline. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
