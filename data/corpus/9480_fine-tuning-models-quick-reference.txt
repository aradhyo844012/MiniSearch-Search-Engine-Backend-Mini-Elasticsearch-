Fine-Tuning Models Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Fine-Tuning Models. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: adapting a pretrained model to a specific task with extra training on your own data.
Key terms: pretrained weights, training data, the learning rate, and evaluation.
Typical uses: domain-specific language tasks, style adaptation, and improving accuracy on narrow problems.
Risks: overfitting on small datasets, forgetting general abilities, and low-quality training data.
Tools: Hugging Face, PyTorch, and LoRA libraries.

Mental picture: teaching an experienced chef a new regional cuisine.

Habit to build: start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
