A Practical Checklist for Android Basics

Use this checklist before you rely on Android Basics in a project.

1. Measure results before and after any change.
2. Guard against this risk: ignoring lifecycle events.
3. Review how Android Basics interacts with Mobile App Performance.
4. Guard against this risk: hard-coded layouts.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example building simple apps.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Android Basics should accomplish.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers activities.
8. Document your decisions in the project README.
