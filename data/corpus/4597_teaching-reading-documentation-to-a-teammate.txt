Teaching Reading Documentation to a Teammate

Explaining Reading Documentation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: reading the manual before assembling the furniture. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Reading Documentation is the skill of finding accurate answers in official guides and references. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving learning new libraries, naming search and tutorials versus references as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain examples in Reading Documentation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, reading the wrong version, and suggest this habit: check that the documentation version matches your installed version.
