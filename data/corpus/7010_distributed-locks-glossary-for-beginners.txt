Distributed Locks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Distributed Locks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Distributed Locks: mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines.
lease times: a core term, most visible in running scheduled jobs once.
fencing tokens: a core term that beginners often confuse with lock owners.
lock owners: a core term that connects to timeouts.
timeouts: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Redis: a tool commonly used for protecting shared resources.

Come back to this Distributed Locks list whenever documentation for ZooKeeper uses a word you do not recognize.
