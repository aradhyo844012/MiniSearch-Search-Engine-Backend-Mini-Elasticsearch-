Data Quality Checks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Data Quality Checks.

Do:
- Learn null checks before anything else.
- Try Great Expectations on a small example.
- Make it a habit to check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
- Test the case of protecting dashboards early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: alerts nobody reads.
- Ignore this: checks only at the end of pipelines.
- Ignore this: no ownership of failures.
- Adopt Data Quality Checks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Data Quality Checks, a little patience with uniqueness pays off more than any clever shortcut.
