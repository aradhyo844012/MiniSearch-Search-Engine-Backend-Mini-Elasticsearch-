Background Jobs in Real Projects

Reading about Background Jobs is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: sending emails. Here, queues drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against jobs that are not idempotent.

Scenario two: resizing images. This time workers matters most, and tools such as Celery help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: generating reports. The key idea is retries, and the biggest risk is unbounded retries.

Across all three scenarios, Background Jobs stays the same idea: tasks that run outside the request cycle so users do not have to wait for them. Only the details change.
