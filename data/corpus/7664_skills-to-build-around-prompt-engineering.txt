Skills to Build Around Prompt Engineering

Knowing the definition of Prompt Engineering is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with instructions and examples, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with model playgrounds on a Prompt Engineering task, and try at least one alternative such as LangChain.

Judgment: learn to decide when Prompt Engineering fits a problem such as drafting content, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example giving clear directions to a very literal helper.

Habits: specify the output format and include one or two examples, and write down what you learn after each project.
