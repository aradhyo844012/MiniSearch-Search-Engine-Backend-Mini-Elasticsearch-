Speech Recognition Explained Simply

Here is Speech Recognition without the jargon: it is converting spoken language into text using machine learning.

Think of Speech Recognition as a stenographer who types as people talk. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: audio features, acoustic models, language models, and word error rate. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with audio features, then add acoustic models.

You will meet Speech Recognition in work such as voice assistants, captioning, and dictation.

One practical tip is to measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.

After Speech Recognition, a natural next topic is AI Agents.
