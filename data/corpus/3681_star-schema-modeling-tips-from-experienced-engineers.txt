Star Schema Modeling Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Star Schema Modeling regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Star Schema Modeling so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: mixing different grains.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit dimension tables before anything else.
4. Test the case of business intelligence reports early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to data warehouses as well.
