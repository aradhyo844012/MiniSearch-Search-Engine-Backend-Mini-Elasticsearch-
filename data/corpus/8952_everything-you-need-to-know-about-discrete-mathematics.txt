Everything You Need to Know About Discrete Mathematics

This page collects the essentials about Discrete Mathematics in one place. In short, Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.

Core concepts:
- sets
- logic
- relations
- proofs

Where it is used:
- algorithm analysis
- database theory
- cryptography

Common problems:
- skipping proofs
- confusing sets with sequences
- careless notation

Tools to know:
- Python sets
- proof assistants
- textbooks and problem sets

Remember this above all: the grammar underneath every program is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to practice writing small proofs by hand.
