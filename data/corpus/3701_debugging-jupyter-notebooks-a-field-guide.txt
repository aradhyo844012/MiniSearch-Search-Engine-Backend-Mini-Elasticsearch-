Debugging Jupyter Notebooks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Jupyter Notebooks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: running cells out of order, hidden state, and committing outputs that contain secrets.
Step 3: revisit cells and kernels, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use JupyterLab or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Jupyter Notebooks in work such as exploring data come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
