Physics Engines in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Physics Engines.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn rigid bodies, forces and impulses, constraints, and fixed time steps.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through vehicles and ragdolls, puzzle games, and training simulations.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for variable time steps and unstable stacks of objects, then try Box2D for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use a fixed time step for stable results.
