MQTT Glossary for Beginners

New words can make MQTT feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

MQTT: a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.
publish and subscribe: a core term, most visible in sensor networks.
topics: a core term that beginners often confuse with brokers.
brokers: a core term that connects to quality of service levels.
quality of service levels: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Mosquitto: a tool commonly used for smart home devices.

Come back to this MQTT list whenever documentation for paho-mqtt uses a word you do not recognize.
