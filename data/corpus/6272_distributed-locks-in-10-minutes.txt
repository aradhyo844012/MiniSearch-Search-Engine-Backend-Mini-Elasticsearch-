Distributed Locks in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Distributed Locks.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Distributed Locks is mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn lease times, fencing tokens, lock owners, and timeouts.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a single key to a shared meeting room that expires automatically. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through running scheduled jobs once, protecting shared resources, and selecting a leader.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for locks that never expire and clock drift, then try Redis for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
