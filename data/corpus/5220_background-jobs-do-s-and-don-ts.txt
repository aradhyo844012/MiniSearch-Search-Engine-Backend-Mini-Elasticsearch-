Background Jobs Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Background Jobs.

Do:
- Learn queues before anything else.
- Try Celery on a small example.
- Make it a habit to give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
- Test the case of sending emails early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: jobs that are not idempotent.
- Ignore this: unbounded retries.
- Ignore this: losing jobs on restarts.
- Adopt Background Jobs just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Background Jobs, a little patience with workers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
