The Pros and Cons of Domain-Driven Design

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Domain-Driven Design is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports complex business systems
- Makes aligning developers with domain experts easier
- Helps with splitting large systems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using it for simple CRUD apps
- Risk: models that never match the business
- Risk: ignoring context boundaries

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as complex business systems, and you can manage the risks, Domain-Driven Design is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes.
