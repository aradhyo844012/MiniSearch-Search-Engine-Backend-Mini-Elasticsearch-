Containers: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Containers?
A: The term Containers refers to lightweight packages that bundle an application with its dependencies and run in isolation.

Q: How does it relate to File Systems?
A: Containers and File Systems often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Operating Systems.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Docker, Podman, and containerd.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Containers.

Q: Why do developers use Containers?
A: Common uses include consistent development environments, deploying microservices, and running tests in CI.
