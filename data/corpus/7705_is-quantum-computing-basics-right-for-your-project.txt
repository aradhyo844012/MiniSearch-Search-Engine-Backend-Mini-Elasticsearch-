Is Quantum Computing Basics Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Quantum Computing Basics, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems.

It is a good fit when your work involves research on cryptography or simulating molecules, and when your team understands qubits.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn superposition, or when you cannot manage these risks: expecting speedups for every problem, noise and errors, and hype about current machines.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether entanglement and quantum gates are part of the answer. If they are, Quantum Computing Basics is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with optimization studies and start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
