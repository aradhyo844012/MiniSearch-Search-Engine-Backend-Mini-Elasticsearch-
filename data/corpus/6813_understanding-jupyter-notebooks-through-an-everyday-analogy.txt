Understanding Jupyter Notebooks Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a lab notebook that can run its own experiments.

In that picture, cells is the first rule you follow, kernels is the next step, and markdown and outputs keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Jupyter Notebooks is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes, and it involves details such as running cells out of order that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for JupyterLab to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain exploring data to a friend.
