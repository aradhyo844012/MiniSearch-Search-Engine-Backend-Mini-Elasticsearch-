Everything You Need to Know About Eventual Consistency

This page collects the essentials about Eventual Consistency in one place. In short, Eventual Consistency is a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough.

Core concepts:
- replication delay
- conflict resolution
- read-your-writes
- convergence

Where it is used:
- global social feeds
- shopping cart services
- DNS records

Common problems:
- assuming reads are always fresh
- unresolved write conflicts
- no user feedback during delays

Tools to know:
- Amazon DynamoDB
- Apache Cassandra
- CRDT libraries

Remember this above all: news spreading through a village until everyone has heard it is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
