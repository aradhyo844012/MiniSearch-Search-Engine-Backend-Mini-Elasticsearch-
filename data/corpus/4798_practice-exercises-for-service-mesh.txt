Practice Exercises for Service Mesh

These exercises build real understanding of Service Mesh. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: added latency and complexity. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick Istio and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble securing service traffic or retries and timeouts.
Exercise 4: Explain how traffic policies and observability differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Service Mesh and include the term sidecar proxies.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.
