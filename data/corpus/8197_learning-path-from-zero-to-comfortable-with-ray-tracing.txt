Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Ray Tracing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Ray Tracing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images) and learn rays and intersections.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Blender Cycles, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving movie visuals.

Stage 3, depth: study reflection and refraction and shadows, then deliberately explore this problem: slow render times.

Stage 4, application: apply Ray Tracing to architectural visualization, compare NVIDIA OptiX with POV-Ray, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Ray Tracing to someone else, and then move on to Vector Graphics (SVG).
