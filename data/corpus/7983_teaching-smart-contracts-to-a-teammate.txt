Teaching Smart Contracts to a Teammate

Explaining Smart Contracts to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a vending machine that follows its rules with no clerk. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving token systems, naming contract code and transactions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain gas fees in Smart Contracts back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, bugs that cannot be patched, and suggest this habit: audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
