Myths and Facts About Data Cleaning

A lot of folklore has built up around Data Cleaning. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. Many students first meet Data Cleaning in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data.

If you are comfortable with Data Cleaning, explore Pandas DataFrames next. The two often appear in the same project.

Myth: Data Cleaning is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand missing values and duplicates.

Myth: Data Cleaning is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as preparing logs benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: pandas, OpenRefine, and regular expressions make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Data Cleaning are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example dropping data without checking why it is missing.

In short, careful study of Data Cleaning pays off. Spend time on missing values and duplicates, and the rest will follow.
