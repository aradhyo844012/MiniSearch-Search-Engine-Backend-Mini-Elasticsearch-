Scaling Considerations for Idempotency

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Idempotency changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding idempotency keys and getting payment APIs right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for keys that expire too soon and side effects outside the guarded step, and measure with Stripe idempotency keys before changing anything.

Large scale: deduplication and at-least-once delivery move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around message processing at this stage.

Before you scale, store the result with the key so retries return the same response. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
