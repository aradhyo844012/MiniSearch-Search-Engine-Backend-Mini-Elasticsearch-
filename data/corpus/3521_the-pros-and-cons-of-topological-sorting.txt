The Pros and Cons of Topological Sorting

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Topological Sorting is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports build systems
- Makes course prerequisite planning easier
- Helps with task scheduling

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring cycles
- Risk: assuming the order is unique
- Risk: mixing up edge directions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as build systems, and you can manage the risks, Topological Sorting is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, check for cycles first so you can report a clear error.
