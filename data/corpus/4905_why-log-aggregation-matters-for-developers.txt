Why Log Aggregation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Log Aggregation make better decisions, because Log Aggregation is collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place.

Three situations show why it matters. First, troubleshooting across services. Second, security investigations. Third, usage analysis. In each case, a solid grasp of shippers and indexing separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: unbounded storage growth, unstructured logs, and missing timestamps.

Good tools exist, including Elasticsearch, Fluent Bit, and Grafana Loki, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
