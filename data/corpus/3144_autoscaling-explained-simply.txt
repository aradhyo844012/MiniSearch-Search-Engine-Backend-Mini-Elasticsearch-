Autoscaling Explained Simply

Here is Autoscaling without the jargon: it is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Think of Autoscaling as a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: scaling metrics, cooldown periods, minimum and maximum limits, and horizontal scaling. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with scaling metrics, then add cooldown periods.

People reach for Autoscaling when they work on handling traffic spikes, saving money overnight, and keeping latency steady.

One practical tip is to load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
