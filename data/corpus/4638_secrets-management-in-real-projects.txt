Secrets Management in Real Projects

Reading about Secrets Management is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: database credentials. Here, vaults drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against secrets in source control.

Scenario two: API keys. This time rotation matters most, and tools such as HashiCorp Vault help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: TLS certificates. The key idea is access policies, and the biggest risk is long-lived shared credentials.

Across all three scenarios, Secrets Management stays the same idea: storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely. Only the details change.
