Disk Partitioning and Mounting in Real Projects

Reading about Disk Partitioning and Mounting is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: adding a new drive. Here, partitions drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against wrong device names.

Scenario two: separating data from the system. This time file system types matters most, and tools such as lsblk help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: encrypting volumes. The key idea is mount points, and the biggest risk is mounting without fstab entries.

Across all three scenarios, Disk Partitioning and Mounting stays the same idea: dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree. Only the details change.
