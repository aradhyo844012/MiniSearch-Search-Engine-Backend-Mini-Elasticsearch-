How to Learn Data Visualization in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Data Visualization. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Data Visualization covers presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn chart types and axes and labels, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with matplotlib on a small task such as explaining results.
Week 3: build a mini project around exploring data.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as truncated axes, and explain Data Visualization to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly.

Data Visualization and Stream Processing are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Data Engineering.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Data Visualization.
