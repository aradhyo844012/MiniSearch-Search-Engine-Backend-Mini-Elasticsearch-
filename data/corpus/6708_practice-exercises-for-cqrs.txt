Practice Exercises for CQRS

These exercises build real understanding of CQRS. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble high-read dashboards or complex domains.
Exercise 2: Pick Axon Framework and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Explain how read models and eventual consistency differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Compare MediatR and Apache Kafka for scaling reads independently and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: adding it to simple apps. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.
