Data Augmentation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Data Augmentation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Data Augmentation: creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.
transformations: a core term, most visible in image models.
label preservation: a core term that beginners often confuse with synthetic samples.
synthetic samples: a core term that connects to augmentation pipelines.
augmentation pipelines: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Albumentations: a tool commonly used for text classification.

Come back to this Data Augmentation list whenever documentation for torchvision uses a word you do not recognize.
