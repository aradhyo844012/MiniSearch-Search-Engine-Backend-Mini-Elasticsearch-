The Pros and Cons of Property-Based Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Property-Based Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports testing parsers
- Makes checking sorting and math functions easier
- Helps with finding edge cases

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: vague properties
- Risk: slow generators
- Risk: ignoring random seed reports

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as testing parsers, and you can manage the risks, Property-Based Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, save failing examples as regular tests.
