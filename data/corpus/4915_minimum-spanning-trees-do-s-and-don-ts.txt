Minimum Spanning Trees Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Minimum Spanning Trees.

Do:
- Learn weighted graphs before anything else.
- Try NetworkX on a small example.
- Make it a habit to sort the edges once and reuse the order when testing variations.
- Test the case of designing cable networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using it on directed graphs.
- Ignore this: forgetting that the graph must be connected.
- Ignore this: treating ties as errors.
- Adopt Minimum Spanning Trees just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Minimum Spanning Trees, a little patience with Kruskal's algorithm pays off more than any clever shortcut.
