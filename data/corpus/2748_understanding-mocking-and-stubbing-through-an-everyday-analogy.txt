Understanding Mocking and Stubbing Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger.

In that picture, mocks is the first rule you follow, stubs is the next step, and spies and fakes keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests, and it involves details such as over-mocking that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for unittest.mock to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain isolating units to a friend.
