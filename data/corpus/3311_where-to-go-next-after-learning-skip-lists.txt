Where to Go Next After Learning Skip Lists

You have met the basics of Skip Lists. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Backtracking, which belongs to the same area as Skip Lists (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of multiple levels, random level assignment, forward pointers or expected logarithmic time, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around sorted sets in caches with Redis sorted sets, and note every problem you meet, starting with poor random number choices.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Skip Lists to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
