Combining Feature Flags with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Feature Flags and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves dark launches and surviving regional outages. Feature Flags brings flag evaluation, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with LaunchDarkly for the Feature Flags part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: flags that depend on each other on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as light switches wired to every room of a house, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, give every flag an owner and a removal date; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
