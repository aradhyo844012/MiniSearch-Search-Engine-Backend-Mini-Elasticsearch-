Skills to Build Around Ray Tracing

Knowing the definition of Ray Tracing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with rays and intersections, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Blender Cycles on a Ray Tracing task, and try at least one alternative such as NVIDIA OptiX.

Judgment: learn to decide when Ray Tracing fits a problem such as movie visuals, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene.

Habits: start with spheres and a single light before adding features, and write down what you learn after each project.
