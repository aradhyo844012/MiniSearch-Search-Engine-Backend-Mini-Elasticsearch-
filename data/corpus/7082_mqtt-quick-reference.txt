MQTT Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for MQTT. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.
Key terms: publish and subscribe, topics, brokers, and quality of service levels.
Typical uses: sensor networks, smart home devices, and telemetry.
Risks: unsecured brokers, messy topic names, and wrong quality of service choices.
Tools: Mosquitto, paho-mqtt, and MQTT Explorer.

Mental picture: a radio station with channels that devices can tune into.

Habit to build: design topic names like a clear folder structure.

See also: Push Notifications, which sits close to MQTT in the area of Mobile, Embedded and IoT.
