Choosing Tools for Connection Pooling

There is no single best tool for Connection Pooling. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PgBouncer is a common first choice, especially for web backends. HikariCP suits teams that already work with similar technology, and SQLAlchemy pools is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle serverless functions that call databases well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: pools larger than the database allows.

As a rule of thumb, always return connections in a finally block, no matter which tool you use.
