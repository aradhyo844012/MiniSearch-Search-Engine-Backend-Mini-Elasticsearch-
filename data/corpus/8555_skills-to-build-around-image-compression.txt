Skills to Build Around Image Compression

Knowing the definition of Image Compression is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with lossy and lossless formats and quantization, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with ImageMagick on a Image Compression task, and try at least one alternative such as mozjpeg.

Judgment: learn to decide when Image Compression fits a problem such as web images, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly.

Habits: pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics, and write down what you learn after each project.
