Internationalization Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Internationalization.

Do:
- Learn locale detection before anything else.
- Try i18next on a small example.
- Make it a habit to move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
- Test the case of global products early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hard-coded strings.
- Ignore this: text that overflows layouts.
- Ignore this: assuming left-to-right text.
- Adopt Internationalization just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Internationalization, a little patience with translation files pays off more than any clever shortcut.
