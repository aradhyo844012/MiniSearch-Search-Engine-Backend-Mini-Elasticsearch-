Why Graph Databases Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Graph Databases make better decisions, because Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.

Three situations show why it matters. First, social networks. Second, recommendation engines. Third, fraud detection. In each case, a solid grasp of nodes and edges separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: using them for simple tabular data, unbounded traversals, and supernodes with millions of edges.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Neo4j, Cypher, and Amazon Neptune cover most needs.
