Choosing Tools for Virtual Private Clouds

There is no single best tool for Virtual Private Clouds. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

AWS VPC is a common first choice, especially for separating public and private services. Terraform suits teams that already work with similar technology, and VPC Flow Logs is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Virtual Private Clouds by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle connecting to offices well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Virtual Private Clouds, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: overlapping address ranges.

As a rule of thumb, keep databases in private subnets, no matter which tool you use.
