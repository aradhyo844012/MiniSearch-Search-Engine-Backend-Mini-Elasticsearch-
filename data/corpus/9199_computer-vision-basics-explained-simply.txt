Computer Vision Basics Explained Simply

If Computer Vision Basics sounds intimidating, relax. It is teaching computers to extract information from images and video, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Computer Vision Basics as teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: pixels, filters and edges, feature detection, and classification. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with pixels, then add filters and edges.

People reach for Computer Vision Basics when they work on face detection, document scanning, and quality inspection.

One practical tip is to visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.

Once Computer Vision Basics feels comfortable, read about Video Codecs, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
