Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Push Notifications

This path is for a learner who has never studied Push Notifications before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed) and learn device tokens and push services.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Firebase Cloud Messaging, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving order updates.

Stage 3, depth: study payloads and permissions, then deliberately explore this problem: asking for permission too early.

Stage 4, application: apply Push Notifications to chat alerts, compare Apple Push Notification service with OneSignal, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Push Notifications to someone else, and then move on to Arduino Basics.
