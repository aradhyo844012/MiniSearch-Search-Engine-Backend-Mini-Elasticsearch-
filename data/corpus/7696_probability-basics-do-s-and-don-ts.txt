Probability Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Probability Basics.

Do:
- Learn events before anything else.
- Try Python random on a small example.
- Make it a habit to simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.
- Test the case of randomized algorithms early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: confusing independent and exclusive events.
- Ignore this: ignoring base rates.
- Ignore this: falling for the gambler's fallacy.
- Adopt Probability Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Probability Basics, a little patience with conditional probability pays off more than any clever shortcut.
