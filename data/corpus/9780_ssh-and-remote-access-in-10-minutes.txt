SSH and Remote Access in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through SSH and Remote Access.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: SSH and Remote Access is a secure protocol for logging in to and controlling remote machines.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn key pairs, host keys, port forwarding, and config files.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a private phone line with a secret handshake to a faraway office. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through administering servers, copying files securely, and tunneling to private services.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for password logins left enabled and sharing private keys, then try OpenSSH for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use key-based login and disable password authentication.
