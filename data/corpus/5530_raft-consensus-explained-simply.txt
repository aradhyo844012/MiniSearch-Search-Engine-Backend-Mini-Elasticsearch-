Raft Consensus Explained Simply

If Raft Consensus sounds intimidating, relax. It is an algorithm that lets a group of servers agree on a shared log of operations, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Raft Consensus as a committee that elects a chair and only records decisions most members confirm. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: leader election, log replication, terms, and majority quorum. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with leader election, then add log replication.

You will meet Raft Consensus in work such as configuration stores, distributed locks, and replicated state machines.

One practical tip is to use an odd number of nodes.

After Raft Consensus, a natural next topic is Distributed Locks.
