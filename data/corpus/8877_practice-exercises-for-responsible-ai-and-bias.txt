Practice Exercises for Responsible AI and Bias

These exercises build real understanding of Responsible AI and Bias. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare AI Fairness 360 and model cards for healthcare decisions and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Responsible AI and Bias and include the term bias in data.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble hiring tools or lending models.
Exercise 4: Explain how transparency and human oversight differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: assuming data is neutral. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.
