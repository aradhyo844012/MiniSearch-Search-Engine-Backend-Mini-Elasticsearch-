Apache Spark Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Apache Spark regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit transformations and actions before anything else.
2. Test the case of large ETL jobs early, before the design hardens.
3. Write down why you chose Apache Spark so the next person understands the decision.
4. Make sure you can avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.
5. Do not skip DataFrames; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to machine learning on big data as well.
