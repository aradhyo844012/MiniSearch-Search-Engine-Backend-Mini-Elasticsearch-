Graph Theory Basics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Graph Theory Basics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: mixing directed and undirected graphs.
2. Make sure you can draw a small example before writing code.
3. Write down why you chose Graph Theory Basics so the next person understands the decision.
4. Reach for NetworkX first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Graphviz.
5. Test the case of social networks early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to maps as well as to Graph Theory Basics in general.
