Teaching Hyperparameter Tuning to a Teammate

Explaining Hyperparameter Tuning to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Hyperparameter Tuning is the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving improving model accuracy, naming search spaces and grid search as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain random search in Hyperparameter Tuning back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, tuning on the test set, and suggest this habit: try random search before anything fancier.
