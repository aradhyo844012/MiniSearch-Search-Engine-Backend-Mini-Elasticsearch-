Scaling Considerations for Vector Databases

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Vector Databases changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding embeddings and getting semantic search right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mixing embedding models and ignoring recall trade-offs, and measure with FAISS before changing anything.

Large scale: distance metrics and metadata filters move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around recommendations at this stage.

Before you scale, measure recall on a small labeled test set. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
