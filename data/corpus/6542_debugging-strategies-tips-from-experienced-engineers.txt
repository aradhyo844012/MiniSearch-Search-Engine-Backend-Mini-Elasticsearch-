Debugging Strategies Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Debugging Strategies regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of fixing production issues early, before the design hardens.
2. Reach for debuggers first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as logging.
3. Do not skip reproducing the bug; everything else builds on it.
4. Write down why you chose Debugging Strategies so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: changing many things at once.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to learning unfamiliar code as well.
