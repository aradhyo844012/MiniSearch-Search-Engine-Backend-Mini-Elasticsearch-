Teaching SQL Basics to a Teammate

Explaining SQL Basics to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: asking a very organized librarian for exactly the books you need. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: SQL Basics is the core SQL commands for reading and changing data in tables. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving reading customer records, naming SELECT and WHERE as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain ORDER BY in SQL Basics back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, forgetting the WHERE clause on updates, and suggest this habit: run a SELECT with the same WHERE clause before any UPDATE or DELETE.
