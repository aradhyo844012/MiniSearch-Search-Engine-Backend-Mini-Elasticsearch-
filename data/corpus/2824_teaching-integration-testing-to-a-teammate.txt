Teaching Integration Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Integration Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: checking that the gears of a watch mesh before closing the case. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Integration Testing is tests that check how several parts of a system work together. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving verifying API and database interaction, naming test databases and service boundaries as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain fixtures in Integration Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow suites, and suggest this habit: use disposable containers for test dependencies.
