Test-Driven Development Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Test-Driven Development.

Do:
- Learn red, green, refactor before anything else.
- Try pytest on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep each red-green cycle to a few minutes.
- Test the case of designing small modules early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: writing tests that are too large.
- Ignore this: skipping the refactor step.
- Ignore this: testing implementation details.
- Adopt Test-Driven Development just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Test-Driven Development, a little patience with small steps pays off more than any clever shortcut.
