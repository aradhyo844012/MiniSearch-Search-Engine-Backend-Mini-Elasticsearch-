Debugging Blockchain Fundamentals: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Blockchain Fundamentals, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: treating it as a database replacement, slow throughput, and the energy use of some designs.
Step 3: revisit blocks and hashes, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Bitcoin Core or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Blockchain Fundamentals in work such as digital currencies come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, ask whether a normal database would solve the problem. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
