Natural Language Processing Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Natural Language Processing.

- Definition: the field of teaching computers to work with human language.
- Core ideas: tokenization; embeddings; language models; evaluation metrics.
- Use it for: search engines; chatbots; sentiment analysis.
- Watch out for: ignoring language variety; messy text preprocessing; trusting metrics on tiny samples.
- Tools: spaCy, NLTK, Hugging Face.
- Quick tip: practice tokenization until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: Gradient Descent.
