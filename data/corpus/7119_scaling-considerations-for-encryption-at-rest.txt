Scaling Considerations for Encryption at Rest

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Encryption at Rest changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding symmetric keys and getting protecting backups right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for storing keys next to the data and no key rotation, and measure with LUKS before changing anything.

Large scale: disk encryption and envelope encryption move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around meeting compliance rules at this stage.

Before you scale, keep keys in a separate service from the data. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
