Where to Go Next After Learning Multi-Cloud Strategy

You have met the basics of Multi-Cloud Strategy. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about High Availability Design, which belongs to the same area as Multi-Cloud Strategy (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of portability, vendor lock-in, abstraction layers or cost comparison, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around meeting regional rules with Terraform, and note every problem you meet, starting with doubling operational complexity.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Multi-Cloud Strategy to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
