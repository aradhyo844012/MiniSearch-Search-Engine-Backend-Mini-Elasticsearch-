Scaling Considerations for gRPC

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how gRPC changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding protocol buffers and getting service-to-service calls right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for breaking changes in message fields and poor browser support without a proxy, and measure with protoc before changing anything.

Large scale: streaming and HTTP/2 move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around low-latency APIs at this stage.

Before you scale, never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
