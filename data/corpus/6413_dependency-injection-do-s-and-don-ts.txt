Dependency Injection Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Dependency Injection.

Do:
- Learn constructor injection before anything else.
- Try Spring on a small example.
- Make it a habit to inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
- Test the case of easier unit testing early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hiding too much behind containers.
- Ignore this: circular dependencies.
- Ignore this: injecting too many dependencies.
- Adopt Dependency Injection just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Dependency Injection, a little patience with interfaces pays off more than any clever shortcut.
