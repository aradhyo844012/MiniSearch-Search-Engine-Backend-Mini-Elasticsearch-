Teaching Feature Stores to a Teammate

Explaining Feature Stores to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a shared pantry of prepared ingredients for many recipes. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Feature Stores is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sharing features across models, naming offline store and online store as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain point-in-time correctness in Feature Stores back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, leaking future data, and suggest this habit: define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
