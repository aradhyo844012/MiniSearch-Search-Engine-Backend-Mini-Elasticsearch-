Debugging Sprite Animation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Sprite Animation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: inconsistent frame timing, huge uncompressed sheets, and animation states that never end.
Step 3: revisit sprite sheets and frames, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Aseprite or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Sprite Animation in work such as 2D platformers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
