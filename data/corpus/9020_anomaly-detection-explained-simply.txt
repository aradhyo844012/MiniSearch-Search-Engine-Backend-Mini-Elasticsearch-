Anomaly Detection Explained Simply

Here is Anomaly Detection without the jargon: it is finding data points or events that differ sharply from normal behavior.

Think of Anomaly Detection as a security guard who notices the one person acting differently. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: baselines, thresholds, isolation methods, and false positives. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with baselines, then add thresholds.

People reach for Anomaly Detection when they work on fraud detection, server monitoring, and manufacturing quality.

One practical tip is to tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
