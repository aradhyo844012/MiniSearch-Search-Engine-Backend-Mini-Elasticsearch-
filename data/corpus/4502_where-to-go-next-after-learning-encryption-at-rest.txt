Where to Go Next After Learning Encryption at Rest

You have met the basics of Encryption at Rest. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Single Sign-On, which belongs to the same area as Encryption at Rest (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of symmetric keys, key management, disk encryption or envelope encryption, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around meeting compliance rules with LUKS, and note every problem you meet, starting with storing keys next to the data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Encryption at Rest to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
