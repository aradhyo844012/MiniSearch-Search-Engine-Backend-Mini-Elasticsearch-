The Pros and Cons of Backtracking

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Backtracking is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports solving Sudoku
- Makes generating permutations easier
- Helps with N-Queens puzzles

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: forgetting to undo changes
- Risk: missing pruning rules
- Risk: copying large state at every step

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as solving Sudoku, and you can manage the risks, Backtracking is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
