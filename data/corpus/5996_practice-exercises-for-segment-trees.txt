Practice Exercises for Segment Trees

These exercises build real understanding of Segment Trees. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble range sum queries or finding minimums in a window.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: off-by-one errors in ranges. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Segment Trees and include the term tree nodes covering ranges.
Exercise 4: Compare Python lists and Codeforces problem sets for competitive programming problems and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how point updates and lazy propagation differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Segment Trees is a tree structure that answers range queries and handles updates in logarithmic time.
