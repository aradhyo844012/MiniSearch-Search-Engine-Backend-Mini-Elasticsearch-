Everything You Need to Know About Process Signals and Job Control

This page collects the essentials about Process Signals and Job Control in one place. In short, Process Signals and Job Control is messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs.

Core concepts:
- SIGTERM and SIGKILL
- foreground and background jobs
- kill and trap
- process groups

Where it is used:
- stopping runaway programs
- shutting down gracefully
- running long tasks in the background

Common problems:
- using kill -9 first
- orphaned child processes
- ignoring cleanup in handlers

Tools to know:
- kill
- jobs and fg
- nohup

Remember this above all: a traffic officer's hand signals for running programs is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.
