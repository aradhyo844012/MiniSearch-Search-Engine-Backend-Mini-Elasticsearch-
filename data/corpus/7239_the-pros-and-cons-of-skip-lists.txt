The Pros and Cons of Skip Lists

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Skip Lists is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports ordered in-memory indexes
- Makes sorted sets in caches easier
- Helps with concurrent ordered maps

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: poor random number choices
- Risk: memory overhead
- Risk: harder debugging than arrays

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as ordered in-memory indexes, and you can manage the risks, Skip Lists is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, draw several levels by hand to see how a search moves down.
