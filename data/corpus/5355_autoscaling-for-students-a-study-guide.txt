Autoscaling for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Autoscaling.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Autoscaling in your own words.
2. Describe scaling metrics, cooldown periods, minimum and maximum limits, and horizontal scaling.
3. Give examples from handling traffic spikes and saving money overnight.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes. Then draw a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is scaling too slowly a problem? How does cooldown periods relate to minimum and maximum limits? When would you choose Kubernetes HPA or AWS Auto Scaling?

Study habit: load test the system to find realistic thresholds.

When you feel ready, compare Autoscaling with Blue-Green Deployment and write down three differences.
