Anomaly Detection Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Anomaly Detection.

Do:
- Learn baselines before anything else.
- Try scikit-learn on a small example.
- Make it a habit to tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.
- Test the case of fraud detection early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too many false alarms.
- Ignore this: shifting baselines.
- Ignore this: labeling normal data incorrectly.
- Adopt Anomaly Detection just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Anomaly Detection, a little patience with thresholds pays off more than any clever shortcut.
