TCP and UDP Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about TCP and UDP.

Q: Explain TCP and UDP in simple words.
A: TCP and UDP refers to the two main transport protocols, one reliable and ordered and the other fast and connectionless. I would add an example involving web traffic.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: assuming UDP guarantees delivery.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: handshakes, acknowledgements, ports, and congestion control.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with video calls, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to WebSockets?
A: Both belong to the area of Networking, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Wireshark, netcat, and tcpdump.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as handshakes, and add tests aimed at the known risks.
