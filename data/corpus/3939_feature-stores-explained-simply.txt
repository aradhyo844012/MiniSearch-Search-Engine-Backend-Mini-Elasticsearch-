Feature Stores Explained Simply

If Feature Stores sounds intimidating, relax. It is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Feature Stores as a shared pantry of prepared ingredients for many recipes. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: offline store, online store, point-in-time correctness, and feature definitions. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with offline store, then add online store.

You will meet Feature Stores in work such as sharing features across models, serving real-time predictions, and avoiding training-serving skew.

One practical tip is to define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.

After Feature Stores, a natural next topic is Jupyter Notebooks.
