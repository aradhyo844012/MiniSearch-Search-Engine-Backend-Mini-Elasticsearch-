Practice Exercises for K-Means Clustering

These exercises build real understanding of K-Means Clustering. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: choosing k blindly. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick scikit-learn and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing K-Means Clustering and include the term centroids.
Exercise 4: Compare NumPy and matplotlib for grouping documents and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble customer segmentation or image compression.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: K-Means Clustering is an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points.
