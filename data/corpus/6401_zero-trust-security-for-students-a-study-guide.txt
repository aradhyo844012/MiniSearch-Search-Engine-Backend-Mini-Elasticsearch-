Zero Trust Security for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Zero Trust Security.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Zero Trust Security in your own words.
2. Describe identity verification, least privilege, micro-segmentation, and continuous checks.
3. Give examples from remote work access and protecting internal services.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified. Then draw a building where every door checks your badge, not just the front entrance on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is treating it as a product to buy a problem? How does least privilege relate to micro-segmentation? When would you choose Okta or Tailscale?

Study habit worth keeping: start with strong identity and device checks.

When you feel ready, compare Zero Trust Security with Penetration Testing and write down three differences.
