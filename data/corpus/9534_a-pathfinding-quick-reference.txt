A* Pathfinding Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for A* Pathfinding. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining.
Key terms: open and closed sets, cost so far, heuristics, and grids and graphs.
Typical uses: game characters, robot navigation, and map routing.
Risks: overestimating the heuristic, slow searches on huge maps, and ignoring moving obstacles.
Tools: Unity NavMesh, Python heapq, and Recast Navigation.

Mental picture: a hiker who prefers trails that seem to head toward the summit.

Habit to build: choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
