Statistical Significance Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Statistical Significance feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Statistical Significance: a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect.
p-values: a core term, most visible in judging experiment results.
the null hypothesis: a core term that beginners often confuse with confidence intervals.
confidence intervals: a core term that connects to effect size.
effect size: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
SciPy: a tool commonly used for comparing model performance.

Come back to this Statistical Significance list whenever documentation for statsmodels uses a word you do not recognize.
