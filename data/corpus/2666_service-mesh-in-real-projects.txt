Service Mesh in Real Projects

Reading about Service Mesh is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: securing service traffic. Here, sidecar proxies drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against added latency and complexity.

Scenario two: retries and timeouts. This time mutual TLS matters most, and tools such as Istio help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: fine-grained routing. The key idea is traffic policies, and the biggest risk is adopting it too early.

Across all three scenarios, Service Mesh stays the same idea: an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Only the details change.
