Disk Partitioning and Mounting Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Disk Partitioning and Mounting.

Do:
- Learn partitions before anything else.
- Try lsblk on a small example.
- Make it a habit to double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
- Test the case of adding a new drive early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: wrong device names.
- Ignore this: mounting without fstab entries.
- Ignore this: formatting the wrong disk.
- Adopt Disk Partitioning and Mounting just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Disk Partitioning and Mounting, a little patience with file system types pays off more than any clever shortcut.
