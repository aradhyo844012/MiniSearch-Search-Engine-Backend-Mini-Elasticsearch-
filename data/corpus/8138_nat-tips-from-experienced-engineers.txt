NAT Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with NAT regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of home routers early, before the design hardens.
2. Write down why you chose NAT so the next person understands the decision.
3. Make sure you can document every port forward you create.
4. Reach for iptables first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as router admin pages.
5. Do not skip private addresses; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to sharing limited IPv4 addresses as well as to NAT in general.
