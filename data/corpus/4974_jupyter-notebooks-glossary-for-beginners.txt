Jupyter Notebooks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Jupyter Notebooks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Jupyter Notebooks: interactive documents that combine code, results and notes.
cells: a core term, most visible in exploring data.
kernels: a core term that beginners often confuse with markdown.
markdown: a core term that connects to outputs.
outputs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
JupyterLab: a tool commonly used for teaching and demos.

Come back to this Jupyter Notebooks list whenever documentation for VS Code uses a word you do not recognize.
