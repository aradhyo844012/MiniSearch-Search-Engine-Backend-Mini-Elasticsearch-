Decision Trees Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Decision Trees.

Q: Explain Decision Trees in simple words.
A: Decision Trees refers to models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data. I would add an example involving classifying customers.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as splits, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with explaining predictions, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: deep trees that overfit; ignoring class imbalance; and unstable results from small data changes.

Q: How does it connect to Large Language Models?
A: Both belong to the area of Machine Learning, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: scikit-learn, XGBoost, and Graphviz.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: splits, entropy and Gini impurity, leaf nodes, and pruning.
