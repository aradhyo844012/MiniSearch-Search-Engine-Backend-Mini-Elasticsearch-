Scaling Considerations for Probability Basics

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Probability Basics changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding events and getting randomized algorithms right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for confusing independent and exclusive events and ignoring base rates, and measure with Python random before changing anything.

Large scale: independence and expected value move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around machine learning at this stage.

Before you scale, simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
