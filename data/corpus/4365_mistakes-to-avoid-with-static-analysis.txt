Mistakes to Avoid with Static Analysis

Everyone learns Static Analysis partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring warnings. It usually appears when someone skips linters and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: too many noisy rules. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of catching bugs early. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: disabling checks permanently. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on false positives to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run it in CI so problems never reach the main branch.
