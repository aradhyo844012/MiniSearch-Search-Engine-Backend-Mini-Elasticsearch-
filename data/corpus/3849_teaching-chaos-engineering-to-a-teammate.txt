Teaching Chaos Engineering to a Teammate

Explaining Chaos Engineering to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a fire drill where you secretly block one exit. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Chaos Engineering is deliberately causing failures in a system to learn how it responds. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving testing resilience, naming steady state and hypotheses as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain blast radius in Chaos Engineering back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, experimenting without safeguards, and suggest this habit: start with small experiments in a test environment.
