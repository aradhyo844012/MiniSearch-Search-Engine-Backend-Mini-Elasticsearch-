Data Augmentation Explained Simply

If Data Augmentation sounds intimidating, relax. It is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Data Augmentation as practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: transformations, label preservation, synthetic samples, and augmentation pipelines. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with transformations, then add label preservation.

People reach for Data Augmentation when they work on image models, text classification, and small datasets.

One practical tip is to augment only the training data.

After Data Augmentation, a natural next topic is Hyperparameter Tuning.
