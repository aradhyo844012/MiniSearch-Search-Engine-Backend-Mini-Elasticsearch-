Debugging MQTT: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around MQTT, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: unsecured brokers, messy topic names, and wrong quality of service choices.
Step 3: revisit publish and subscribe and topics, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Mosquitto or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving MQTT in work such as sensor networks come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, design topic names like a clear folder structure. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
