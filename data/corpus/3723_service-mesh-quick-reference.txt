Service Mesh Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Service Mesh. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.
Key terms: sidecar proxies, mutual TLS, traffic policies, and observability.
Typical uses: securing service traffic, retries and timeouts, and fine-grained routing.
Risks: added latency and complexity, adopting it too early, and hard-to-debug proxy configs.
Tools: Istio, Linkerd, and Envoy.

Mental picture: a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings.

Habit to build: adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
