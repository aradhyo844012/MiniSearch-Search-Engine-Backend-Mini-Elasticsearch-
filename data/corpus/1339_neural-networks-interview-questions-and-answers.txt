Neural Networks Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Neural Networks.

Q: Explain Neural Networks in simple words.
A: Neural Networks refers to models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. I would add an example involving image recognition.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: too little training data; poor learning rate choices; and not monitoring validation loss.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: too little training data.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: layers, weights, activation functions, and backpropagation.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as layers, and add tests aimed at the known risks.
