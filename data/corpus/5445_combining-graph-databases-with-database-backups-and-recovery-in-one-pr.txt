Combining Graph Databases with Database Backups and Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Graph Databases and Database Backups and Recovery can work together in a single project.

Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly. Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.

Imagine a project that involves social networks and recovering deleted tables. Graph Databases brings nodes, while Database Backups and Recovery brings full and incremental backups.

A sensible plan is to start with Neo4j for the Graph Databases part and pg_dump for the Database Backups and Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: unbounded traversals on one side, and storing backups on the same disk on the other.

Picture the whole as a city map where intersections are records and roads are relationships, working alongside a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home.

Two habits help: first, limit traversal depth in every exploratory query; second, schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
