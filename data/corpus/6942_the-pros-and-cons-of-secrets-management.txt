The Pros and Cons of Secrets Management

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Secrets Management is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports database credentials
- Makes API keys easier
- Helps with TLS certificates

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: secrets in source control
- Risk: long-lived shared credentials
- Risk: no rotation plan

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as database credentials, and you can manage the risks, Secrets Management is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
