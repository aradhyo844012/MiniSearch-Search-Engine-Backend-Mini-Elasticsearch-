Choosing Tools for Distributed Locks

There is no single best tool for Distributed Locks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Redis is a common first choice, especially for running scheduled jobs once. ZooKeeper suits teams that already work with similar technology, and etcd is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle protecting shared resources well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: locks that never expire.

As a rule of thumb, always use a timeout together with a fencing token, no matter which tool you use.
