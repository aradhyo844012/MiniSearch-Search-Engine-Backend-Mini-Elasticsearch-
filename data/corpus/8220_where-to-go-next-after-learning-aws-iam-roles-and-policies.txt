Where to Go Next After Learning AWS IAM Roles and Policies

You have met the basics of AWS IAM Roles and Policies. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Cloud-Native Applications, which belongs to the same area as AWS IAM Roles and Policies (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of users and roles, policies, least privilege or temporary credentials, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around granting services access with IAM Access Analyzer, and note every problem you meet, starting with wildcard permissions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining AWS IAM Roles and Policies to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
