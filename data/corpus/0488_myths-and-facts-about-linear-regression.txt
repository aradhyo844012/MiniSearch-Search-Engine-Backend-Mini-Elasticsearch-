Myths and Facts About Linear Regression

A lot of folklore has built up around Linear Regression. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. The term Linear Regression refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Myth: Linear Regression is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand coefficients and residuals.

Myth: Linear Regression is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as predicting prices benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: scikit-learn, NumPy, and statsmodels make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Linear Regression are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example extrapolating far beyond the data.

Reproducible experiments make it far easier to trust and improve a model.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Linear Regression.
