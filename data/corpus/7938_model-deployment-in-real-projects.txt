Model Deployment in Real Projects

Reading about Model Deployment is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: recommendation endpoints. Here, serving APIs drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against training-serving mismatch.

Scenario two: fraud checks. This time batch versus online inference matters most, and tools such as FastAPI help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: mobile app features. The key idea is containers, and the biggest risk is slow cold starts.

Across all three scenarios, Model Deployment stays the same idea: making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application. Only the details change.
