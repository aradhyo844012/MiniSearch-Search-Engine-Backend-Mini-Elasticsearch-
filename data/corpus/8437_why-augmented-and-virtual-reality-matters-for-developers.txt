Why Augmented and Virtual Reality Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Augmented and Virtual Reality make better decisions, because Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.

Three situations show why it matters. First, training simulations. Second, games. Third, remote collaboration. In each case, a solid grasp of head tracking and rendering at high frame rates separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: motion sickness from low frame rates, heavy assets, and poor interface design in 3D.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Unity XR, WebXR, and ARKit cover most needs.
