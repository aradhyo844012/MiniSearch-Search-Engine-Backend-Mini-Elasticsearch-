Monitoring and Observability Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Monitoring and Observability.

- Definition: collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves.
- Core ideas: metrics; logs; traces; alerts.
- Use it for: detecting outages; finding slow requests; capacity planning.
- Watch out for: noisy alerts; logs without context; dashboards nobody reads.
- Tools: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry.
- Quick tip: practice metrics until it feels automatic.
- Related topic: Infrastructure as Code.
