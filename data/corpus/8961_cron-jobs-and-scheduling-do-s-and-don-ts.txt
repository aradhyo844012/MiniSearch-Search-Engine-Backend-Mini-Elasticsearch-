Cron Jobs and Scheduling Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Cron Jobs and Scheduling.

Do:
- Learn crontab syntax before anything else.
- Try crontab on a small example.
- Make it a habit to redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths.
- Test the case of nightly backups early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming cron has your shell environment.
- Ignore this: overlapping runs of slow jobs.
- Ignore this: no logging of failures.
- Adopt Cron Jobs and Scheduling just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Cron Jobs and Scheduling, a little patience with schedule fields pays off more than any clever shortcut.
