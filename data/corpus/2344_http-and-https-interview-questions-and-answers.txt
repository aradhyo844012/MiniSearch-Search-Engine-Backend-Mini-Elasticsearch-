HTTP and HTTPS Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about HTTP and HTTPS.

Q: Explain HTTP and HTTPS in simple words.
A: HTTP and HTTPS refers to the request and response protocol of the web, with HTTPS adding encryption. I would add an example involving loading web pages.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: mixing up status codes; sending secrets in URLs; and ignoring caching headers.

Q: How does it connect to Load Balancing?
A: Both belong to the area of Networking, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: curl, Postman, and browser developer tools.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with calling APIs, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
