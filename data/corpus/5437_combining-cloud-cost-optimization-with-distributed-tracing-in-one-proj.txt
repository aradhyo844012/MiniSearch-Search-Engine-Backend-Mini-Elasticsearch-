Combining Cloud Cost Optimization with Distributed Tracing in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Cloud Cost Optimization and Distributed Tracing can work together in a single project.

Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability. Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Imagine a project that involves cutting idle resources and finding slow services. Cloud Cost Optimization brings right-sizing, while Distributed Tracing brings spans.

A sensible plan is to start with AWS Cost Explorer for the Cloud Cost Optimization part and OpenTelemetry for the Distributed Tracing part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: over-provisioned databases on one side, and sampling too aggressively on the other.

Picture the whole as turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using, working alongside a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route.

Two habits help: first, tag every resource with an owner; second, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
