Collision Detection in Real Projects

Reading about Collision Detection is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: platformers. Here, bounding boxes drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against tunneling through thin objects.

Scenario two: shooters. This time broad and narrow phases matters most, and tools such as Box2D help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: physics puzzles. The key idea is spatial partitioning, and the biggest risk is checking every pair of objects.

Across all three scenarios, Collision Detection stays the same idea: the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap. Only the details change.
