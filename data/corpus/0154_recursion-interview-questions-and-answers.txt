Recursion Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Recursion.

Q: Explain Recursion in simple words.
A: Recursion refers to a technique where a function solves a problem by calling itself on smaller inputs. I would add an example involving traversing trees.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as base case, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with divide and conquer algorithms, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: base case, recursive case, call stack, and tail calls.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: missing base cases.

Q: How does it connect to Dynamic Programming?
A: Both belong to the area of Data Structures and Algorithms, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
