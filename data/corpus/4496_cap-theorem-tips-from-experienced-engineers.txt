CAP Theorem Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with CAP Theorem regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Apache Cassandra first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as etcd.
2. Test the case of choosing database types early, before the design hardens.
3. Write down why you chose CAP Theorem so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: treating it as a three-way menu.
5. Make sure you can ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to designing multi-region services as well.
