How Threads and Concurrency Connects to Containers

Threads and Concurrency and Containers are closely connected parts of Operating Systems. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving handling many requests may also involve consistent development environments. In such a project, threads and images often influence each other.

Start with Threads and Concurrency: the term refers to ways of making progress on several tasks at once within a program. Then look at Containers, which refers to lightweight packages that bundle an application with its dependencies and run in isolation.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as keeping interfaces responsive, and explain how layers would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Threads and Concurrency, watch for this: creating too many threads. For Containers, watch for this: bloated images. Logs and system monitors are the first place to look when a machine misbehaves.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Python threading or Docker.
