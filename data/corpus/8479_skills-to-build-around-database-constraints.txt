Skills to Build Around Database Constraints

Knowing the definition of Database Constraints is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with primary keys and foreign keys, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with PostgreSQL on a Database Constraints task, and try at least one alternative such as SQLite.

Judgment: learn to decide when Database Constraints fits a problem such as preventing bad data, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example guard rails along a mountain road.

Habits: put critical rules in the database as well as in the app, and write down what you learn after each project.
