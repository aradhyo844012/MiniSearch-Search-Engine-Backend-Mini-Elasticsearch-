Understanding VPNs Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a private tunnel under a public road.

In that picture, tunneling is the first rule you follow, encryption is the next step, and authentication and split tunneling keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet, and it involves details such as assuming it makes you anonymous that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for WireGuard to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain remote work access to a friend.
