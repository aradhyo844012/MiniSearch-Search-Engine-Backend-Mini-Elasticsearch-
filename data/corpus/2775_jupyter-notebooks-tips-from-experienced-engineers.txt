Jupyter Notebooks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Jupyter Notebooks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: running cells out of order.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit kernels before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Jupyter Notebooks so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip cells; everything else builds on it.
5. Make sure you can restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to teaching and demos as well.
