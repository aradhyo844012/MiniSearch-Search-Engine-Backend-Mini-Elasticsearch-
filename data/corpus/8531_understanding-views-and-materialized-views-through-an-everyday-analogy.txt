Understanding Views and Materialized Views Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a window cut into a wall that shows only part of the room.

In that picture, view definitions is the first rule you follow, refresh strategies is the next step, and permissions and dependencies keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results, and it involves details such as stale materialized data that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for PostgreSQL to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain simplifying repeated queries to a friend.
