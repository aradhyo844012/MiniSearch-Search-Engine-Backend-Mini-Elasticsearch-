A Practical Checklist for Responsive Design

Use this checklist before you rely on Responsive Design in a project.

1. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on fluid grids.
2. Confirm that your team agrees on what Responsive Design should accomplish.
3. Review how Responsive Design interacts with DOM Manipulation.
4. Guard against this risk: horizontal scrolling.
5. Measure results before and after any change.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Pick a tool to start with, such as Chrome DevTools device mode or Bootstrap grid.
8. Try the approach on a small case, for example websites used on many devices.
