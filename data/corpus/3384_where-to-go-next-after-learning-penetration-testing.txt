Where to Go Next After Learning Penetration Testing

You have met the basics of Penetration Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Content Security Policy, which belongs to the same area as Penetration Testing (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation or reporting, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around compliance audits with OWASP ZAP, and note every problem you meet, starting with testing without written permission.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Penetration Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
