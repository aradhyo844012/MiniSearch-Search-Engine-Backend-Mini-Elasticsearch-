Where to Go Next After Learning Rasterization

You have met the basics of Rasterization. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Vector Graphics (SVG), which belongs to the same area as Rasterization (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of triangles, pixel coverage, the depth buffer or interpolation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around user interfaces with OpenGL, and note every problem you meet, starting with aliasing along edges.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Rasterization to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
