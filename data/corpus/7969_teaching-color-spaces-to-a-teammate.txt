Teaching Color Spaces to a Teammate

Explaining Color Spaces to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: different ways of giving directions to the same destination. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving web design, naming RGB and HSL as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain CMYK in Color Spaces back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, ignoring gamma, and suggest this habit: work in one color space and convert only at the end.
