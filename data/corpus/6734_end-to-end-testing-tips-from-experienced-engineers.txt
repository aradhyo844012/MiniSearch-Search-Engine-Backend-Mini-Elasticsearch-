End-to-End Testing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with End-to-End Testing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip browser automation; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose End-to-End Testing so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of checking login and checkout flows early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can cover only the most important user journeys.
5. Reach for Playwright first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Cypress.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to release verification as well.
