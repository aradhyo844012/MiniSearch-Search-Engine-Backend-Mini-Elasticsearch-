Where to Go Next After Learning State Management

You have met the basics of State Management. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CSS Variables, which belongs to the same area as State Management (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of single source of truth, actions, reducers or derived state, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around shared shopping carts with Redux, and note every problem you meet, starting with putting everything in global state.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining State Management to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
