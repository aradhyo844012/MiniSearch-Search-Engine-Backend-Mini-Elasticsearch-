Subqueries and CTEs Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Subqueries and CTEs regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip subqueries; everything else builds on it.
2. Test the case of breaking complex reports into steps early, before the design hardens.
3. Make sure you can name each CTE after what it returns.
4. Be careful with this one: correlated subqueries that run slowly.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit WITH clauses before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to filtering with computed results as well as to Subqueries and CTEs in general.
