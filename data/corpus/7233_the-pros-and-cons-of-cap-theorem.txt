The Pros and Cons of CAP Theorem

No technology is free of trade-offs, and CAP Theorem is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports choosing database types
- Makes designing multi-region services easier
- Helps with explaining system behavior in interviews

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating it as a three-way menu
- Risk: ignoring latency trade-offs in normal operation
- Risk: assuming partitions never happen

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as choosing database types, and you can manage the risks, CAP Theorem is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors.
