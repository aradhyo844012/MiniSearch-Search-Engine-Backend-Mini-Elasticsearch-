Skills to Build Around Pagination

Knowing the definition of Pagination is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with offset pagination and cursor pagination, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with SQL LIMIT and OFFSET on a Pagination task, and try at least one alternative such as keyset queries.

Judgment: learn to decide when Pagination fits a problem such as search results, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark.

Habits: prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data, and write down what you learn after each project.
