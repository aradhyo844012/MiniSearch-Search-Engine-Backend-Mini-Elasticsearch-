5G Networks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with 5G Networks.

Do:
- Learn spectrum bands before anything else.
- Try speed test tools on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds.
- Test the case of mobile broadband early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: limited coverage on the fastest bands.
- Ignore this: battery drain.
- Ignore this: marketing exaggeration.
- Adopt 5G Networks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with 5G Networks, a little patience with latency pays off more than any clever shortcut.
