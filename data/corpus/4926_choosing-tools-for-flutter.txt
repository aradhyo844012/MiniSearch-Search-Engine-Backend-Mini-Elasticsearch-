Choosing Tools for Flutter

There is no single best tool for Flutter. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Dart is a common first choice, especially for cross-platform mobile apps. Flutter DevTools suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Android Studio is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle desktop apps well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: deep widget trees.

As a rule of thumb, split big widgets into small reusable ones, no matter which tool you use.
