Probability Basics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Probability Basics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Probability Basics is the study of how likely events are to happen.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn events, conditional probability, independence, and expected value.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine predicting how often a dice roll will land on each face. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through randomized algorithms, machine learning, and risk estimates.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for confusing independent and exclusive events and ignoring base rates, then try Python random for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning.
