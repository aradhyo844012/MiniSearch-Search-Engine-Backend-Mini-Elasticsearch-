Debugging Game AI Behavior Trees: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Game AI Behavior Trees, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: trees that become tangled, expensive conditions checked every frame, and no way to debug decisions.
Step 3: revisit selector nodes and sequence nodes, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Unreal Engine behavior trees or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Game AI Behavior Trees in work such as enemy behavior come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, visualize the tree while the game is running. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
