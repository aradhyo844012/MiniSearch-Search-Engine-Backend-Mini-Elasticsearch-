The Pros and Cons of Column-Oriented Storage

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Column-Oriented Storage is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports analytics dashboards
- Makes large aggregations easier
- Helps with data warehouses

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow single-row lookups
- Risk: many small writes
- Risk: wide tables with unused columns

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as analytics dashboards, and you can manage the risks, Column-Oriented Storage is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, select only the columns you really need.
