Why Mocking and Stubbing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Mocking and Stubbing make better decisions, because Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests.

Three situations show why it matters. First, isolating units. Second, simulating failures. Third, avoiding network calls. In each case, a solid grasp of mocks and stubs separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: over-mocking, tests that mirror the implementation, and mocks that drift from reality.

Good tools exist, including unittest.mock, Mockito, and Sinon.js, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
