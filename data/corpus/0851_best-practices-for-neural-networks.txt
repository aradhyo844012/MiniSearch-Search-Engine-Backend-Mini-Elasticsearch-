Best Practices for Neural Networks

The practices below come from patterns that tend to work well across many teams and projects. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Neural Networks. The term refers to models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data.

1. Read the official documentation for TensorFlow; it often explains weights better than blog posts do.
2. Start small by applying Neural Networks to one case, such as image recognition, before rolling it out more widely.
3. Keep notes on what worked. A short README explaining your decisions about Neural Networks helps the next developer.
4. Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce.
5. Practice layers with small exercises until it feels natural.

Popular tools and libraries include PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Keras.

If you are comfortable with Neural Networks, explore Gradient Descent next. The two often appear in the same project.

With steady practice, Neural Networks will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
