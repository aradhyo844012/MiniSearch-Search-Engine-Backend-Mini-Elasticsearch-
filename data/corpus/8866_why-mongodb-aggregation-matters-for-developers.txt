Why MongoDB Aggregation Matters for Developers

Developers who understand MongoDB Aggregation make better decisions, because MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.

Three situations show why it matters. First, summarizing orders. Second, building analytics queries. Third, reshaping documents. In each case, a solid grasp of pipeline stages and $match and $group separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: putting $match late, huge intermediate results, and hitting memory limits.

Good tools exist, including MongoDB Compass, mongosh, and PyMongo, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
