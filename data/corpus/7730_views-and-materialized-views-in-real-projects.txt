Views and Materialized Views in Real Projects

Reading about Views and Materialized Views is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: simplifying repeated queries. Here, view definitions drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against stale materialized data.

Scenario two: hiding sensitive columns. This time refresh strategies matters most, and tools such as PostgreSQL help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: speeding up reports. The key idea is permissions, and the biggest risk is views stacked on views.

Across all three scenarios, Views and Materialized Views stays the same idea: saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results. Only the details change.
