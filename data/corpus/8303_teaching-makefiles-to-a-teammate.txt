Teaching Makefiles to a Teammate

Explaining Makefiles to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a to-do list that knows which steps must come first. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Makefiles is plain files that describe how to build programs and run common tasks with the make tool. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving compiling C projects, naming targets and prerequisites as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain recipes in Makefiles back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, tabs versus spaces, and suggest this habit: use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.
