GraphQL for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover GraphQL.

Learning goals:
1. Explain GraphQL in your own words.
2. Describe schema, queries and mutations, resolvers, and types.
3. Give examples from mobile apps with limited bandwidth and aggregating many services.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need. Then draw ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is the N+1 query problem a problem? How does queries and mutations relate to resolvers? When would you choose Apollo or GraphQL Playground?

Study habit: use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.

When you feel ready, compare GraphQL with Dependency Injection and write down three differences.
