Debugging Boolean Logic and Gates: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Boolean Logic and Gates, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: operator precedence mistakes, forgetting short-circuit behavior, and negating complex conditions incorrectly.
Step 3: revisit AND, OR and NOT and truth tables, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Logisim or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Boolean Logic and Gates in work such as writing conditions come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
