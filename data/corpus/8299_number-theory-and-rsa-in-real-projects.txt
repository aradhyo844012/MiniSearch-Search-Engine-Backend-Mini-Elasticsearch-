Number Theory and RSA in Real Projects

Reading about Number Theory and RSA is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: public key cryptography. Here, prime numbers drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against using small primes.

Scenario two: hash functions. This time modular arithmetic matters most, and tools such as Python pow help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: digital signatures. The key idea is greatest common divisor, and the biggest risk is implementing crypto by hand in production.

Across all three scenarios, Number Theory and RSA stays the same idea: the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption. Only the details change.
