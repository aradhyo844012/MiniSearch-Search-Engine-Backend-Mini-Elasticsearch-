Skills to Build Around Apache Spark

Knowing the definition of Apache Spark is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with DataFrames and transformations and actions, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with PySpark on a Apache Spark task, and try at least one alternative such as Databricks.

Judgment: learn to decide when Apache Spark fits a problem such as large ETL jobs, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers.

Habits: avoid collecting large results onto a single machine, and write down what you learn after each project.
