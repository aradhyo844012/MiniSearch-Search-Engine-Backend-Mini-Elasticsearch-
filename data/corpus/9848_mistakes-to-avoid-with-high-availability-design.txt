Mistakes to Avoid with High Availability Design

Everyone learns High Availability Design partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hidden single points of failure. It usually appears when someone skips redundancy and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: untested failover. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of online stores. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: correlated failures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on no single point of failure to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
