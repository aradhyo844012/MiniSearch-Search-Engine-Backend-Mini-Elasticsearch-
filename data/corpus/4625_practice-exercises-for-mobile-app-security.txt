Practice Exercises for Mobile App Security

These exercises build real understanding of Mobile App Security. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble banking apps or health apps.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Mobile App Security and include the term secure storage.
Exercise 3: Explain how obfuscation and permissions differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Compare Android Keystore and iOS Keychain for enterprise apps and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: storing tokens in plain text. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks.
