Scaling Considerations for Feature Stores

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Feature Stores changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding offline store and getting sharing features across models right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for leaking future data and duplicate definitions, and measure with Feast before changing anything.

Large scale: point-in-time correctness and feature definitions move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around serving real-time predictions at this stage.

Before you scale, define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
