Skills to Build Around Offline-First Apps

Knowing the definition of Offline-First Apps is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with local storage and sync queues, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with SQLite on a Offline-First Apps task, and try at least one alternative such as PouchDB.

Judgment: learn to decide when Offline-First Apps fits a problem such as field service tools, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example writing in a notebook and mailing the pages later.

Habits: show users which changes have not synced yet, and write down what you learn after each project.
