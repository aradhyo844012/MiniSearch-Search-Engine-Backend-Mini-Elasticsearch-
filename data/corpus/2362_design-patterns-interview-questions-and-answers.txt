Design Patterns Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Design Patterns.

Q: Explain Design Patterns in simple words.
A: Design Patterns refers to reusable solutions to common problems in software design. I would add an example involving structuring applications.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: UML diagrams, refactoring guides, and code examples.

Q: How does it connect to Version Control Workflow?
A: Both belong to the area of Software Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as singleton, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: forcing patterns where they are unnecessary; over-engineering; and copying patterns without understanding the problem.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: forcing patterns where they are unnecessary.
