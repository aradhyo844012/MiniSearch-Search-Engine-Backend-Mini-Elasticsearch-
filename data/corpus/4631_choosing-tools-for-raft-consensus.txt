Choosing Tools for Raft Consensus

There is no single best tool for Raft Consensus. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

etcd is a common first choice, especially for configuration stores. Consul suits teams that already work with similar technology, and HashiCorp Raft library is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle distributed locks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: even-sized clusters.

As a rule of thumb, use an odd number of nodes, no matter which tool you use.
