The Pros and Cons of Vector Graphics (SVG)

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Vector Graphics (SVG) is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports logos
- Makes icons easier
- Helps with charts

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overly complex paths
- Risk: a missing viewBox
- Risk: inconsistent stroke widths

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as logos, and you can manage the risks, Vector Graphics (SVG) is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
