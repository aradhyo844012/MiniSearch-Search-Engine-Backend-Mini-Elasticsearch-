OAuth 2.0 Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about OAuth 2.0.

Q: Explain OAuth 2.0 in simple words.
A: OAuth 2.0 refers to a framework that lets applications access resources on a user's behalf without sharing passwords. I would add an example involving sign in with another account.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as authorization server, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: loose redirect URI checks; requesting excessive scopes; and storing tokens insecurely.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Auth0, Keycloak, and authlib.

Q: How does it connect to SQL Injection?
A: Both belong to the area of Security, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with API access for third-party apps, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
