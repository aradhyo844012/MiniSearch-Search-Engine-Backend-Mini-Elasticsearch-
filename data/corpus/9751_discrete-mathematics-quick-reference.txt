Discrete Mathematics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Discrete Mathematics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.
Key terms: sets, logic, relations, and proofs.
Typical uses: algorithm analysis, database theory, and cryptography.
Risks: skipping proofs, confusing sets with sequences, and careless notation.
Tools: Python sets, proof assistants, and textbooks and problem sets.

Mental picture: the grammar underneath every program.

Habit to build: practice writing small proofs by hand.
