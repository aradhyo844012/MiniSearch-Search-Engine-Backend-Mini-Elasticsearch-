HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help.
3. Do not skip multiplexing; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for curl with HTTP/3 support first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Chrome DevTools.
5. Test the case of faster page loads early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to improving mobile performance as well as to HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 in general.
