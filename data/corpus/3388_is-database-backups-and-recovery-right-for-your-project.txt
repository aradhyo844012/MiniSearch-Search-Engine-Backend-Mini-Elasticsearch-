Is Database Backups and Recovery Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Database Backups and Recovery, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.

It is a good fit when your work involves recovering deleted tables or migrating servers, and when your team understands full and incremental backups.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn recovery point objective, or when you cannot manage these risks: never testing restores, storing backups on the same disk, and keeping credentials inside backup scripts.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether recovery time objective and restore testing are part of the answer. If they are, Database Backups and Recovery is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with meeting compliance rules and schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
