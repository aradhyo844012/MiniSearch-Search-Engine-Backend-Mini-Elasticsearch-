Why Modular Monoliths Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Modular Monoliths make better decisions, because Modular Monoliths is single deployable applications built from well-separated internal modules.

Three situations show why it matters. First, small teams. Second, early-stage products. Third, preparing for later service extraction. In each case, a solid grasp of module boundaries and public interfaces separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: modules reaching into each other's tables, no enforced boundaries, and treating modules as just folders.

You will not need to build everything yourself; ArchUnit, import-linter, and Gradle modules cover most needs.

Connect Modular Monoliths with Domain-Driven Design to see the bigger picture of Software Design.
