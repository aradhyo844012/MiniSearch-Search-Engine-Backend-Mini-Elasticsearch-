The Pros and Cons of Leader Election

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Leader Election is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports database primaries
- Makes job schedulers easier
- Helps with cluster managers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: split brain
- Risk: flapping leaders
- Risk: slow failure detection

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as database primaries, and you can manage the risks, Leader Election is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
