Distributed Locks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Distributed Locks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines.
Key terms: lease times, fencing tokens, lock owners, and timeouts.
Typical uses: running scheduled jobs once, protecting shared resources, and selecting a leader.
Risks: locks that never expire, clock drift, and assuming the lock holder is still alive.
Tools: Redis, ZooKeeper, and etcd.

Mental picture: a single key to a shared meeting room that expires automatically.

Habit to build: always use a timeout together with a fencing token.

See also: Saga Pattern, which sits close to Distributed Locks in the area of Distributed Systems.
