Debugging Service Mesh: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Service Mesh, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: added latency and complexity, adopting it too early, and hard-to-debug proxy configs.
Step 3: revisit sidecar proxies and mutual TLS, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Istio or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Service Mesh in work such as securing service traffic come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
