Cloud Cost Optimization Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Cloud Cost Optimization regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Cloud Cost Optimization so the next person understands the decision.
2. Make sure you can tag every resource with an owner.
3. Reach for AWS Cost Explorer first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Infracost.
4. Test the case of cutting idle resources early, before the design hardens.
5. Be careful with this one: forgotten test environments.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to budgeting by team as well.
