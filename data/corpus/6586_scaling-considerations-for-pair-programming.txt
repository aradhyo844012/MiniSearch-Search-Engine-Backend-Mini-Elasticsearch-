Scaling Considerations for Pair Programming

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Pair Programming changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding driver and navigator and getting teaching new teammates right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for one person dominating and fatigue from long sessions, and measure with VS Code Live Share before changing anything.

Large scale: communication and shared ownership move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around solving hard bugs at this stage.

Before you scale, swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
