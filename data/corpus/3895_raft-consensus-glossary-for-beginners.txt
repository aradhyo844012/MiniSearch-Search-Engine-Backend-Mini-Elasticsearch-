Raft Consensus Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Raft Consensus feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Raft Consensus: an algorithm that lets a group of servers agree on a shared log of operations.
leader election: a core term, most visible in configuration stores.
log replication: a core term that beginners often confuse with terms.
terms: a core term that connects to majority quorum.
majority quorum: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
etcd: a tool commonly used for distributed locks.

Come back to this Raft Consensus list whenever documentation for Consul uses a word you do not recognize.
