The Pros and Cons of MQTT

No technology is free of trade-offs, and MQTT is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sensor networks
- Makes smart home devices easier
- Helps with telemetry

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: unsecured brokers
- Risk: messy topic names
- Risk: wrong quality of service choices

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sensor networks, and you can manage the risks, MQTT is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, design topic names like a clear folder structure.
