A Practical Checklist for TF-IDF

Use this checklist before you rely on TF-IDF in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on inverse document frequency.
3. Review the core ideas: term frequency, inverse document frequency, weighting, and normalization.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer or Lucene.
5. Guard against this risk: over-weighting common words.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers term frequency.
7. Guard against this risk: using it on tiny corpora.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
9. Try the approach on a small case, for example ranking search results.
