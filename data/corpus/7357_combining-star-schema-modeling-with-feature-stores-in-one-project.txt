Combining Star Schema Modeling with Feature Stores in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Star Schema Modeling and Feature Stores can work together in a single project.

Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables. Feature Stores is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models.

Imagine a project that involves business intelligence reports and sharing features across models. Star Schema Modeling brings fact tables, while Feature Stores brings offline store.

A sensible plan is to start with dbt for the Star Schema Modeling part and Feast for the Feature Stores part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: over-normalized dimensions on one side, and duplicate definitions on the other.

Picture the whole as a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions, working alongside a shared pantry of prepared ingredients for many recipes.

Two habits help: first, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else; second, define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
