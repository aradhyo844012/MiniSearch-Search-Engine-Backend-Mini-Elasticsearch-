Scaling Considerations for Static Analysis

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Static Analysis changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding linters and getting catching bugs early right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring warnings and too many noisy rules, and measure with ESLint before changing anything.

Large scale: rule sets and false positives move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around enforcing team style at this stage.

Before you scale, run it in CI so problems never reach the main branch. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
