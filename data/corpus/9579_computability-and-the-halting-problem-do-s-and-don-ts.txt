Computability and the Halting Problem Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Computability and the Halting Problem.

Do:
- Learn Turing machines before anything else.
- Try Turing machine simulators on a small example.
- Make it a habit to learn the diagonal argument step by step.
- Test the case of understanding the limits of tools early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming everything is solvable.
- Ignore this: confusing hard with undecidable.
- Ignore this: hand-wavy proofs.
- Adopt Computability and the Halting Problem just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Computability and the Halting Problem, a little patience with decidability pays off more than any clever shortcut.
