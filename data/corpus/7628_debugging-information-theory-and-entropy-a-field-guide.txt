Debugging Information Theory and Entropy: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Information Theory and Entropy, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: confusing information with meaning, ignoring noise, and misreading entropy values.
Step 3: revisit bits and entropy, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use the Python math module or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Information Theory and Entropy in work such as data compression come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, compute the entropy of a short text by hand. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
