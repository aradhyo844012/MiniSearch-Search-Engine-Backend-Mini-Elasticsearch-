Boolean Logic and Gates in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Boolean Logic and Gates.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn AND, OR and NOT, truth tables, De Morgan's laws, and logic gates.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine light switches wired in series or in parallel. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through writing conditions, designing circuits, and simplifying expressions.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for operator precedence mistakes and forgetting short-circuit behavior, then try Logisim for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
