Practice Exercises for Convolutional Neural Networks

These exercises build real understanding of Convolutional Neural Networks. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble image classification or medical image analysis.
Exercise 2: Explain how feature maps and stride differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick PyTorch and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare TensorFlow and Keras for object detection and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Convolutional Neural Networks and include the term filters.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.
