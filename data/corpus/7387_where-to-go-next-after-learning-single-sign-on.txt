Where to Go Next After Learning Single Sign-On

You have met the basics of Single Sign-On. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CSRF, which belongs to the same area as Single Sign-On (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of identity provider, SAML, OpenID Connect or session lifetime, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reducing password fatigue with Okta, and note every problem you meet, starting with a single point of failure.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Single Sign-On to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
