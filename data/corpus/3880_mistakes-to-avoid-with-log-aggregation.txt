Mistakes to Avoid with Log Aggregation

Everyone learns Log Aggregation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: unbounded storage growth. It usually appears when someone skips shippers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unstructured logs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of troubleshooting across services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: missing timestamps. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on search queries to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.
