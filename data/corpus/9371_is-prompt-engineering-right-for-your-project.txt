Is Prompt Engineering Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Prompt Engineering, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models.

It is a good fit when your work involves drafting content or extracting data from text, and when your team understands instructions.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn examples, or when you cannot manage these risks: vague requests, missing context, and trusting outputs without verification.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether context and output format are part of the answer. If they are, Prompt Engineering is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with building assistants and specify the output format and include one or two examples.
