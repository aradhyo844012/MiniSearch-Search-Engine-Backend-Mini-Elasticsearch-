Mistakes to Avoid with Rasterization

Everyone learns Rasterization partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: aliasing along edges. It usually appears when someone skips triangles and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: overdraw. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of real-time games. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: depth fighting. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on interpolation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline.
