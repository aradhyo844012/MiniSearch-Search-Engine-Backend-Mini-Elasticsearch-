A Practical Checklist for Hash Tables

Use this checklist before you rely on Hash Tables in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: iteration order is not always stable.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Review the core ideas: hash function, buckets, collision handling, and load factor.
4. Write at least one automated test that covers hash function.
5. Review how Hash Tables interacts with Bloom Filters.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Hash Tables should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: resizing is expensive at scale.
