Where to Go Next After Learning K-Means Clustering

You have met the basics of K-Means Clustering. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Support Vector Machines, which belongs to the same area as K-Means Clustering (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of centroids, distance measures, choosing k or iterations, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around image compression with scikit-learn, and note every problem you meet, starting with choosing k blindly.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining K-Means Clustering to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
