How to Learn Responsive Design in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Responsive Design. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Responsive Design in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to building pages that adapt smoothly to phones, tablets and large screens.

Week 1: learn media queries and fluid grids, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Chrome DevTools device mode on a small task such as websites used on many devices.
Week 3: build a mini project around dashboards.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as testing only on one screen size, and explain Responsive Design to a friend.

In practice you can start with Chrome DevTools device mode, then explore Bootstrap grid or Lighthouse as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Responsive Design.
