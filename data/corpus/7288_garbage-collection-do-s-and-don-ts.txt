Garbage Collection Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Garbage Collection.

Do:
- Learn reachability before anything else.
- Try JVM GC logs on a small example.
- Make it a habit to measure allocation rates before tuning collector settings.
- Test the case of managed languages like Java and Python early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: creating huge numbers of short-lived objects.
- Ignore this: holding references longer than needed.
- Ignore this: ignoring pause times in latency-sensitive code.
- Adopt Garbage Collection just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Garbage Collection, a little patience with generations pays off more than any clever shortcut.
