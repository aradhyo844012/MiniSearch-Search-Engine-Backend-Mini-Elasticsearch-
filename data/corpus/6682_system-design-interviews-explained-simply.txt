System Design Interviews Explained Simply

If System Design Interviews sounds intimidating, relax. It is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of System Design Interviews as sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: requirements, scaling estimates, trade-offs, and components. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with requirements, then add scaling estimates.

You will meet System Design Interviews in work such as preparing for senior roles, practicing architectural thinking, and explaining real systems.

One practical tip is to clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
