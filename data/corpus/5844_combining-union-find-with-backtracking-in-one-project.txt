Combining Union-Find with Backtracking in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Union-Find and Backtracking can work together in a single project.

Union-Find is a data structure that tracks which items belong to the same group and merges groups quickly. Backtracking is a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends.

Imagine a project that involves detecting connected components and solving Sudoku. Union-Find brings parent pointers, while Backtracking brings decision tree.

A sensible plan is to start with Python lists for the Union-Find part and Python recursion for the Backtracking part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: merging without checking roots on one side, and missing pruning rules on the other.

Picture the whole as a set of club rosters where two clubs can merge into one, working alongside walking a maze and returning to the last fork whenever you hit a wall.

Two habits help: first, implement path compression and union by rank together; second, prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
