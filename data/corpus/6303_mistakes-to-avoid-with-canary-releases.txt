Mistakes to Avoid with Canary Releases

Everyone learns Canary Releases partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too little traffic to reveal problems. It usually appears when someone skips traffic percentages and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no clear success metrics. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of safer releases. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: manual promotion mistakes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on progressive rollout to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.
