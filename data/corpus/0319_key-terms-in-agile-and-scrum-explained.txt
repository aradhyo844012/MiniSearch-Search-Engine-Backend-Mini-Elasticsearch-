Key Terms in Agile and Scrum Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Agile and Scrum much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Agile and Scrum, they usually mean iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

sprints: a key term in Agile and Scrum. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
backlogs: a key term in Agile and Scrum. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
