Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Boolean Logic and Gates

This path is for a learner who has never studied Boolean Logic and Gates before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from) and learn AND, OR and NOT and truth tables.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Logisim, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving writing conditions.

Stage 3, depth: study De Morgan's laws and logic gates, then deliberately explore this problem: operator precedence mistakes.

Stage 4, application: apply Boolean Logic and Gates to designing circuits, compare Python bitwise operators with Karnaugh maps, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Boolean Logic and Gates to someone else, and then move on to GPUs and Parallelism.
