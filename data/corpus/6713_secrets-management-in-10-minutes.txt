Secrets Management in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Secrets Management.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn vaults, rotation, access policies, and audit logs.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through database credentials, API keys, and TLS certificates.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for secrets in source control and long-lived shared credentials, then try HashiCorp Vault for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
