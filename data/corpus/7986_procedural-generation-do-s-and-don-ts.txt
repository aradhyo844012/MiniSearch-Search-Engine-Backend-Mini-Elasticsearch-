Procedural Generation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Procedural Generation.

Do:
- Learn random seeds before anything else.
- Try Perlin noise on a small example.
- Make it a habit to log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
- Test the case of roguelike dungeons early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: levels that are unfair or unwinnable.
- Ignore this: repetitive results.
- Ignore this: bugs that cannot be reproduced without seeds.
- Adopt Procedural Generation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Procedural Generation, a little patience with noise functions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
