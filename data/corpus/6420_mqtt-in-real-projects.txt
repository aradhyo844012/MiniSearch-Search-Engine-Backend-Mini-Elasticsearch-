MQTT in Real Projects

Reading about MQTT is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: sensor networks. Here, publish and subscribe drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against unsecured brokers.

Scenario two: smart home devices. This time topics matters most, and tools such as Mosquitto help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: telemetry. The key idea is brokers, and the biggest risk is messy topic names.

Across all three scenarios, MQTT stays the same idea: a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks. Only the details change.
