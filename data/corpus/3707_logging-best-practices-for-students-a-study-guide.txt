Logging Best Practices for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Logging Best Practices.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Logging Best Practices in your own words.
2. Describe log levels, structured logs, correlation IDs, and log rotation.
3. Give examples from debugging production issues and auditing actions.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Logging Best Practices is recording useful, structured information about what an application is doing. Then draw a ship's logbook that explains what happened and when on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is logging sensitive data a problem? How does structured logs relate to correlation IDs? When would you choose the Python logging module or the ELK stack?

Study habit: include a request ID in every log line.

When you feel ready, compare Logging Best Practices with API Versioning and write down three differences.
