Edge Computing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Edge Computing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Edge Computing in your own words.
2. Describe edge nodes, latency, bandwidth savings, and synchronization.
3. Give examples from factory sensors and video analytics.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Edge Computing is processing data close to where it is produced instead of in a distant data center. Then draw a local branch office that handles tasks without calling headquarters on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is hard-to-update devices a problem? How does latency relate to bandwidth savings? When would you choose AWS IoT Greengrass or K3s?

Study habit worth keeping: decide which data must stay local before designing the system.

When you feel ready, compare Edge Computing with 5G Networks and write down three differences.
