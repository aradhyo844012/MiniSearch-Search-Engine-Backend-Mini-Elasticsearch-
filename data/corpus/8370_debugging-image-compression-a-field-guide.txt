Debugging Image Compression: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Image Compression, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: repeated recompression, the wrong format for graphics, and excessive quality loss.
Step 3: revisit lossy and lossless formats and quantization, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ImageMagick or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Image Compression in work such as web images come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
