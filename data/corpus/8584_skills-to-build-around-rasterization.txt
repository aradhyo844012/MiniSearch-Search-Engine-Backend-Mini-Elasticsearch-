Skills to Build Around Rasterization

Knowing the definition of Rasterization is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with triangles and pixel coverage, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with OpenGL on a Rasterization task, and try at least one alternative such as Vulkan.

Judgment: learn to decide when Rasterization fits a problem such as real-time games, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example coloring squares on graph paper to match a drawn outline.

Habits: draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline, and write down what you learn after each project.
