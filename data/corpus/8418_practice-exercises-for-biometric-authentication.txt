Practice Exercises for Biometric Authentication

These exercises build real understanding of Biometric Authentication. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: spoofing attacks. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare Face ID APIs and FIDO2 for payment approval and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how false accept and false reject rates and liveness detection differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Android BiometricPrompt and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble phone unlock or building access.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Biometric Authentication is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices.
