Computability and the Halting Problem Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Computability and the Halting Problem feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Computability and the Halting Problem: the study of what problems computers can and cannot solve.
Turing machines: a core term, most visible in understanding the limits of tools.
decidability: a core term that beginners often confuse with the halting problem.
the halting problem: a core term that connects to reductions.
reductions: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Turing machine simulators: a tool commonly used for proofs of impossibility.

Come back to this Computability and the Halting Problem list whenever documentation for textbooks uses a word you do not recognize.
