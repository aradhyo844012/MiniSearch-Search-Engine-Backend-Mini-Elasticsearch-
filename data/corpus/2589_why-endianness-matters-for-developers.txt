Why Endianness Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Endianness make better decisions, because Endianness is the order in which a computer stores the bytes of a multi-byte number.

Three situations show why it matters. First, network protocols. Second, binary file formats. Third, cross-platform data exchange. In each case, a solid grasp of big-endian and little-endian separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: mixing byte orders, assuming all machines are alike, and forgetting to convert before sending.

Good tools exist, including Python struct, htons and ntohl, and hexdump, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
