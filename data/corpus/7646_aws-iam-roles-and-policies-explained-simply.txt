AWS IAM Roles and Policies Explained Simply

Here is AWS IAM Roles and Policies without the jargon: it is the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account.

Think of AWS IAM Roles and Policies as key cards that open only the doors a person needs. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: users and roles, policies, least privilege, and temporary credentials. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with users and roles, then add policies.

You will meet AWS IAM Roles and Policies in work such as securing deployments, granting services access, and auditing permissions.

One practical tip is to grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.

Once AWS IAM Roles and Policies feels comfortable, read about Regions and Availability Zones, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
