DNS Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of DNS.

- Definition: the system that translates human-friendly domain names into IP addresses.
- Core ideas: resolvers; records; caching and TTL; name servers.
- Use it for: mapping domains to servers; email routing; load distribution.
- Watch out for: forgetting TTLs when migrating; mistyped records; assuming changes apply instantly.
- Tools: dig, nslookup, Cloudflare DNS.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for records before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice resolvers until it feels automatic.
