Understanding Recommendation Systems Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked.

In that picture, collaborative filtering is the first rule you follow, content-based filtering is the next step, and ranking and the cold start problem keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want, and it involves details such as feedback loops that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Surprise to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain streaming services to a friend.
