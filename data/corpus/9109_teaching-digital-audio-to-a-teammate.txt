Teaching Digital Audio to a Teammate

Explaining Digital Audio to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving music production, naming sampling rate and bit depth as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain waveforms in Digital Audio back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, clipping from too high a level, and suggest this habit: keep recordings below the clipping level.
