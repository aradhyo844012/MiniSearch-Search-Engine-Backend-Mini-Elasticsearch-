The Pros and Cons of Mobile App Security

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Mobile App Security is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports banking apps
- Makes health apps easier
- Helps with enterprise apps

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: storing tokens in plain text
- Risk: hard-coded API keys
- Risk: requesting unnecessary permissions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as banking apps, and you can manage the risks, Mobile App Security is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
