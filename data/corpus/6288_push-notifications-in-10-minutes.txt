Push Notifications in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Push Notifications.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn device tokens, push services, payloads, and permissions.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through order updates, chat alerts, and re-engaging users.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for asking for permission too early and sending too many messages, then try Firebase Cloud Messaging for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
