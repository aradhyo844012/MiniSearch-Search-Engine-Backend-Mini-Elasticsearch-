Makefiles for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Makefiles.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Makefiles in your own words.
2. Describe targets, prerequisites, recipes, and phony targets.
3. Give examples from compiling C projects and shortcuts for common commands.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Makefiles is plain files that describe how to build programs and run common tasks with the make tool. Then draw a to-do list that knows which steps must come first on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is tabs versus spaces a problem? How does prerequisites relate to recipes? When would you choose GNU Make or CMake?

Study habit worth keeping: use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.

When you feel ready, compare Makefiles with Monorepos and write down three differences.
