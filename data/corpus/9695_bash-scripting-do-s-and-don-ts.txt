Bash Scripting Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Bash Scripting.

Do:
- Learn variables before anything else.
- Try Bash on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start scripts with set -euo pipefail and quote every variable.
- Test the case of automating deployments early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: unquoted variables.
- Ignore this: ignoring errors in pipelines.
- Ignore this: scripts that are hard to read.
- Adopt Bash Scripting just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Bash Scripting, a little patience with conditionals and loops pays off more than any clever shortcut.
