Distributed Tracing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Distributed Tracing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Distributed Tracing in your own words.
2. Describe spans, trace IDs, context propagation, and sampling.
3. Give examples from finding slow services and understanding dependencies.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services. Then draw a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is missing context propagation a problem? How does trace IDs relate to context propagation? When would you choose OpenTelemetry or Jaeger?

Study habit: propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.

When you feel ready, compare Distributed Tracing with Feature Flags and write down three differences.
