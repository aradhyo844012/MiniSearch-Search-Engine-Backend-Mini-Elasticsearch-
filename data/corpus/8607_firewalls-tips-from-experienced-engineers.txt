Firewalls Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Firewalls regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can start with default deny and open only what you need.
2. Do not skip rules; everything else builds on it.
3. Be careful with this one: rules in the wrong order.
4. Write down why you chose Firewalls so the next person understands the decision.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit stateful inspection before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to segmenting networks as well as to Firewalls in general.
