Mistakes to Avoid with Python Asyncio

Everyone learns Python Asyncio partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: blocking calls inside coroutines. It usually appears when someone skips event loop and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: forgetting to await. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of calling many APIs at once. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: mixing threads and event loops carelessly. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on tasks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: never call blocking code directly inside a coroutine.
