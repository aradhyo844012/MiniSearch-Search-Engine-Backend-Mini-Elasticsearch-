Debugging Push Notifications: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Push Notifications, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: asking for permission too early, sending too many messages, and ignoring token refresh.
Step 3: revisit device tokens and push services, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Firebase Cloud Messaging or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Push Notifications in work such as order updates come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
