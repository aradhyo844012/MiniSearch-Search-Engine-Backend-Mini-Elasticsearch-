Speech Recognition for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Speech Recognition.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Speech Recognition in your own words.
2. Describe audio features, acoustic models, language models, and word error rate.
3. Give examples from voice assistants and captioning.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning. Then draw a stenographer who types as people talk on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is accents and background noise a problem? How does acoustic models relate to language models? When would you choose Whisper or Kaldi?

Study habit: measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.

When you feel ready, compare Speech Recognition with Model Drift and Monitoring and write down three differences.
