Views and Materialized Views Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Views and Materialized Views.

Do:
- Learn view definitions before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
- Test the case of simplifying repeated queries early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: stale materialized data.
- Ignore this: views stacked on views.
- Ignore this: surprising performance costs.
- Adopt Views and Materialized Views just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Views and Materialized Views, a little patience with refresh strategies pays off more than any clever shortcut.
