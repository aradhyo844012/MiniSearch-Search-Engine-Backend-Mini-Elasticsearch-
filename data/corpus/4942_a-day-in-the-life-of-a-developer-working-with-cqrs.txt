A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with CQRS

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on CQRS.

Morning: Rahul starts by reviewing a ticket about high-read dashboards. Before touching any code, Rahul re-reads the notes on commands to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Rahul pairs with a teammate to explore queries with Axon Framework. A small test exposes the problem of adding it to simple apps, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving complex domains. Rahul sketches a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Rahul writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.

Days like this are typical. CQRS is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
