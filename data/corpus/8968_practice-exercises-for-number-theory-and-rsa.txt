Practice Exercises for Number Theory and RSA

These exercises build real understanding of Number Theory and RSA. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Number Theory and RSA and include the term prime numbers.
Exercise 2: Explain how greatest common divisor and modular exponentiation differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: using small primes. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble public key cryptography or hash functions.
Exercise 5: Compare OpenSSL and SageMath for digital signatures and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.
