Where to Go Next After Learning Cloud-Native Applications

You have met the basics of Cloud-Native Applications. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about API Gateways, which belongs to the same area as Cloud-Native Applications (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of containers, microservices, declarative configuration or elastic scaling, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around data-heavy services with Kubernetes, and note every problem you meet, starting with treating the cloud like a data center.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Cloud-Native Applications to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
