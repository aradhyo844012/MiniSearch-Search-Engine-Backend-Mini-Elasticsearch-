Edge Computing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Edge Computing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Edge Computing so the next person understands the decision.
2. Do not skip edge nodes; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can decide which data must stay local before designing the system.
4. Test the case of factory sensors early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit latency before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to video analytics as well as to Edge Computing in general.
