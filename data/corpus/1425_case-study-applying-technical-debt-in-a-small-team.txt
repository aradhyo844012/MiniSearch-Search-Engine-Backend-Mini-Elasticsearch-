Case Study: Applying Technical Debt in a Small Team

Imagine a small student team building a project that depends on Technical Debt. Put simply, Technical Debt covers the future cost of shortcuts taken in code, design or documentation. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

The team first spent a week learning shortcuts and interest. Early on they hit a familiar problem: never paying it down. After fixing it, they used SonarQube to check their work and adopted Technical Debt for deciding when to cut corners. Looking back, they said the biggest lesson was to measure before optimizing.

Watch out for these problems: never paying it down; hiding it from stakeholders; and unclear ownership.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Technical Debt whenever a new project gives you a reason.
