GraphQL vs Pagination: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study GraphQL or Pagination first. Both sit in the area of Backend Engineering, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need, and it is closely tied to schema and queries and mutations. Pagination, by contrast, is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time, and it is closely tied to offset pagination and cursor pagination.

Choose GraphQL first if you care about mobile apps with limited bandwidth or aggregating many services. Choose Pagination first if you care about search results or activity feeds.

Think of GraphQL as ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu, and of Pagination as reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With GraphQL, avoid the N+1 query problem. With Pagination, avoid offsets getting slow on huge tables.

A good habit for GraphQL is to use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries. A good habit for Pagination is to prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
