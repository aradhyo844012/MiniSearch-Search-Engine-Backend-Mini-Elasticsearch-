Quantum Computing Basics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Quantum Computing Basics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Quantum Computing Basics in your own words.
2. Describe qubits, superposition, entanglement, and quantum gates.
3. Give examples from research on cryptography and simulating molecules.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems. Then draw a coin spinning in the air before it lands on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is expecting speedups for every problem a problem? How does superposition relate to entanglement? When would you choose Qiskit or Cirq?

Study habit worth keeping: start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.

When you feel ready, compare Quantum Computing Basics with Augmented and Virtual Reality and write down three differences.
