Where to Go Next After Learning Vector Clocks

You have met the basics of Vector Clocks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CQRS, which belongs to the same area as Vector Clocks (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of logical time, per-process counters, the happened-before relation or conflict detection, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around replicated databases with Riak, and note every problem you meet, starting with clocks that grow with many nodes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Vector Clocks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
