SOLID Principles Glossary for Beginners

New words can make SOLID Principles feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

SOLID Principles: five guidelines for designing object-oriented code that is easy to change.
single responsibility: a core term, most visible in structuring classes.
the open-closed principle: a core term that beginners often confuse with Liskov substitution.
Liskov substitution: a core term that connects to dependency inversion.
dependency inversion: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
UML diagrams: a tool commonly used for reviewing designs.
