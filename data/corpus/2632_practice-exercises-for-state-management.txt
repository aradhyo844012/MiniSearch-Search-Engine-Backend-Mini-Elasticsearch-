Practice Exercises for State Management

These exercises build real understanding of State Management. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Redux and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: putting everything in global state. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble large single-page apps or shared shopping carts.
Exercise 4: Compare Zustand and Vue Pinia for multi-step form wizards and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how reducers and derived state differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data.
