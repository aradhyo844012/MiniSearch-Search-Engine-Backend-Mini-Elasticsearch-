DHCP Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with DHCP regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose DHCP so the next person understands the decision.
2. Do not skip leases; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.
4. Test the case of home and office networks early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit address pools before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to guest Wi-Fi as well as to DHCP in general.
