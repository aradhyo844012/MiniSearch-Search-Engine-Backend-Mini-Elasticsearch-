Saga Pattern Explained Simply

Here is Saga Pattern without the jargon: it is a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions.

Think of Saga Pattern as booking a trip with separate hotel, flight and car reservations that can each be cancelled. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: local transactions, compensating actions, orchestration, and choreography. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with local transactions, then add compensating actions.

You will meet Saga Pattern in work such as order processing, travel bookings, and payment workflows.

One practical tip is to make every step and its compensation idempotent.

Once Saga Pattern feels comfortable, read about CAP Theorem, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
