Understanding MLOps Pipelines Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a factory line that turns data into a tested, packaged model.

In that picture, data versioning is the first rule you follow, training pipelines is the next step, and the model registry and continuous delivery keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real MLOps Pipelines is automated workflows that train, test, deploy and monitor machine learning models, and it involves details such as untracked data changes that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for MLflow to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain repeatable model releases to a friend.
