Skills to Build Around Object-Relational Mappers

Knowing the definition of Object-Relational Mappers is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with models and queries, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with SQLAlchemy on a Object-Relational Mappers task, and try at least one alternative such as the Django ORM.

Judgment: learn to decide when Object-Relational Mappers fits a problem such as building CRUD apps quickly, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a translator between the language of tables and the language of objects.

Habits: log the generated SQL during development, and write down what you learn after each project.
