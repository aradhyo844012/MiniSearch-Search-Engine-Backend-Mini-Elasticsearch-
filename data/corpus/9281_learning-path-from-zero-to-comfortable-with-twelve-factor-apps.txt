Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Twelve-Factor Apps

This path is for a learner who has never studied Twelve-Factor Apps before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Twelve-Factor Apps is a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly) and learn config in the environment and stateless processes.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Heroku, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving building web services.

Stage 3, depth: study backing services and disposability, then deliberately explore this problem: storing state on local disks.

Stage 4, application: apply Twelve-Factor Apps to deploying to platforms, compare Docker with Cloud Foundry, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Twelve-Factor Apps to someone else, and then move on to Multi-Cloud Strategy.
