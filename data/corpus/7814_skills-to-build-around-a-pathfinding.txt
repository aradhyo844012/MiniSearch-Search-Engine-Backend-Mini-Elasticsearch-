Skills to Build Around A* Pathfinding

Knowing the definition of A* Pathfinding is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with open and closed sets and cost so far, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Unity NavMesh on a A* Pathfinding task, and try at least one alternative such as Python heapq.

Judgment: learn to decide when A* Pathfinding fits a problem such as game characters, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a hiker who prefers trails that seem to head toward the summit.

Habits: choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal, and write down what you learn after each project.
