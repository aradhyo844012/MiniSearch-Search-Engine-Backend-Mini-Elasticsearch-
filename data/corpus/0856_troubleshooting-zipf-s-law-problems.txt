Troubleshooting Zipf's Law Problems

When something breaks around Zipf's Law, a calm and systematic approach saves the most time. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Zipf's Law. The term refers to the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language.

Start by reproducing the problem with the smallest possible example. Then change one thing at a time and note what happens. Logs, tests and matplotlib usually reveal the cause faster than guessing.

Common culprits: testing it on random text; reading too much into a perfect fit; ignoring small samples. If none of these explains the symptom, re-read the basics of rank and frequency and power laws.

In practice you can start with matplotlib, then explore NumPy or pandas as your needs grow.

With steady practice, Zipf's Law will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
