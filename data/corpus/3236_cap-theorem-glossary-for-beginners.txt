CAP Theorem Glossary for Beginners

New words can make CAP Theorem feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

CAP Theorem: a result stating that a distributed system cannot guarantee consistency, availability and partition tolerance all at once.
consistency: a core term, most visible in choosing database types.
availability: a core term that beginners often confuse with partition tolerance.
partition tolerance: a core term that connects to trade-offs.
trade-offs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Apache Cassandra: a tool commonly used for designing multi-region services.

Come back to this CAP Theorem list whenever documentation for etcd uses a word you do not recognize.
