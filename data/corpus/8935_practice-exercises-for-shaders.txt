Practice Exercises for Shaders

These exercises build real understanding of Shaders. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick GLSL and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble lighting effects or water and fire visuals.
Exercise 3: Compare Shadertoy and RenderDoc for post-processing and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Explain how uniforms and GLSL differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Shaders and include the term vertex shaders.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Shaders is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.
