Domain-Driven Design Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Domain-Driven Design.

Do:
- Learn ubiquitous language before anything else.
- Try event storming on a small example.
- Make it a habit to agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes.
- Test the case of complex business systems early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using it for simple CRUD apps.
- Ignore this: models that never match the business.
- Ignore this: ignoring context boundaries.
- Adopt Domain-Driven Design just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Domain-Driven Design, a little patience with bounded contexts pays off more than any clever shortcut.
