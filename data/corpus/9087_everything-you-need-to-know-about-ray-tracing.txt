Everything You Need to Know About Ray Tracing

This page collects the essentials about Ray Tracing in one place. In short, Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.

Core concepts:
- rays
- intersections
- reflection and refraction
- shadows

Where it is used:
- movie visuals
- architectural visualization
- realistic game lighting

Common problems:
- slow render times
- noise from too few samples
- numerical precision errors

Tools to know:
- Blender Cycles
- NVIDIA OptiX
- POV-Ray

Remember this above all: tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
