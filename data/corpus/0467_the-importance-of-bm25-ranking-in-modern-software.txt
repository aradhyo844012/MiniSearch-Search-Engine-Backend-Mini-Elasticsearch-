The Importance of BM25 Ranking in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate BM25 Ranking until something goes wrong without it. If you build software for long enough, you will run into BM25 Ranking. The term refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization.

Search quality depends on how text is prepared before it is ever indexed. Users judge a search engine by whether the right result appears near the top.
