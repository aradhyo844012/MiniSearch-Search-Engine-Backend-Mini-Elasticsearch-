Mistakes to Avoid with React Hooks

Everyone learns React Hooks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: missing dependencies in effects. It usually appears when someone skips useState and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: calling hooks conditionally. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of managing form input. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: stale closures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on custom hooks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: enable the hooks lint rules from the start.
