Stream Processing Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Stream Processing.

Q: Explain Stream Processing in simple words.
A: Stream Processing refers to handling data continuously as it arrives rather than in large batches. I would add an example involving live dashboards.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, and Spark Structured Streaming.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: late events.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as events, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: late events; losing state after crashes; and duplicate processing.
