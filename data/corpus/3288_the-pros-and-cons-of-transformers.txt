The Pros and Cons of Transformers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Transformers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports language translation
- Makes text generation easier
- Helps with code assistance

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: huge compute requirements
- Risk: limited context windows
- Risk: biased training data

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as language translation, and you can manage the risks, Transformers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, begin with small pretrained models.
