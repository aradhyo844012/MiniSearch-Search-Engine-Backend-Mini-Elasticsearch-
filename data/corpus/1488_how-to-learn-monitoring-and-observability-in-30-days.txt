How to Learn Monitoring and Observability in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Monitoring and Observability. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Monitoring and Observability, they usually mean collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn metrics and logs, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Prometheus on a small task such as detecting outages.
Week 3: build a mini project around finding slow requests.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as noisy alerts, and explain Monitoring and Observability to a friend.

In practice you can start with Prometheus, then explore Grafana or OpenTelemetry as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Monitoring and Observability.
