Debugging Go Goroutines: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Go Goroutines, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: goroutine leaks, data races, and unbuffered channels causing deadlocks.
Step 3: revisit goroutines and channels, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use the go command or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Go Goroutines in work such as servers handling many connections come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, run your tests with the race detector turned on. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
