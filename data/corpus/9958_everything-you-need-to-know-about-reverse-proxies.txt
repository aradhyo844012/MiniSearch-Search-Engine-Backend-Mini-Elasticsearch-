Everything You Need to Know About Reverse Proxies

This page collects the essentials about Reverse Proxies in one place. In short, Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.

Core concepts:
- upstream servers
- proxy headers
- TLS termination
- caching

Where it is used:
- hiding backend servers
- terminating HTTPS
- routing requests to several apps

Common problems:
- losing the client IP address
- wrong timeouts
- exposing internal paths

Tools to know:
- NGINX
- Caddy
- HAProxy

Remember this above all: a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
