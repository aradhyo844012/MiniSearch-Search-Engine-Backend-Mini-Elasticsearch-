Stop Words Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Stop Words.

- Definition: very common words such as the and of that are often ignored by search systems.
- Core ideas: frequency lists; index size; query relevance; language-specific lists.
- Use it for: shrinking indexes; speeding up queries; reducing noise in ranking.
- Watch out for: removing words that matter in phrases; using the wrong language list; inconsistent handling at index and query time.
- Tools: NLTK stopwords, scikit-learn, custom word lists.
- Related topic: Inverted Index.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for index size before building anything large.
