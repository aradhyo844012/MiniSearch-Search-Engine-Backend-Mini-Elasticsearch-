Practice Exercises for Software Estimation

These exercises build real understanding of Software Estimation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: treating estimates as promises. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare Jira and spreadsheets for negotiating scope and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble sprint planning or setting deadlines.
Exercise 4: Explain how uncertainty and velocity differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick planning poker and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.
