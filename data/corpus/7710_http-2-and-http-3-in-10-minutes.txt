HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through HTTP/2 and HTTP/3.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 is newer versions of the web protocol that load pages faster over fewer connections.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn multiplexing, header compression, QUIC, and server push.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a multi-lane highway replacing a single-lane road. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through faster page loads, improving mobile performance, and reducing connection overhead.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help and middleboxes blocking UDP, then try curl with HTTP/3 support for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to keep a working HTTP/1.1 fallback.
