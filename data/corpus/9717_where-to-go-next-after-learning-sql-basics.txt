Where to Go Next After Learning SQL Basics

You have met the basics of SQL Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Views and Materialized Views, which belongs to the same area as SQL Basics (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY or INSERT and UPDATE, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around building reports with SQLite, and note every problem you meet, starting with forgetting the WHERE clause on updates.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining SQL Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
