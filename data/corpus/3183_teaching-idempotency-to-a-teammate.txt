Teaching Idempotency to a Teammate

Explaining Idempotency to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: pressing an elevator call button many times and still getting one elevator. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving payment APIs, naming idempotency keys and safe retries as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain deduplication in Idempotency back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, keys that expire too soon, and suggest this habit: store the result with the key so retries return the same response.
