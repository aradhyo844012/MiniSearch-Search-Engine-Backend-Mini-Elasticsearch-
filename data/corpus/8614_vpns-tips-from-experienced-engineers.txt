VPNs Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with VPNs regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit encryption before anything else.
2. Make sure you can test for DNS leaks after connecting.
3. Test the case of remote work access early, before the design hardens.
4. Do not skip tunneling; everything else builds on it.
5. Reach for WireGuard first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as OpenVPN.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to linking offices as well as to VPNs in general.
