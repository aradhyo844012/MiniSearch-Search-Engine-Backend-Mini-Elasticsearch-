Scaling Considerations for CPU Caches

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how CPU Caches changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding cache lines and getting speeding up loops right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for false sharing and poor access patterns, and measure with perf before changing anything.

Large scale: levels L1 to L3 and locality move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around explaining performance differences at this stage.

Before you scale, access arrays in the order they are stored. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
