Why Load Testing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Load Testing make better decisions, because Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic.

Three situations show why it matters. First, capacity planning. Second, finding bottlenecks. Third, validating autoscaling. In each case, a solid grasp of virtual users and throughput separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: testing unrealistic traffic, ignoring warm-up, and using average latency only.

Good tools exist, including k6, Locust, and Apache JMeter, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
