High Availability Design Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with High Availability Design regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip redundancy; everything else builds on it.
2. Make sure you can list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
3. Be careful with this one: hidden single points of failure.
4. Test the case of online stores early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit failover before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to banking systems as well as to High Availability Design in general.
