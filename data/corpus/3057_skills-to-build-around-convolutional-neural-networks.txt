Skills to Build Around Convolutional Neural Networks

Knowing the definition of Convolutional Neural Networks is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with filters and pooling, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with PyTorch on a Convolutional Neural Networks task, and try at least one alternative such as TensorFlow.

Judgment: learn to decide when Convolutional Neural Networks fits a problem such as image classification, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes.

Habits: start from a pretrained model, and write down what you learn after each project.
