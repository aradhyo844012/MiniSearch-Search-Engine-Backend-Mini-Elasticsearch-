Debugging Disaster Recovery: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Disaster Recovery, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: untested plans, outdated runbooks, and dependencies missed in the plan.
Step 3: revisit recovery time objective and recovery point objective, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use AWS Backup or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Disaster Recovery in work such as surviving regional outages come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
