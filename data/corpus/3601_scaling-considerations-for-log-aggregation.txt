Scaling Considerations for Log Aggregation

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Log Aggregation changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding shippers and getting troubleshooting across services right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for unbounded storage growth and unstructured logs, and measure with Elasticsearch before changing anything.

Large scale: retention and search queries move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around security investigations at this stage.

Before you scale, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
