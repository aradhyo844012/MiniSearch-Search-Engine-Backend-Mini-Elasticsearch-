Choosing Tools for CPU Caches

There is no single best tool for CPU Caches. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

perf is a common first choice, especially for speeding up loops. Valgrind cachegrind suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Intel VTune is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle explaining performance differences well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: false sharing.

As a rule of thumb, access arrays in the order they are stored, no matter which tool you use.
