5G Networks Glossary for Beginners

New words can make 5G Networks feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

5G Networks: the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency.
spectrum bands: a core term, most visible in mobile broadband.
latency: a core term that beginners often confuse with network slicing.
network slicing: a core term that connects to small cells.
small cells: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
speed test tools: a tool commonly used for connected vehicles.

Come back to this 5G Networks list whenever documentation for network simulators uses a word you do not recognize.
