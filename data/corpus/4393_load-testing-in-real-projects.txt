Load Testing in Real Projects

Reading about Load Testing is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: capacity planning. Here, virtual users drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against testing unrealistic traffic.

Scenario two: finding bottlenecks. This time throughput matters most, and tools such as k6 help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: validating autoscaling. The key idea is latency percentiles, and the biggest risk is ignoring warm-up.

Across all three scenarios, Load Testing stays the same idea: measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic. Only the details change.
