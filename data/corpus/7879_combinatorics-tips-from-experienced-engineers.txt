Combinatorics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Combinatorics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: double counting.
2. Test the case of analyzing algorithms early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit combinations before anything else.
4. Make sure you can test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
5. Reach for Python itertools first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as math.comb.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to probability as well as to Combinatorics in general.
