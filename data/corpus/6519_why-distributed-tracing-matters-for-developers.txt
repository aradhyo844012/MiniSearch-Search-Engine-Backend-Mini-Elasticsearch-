Why Distributed Tracing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Distributed Tracing make better decisions, because Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Three situations show why it matters. First, finding slow services. Second, understanding dependencies. Third, debugging errors across services. In each case, a solid grasp of spans and trace IDs separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: missing context propagation, sampling too aggressively, and overhead from too many spans.

Good tools exist, including OpenTelemetry, Jaeger, and Zipkin, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
