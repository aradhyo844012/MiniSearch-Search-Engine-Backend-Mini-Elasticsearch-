Rasterization Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Rasterization feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Rasterization: converting shapes described by geometry into pixels on the screen.
triangles: a core term, most visible in real-time games.
pixel coverage: a core term that beginners often confuse with the depth buffer.
the depth buffer: a core term that connects to interpolation.
interpolation: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
OpenGL: a tool commonly used for user interfaces.

Come back to this Rasterization list whenever documentation for Vulkan uses a word you do not recognize.
