Logging Best Practices Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Logging Best Practices.

Do:
- Learn log levels before anything else.
- Try the Python logging module on a small example.
- Make it a habit to include a request ID in every log line.
- Test the case of debugging production issues early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: logging sensitive data.
- Ignore this: too much noise.
- Ignore this: missing context.
- Adopt Logging Best Practices just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Logging Best Practices, a little patience with structured logs pays off more than any clever shortcut.
