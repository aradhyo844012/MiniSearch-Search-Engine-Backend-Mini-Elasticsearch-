Information Theory and Entropy Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Information Theory and Entropy regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of data compression early, before the design hardens.
2. Reach for the Python math module first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as NumPy.
3. Write down why you chose Information Theory and Entropy so the next person understands the decision.
4. Make sure you can compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
5. Be careful with this one: confusing information with meaning.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to error-correcting codes as well as to Information Theory and Entropy in general.
