Mistakes to Avoid with Managed Kubernetes Services

Everyone learns Managed Kubernetes Services partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: neglecting version upgrades. It usually appears when someone skips the control plane and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: oversized node pools. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of running containerized apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: costly idle clusters. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on integrated load balancers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: plan regular cluster upgrades.
