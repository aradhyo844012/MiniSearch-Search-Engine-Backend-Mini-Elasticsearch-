Practice Exercises for Single Sign-On

These exercises build real understanding of Single Sign-On. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Single Sign-On and include the term identity provider.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: a single point of failure. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble company intranets or reducing password fatigue.
Exercise 4: Pick Okta and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare Keycloak and Azure AD for centralized access control and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications.
