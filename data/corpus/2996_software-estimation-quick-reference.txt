Software Estimation Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Software Estimation. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take.
Key terms: story points, ranges, uncertainty, and velocity.
Typical uses: sprint planning, setting deadlines, and negotiating scope.
Risks: treating estimates as promises, ignoring unknowns, and estimating alone.
Tools: planning poker, Jira, and spreadsheets.

Mental picture: forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable.

Habit to build: give ranges instead of single numbers.
