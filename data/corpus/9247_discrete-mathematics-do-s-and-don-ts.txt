Discrete Mathematics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Discrete Mathematics.

Do:
- Learn sets before anything else.
- Try Python sets on a small example.
- Make it a habit to practice writing small proofs by hand.
- Test the case of algorithm analysis early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: skipping proofs.
- Ignore this: confusing sets with sequences.
- Ignore this: careless notation.
- Adopt Discrete Mathematics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Discrete Mathematics, a little patience with logic pays off more than any clever shortcut.
