Game AI Behavior Trees for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Game AI Behavior Trees.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Game AI Behavior Trees in your own words.
2. Describe selector nodes, sequence nodes, conditions, and actions.
3. Give examples from enemy behavior and companion characters.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Game AI Behavior Trees is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does. Then draw a flowchart a character consults every moment before acting on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is trees that become tangled a problem? How does sequence nodes relate to conditions? When would you choose Unreal Engine behavior trees or py_trees?

Study habit worth keeping: visualize the tree while the game is running.

When you feel ready, compare Game AI Behavior Trees with A* Pathfinding and write down three differences.
