The Importance of OAuth 2.0 in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate OAuth 2.0 until something goes wrong without it. If you build software for long enough, you will run into OAuth 2.0. The term refers to a framework that lets applications access resources on a user's behalf without sharing passwords.

Keeping dependencies updated removes a large share of known risks.

A team working on sign in with another account might adopt OAuth 2.0 after hitting the limits of a simpler approach. Other teams turn to it for API access for third-party apps or mobile logins.

Watch out for these problems: loose redirect URI checks; requesting excessive scopes; and storing tokens insecurely.

With steady practice, OAuth 2.0 will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
