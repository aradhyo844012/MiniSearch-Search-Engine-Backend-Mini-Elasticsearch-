The Pros and Cons of BGP and Internet Routing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and BGP and Internet Routing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports connecting ISPs
- Makes directing traffic between large networks easier
- Helps with explaining internet outages

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: route leaks
- Risk: hijacked prefixes
- Risk: misconfigured filters

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as connecting ISPs, and you can manage the risks, BGP and Internet Routing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, filter route announcements strictly.
