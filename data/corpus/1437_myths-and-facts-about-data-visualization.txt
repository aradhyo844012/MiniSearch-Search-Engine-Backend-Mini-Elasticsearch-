Myths and Facts About Data Visualization

A lot of folklore has built up around Data Visualization. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. The term Data Visualization refers to presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

A solid grasp of Data Visualization also makes Data Cleaning easier to understand.

Myth: Data Visualization is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand chart types and axes and labels.

Myth: Data Visualization is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as explaining results benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Data Visualization are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example truncated axes.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Data Visualization.
