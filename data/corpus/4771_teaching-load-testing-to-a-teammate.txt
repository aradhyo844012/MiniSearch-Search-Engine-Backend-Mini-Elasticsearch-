Teaching Load Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Load Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stress test that fills a stadium to see how the exits cope. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving capacity planning, naming virtual users and throughput as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain latency percentiles in Load Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, testing unrealistic traffic, and suggest this habit: report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
