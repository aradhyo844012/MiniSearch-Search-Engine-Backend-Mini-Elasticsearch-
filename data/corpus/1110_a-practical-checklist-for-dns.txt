A Practical Checklist for DNS

Use this checklist before you rely on DNS in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: forgetting TTLs when migrating.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as dig or nslookup.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example mapping domains to servers.
4. Review the core ideas: resolvers, records, caching and TTL, and name servers.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on records.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what DNS should accomplish.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers resolvers.
8. Guard against this risk: assuming changes apply instantly.
