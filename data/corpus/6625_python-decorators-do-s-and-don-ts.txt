Python Decorators Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Python Decorators.

Do:
- Learn higher-order functions before anything else.
- Try functools on a small example.
- Make it a habit to always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator.
- Test the case of logging calls early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: losing function metadata.
- Ignore this: stacking decorators in the wrong order.
- Ignore this: hiding side effects.
- Adopt Python Decorators just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Python Decorators, a little patience with wrapper functions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
