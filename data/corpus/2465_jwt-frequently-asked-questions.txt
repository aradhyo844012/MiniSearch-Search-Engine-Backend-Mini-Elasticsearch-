JWT: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is JWT?
A: The term JWT refers to a compact token format that carries signed claims between a client and a server.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with JWT.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include jwt.io, PyJWT, and jsonwebtoken.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with header, payload and signature and claims, then move on to expiry and signing keys.

Q: How does it relate to REST APIs?
A: JWT and REST APIs often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Backend and APIs.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: storing tokens in insecure places; skipping signature checks; and very long expiry times.
