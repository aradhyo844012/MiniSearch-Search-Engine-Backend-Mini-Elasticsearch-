Choosing Tools for Monorepos

There is no single best tool for Monorepos. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Nx is a common first choice, especially for sharing libraries across apps. Bazel suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Turborepo is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Monorepos by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle coordinated refactors well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Monorepos, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow checkouts.

As a rule of thumb, set up selective builds and tests early, no matter which tool you use.
