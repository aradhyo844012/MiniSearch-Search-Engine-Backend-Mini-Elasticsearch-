Information Theory and Entropy in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Information Theory and Entropy.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Information Theory and Entropy is the mathematics of measuring and compressing information.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn bits, entropy, compression limits, and noise.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine measuring how surprised you are by each new message. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through data compression, error-correcting codes, and communication systems.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for confusing information with meaning and ignoring noise, then try the Python math module for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
