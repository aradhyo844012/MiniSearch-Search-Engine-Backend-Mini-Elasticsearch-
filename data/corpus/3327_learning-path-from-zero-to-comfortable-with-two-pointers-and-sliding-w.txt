Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Two Pointers and Sliding Window

This path is for a learner who has never studied Two Pointers and Sliding Window before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Two Pointers and Sliding Window is techniques that scan arrays with two moving positions to avoid nested loops) and learn left and right pointers and window boundaries.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Python lists, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving finding pairs with a given sum.

Stage 3, depth: study invariants and shrinking and expanding, then deliberately explore this problem: moving the wrong pointer.

Stage 4, application: apply Two Pointers and Sliding Window to longest substring problems, compare collections.deque with LeetCode, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Two Pointers and Sliding Window to someone else, and then move on to Dijkstra's Algorithm.
