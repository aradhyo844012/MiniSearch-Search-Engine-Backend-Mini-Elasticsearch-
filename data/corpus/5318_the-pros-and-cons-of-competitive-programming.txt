The Pros and Cons of Competitive Programming

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Competitive Programming is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports interview preparation
- Makes learning algorithms easier
- Helps with practicing under pressure

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: memorizing solutions without understanding
- Risk: skipping fundamentals
- Risk: burning out

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as interview preparation, and you can manage the risks, Competitive Programming is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
