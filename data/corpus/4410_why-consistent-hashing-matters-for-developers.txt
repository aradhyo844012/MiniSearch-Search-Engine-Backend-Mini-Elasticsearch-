Why Consistent Hashing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Consistent Hashing make better decisions, because Consistent Hashing is a way of mapping keys to servers so that adding or removing servers moves very few keys.

Three situations show why it matters. First, distributed caches. Second, sharded databases. Third, load balancing. In each case, a solid grasp of hash ring and virtual nodes separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: too few virtual nodes, uneven load, and hot keys.

Good tools exist, including Redis Cluster, Memcached clients, and Apache Cassandra, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
