Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Interrupts

This path is for a learner who has never studied Interrupts before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Interrupts is signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event) and learn interrupt handlers and interrupt vectors.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Linux /proc/interrupts, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving keyboard and mouse input.

Stage 3, depth: study priorities and context saving, then deliberately explore this problem: long handlers.

Stage 4, application: apply Interrupts to timers, compare logic analyzers with embedded debuggers, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Interrupts to someone else, and then move on to Instruction Pipelines.
