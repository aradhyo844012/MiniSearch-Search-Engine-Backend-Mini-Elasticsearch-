Key Terms in OAuth 2.0 Explained

Clear vocabulary makes OAuth 2.0 much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about OAuth 2.0, they usually mean a framework that lets applications access resources on a user's behalf without sharing passwords. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

authorization server: a key term in OAuth 2.0. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
access tokens: a key term in OAuth 2.0. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
scopes: a key term in OAuth 2.0. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
redirect URIs: a key term in OAuth 2.0. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.

With steady practice, OAuth 2.0 will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
