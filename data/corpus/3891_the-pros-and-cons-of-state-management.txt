The Pros and Cons of State Management

No technology is free of trade-offs, and State Management is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports large single-page apps
- Makes shared shopping carts easier
- Helps with multi-step form wizards

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: putting everything in global state
- Risk: duplicated state that drifts apart
- Risk: unclear update paths

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as large single-page apps, and you can manage the risks, State Management is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
