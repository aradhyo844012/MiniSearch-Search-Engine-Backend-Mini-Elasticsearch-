The Pros and Cons of Feature Stores

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Feature Stores is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sharing features across models
- Makes serving real-time predictions easier
- Helps with avoiding training-serving skew

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: leaking future data
- Risk: duplicate definitions
- Risk: stale online values

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sharing features across models, and you can manage the risks, Feature Stores is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
