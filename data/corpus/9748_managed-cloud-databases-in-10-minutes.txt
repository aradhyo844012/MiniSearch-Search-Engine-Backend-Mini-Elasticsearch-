Managed Cloud Databases in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Managed Cloud Databases.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Managed Cloud Databases is database services where the cloud provider handles backups, patching and failover.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn automated backups, read replicas, maintenance windows, and scaling options.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a restaurant that hires a kitchen crew to handle the cleaning. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through launching apps quickly, reducing database administration, and high availability setups.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for surprise costs and limited configuration control, then try Amazon RDS for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test restoring a backup before you need it.
