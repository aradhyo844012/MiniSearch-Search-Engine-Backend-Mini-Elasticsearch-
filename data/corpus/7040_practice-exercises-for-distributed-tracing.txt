Practice Exercises for Distributed Tracing

These exercises build real understanding of Distributed Tracing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how context propagation and sampling differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: missing context propagation. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Distributed Tracing and include the term spans.
Exercise 4: Pick OpenTelemetry and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble finding slow services or understanding dependencies.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.
