Mistakes to Avoid with Arduino Basics

Everyone learns Arduino Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: wiring errors. It usually appears when someone skips sketches and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: blocking delays in the loop. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of reading sensors. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: insufficient power for motors. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the serial monitor to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test each sensor on its own before combining them.
