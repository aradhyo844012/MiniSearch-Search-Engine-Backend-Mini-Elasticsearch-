Modular Monoliths Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Modular Monoliths.

Do:
- Learn module boundaries before anything else.
- Try ArchUnit on a small example.
- Make it a habit to enforce boundaries with automated checks.
- Test the case of small teams early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: modules reaching into each other's tables.
- Ignore this: no enforced boundaries.
- Ignore this: treating modules as just folders.
- Adopt Modular Monoliths just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Modular Monoliths, a little patience with public interfaces pays off more than any clever shortcut.
