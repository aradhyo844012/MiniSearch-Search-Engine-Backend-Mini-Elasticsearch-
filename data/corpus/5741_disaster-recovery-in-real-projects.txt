Disaster Recovery in Real Projects

Reading about Disaster Recovery is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: surviving regional outages. Here, recovery time objective drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against untested plans.

Scenario two: meeting compliance needs. This time recovery point objective matters most, and tools such as AWS Backup help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: protecting critical data. The key idea is failover regions, and the biggest risk is outdated runbooks.

Across all three scenarios, Disaster Recovery stays the same idea: the plans and systems that restore services after major failures. Only the details change.
