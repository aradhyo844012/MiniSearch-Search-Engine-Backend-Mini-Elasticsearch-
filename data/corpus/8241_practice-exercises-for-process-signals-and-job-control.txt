Practice Exercises for Process Signals and Job Control

These exercises build real understanding of Process Signals and Job Control. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare jobs and fg and nohup for running long tasks in the background and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Process Signals and Job Control and include the term SIGTERM and SIGKILL.
Exercise 3: Pick kill and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble stopping runaway programs or shutting down gracefully.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: using kill -9 first. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Process Signals and Job Control is messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs.
