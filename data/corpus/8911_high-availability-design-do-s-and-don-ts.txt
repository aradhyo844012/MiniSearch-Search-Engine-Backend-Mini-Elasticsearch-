High Availability Design Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with High Availability Design.

Do:
- Learn redundancy before anything else.
- Try load balancers on a small example.
- Make it a habit to list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
- Test the case of online stores early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hidden single points of failure.
- Ignore this: untested failover.
- Ignore this: correlated failures.
- Adopt High Availability Design just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with High Availability Design, a little patience with failover pays off more than any clever shortcut.
