The Pros and Cons of Font Rendering

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Font Rendering is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports web pages
- Makes operating systems easier
- Helps with PDF viewers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing font fallbacks
- Risk: blurry text from scaling
- Risk: large font files

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as web pages, and you can manage the risks, Font Rendering is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, subset fonts to the characters you need.
