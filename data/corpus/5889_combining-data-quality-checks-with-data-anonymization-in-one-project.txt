Combining Data Quality Checks with Data Anonymization in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Data Quality Checks and Data Anonymization can work together in a single project.

Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent. Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.

Imagine a project that involves protecting dashboards and sharing datasets with researchers. Data Quality Checks brings null checks, while Data Anonymization brings masking.

A sensible plan is to start with Great Expectations for the Data Quality Checks part and ARX for the Data Anonymization part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: checks only at the end of pipelines on one side, and removing too little on the other.

Picture the whole as a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line, working alongside blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene.

Two habits help: first, check data as early as it enters the pipeline; second, test whether combining fields could identify a person.
