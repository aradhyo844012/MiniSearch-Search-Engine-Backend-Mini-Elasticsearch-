The Pros and Cons of Views and Materialized Views

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Views and Materialized Views is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports simplifying repeated queries
- Makes hiding sensitive columns easier
- Helps with speeding up reports

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: stale materialized data
- Risk: views stacked on views
- Risk: surprising performance costs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as simplifying repeated queries, and you can manage the risks, Views and Materialized Views is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, document how and when each materialized view is refreshed.
