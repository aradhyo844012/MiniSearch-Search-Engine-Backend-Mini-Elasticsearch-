Scaling Considerations for Open Source Contribution

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Open Source Contribution changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding forks and getting building a portfolio right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for opening huge pull requests and ignoring contribution rules, and measure with GitHub before changing anything.

Large scale: issues and contributing guides move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around learning from experienced maintainers at this stage.

Before you scale, start with documentation fixes or small bugs. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
