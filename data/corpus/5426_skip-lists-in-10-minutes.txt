Skip Lists in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Skip Lists.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Skip Lists is layered linked lists that allow fast searching by skipping over many elements.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn multiple levels, random level assignment, forward pointers, and expected logarithmic time.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine an express train line layered over a local train line. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through ordered in-memory indexes, sorted sets in caches, and concurrent ordered maps.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for poor random number choices and memory overhead, then try Redis sorted sets for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to draw several levels by hand to see how a search moves down.
