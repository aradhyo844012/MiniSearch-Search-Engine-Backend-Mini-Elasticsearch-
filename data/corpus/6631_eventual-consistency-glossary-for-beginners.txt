Eventual Consistency Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Eventual Consistency feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Eventual Consistency: a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough.
replication delay: a core term, most visible in global social feeds.
conflict resolution: a core term that beginners often confuse with read-your-writes.
read-your-writes: a core term that connects to convergence.
convergence: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Amazon DynamoDB: a tool commonly used for shopping cart services.

Come back to this Eventual Consistency list whenever documentation for Apache Cassandra uses a word you do not recognize.
