Skills to Build Around Digital Audio

Knowing the definition of Digital Audio is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with sampling rate and bit depth, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Audacity on a Digital Audio task, and try at least one alternative such as SoX.

Judgment: learn to decide when Digital Audio fits a problem such as music production, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later.

Habits: keep recordings below the clipping level, and write down what you learn after each project.
