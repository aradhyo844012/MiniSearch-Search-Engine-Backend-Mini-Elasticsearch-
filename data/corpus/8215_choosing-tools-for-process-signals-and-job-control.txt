Choosing Tools for Process Signals and Job Control

There is no single best tool for Process Signals and Job Control. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

kill is a common first choice, especially for stopping runaway programs. jobs and fg suits teams that already work with similar technology, and nohup is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Process Signals and Job Control by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle shutting down gracefully well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Process Signals and Job Control, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: using kill -9 first.

As a rule of thumb, try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up, no matter which tool you use.
