A Practical Checklist for Tries

Use this checklist before you rely on Tries in a project.

1. Review how Tries interacts with Heaps and Priority Queues.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers prefix sharing.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Tries should accomplish.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on nodes and edges.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example autocomplete.
6. Guard against this risk: assuming case-insensitive matching.
7. Guard against this risk: forgetting end-of-word markers.
