Teaching AWS IAM Roles and Policies to a Teammate

Explaining AWS IAM Roles and Policies to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: key cards that open only the doors a person needs. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: AWS IAM Roles and Policies is the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving securing deployments, naming users and roles and policies as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain least privilege in AWS IAM Roles and Policies back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, wildcard permissions, and suggest this habit: grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.
