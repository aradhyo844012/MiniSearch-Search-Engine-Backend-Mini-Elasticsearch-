Where to Go Next After Learning Minimum Spanning Trees

You have met the basics of Minimum Spanning Trees. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Greedy Algorithms, which belongs to the same area as Minimum Spanning Trees (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of weighted graphs, Kruskal's algorithm, Prim's algorithm or the cut property, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around clustering data with NetworkX, and note every problem you meet, starting with using it on directed graphs.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Minimum Spanning Trees to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
