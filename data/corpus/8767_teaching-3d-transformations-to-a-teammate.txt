Teaching 3D Transformations to a Teammate

Explaining 3D Transformations to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving games, naming translation and rotation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain scaling in 3D Transformations back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, wrong multiplication order, and suggest this habit: write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
