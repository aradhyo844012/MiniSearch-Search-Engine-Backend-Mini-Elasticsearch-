Write-Ahead Logging Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Write-Ahead Logging feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Write-Ahead Logging: a technique that records changes in a log before they are applied to the main data.
log records: a core term, most visible in surviving crashes.
checkpoints: a core term that beginners often confuse with crash recovery.
crash recovery: a core term that connects to fsync.
fsync: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
PostgreSQL WAL: a tool commonly used for feeding replication streams.
