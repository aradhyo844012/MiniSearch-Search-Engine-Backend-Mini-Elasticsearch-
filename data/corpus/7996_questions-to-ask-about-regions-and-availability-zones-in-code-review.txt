Questions to Ask About Regions and Availability Zones in Code Review

Reviewing code that involves Regions and Availability Zones is easier with a short list of questions. Keep these in mind as you read the diff.

- Does the code handle the case of placing services near users correctly?
- Would a test catch cross-region transfer fees if it appeared later?
- Is AWS Regions used consistently, or are we mixing approaches?
- Is availability zones clear to someone reading the code for the first time?
- Have we considered putting everything in one zone?
- Could we spread critical services across at least two zones?

Good reviews of work on Regions and Availability Zones are specific, kind and focused on latency, not on personal style.
