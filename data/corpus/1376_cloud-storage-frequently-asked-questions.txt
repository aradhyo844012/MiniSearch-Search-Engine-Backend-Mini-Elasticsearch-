Cloud Storage: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Cloud Storage?
A: The term Cloud Storage refers to storing files and objects on remote servers that scale on demand.

Q: Why do developers use Cloud Storage?
A: Common uses include backups, serving images, and data lakes.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Cloud Storage.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, and Azure Blob Storage.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with buckets and objects, then move on to access policies and storage classes.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: public buckets by mistake; surprise egress costs; and no lifecycle rules.

Q: How does it relate to Monitoring and Observability?
A: Cloud Storage and Monitoring and Observability often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Cloud and DevOps.
