Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Debugging Strategies

This path is for a learner who has never studied Debugging Strategies before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Debugging Strategies is systematic ways of finding and fixing the cause of unexpected program behavior) and learn reproducing the bug and isolating the cause.

Stage 2, hands-on: install debuggers, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving fixing production issues.

Stage 3, depth: study forming hypotheses and binary search through code, then deliberately explore this problem: changing many things at once.

Stage 4, application: apply Debugging Strategies to learning unfamiliar code, compare logging with git bisect, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Debugging Strategies to someone else, and then move on to Developer Portfolios.
