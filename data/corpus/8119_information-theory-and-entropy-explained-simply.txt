Information Theory and Entropy Explained Simply

Here is Information Theory and Entropy without the jargon: it is the mathematics of measuring and compressing information.

Think of Information Theory and Entropy as measuring how surprised you are by each new message. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: bits, entropy, compression limits, and noise. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with bits, then add entropy.

People reach for Information Theory and Entropy when they work on data compression, error-correcting codes, and communication systems.

One practical tip is to compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
