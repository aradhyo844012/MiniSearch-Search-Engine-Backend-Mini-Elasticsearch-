A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Monorepos

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Monorepos.

Morning: Meera starts by reviewing a ticket about sharing libraries across apps. Before touching any code, Meera re-reads the notes on shared tooling to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Meera pairs with a teammate to explore code ownership with Nx. A small test exposes the problem of slow checkouts, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving coordinated refactors. Meera sketches one big toolbox for the whole workshop on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Meera writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to set up selective builds and tests early.

Days like this are typical. Monorepos is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
