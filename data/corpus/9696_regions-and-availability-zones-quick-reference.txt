Regions and Availability Zones Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Regions and Availability Zones. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources.
Key terms: regions, availability zones, latency, and data residency.
Typical uses: placing services near users, surviving data center failures, and meeting data laws.
Risks: putting everything in one zone, cross-region transfer fees, and forgetting data residency rules.
Tools: AWS Regions, Azure regions, and Google Cloud regions.

Mental picture: branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings.

Habit to build: spread critical services across at least two zones.
