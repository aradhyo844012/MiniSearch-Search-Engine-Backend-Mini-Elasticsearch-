The Pros and Cons of Digital Audio

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Digital Audio is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports music production
- Makes voice calls easier
- Helps with podcasts

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: clipping from too high a level
- Risk: aliasing at low sample rates
- Risk: mixing sample rates

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as music production, and you can manage the risks, Digital Audio is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep recordings below the clipping level.
