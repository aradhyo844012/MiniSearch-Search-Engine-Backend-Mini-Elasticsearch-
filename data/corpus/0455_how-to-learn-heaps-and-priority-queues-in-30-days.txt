How to Learn Heaps and Priority Queues in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Heaps and Priority Queues. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Heaps and Priority Queues in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to structures that always give quick access to the smallest or largest element.

Week 1: learn heap property and sift up and sift down, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Python heapq on a small task such as scheduling tasks.
Week 3: build a mini project around selecting the top results from many candidates.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as assuming a heap is fully sorted, and explain Heaps and Priority Queues to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include Python heapq, Java PriorityQueue, and C++ priority_queue.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Heaps and Priority Queues.
