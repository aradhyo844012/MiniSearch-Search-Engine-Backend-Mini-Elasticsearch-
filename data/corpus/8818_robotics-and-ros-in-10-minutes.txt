Robotics and ROS in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Robotics and ROS.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sensors, actuators, nodes and topics, and control loops.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a body with senses and muscles controlled by a nervous system of messages. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through warehouse robots, drones, and educational robots.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for ignoring sensor noise and unsafe motion commands, then try ROS 2 for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test in simulation before moving real hardware.
