Why Leader Election Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Leader Election make better decisions, because Leader Election is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work.

Three situations show why it matters. First, database primaries. Second, job schedulers. Third, cluster managers. In each case, a solid grasp of heartbeats and terms or epochs separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: split brain, flapping leaders, and slow failure detection.

You will not need to build everything yourself; ZooKeeper, etcd, and Kubernetes leases cover most needs.
