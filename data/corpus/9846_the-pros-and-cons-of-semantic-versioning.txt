The Pros and Cons of Semantic Versioning

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Semantic Versioning is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports publishing libraries
- Makes declaring dependencies easier
- Helps with planning upgrades

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: breaking changes in minor releases
- Risk: overly loose ranges
- Risk: forgetting to document changes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as publishing libraries, and you can manage the risks, Semantic Versioning is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, write a changelog entry with every release.
