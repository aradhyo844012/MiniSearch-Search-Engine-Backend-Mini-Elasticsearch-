Flutter Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Flutter feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Flutter: a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.
widgets: a core term, most visible in cross-platform mobile apps.
state: a core term that beginners often confuse with hot reload.
hot reload: a core term that connects to the render tree.
the render tree: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Dart: a tool commonly used for desktop apps.

Come back to this Flutter list whenever documentation for Flutter DevTools uses a word you do not recognize.
