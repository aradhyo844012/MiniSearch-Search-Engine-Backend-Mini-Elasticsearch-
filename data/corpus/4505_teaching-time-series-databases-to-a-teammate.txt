Teaching Time-Series Databases to a Teammate

Explaining Time-Series Databases to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a long logbook where every line starts with the time. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving server metrics, naming timestamps and retention policies as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain downsampling in Time-Series Databases back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, high-cardinality tags, and suggest this habit: include a time range in every query.
