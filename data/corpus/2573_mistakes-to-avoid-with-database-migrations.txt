Mistakes to Avoid with Database Migrations

Everyone learns Database Migrations partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: locking large tables. It usually appears when someone skips up and down scripts and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: editing old migrations. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of adding columns safely. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: data loss on rollbacks. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on zero-downtime changes to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
