Why Technical Writing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Technical Writing make better decisions, because Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.

Three situations show why it matters. First, documentation. Second, blog posts. Third, design proposals. In each case, a solid grasp of audience and structure separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: assuming too much knowledge, burying the main point, and long unbroken paragraphs.

Good tools exist, including Markdown, Grammarly, and Vale, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
