Why Biometric Authentication Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Biometric Authentication make better decisions, because Biometric Authentication is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices.

Three situations show why it matters. First, phone unlock. Second, building access. Third, payment approval. In each case, a solid grasp of enrollment and matching separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: spoofing attacks, biased recognition, and credentials that cannot be changed once leaked.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Android BiometricPrompt, Face ID APIs, and FIDO2 cover most needs.
