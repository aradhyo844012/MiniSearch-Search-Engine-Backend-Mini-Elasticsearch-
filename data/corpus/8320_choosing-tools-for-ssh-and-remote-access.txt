Choosing Tools for SSH and Remote Access

There is no single best tool for SSH and Remote Access. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

OpenSSH is a common first choice, especially for administering servers. ssh-keygen suits teams that already work with similar technology, and scp is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for SSH and Remote Access by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle copying files securely well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for SSH and Remote Access, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: password logins left enabled.

As a rule of thumb, use key-based login and disable password authentication, no matter which tool you use.
