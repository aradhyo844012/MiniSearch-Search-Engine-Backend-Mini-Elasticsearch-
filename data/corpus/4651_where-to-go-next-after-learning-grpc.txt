Where to Go Next After Learning gRPC

You have met the basics of gRPC. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about GraphQL, which belongs to the same area as gRPC (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of protocol buffers, service definitions, streaming or HTTP/2, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around low-latency APIs with protoc, and note every problem you meet, starting with breaking changes in message fields.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining gRPC to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
