Scaling Considerations for Configuration Management

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Configuration Management changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding environment variables and getting separating development and production right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for committing secrets and inconsistent defaults, and measure with dotenv before changing anything.

Large scale: defaults and secrets handling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around toggling features at this stage.

Before you scale, fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
