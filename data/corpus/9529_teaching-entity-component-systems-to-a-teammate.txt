Teaching Entity Component Systems to a Teammate

Explaining Entity Component Systems to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: building characters from interchangeable parts snapped onto an ID tag. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Entity Component Systems is a game architecture that builds objects from data components processed by systems. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving large simulations, naming entities and components as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain systems in Entity Component Systems back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, overengineering small games, and suggest this habit: keep components as plain data.
