Debugging Progressive Web Apps: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Progressive Web Apps, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: ignoring platform limitations, poor offline fallbacks, and heavy first loads.
Step 3: revisit web app manifest and service worker, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Lighthouse or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Progressive Web Apps in work such as offline-friendly tools come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test the app in airplane mode. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
