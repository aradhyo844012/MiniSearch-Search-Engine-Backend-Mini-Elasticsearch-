Choosing Tools for Time-Series Databases

There is no single best tool for Time-Series Databases. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

InfluxDB is a common first choice, especially for server metrics. TimescaleDB suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Prometheus is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle sensor readings well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: high-cardinality tags.

As a rule of thumb, include a time range in every query, no matter which tool you use.
