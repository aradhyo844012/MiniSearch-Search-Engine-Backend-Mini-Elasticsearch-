Eventual Consistency Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Eventual Consistency.

Do:
- Learn replication delay before anything else.
- Try Amazon DynamoDB on a small example.
- Make it a habit to decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
- Test the case of global social feeds early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming reads are always fresh.
- Ignore this: unresolved write conflicts.
- Ignore this: no user feedback during delays.
- Adopt Eventual Consistency just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Eventual Consistency, a little patience with conflict resolution pays off more than any clever shortcut.
