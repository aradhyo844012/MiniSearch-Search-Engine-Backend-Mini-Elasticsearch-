Edge Computing Explained Simply

Here is Edge Computing without the jargon: it is processing data close to where it is produced instead of in a distant data center.

Think of Edge Computing as a local branch office that handles tasks without calling headquarters. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: edge nodes, latency, bandwidth savings, and synchronization. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with edge nodes, then add latency.

You will meet Edge Computing in work such as factory sensors, video analytics, and smart cities.

One practical tip is to decide which data must stay local before designing the system.

Once Edge Computing feels comfortable, read about Digital Twins, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
