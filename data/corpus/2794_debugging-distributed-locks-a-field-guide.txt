Debugging Distributed Locks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Distributed Locks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: locks that never expire, clock drift, and assuming the lock holder is still alive.
Step 3: revisit lease times and fencing tokens, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Redis or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Distributed Locks in work such as running scheduled jobs once come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, always use a timeout together with a fencing token. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
