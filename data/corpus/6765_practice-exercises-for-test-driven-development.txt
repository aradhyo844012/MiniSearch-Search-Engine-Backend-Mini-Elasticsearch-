Practice Exercises for Test-Driven Development

These exercises build real understanding of Test-Driven Development. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: writing tests that are too large. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble designing small modules or preventing regressions.
Exercise 3: Pick pytest and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Explain how test first and fast feedback differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Test-Driven Development and include the term red, green, refactor.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Test-Driven Development is a workflow where you write a failing test first and then write code to make it pass.
