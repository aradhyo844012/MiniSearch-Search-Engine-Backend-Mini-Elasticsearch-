The Pros and Cons of Physics Engines

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Physics Engines is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports vehicles and ragdolls
- Makes puzzle games easier
- Helps with training simulations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: variable time steps
- Risk: unstable stacks of objects
- Risk: extreme mass ratios

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as vehicles and ragdolls, and you can manage the risks, Physics Engines is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use a fixed time step for stable results.
