System Design Interviews Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for System Design Interviews. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices.
Key terms: requirements, scaling estimates, trade-offs, and components.
Typical uses: preparing for senior roles, practicing architectural thinking, and explaining real systems.
Risks: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements, ignoring trade-offs, and overcomplicating designs.
Tools: whiteboard sketches, Excalidraw, and system design primers.

Mental picture: sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists.

Habit to build: clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
