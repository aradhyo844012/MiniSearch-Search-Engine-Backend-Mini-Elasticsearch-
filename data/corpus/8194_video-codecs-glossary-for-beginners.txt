Video Codecs Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Video Codecs feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Video Codecs: programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.
frames: a core term, most visible in streaming services.
motion compensation: a core term that beginners often confuse with bitrate.
bitrate: a core term that connects to containers.
containers: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
FFmpeg: a tool commonly used for video calls.

Come back to this Video Codecs list whenever documentation for H.264 uses a word you do not recognize.
