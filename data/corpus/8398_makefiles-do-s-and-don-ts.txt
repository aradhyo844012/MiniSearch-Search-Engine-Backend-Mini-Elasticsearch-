Makefiles Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Makefiles.

Do:
- Learn targets before anything else.
- Try GNU Make on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.
- Test the case of compiling C projects early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: tabs versus spaces.
- Ignore this: missing phony declarations.
- Ignore this: hidden environment assumptions.
- Adopt Makefiles just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Makefiles, a little patience with prerequisites pays off more than any clever shortcut.
