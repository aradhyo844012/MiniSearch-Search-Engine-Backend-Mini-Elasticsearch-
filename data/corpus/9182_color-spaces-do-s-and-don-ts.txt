Color Spaces Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Color Spaces.

Do:
- Learn RGB before anything else.
- Try browser color pickers on a small example.
- Make it a habit to work in one color space and convert only at the end.
- Test the case of web design early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring gamma.
- Ignore this: mixing color spaces.
- Ignore this: poor contrast choices.
- Adopt Color Spaces just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Color Spaces, a little patience with HSL pays off more than any clever shortcut.
