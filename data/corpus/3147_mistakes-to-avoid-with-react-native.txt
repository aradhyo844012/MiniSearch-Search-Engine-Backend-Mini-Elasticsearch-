Mistakes to Avoid with React Native

Everyone learns React Native partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: heavy re-renders. It usually appears when someone skips components and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poorly tuned lists. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of cross-platform apps. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: relying on outdated native modules. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on navigation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test on real devices for both platforms.
