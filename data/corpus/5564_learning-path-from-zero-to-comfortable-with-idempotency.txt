Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Idempotency

This path is for a learner who has never studied Idempotency before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once) and learn idempotency keys and safe retries.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Stripe idempotency keys, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving payment APIs.

Stage 3, depth: study deduplication and at-least-once delivery, then deliberately explore this problem: keys that expire too soon.

Stage 4, application: apply Idempotency to message processing, compare Redis with database unique constraints, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Idempotency to someone else, and then move on to Vector Clocks.
