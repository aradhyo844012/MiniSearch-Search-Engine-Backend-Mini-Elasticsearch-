The Pros and Cons of Python Virtual Environments

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Python Virtual Environments is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports keeping projects separate
- Makes reproducing setups easier
- Helps with testing against different versions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: installing packages globally
- Risk: forgetting to activate
- Risk: committing the environment folder

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as keeping projects separate, and you can manage the risks, Python Virtual Environments is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, create one environment per project and record its requirements.
