Practice Exercises for Wearable Technology

These exercises build real understanding of Wearable Technology. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble fitness tracking or notifications.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Wearable Technology and include the term sensors.
Exercise 3: Compare watchOS and Bluetooth Low Energy for health monitoring and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Explain how companion apps and data syncing differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick Wear OS and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.
