Mistakes to Avoid with Augmented and Virtual Reality

Everyone learns Augmented and Virtual Reality partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: motion sickness from low frame rates. It usually appears when someone skips head tracking and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: heavy assets. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of training simulations. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor interface design in 3D. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on input methods to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
