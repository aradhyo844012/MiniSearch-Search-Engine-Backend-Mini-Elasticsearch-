The Pros and Cons of Random Forests

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Random Forests is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports tabular predictions
- Makes feature importance ranking easier
- Helps with strong baseline models

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating importance scores as causes
- Risk: very large forests being slow
- Risk: ignoring class imbalance

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as tabular predictions, and you can manage the risks, Random Forests is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, tune the number of trees only after the basics work.
