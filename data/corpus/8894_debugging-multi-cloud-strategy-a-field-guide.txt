Debugging Multi-Cloud Strategy: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Multi-Cloud Strategy, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: doubling operational complexity, lowest-common-denominator designs, and hidden data transfer costs.
Step 3: revisit portability and vendor lock-in, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Terraform or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Multi-Cloud Strategy in work such as avoiding single-provider risk come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
