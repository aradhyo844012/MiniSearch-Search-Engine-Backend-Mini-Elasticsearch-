Combining React Native with Bluetooth Low Energy in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how React Native and Bluetooth Low Energy can work together in a single project.

React Native is a framework for building mobile apps for several platforms using JavaScript and React. Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

Imagine a project that involves cross-platform apps and fitness trackers. React Native brings components, while Bluetooth Low Energy brings advertising.

A sensible plan is to start with Expo for the React Native part and nRF Connect for the Bluetooth Low Energy part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: poorly tuned lists on one side, and weak pairing security on the other.

Picture the whole as one script performed by actors on two different stages, working alongside a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy.

Two habits help: first, test on real devices for both platforms; second, test with several phone models.
