Everything You Need to Know About Blue-Green Deployment

This page collects the essentials about Blue-Green Deployment in one place. In short, Blue-Green Deployment is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one.

Core concepts:
- blue and green environments
- traffic switching
- rollback
- database compatibility

Where it is used:
- zero-downtime releases
- quick rollbacks
- testing in production-like settings

Common problems:
- database schema mismatches
- doubling infrastructure costs
- forgetting to drain old connections

Tools to know:
- AWS CodeDeploy
- Kubernetes services
- NGINX

Remember this above all: swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
