Is A* Pathfinding Right for Your Project?

Before adopting A* Pathfinding, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that A* Pathfinding is a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining.

It is a good fit when your work involves game characters or robot navigation, and when your team understands open and closed sets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn cost so far, or when you cannot manage these risks: overestimating the heuristic, slow searches on huge maps, and ignoring moving obstacles.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether heuristics and grids and graphs are part of the answer. If they are, A* Pathfinding is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with map routing and choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
