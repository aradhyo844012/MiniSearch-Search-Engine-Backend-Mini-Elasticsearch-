Mistakes to Avoid with Command Line Productivity

Everyone learns Command Line Productivity partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: copying commands without understanding them. It usually appears when someone skips aliases and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: dangerous wildcard use. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of automating repetitive tasks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: undocumented scripts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on scripts to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: turn commands you repeat into small scripts.
