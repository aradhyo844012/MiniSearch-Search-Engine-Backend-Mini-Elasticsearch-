The Pros and Cons of Makefiles

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Makefiles is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports compiling C projects
- Makes shortcuts for common commands easier
- Helps with automating documentation builds

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: tabs versus spaces
- Risk: missing phony declarations
- Risk: hidden environment assumptions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as compiling C projects, and you can manage the risks, Makefiles is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.
