Teaching Vector Graphics (SVG) to a Teammate

Explaining Vector Graphics (SVG) to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a recipe for drawing a picture rather than a photo of it. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Vector Graphics (SVG) is images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving logos, naming paths and the viewBox as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain fill and stroke in Vector Graphics (SVG) back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, overly complex paths, and suggest this habit: set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
