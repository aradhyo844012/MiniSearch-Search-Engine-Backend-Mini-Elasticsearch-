Mistakes to Avoid with Python Virtual Environments

Everyone learns Python Virtual Environments partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: installing packages globally. It usually appears when someone skips venv and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: forgetting to activate. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of keeping projects separate. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: committing the environment folder. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on activation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: create one environment per project and record its requirements.
