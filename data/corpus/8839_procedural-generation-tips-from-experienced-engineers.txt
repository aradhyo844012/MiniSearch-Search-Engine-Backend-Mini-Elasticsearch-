Procedural Generation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Procedural Generation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip random seeds; everything else builds on it.
2. Test the case of roguelike dungeons early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit noise functions before anything else.
4. Be careful with this one: levels that are unfair or unwinnable.
5. Reach for Perlin noise first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Wave Function Collapse.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to infinite terrain as well as to Procedural Generation in general.
