Python Asyncio in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Python Asyncio.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Python Asyncio is a library for running many waiting tasks concurrently on a single thread.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn event loop, coroutines, await, and tasks.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a chef who starts several dishes and checks each one when its timer rings. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through calling many APIs at once, web servers, and network clients.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for blocking calls inside coroutines and forgetting to await, then try asyncio for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to never call blocking code directly inside a coroutine.
