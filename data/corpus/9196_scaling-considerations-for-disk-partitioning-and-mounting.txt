Scaling Considerations for Disk Partitioning and Mounting

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Disk Partitioning and Mounting changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding partitions and getting adding a new drive right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for wrong device names and mounting without fstab entries, and measure with lsblk before changing anything.

Large scale: mount points and fstab move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around separating data from the system at this stage.

Before you scale, double-check device names with lsblk before formatting. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
