System Calls for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover System Calls.

Learning goals:
1. Explain System Calls in your own words.
2. Describe user mode and kernel mode, syscall numbers, file descriptors, and libc wrappers.
3. Give examples from reading and writing files and creating processes.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: System Calls is the controlled doorway through which programs ask the operating system to do privileged work. Then draw a service window where a customer asks staff to do things in the back office on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring error codes a problem? How does syscall numbers relate to file descriptors? When would you choose strace or man pages?

Study habit worth keeping: use strace to see what your program really asks the kernel to do.

When you feel ready, compare System Calls with Endianness and write down three differences.
