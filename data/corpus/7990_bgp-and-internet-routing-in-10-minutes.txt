BGP and Internet Routing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through BGP and Internet Routing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: BGP and Internet Routing is the protocol networks use to tell each other which addresses they can reach.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn autonomous systems, route announcements, peering, and path selection.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through connecting ISPs, directing traffic between large networks, and explaining internet outages.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for route leaks and hijacked prefixes, then try bgpq4 for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to filter route announcements strictly.
