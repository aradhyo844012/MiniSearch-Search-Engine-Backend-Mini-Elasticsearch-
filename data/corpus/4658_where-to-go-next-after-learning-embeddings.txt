Where to Go Next After Learning Embeddings

You have met the basics of Embeddings. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Augmentation, which belongs to the same area as Embeddings (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of vectors, cosine similarity, dimensionality or nearest neighbor search, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around recommendations with sentence-transformers, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing models between indexing and querying.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Embeddings to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
