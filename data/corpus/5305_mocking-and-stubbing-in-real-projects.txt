Mocking and Stubbing in Real Projects

Reading about Mocking and Stubbing is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: isolating units. Here, mocks drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against over-mocking.

Scenario two: simulating failures. This time stubs matters most, and tools such as unittest.mock help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: avoiding network calls. The key idea is spies, and the biggest risk is tests that mirror the implementation.

Across all three scenarios, Mocking and Stubbing stays the same idea: replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests. Only the details change.
