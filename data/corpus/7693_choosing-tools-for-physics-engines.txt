Choosing Tools for Physics Engines

There is no single best tool for Physics Engines. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Box2D is a common first choice, especially for vehicles and ragdolls. Bullet suits teams that already work with similar technology, and PhysX is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Physics Engines by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle puzzle games well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Physics Engines, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: variable time steps.

As a rule of thumb, use a fixed time step for stable results, no matter which tool you use.
