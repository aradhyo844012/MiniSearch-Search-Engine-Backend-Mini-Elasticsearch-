Where to Go Next After Learning Stored Procedures and Triggers

You have met the basics of Stored Procedures and Triggers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about NULL Handling in SQL, which belongs to the same area as Stored Procedures and Triggers (SQL and Data Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of procedures, triggers, parameters or side effects, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around audit logging with PL/pgSQL, and note every problem you meet, starting with hidden logic that surprises developers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Stored Procedures and Triggers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
