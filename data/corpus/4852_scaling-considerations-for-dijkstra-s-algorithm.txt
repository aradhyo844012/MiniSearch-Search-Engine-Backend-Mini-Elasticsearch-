Scaling Considerations for Dijkstra's Algorithm

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Dijkstra's Algorithm changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding priority queue and getting route planning right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using it with negative edge weights and skipping the priority queue and getting slow code, and measure with Python heapq before changing anything.

Large scale: edge relaxation and visited set move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around network routing at this stage.

Before you scale, stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
