Chaos Engineering Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Chaos Engineering regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: experimenting without safeguards.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit hypotheses before anything else.
3. Do not skip steady state; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of testing resilience early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for Chaos Monkey first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Gremlin.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to validating failover as well.
