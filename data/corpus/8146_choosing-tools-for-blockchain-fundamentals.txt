Choosing Tools for Blockchain Fundamentals

There is no single best tool for Blockchain Fundamentals. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Bitcoin Core is a common first choice, especially for digital currencies. Ethereum suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Hyperledger is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Blockchain Fundamentals by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle supply chain tracking well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Blockchain Fundamentals, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: treating it as a database replacement.

As a rule of thumb, ask whether a normal database would solve the problem, no matter which tool you use.
