Debugging Modular Monoliths: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Modular Monoliths, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: modules reaching into each other's tables, no enforced boundaries, and treating modules as just folders.
Step 3: revisit module boundaries and public interfaces, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ArchUnit or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Modular Monoliths in work such as small teams come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, enforce boundaries with automated checks. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
