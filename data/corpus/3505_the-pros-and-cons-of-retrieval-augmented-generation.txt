The Pros and Cons of Retrieval-Augmented Generation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports company knowledge assistants
- Makes question answering over documents easier
- Helps with support bots

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: poor chunking
- Risk: irrelevant retrieved text
- Risk: no evaluation of the answers

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as company knowledge assistants, and you can manage the risks, Retrieval-Augmented Generation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality.
