Is Shaders Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Shaders, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Shaders is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.

It is a good fit when your work involves lighting effects or water and fire visuals, and when your team understands vertex shaders.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn fragment shaders, or when you cannot manage these risks: branches that slow the GPU, precision errors, and platform differences.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether uniforms and GLSL are part of the answer. If they are, Shaders is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with post-processing and start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
