Choosing Tools for Star Schema Modeling

There is no single best tool for Star Schema Modeling. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

dbt is a common first choice, especially for business intelligence reports. Snowflake suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Kimball techniques is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle data warehouses well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: mixing different grains.

As a rule of thumb, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else, no matter which tool you use.
