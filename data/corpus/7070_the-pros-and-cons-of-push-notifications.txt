The Pros and Cons of Push Notifications

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Push Notifications is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports order updates
- Makes chat alerts easier
- Helps with re-engaging users

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: asking for permission too early
- Risk: sending too many messages
- Risk: ignoring token refresh

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as order updates, and you can manage the risks, Push Notifications is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
