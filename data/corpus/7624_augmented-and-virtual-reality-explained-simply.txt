Augmented and Virtual Reality Explained Simply

Here is Augmented and Virtual Reality without the jargon: it is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.

Think of Augmented and Virtual Reality as wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: head tracking, rendering at high frame rates, spatial mapping, and input methods. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with head tracking, then add rendering at high frame rates.

You will meet Augmented and Virtual Reality in work such as training simulations, games, and remote collaboration.

One practical tip is to keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
