The Pros and Cons of Game AI Behavior Trees

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Game AI Behavior Trees is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports enemy behavior
- Makes companion characters easier
- Helps with simulated crowds

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: trees that become tangled
- Risk: expensive conditions checked every frame
- Risk: no way to debug decisions

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as enemy behavior, and you can manage the risks, Game AI Behavior Trees is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, visualize the tree while the game is running.
