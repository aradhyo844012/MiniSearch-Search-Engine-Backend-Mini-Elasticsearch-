Message Queues Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Message Queues.

Q: Explain Message Queues in simple words.
A: Message Queues refers to systems that let services send work to each other asynchronously through a buffer. I would add an example involving sending emails.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: losing messages without acknowledgements.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: producers, consumers, acknowledgements, and retries.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: losing messages without acknowledgements; poison messages that retry forever; and duplicate processing.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka, and Amazon SQS.
