Mistakes to Avoid with Object-Relational Mappers

Everyone learns Object-Relational Mappers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: N+1 queries. It usually appears when someone skips models and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: loading entire tables. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of building CRUD apps quickly. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hiding slow SQL. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on migrations to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: log the generated SQL during development.
