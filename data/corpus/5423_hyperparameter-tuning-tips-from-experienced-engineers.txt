Hyperparameter Tuning Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Hyperparameter Tuning regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip search spaces; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit grid search before anything else.
3. Write down why you chose Hyperparameter Tuning so the next person understands the decision.
4. Make sure you can try random search before anything fancier.
5. Reach for Optuna first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as scikit-learn GridSearchCV.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to comparing algorithms as well.
