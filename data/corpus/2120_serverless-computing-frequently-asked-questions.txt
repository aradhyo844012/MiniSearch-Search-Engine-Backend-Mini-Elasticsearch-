Serverless Computing: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Serverless Computing?
A: The term Serverless Computing refers to running code in managed functions without maintaining servers yourself.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with functions and event triggers, then move on to cold starts and pay per use.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: cold start delays; vendor lock-in; and hard local debugging.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include AWS Lambda, Cloud Functions, and Azure Functions.
