Understanding Quantum Computing Basics Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a coin spinning in the air before it lands.

In that picture, qubits is the first rule you follow, superposition is the next step, and entanglement and quantum gates keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems, and it involves details such as expecting speedups for every problem that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Qiskit to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain research on cryptography to a friend.
