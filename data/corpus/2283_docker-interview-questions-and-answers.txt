Docker Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Docker.

Q: Explain Docker in simple words.
A: Docker refers to a platform for building and running applications in containers. I would add an example involving reproducible development setups.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: Dockerfile, image layers, volumes, and networks.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: huge images.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Docker Compose, Docker Hub, and Podman.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as Dockerfile, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: How does it connect to Kubernetes?
A: Both belong to the area of Cloud and DevOps, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
