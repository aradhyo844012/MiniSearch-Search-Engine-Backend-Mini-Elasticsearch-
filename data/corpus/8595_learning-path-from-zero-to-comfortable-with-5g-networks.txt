Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with 5G Networks

This path is for a learner who has never studied 5G Networks before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (5G Networks is the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency) and learn spectrum bands and latency.

Stage 2, hands-on: install speed test tools, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving mobile broadband.

Stage 3, depth: study network slicing and small cells, then deliberately explore this problem: limited coverage on the fastest bands.

Stage 4, application: apply 5G Networks to connected vehicles, compare network simulators with 3GPP documents, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach 5G Networks to someone else, and then move on to Quantum Computing Basics.
