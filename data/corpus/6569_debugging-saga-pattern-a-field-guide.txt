Debugging Saga Pattern: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Saga Pattern, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: missing compensations, steps that are not idempotent, and flows that are hard to trace.
Step 3: revisit local transactions and compensating actions, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Temporal or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Saga Pattern in work such as order processing come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, make every step and its compensation idempotent. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
