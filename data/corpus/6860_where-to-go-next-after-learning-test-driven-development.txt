Where to Go Next After Learning Test-Driven Development

You have met the basics of Test-Driven Development. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Pair Programming, which belongs to the same area as Test-Driven Development (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of red, green, refactor, small steps, test first or fast feedback, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around preventing regressions with pytest, and note every problem you meet, starting with writing tests that are too large.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Test-Driven Development to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
