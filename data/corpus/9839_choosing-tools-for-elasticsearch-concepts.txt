Choosing Tools for Elasticsearch Concepts

There is no single best tool for Elasticsearch Concepts. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Elasticsearch is a common first choice, especially for site search. Kibana suits teams that already work with similar technology, and OpenSearch is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Elasticsearch Concepts by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle log analytics well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Elasticsearch Concepts, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too many shards.

As a rule of thumb, define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults, no matter which tool you use.
