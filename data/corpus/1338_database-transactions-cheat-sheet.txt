Database Transactions Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Database Transactions.

- Definition: groups of operations that succeed or fail together.
- Core ideas: atomicity; consistency; isolation; durability.
- Use it for: transferring money between accounts; placing orders; updating inventory.
- Watch out for: holding transactions open too long; ignoring isolation levels; forgetting to roll back on errors.
- Tools: PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, JDBC.
- Related topic: Database Sharding.
- Quick tip: practice atomicity until it feels automatic.
