Skills to Build Around VPNs

Knowing the definition of VPNs is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with tunneling and encryption, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with WireGuard on a VPNs task, and try at least one alternative such as OpenVPN.

Judgment: learn to decide when VPNs fits a problem such as remote work access, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a private tunnel under a public road.

Habits: test for DNS leaks after connecting, and write down what you learn after each project.
