Content Security Policy in Real Projects

Reading about Content Security Policy is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: reducing the impact of XSS. Here, directives drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against allowing unsafe-inline.

Scenario two: blocking unwanted scripts. This time nonces matters most, and tools such as browser DevTools help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: monitoring policy violations. The key idea is report-only mode, and the biggest risk is policies too broad to help.

Across all three scenarios, Content Security Policy stays the same idea: a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load. Only the details change.
