Choosing Tools for Apache Spark

There is no single best tool for Apache Spark. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PySpark is a common first choice, especially for large ETL jobs. Databricks suits teams that already work with similar technology, and the Spark UI is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle machine learning on big data well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: data skew.

As a rule of thumb, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine, no matter which tool you use.
