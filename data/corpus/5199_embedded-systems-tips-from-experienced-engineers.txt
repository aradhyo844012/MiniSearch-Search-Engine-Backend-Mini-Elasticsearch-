Embedded Systems Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Embedded Systems regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Embedded Systems so the next person understands the decision.
2. Do not skip microcontrollers; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
4. Test the case of appliances early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for C first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as FreeRTOS.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to medical devices as well.
