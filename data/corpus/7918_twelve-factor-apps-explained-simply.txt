Twelve-Factor Apps Explained Simply

If Twelve-Factor Apps sounds intimidating, relax. It is a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Twelve-Factor Apps as a checklist for packing a suitcase that fits any airline. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: config in the environment, stateless processes, backing services, and disposability. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with config in the environment, then add stateless processes.

People reach for Twelve-Factor Apps when they work on building web services, deploying to platforms, and keeping environments consistent.

One practical tip is to keep config out of the code.
