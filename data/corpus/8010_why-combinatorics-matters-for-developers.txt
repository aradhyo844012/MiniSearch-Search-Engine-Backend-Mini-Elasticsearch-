Why Combinatorics Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Combinatorics make better decisions, because Combinatorics is the art of counting arrangements and selections.

Three situations show why it matters. First, analyzing algorithms. Second, probability. Third, estimating password strength. In each case, a solid grasp of permutations and combinations separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: double counting, mixing order and no-order cases, and ignoring repetition.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Python itertools, math.comb, and SymPy cover most needs.
