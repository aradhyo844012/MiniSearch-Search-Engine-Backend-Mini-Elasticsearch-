Practice Exercises for MQTT

These exercises build real understanding of MQTT. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing MQTT and include the term publish and subscribe.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble sensor networks or smart home devices.
Exercise 3: Explain how brokers and quality of service levels differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Mosquitto and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: unsecured brokers. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.
