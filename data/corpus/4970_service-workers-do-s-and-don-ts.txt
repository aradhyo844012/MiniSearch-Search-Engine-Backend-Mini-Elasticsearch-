Service Workers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Service Workers.

Do:
- Learn registration before anything else.
- Try Workbox on a small example.
- Make it a habit to version your caches so old files can be removed.
- Test the case of offline support early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: caching stale files forever.
- Ignore this: bugs surviving across deployments.
- Ignore this: forgetting that HTTPS is required.
- Adopt Service Workers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Service Workers, a little patience with install and activate events pays off more than any clever shortcut.
