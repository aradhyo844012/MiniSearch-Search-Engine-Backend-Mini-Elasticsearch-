Scaling Considerations for NAT

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how NAT changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding private addresses and getting home routers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for breaking peer-to-peer connections and exhausted translation tables, and measure with iptables before changing anything.

Large scale: port mapping and port forwarding move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around sharing limited IPv4 addresses at this stage.

Before you scale, document every port forward you create. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
