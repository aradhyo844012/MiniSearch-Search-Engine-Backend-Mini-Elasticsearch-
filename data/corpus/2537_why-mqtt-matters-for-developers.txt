Why MQTT Matters for Developers

Developers who understand MQTT make better decisions, because MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.

Three situations show why it matters. First, sensor networks. Second, smart home devices. Third, telemetry. In each case, a solid grasp of publish and subscribe and topics separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: unsecured brokers, messy topic names, and wrong quality of service choices.

Good tools exist, including Mosquitto, paho-mqtt, and MQTT Explorer, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
