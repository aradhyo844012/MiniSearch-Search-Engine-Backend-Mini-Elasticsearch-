Where to Go Next After Learning Statistical Significance

You have met the basics of Statistical Significance. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Feature Stores, which belongs to the same area as Statistical Significance (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of p-values, the null hypothesis, confidence intervals or effect size, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around comparing model performance with SciPy, and note every problem you meet, starting with mistaking significance for importance.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Statistical Significance to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
