The Pros and Cons of Jupyter Notebooks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Jupyter Notebooks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports exploring data
- Makes teaching and demos easier
- Helps with sharing analysis

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: running cells out of order
- Risk: hidden state
- Risk: committing outputs that contain secrets

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as exploring data, and you can manage the risks, Jupyter Notebooks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
