The Pros and Cons of Webhooks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Webhooks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports payment notifications
- Makes CI triggers easier
- Helps with chat integrations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: not verifying signatures
- Risk: slow handlers causing timeouts
- Risk: processing duplicate events

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as payment notifications, and you can manage the risks, Webhooks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
