Where to Go Next After Learning Feature Flags

You have met the basics of Feature Flags. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Autoscaling, which belongs to the same area as Feature Flags (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of flag evaluation, targeting rules, gradual rollout or flag cleanup, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around A/B tests with LaunchDarkly, and note every problem you meet, starting with never removing old flags.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Feature Flags to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
