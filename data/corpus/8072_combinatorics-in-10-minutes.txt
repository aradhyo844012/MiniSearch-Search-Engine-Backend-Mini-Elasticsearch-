Combinatorics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Combinatorics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Combinatorics is the art of counting arrangements and selections.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn permutations, combinations, the pigeonhole principle, and inclusion-exclusion.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine counting the ways to seat guests at a table. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through analyzing algorithms, probability, and estimating password strength.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for double counting and mixing order and no-order cases, then try Python itertools for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
