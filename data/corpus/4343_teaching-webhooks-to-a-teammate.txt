Teaching Webhooks to a Teammate

Explaining Webhooks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving payment notifications, naming event payloads and signature verification as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain retries in Webhooks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, not verifying signatures, and suggest this habit: respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
