Data Augmentation Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Data Augmentation. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.
Key terms: transformations, label preservation, synthetic samples, and augmentation pipelines.
Typical uses: image models, text classification, and small datasets.
Risks: changes that alter the label, augmenting the test set, and unrealistic transformations.
Tools: Albumentations, torchvision, and nlpaug.

Mental picture: practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields.

Habit to build: augment only the training data.
