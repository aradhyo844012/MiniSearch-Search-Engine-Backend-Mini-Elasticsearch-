Where to Go Next After Learning Hexagonal Architecture

You have met the basics of Hexagonal Architecture. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Event-Driven Architecture, which belongs to the same area as Hexagonal Architecture (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of ports, adapters, the core domain or dependency direction, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around swapping databases with Spring, and note every problem you meet, starting with too many layers for small apps.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Hexagonal Architecture to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
