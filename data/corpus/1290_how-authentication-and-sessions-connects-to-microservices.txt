How Authentication and Sessions Connects to Microservices

Authentication and Sessions and Microservices are closely connected parts of Backend and APIs. Seeing how they fit together helps you learn both faster.

A project involving logging in to web apps may also involve large teams working in parallel. In such a project, credentials and service boundaries often influence each other.

Start with Authentication and Sessions: the term refers to the process of proving who a user is and remembering them between requests. Then look at Microservices, which refers to an architecture that splits an application into small services that can be deployed independently.

A useful exercise is to take one example, such as protecting admin pages, and explain how APIs would change the design.

Common problems are shared too. For Authentication and Sessions, watch for this: never expiring sessions. For Microservices, watch for this: chatty services. Good APIs are boring, predictable and well documented.

Spend a few hours on each topic, then combine them in one small project using Flask-Login or Docker.
