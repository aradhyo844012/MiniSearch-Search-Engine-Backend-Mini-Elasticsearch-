A/B Testing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through A/B Testing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn control and variant, random assignment, metrics, and sample size.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through testing page designs, comparing ranking algorithms, and evaluating new features.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for stopping tests early and testing too many variants, then try Optimizely for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
