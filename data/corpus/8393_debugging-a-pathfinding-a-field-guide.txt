Debugging A* Pathfinding: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around A* Pathfinding, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: overestimating the heuristic, slow searches on huge maps, and ignoring moving obstacles.
Step 3: revisit open and closed sets and cost so far, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Unity NavMesh or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving A* Pathfinding in work such as game characters come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
