A Short History of Mobile App Performance

Ideas behind Mobile App Performance developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. The term Mobile App Performance refers to making mobile apps start quickly, run smoothly and use little battery. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Mobile users expect apps to start quickly and respond immediately.

To understand Mobile App Performance, start with startup time. Then move on to frame rate, and finally look at memory use and network calls. A good way to remember startup time is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

If you are comfortable with Mobile App Performance, explore Android Basics next. The two often appear in the same project.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Mobile App Performance.
