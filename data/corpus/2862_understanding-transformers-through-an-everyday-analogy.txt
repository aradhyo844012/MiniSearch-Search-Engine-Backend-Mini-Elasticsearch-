Understanding Transformers Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word.

In that picture, self-attention is the first rule you follow, positional encoding is the next step, and encoder and decoder blocks and tokens keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Transformers is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other, and it involves details such as huge compute requirements that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Hugging Face to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain language translation to a friend.
