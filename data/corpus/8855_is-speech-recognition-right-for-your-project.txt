Is Speech Recognition Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Speech Recognition, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Speech Recognition is converting spoken language into text using machine learning.

It is a good fit when your work involves voice assistants or captioning, and when your team understands audio features.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn acoustic models, or when you cannot manage these risks: accents and background noise, domain-specific vocabulary, and privacy of recordings.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether language models and word error rate are part of the answer. If they are, Speech Recognition is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with dictation and measure word error rate on audio like your real users'.
