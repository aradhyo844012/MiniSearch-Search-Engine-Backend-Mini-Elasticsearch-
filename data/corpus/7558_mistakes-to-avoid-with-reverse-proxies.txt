Mistakes to Avoid with Reverse Proxies

Everyone learns Reverse Proxies partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: losing the client IP address. It usually appears when someone skips upstream servers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: wrong timeouts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of hiding backend servers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: exposing internal paths. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on caching to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
