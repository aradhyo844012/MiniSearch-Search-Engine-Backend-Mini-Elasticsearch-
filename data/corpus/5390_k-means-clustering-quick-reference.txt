K-Means Clustering Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for K-Means Clustering. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points.
Key terms: centroids, distance measures, choosing k, and iterations.
Typical uses: customer segmentation, image compression, and grouping documents.
Risks: choosing k blindly, unscaled features, and sensitivity to starting points.
Tools: scikit-learn, NumPy, and matplotlib.

Mental picture: dividing students into study groups around the most central member.

Habit to build: scale features before clustering.
