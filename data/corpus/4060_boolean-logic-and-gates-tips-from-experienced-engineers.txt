Boolean Logic and Gates Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Boolean Logic and Gates regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit truth tables before anything else.
2. Do not skip AND, OR and NOT; everything else builds on it.
3. Reach for Logisim first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Python bitwise operators.
4. Write down why you chose Boolean Logic and Gates so the next person understands the decision.
5. Test the case of writing conditions early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to designing circuits as well.
