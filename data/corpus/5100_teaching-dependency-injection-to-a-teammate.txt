Teaching Dependency Injection to a Teammate

Explaining Dependency Injection to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving easier unit testing, naming constructor injection and interfaces as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain containers in Dependency Injection back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, hiding too much behind containers, and suggest this habit: inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
