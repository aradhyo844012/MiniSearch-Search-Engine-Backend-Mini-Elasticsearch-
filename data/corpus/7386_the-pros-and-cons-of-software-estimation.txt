The Pros and Cons of Software Estimation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Software Estimation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sprint planning
- Makes setting deadlines easier
- Helps with negotiating scope

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating estimates as promises
- Risk: ignoring unknowns
- Risk: estimating alone

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sprint planning, and you can manage the risks, Software Estimation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, give ranges instead of single numbers.
