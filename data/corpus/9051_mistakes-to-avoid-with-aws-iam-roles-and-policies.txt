Mistakes to Avoid with AWS IAM Roles and Policies

Everyone learns AWS IAM Roles and Policies partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: wildcard permissions. It usually appears when someone skips users and roles and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: long-lived access keys. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of securing deployments. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unused roles. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on temporary credentials to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.
