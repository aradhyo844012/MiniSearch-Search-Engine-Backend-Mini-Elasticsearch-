Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Blockchain Fundamentals

This path is for a learner who has never studied Blockchain Fundamentals before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes) and learn blocks and hashes.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Bitcoin Core, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving digital currencies.

Stage 3, depth: study consensus and decentralization, then deliberately explore this problem: treating it as a database replacement.

Stage 4, application: apply Blockchain Fundamentals to supply chain tracking, compare Ethereum with Hyperledger, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Blockchain Fundamentals to someone else, and then move on to Digital Twins.
