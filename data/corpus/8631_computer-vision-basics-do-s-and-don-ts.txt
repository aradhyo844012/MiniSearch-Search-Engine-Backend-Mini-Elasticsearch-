Computer Vision Basics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Computer Vision Basics.

Do:
- Learn pixels before anything else.
- Try OpenCV on a small example.
- Make it a habit to visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
- Test the case of face detection early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: lighting changes.
- Ignore this: biased datasets.
- Ignore this: overfitting to a small set of photos.
- Adopt Computer Vision Basics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Computer Vision Basics, a little patience with filters and edges pays off more than any clever shortcut.
