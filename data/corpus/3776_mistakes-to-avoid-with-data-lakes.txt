Mistakes to Avoid with Data Lakes

Everyone learns Data Lakes partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: turning into a data swamp. It usually appears when someone skips raw storage and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no catalog or ownership. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of storing logs and events. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor access controls. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on metadata catalogs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.
