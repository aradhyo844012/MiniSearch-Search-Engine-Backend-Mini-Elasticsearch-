Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Quantum Computing Basics

This path is for a learner who has never studied Quantum Computing Basics before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems) and learn qubits and superposition.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Qiskit, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving research on cryptography.

Stage 3, depth: study entanglement and quantum gates, then deliberately explore this problem: expecting speedups for every problem.

Stage 4, application: apply Quantum Computing Basics to simulating molecules, compare Cirq with IBM Quantum, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Quantum Computing Basics to someone else, and then move on to Wearable Technology.
