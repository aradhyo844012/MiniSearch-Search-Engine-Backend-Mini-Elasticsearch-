Reverse Proxies Explained Simply

Here is Reverse Proxies without the jargon: it is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.

Think of Reverse Proxies as a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: upstream servers, proxy headers, TLS termination, and caching. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with upstream servers, then add proxy headers.

You will meet Reverse Proxies in work such as hiding backend servers, terminating HTTPS, and routing requests to several apps.

One practical tip is to forward the real client IP in a trusted header.

After Reverse Proxies, a natural next topic is VPNs.
