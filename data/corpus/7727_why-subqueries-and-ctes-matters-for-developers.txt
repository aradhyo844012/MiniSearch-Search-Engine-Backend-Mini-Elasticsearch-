Why Subqueries and CTEs Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Subqueries and CTEs make better decisions, because Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.

Three situations show why it matters. First, breaking complex reports into steps. Second, filtering with computed results. Third, walking recursive hierarchies. In each case, a solid grasp of subqueries and WITH clauses separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: correlated subqueries that run slowly, deep nesting, and assuming CTEs are always materialized.

Good tools exist, including PostgreSQL, SQLite, and dbt, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
