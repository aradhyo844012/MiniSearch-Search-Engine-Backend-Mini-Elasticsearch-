Regions and Availability Zones for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Regions and Availability Zones.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Regions and Availability Zones in your own words.
2. Describe regions, availability zones, latency, and data residency.
3. Give examples from placing services near users and surviving data center failures.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Regions and Availability Zones is the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources. Then draw branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is putting everything in one zone a problem? How does availability zones relate to latency? When would you choose AWS Regions or Azure regions?

Study habit worth keeping: spread critical services across at least two zones.

When you feel ready, compare Regions and Availability Zones with High Availability Design and write down three differences.
