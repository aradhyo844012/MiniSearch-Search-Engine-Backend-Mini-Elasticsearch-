Mistakes to Avoid with Stored Procedures and Triggers

Everyone learns Stored Procedures and Triggers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hidden logic that surprises developers. It usually appears when someone skips procedures and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: hard-to-test code. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of enforcing rules close to the data. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: trigger chains. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on side effects to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep triggers small and well documented.
