A Short History of Database Replication

Ideas behind Database Replication developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Many students first meet Database Replication in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to copying data from one database server to others to improve availability and read capacity.
