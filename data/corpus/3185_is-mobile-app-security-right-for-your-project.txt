Is Mobile App Security Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Mobile App Security, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks.

It is a good fit when your work involves banking apps or health apps, and when your team understands secure storage.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn certificate pinning, or when you cannot manage these risks: storing tokens in plain text, hard-coded API keys, and requesting unnecessary permissions.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether obfuscation and permissions are part of the answer. If they are, Mobile App Security is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with enterprise apps and store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.
