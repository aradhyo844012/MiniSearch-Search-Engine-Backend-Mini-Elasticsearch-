Scaling Considerations for GPUs and Parallelism

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how GPUs and Parallelism changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding threads and blocks and getting training neural networks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow data transfers and branch-heavy code, and measure with CUDA before changing anything.

Large scale: memory transfers and SIMD move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around rendering graphics at this stage.

Before you scale, minimize copies between CPU and GPU memory. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
