Debugging Cron Jobs and Scheduling: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Cron Jobs and Scheduling, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: assuming cron has your shell environment, overlapping runs of slow jobs, and no logging of failures.
Step 3: revisit crontab syntax and schedule fields, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use crontab or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Cron Jobs and Scheduling in work such as nightly backups come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
