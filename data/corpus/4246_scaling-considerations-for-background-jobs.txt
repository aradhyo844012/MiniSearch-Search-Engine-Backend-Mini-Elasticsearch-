Scaling Considerations for Background Jobs

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Background Jobs changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding queues and getting sending emails right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for jobs that are not idempotent and unbounded retries, and measure with Celery before changing anything.

Large scale: retries and scheduling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around resizing images at this stage.

Before you scale, give every job a timeout and a retry limit. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
