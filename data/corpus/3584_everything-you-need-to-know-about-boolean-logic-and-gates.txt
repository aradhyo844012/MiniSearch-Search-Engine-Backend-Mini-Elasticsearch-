Everything You Need to Know About Boolean Logic and Gates

This page collects the essentials about Boolean Logic and Gates in one place. In short, Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from.

Core concepts:
- AND, OR and NOT
- truth tables
- De Morgan's laws
- logic gates

Where it is used:
- writing conditions
- designing circuits
- simplifying expressions

Common problems:
- operator precedence mistakes
- forgetting short-circuit behavior
- negating complex conditions incorrectly

Tools to know:
- Logisim
- Python bitwise operators
- Karnaugh maps

Remember this above all: light switches wired in series or in parallel is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
