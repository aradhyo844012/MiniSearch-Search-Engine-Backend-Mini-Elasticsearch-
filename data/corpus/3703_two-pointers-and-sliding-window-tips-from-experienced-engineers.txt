Two Pointers and Sliding Window Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Two Pointers and Sliding Window regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of finding pairs with a given sum early, before the design hardens.
2. Be careful with this one: moving the wrong pointer.
3. Make sure you can write down the window invariant before coding the loop.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit window boundaries before anything else.
5. Write down why you chose Two Pointers and Sliding Window so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to longest substring problems as well.
