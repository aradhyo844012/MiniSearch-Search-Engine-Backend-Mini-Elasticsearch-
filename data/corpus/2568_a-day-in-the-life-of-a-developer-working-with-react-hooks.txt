A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with React Hooks

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on React Hooks.

Morning: Arjun starts by reviewing a ticket about managing form input. Before touching any code, Arjun re-reads the notes on useState to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Arjun pairs with a teammate to explore useEffect with React DevTools. A small test exposes the problem of missing dependencies in effects, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving fetching data on load. Arjun sketches sticky notes a component keeps between redraws on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Arjun writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to enable the hooks lint rules from the start.

Days like this are typical. React Hooks is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
