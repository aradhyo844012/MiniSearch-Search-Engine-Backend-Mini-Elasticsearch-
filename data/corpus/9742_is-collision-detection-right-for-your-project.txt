Is Collision Detection Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Collision Detection, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Collision Detection is the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap.

It is a good fit when your work involves platformers or shooters, and when your team understands bounding boxes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn broad and narrow phases, or when you cannot manage these risks: tunneling through thin objects, checking every pair of objects, and inconsistent hitboxes.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether spatial partitioning and collision response are part of the answer. If they are, Collision Detection is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with physics puzzles and use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
