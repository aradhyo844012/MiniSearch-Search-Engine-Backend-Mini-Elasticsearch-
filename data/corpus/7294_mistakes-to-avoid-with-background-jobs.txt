Mistakes to Avoid with Background Jobs

Everyone learns Background Jobs partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: jobs that are not idempotent. It usually appears when someone skips queues and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unbounded retries. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sending emails. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: losing jobs on restarts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on scheduling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
