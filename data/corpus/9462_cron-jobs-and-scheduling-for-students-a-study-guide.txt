Cron Jobs and Scheduling for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Cron Jobs and Scheduling.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Cron Jobs and Scheduling in your own words.
2. Describe crontab syntax, schedule fields, environment differences, and output redirection.
3. Give examples from nightly backups and log rotation.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Cron Jobs and Scheduling is a Unix tool that runs commands automatically at scheduled times. Then draw an alarm clock that starts chores for you on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is assuming cron has your shell environment a problem? How does schedule fields relate to environment differences? When would you choose crontab or systemd timers?

Study habit: redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths.

When you feel ready, compare Cron Jobs and Scheduling with SSH and Remote Access and write down three differences.
