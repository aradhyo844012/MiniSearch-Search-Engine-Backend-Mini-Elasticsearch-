Choosing Tools for Assembly Language Basics

There is no single best tool for Assembly Language Basics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GDB is a common first choice, especially for understanding compiled code. objdump suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Compiler Explorer is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle debugging crashes well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: wrong calling conventions.

As a rule of thumb, read the assembly of a tiny C function to see what the compiler does, no matter which tool you use.
