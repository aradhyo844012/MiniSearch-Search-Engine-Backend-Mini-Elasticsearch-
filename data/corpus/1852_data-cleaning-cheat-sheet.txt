Data Cleaning Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Data Cleaning.

- Definition: finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data.
- Core ideas: missing values; duplicates; inconsistent formats; outliers.
- Use it for: preparing logs; merging data sources; improving model inputs.
- Watch out for: dropping data without checking why it is missing; cleaning by hand without a record; changing the original files.
- Tools: pandas, OpenRefine, regular expressions.
- Related topic: Pandas DataFrames.
- Quick tip: practice missing values until it feels automatic.
