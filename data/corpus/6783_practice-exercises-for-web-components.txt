Practice Exercises for Web Components

These exercises build real understanding of Web Components. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Lit and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Compare Stencil and browser DevTools for embeddable tools and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble design system components or widgets reused across frameworks.
Exercise 4: Explain how templates and slots differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: styling surprises across the shadow boundary. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Web Components is browser-native custom elements with their own markup, style and behavior.
