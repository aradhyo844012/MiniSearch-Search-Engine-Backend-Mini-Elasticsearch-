Practice Exercises for Idempotency

These exercises build real understanding of Idempotency. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Idempotency and include the term idempotency keys.
Exercise 2: Pick Stripe idempotency keys and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble payment APIs or message processing.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: keys that expire too soon. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Compare Redis and database unique constraints for retrying failed requests and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once.
