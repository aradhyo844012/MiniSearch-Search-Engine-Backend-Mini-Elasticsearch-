Where to Go Next After Learning Color Spaces

You have met the basics of Color Spaces. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Rasterization, which belongs to the same area as Color Spaces (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of RGB, HSL, CMYK or gamma, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around photo editing with browser color pickers, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring gamma.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Color Spaces to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
