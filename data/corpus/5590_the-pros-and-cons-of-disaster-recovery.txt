The Pros and Cons of Disaster Recovery

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Disaster Recovery is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports surviving regional outages
- Makes meeting compliance needs easier
- Helps with protecting critical data

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: untested plans
- Risk: outdated runbooks
- Risk: dependencies missed in the plan

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as surviving regional outages, and you can manage the risks, Disaster Recovery is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
