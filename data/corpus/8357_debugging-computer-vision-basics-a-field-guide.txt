Debugging Computer Vision Basics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Computer Vision Basics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: lighting changes, biased datasets, and overfitting to a small set of photos.
Step 3: revisit pixels and filters and edges, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use OpenCV or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Computer Vision Basics in work such as face detection come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
