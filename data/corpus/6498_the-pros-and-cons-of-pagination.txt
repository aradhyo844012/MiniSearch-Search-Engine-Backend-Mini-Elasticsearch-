The Pros and Cons of Pagination

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Pagination is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports search results
- Makes activity feeds easier
- Helps with admin tables

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: offsets getting slow on huge tables
- Risk: unstable sort order
- Risk: missing total counts when users need them

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as search results, and you can manage the risks, Pagination is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
