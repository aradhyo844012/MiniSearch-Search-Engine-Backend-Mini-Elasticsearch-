Mistakes to Avoid with Data Quality Checks

Everyone learns Data Quality Checks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: alerts nobody reads. It usually appears when someone skips null checks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: checks only at the end of pipelines. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting dashboards. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no ownership of failures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on schema validation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
