Is Go Goroutines Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Go Goroutines, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Go Goroutines is lightweight threads managed by the Go runtime that run functions concurrently.

It is a good fit when your work involves servers handling many connections or parallel data processing, and when your team understands goroutines.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn channels, or when you cannot manage these risks: goroutine leaks, data races, and unbuffered channels causing deadlocks.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether select statements and wait groups are part of the answer. If they are, Go Goroutines is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with background workers and run your tests with the race detector turned on.
