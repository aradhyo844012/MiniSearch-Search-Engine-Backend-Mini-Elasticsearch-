Recommendation Systems Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Recommendation Systems regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Recommendation Systems so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for Surprise first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as implicit.
3. Do not skip collaborative filtering; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of streaming services early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit content-based filtering before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to online shops as well as to Recommendation Systems in general.
