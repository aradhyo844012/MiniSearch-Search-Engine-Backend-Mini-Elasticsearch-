Why Service Mesh Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Service Mesh make better decisions, because Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.

Three situations show why it matters. First, securing service traffic. Second, retries and timeouts. Third, fine-grained routing. In each case, a solid grasp of sidecar proxies and mutual TLS separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: added latency and complexity, adopting it too early, and hard-to-debug proxy configs.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Istio, Linkerd, and Envoy cover most needs.
