Teaching Support Vector Machines to a Teammate

Explaining Support Vector Machines to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: drawing the widest road between two neighborhoods. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Support Vector Machines is models that classify data by finding the boundary with the widest margin between classes. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving text classification, naming margin and support vectors as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain kernels in Support Vector Machines back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow training on large data, and suggest this habit: scale features and try a linear kernel first.
