Vector Graphics (SVG) Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Vector Graphics (SVG) feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Vector Graphics (SVG): images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size.
paths: a core term, most visible in logos.
the viewBox: a core term that beginners often confuse with fill and stroke.
fill and stroke: a core term that connects to transforms.
transforms: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Inkscape: a tool commonly used for icons.

Come back to this Vector Graphics (SVG) list whenever documentation for SVGO uses a word you do not recognize.
