Internationalization Explained Simply

Here is Internationalization without the jargon: it is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions.

Think of Internationalization as a stage set where the backdrop can be swapped for each audience. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: locale detection, translation files, pluralization, and date and number formatting. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with locale detection, then add translation files.

People reach for Internationalization when they work on global products, multi-language websites, and regional pricing.

One practical tip is to move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
