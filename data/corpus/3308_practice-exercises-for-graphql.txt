Practice Exercises for GraphQL

These exercises build real understanding of GraphQL. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how resolvers and types differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: the N+1 query problem. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Pick Apollo and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare GraphQL Playground and Hasura for flexible dashboards and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing GraphQL and include the term schema.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.
