Mistakes to Avoid with Push Notifications

Everyone learns Push Notifications partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: asking for permission too early. It usually appears when someone skips device tokens and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: sending too many messages. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of order updates. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring token refresh. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on permissions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
