Blockchain Fundamentals Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Blockchain Fundamentals feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Blockchain Fundamentals: a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.
blocks: a core term, most visible in digital currencies.
hashes: a core term that beginners often confuse with consensus.
consensus: a core term that connects to decentralization.
decentralization: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Bitcoin Core: a tool commonly used for supply chain tracking.

Come back to this Blockchain Fundamentals list whenever documentation for Ethereum uses a word you do not recognize.
