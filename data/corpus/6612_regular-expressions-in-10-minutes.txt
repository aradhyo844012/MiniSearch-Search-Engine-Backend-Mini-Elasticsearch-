Regular Expressions in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Regular Expressions.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn character classes, quantifiers, groups, and anchors.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a stencil that only lets certain shapes of text show through. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through validating input, searching logs, and extracting fields from text.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for catastrophic backtracking and patterns that are too greedy, then try the Python re module for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to build patterns in small steps and test each one.
