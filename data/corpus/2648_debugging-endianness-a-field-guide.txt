Debugging Endianness: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Endianness, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: mixing byte orders, assuming all machines are alike, and forgetting to convert before sending.
Step 3: revisit big-endian and little-endian, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python struct or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Endianness in work such as network protocols come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, always specify byte order in file and protocol formats. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
