Everything You Need to Know About Responsible AI and Bias

This page collects the essentials about Responsible AI and Bias in one place. In short, Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe.

Core concepts:
- bias in data
- fairness metrics
- transparency
- human oversight

Where it is used:
- hiring tools
- lending models
- healthcare decisions

Common problems:
- assuming data is neutral
- testing only on average performance
- giving people no way to appeal

Tools to know:
- Fairlearn
- AI Fairness 360
- model cards

Remember this above all: a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to test performance separately for different groups.
