Practice Exercises for Distributed Locks

These exercises build real understanding of Distributed Locks. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: locks that never expire. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Distributed Locks and include the term lease times.
Exercise 3: Explain how lock owners and timeouts differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Redis and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare ZooKeeper and etcd for selecting a leader and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Distributed Locks is mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines.
