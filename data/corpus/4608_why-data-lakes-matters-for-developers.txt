Why Data Lakes Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Data Lakes make better decisions, because Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed.

Three situations show why it matters. First, storing logs and events. Second, machine learning datasets. Third, analytics across many sources. In each case, a solid grasp of raw storage and schema on read separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: turning into a data swamp, no catalog or ownership, and poor access controls.

Good tools exist, including Amazon S3, Delta Lake, and Apache Iceberg, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
