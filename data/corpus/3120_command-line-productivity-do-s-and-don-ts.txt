Command Line Productivity Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Command Line Productivity.

Do:
- Learn aliases before anything else.
- Try bash on a small example.
- Make it a habit to turn commands you repeat into small scripts.
- Test the case of automating repetitive tasks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: copying commands without understanding them.
- Ignore this: dangerous wildcard use.
- Ignore this: undocumented scripts.
- Adopt Command Line Productivity just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Command Line Productivity, a little patience with pipes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
