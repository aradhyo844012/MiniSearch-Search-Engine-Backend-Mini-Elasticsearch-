Linear Algebra for Programmers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Linear Algebra for Programmers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Linear Algebra for Programmers in your own words.
2. Describe vectors, matrices, dot products, and eigenvalues.
3. Give examples from 3D graphics and machine learning.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning. Then draw a set of arrows and stretching machines acting on space on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing row and column conventions a problem? How does matrices relate to dot products? When would you choose NumPy or MATLAB?

Study habit worth keeping: check shapes before multiplying.

When you feel ready, compare Linear Algebra for Programmers with Number Theory and RSA and write down three differences.
