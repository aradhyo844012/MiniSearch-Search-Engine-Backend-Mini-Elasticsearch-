The Pros and Cons of API Versioning

No technology is free of trade-offs, and API Versioning is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports public APIs
- Makes mobile apps that cannot update instantly easier
- Helps with partner integrations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: versioning too often
- Risk: removing fields without notice
- Risk: having no deprecation schedule

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as public APIs, and you can manage the risks, API Versioning is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, announce deprecations with a clear sunset date.
