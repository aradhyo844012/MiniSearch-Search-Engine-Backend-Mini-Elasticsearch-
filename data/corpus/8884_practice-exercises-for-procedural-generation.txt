Practice Exercises for Procedural Generation

These exercises build real understanding of Procedural Generation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: levels that are unfair or unwinnable. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble roguelike dungeons or infinite terrain.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Procedural Generation and include the term random seeds.
Exercise 4: Pick Perlin noise and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare Wave Function Collapse and Unity for varied item drops and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Procedural Generation is creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms.
