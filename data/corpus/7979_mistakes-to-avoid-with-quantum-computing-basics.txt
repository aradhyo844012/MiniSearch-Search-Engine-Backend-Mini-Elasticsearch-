Mistakes to Avoid with Quantum Computing Basics

Everyone learns Quantum Computing Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: expecting speedups for every problem. It usually appears when someone skips qubits and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: noise and errors. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of research on cryptography. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hype about current machines. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on quantum gates to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
