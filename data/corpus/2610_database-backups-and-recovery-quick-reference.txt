Database Backups and Recovery Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Database Backups and Recovery. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.
Key terms: full and incremental backups, recovery point objective, recovery time objective, and restore testing.
Typical uses: recovering deleted tables, migrating servers, and meeting compliance rules.
Risks: never testing restores, storing backups on the same disk, and keeping credentials inside backup scripts.
Tools: pg_dump, mysqldump, and AWS Backup.

Mental picture: a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home.

Habit to build: schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
