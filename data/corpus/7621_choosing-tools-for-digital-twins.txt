Choosing Tools for Digital Twins

There is no single best tool for Digital Twins. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Azure Digital Twins is a common first choice, especially for factory monitoring. Unity suits teams that already work with similar technology, and MQTT is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Digital Twins by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle building management well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Digital Twins, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: stale data.

As a rule of thumb, define the question the twin must answer first, no matter which tool you use.
