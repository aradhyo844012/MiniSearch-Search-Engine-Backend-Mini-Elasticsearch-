Feature Flags Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Feature Flags.

Do:
- Learn flag evaluation before anything else.
- Try LaunchDarkly on a small example.
- Make it a habit to give every flag an owner and a removal date.
- Test the case of dark launches early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: never removing old flags.
- Ignore this: flags that depend on each other.
- Ignore this: untested flag combinations.
- Adopt Feature Flags just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Feature Flags, a little patience with targeting rules pays off more than any clever shortcut.
