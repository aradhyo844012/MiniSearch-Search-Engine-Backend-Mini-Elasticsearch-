Autocomplete Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Autocomplete.

Q: Explain Autocomplete in simple words.
A: Autocomplete refers to suggesting likely completions while a person types a query. I would add an example involving search boxes.

Q: How does it connect to Edit Distance?
A: Both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: slow suggestions; offensive or irrelevant completions; and ignoring typos in the prefix.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: tries, Redis sorted sets, and Elasticsearch suggesters.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: slow suggestions.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as prefixes, and add tests aimed at the known risks.
