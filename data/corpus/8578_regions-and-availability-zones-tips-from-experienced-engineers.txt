Regions and Availability Zones Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Regions and Availability Zones regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit availability zones before anything else.
2. Write down why you chose Regions and Availability Zones so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of placing services near users early, before the design hardens.
4. Be careful with this one: putting everything in one zone.
5. Make sure you can spread critical services across at least two zones.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to surviving data center failures as well as to Regions and Availability Zones in general.
