Where to Go Next After Learning MLOps Pipelines

You have met the basics of MLOps Pipelines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about AI Agents, which belongs to the same area as MLOps Pipelines (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of data versioning, training pipelines, the model registry or continuous delivery, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around tracking experiments with MLflow, and note every problem you meet, starting with untracked data changes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining MLOps Pipelines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
