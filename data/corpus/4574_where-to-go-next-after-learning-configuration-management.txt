Where to Go Next After Learning Configuration Management

You have met the basics of Configuration Management. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Webhooks, which belongs to the same area as Configuration Management (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of environment variables, config files, defaults or secrets handling, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around toggling features with dotenv, and note every problem you meet, starting with committing secrets.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Configuration Management to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
