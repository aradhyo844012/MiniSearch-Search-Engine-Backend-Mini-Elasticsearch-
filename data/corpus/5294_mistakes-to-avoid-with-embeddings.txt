Mistakes to Avoid with Embeddings

Everyone learns Embeddings partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing models between indexing and querying. It usually appears when someone skips vectors and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring normalization. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of semantic search. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: stale embeddings after data changes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on nearest neighbor search to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
