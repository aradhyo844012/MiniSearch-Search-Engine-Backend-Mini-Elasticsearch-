Probability Basics in Real Projects

Reading about Probability Basics is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: randomized algorithms. Here, events drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against confusing independent and exclusive events.

Scenario two: machine learning. This time conditional probability matters most, and tools such as Python random help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: risk estimates. The key idea is independence, and the biggest risk is ignoring base rates.

Across all three scenarios, Probability Basics stays the same idea: the study of how likely events are to happen. Only the details change.
