Bluetooth Low Energy in Real Projects

Reading about Bluetooth Low Energy is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: fitness trackers. Here, advertising drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against ignoring connection limits.

Scenario two: smart locks. This time GATT services matters most, and tools such as nRF Connect help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: proximity beacons. The key idea is characteristics, and the biggest risk is weak pairing security.

Across all three scenarios, Bluetooth Low Energy stays the same idea: a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use. Only the details change.
