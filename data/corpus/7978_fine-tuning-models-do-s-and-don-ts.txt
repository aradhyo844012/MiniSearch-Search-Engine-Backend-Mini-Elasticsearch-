Fine-Tuning Models Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Fine-Tuning Models.

Do:
- Learn pretrained weights before anything else.
- Try Hugging Face on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
- Test the case of domain-specific language tasks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overfitting on small datasets.
- Ignore this: forgetting general abilities.
- Ignore this: low-quality training data.
- Adopt Fine-Tuning Models just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Fine-Tuning Models, a little patience with training data pays off more than any clever shortcut.
