Cron Jobs and Scheduling Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Cron Jobs and Scheduling regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: assuming cron has your shell environment.
2. Reach for crontab first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as systemd timers.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit schedule fields before anything else.
4. Test the case of nightly backups early, before the design hardens.
5. Do not skip crontab syntax; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to log rotation as well as to Cron Jobs and Scheduling in general.
