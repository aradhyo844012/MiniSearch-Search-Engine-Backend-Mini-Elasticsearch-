Skills to Build Around Process Signals and Job Control

Knowing the definition of Process Signals and Job Control is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with SIGTERM and SIGKILL and foreground and background jobs, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with kill on a Process Signals and Job Control task, and try at least one alternative such as jobs and fg.

Judgment: learn to decide when Process Signals and Job Control fits a problem such as stopping runaway programs, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a traffic officer's hand signals for running programs.

Habits: try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up, and write down what you learn after each project.
