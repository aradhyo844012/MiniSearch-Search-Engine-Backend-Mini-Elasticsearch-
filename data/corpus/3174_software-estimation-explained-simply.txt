Software Estimation Explained Simply

If Software Estimation sounds intimidating, relax. It is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Software Estimation as forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: story points, ranges, uncertainty, and velocity. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with story points, then add ranges.

People reach for Software Estimation when they work on sprint planning, setting deadlines, and negotiating scope.

One practical tip is to give ranges instead of single numbers.
