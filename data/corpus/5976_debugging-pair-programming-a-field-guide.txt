Debugging Pair Programming: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Pair Programming, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: one person dominating, fatigue from long sessions, and pairing on every task.
Step 3: revisit driver and navigator and rotation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use VS Code Live Share or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Pair Programming in work such as teaching new teammates come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
