Practice Exercises for Font Rendering

These exercises build real understanding of Font Rendering. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble web pages or operating systems.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Font Rendering and include the term glyphs.
Exercise 3: Compare Google Fonts and font inspector tools for PDF viewers and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: missing font fallbacks. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Pick FreeType and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen.
