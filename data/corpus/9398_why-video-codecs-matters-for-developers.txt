Why Video Codecs Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Video Codecs make better decisions, because Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.

Three situations show why it matters. First, streaming services. Second, video calls. Third, archiving footage. In each case, a solid grasp of frames and motion compensation separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: mismatched codec support, too low a bitrate, and ignoring hardware decoding.

You will not need to build everything yourself; FFmpeg, H.264, and AV1 cover most needs.
