Everything You Need to Know About Shaders

This page collects the essentials about Shaders in one place. In short, Shaders is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn.

Core concepts:
- vertex shaders
- fragment shaders
- uniforms
- GLSL

Where it is used:
- lighting effects
- water and fire visuals
- post-processing

Common problems:
- branches that slow the GPU
- precision errors
- platform differences

Tools to know:
- GLSL
- Shadertoy
- RenderDoc

Remember this above all: a tiny painter that colors each pixel following your instructions is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.
