Debugging Zero Trust Security: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Zero Trust Security, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: treating it as a product to buy, poor identity hygiene, and breaking usability.
Step 3: revisit identity verification and least privilege, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Okta or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Zero Trust Security in work such as remote work access come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start with strong identity and device checks. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
