Recommendation Systems Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Recommendation Systems. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.
Key terms: collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, ranking, and the cold start problem.
Typical uses: streaming services, online shops, and news feeds.
Risks: feedback loops, popularity bias, and ignoring new users.
Tools: Surprise, implicit, and TensorFlow Recommenders.

Mental picture: a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked.

Habit to build: evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
