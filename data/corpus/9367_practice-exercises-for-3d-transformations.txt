Practice Exercises for 3D Transformations

These exercises build real understanding of 3D Transformations. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing 3D Transformations and include the term translation.
Exercise 2: Compare three.js and Blender for robotics and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: wrong multiplication order. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Explain how scaling and matrices differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick GLM and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.
