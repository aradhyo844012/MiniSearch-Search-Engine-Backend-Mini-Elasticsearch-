Debugging Connection Pooling: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Connection Pooling, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: pools larger than the database allows, leaked connections, and long transactions that hold connections.
Step 3: revisit pool size and idle connections, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use PgBouncer or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Connection Pooling in work such as web backends come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, always return connections in a finally block. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
