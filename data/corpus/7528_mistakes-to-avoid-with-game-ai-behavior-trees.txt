Mistakes to Avoid with Game AI Behavior Trees

Everyone learns Game AI Behavior Trees partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: trees that become tangled. It usually appears when someone skips selector nodes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: expensive conditions checked every frame. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of enemy behavior. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no way to debug decisions. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on actions to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: visualize the tree while the game is running.
