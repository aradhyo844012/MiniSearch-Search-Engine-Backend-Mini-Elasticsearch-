Skills to Build Around AI Agents

Knowing the definition of AI Agents is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with planning and tool calls, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with LangGraph on a AI Agents task, and try at least one alternative such as AutoGen.

Judgment: learn to decide when AI Agents fits a problem such as research assistants, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out.

Habits: limit what tools the agent may use and log every action, and write down what you learn after each project.
