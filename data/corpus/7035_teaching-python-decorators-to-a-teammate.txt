Teaching Python Decorators to a Teammate

Explaining Python Decorators to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: gift wrapping that adds a bow without changing the present. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Python Decorators is functions that wrap other functions to add behavior without changing their code. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving logging calls, naming higher-order functions and wrapper functions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain functools.wraps in Python Decorators back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, losing function metadata, and suggest this habit: always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator.
