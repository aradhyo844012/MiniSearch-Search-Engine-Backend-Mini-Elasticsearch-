Pair Programming Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Pair Programming.

Do:
- Learn driver and navigator before anything else.
- Try VS Code Live Share on a small example.
- Make it a habit to swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.
- Test the case of teaching new teammates early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: one person dominating.
- Ignore this: fatigue from long sessions.
- Ignore this: pairing on every task.
- Adopt Pair Programming just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Pair Programming, a little patience with rotation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
