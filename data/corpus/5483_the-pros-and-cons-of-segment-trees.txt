The Pros and Cons of Segment Trees

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Segment Trees is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports range sum queries
- Makes finding minimums in a window easier
- Helps with competitive programming problems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: off-by-one errors in ranges
- Risk: wrong tree size allocation
- Risk: forgetting to push lazy updates

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as range sum queries, and you can manage the risks, Segment Trees is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test your tree against a slow brute-force version on small inputs.
