The Pros and Cons of Fine-Tuning Models

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Fine-Tuning Models is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports domain-specific language tasks
- Makes style adaptation easier
- Helps with improving accuracy on narrow problems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overfitting on small datasets
- Risk: forgetting general abilities
- Risk: low-quality training data

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as domain-specific language tasks, and you can manage the risks, Fine-Tuning Models is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with a small, clean dataset and a held-out test set.
