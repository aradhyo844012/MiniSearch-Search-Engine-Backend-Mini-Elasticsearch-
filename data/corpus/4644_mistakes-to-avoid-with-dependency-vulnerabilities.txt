Mistakes to Avoid with Dependency Vulnerabilities

Everyone learns Dependency Vulnerabilities partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring transitive dependencies. It usually appears when someone skips CVE identifiers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: never updating. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of auditing projects. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: trusting packages without checking them. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on software bills of materials to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: automate update pull requests.
