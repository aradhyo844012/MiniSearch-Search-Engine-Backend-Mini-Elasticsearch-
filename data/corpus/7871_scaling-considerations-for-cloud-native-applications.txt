Scaling Considerations for Cloud-Native Applications

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Cloud-Native Applications changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding containers and getting web platforms right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for treating the cloud like a data center and too many services, and measure with Kubernetes before changing anything.

Large scale: declarative configuration and elastic scaling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around data-heavy services at this stage.

Before you scale, design every service to be stateless where possible. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
