The Pros and Cons of Connection Pooling

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Connection Pooling is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports web backends
- Makes serverless functions that call databases easier
- Helps with high-traffic APIs

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: pools larger than the database allows
- Risk: leaked connections
- Risk: long transactions that hold connections

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as web backends, and you can manage the risks, Connection Pooling is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, always return connections in a finally block.
