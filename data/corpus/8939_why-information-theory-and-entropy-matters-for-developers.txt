Why Information Theory and Entropy Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Information Theory and Entropy make better decisions, because Information Theory and Entropy is the mathematics of measuring and compressing information.

Three situations show why it matters. First, data compression. Second, error-correcting codes. Third, communication systems. In each case, a solid grasp of bits and entropy separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: confusing information with meaning, ignoring noise, and misreading entropy values.

Good tools exist, including the Python math module, NumPy, and zlib, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
