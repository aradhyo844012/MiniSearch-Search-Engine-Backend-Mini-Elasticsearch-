Digital Audio: Core Concepts and Terminology

This page defines the vocabulary you need to read about Digital Audio with confidence. The subject itself is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

sampling rate: one of the building blocks of Digital Audio. Documentation for Audacity usually mentions it early.
bit depth: one of the building blocks of Digital Audio. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
waveforms: one of the building blocks of Digital Audio. Documentation for Audacity usually mentions it early.
the Nyquist limit: one of the building blocks of Digital Audio. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.

Practice using these four Digital Audio terms in sentences about voice calls. If the sentences feel natural, the vocabulary has stuck.
