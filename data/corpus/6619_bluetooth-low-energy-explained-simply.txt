Bluetooth Low Energy Explained Simply

If Bluetooth Low Energy sounds intimidating, relax. It is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Bluetooth Low Energy as a whisper between two nearby devices that barely uses any energy. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: advertising, GATT services, characteristics, and pairing. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with advertising, then add GATT services.

People reach for Bluetooth Low Energy when they work on fitness trackers, smart locks, and proximity beacons.

One practical tip is to test with several phone models.

Once Bluetooth Low Energy feels comfortable, read about Arduino Basics, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
