Scaling Considerations for Speech Recognition

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Speech Recognition changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding audio features and getting voice assistants right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for accents and background noise and domain-specific vocabulary, and measure with Whisper before changing anything.

Large scale: language models and word error rate move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around captioning at this stage.

Before you scale, measure word error rate on audio like your real users'. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
