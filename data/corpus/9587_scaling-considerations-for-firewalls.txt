Scaling Considerations for Firewalls

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Firewalls changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding rules and getting protecting servers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for rules in the wrong order and overly broad allow rules, and measure with ufw before changing anything.

Large scale: default deny and zones move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around segmenting networks at this stage.

Before you scale, start with default deny and open only what you need. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
