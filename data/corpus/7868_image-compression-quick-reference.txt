Image Compression Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Image Compression. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail.
Key terms: lossy and lossless formats, quantization, transform coding, and quality settings.
Typical uses: web images, photo storage, and sending pictures over slow networks.
Risks: repeated recompression, the wrong format for graphics, and excessive quality loss.
Tools: ImageMagick, mozjpeg, and Squoosh.

Mental picture: packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly.

Habit to build: pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
