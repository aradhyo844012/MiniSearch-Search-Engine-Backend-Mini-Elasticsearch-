Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Reinforcement Learning

This path is for a learner who has never studied Reinforcement Learning before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes) and learn agent and environment.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Gymnasium, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving game playing.

Stage 3, depth: study reward and policy, then deliberately explore this problem: badly designed rewards.

Stage 4, application: apply Reinforcement Learning to robot control, compare Stable-Baselines3 with PyTorch, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Reinforcement Learning to someone else, and then move on to Embeddings.
