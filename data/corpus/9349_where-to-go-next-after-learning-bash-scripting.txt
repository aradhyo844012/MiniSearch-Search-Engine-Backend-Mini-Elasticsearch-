Where to Go Next After Learning Bash Scripting

You have met the basics of Bash Scripting. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Environment Variables, which belongs to the same area as Bash Scripting (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of variables, conditionals and loops, exit codes or functions, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around processing text files with Bash, and note every problem you meet, starting with unquoted variables.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Bash Scripting to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
