Practice Exercises for Web Storage

These exercises build real understanding of Web Storage. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare localForage and Dexie.js for caching small API results and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Explain how cookies and IndexedDB differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Web Storage and include the term localStorage.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble saving user preferences or remembering form drafts.
Exercise 5: Pick browser DevTools and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Web Storage is browser features for keeping small amounts of data on the user's device.
