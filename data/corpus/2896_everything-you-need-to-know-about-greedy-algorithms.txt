Everything You Need to Know About Greedy Algorithms

This page collects the essentials about Greedy Algorithms in one place. In short, Greedy Algorithms is algorithms that build a solution by always taking the locally best choice.

Core concepts:
- greedy choice property
- optimal substructure
- exchange arguments
- local versus global optimum

Where it is used:
- interval scheduling
- Huffman coding
- making change with standard coins

Common problems:
- assuming greedy always works
- missing counterexamples
- skipping the correctness proof

Tools to know:
- pen and paper proofs
- Python sorted
- unit tests

Remember this above all: picking the closest open parking spot at every junction and hoping it works out is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it.
