A Practical Checklist for Microservices

Use this checklist before you rely on Microservices in a project.

1. Document your decisions in the project README.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as Docker or Kubernetes.
3. Guard against this risk: splitting too early.
4. Review how Microservices interacts with REST APIs.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on APIs.
6. Measure results before and after any change.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers service boundaries.
