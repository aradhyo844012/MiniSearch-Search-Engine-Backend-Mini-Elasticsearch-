Embedded Systems in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Embedded Systems.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Embedded Systems is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn microcontrollers, firmware, real-time constraints, and memory limits.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a small brain inside an appliance that only knows one job. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through appliances, medical devices, and vehicles.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for memory leaks on tiny devices and no plan for updates, then try C for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
