Scaling Considerations for Reading Documentation

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Reading Documentation changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding search and getting learning new libraries right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for reading the wrong version and skipping the overview, and measure with MDN Web Docs before changing anything.

Large scale: examples and version numbers move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around solving errors at this stage.

Before you scale, check that the documentation version matches your installed version. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
