Skills to Build Around Compilers and Interpreters

Knowing the definition of Compilers and Interpreters is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with lexing and parsing, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with GCC on a Compilers and Interpreters task, and try at least one alternative such as LLVM.

Judgment: learn to decide when Compilers and Interpreters fits a problem such as running Python and JavaScript, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a translator who either translates a whole book first or interprets sentence by sentence.

Habits: turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously, and write down what you learn after each project.
