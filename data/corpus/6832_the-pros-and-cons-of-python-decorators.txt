The Pros and Cons of Python Decorators

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Python Decorators is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports logging calls
- Makes caching results easier
- Helps with checking permissions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: losing function metadata
- Risk: stacking decorators in the wrong order
- Risk: hiding side effects

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as logging calls, and you can manage the risks, Python Decorators is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, always apply functools.wraps inside your decorator.
