Random Forests Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Random Forests feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Random Forests: ensembles of many decision trees whose predictions are combined.
bagging: a core term, most visible in tabular predictions.
feature randomness: a core term that beginners often confuse with voting.
voting: a core term that connects to out-of-bag error.
out-of-bag error: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
scikit-learn: a tool commonly used for feature importance ranking.

Come back to this Random Forests list whenever documentation for XGBoost uses a word you do not recognize.
