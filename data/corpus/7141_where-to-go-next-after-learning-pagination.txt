Where to Go Next After Learning Pagination

You have met the basics of Pagination. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Webhooks, which belongs to the same area as Pagination (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of offset pagination, cursor pagination, page size or stable ordering, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around activity feeds with SQL LIMIT and OFFSET, and note every problem you meet, starting with offsets getting slow on huge tables.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Pagination to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
