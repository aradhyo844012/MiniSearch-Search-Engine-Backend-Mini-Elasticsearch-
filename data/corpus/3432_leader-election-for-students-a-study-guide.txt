Leader Election for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Leader Election.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Leader Election in your own words.
2. Describe heartbeats, terms or epochs, candidates, and failover.
3. Give examples from database primaries and job schedulers.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Leader Election is a process by which nodes in a cluster choose one node to coordinate the work. Then draw a classroom picking a monitor when the teacher leaves on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is split brain a problem? How does terms or epochs relate to candidates? When would you choose ZooKeeper or etcd?

Study habit worth keeping: tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.

When you feel ready, compare Leader Election with Idempotency and write down three differences.
