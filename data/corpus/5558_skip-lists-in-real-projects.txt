Skip Lists in Real Projects

Reading about Skip Lists is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: ordered in-memory indexes. Here, multiple levels drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against poor random number choices.

Scenario two: sorted sets in caches. This time random level assignment matters most, and tools such as Redis sorted sets help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: concurrent ordered maps. The key idea is forward pointers, and the biggest risk is memory overhead.

Across all three scenarios, Skip Lists stays the same idea: layered linked lists that allow fast searching by skipping over many elements. Only the details change.
