Scaling Considerations for Service Workers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Service Workers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding registration and getting offline support right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for caching stale files forever and bugs surviving across deployments, and measure with Workbox before changing anything.

Large scale: cache storage and fetch handlers move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around faster repeat visits at this stage.

Before you scale, version your caches so old files can be removed. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
