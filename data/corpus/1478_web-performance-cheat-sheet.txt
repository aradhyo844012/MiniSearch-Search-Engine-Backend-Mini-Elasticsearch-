Web Performance Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Web Performance.

- Definition: the practice of making websites load quickly and respond smoothly.
- Core ideas: page weight; caching; lazy loading; critical rendering path.
- Use it for: shopping sites; news pages; search interfaces.
- Watch out for: shipping large JavaScript bundles; unoptimized images; too many network requests.
- Tools: Lighthouse, WebPageTest, Chrome DevTools.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for caching before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice page weight until it feels automatic.
