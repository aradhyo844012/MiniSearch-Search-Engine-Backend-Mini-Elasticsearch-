Practice Exercises for SSH and Remote Access

These exercises build real understanding of SSH and Remote Access. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble administering servers or copying files securely.
Exercise 2: Explain how port forwarding and config files differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing SSH and Remote Access and include the term key pairs.
Exercise 4: Compare ssh-keygen and scp for tunneling to private services and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Pick OpenSSH and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: SSH and Remote Access is a secure protocol for logging in to and controlling remote machines.
