Choosing Tools for 5G Networks

There is no single best tool for 5G Networks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

speed test tools is a common first choice, especially for mobile broadband. network simulators suits teams that already work with similar technology, and 3GPP documents is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for 5G Networks by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle connected vehicles well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for 5G Networks, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: limited coverage on the fastest bands.

As a rule of thumb, test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds, no matter which tool you use.
