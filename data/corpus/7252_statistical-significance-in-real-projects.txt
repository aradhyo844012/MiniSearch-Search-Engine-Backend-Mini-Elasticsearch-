Statistical Significance in Real Projects

Reading about Statistical Significance is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: judging experiment results. Here, p-values drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mistaking significance for importance.

Scenario two: comparing model performance. This time the null hypothesis matters most, and tools such as SciPy help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: reporting research findings. The key idea is confidence intervals, and the biggest risk is p-hacking.

Across all three scenarios, Statistical Significance stays the same idea: a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect. Only the details change.
