Scaling Considerations for Graph Databases

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Graph Databases changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding nodes and getting social networks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using them for simple tabular data and unbounded traversals, and measure with Neo4j before changing anything.

Large scale: properties and traversal queries move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around recommendation engines at this stage.

Before you scale, limit traversal depth in every exploratory query. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
