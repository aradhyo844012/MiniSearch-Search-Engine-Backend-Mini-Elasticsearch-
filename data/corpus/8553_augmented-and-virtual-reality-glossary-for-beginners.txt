Augmented and Virtual Reality Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Augmented and Virtual Reality feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Augmented and Virtual Reality: technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.
head tracking: a core term, most visible in training simulations.
rendering at high frame rates: a core term that beginners often confuse with spatial mapping.
spatial mapping: a core term that connects to input methods.
input methods: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Unity XR: a tool commonly used for games.

Come back to this Augmented and Virtual Reality list whenever documentation for WebXR uses a word you do not recognize.
