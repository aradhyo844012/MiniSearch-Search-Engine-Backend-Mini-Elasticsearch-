Understanding Zero Trust Security Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a building where every door checks your badge, not just the front entrance.

In that picture, identity verification is the first rule you follow, least privilege is the next step, and micro-segmentation and continuous checks keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified, and it involves details such as treating it as a product to buy that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Okta to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain remote work access to a friend.
