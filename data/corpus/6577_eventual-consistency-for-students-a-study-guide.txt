Eventual Consistency for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Eventual Consistency.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Eventual Consistency in your own words.
2. Describe replication delay, conflict resolution, read-your-writes, and convergence.
3. Give examples from global social feeds and shopping cart services.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Eventual Consistency is a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough. Then draw news spreading through a village until everyone has heard it on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is assuming reads are always fresh a problem? How does conflict resolution relate to read-your-writes? When would you choose Amazon DynamoDB or Apache Cassandra?

Study habit: decide up front how your app handles stale reads.

When you feel ready, compare Eventual Consistency with Column-Oriented Storage and write down three differences.
