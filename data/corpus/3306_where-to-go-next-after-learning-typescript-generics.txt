Where to Go Next After Learning TypeScript Generics

You have met the basics of TypeScript Generics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Java Streams, which belongs to the same area as TypeScript Generics (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of type parameters, constraints, type inference or generic interfaces, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around typed API clients with tsc, and note every problem you meet, starting with overusing any.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining TypeScript Generics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
