Where to Go Next After Learning React Native

You have met the basics of React Native. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Arduino Basics, which belongs to the same area as React Native (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of components, native modules, styling with flexbox or navigation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around rapid prototypes with Expo, and note every problem you meet, starting with heavy re-renders.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining React Native to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
