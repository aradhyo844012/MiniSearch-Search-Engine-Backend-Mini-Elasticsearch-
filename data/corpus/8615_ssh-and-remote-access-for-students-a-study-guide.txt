SSH and Remote Access for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover SSH and Remote Access.

Learning goals:
1. Explain SSH and Remote Access in your own words.
2. Describe key pairs, host keys, port forwarding, and config files.
3. Give examples from administering servers and copying files securely.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: SSH and Remote Access is a secure protocol for logging in to and controlling remote machines. Then draw a private phone line with a secret handshake to a faraway office on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is password logins left enabled a problem? How does host keys relate to port forwarding? When would you choose OpenSSH or ssh-keygen?

Study habit worth keeping: use key-based login and disable password authentication.

When you feel ready, compare SSH and Remote Access with systemd Services and write down three differences.
