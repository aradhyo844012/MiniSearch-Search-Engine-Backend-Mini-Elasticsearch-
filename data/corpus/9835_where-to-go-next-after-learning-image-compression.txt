Where to Go Next After Learning Image Compression

You have met the basics of Image Compression. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about 3D Transformations, which belongs to the same area as Image Compression (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of lossy and lossless formats, quantization, transform coding or quality settings, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around photo storage with ImageMagick, and note every problem you meet, starting with repeated recompression.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Image Compression to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
