Mistakes to Avoid with Pagination

Everyone learns Pagination partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: offsets getting slow on huge tables. It usually appears when someone skips offset pagination and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unstable sort order. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of search results. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: missing total counts when users need them. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on stable ordering to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
