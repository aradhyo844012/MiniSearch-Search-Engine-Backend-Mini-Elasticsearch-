Unit Testing Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Unit Testing.

Q: Explain Unit Testing in simple words.
A: Unit Testing refers to writing small automated tests that check one piece of code at a time. I would add an example involving catching regressions.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as test cases, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: testing implementation details.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: pytest, unittest, and coverage.py.

Q: How does it connect to Version Control Workflow?
A: Both belong to the area of Software Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: test cases, assertions, fixtures, and mocking.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: testing implementation details; slow tests; and tests that depend on each other.
