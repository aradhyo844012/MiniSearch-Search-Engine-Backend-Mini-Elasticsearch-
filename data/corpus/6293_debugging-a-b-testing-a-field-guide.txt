Debugging A/B Testing: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around A/B Testing, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: stopping tests early, testing too many variants, and ignoring sample size.
Step 3: revisit control and variant and random assignment, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Optimizely or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving A/B Testing in work such as testing page designs come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, decide the success metric and sample size before you begin. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
