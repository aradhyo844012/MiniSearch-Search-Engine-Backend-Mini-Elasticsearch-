Scaling Considerations for Vector Graphics (SVG)

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Vector Graphics (SVG) changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding paths and getting logos right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for overly complex paths and a missing viewBox, and measure with Inkscape before changing anything.

Large scale: fill and stroke and transforms move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around icons at this stage.

Before you scale, set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
