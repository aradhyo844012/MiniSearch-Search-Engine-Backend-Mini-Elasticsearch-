Anomaly Detection for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Anomaly Detection.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Anomaly Detection in your own words.
2. Describe baselines, thresholds, isolation methods, and false positives.
3. Give examples from fraud detection and server monitoring.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Anomaly Detection is finding data points or events that differ sharply from normal behavior. Then draw a security guard who notices the one person acting differently on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too many false alarms a problem? How does thresholds relate to isolation methods? When would you choose scikit-learn or PyOD?

Study habit worth keeping: tune alerts using real examples of past incidents.

When you feel ready, compare Anomaly Detection with Fine-Tuning Models and write down three differences.
