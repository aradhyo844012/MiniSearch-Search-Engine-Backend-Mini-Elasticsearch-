Client-Side Routing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Client-Side Routing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: broken deep links.
2. Write down why you chose Client-Side Routing so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit route matching before anything else.
4. Test the case of single-page apps early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for React Router first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Vue Router.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to dashboards as well.
