Where to Go Next After Learning Go Goroutines

You have met the basics of Go Goroutines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about TypeScript Generics, which belongs to the same area as Go Goroutines (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of goroutines, channels, select statements or wait groups, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around parallel data processing with the go command, and note every problem you meet, starting with goroutine leaks.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Go Goroutines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
