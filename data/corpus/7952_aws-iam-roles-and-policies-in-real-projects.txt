AWS IAM Roles and Policies in Real Projects

Reading about AWS IAM Roles and Policies is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: securing deployments. Here, users and roles drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against wildcard permissions.

Scenario two: granting services access. This time policies matters most, and tools such as IAM Access Analyzer help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: auditing permissions. The key idea is least privilege, and the biggest risk is long-lived access keys.

Across all three scenarios, AWS IAM Roles and Policies stays the same idea: the permission system that controls who can do what in an AWS account. Only the details change.
