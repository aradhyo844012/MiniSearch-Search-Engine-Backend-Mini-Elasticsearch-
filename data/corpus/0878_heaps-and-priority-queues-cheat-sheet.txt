Heaps and Priority Queues Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Heaps and Priority Queues.

- Definition: structures that always give quick access to the smallest or largest element.
- Core ideas: heap property; sift up and sift down; array representation; top-k selection.
- Use it for: scheduling tasks; selecting the top results from many candidates; Dijkstra's shortest path algorithm.
- Watch out for: assuming a heap is fully sorted; using a full sort when only the top k are needed; mixing up min-heaps and max-heaps.
- Tools: Python heapq, Java PriorityQueue, C++ priority_queue.
- Related topic: Recursion.
- Quick tip: practice heap property until it feels automatic.
