Is Feature Flags Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Feature Flags, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code.

It is a good fit when your work involves dark launches or A/B tests, and when your team understands flag evaluation.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn targeting rules, or when you cannot manage these risks: never removing old flags, flags that depend on each other, and untested flag combinations.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether gradual rollout and flag cleanup are part of the answer. If they are, Feature Flags is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with emergency kill switches and give every flag an owner and a removal date.
