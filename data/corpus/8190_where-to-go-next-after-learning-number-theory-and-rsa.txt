Where to Go Next After Learning Number Theory and RSA

You have met the basics of Number Theory and RSA. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Floating-Point Arithmetic, which belongs to the same area as Number Theory and RSA (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of prime numbers, modular arithmetic, greatest common divisor or modular exponentiation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around hash functions with Python pow, and note every problem you meet, starting with using small primes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Number Theory and RSA to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
