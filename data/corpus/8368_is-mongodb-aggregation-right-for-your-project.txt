Is MongoDB Aggregation Right for Your Project?

Before adopting MongoDB Aggregation, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.

It is a good fit when your work involves summarizing orders or building analytics queries, and when your team understands pipeline stages.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn $match and $group, or when you cannot manage these risks: putting $match late, huge intermediate results, and hitting memory limits.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether projection and indexes are part of the answer. If they are, MongoDB Aggregation is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with reshaping documents and filter with $match as early as possible.
