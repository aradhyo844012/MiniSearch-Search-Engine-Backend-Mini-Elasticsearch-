Why Digital Twins Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Digital Twins make better decisions, because Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.

Three situations show why it matters. First, factory monitoring. Second, building management. Third, predictive maintenance. In each case, a solid grasp of sensor data and simulation models separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: stale data, over-detailed models, and unclear goals.

Good tools exist, including Azure Digital Twins, Unity, and MQTT, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
