Monorepos Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Monorepos regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Monorepos so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for Nx first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Bazel.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit code ownership before anything else.
4. Do not skip shared tooling; everything else builds on it.
5. Test the case of sharing libraries across apps early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to coordinated refactors as well as to Monorepos in general.
