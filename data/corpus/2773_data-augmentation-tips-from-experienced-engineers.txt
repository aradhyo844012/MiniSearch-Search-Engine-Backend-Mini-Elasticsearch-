Data Augmentation Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Data Augmentation regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Albumentations first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as torchvision.
2. Make sure you can augment only the training data.
3. Write down why you chose Data Augmentation so the next person understands the decision.
4. Do not skip transformations; everything else builds on it.
5. Be careful with this one: changes that alter the label.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to text classification as well.
