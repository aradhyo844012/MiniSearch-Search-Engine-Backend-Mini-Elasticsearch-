Everything You Need to Know About CAP Theorem

This page collects the essentials about CAP Theorem in one place. In short, CAP Theorem is a result stating that a distributed system cannot guarantee consistency, availability and partition tolerance all at once.

Core concepts:
- consistency
- availability
- partition tolerance
- trade-offs

Where it is used:
- choosing database types
- designing multi-region services
- explaining system behavior in interviews

Common problems:
- treating it as a three-way menu
- ignoring latency trade-offs in normal operation
- assuming partitions never happen

Tools to know:
- Apache Cassandra
- etcd
- ZooKeeper

Remember this above all: a phone tree that must choose between giving old information or staying silent when a line is cut is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors.
