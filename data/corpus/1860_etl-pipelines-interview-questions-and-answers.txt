ETL Pipelines Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about ETL Pipelines.

Q: Explain ETL Pipelines in simple words.
A: ETL Pipelines refers to processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination. I would add an example involving building reports.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: extraction, transformation, loading, and scheduling.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as extraction, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Apache Airflow, dbt, and Python scripts.

Q: How does it connect to Pandas DataFrames?
A: Both belong to the area of Data Engineering, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
