Is Robotics and ROS Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Robotics and ROS, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Robotics and ROS is building machines that sense and act, often using the Robot Operating System to connect their software.

It is a good fit when your work involves warehouse robots or drones, and when your team understands sensors.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn actuators, or when you cannot manage these risks: ignoring sensor noise, unsafe motion commands, and tight coupling between nodes.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether nodes and topics and control loops are part of the answer. If they are, Robotics and ROS is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with educational robots and test in simulation before moving real hardware.
