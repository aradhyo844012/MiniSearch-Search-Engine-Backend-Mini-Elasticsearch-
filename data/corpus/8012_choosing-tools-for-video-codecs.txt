Choosing Tools for Video Codecs

There is no single best tool for Video Codecs. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

FFmpeg is a common first choice, especially for streaming services. H.264 suits teams that already work with similar technology, and AV1 is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Video Codecs by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle video calls well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Video Codecs, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: mismatched codec support.

As a rule of thumb, test playback on the weakest target device, no matter which tool you use.
