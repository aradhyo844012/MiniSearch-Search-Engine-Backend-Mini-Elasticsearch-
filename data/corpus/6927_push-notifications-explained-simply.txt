Push Notifications Explained Simply

Here is Push Notifications without the jargon: it is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed.

Think of Push Notifications as a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: device tokens, push services, payloads, and permissions. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with device tokens, then add push services.

People reach for Push Notifications when they work on order updates, chat alerts, and re-engaging users.

One practical tip is to ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
