Data Quality Checks Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Data Quality Checks. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.
Key terms: null checks, uniqueness, freshness, and schema validation.
Typical uses: protecting dashboards, validating ingestion, and catching pipeline bugs.
Risks: alerts nobody reads, checks only at the end of pipelines, and no ownership of failures.
Tools: Great Expectations, dbt tests, and Deequ.

Mental picture: a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line.

Habit to build: check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
