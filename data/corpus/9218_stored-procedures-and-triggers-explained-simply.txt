Stored Procedures and Triggers Explained Simply

Here is Stored Procedures and Triggers without the jargon: it is code stored inside the database that runs on demand or automatically when data changes.

Think of Stored Procedures and Triggers as a house rule that fires automatically whenever something is placed on a shelf. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: procedures, triggers, parameters, and side effects. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with procedures, then add triggers.

People reach for Stored Procedures and Triggers when they work on enforcing rules close to the data, audit logging, and batch updates.

One practical tip is to keep triggers small and well documented.
