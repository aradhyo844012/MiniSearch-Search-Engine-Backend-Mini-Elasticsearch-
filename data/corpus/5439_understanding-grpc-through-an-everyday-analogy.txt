Understanding gRPC Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a phone line with a pre-agreed script for every call.

In that picture, protocol buffers is the first rule you follow, service definitions is the next step, and streaming and HTTP/2 keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts, and it involves details such as breaking changes in message fields that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for protoc to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain service-to-service calls to a friend.
