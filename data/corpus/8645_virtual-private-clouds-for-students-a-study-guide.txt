Virtual Private Clouds for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Virtual Private Clouds.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Virtual Private Clouds in your own words.
2. Describe subnets, route tables, security groups, and gateways.
3. Give examples from separating public and private services and connecting to offices.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Virtual Private Clouds is isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources. Then draw a gated neighborhood inside a big city on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is overlapping address ranges a problem? How does route tables relate to security groups? When would you choose AWS VPC or Terraform?

Study habit: keep databases in private subnets.

When you feel ready, compare Virtual Private Clouds with Multi-Cloud Strategy and write down three differences.
