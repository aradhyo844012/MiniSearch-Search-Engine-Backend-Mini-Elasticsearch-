The Pros and Cons of Linux Boot Process

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Linux Boot Process is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports troubleshooting machines that do not start
- Makes configuring dual boot easier
- Helps with building custom images

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: overwriting the bootloader
- Risk: wrong kernel parameters
- Risk: ignoring recovery options

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as troubleshooting machines that do not start, and you can manage the risks, Linux Boot Process is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep a recovery option available before editing boot settings.
