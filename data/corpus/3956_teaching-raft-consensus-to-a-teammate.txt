Teaching Raft Consensus to a Teammate

Explaining Raft Consensus to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a committee that elects a chair and only records decisions most members confirm. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Raft Consensus is an algorithm that lets a group of servers agree on a shared log of operations. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving configuration stores, naming leader election and log replication as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain terms in Raft Consensus back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, even-sized clusters, and suggest this habit: use an odd number of nodes.
