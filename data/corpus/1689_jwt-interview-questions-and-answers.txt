JWT Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about JWT.

Q: Explain JWT in simple words.
A: JWT refers to a compact token format that carries signed claims between a client and a server. I would add an example involving stateless API authentication.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as header, payload and signature, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: header, payload and signature, claims, expiry, and signing keys.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with service-to-service calls, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: storing tokens in insecure places.

Q: How does it connect to REST APIs?
A: Both belong to the area of Backend and APIs, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: jwt.io, PyJWT, and jsonwebtoken.
