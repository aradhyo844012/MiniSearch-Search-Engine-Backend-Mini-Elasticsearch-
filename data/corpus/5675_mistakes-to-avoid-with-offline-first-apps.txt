Mistakes to Avoid with Offline-First Apps

Everyone learns Offline-First Apps partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: unresolved sync conflicts. It usually appears when someone skips local storage and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unbounded local data. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of field service tools. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: poor feedback about pending changes. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on optimistic updates to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: show users which changes have not synced yet.
