Practice Exercises for Flutter

These exercises build real understanding of Flutter. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Dart and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: deep widget trees. Then fix it.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Flutter and include the term widgets.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble cross-platform mobile apps or desktop apps.
Exercise 5: Explain how hot reload and the render tree differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.
