Skills to Build Around Reinforcement Learning

Knowing the definition of Reinforcement Learning is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with agent and environment, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Gymnasium on a Reinforcement Learning task, and try at least one alternative such as Stable-Baselines3.

Judgment: learn to decide when Reinforcement Learning fits a problem such as game playing, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example training a dog with treats for good behavior.

Habits: start with simple environments, and write down what you learn after each project.
