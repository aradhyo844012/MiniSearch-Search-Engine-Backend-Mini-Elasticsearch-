Teaching Web Components to a Teammate

Explaining Web Components to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a Lego brick in its own sealed packaging. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Web Components is browser-native custom elements with their own markup, style and behavior. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving design system components, naming custom elements and shadow DOM as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain templates in Web Components back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, styling surprises across the shadow boundary, and suggest this habit: document the attributes and events like a public API.
