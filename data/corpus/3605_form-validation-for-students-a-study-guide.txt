Form Validation for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Form Validation.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Form Validation in your own words.
2. Describe client-side checks, server-side checks, error messages, and constraint attributes.
3. Give examples from sign-up forms and payment pages.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Form Validation is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted. Then draw a proofreader checking a form before it is submitted on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is validating only on the client a problem? How does server-side checks relate to error messages? When would you choose HTML constraint validation or Zod?

Study habit worth keeping: validate on the server even when the browser checks too.

When you feel ready, compare Form Validation with State Management and write down three differences.
