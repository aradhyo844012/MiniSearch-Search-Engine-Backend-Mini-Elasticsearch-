Practice Exercises for Offline-First Apps

These exercises build real understanding of Offline-First Apps. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how conflict resolution and optimistic updates differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Offline-First Apps and include the term local storage.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble field service tools or note-taking apps.
Exercise 4: Pick SQLite and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare PouchDB and Room for apps for low-connectivity regions and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Offline-First Apps is apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns.
