The Pros and Cons of Blockchain Fundamentals

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Blockchain Fundamentals is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports digital currencies
- Makes supply chain tracking easier
- Helps with public records

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating it as a database replacement
- Risk: slow throughput
- Risk: the energy use of some designs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as digital currencies, and you can manage the risks, Blockchain Fundamentals is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
