Teaching Elasticsearch Concepts to a Teammate

Explaining Elasticsearch Concepts to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a giant library with an index card for every word. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving site search, naming indexes and shards as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain analyzers in Elasticsearch Concepts back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, too many shards, and suggest this habit: define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
