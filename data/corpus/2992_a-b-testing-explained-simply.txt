A/B Testing Explained Simply

Here is A/B Testing without the jargon: it is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.

Think of A/B Testing as taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: control and variant, random assignment, metrics, and sample size. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with control and variant, then add random assignment.

You will meet A/B Testing in work such as testing page designs, comparing ranking algorithms, and evaluating new features.

One practical tip is to decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
