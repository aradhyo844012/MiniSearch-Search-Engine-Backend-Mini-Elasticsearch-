Overfitting Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Overfitting.

- Definition: when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data.
- Core ideas: training and validation error; model complexity; regularization; cross-validation.
- Use it for: diagnosing weak models; choosing simpler models; tuning hyperparameters.
- Watch out for: evaluating only on training data; leaking test data; adding complexity without evidence.
- Tools: scikit-learn, early stopping, learning curves.
- Related topic: Large Language Models.
- Quick tip: practice training and validation error until it feels automatic.
