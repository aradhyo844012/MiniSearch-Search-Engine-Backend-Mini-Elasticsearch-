Embeddings for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Embeddings.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Embeddings in your own words.
2. Describe vectors, cosine similarity, dimensionality, and nearest neighbor search.
3. Give examples from semantic search and recommendations.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together. Then draw coordinates on a map where similar places are close together on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is mixing models between indexing and querying a problem? How does cosine similarity relate to dimensionality? When would you choose sentence-transformers or FAISS?

Study habit: use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.

When you feel ready, compare Embeddings with Retrieval-Augmented Generation and write down three differences.
