Where to Go Next After Learning 3D Transformations

You have met the basics of 3D Transformations. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Digital Audio, which belongs to the same area as 3D Transformations (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of translation, rotation, scaling or matrices, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around modeling tools with GLM, and note every problem you meet, starting with wrong multiplication order.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining 3D Transformations to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
