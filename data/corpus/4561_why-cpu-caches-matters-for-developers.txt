Why CPU Caches Matters for Developers

Developers who understand CPU Caches make better decisions, because CPU Caches is small, fast memory close to the processor that stores recently used data.

Three situations show why it matters. First, speeding up loops. Second, explaining performance differences. Third, designing data layouts. In each case, a solid grasp of cache lines and hit and miss rates separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: false sharing, poor access patterns, and assuming memory is uniformly fast.

You will not need to build everything yourself; perf, Valgrind cachegrind, and Intel VTune cover most needs.
