Scaling Considerations for Technical Writing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Technical Writing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding audience and getting documentation right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for assuming too much knowledge and burying the main point, and measure with Markdown before changing anything.

Large scale: examples and editing move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around blog posts at this stage.

Before you scale, state the main point in the first two sentences. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
