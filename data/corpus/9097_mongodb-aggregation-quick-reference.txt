MongoDB Aggregation Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for MongoDB Aggregation. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.
Key terms: pipeline stages, $match and $group, projection, and indexes.
Typical uses: summarizing orders, building analytics queries, and reshaping documents.
Risks: putting $match late, huge intermediate results, and hitting memory limits.
Tools: MongoDB Compass, mongosh, and PyMongo.

Mental picture: an assembly line where each station filters or reshapes the parts.

Habit to build: filter with $match as early as possible.
