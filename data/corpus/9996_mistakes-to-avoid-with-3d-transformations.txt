Mistakes to Avoid with 3D Transformations

Everyone learns 3D Transformations partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: wrong multiplication order. It usually appears when someone skips translation and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: gimbal lock. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of games. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: mixing coordinate systems. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on matrices to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
