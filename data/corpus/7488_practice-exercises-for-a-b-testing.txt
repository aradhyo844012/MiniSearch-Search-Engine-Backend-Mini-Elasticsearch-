Practice Exercises for A/B Testing

These exercises build real understanding of A/B Testing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Optimizely and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Explain how metrics and sample size differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing A/B Testing and include the term control and variant.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble testing page designs or comparing ranking algorithms.
Exercise 5: Compare GrowthBook and statsmodels for evaluating new features and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.
