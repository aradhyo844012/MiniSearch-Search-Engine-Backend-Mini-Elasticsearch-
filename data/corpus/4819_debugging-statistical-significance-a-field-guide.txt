Debugging Statistical Significance: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Statistical Significance, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: mistaking significance for importance, p-hacking, and ignoring sample size.
Step 3: revisit p-values and the null hypothesis, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use SciPy or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Statistical Significance in work such as judging experiment results come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, always report the effect size alongside the p-value. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
