Disk Partitioning and Mounting Explained Simply

If Disk Partitioning and Mounting sounds intimidating, relax. It is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Disk Partitioning and Mounting as building rooms inside a warehouse and putting door numbers on them. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: partitions, file system types, mount points, and fstab. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with partitions, then add file system types.

You will meet Disk Partitioning and Mounting in work such as adding a new drive, separating data from the system, and encrypting volumes.

One practical tip is to double-check device names with lsblk before formatting.
