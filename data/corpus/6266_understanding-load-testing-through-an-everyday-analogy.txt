Understanding Load Testing Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a stress test that fills a stadium to see how the exits cope.

In that picture, virtual users is the first rule you follow, throughput is the next step, and latency percentiles and ramp-up keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic, and it involves details such as testing unrealistic traffic that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for k6 to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain capacity planning to a friend.
