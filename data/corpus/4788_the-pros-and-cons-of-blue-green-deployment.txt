The Pros and Cons of Blue-Green Deployment

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Blue-Green Deployment is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports zero-downtime releases
- Makes quick rollbacks easier
- Helps with testing in production-like settings

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: database schema mismatches
- Risk: doubling infrastructure costs
- Risk: forgetting to drain old connections

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as zero-downtime releases, and you can manage the risks, Blue-Green Deployment is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
