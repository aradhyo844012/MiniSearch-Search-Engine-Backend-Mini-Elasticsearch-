IP Addressing and Subnets Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about IP Addressing and Subnets.

Q: Explain IP Addressing and Subnets in simple words.
A: IP Addressing and Subnets refers to the rules for numbering devices on a network and dividing networks into smaller parts. I would add an example involving planning office networks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: IPv4 and IPv6, subnet masks, CIDR notation, and private ranges.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: overlapping address ranges.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with configuring cloud networks, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to WebSockets?
A: Both belong to the area of Networking, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: ipcalc, ping, and traceroute.
