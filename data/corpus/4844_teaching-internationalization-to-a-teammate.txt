Teaching Internationalization to a Teammate

Explaining Internationalization to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stage set where the backdrop can be swapped for each audience. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving global products, naming locale detection and translation files as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain pluralization in Internationalization back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, hard-coded strings, and suggest this habit: move every user-facing string into translation files from the first day.
