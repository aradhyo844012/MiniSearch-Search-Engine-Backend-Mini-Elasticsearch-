Everything You Need to Know About Hashing vs Encryption

This page collects the essentials about Hashing vs Encryption in one place. In short, Hashing vs Encryption is the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data.

Core concepts:
- one-way functions
- keys
- collision resistance
- reversibility

Where it is used:
- storing passwords
- verifying file integrity
- protecting data in transit

Common problems:
- encrypting passwords instead of hashing them
- using broken hash functions
- confusing encoding with encryption

Tools to know:
- SHA-256
- AES
- OpenSSL

Remember this above all: a paper shredder compared with a locked suitcase is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
