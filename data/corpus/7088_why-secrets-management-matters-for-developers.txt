Why Secrets Management Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Secrets Management make better decisions, because Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.

Three situations show why it matters. First, database credentials. Second, API keys. Third, TLS certificates. In each case, a solid grasp of vaults and rotation separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: secrets in source control, long-lived shared credentials, and no rotation plan.

Good tools exist, including HashiCorp Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, and SOPS, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
