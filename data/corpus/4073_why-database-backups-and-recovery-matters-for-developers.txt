Why Database Backups and Recovery Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Database Backups and Recovery make better decisions, because Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.

Three situations show why it matters. First, recovering deleted tables. Second, migrating servers. Third, meeting compliance rules. In each case, a solid grasp of full and incremental backups and recovery point objective separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: never testing restores, storing backups on the same disk, and keeping credentials inside backup scripts.

You will not need to build everything yourself; pg_dump, mysqldump, and AWS Backup cover most needs.
