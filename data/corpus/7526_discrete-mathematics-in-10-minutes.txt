Discrete Mathematics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Discrete Mathematics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sets, logic, relations, and proofs.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine the grammar underneath every program. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through algorithm analysis, database theory, and cryptography.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for skipping proofs and confusing sets with sequences, then try Python sets for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to practice writing small proofs by hand.
