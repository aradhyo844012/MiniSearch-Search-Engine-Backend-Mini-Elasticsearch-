Monitoring and Observability Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Monitoring and Observability.

Q: Explain Monitoring and Observability in simple words.
A: Monitoring and Observability refers to collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves. I would add an example involving detecting outages.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry.

Q: How does it connect to Serverless Computing?
A: Both belong to the area of Cloud and DevOps, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as metrics, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with finding slow requests, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: noisy alerts.
