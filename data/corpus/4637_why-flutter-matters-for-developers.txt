Why Flutter Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Flutter make better decisions, because Flutter is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.

Three situations show why it matters. First, cross-platform mobile apps. Second, desktop apps. Third, custom user interfaces. In each case, a solid grasp of widgets and state separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: deep widget trees, poor state organization, and large app sizes.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Dart, Flutter DevTools, and Android Studio cover most needs.
