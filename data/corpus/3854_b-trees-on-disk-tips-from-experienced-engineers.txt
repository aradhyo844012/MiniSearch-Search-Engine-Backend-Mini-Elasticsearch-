B-Trees on Disk Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with B-Trees on Disk regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip pages; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit branching factor before anything else.
3. Make sure you can keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page.
4. Be careful with this one: random writes on fragmented pages.
5. Test the case of database indexes early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to file system directories as well.
