Mistakes to Avoid with Prompt Engineering

Everyone learns Prompt Engineering partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: vague requests. It usually appears when someone skips instructions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing context. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of drafting content. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: trusting outputs without verification. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on output format to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: specify the output format and include one or two examples.
