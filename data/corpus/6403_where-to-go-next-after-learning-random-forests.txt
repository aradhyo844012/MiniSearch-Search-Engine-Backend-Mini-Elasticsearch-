Where to Go Next After Learning Random Forests

You have met the basics of Random Forests. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Augmentation, which belongs to the same area as Random Forests (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of bagging, feature randomness, voting or out-of-bag error, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around feature importance ranking with scikit-learn, and note every problem you meet, starting with treating importance scores as causes.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Random Forests to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
