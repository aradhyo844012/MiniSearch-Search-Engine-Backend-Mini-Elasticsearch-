Reverse Proxies Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Reverse Proxies. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them.
Key terms: upstream servers, proxy headers, TLS termination, and caching.
Typical uses: hiding backend servers, terminating HTTPS, and routing requests to several apps.
Risks: losing the client IP address, wrong timeouts, and exposing internal paths.
Tools: NGINX, Caddy, and HAProxy.

Mental picture: a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room.

Habit to build: forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
