Transformers Explained Simply

Here is Transformers without the jargon: it is neural network architectures that use attention to relate all parts of an input to each other.

Think of Transformers as a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: self-attention, positional encoding, encoder and decoder blocks, and tokens. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with self-attention, then add positional encoding.

People reach for Transformers when they work on language translation, text generation, and code assistance.

One practical tip is to begin with small pretrained models.

After Transformers, a natural next topic is Hyperparameter Tuning.
