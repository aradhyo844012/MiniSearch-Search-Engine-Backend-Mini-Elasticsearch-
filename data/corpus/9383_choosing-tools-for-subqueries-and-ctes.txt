Choosing Tools for Subqueries and CTEs

There is no single best tool for Subqueries and CTEs. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PostgreSQL is a common first choice, especially for breaking complex reports into steps. SQLite suits teams that already work with similar technology, and dbt is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Subqueries and CTEs by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle filtering with computed results well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Subqueries and CTEs, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: correlated subqueries that run slowly.

As a rule of thumb, name each CTE after what it returns, no matter which tool you use.
