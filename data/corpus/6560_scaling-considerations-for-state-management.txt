Scaling Considerations for State Management

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how State Management changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding single source of truth and getting large single-page apps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for putting everything in global state and duplicated state that drifts apart, and measure with Redux before changing anything.

Large scale: reducers and derived state move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around shared shopping carts at this stage.

Before you scale, keep state as close as possible to where it is used. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
