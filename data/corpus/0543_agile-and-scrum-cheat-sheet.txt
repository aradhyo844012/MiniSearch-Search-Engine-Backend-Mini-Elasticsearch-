Agile and Scrum Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Agile and Scrum.

- Definition: iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles.
- Core ideas: sprints; backlogs; stand-ups; retrospectives.
- Use it for: student team projects; product development; handling changing requirements.
- Watch out for: skipping retrospectives; overloading sprints; treating estimates as promises.
- Tools: Jira, Trello, GitHub Projects.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for backlogs before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice sprints until it feels automatic.
