Practice Exercises for Backtracking

These exercises build real understanding of Backtracking. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Python recursion and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble solving Sudoku or generating permutations.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: forgetting to undo changes. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Backtracking and include the term decision tree.
Exercise 5: Compare itertools and debuggers for N-Queens puzzles and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Backtracking is a search technique that builds solutions step by step and undoes steps that lead to dead ends.
