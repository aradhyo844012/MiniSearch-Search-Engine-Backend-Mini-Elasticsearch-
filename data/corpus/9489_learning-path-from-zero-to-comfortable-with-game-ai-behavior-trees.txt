Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Game AI Behavior Trees

This path is for a learner who has never studied Game AI Behavior Trees before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Game AI Behavior Trees is tree-shaped plans that decide what a computer-controlled character does) and learn selector nodes and sequence nodes.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Unreal Engine behavior trees, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving enemy behavior.

Stage 3, depth: study conditions and actions, then deliberately explore this problem: trees that become tangled.

Stage 4, application: apply Game AI Behavior Trees to companion characters, compare py_trees with Behavior Designer, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Game AI Behavior Trees to someone else, and then move on to Physics Engines.
