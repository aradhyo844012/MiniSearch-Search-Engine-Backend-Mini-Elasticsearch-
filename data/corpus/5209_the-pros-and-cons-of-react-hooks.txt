The Pros and Cons of React Hooks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and React Hooks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports managing form input
- Makes fetching data on load easier
- Helps with sharing logic between components

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing dependencies in effects
- Risk: calling hooks conditionally
- Risk: stale closures

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as managing form input, and you can manage the risks, React Hooks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, enable the hooks lint rules from the start.
