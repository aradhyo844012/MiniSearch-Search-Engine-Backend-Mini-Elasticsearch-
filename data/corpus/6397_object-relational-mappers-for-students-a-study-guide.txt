Object-Relational Mappers for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Object-Relational Mappers.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Object-Relational Mappers in your own words.
2. Describe models, queries, relationships, and migrations.
3. Give examples from building CRUD apps quickly and switching databases.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code. Then draw a translator between the language of tables and the language of objects on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is N+1 queries a problem? How does queries relate to relationships? When would you choose SQLAlchemy or the Django ORM?

Study habit worth keeping: log the generated SQL during development.

When you feel ready, compare Object-Relational Mappers with Dependency Injection and write down three differences.
