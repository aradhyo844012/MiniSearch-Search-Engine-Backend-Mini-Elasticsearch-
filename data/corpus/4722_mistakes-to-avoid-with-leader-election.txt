Mistakes to Avoid with Leader Election

Everyone learns Leader Election partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: split brain. It usually appears when someone skips heartbeats and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: flapping leaders. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of database primaries. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: slow failure detection. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on failover to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays.
