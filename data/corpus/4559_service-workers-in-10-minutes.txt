Service Workers in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Service Workers.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn registration, install and activate events, cache storage, and fetch handlers.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through offline support, faster repeat visits, and push notifications.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for caching stale files forever and bugs surviving across deployments, then try Workbox for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to version your caches so old files can be removed.
