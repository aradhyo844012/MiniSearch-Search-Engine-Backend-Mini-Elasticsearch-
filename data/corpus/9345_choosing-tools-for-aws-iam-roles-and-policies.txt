Choosing Tools for AWS IAM Roles and Policies

There is no single best tool for AWS IAM Roles and Policies. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

IAM Access Analyzer is a common first choice, especially for securing deployments. the AWS CLI suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Terraform is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for AWS IAM Roles and Policies by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle granting services access well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for AWS IAM Roles and Policies, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: wildcard permissions.

As a rule of thumb, grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly, no matter which tool you use.
