Choosing Tools for Reinforcement Learning

There is no single best tool for Reinforcement Learning. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Gymnasium is a common first choice, especially for game playing. Stable-Baselines3 suits teams that already work with similar technology, and PyTorch is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle robot control well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: badly designed rewards.

As a rule of thumb, start with simple environments, no matter which tool you use.
