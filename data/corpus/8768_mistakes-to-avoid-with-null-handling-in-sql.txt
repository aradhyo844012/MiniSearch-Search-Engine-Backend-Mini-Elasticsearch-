Mistakes to Avoid with NULL Handling in SQL

Everyone learns NULL Handling in SQL partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: comparing with equals instead of IS NULL. It usually appears when someone skips three-valued logic and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: NOT IN with NULLs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of optional fields. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting NULLs in counts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on NULL in aggregates to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use IS NULL and COALESCE instead of comparing with equals.
