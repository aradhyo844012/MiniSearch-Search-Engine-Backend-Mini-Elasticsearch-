BGP and Internet Routing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with BGP and Internet Routing.

Do:
- Learn autonomous systems before anything else.
- Try bgpq4 on a small example.
- Make it a habit to filter route announcements strictly.
- Test the case of connecting ISPs early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: route leaks.
- Ignore this: hijacked prefixes.
- Ignore this: misconfigured filters.
- Adopt BGP and Internet Routing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with BGP and Internet Routing, a little patience with route announcements pays off more than any clever shortcut.
