Agile and Scrum: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Agile and Scrum?
A: The term Agile and Scrum refers to iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Jira, Trello, and GitHub Projects.

Q: Why do developers use Agile and Scrum?
A: Common uses include student team projects, product development, and handling changing requirements.

Q: How does it relate to Code Review?
A: Agile and Scrum and Code Review often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Software Engineering.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Agile and Scrum.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: skipping retrospectives; overloading sprints; and treating estimates as promises.
