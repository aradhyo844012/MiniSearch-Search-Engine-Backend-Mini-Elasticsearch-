Mistakes to Avoid with Two Pointers and Sliding Window

Everyone learns Two Pointers and Sliding Window partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: moving the wrong pointer. It usually appears when someone skips left and right pointers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: forgetting empty inputs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of finding pairs with a given sum. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: breaking the invariant. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on shrinking and expanding to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: write down the window invariant before coding the loop.
