Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Computer Vision Basics

This path is for a learner who has never studied Computer Vision Basics before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video) and learn pixels and filters and edges.

Stage 2, hands-on: install OpenCV, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving face detection.

Stage 3, depth: study feature detection and classification, then deliberately explore this problem: lighting changes.

Stage 4, application: apply Computer Vision Basics to document scanning, compare Pillow with scikit-image, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Computer Vision Basics to someone else, and then move on to Vector Graphics (SVG).
