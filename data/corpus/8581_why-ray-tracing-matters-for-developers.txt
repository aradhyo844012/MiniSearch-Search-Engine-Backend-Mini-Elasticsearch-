Why Ray Tracing Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Ray Tracing make better decisions, because Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.

Three situations show why it matters. First, movie visuals. Second, architectural visualization. Third, realistic game lighting. In each case, a solid grasp of rays and intersections separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: slow render times, noise from too few samples, and numerical precision errors.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Blender Cycles, NVIDIA OptiX, and POV-Ray cover most needs.
