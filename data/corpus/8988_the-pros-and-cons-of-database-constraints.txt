The Pros and Cons of Database Constraints

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Database Constraints is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports preventing bad data
- Makes protecting relationships easier
- Helps with documenting business rules

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: skipping constraints for speed
- Risk: missing indexes on foreign keys
- Risk: rules enforced only in the app

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as preventing bad data, and you can manage the risks, Database Constraints is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
