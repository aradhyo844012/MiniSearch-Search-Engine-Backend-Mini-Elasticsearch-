Mistakes to Avoid with SQL Basics

Everyone learns SQL Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: forgetting the WHERE clause on updates. It usually appears when someone skips SELECT and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: selecting every column. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of reading customer records. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming row order without ORDER BY. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on INSERT and UPDATE to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run a SELECT with the same WHERE clause before any UPDATE or DELETE.
