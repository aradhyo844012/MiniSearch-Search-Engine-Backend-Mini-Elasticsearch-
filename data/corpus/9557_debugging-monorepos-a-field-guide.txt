Debugging Monorepos: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Monorepos, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: slow checkouts, unclear ownership, and CI that rebuilds everything.
Step 3: revisit shared tooling and code ownership, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Nx or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Monorepos in work such as sharing libraries across apps come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, set up selective builds and tests early. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
