Choosing Tools for Form Validation

There is no single best tool for Form Validation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

HTML constraint validation is a common first choice, especially for sign-up forms. Zod suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Yup is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle payment pages well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: validating only on the client.

As a rule of thumb, validate on the server even when the browser checks too, no matter which tool you use.
