State Management Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with State Management regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit actions before anything else.
2. Test the case of large single-page apps early, before the design hardens.
3. Make sure you can keep state as close as possible to where it is used.
4. Reach for Redux first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Zustand.
5. Write down why you chose State Management so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to shared shopping carts as well.
