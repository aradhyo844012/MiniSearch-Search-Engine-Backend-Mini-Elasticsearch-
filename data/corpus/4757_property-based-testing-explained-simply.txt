Property-Based Testing Explained Simply

If Property-Based Testing sounds intimidating, relax. It is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Property-Based Testing as a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: properties, generators, shrinking, and invariants. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with properties, then add generators.

You will meet Property-Based Testing in work such as testing parsers, checking sorting and math functions, and finding edge cases.

One practical tip is to save failing examples as regular tests.

After Property-Based Testing, a natural next topic is Fuzz Testing.
