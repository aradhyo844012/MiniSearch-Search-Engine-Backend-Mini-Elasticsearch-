Chaos Engineering Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Chaos Engineering.

Do:
- Learn steady state before anything else.
- Try Chaos Monkey on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start with small experiments in a test environment.
- Test the case of testing resilience early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: experimenting without safeguards.
- Ignore this: having no steady-state metrics.
- Ignore this: running first in production.
- Adopt Chaos Engineering just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Chaos Engineering, a little patience with hypotheses pays off more than any clever shortcut.
