System Design Interviews in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through System Design Interviews.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn requirements, scaling estimates, trade-offs, and components.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through preparing for senior roles, practicing architectural thinking, and explaining real systems.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements and ignoring trade-offs, then try whiteboard sketches for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
