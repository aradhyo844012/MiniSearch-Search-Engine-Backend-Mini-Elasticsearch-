Data Augmentation for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Data Augmentation.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Data Augmentation in your own words.
2. Describe transformations, label preservation, synthetic samples, and augmentation pipelines.
3. Give examples from image models and text classification.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize. Then draw practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is changes that alter the label a problem? How does label preservation relate to synthetic samples? When would you choose Albumentations or torchvision?

Study habit worth keeping: augment only the training data.

When you feel ready, compare Data Augmentation with Hyperparameter Tuning and write down three differences.
