A* Pathfinding Glossary for Beginners

New words can make A* Pathfinding feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

A* Pathfinding: a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining.
open and closed sets: a core term, most visible in game characters.
cost so far: a core term that beginners often confuse with heuristics.
heuristics: a core term that connects to grids and graphs.
grids and graphs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Unity NavMesh: a tool commonly used for robot navigation.

Come back to this A* Pathfinding list whenever documentation for Python heapq uses a word you do not recognize.
