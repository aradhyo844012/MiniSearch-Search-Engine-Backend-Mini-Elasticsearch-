The Pros and Cons of AI Agents

No technology is free of trade-offs, and AI Agents is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports research assistants
- Makes workflow automation easier
- Helps with customer support

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: runaway loops
- Risk: unchecked tool permissions
- Risk: hard-to-debug behavior

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as research assistants, and you can manage the risks, AI Agents is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
