Practice Exercises for Digital Audio

These exercises build real understanding of Digital Audio. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: clipping from too high a level. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Explain how waveforms and the Nyquist limit differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick Audacity and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare SoX and the Web Audio API for podcasts and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble music production or voice calls.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.
