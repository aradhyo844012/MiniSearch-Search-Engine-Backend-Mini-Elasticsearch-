Graph Databases in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Graph Databases.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn nodes, edges, properties, and traversal queries.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a city map where intersections are records and roads are relationships. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through social networks, recommendation engines, and fraud detection.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for using them for simple tabular data and unbounded traversals, then try Neo4j for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
