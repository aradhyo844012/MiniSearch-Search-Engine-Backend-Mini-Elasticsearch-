The Pros and Cons of MongoDB Aggregation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and MongoDB Aggregation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports summarizing orders
- Makes building analytics queries easier
- Helps with reshaping documents

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: putting $match late
- Risk: huge intermediate results
- Risk: hitting memory limits

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as summarizing orders, and you can manage the risks, MongoDB Aggregation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, filter with $match as early as possible.
