Choosing Tools for Support Vector Machines

There is no single best tool for Support Vector Machines. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

scikit-learn is a common first choice, especially for text classification. LIBSVM suits teams that already work with similar technology, and NumPy is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle small high-dimensional datasets well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow training on large data.

As a rule of thumb, scale features and try a linear kernel first, no matter which tool you use.
