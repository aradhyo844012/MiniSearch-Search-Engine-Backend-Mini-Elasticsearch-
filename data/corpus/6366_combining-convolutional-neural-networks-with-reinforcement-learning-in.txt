Combining Convolutional Neural Networks with Reinforcement Learning in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Convolutional Neural Networks and Reinforcement Learning can work together in a single project.

Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

Imagine a project that involves image classification and game playing. Convolutional Neural Networks brings filters, while Reinforcement Learning brings agent.

A sensible plan is to start with PyTorch for the Convolutional Neural Networks part and Gymnasium for the Reinforcement Learning part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: ignoring data augmentation on one side, and slow training on the other.

Picture the whole as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes, working alongside training a dog with treats for good behavior.

Two habits help: first, start from a pretrained model; second, start with simple environments.
