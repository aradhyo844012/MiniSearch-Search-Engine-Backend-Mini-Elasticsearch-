CDNs Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of CDNs.

- Definition: networks of servers around the world that deliver static content from a location close to the user.
- Core ideas: edge servers; cache rules; origin servers; cache purging.
- Use it for: serving images and scripts; speeding up global sites; absorbing traffic spikes.
- Watch out for: caching private data; forgetting to purge old files; misconfigured cache headers.
- Tools: Cloudflare, Akamai, Amazon CloudFront.
- Quick tip: practice edge servers until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for cache rules before building anything large.
