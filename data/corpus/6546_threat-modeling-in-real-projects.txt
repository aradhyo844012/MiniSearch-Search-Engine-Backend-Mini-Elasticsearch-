Threat Modeling in Real Projects

Reading about Threat Modeling is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: designing new features. Here, assets drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against doing it once and never again.

Scenario two: reviewing architecture. This time attackers matters most, and tools such as STRIDE help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: prioritizing security work. The key idea is trust boundaries, and the biggest risk is ignoring data flows.

Across all three scenarios, Threat Modeling stays the same idea: a structured way of identifying what could go wrong in a system and how to prevent it. Only the details change.
