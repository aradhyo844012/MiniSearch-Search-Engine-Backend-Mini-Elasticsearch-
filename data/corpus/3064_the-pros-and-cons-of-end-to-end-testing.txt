The Pros and Cons of End-to-End Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and End-to-End Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports checking login and checkout flows
- Makes release verification easier
- Helps with cross-browser checks

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: flaky tests
- Risk: slow runs
- Risk: brittle selectors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as checking login and checkout flows, and you can manage the risks, End-to-End Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, cover only the most important user journeys.
