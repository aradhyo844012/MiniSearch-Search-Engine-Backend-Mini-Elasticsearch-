Scaling Considerations for Model Drift and Monitoring

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Model Drift and Monitoring changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding data drift and getting catching degrading models right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for monitoring only system health and having no ground truth labels, and measure with Evidently before changing anything.

Large scale: performance metrics and alerts move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around scheduling retraining at this stage.

Before you scale, monitor input data as well as accuracy. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
