Is Dependency Injection Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Dependency Injection, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them.

It is a good fit when your work involves easier unit testing or swapping implementations, and when your team understands constructor injection.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn interfaces, or when you cannot manage these risks: hiding too much behind containers, circular dependencies, and injecting too many dependencies.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether containers and inversion of control are part of the answer. If they are, Dependency Injection is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with configuring services and inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
