Mistakes to Avoid with Open Source Contribution

Everyone learns Open Source Contribution partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: opening huge pull requests. It usually appears when someone skips forks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring contribution rules. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of building a portfolio. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: expecting instant replies. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on contributing guides to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with documentation fixes or small bugs.
