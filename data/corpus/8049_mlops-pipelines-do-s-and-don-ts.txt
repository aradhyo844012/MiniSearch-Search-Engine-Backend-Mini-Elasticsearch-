MLOps Pipelines Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with MLOps Pipelines.

Do:
- Learn data versioning before anything else.
- Try MLflow on a small example.
- Make it a habit to version both the code and the data.
- Test the case of repeatable model releases early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: untracked data changes.
- Ignore this: manual deployment steps.
- Ignore this: no testing of data.
- Adopt MLOps Pipelines just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with MLOps Pipelines, a little patience with training pipelines pays off more than any clever shortcut.
