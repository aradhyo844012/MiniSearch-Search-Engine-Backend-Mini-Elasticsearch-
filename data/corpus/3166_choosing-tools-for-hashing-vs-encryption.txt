Choosing Tools for Hashing vs Encryption

There is no single best tool for Hashing vs Encryption. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

SHA-256 is a common first choice, especially for storing passwords. AES suits teams that already work with similar technology, and OpenSSL is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle verifying file integrity well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them.

As a rule of thumb, ask whether you will ever need the original value back, no matter which tool you use.
