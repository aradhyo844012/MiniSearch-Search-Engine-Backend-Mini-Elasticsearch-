Arduino Basics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Arduino Basics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices.
Key terms: sketches, digital and analog pins, setup and loop functions, and the serial monitor.
Typical uses: reading sensors, driving motors and LEDs, and learning electronics.
Risks: wiring errors, blocking delays in the loop, and insufficient power for motors.
Tools: Arduino IDE, PlatformIO, and Tinkercad.

Mental picture: a tiny brain that watches inputs and flips switches.

Habit to build: test each sensor on its own before combining them.
