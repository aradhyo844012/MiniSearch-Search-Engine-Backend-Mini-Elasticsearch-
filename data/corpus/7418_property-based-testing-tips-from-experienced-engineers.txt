Property-Based Testing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Property-Based Testing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Property-Based Testing so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for Hypothesis first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as QuickCheck.
3. Test the case of testing parsers early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can save failing examples as regular tests.
5. Do not skip properties; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to checking sorting and math functions as well.
