Why Cron Jobs and Scheduling Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Cron Jobs and Scheduling make better decisions, because Cron Jobs and Scheduling is a Unix tool that runs commands automatically at scheduled times.

Three situations show why it matters. First, nightly backups. Second, log rotation. Third, periodic reports. In each case, a solid grasp of crontab syntax and schedule fields separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: assuming cron has your shell environment, overlapping runs of slow jobs, and no logging of failures.

You will not need to build everything yourself; crontab, systemd timers, and flock cover most needs.
