Choosing Tools for Column-Oriented Storage

There is no single best tool for Column-Oriented Storage. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Apache Parquet is a common first choice, especially for analytics dashboards. ClickHouse suits teams that already work with similar technology, and DuckDB is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle large aggregations well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: slow single-row lookups.

As a rule of thumb, select only the columns you really need, no matter which tool you use.
