Where to Go Next After Learning Event Sourcing

You have met the basics of Event Sourcing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Leader Election, which belongs to the same area as Event Sourcing (Distributed Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of event log, projections, snapshots or replay, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around financial ledgers with Apache Kafka, and note every problem you meet, starting with changing event formats carelessly.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Event Sourcing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
