Flutter Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Flutter.

Do:
- Learn widgets before anything else.
- Try Dart on a small example.
- Make it a habit to split big widgets into small reusable ones.
- Test the case of cross-platform mobile apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: deep widget trees.
- Ignore this: poor state organization.
- Ignore this: large app sizes.
- Adopt Flutter just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Flutter, a little patience with state pays off more than any clever shortcut.
