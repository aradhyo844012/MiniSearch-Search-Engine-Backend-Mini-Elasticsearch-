A Short History of ETL Pipelines

Ideas behind ETL Pipelines developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Many students first meet ETL Pipelines in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination.

Data work is mostly preparation, and cleaning often takes longer than analysis.

To understand ETL Pipelines, start with extraction. Then move on to transformation, and finally look at loading and scheduling. A good way to remember extraction is to connect it with a concrete example from your own code.

A solid grasp of ETL Pipelines also makes Data Visualization easier to understand.
