Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Property-Based Testing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Property-Based Testing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Property-Based Testing is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold) and learn properties and generators.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Hypothesis, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving testing parsers.

Stage 3, depth: study shrinking and invariants, then deliberately explore this problem: vague properties.

Stage 4, application: apply Property-Based Testing to checking sorting and math functions, compare QuickCheck with fast-check, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Property-Based Testing to someone else, and then move on to Chaos Engineering.
