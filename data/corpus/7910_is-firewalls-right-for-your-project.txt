Is Firewalls Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Firewalls, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Firewalls is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules.

It is a good fit when your work involves protecting servers or segmenting networks, and when your team understands rules.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn stateful inspection, or when you cannot manage these risks: rules in the wrong order, overly broad allow rules, and locking yourself out remotely.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether default deny and zones are part of the answer. If they are, Firewalls is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with logging suspicious traffic and start with default deny and open only what you need.
