The Pros and Cons of gRPC

No technology is free of trade-offs, and gRPC is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports service-to-service calls
- Makes low-latency APIs easier
- Helps with streaming data

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: breaking changes in message fields
- Risk: poor browser support without a proxy
- Risk: harder manual debugging

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as service-to-service calls, and you can manage the risks, gRPC is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file.
