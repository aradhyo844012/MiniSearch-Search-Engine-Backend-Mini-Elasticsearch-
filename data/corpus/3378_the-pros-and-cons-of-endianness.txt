The Pros and Cons of Endianness

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Endianness is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports network protocols
- Makes binary file formats easier
- Helps with cross-platform data exchange

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing byte orders
- Risk: assuming all machines are alike
- Risk: forgetting to convert before sending

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as network protocols, and you can manage the risks, Endianness is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, always specify byte order in file and protocol formats.
