MongoDB Aggregation in Real Projects

Reading about MongoDB Aggregation is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: summarizing orders. Here, pipeline stages drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against putting $match late.

Scenario two: building analytics queries. This time $match and $group matters most, and tools such as MongoDB Compass help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: reshaping documents. The key idea is projection, and the biggest risk is huge intermediate results.

Across all three scenarios, MongoDB Aggregation stays the same idea: a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB. Only the details change.
