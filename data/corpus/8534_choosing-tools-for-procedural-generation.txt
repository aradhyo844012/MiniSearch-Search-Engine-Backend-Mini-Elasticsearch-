Choosing Tools for Procedural Generation

There is no single best tool for Procedural Generation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Perlin noise is a common first choice, especially for roguelike dungeons. Wave Function Collapse suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Unity is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Procedural Generation by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle infinite terrain well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Procedural Generation, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: levels that are unfair or unwinnable.

As a rule of thumb, log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced, no matter which tool you use.
