Understanding AI Agents Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out.

In that picture, planning is the first rule you follow, tool calls is the next step, and memory and guardrails keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal, and it involves details such as runaway loops that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for LangGraph to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain research assistants to a friend.
