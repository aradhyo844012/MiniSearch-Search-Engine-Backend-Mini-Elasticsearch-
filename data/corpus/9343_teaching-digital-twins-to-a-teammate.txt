Teaching Digital Twins to a Teammate

Explaining Digital Twins to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving factory monitoring, naming sensor data and simulation models as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain synchronization in Digital Twins back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, stale data, and suggest this habit: define the question the twin must answer first.
