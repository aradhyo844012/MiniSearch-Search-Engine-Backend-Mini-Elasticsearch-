The Pros and Cons of API Gateways

No technology is free of trade-offs, and API Gateways is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports exposing microservices
- Makes securing public APIs easier
- Helps with collecting usage metrics

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: a single point of failure
- Risk: business logic leaking into the gateway
- Risk: added latency

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as exposing microservices, and you can manage the risks, API Gateways is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep the gateway thin and put business logic in services.
