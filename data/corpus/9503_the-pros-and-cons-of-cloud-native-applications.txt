The Pros and Cons of Cloud-Native Applications

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Cloud-Native Applications is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports web platforms
- Makes data-heavy services easier
- Helps with rapidly changing products

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: treating the cloud like a data center
- Risk: too many services
- Risk: ignoring costs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as web platforms, and you can manage the risks, Cloud-Native Applications is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, design every service to be stateless where possible.
