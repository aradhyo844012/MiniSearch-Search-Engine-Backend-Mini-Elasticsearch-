Everything You Need to Know About Graph Databases

This page collects the essentials about Graph Databases in one place. In short, Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.

Core concepts:
- nodes
- edges
- properties
- traversal queries

Where it is used:
- social networks
- recommendation engines
- fraud detection

Common problems:
- using them for simple tabular data
- unbounded traversals
- supernodes with millions of edges

Tools to know:
- Neo4j
- Cypher
- Amazon Neptune

Remember this above all: a city map where intersections are records and roads are relationships is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
