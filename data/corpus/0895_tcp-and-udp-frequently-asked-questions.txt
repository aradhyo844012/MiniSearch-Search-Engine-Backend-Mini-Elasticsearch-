TCP and UDP: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is TCP and UDP?
A: The term TCP and UDP refers to the two main transport protocols, one reliable and ordered and the other fast and connectionless.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with TCP and UDP.

Q: Why do developers use TCP and UDP?
A: Common uses include web traffic, video calls, and online games.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Wireshark, netcat, and tcpdump.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with handshakes and acknowledgements, then move on to ports and congestion control.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: assuming UDP guarantees delivery; ignoring timeouts; and opening too many connections.
