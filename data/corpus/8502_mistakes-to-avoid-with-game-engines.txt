Mistakes to Avoid with Game Engines

Everyone learns Game Engines partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: choosing an engine by hype. It usually appears when someone skips scenes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: loading everything at start-up. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of indie games. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring platform limits. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on build pipelines to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
