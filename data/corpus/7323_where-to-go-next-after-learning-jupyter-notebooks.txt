Where to Go Next After Learning Jupyter Notebooks

You have met the basics of Jupyter Notebooks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Lakes, which belongs to the same area as Jupyter Notebooks (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of cells, kernels, markdown or outputs, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around teaching and demos with JupyterLab, and note every problem you meet, starting with running cells out of order.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Jupyter Notebooks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
