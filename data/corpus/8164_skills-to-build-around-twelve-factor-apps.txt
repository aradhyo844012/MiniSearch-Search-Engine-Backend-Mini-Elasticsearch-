Skills to Build Around Twelve-Factor Apps

Knowing the definition of Twelve-Factor Apps is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with config in the environment and stateless processes, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Heroku on a Twelve-Factor Apps task, and try at least one alternative such as Docker.

Judgment: learn to decide when Twelve-Factor Apps fits a problem such as building web services, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a checklist for packing a suitcase that fits any airline.

Habits: keep config out of the code, and write down what you learn after each project.
