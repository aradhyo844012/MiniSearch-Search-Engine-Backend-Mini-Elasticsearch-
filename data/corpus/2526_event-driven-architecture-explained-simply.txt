Event-Driven Architecture Explained Simply

Here is Event-Driven Architecture without the jargon: it is a style in which components communicate by publishing events and reacting to them.

Think of Event-Driven Architecture as a notice board where people post news and others react when they see it. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: producers and consumers, event brokers, loose coupling, and asynchronous handling. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with producers and consumers, then add event brokers.

You will meet Event-Driven Architecture in work such as order pipelines, real-time notifications, and integrating many services.

One practical tip is to document every event name and schema.
