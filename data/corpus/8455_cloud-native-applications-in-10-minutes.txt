Cloud-Native Applications in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Cloud-Native Applications.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Cloud-Native Applications is applications designed from the start to run in the cloud using containers and managed services.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn containers, microservices, declarative configuration, and elastic scaling.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a plant that grows best in the soil it was designed for. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through web platforms, data-heavy services, and rapidly changing products.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for treating the cloud like a data center and too many services, then try Kubernetes for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to design every service to be stateless where possible.
