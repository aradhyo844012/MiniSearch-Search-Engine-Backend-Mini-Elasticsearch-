Teaching Time Management for Developers to a Teammate

Explaining Time Management for Developers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: packing a suitcase with the big items first. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving managing coursework and projects, naming prioritization and time blocking as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain breaking down tasks in Time Management for Developers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, multitasking, and suggest this habit: break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
