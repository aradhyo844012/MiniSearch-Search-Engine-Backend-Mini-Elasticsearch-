Teaching Reverse Proxies to a Teammate

Explaining Reverse Proxies to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a hotel front desk that routes guests to the right room. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Reverse Proxies is servers that sit in front of applications and forward client requests to them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving hiding backend servers, naming upstream servers and proxy headers as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain TLS termination in Reverse Proxies back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, losing the client IP address, and suggest this habit: forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
