Rate Limiting: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Rate Limiting?
A: The term Rate Limiting refers to controlling how many requests a client can make in a given period.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: limits that are too strict; keeping counters only on one server; and no helpful error messages.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with token bucket and fixed window, then move on to sliding window and 429 responses.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Redis, NGINX, and API gateways.

Q: How does it relate to JSON?
A: Rate Limiting and JSON often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Backend and APIs.

Q: Why do developers use Rate Limiting?
A: Common uses include protecting APIs, stopping brute force attempts, and fair usage across customers.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Rate Limiting.
