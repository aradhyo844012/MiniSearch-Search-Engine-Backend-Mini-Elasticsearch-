Regions and Availability Zones Explained Simply

Here is Regions and Availability Zones without the jargon: it is the geographic locations and isolated data centers a cloud provider uses to host resources.

Think of Regions and Availability Zones as branches of a bank in different cities and different buildings. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: regions, availability zones, latency, and data residency. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with regions, then add availability zones.

People reach for Regions and Availability Zones when they work on placing services near users, surviving data center failures, and meeting data laws.

One practical tip is to spread critical services across at least two zones.
