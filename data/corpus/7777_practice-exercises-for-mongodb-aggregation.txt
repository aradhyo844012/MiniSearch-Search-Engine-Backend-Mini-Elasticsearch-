Practice Exercises for MongoDB Aggregation

These exercises build real understanding of MongoDB Aggregation. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how projection and indexes differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing MongoDB Aggregation and include the term pipeline stages.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble summarizing orders or building analytics queries.
Exercise 4: Pick MongoDB Compass and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare mongosh and PyMongo for reshaping documents and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: MongoDB Aggregation is a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.
