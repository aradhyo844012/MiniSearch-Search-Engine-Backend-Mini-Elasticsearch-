VPNs for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover VPNs.

Learning goals:
1. Explain VPNs in your own words.
2. Describe tunneling, encryption, authentication, and split tunneling.
3. Give examples from remote work access and linking offices.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet. Then draw a private tunnel under a public road on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is assuming it makes you anonymous a problem? How does encryption relate to authentication? When would you choose WireGuard or OpenVPN?

Study habit: test for DNS leaks after connecting.

When you feel ready, compare VPNs with Reverse Proxies and write down three differences.
