Mistakes to Avoid with gRPC

Everyone learns gRPC partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: breaking changes in message fields. It usually appears when someone skips protocol buffers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor browser support without a proxy. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of service-to-service calls. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: harder manual debugging. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on HTTP/2 to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file.
