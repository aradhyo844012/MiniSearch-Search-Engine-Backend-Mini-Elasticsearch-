B-Trees on Disk Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with B-Trees on Disk.

Do:
- Learn pages before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page.
- Test the case of database indexes early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: random writes on fragmented pages.
- Ignore this: oversized keys.
- Ignore this: ignoring page fill factor.
- Adopt B-Trees on Disk just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with B-Trees on Disk, a little patience with branching factor pays off more than any clever shortcut.
