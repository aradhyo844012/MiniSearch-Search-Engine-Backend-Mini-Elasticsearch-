Debugging TypeScript Generics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around TypeScript Generics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: overusing any, overly complex generic types, and forgetting constraints.
Step 3: revisit type parameters and constraints, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use tsc or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving TypeScript Generics in work such as reusable utility functions come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, add constraints so that error messages become clearer. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
