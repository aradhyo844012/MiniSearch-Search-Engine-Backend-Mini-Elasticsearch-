Combining Secrets Management with Distributed Tracing in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Secrets Management and Distributed Tracing can work together in a single project.

Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely. Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Imagine a project that involves database credentials and finding slow services. Secrets Management brings vaults, while Distributed Tracing brings spans.

A sensible plan is to start with HashiCorp Vault for the Secrets Management part and OpenTelemetry for the Distributed Tracing part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: long-lived shared credentials on one side, and sampling too aggressively on the other.

Picture the whole as a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet, working alongside a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route.

Two habits help: first, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak; second, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
