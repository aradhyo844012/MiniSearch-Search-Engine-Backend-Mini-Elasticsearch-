Time-Series Databases Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Time-Series Databases. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.
Key terms: timestamps, retention policies, downsampling, and tags and fields.
Typical uses: server metrics, sensor readings, and financial tick data.
Risks: high-cardinality tags, keeping raw data forever, and querying without time bounds.
Tools: InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, and Prometheus.

Mental picture: a long logbook where every line starts with the time.

Habit to build: include a time range in every query.
