Penetration Testing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Penetration Testing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of pre-launch security checks early, before the design hardens.
2. Write down why you chose Penetration Testing so the next person understands the decision.
3. Reach for OWASP ZAP first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Burp Suite.
4. Be careful with this one: testing without written permission.
5. Do not skip scoping; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to compliance audits as well.
