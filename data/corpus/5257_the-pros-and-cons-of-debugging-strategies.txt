The Pros and Cons of Debugging Strategies

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Debugging Strategies is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports fixing production issues
- Makes learning unfamiliar code easier
- Helps with reducing repeat bugs

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: changing many things at once
- Risk: guessing without evidence
- Risk: not writing a test afterward

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as fixing production issues, and you can manage the risks, Debugging Strategies is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, change one thing at a time and record the result.
