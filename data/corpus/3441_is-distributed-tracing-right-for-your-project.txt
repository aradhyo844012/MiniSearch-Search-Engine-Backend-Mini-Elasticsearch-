Is Distributed Tracing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Distributed Tracing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

It is a good fit when your work involves finding slow services or understanding dependencies, and when your team understands spans.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn trace IDs, or when you cannot manage these risks: missing context propagation, sampling too aggressively, and overhead from too many spans.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether context propagation and sampling are part of the answer. If they are, Distributed Tracing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with debugging errors across services and propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
