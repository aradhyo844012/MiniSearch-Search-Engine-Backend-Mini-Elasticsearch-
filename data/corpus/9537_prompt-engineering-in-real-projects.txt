Prompt Engineering in Real Projects

Reading about Prompt Engineering is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: drafting content. Here, instructions drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against vague requests.

Scenario two: extracting data from text. This time examples matters most, and tools such as model playgrounds help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: building assistants. The key idea is context, and the biggest risk is missing context.

Across all three scenarios, Prompt Engineering stays the same idea: the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models. Only the details change.
