Vector Graphics (SVG) Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Vector Graphics (SVG). Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size.
Key terms: paths, the viewBox, fill and stroke, and transforms.
Typical uses: logos, icons, and charts.
Risks: overly complex paths, a missing viewBox, and inconsistent stroke widths.
Tools: Inkscape, SVGO, and Figma.

Mental picture: a recipe for drawing a picture rather than a photo of it.

Habit to build: set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
