Mistakes to Avoid with Game Loop

Everyone learns Game Loop partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: tying logic to frame rate. It usually appears when someone skips input handling and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow update steps. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of running every real-time game. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring delta time. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on frame timing to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use delta time so speed does not depend on frame rate.
