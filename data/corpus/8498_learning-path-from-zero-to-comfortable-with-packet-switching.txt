Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Packet Switching

This path is for a learner who has never studied Packet Switching before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Packet Switching is a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network) and learn packets and headers.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Wireshark, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving internet traffic.

Stage 3, depth: study routing and reassembly, then deliberately explore this problem: packet loss.

Stage 4, application: apply Packet Switching to voice over IP, compare tcpdump with iperf, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Packet Switching to someone else, and then move on to Network Troubleshooting.
