Clear vocabulary makes Cross-Site Scripting much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Cross-Site Scripting, they usually mean a vulnerability where attackers get a site to run their scripts in other users' browsers. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.
