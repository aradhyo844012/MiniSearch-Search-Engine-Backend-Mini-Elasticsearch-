Smart Contracts Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Smart Contracts feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Smart Contracts: programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.
contract code: a core term, most visible in token systems.
transactions: a core term that beginners often confuse with gas fees.
gas fees: a core term that connects to immutability.
immutability: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Solidity: a tool commonly used for automated escrow.

Come back to this Smart Contracts list whenever documentation for Hardhat uses a word you do not recognize.
