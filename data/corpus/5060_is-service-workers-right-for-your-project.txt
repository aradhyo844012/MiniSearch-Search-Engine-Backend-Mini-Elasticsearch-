Is Service Workers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Service Workers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests.

It is a good fit when your work involves offline support or faster repeat visits, and when your team understands registration.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn install and activate events, or when you cannot manage these risks: caching stale files forever, bugs surviving across deployments, and forgetting that HTTPS is required.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether cache storage and fetch handlers are part of the answer. If they are, Service Workers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with push notifications and version your caches so old files can be removed.
