Cloud Storage Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Cloud Storage.

- Definition: storing files and objects on remote servers that scale on demand.
- Core ideas: buckets; objects; access policies; storage classes.
- Use it for: backups; serving images; data lakes.
- Watch out for: public buckets by mistake; surprise egress costs; no lifecycle rules.
- Tools: Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, Azure Blob Storage.
- Related topic: CI/CD Pipelines.
- Quick tip: practice buckets until it feels automatic.
