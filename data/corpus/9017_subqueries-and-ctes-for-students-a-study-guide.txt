Subqueries and CTEs for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Subqueries and CTEs.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Subqueries and CTEs in your own words.
2. Describe subqueries, WITH clauses, correlated queries, and readability.
3. Give examples from breaking complex reports into steps and filtering with computed results.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets. Then draw solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is correlated subqueries that run slowly a problem? How does WITH clauses relate to correlated queries? When would you choose PostgreSQL or SQLite?

Study habit worth keeping: name each CTE after what it returns.

When you feel ready, compare Subqueries and CTEs with Stored Procedures and Triggers and write down three differences.
