Competitive Programming Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Competitive Programming.

Do:
- Learn problem patterns before anything else.
- Try Codeforces on a small example.
- Make it a habit to review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
- Test the case of interview preparation early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: memorizing solutions without understanding.
- Ignore this: skipping fundamentals.
- Ignore this: burning out.
- Adopt Competitive Programming just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Competitive Programming, a little patience with time limits pays off more than any clever shortcut.
