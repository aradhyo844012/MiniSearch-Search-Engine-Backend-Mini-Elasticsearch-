Software Estimation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Software Estimation.

Do:
- Learn story points before anything else.
- Try planning poker on a small example.
- Make it a habit to give ranges instead of single numbers.
- Test the case of sprint planning early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating estimates as promises.
- Ignore this: ignoring unknowns.
- Ignore this: estimating alone.
- Adopt Software Estimation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Software Estimation, a little patience with ranges pays off more than any clever shortcut.
