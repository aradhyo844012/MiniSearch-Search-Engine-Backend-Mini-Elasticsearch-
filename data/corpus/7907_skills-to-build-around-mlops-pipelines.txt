Skills to Build Around MLOps Pipelines

Knowing the definition of MLOps Pipelines is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with data versioning and training pipelines, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with MLflow on a MLOps Pipelines task, and try at least one alternative such as Kubeflow.

Judgment: learn to decide when MLOps Pipelines fits a problem such as repeatable model releases, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a factory line that turns data into a tested, packaged model.

Habits: version both the code and the data, and write down what you learn after each project.
