Discrete Mathematics Explained Simply

Here is Discrete Mathematics without the jargon: it is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.

Think of Discrete Mathematics as the grammar underneath every program. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sets, logic, relations, and proofs. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sets, then add logic.

You will meet Discrete Mathematics in work such as algorithm analysis, database theory, and cryptography.

One practical tip is to practice writing small proofs by hand.

After Discrete Mathematics, a natural next topic is Information Theory and Entropy.
