Write-Ahead Logging Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Write-Ahead Logging regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip log records; everything else builds on it.
2. Test the case of surviving crashes early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit checkpoints before anything else.
4. Reach for PostgreSQL WAL first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as SQLite journal.
5. Make sure you can test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to feeding replication streams as well.
