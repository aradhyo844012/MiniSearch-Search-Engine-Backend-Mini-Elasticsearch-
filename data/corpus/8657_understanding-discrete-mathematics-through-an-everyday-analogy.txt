Understanding Discrete Mathematics Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture the grammar underneath every program.

In that picture, sets is the first rule you follow, logic is the next step, and relations and proofs keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs, and it involves details such as skipping proofs that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Python sets to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain algorithm analysis to a friend.
