Process Signals and Job Control Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Process Signals and Job Control regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Process Signals and Job Control so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: using kill -9 first.
3. Test the case of stopping runaway programs early, before the design hardens.
4. Make sure you can try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit foreground and background jobs before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to shutting down gracefully as well as to Process Signals and Job Control in general.
