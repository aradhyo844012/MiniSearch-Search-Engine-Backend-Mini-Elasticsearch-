Dijkstra's Algorithm Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Dijkstra's Algorithm.

Do:
- Learn priority queue before anything else.
- Try Python heapq on a small example.
- Make it a habit to stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue.
- Test the case of route planning early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using it with negative edge weights.
- Ignore this: skipping the priority queue and getting slow code.
- Ignore this: not stopping early when only one target is needed.
- Adopt Dijkstra's Algorithm just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Dijkstra's Algorithm, a little patience with distance table pays off more than any clever shortcut.
