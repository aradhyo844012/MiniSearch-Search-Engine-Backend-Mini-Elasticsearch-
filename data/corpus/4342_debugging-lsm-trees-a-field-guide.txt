Debugging LSM Trees: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around LSM Trees, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: read amplification, compaction stalls, and poor bloom filter tuning.
Step 3: revisit memtable and sorted string tables, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use RocksDB or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving LSM Trees in work such as write-heavy databases come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, watch compaction metrics before blaming the application. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
