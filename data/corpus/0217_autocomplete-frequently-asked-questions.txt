Autocomplete: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Autocomplete?
A: The term Autocomplete refers to suggesting likely completions while a person types a query.

Q: Why do developers use Autocomplete?
A: Common uses include search boxes, command palettes, and address entry.

Q: How does it relate to Stop Words?
A: Autocomplete and Stop Words often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: slow suggestions; offensive or irrelevant completions; and ignoring typos in the prefix.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include tries, Redis sorted sets, and Elasticsearch suggesters.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with prefixes and ranking suggestions, then move on to latency and query logs.
