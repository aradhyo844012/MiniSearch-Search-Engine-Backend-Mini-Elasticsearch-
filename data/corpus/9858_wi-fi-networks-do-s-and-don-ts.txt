Wi-Fi Networks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Wi-Fi Networks.

Do:
- Learn SSIDs before anything else.
- Try Wi-Fi analyzer apps on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
- Test the case of home internet early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overlapping channels.
- Ignore this: weak passwords.
- Ignore this: poor access point placement.
- Adopt Wi-Fi Networks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Wi-Fi Networks, a little patience with channels and bands pays off more than any clever shortcut.
