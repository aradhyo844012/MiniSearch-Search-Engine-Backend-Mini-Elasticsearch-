Where to Go Next After Learning Modular Monoliths

You have met the basics of Modular Monoliths. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Test-Driven Development, which belongs to the same area as Modular Monoliths (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of module boundaries, public interfaces, shared database rules or dependency checks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around early-stage products with ArchUnit, and note every problem you meet, starting with modules reaching into each other's tables.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Modular Monoliths to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
