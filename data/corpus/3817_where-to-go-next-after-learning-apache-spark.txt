Where to Go Next After Learning Apache Spark

You have met the basics of Apache Spark. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Lakes, which belongs to the same area as Apache Spark (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of DataFrames, transformations and actions, partitions or lazy execution, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around machine learning on big data with PySpark, and note every problem you meet, starting with data skew.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Apache Spark to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
