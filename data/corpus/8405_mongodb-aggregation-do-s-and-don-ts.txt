MongoDB Aggregation Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with MongoDB Aggregation.

Do:
- Learn pipeline stages before anything else.
- Try MongoDB Compass on a small example.
- Make it a habit to filter with $match as early as possible.
- Test the case of summarizing orders early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: putting $match late.
- Ignore this: huge intermediate results.
- Ignore this: hitting memory limits.
- Adopt MongoDB Aggregation just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with MongoDB Aggregation, a little patience with $match and $group pays off more than any clever shortcut.
