Digital Audio Explained Simply

Here is Digital Audio without the jargon: it is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

Think of Digital Audio as taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: sampling rate, bit depth, waveforms, and the Nyquist limit. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with sampling rate, then add bit depth.

You will meet Digital Audio in work such as music production, voice calls, and podcasts.

One practical tip is to keep recordings below the clipping level.

Once Digital Audio feels comfortable, read about Image Compression, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
