Rate Limiting Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Rate Limiting.

Q: Explain Rate Limiting in simple words.
A: Rate Limiting refers to controlling how many requests a client can make in a given period. I would add an example involving protecting APIs.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as token bucket, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: limits that are too strict; keeping counters only on one server; and no helpful error messages.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with stopping brute force attempts, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: token bucket, fixed window, sliding window, and 429 responses.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: limits that are too strict.
