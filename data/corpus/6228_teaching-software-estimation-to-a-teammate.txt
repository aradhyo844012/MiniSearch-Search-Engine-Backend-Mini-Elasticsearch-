Teaching Software Estimation to a Teammate

Explaining Software Estimation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: forecasting the weather rather than reading a timetable. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Software Estimation is predicting how much time and effort a piece of work will take. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sprint planning, naming story points and ranges as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain uncertainty in Software Estimation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, treating estimates as promises, and suggest this habit: give ranges instead of single numbers.
