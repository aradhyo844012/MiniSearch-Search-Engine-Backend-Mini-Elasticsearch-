Understanding Modular Monoliths Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture an apartment building with separate flats under one roof.

In that picture, module boundaries is the first rule you follow, public interfaces is the next step, and shared database rules and dependency checks keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Modular Monoliths is single deployable applications built from well-separated internal modules, and it involves details such as modules reaching into each other's tables that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for ArchUnit to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain small teams to a friend.
