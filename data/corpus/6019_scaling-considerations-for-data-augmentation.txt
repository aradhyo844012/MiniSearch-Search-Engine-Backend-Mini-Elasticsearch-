Scaling Considerations for Data Augmentation

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Data Augmentation changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding transformations and getting image models right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for changes that alter the label and augmenting the test set, and measure with Albumentations before changing anything.

Large scale: synthetic samples and augmentation pipelines move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around text classification at this stage.

Before you scale, augment only the training data. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
