Practice Exercises for Managed Cloud Databases

These exercises build real understanding of Managed Cloud Databases. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Amazon RDS and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Compare Cloud SQL and Azure Database for high availability setups and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Managed Cloud Databases and include the term automated backups.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble launching apps quickly or reducing database administration.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: surprise costs. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Managed Cloud Databases is database services where the cloud provider handles backups, patching and failover.
