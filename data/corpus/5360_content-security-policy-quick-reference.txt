Content Security Policy Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Content Security Policy. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load.
Key terms: directives, nonces, report-only mode, and trusted sources.
Typical uses: reducing the impact of XSS, blocking unwanted scripts, and monitoring policy violations.
Risks: allowing unsafe-inline, policies too broad to help, and breaking third-party widgets.
Tools: browser DevTools, CSP Evaluator, and Report URI.

Mental picture: a guest list at the door of a building.

Habit to build: start in report-only mode to learn what would break.

See also: Encryption at Rest, which sits close to Content Security Policy in the area of Application Security.
