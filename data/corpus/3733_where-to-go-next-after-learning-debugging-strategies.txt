Where to Go Next After Learning Debugging Strategies

You have met the basics of Debugging Strategies. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Time Management for Developers, which belongs to the same area as Debugging Strategies (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of reproducing the bug, isolating the cause, forming hypotheses or binary search through code, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around learning unfamiliar code with debuggers, and note every problem you meet, starting with changing many things at once.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Debugging Strategies to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
