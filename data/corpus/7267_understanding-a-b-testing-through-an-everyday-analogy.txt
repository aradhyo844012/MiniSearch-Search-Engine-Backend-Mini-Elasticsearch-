Understanding A/B Testing Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests.

In that picture, control and variant is the first rule you follow, random assignment is the next step, and metrics and sample size keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better, and it involves details such as stopping tests early that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Optimizely to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain testing page designs to a friend.
