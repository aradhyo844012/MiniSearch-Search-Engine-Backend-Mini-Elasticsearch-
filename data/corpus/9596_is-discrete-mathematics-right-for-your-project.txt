Is Discrete Mathematics Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Discrete Mathematics, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.

It is a good fit when your work involves algorithm analysis or database theory, and when your team understands sets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn logic, or when you cannot manage these risks: skipping proofs, confusing sets with sequences, and careless notation.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether relations and proofs are part of the answer. If they are, Discrete Mathematics is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with cryptography and practice writing small proofs by hand.
