Star Schema Modeling Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Star Schema Modeling feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Star Schema Modeling: a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.
fact tables: a core term, most visible in business intelligence reports.
dimension tables: a core term that beginners often confuse with surrogate keys.
surrogate keys: a core term that connects to grain.
grain: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
dbt: a tool commonly used for data warehouses.

Come back to this Star Schema Modeling list whenever documentation for Snowflake uses a word you do not recognize.
