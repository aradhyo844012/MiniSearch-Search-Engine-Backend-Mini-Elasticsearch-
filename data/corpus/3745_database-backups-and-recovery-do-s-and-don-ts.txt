Database Backups and Recovery Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Database Backups and Recovery.

Do:
- Learn full and incremental backups before anything else.
- Try pg_dump on a small example.
- Make it a habit to schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
- Test the case of recovering deleted tables early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: never testing restores.
- Ignore this: storing backups on the same disk.
- Ignore this: keeping credentials inside backup scripts.
- Adopt Database Backups and Recovery just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Database Backups and Recovery, a little patience with recovery point objective pays off more than any clever shortcut.
