Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Firewalls

This path is for a learner who has never studied Firewalls before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Firewalls is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules) and learn rules and stateful inspection.

Stage 2, hands-on: install ufw, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving protecting servers.

Stage 3, depth: study default deny and zones, then deliberately explore this problem: rules in the wrong order.

Stage 4, application: apply Firewalls to segmenting networks, compare iptables with pfSense, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Firewalls to someone else, and then move on to Reverse Proxies.
