Choosing Tools for Regular Expressions

There is no single best tool for Regular Expressions. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

the Python re module is a common first choice, especially for validating input. regex101 suits teams that already work with similar technology, and grep is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle searching logs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: catastrophic backtracking.

As a rule of thumb, build patterns in small steps and test each one, no matter which tool you use.
