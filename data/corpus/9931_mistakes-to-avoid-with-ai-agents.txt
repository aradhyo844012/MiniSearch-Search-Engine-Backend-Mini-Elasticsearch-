Mistakes to Avoid with AI Agents

Everyone learns AI Agents partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: runaway loops. It usually appears when someone skips planning and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unchecked tool permissions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of research assistants. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hard-to-debug behavior. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on guardrails to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
