Is Eventual Consistency Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Eventual Consistency, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Eventual Consistency is a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough.

It is a good fit when your work involves global social feeds or shopping cart services, and when your team understands replication delay.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn conflict resolution, or when you cannot manage these risks: assuming reads are always fresh, unresolved write conflicts, and no user feedback during delays.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether read-your-writes and convergence are part of the answer. If they are, Eventual Consistency is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with DNS records and decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
