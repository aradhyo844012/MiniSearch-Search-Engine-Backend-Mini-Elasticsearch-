Regular Expressions Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Regular Expressions. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: compact patterns for matching and extracting text.
Key terms: character classes, quantifiers, groups, and anchors.
Typical uses: validating input, searching logs, and extracting fields from text.
Risks: catastrophic backtracking, patterns that are too greedy, and using them to parse HTML.
Tools: the Python re module, regex101, and grep.

Mental picture: a stencil that only lets certain shapes of text show through.

Habit to build: build patterns in small steps and test each one.
