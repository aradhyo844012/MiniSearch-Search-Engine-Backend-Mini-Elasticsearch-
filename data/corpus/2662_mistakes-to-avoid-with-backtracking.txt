Mistakes to Avoid with Backtracking

Everyone learns Backtracking partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: forgetting to undo changes. It usually appears when someone skips decision tree and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: missing pruning rules. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of solving Sudoku. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: copying large state at every step. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on constraint checking to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: prune as early as possible because the savings multiply.
