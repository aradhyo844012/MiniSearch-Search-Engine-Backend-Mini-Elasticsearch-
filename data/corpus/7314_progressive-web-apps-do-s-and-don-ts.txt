Progressive Web Apps Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Progressive Web Apps.

Do:
- Learn web app manifest before anything else.
- Try Lighthouse on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test the app in airplane mode.
- Test the case of offline-friendly tools early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring platform limitations.
- Ignore this: poor offline fallbacks.
- Ignore this: heavy first loads.
- Adopt Progressive Web Apps just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Progressive Web Apps, a little patience with service worker pays off more than any clever shortcut.
