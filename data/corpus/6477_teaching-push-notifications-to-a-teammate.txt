Teaching Push Notifications to a Teammate

Explaining Push Notifications to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving order updates, naming device tokens and push services as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain payloads in Push Notifications back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, asking for permission too early, and suggest this habit: ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.
