Offline-First Apps Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Offline-First Apps regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can show users which changes have not synced yet.
2. Be careful with this one: unresolved sync conflicts.
3. Reach for SQLite first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as PouchDB.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit sync queues before anything else.
5. Test the case of field service tools early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to note-taking apps as well.
