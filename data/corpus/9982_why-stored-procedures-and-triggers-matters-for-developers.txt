Why Stored Procedures and Triggers Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Stored Procedures and Triggers make better decisions, because Stored Procedures and Triggers is code stored inside the database that runs on demand or automatically when data changes.

Three situations show why it matters. First, enforcing rules close to the data. Second, audit logging. Third, batch updates. In each case, a solid grasp of procedures and triggers separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: hidden logic that surprises developers, hard-to-test code, and trigger chains.

Good tools exist, including PL/pgSQL, MySQL Workbench, and pgTAP, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
