Where to Go Next After Learning Web Storage

You have met the basics of Web Storage. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Service Workers, which belongs to the same area as Web Storage (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies or IndexedDB, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around remembering form drafts with browser DevTools, and note every problem you meet, starting with storing sensitive data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Web Storage to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
