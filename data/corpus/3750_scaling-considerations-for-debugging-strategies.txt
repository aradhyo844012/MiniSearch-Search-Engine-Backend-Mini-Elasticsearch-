Scaling Considerations for Debugging Strategies

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Debugging Strategies changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding reproducing the bug and getting fixing production issues right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for changing many things at once and guessing without evidence, and measure with debuggers before changing anything.

Large scale: forming hypotheses and binary search through code move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around learning unfamiliar code at this stage.

Before you scale, change one thing at a time and record the result. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
