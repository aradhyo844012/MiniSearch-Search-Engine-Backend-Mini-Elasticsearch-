Skills to Build Around Greedy Algorithms

Knowing the definition of Greedy Algorithms is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with greedy choice property and optimal substructure, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with pen and paper proofs on a Greedy Algorithms task, and try at least one alternative such as Python sorted.

Judgment: learn to decide when Greedy Algorithms fits a problem such as interval scheduling, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example picking the closest open parking spot at every junction and hoping it works out.

Habits: try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it, and write down what you learn after each project.
