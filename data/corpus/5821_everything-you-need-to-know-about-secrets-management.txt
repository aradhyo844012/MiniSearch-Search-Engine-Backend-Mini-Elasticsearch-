Everything You Need to Know About Secrets Management

This page collects the essentials about Secrets Management in one place. In short, Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.

Core concepts:
- vaults
- rotation
- access policies
- audit logs

Where it is used:
- database credentials
- API keys
- TLS certificates

Common problems:
- secrets in source control
- long-lived shared credentials
- no rotation plan

Tools to know:
- HashiCorp Vault
- AWS Secrets Manager
- SOPS

Remember this above all: a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
