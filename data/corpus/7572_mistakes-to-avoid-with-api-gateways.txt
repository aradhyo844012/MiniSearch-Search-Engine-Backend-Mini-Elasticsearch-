Mistakes to Avoid with API Gateways

Everyone learns API Gateways partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: a single point of failure. It usually appears when someone skips routing and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: business logic leaking into the gateway. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of exposing microservices. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: added latency. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on request transformation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep the gateway thin and put business logic in services.
