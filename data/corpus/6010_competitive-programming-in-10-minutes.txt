Competitive Programming in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Competitive Programming.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Competitive Programming is solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn problem patterns, time limits, contests, and upsolving.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine athletic training for problem solving. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through interview preparation, learning algorithms, and practicing under pressure.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for memorizing solutions without understanding and skipping fundamentals, then try Codeforces for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
