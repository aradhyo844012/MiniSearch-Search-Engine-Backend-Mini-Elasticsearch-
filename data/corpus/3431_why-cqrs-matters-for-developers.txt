Why CQRS Matters for Developers

Developers who understand CQRS make better decisions, because CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.

Three situations show why it matters. First, high-read dashboards. Second, complex domains. Third, scaling reads independently. In each case, a solid grasp of commands and queries separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: adding it to simple apps, read models drifting out of sync, and duplicated logic.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Axon Framework, MediatR, and Apache Kafka cover most needs.
