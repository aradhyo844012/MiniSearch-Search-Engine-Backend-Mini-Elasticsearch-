A Practical Checklist for WebSockets

Use this checklist before you rely on WebSockets in a project.

1. Measure results before and after any change.
2. Review the core ideas: persistent connections, messages, heartbeats, and reconnection.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as Socket.IO or ws library.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Write at least one automated test that covers persistent connections.
6. Guard against this risk: unbounded message queues.
7. Guard against this risk: forgetting reconnection logic.
8. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on messages.
9. Review how WebSockets interacts with TCP and UDP.
