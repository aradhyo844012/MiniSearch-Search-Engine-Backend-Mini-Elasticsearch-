Mistakes to Avoid with Recommendation Systems

Everyone learns Recommendation Systems partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: feedback loops. It usually appears when someone skips collaborative filtering and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: popularity bias. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of streaming services. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring new users. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on the cold start problem to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
