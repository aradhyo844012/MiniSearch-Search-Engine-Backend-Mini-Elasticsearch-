Linear Algebra for Programmers in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Linear Algebra for Programmers.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn vectors, matrices, dot products, and eigenvalues.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a set of arrows and stretching machines acting on space. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through 3D graphics, machine learning, and recommendation systems.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for mixing row and column conventions and shape mismatches, then try NumPy for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to check shapes before multiplying.
