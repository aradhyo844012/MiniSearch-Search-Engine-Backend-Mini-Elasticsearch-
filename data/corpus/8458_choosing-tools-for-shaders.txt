Choosing Tools for Shaders

There is no single best tool for Shaders. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GLSL is a common first choice, especially for lighting effects. Shadertoy suits teams that already work with similar technology, and RenderDoc is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Shaders by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle water and fire visuals well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Shaders, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: branches that slow the GPU.

As a rule of thumb, start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time, no matter which tool you use.
