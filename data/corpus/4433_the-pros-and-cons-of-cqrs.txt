The Pros and Cons of CQRS

No technology is free of trade-offs, and CQRS is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports high-read dashboards
- Makes complex domains easier
- Helps with scaling reads independently

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: adding it to simple apps
- Risk: read models drifting out of sync
- Risk: duplicated logic

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as high-read dashboards, and you can manage the risks, CQRS is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
