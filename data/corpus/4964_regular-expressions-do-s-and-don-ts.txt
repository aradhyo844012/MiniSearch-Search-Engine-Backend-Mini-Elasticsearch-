Regular Expressions Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Regular Expressions.

Do:
- Learn character classes before anything else.
- Try the Python re module on a small example.
- Make it a habit to build patterns in small steps and test each one.
- Test the case of validating input early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: catastrophic backtracking.
- Ignore this: patterns that are too greedy.
- Ignore this: using them to parse HTML.
- Adopt Regular Expressions just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Regular Expressions, a little patience with quantifiers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
