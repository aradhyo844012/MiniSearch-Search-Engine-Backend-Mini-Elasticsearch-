Font Rendering Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Font Rendering.

Do:
- Learn glyphs before anything else.
- Try FreeType on a small example.
- Make it a habit to subset fonts to the characters you need.
- Test the case of web pages early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: missing font fallbacks.
- Ignore this: blurry text from scaling.
- Ignore this: large font files.
- Adopt Font Rendering just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Font Rendering, a little patience with hinting pays off more than any clever shortcut.
