Canary Releases Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Canary Releases. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a rollout strategy that sends a small share of traffic to a new version before everyone gets it.
Key terms: traffic percentages, health metrics, automatic rollback, and progressive rollout.
Typical uses: safer releases, testing with real traffic, and reducing the blast radius of bugs.
Risks: too little traffic to reveal problems, no clear success metrics, and manual promotion mistakes.
Tools: Argo Rollouts, Istio, and Flagger.

Mental picture: a canary in a coal mine warning miners before the danger spreads.

Habit to build: define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.
