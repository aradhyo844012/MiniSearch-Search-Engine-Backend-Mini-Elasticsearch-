Managed Kubernetes Services Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Managed Kubernetes Services.

Do:
- Learn the control plane before anything else.
- Try Amazon EKS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to plan regular cluster upgrades.
- Test the case of running containerized apps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: neglecting version upgrades.
- Ignore this: oversized node pools.
- Ignore this: costly idle clusters.
- Adopt Managed Kubernetes Services just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Managed Kubernetes Services, a little patience with node pools pays off more than any clever shortcut.
