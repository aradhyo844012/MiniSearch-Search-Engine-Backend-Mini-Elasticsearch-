Mistakes to Avoid with MQTT

Everyone learns MQTT partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: unsecured brokers. It usually appears when someone skips publish and subscribe and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: messy topic names. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sensor networks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: wrong quality of service choices. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on quality of service levels to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: design topic names like a clear folder structure.
