Why Zero Trust Security Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Zero Trust Security make better decisions, because Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified.

Three situations show why it matters. First, remote work access. Second, protecting internal services. Third, limiting breach damage. In each case, a solid grasp of identity verification and least privilege separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: treating it as a product to buy, poor identity hygiene, and breaking usability.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Okta, Tailscale, and identity-aware proxies cover most needs.
