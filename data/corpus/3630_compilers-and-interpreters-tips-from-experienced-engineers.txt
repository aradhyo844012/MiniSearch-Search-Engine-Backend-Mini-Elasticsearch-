Compilers and Interpreters Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Compilers and Interpreters regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of running Python and JavaScript early, before the design hardens.
2. Be careful with this one: assuming interpreted means slow without measuring.
3. Make sure you can turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously.
4. Write down why you chose Compilers and Interpreters so the next person understands the decision.
5. Do not skip lexing; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to building native programs as well.
