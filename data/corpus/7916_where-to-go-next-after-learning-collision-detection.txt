Where to Go Next After Learning Collision Detection

You have met the basics of Collision Detection. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Physics Engines, which belongs to the same area as Collision Detection (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of bounding boxes, broad and narrow phases, spatial partitioning or collision response, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around shooters with Box2D, and note every problem you meet, starting with tunneling through thin objects.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Collision Detection to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
