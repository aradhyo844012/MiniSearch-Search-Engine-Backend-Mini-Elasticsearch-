Mistakes to Avoid with Cron Jobs and Scheduling

Everyone learns Cron Jobs and Scheduling partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming cron has your shell environment. It usually appears when someone skips crontab syntax and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: overlapping runs of slow jobs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of nightly backups. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no logging of failures. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on output redirection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths.
