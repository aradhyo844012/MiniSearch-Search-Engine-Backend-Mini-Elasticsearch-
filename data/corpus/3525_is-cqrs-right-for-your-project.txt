Is CQRS Right for Your Project?

Before adopting CQRS, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that CQRS is a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.

It is a good fit when your work involves high-read dashboards or complex domains, and when your team understands commands.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn queries, or when you cannot manage these risks: adding it to simple apps, read models drifting out of sync, and duplicated logic.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether read models and eventual consistency are part of the answer. If they are, CQRS is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with scaling reads independently and start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
