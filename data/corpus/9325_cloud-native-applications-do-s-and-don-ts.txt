Cloud-Native Applications Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Cloud-Native Applications.

Do:
- Learn containers before anything else.
- Try Kubernetes on a small example.
- Make it a habit to design every service to be stateless where possible.
- Test the case of web platforms early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating the cloud like a data center.
- Ignore this: too many services.
- Ignore this: ignoring costs.
- Adopt Cloud-Native Applications just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Cloud-Native Applications, a little patience with microservices pays off more than any clever shortcut.
