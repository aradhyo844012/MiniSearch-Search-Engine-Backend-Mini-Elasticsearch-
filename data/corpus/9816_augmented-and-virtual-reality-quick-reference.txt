Augmented and Virtual Reality Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Augmented and Virtual Reality. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one.
Key terms: head tracking, rendering at high frame rates, spatial mapping, and input methods.
Typical uses: training simulations, games, and remote collaboration.
Risks: motion sickness from low frame rates, heavy assets, and poor interface design in 3D.
Tools: Unity XR, WebXR, and ARKit.

Mental picture: wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world.

Habit to build: keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
