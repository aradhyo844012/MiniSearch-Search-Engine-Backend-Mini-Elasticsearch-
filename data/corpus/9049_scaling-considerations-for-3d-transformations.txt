Scaling Considerations for 3D Transformations

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how 3D Transformations changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding translation and getting games right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for wrong multiplication order and gimbal lock, and measure with GLM before changing anything.

Large scale: scaling and matrices move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around modeling tools at this stage.

Before you scale, write down the order of transformations before multiplying. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
