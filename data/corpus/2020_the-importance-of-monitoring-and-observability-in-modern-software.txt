The Importance of Monitoring and Observability in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Monitoring and Observability until something goes wrong without it. The term Monitoring and Observability refers to collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Cloud resources are easy to create and just as easy to forget, which makes costs grow quietly.

People use Monitoring and Observability for detecting outages, for finding slow requests, and for capacity planning.

Three issues come up again and again: noisy alerts; logs without context; and dashboards nobody reads. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

Monitoring and Observability and Kubernetes are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Cloud and DevOps.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Monitoring and Observability.
