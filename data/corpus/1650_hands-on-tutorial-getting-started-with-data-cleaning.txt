Hands-On Tutorial: Getting Started with Data Cleaning

This hands-on tutorial takes you from zero to a small working experiment with Data Cleaning. The term Data Cleaning refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Step 1: Install or open pandas and create a new empty project folder.
Step 2: Read about missing values and write down a definition in your own words.
Step 3: Build the smallest possible example that uses duplicates.
Step 4: Add one test or print statement that proves it works.
Step 5: Extend the example to handle a case such as preparing logs.
Step 6: Break your example on purpose and see what happens. A good experiment: dropping data without checking why it is missing.

Read the official documentation for OpenRefine; it often explains duplicates better than blog posts do. Measure before you optimize. Use pandas or a simple timer to see where time is actually spent. Keep notes on what worked. A short README explaining your decisions about Data Cleaning helps the next developer.

In practice you can start with pandas, then explore OpenRefine or regular expressions as your needs grow.

Watch out for these problems: dropping data without checking why it is missing; cleaning by hand without a record; and changing the original files.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Data Cleaning whenever a new project gives you a reason.
