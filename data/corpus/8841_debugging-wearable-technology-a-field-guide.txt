Debugging Wearable Technology: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Wearable Technology, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: battery drain, inaccurate sensor readings, and privacy of personal data.
Step 3: revisit sensors and low-power design, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Wear OS or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Wearable Technology in work such as fitness tracking come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, design interactions that take only a few seconds. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
