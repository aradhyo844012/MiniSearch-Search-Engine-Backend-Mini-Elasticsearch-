Where to Go Next After Learning Physics Engines

You have met the basics of Physics Engines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Collision Detection, which belongs to the same area as Physics Engines (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of rigid bodies, forces and impulses, constraints or fixed time steps, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around puzzle games with Box2D, and note every problem you meet, starting with variable time steps.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Physics Engines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
