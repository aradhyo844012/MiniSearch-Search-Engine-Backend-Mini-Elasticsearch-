Number Theory and RSA for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Number Theory and RSA.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Number Theory and RSA in your own words.
2. Describe prime numbers, modular arithmetic, greatest common divisor, and modular exponentiation.
3. Give examples from public key cryptography and hash functions.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption. Then draw a clock whose numbers wrap around on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is using small primes a problem? How does modular arithmetic relate to greatest common divisor? When would you choose Python pow or OpenSSL?

Study habit: use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.

When you feel ready, compare Number Theory and RSA with Floating-Point Arithmetic and write down three differences.
