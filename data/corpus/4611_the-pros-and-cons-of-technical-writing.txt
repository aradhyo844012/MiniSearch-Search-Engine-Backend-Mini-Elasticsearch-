The Pros and Cons of Technical Writing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Technical Writing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports documentation
- Makes blog posts easier
- Helps with design proposals

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming too much knowledge
- Risk: burying the main point
- Risk: long unbroken paragraphs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as documentation, and you can manage the risks, Technical Writing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, state the main point in the first two sentences.
