Digital Twins Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Digital Twins.

Do:
- Learn sensor data before anything else.
- Try Azure Digital Twins on a small example.
- Make it a habit to define the question the twin must answer first.
- Test the case of factory monitoring early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: stale data.
- Ignore this: over-detailed models.
- Ignore this: unclear goals.
- Adopt Digital Twins just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Digital Twins, a little patience with simulation models pays off more than any clever shortcut.
