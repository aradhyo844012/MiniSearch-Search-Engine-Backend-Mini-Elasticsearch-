Push Notifications for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Push Notifications.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Push Notifications in your own words.
2. Describe device tokens, push services, payloads, and permissions.
3. Give examples from order updates and chat alerts.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed. Then draw a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is asking for permission too early a problem? How does push services relate to payloads? When would you choose Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service?

Study habit: ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value.

When you feel ready, compare Push Notifications with Arduino Basics and write down three differences.
