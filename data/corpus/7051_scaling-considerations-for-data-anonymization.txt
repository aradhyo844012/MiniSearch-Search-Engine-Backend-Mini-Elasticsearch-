Scaling Considerations for Data Anonymization

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Data Anonymization changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding masking and getting sharing datasets with researchers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for re-identification through combined fields and removing too little, and measure with ARX before changing anything.

Large scale: k-anonymity and differential privacy move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around testing with production-like data at this stage.

Before you scale, test whether combining fields could identify a person. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
