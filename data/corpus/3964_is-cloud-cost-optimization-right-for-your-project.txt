Is Cloud Cost Optimization Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Cloud Cost Optimization, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.

It is a good fit when your work involves cutting idle resources or budgeting by team, and when your team understands right-sizing.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn reserved capacity, or when you cannot manage these risks: forgotten test environments, over-provisioned databases, and surprise data transfer charges.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether tagging and usage reports are part of the answer. If they are, Cloud Cost Optimization is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with choosing storage tiers and tag every resource with an owner.
