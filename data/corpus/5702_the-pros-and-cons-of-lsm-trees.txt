The Pros and Cons of LSM Trees

No technology is free of trade-offs, and LSM Trees is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports write-heavy databases
- Makes time-ordered data easier
- Helps with key-value stores

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: read amplification
- Risk: compaction stalls
- Risk: poor bloom filter tuning

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as write-heavy databases, and you can manage the risks, LSM Trees is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, watch compaction metrics before blaming the application.
