A Practical Checklist for Kubernetes

Use this checklist before you rely on Kubernetes in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: over-complicated setups.
2. Review the core ideas: pods, deployments, services, and ingress.
3. Measure results before and after any change.
4. Guard against this risk: ignoring health probes.
5. Guard against this risk: resource limits missing.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on deployments.
7. Try the approach on a small case, for example running many containers.
8. Write at least one automated test that covers pods.
