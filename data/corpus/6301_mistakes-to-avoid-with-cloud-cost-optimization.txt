Mistakes to Avoid with Cloud Cost Optimization

Everyone learns Cloud Cost Optimization partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: forgotten test environments. It usually appears when someone skips right-sizing and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: over-provisioned databases. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of cutting idle resources. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: surprise data transfer charges. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on usage reports to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: tag every resource with an owner.
