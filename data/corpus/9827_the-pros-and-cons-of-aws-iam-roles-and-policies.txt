The Pros and Cons of AWS IAM Roles and Policies

No technology is free of trade-offs, and AWS IAM Roles and Policies is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports securing deployments
- Makes granting services access easier
- Helps with auditing permissions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: wildcard permissions
- Risk: long-lived access keys
- Risk: unused roles

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as securing deployments, and you can manage the risks, AWS IAM Roles and Policies is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, grant the narrowest policy that works and review it regularly.
