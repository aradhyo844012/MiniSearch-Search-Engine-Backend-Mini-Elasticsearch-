Quantum Computing Basics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Quantum Computing Basics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Quantum Computing Basics: computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems.
qubits: a core term, most visible in research on cryptography.
superposition: a core term that beginners often confuse with entanglement.
entanglement: a core term that connects to quantum gates.
quantum gates: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Qiskit: a tool commonly used for simulating molecules.

Come back to this Quantum Computing Basics list whenever documentation for Cirq uses a word you do not recognize.
