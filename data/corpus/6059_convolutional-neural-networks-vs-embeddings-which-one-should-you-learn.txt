Convolutional Neural Networks vs Embeddings: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Convolutional Neural Networks or Embeddings first. Both sit in the area of AI and Machine Learning, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns, and it is closely tied to filters and pooling. Embeddings, by contrast, is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together, and it is closely tied to vectors and cosine similarity.

Choose Convolutional Neural Networks first if you care about image classification or medical image analysis. Choose Embeddings first if you care about semantic search or recommendations.

Think of Convolutional Neural Networks as a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes, and of Embeddings as coordinates on a map where similar places are close together. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Convolutional Neural Networks, avoid too little training data. With Embeddings, avoid mixing models between indexing and querying.

A good habit for Convolutional Neural Networks is to start from a pretrained model. A good habit for Embeddings is to use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
