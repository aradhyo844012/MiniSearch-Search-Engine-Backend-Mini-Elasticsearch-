Where to Go Next After Learning Cron Jobs and Scheduling

You have met the basics of Cron Jobs and Scheduling. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Disk Partitioning and Mounting, which belongs to the same area as Cron Jobs and Scheduling (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of crontab syntax, schedule fields, environment differences or output redirection, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around log rotation with crontab, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming cron has your shell environment.
