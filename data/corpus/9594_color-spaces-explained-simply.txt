Color Spaces Explained Simply

Here is Color Spaces without the jargon: it is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.

Think of Color Spaces as different ways of giving directions to the same destination. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: RGB, HSL, CMYK, and gamma. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with RGB, then add HSL.

People reach for Color Spaces when they work on web design, photo editing, and printing.

One practical tip is to work in one color space and convert only at the end.

After Color Spaces, a natural next topic is Vector Graphics (SVG).
