Responsible AI and Bias Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Responsible AI and Bias regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip bias in data; everything else builds on it.
2. Be careful with this one: assuming data is neutral.
3. Make sure you can test performance separately for different groups.
4. Write down why you chose Responsible AI and Bias so the next person understands the decision.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit fairness metrics before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to lending models as well as to Responsible AI and Bias in general.
