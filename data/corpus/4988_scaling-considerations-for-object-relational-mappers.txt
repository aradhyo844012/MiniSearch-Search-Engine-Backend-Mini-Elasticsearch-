Scaling Considerations for Object-Relational Mappers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Object-Relational Mappers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding models and getting building CRUD apps quickly right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for N+1 queries and loading entire tables, and measure with SQLAlchemy before changing anything.

Large scale: relationships and migrations move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around switching databases at this stage.

Before you scale, log the generated SQL during development. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
