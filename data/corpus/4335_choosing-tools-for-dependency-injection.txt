Choosing Tools for Dependency Injection

There is no single best tool for Dependency Injection. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Spring is a common first choice, especially for easier unit testing. FastAPI Depends suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Dagger is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle swapping implementations well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: hiding too much behind containers.

As a rule of thumb, inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes, no matter which tool you use.
