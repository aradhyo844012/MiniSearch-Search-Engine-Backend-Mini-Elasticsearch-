Mistakes to Avoid with Biometric Authentication

Everyone learns Biometric Authentication partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: spoofing attacks. It usually appears when someone skips enrollment and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: biased recognition. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of phone unlock. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: credentials that cannot be changed once leaked. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on liveness detection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.
