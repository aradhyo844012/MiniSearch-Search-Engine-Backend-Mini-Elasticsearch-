Why Domain-Driven Design Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Domain-Driven Design make better decisions, because Domain-Driven Design is an approach to software that models the code around the real business domain.

Three situations show why it matters. First, complex business systems. Second, aligning developers with domain experts. Third, splitting large systems. In each case, a solid grasp of ubiquitous language and bounded contexts separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: using it for simple CRUD apps, models that never match the business, and ignoring context boundaries.

You will not need to build everything yourself; event storming, UML, and Miro cover most needs.

Connect Domain-Driven Design with SOLID Principles to see the bigger picture of Software Design.
