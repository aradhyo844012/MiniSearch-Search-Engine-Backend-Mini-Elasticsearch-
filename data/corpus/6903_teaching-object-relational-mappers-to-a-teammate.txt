Teaching Object-Relational Mappers to a Teammate

Explaining Object-Relational Mappers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a translator between the language of tables and the language of objects. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving building CRUD apps quickly, naming models and queries as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain relationships in Object-Relational Mappers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, N+1 queries, and suggest this habit: log the generated SQL during development.
