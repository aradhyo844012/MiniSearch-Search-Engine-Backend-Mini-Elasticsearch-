Virtual Private Clouds Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Virtual Private Clouds.

Do:
- Learn subnets before anything else.
- Try AWS VPC on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep databases in private subnets.
- Test the case of separating public and private services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overlapping address ranges.
- Ignore this: databases in public subnets.
- Ignore this: missing routes.
- Adopt Virtual Private Clouds just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Virtual Private Clouds, a little patience with route tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
