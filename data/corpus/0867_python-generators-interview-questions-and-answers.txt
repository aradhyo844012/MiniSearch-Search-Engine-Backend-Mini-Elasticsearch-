Python Generators Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Python Generators.

Q: Explain Python Generators in simple words.
A: Python Generators refers to functions that produce values one at a time instead of building a whole list. I would add an example involving reading large files line by line.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: consuming a generator twice; forgetting generators are lazy; and hiding errors until iteration.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: yield, lazy evaluation, iterators, and generator expressions.

Q: How does it connect to Functional Programming?
A: Both belong to the area of Programming, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: itertools, Python itertools docs, and pytest.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with streaming data, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
