Digital Audio Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Digital Audio regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Digital Audio so the next person understands the decision.
2. Make sure you can keep recordings below the clipping level.
3. Do not skip sampling rate; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of music production early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for Audacity first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as SoX.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to voice calls as well as to Digital Audio in general.
