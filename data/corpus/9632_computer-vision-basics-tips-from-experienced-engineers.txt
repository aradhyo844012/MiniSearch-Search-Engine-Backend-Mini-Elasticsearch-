Computer Vision Basics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Computer Vision Basics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Computer Vision Basics so the next person understands the decision.
2. Test the case of face detection early, before the design hardens.
3. Do not skip pixels; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for OpenCV first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Pillow.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit filters and edges before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to document scanning as well as to Computer Vision Basics in general.
