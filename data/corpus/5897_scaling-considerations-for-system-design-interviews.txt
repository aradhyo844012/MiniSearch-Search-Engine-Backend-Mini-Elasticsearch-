Scaling Considerations for System Design Interviews

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how System Design Interviews changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding requirements and getting preparing for senior roles right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements and ignoring trade-offs, and measure with whiteboard sketches before changing anything.

Large scale: trade-offs and components move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around practicing architectural thinking at this stage.

Before you scale, clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
