Skills to Build Around A/B Testing

Knowing the definition of A/B Testing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with control and variant and random assignment, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Optimizely on a A/B Testing task, and try at least one alternative such as GrowthBook.

Judgment: learn to decide when A/B Testing fits a problem such as testing page designs, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests.

Habits: decide the success metric and sample size before you begin, and write down what you learn after each project.
