Practice Exercises for Collision Detection

These exercises build real understanding of Collision Detection. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble platformers or shooters.
Exercise 2: Compare Unity colliders and quadtrees for physics puzzles and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how spatial partitioning and collision response differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Collision Detection and include the term bounding boxes.
Exercise 5: Pick Box2D and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Collision Detection is the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap.
