Debugging Discrete Mathematics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Discrete Mathematics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: skipping proofs, confusing sets with sequences, and careless notation.
Step 3: revisit sets and logic, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python sets or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Discrete Mathematics in work such as algorithm analysis come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, practice writing small proofs by hand. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
