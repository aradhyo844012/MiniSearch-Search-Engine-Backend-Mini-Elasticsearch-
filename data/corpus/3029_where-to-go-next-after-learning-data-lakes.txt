Where to Go Next After Learning Data Lakes

You have met the basics of Data Lakes. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Apache Spark, which belongs to the same area as Data Lakes (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of raw storage, schema on read, partitioning or metadata catalogs, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around machine learning datasets with Amazon S3, and note every problem you meet, starting with turning into a data swamp.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Data Lakes to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
