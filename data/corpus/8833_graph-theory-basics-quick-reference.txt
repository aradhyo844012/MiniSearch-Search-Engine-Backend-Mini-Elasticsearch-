Graph Theory Basics Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Graph Theory Basics. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the study of nodes and the connections between them.
Key terms: vertices and edges, degree, paths and cycles, and connectivity.
Typical uses: social networks, maps, and dependency analysis.
Risks: mixing directed and undirected graphs, ignoring disconnected parts, and choosing the wrong representation for dense graphs.
Tools: NetworkX, Graphviz, and Gephi.

Mental picture: a map of towns and the roads between them.

Habit to build: draw a small example before writing code.

See also: Discrete Mathematics, which sits close to Graph Theory Basics in the area of Mathematics for Computing.
