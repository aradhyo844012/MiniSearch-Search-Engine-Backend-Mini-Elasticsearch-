The Pros and Cons of Code Editors and IDEs

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Code Editors and IDEs is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports daily development
- Makes navigating large codebases easier
- Helps with refactoring

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: installing too many extensions
- Risk: ignoring keyboard shortcuts
- Risk: relying on autocomplete blindly

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as daily development, and you can manage the risks, Code Editors and IDEs is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, learn the shortcuts for the actions you repeat most.
