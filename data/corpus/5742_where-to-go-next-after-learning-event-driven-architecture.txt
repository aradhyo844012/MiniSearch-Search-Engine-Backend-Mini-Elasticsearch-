Where to Go Next After Learning Event-Driven Architecture

You have met the basics of Event-Driven Architecture. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Pair Programming, which belongs to the same area as Event-Driven Architecture (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of producers and consumers, event brokers, loose coupling or asynchronous handling, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around real-time notifications with Apache Kafka, and note every problem you meet, starting with hard-to-trace flows.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Event-Driven Architecture to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
