Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with A* Pathfinding

This path is for a learner who has never studied A* Pathfinding before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (A* Pathfinding is a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining) and learn open and closed sets and cost so far.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Unity NavMesh, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving game characters.

Stage 3, depth: study heuristics and grids and graphs, then deliberately explore this problem: overestimating the heuristic.

Stage 4, application: apply A* Pathfinding to robot navigation, compare Python heapq with Recast Navigation, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach A* Pathfinding to someone else, and then move on to Physics Engines.
