Debugging Strategies Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Debugging Strategies. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: systematic ways of finding and fixing the cause of unexpected program behavior.
Key terms: reproducing the bug, isolating the cause, forming hypotheses, and binary search through code.
Typical uses: fixing production issues, learning unfamiliar code, and reducing repeat bugs.
Risks: changing many things at once, guessing without evidence, and not writing a test afterward.
Tools: debuggers, logging, and git bisect.

Mental picture: a detective narrowing down suspects.

Habit to build: change one thing at a time and record the result.
