Floating-Point Arithmetic Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Floating-Point Arithmetic feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Floating-Point Arithmetic: the way computers approximate real numbers with a fixed number of bits.
mantissa and exponent: a core term, most visible in scientific computing.
rounding: a core term that beginners often confuse with precision.
precision: a core term that connects to special values.
special values: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python decimal: a tool commonly used for graphics.

Come back to this Floating-Point Arithmetic list whenever documentation for NumPy uses a word you do not recognize.
