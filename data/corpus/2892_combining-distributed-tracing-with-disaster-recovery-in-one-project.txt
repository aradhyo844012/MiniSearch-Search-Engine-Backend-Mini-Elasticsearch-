Combining Distributed Tracing with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Distributed Tracing and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves finding slow services and surviving regional outages. Distributed Tracing brings spans, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with OpenTelemetry for the Distributed Tracing part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: sampling too aggressively on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
