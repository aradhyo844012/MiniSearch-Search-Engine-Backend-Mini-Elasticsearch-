Teaching Blockchain Fundamentals to a Teammate

Explaining Blockchain Fundamentals to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving digital currencies, naming blocks and hashes as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain consensus in Blockchain Fundamentals back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, treating it as a database replacement, and suggest this habit: ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
