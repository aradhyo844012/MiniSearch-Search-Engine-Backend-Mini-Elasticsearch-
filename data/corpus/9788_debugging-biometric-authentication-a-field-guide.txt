Debugging Biometric Authentication: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Biometric Authentication, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: spoofing attacks, biased recognition, and credentials that cannot be changed once leaked.
Step 3: revisit enrollment and matching, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Android BiometricPrompt or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Biometric Authentication in work such as phone unlock come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
