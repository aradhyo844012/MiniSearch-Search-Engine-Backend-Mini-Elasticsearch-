Where to Go Next After Learning C Pointers

You have met the basics of C Pointers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Regular Expressions, which belongs to the same area as C Pointers (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of addresses, dereferencing, pointer arithmetic or null pointers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around passing large data efficiently with GCC, and note every problem you meet, starting with dereferencing null pointers.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining C Pointers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
