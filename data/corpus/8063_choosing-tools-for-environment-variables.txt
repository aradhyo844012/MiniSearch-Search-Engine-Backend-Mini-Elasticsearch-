Choosing Tools for Environment Variables

There is no single best tool for Environment Variables. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

env is a common first choice, especially for storing configuration. printenv suits teams that already work with similar technology, and dotenv files is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Environment Variables by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle selecting program paths well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Environment Variables, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: committing secrets in dotfiles.

As a rule of thumb, print a variable to confirm its value before blaming the code, no matter which tool you use.
