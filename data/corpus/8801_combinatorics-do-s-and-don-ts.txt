Combinatorics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Combinatorics.

Do:
- Learn permutations before anything else.
- Try Python itertools on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
- Test the case of analyzing algorithms early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: double counting.
- Ignore this: mixing order and no-order cases.
- Ignore this: ignoring repetition.
- Adopt Combinatorics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Combinatorics, a little patience with combinations pays off more than any clever shortcut.
