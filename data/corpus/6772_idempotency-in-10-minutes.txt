Idempotency in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Idempotency.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn idempotency keys, safe retries, deduplication, and at-least-once delivery.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine pressing an elevator call button many times and still getting one elevator. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through payment APIs, message processing, and retrying failed requests.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for keys that expire too soon and side effects outside the guarded step, then try Stripe idempotency keys for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to store the result with the key so retries return the same response.
