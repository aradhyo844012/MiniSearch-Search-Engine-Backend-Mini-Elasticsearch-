Scaling Considerations for Push Notifications

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Push Notifications changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding device tokens and getting order updates right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for asking for permission too early and sending too many messages, and measure with Firebase Cloud Messaging before changing anything.

Large scale: payloads and permissions move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around chat alerts at this stage.

Before you scale, ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
