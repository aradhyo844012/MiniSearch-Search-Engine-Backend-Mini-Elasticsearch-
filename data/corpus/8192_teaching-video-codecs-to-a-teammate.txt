Teaching Video Codecs to a Teammate

Explaining Video Codecs to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving streaming services, naming frames and motion compensation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain bitrate in Video Codecs back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, mismatched codec support, and suggest this habit: test playback on the weakest target device.
