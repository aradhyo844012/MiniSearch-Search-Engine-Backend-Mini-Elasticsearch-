A Practical Checklist for Large Language Models

Use this checklist before you rely on Large Language Models in a project.

1. Try the approach on a small case, for example drafting text.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers tokens.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on attention.
6. Review how Large Language Models interacts with Decision Trees.
7. Pick a tool to start with, such as Hugging Face or API playgrounds.
8. Guard against this risk: vague prompts.
9. Review the core ideas: tokens, attention, pretraining, and prompts.
