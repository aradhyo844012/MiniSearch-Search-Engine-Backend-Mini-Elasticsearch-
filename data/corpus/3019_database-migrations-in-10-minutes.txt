Database Migrations in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Database Migrations.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Database Migrations is versioned scripts that change a database schema in a controlled and repeatable way.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn up and down scripts, version tables, backward compatibility, and zero-downtime changes.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a numbered set of renovation steps applied to a building in order. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through adding columns safely, renaming tables, and keeping environments in sync.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for locking large tables and editing old migrations, then try Alembic for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
