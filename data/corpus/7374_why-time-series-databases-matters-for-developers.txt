Why Time-Series Databases Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Time-Series Databases make better decisions, because Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.

Three situations show why it matters. First, server metrics. Second, sensor readings. Third, financial tick data. In each case, a solid grasp of timestamps and retention policies separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: high-cardinality tags, keeping raw data forever, and querying without time bounds.

Good tools exist, including InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, and Prometheus, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
