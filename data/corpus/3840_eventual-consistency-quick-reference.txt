Eventual Consistency Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Eventual Consistency. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a guarantee that replicas will agree on the same value if updates stop for long enough.
Key terms: replication delay, conflict resolution, read-your-writes, and convergence.
Typical uses: global social feeds, shopping cart services, and DNS records.
Risks: assuming reads are always fresh, unresolved write conflicts, and no user feedback during delays.
Tools: Amazon DynamoDB, Apache Cassandra, and CRDT libraries.

Mental picture: news spreading through a village until everyone has heard it.

Habit to build: decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
