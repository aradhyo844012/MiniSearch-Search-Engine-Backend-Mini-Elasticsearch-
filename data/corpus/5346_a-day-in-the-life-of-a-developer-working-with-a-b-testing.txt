A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with A/B Testing

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on A/B Testing.

Morning: Meera starts by reviewing a ticket about testing page designs. Before touching any code, Meera re-reads the notes on control and variant to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Meera pairs with a teammate to explore random assignment with Optimizely. A small test exposes the problem of stopping tests early, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving comparing ranking algorithms. Meera sketches taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Meera writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.

Days like this are typical. A/B Testing is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
