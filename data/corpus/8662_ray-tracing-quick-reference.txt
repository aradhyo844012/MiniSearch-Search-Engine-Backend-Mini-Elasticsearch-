Ray Tracing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Ray Tracing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images.
Key terms: rays, intersections, reflection and refraction, and shadows.
Typical uses: movie visuals, architectural visualization, and realistic game lighting.
Risks: slow render times, noise from too few samples, and numerical precision errors.
Tools: Blender Cycles, NVIDIA OptiX, and POV-Ray.

Mental picture: tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene.

Habit to build: start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
