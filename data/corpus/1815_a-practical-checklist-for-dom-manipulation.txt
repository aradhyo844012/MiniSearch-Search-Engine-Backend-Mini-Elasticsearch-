A Practical Checklist for DOM Manipulation

Use this checklist before you rely on DOM Manipulation in a project.

1. Review the core ideas: selectors, events, nodes, and updates.
2. Pick a tool to start with, such as querySelector or browser console.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what DOM Manipulation should accomplish.
4. Guard against this risk: querying the DOM inside tight loops.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example updating search results.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Guard against this risk: inserting untrusted text as HTML.
8. Write at least one automated test that covers selectors.
