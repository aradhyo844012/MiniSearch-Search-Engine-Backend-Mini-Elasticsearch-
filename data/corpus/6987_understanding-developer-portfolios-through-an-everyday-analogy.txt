Understanding Developer Portfolios Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a shop window displaying your best work.

In that picture, projects is the first rule you follow, README quality is the next step, and live demos and clear descriptions keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build, and it involves details such as too many unfinished projects that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for GitHub Pages to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain applying for internships to a friend.
