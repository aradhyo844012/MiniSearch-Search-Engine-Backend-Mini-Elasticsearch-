Practice Exercises for Embeddings

These exercises build real understanding of Embeddings. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick sentence-transformers and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Explain how dimensionality and nearest neighbor search differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: mixing models between indexing and querying. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble semantic search or recommendations.
Exercise 5: Compare FAISS and Word2Vec for clustering text and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.
