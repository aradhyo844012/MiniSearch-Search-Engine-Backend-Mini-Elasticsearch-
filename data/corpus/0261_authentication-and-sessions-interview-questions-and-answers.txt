Authentication and Sessions Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Authentication and Sessions.

Q: Explain Authentication and Sessions in simple words.
A: Authentication and Sessions refers to the process of proving who a user is and remembering them between requests. I would add an example involving logging in to web apps.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Flask-Login, Django auth, and Passport.js.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with protecting admin pages, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: credentials, session cookies, tokens, and expiration.

Q: How does it connect to Microservices?
A: Both belong to the area of Backend and APIs, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
