Skills to Build Around Debugging Strategies

Knowing the definition of Debugging Strategies is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with reproducing the bug and isolating the cause, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with debuggers on a Debugging Strategies task, and try at least one alternative such as logging.

Judgment: learn to decide when Debugging Strategies fits a problem such as fixing production issues, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a detective narrowing down suspects.

Habits: change one thing at a time and record the result, and write down what you learn after each project.
