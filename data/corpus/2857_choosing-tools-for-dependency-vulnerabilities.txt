Choosing Tools for Dependency Vulnerabilities

There is no single best tool for Dependency Vulnerabilities. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Dependabot is a common first choice, especially for auditing projects. pip-audit suits teams that already work with similar technology, and npm audit is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle automating updates well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: ignoring transitive dependencies.

As a rule of thumb, automate update pull requests, no matter which tool you use.
