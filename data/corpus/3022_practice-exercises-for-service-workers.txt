Practice Exercises for Service Workers

These exercises build real understanding of Service Workers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Workbox and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Compare the Chrome DevTools Application panel and Lighthouse for push notifications and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how cache storage and fetch handlers differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble offline support or faster repeat visits.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Service Workers and include the term registration.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests.
