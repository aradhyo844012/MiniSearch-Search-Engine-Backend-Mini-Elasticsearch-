Autoscaling in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Autoscaling.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn scaling metrics, cooldown periods, minimum and maximum limits, and horizontal scaling.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through handling traffic spikes, saving money overnight, and keeping latency steady.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for scaling too slowly and flapping because of short spikes, then try Kubernetes HPA for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
