Mistakes to Avoid with Multi-Cloud Strategy

Everyone learns Multi-Cloud Strategy partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: doubling operational complexity. It usually appears when someone skips portability and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: lowest-common-denominator designs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of avoiding single-provider risk. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hidden data transfer costs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on cost comparison to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
