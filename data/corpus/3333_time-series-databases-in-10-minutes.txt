Time-Series Databases in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Time-Series Databases.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn timestamps, retention policies, downsampling, and tags and fields.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a long logbook where every line starts with the time. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through server metrics, sensor readings, and financial tick data.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for high-cardinality tags and keeping raw data forever, then try InfluxDB for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to include a time range in every query.
