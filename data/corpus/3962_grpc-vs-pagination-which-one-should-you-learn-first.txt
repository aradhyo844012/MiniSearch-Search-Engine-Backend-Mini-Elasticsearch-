gRPC vs Pagination: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study gRPC or Pagination first. Both sit in the area of Backend Engineering, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

gRPC is a framework for fast remote procedure calls using typed contracts, and it is closely tied to protocol buffers and service definitions. Pagination, by contrast, is splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time, and it is closely tied to offset pagination and cursor pagination.

Choose gRPC first if you care about service-to-service calls or low-latency APIs. Choose Pagination first if you care about search results or activity feeds.

Think of gRPC as a phone line with a pre-agreed script for every call, and of Pagination as reading a long book one chapter at a time with a bookmark. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With gRPC, avoid breaking changes in message fields. With Pagination, avoid offsets getting slow on huge tables.

A good habit for gRPC is to never reuse or renumber field numbers in a proto file. A good habit for Pagination is to prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data.
