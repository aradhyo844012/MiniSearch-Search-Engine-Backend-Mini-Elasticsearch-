Graph Theory Basics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Graph Theory Basics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn vertices and edges, degree, paths and cycles, and connectivity.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a map of towns and the roads between them. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through social networks, maps, and dependency analysis.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for mixing directed and undirected graphs and ignoring disconnected parts, then try NetworkX for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to draw a small example before writing code.
