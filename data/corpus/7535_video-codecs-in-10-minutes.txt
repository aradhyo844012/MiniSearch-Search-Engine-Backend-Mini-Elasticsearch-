Video Codecs in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Video Codecs.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn frames, motion compensation, bitrate, and containers.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through streaming services, video calls, and archiving footage.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for mismatched codec support and too low a bitrate, then try FFmpeg for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test playback on the weakest target device.
