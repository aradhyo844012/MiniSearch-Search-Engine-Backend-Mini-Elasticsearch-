Canary Releases for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Canary Releases.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Canary Releases in your own words.
2. Describe traffic percentages, health metrics, automatic rollback, and progressive rollout.
3. Give examples from safer releases and testing with real traffic.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Canary Releases is a rollout strategy that sends a small share of traffic to a new version before everyone gets it. Then draw a canary in a coal mine warning miners before the danger spreads on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too little traffic to reveal problems a problem? How does health metrics relate to automatic rollback? When would you choose Argo Rollouts or Istio?

Study habit worth keeping: define rollback conditions before starting the rollout.

When you feel ready, compare Canary Releases with Cloud Cost Optimization and write down three differences.
