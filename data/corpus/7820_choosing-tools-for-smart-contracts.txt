Choosing Tools for Smart Contracts

There is no single best tool for Smart Contracts. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Solidity is a common first choice, especially for token systems. Hardhat suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Remix is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Smart Contracts by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle automated escrow well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Smart Contracts, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: bugs that cannot be patched.

As a rule of thumb, audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment, no matter which tool you use.
