Is 3D Transformations Right for Your Project?

Before adopting 3D Transformations, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.

It is a good fit when your work involves games or modeling tools, and when your team understands translation.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn rotation, or when you cannot manage these risks: wrong multiplication order, gimbal lock, and mixing coordinate systems.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether scaling and matrices are part of the answer. If they are, 3D Transformations is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with robotics and write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
