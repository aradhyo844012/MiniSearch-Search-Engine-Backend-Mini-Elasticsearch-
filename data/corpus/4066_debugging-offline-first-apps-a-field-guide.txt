Debugging Offline-First Apps: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Offline-First Apps, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: unresolved sync conflicts, unbounded local data, and poor feedback about pending changes.
Step 3: revisit local storage and sync queues, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use SQLite or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Offline-First Apps in work such as field service tools come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, show users which changes have not synced yet. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
