Troubleshooting Overfitting Problems

When something breaks around Overfitting, a calm and systematic approach saves the most time. Put simply, Overfitting covers when a model learns the training data too closely and performs poorly on new data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Start by reproducing the problem with the smallest possible example. Then change one thing at a time and note what happens. Logs, tests and scikit-learn usually reveal the cause faster than guessing.

Common culprits: evaluating only on training data; leaking test data; adding complexity without evidence. If none of these explains the symptom, re-read the basics of training and validation error and model complexity.

In practice you can start with scikit-learn, then explore early stopping or learning curves as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of Overfitting pays off. Spend time on training and validation error and model complexity, and the rest will follow.
