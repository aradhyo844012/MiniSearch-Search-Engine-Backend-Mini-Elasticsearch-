Raft Consensus for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Raft Consensus.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Raft Consensus in your own words.
2. Describe leader election, log replication, terms, and majority quorum.
3. Give examples from configuration stores and distributed locks.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Raft Consensus is an algorithm that lets a group of servers agree on a shared log of operations. Then draw a committee that elects a chair and only records decisions most members confirm on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is even-sized clusters a problem? How does log replication relate to terms? When would you choose etcd or Consul?

Study habit worth keeping: use an odd number of nodes.

When you feel ready, compare Raft Consensus with Saga Pattern and write down three differences.
