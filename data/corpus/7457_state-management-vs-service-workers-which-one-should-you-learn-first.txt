State Management vs Service Workers: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study State Management or Service Workers first. Both sit in the area of Frontend Engineering, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data, and it is closely tied to single source of truth and actions. Service Workers, by contrast, is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests, and it is closely tied to registration and install and activate events.

Choose State Management first if you care about large single-page apps or shared shopping carts. Choose Service Workers first if you care about offline support or faster repeat visits.

Think of State Management as a shared whiteboard that everyone updates through the same rules, and of Service Workers as a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With State Management, avoid putting everything in global state. With Service Workers, avoid caching stale files forever.

A good habit for State Management is to keep state as close as possible to where it is used. A good habit for Service Workers is to version your caches so old files can be removed.
