Choosing Tools for Data Augmentation

There is no single best tool for Data Augmentation. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Albumentations is a common first choice, especially for image models. torchvision suits teams that already work with similar technology, and nlpaug is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle text classification well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: changes that alter the label.

As a rule of thumb, augment only the training data, no matter which tool you use.
