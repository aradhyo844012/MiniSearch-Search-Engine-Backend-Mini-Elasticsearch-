systemd Services Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for systemd Services. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the init system and service manager used by most modern Linux distributions.
Key terms: unit files, targets, service states, and journal logs.
Typical uses: starting services at boot, restarting crashed programs, and managing dependencies between services.
Risks: editing unit files without reloading the daemon, missing restart policies, and ignoring dependency ordering.
Tools: systemctl, journalctl, and systemd-analyze.

Mental picture: a building manager who opens each office in the right order every morning.

Habit to build: run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change.
