Mistakes to Avoid with Elasticsearch Concepts

Everyone learns Elasticsearch Concepts partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too many shards. It usually appears when someone skips indexes and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: mapping explosions. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of site search. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: treating it as a primary database. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on queries to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
