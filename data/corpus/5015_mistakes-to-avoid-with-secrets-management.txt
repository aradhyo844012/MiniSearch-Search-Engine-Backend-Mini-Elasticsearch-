Mistakes to Avoid with Secrets Management

Everyone learns Secrets Management partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: secrets in source control. It usually appears when someone skips vaults and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: long-lived shared credentials. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of database credentials. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no rotation plan. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on audit logs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
