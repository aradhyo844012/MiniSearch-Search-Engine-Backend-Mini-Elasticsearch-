Where to Go Next After Learning Flutter

You have met the basics of Flutter. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Native, which belongs to the same area as Flutter (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of widgets, state, hot reload or the render tree, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around desktop apps with Dart, and note every problem you meet, starting with deep widget trees.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Flutter to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
