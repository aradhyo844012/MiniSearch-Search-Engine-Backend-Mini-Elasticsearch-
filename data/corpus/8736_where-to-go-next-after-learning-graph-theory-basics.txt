Where to Go Next After Learning Graph Theory Basics

You have met the basics of Graph Theory Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Probability Basics, which belongs to the same area as Graph Theory Basics (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of vertices and edges, degree, paths and cycles or connectivity, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around maps with NetworkX, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing directed and undirected graphs.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Graph Theory Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
