Where to Go Next After Learning Digital Twins

You have met the basics of Digital Twins. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Biometric Authentication, which belongs to the same area as Digital Twins (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sensor data, simulation models, synchronization or analytics, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around building management with Azure Digital Twins, and note every problem you meet, starting with stale data.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Digital Twins to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
