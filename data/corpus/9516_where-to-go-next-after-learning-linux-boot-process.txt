Where to Go Next After Learning Linux Boot Process

You have met the basics of Linux Boot Process. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Process Signals and Job Control, which belongs to the same area as Linux Boot Process (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of firmware, bootloader, kernel initialization or the init system, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around configuring dual boot with GRUB, and note every problem you meet, starting with overwriting the bootloader.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Linux Boot Process to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
