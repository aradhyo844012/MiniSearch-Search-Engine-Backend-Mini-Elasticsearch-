Teaching Finite Automata to a Teammate

Explaining Finite Automata to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a turnstile that moves between locked and unlocked. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Finite Automata is simple abstract machines that read input and move between a fixed set of states. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving tokenizers, naming states and transitions as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain accepting states in Finite Automata back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, missing transitions, and suggest this habit: draw the state diagram before writing any code.
