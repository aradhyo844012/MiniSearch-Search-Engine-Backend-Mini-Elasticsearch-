The Pros and Cons of Game Engines

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Game Engines is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports indie games
- Makes simulations easier
- Helps with interactive demos

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: choosing an engine by hype
- Risk: loading everything at start-up
- Risk: ignoring platform limits

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as indie games, and you can manage the risks, Game Engines is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
