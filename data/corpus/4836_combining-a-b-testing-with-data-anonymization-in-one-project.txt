Combining A/B Testing with Data Anonymization in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how A/B Testing and Data Anonymization can work together in a single project.

A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better. Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.

Imagine a project that involves testing page designs and sharing datasets with researchers. A/B Testing brings control and variant, while Data Anonymization brings masking.

A sensible plan is to start with Optimizely for the A/B Testing part and ARX for the Data Anonymization part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: testing too many variants on one side, and removing too little on the other.

Picture the whole as taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests, working alongside blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene.

Two habits help: first, decide the success metric and sample size before you begin; second, test whether combining fields could identify a person.
