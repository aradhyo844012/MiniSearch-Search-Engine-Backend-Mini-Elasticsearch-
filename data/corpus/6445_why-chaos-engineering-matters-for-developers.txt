Why Chaos Engineering Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Chaos Engineering make better decisions, because Chaos Engineering is deliberately causing failures in a system to learn how it responds.

Three situations show why it matters. First, testing resilience. Second, validating failover. Third, preparing teams for incidents. In each case, a solid grasp of steady state and hypotheses separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: experimenting without safeguards, having no steady-state metrics, and running first in production.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Chaos Monkey, Gremlin, and LitmusChaos cover most needs.
