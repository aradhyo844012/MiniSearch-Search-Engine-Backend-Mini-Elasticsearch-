Practice Exercises for Command Line Productivity

These exercises build real understanding of Command Line Productivity. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare tmux and fzf for managing servers and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Explain how history search and scripts differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick bash and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble automating repetitive tasks or exploring files and logs.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Command Line Productivity and include the term aliases.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Command Line Productivity is using shell tools and shortcuts to complete tasks faster.
