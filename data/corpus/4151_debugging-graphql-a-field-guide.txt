Debugging GraphQL: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around GraphQL, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: the N+1 query problem, unbounded query depth, and weak caching.
Step 3: revisit schema and queries and mutations, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Apollo or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving GraphQL in work such as mobile apps with limited bandwidth come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
