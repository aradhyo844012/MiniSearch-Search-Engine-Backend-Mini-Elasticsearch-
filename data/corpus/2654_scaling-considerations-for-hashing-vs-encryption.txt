Scaling Considerations for Hashing vs Encryption

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Hashing vs Encryption changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding one-way functions and getting storing passwords right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for encrypting passwords instead of hashing them and using broken hash functions, and measure with SHA-256 before changing anything.

Large scale: collision resistance and reversibility move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around verifying file integrity at this stage.

Before you scale, ask whether you will ever need the original value back. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
