Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Mocking and Stubbing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Mocking and Stubbing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests) and learn mocks and stubs.

Stage 2, hands-on: install unittest.mock, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving isolating units.

Stage 3, depth: study spies and fakes, then deliberately explore this problem: over-mocking.

Stage 4, application: apply Mocking and Stubbing to simulating failures, compare Mockito with Sinon.js, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Mocking and Stubbing to someone else, and then move on to Integration Testing.
