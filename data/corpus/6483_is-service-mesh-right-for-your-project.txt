Is Service Mesh Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Service Mesh, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies.

It is a good fit when your work involves securing service traffic or retries and timeouts, and when your team understands sidecar proxies.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn mutual TLS, or when you cannot manage these risks: added latency and complexity, adopting it too early, and hard-to-debug proxy configs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether traffic policies and observability are part of the answer. If they are, Service Mesh is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with fine-grained routing and adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
