Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Video Codecs

This path is for a learner who has never studied Video Codecs before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Video Codecs is programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed) and learn frames and motion compensation.

Stage 2, hands-on: install FFmpeg, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving streaming services.

Stage 3, depth: study bitrate and containers, then deliberately explore this problem: mismatched codec support.

Stage 4, application: apply Video Codecs to video calls, compare H.264 with AV1, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Video Codecs to someone else, and then move on to Vector Graphics (SVG).
