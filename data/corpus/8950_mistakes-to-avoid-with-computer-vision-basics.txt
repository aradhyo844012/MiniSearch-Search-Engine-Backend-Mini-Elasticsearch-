Mistakes to Avoid with Computer Vision Basics

Everyone learns Computer Vision Basics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: lighting changes. It usually appears when someone skips pixels and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: biased datasets. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of face detection. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: overfitting to a small set of photos. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on classification to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
