Secrets Management Explained Simply

If Secrets Management sounds intimidating, relax. It is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Secrets Management as a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: vaults, rotation, access policies, and audit logs. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with vaults, then add rotation.

You will meet Secrets Management in work such as database credentials, API keys, and TLS certificates.

One practical tip is to rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
