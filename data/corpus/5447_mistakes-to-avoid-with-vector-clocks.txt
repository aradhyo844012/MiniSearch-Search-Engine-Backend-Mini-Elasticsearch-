Mistakes to Avoid with Vector Clocks

Everyone learns Vector Clocks partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: clocks that grow with many nodes. It usually appears when someone skips logical time and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: confusing them with wall-clock time. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of detecting concurrent edits. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: never pruning old entries. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on conflict detection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
