Skills to Build Around Shaders

Knowing the definition of Shaders is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with vertex shaders and fragment shaders, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with GLSL on a Shaders task, and try at least one alternative such as Shadertoy.

Judgment: learn to decide when Shaders fits a problem such as lighting effects, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a tiny painter that colors each pixel following your instructions.

Habits: start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time, and write down what you learn after each project.
