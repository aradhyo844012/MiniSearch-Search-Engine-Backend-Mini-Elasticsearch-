Mistakes to Avoid with Time-Series Databases

Everyone learns Time-Series Databases partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: high-cardinality tags. It usually appears when someone skips timestamps and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: keeping raw data forever. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of server metrics. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: querying without time bounds. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on tags and fields to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: include a time range in every query.
