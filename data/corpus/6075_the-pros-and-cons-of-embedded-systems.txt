The Pros and Cons of Embedded Systems

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Embedded Systems is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports appliances
- Makes medical devices easier
- Helps with vehicles

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: memory leaks on tiny devices
- Risk: no plan for updates
- Risk: ignoring power use

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as appliances, and you can manage the risks, Embedded Systems is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
