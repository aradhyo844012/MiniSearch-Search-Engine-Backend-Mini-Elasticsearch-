Skills to Build Around Disaster Recovery

Knowing the definition of Disaster Recovery is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with recovery time objective and recovery point objective, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with AWS Backup on a Disaster Recovery task, and try at least one alternative such as Terraform.

Judgment: learn to decide when Disaster Recovery fits a problem such as surviving regional outages, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a fire drill for a whole system.

Habits: run a failover exercise on a regular schedule, and write down what you learn after each project.
