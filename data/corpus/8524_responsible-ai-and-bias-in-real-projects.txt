Responsible AI and Bias in Real Projects

Reading about Responsible AI and Bias is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: hiring tools. Here, bias in data drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against assuming data is neutral.

Scenario two: lending models. This time fairness metrics matters most, and tools such as Fairlearn help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: healthcare decisions. The key idea is transparency, and the biggest risk is testing only on average performance.

Across all three scenarios, Responsible AI and Bias stays the same idea: practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe. Only the details change.
