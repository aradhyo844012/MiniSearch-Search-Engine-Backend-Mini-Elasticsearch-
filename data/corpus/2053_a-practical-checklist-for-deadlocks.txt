A Practical Checklist for Deadlocks

Use this checklist before you rely on Deadlocks in a project.

1. Write at least one automated test that covers mutual exclusion.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example database lock conflicts.
4. Review the core ideas: mutual exclusion, hold and wait, circular wait, and lock ordering.
5. Guard against this risk: no timeouts on locks.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Deadlocks should accomplish.
7. Guard against this risk: acquiring locks in inconsistent order.
8. Pick a tool to start with, such as thread dumps or database deadlock logs.
