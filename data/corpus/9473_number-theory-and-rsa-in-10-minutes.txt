Number Theory and RSA in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Number Theory and RSA.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn prime numbers, modular arithmetic, greatest common divisor, and modular exponentiation.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a clock whose numbers wrap around. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through public key cryptography, hash functions, and digital signatures.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for using small primes and implementing crypto by hand in production, then try Python pow for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
