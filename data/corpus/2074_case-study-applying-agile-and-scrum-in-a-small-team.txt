Case Study: Applying Agile and Scrum in a Small Team

Imagine a small student team building a project that depends on Agile and Scrum. Many students first meet Agile and Scrum in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles.

The team first spent a week learning sprints and backlogs. Early on they hit a familiar problem: skipping retrospectives. After fixing it, they used Jira to check their work and adopted Agile and Scrum for student team projects. Looking back, they said the biggest lesson was to write tests earlier.

Three issues come up again and again: skipping retrospectives; overloading sprints; and treating estimates as promises. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

In short, careful study of Agile and Scrum pays off. Spend time on sprints and backlogs, and the rest will follow.
