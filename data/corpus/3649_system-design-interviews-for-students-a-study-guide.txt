System Design Interviews for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover System Design Interviews.

Learning goals:
1. Explain System Design Interviews in your own words.
2. Describe requirements, scaling estimates, trade-offs, and components.
3. Give examples from preparing for senior roles and practicing architectural thinking.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: System Design Interviews is discussions in which candidates design a large software system and explain their choices. Then draw sketching the blueprint of a city while explaining why each road exists on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements a problem? How does scaling estimates relate to trade-offs? When would you choose whiteboard sketches or Excalidraw?

Study habit worth keeping: clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.

When you feel ready, compare System Design Interviews with Competitive Programming and write down three differences.
