Scaling Considerations for Jupyter Notebooks

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Jupyter Notebooks changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding cells and getting exploring data right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for running cells out of order and hidden state, and measure with JupyterLab before changing anything.

Large scale: markdown and outputs move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around teaching and demos at this stage.

Before you scale, restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
