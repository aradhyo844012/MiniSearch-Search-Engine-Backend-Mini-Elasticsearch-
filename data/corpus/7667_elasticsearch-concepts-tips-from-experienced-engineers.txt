Elasticsearch Concepts Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Elasticsearch Concepts regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit shards before anything else.
3. Do not skip indexes; everything else builds on it.
4. Test the case of site search early, before the design hardens.
5. Reach for Elasticsearch first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Kibana.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to log analytics as well as to Elasticsearch Concepts in general.
