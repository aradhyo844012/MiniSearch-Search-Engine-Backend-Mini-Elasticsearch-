Scaling Considerations for Mocking and Stubbing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Mocking and Stubbing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding mocks and getting isolating units right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for over-mocking and tests that mirror the implementation, and measure with unittest.mock before changing anything.

Large scale: spies and fakes move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around simulating failures at this stage.

Before you scale, prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
