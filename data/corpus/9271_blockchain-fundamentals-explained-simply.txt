Blockchain Fundamentals Explained Simply

If Blockchain Fundamentals sounds intimidating, relax. It is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Blockchain Fundamentals as a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: blocks, hashes, consensus, and decentralization. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with blocks, then add hashes.

You will meet Blockchain Fundamentals in work such as digital currencies, supply chain tracking, and public records.

One practical tip is to ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
