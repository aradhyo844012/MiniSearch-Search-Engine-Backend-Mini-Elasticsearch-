Robotics and ROS Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Robotics and ROS.

Do:
- Learn sensors before anything else.
- Try ROS 2 on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test in simulation before moving real hardware.
- Test the case of warehouse robots early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: ignoring sensor noise.
- Ignore this: unsafe motion commands.
- Ignore this: tight coupling between nodes.
- Adopt Robotics and ROS just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Robotics and ROS, a little patience with actuators pays off more than any clever shortcut.
