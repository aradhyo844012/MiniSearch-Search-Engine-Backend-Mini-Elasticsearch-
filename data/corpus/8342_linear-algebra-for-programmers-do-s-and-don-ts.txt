Linear Algebra for Programmers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Linear Algebra for Programmers.

Do:
- Learn vectors before anything else.
- Try NumPy on a small example.
- Make it a habit to check shapes before multiplying.
- Test the case of 3D graphics early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing row and column conventions.
- Ignore this: shape mismatches.
- Ignore this: forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative.
- Adopt Linear Algebra for Programmers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Linear Algebra for Programmers, a little patience with matrices pays off more than any clever shortcut.
