A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Feature Flags

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Feature Flags.

Morning: Arjun starts by reviewing a ticket about dark launches. Before touching any code, Arjun re-reads the notes on flag evaluation to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Arjun pairs with a teammate to explore targeting rules with LaunchDarkly. A small test exposes the problem of never removing old flags, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving A/B tests. Arjun sketches light switches wired to every room of a house on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Arjun writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to give every flag an owner and a removal date.

Days like this are typical. Feature Flags is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
