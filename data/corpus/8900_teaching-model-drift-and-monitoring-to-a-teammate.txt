Teaching Model Drift and Monitoring to a Teammate

Explaining Model Drift and Monitoring to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: checking a car's gauges on a long trip. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Model Drift and Monitoring is watching deployed models for changes in data or performance over time. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving catching degrading models, naming data drift and concept drift as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain performance metrics in Model Drift and Monitoring back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, monitoring only system health, and suggest this habit: monitor input data as well as accuracy.
