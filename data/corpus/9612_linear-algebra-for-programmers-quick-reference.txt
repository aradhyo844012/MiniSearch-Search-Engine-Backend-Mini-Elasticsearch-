Linear Algebra for Programmers Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Linear Algebra for Programmers. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning.
Key terms: vectors, matrices, dot products, and eigenvalues.
Typical uses: 3D graphics, machine learning, and recommendation systems.
Risks: mixing row and column conventions, shape mismatches, and forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative.
Tools: NumPy, MATLAB, and the 3Blue1Brown video series.

Mental picture: a set of arrows and stretching machines acting on space.

Habit to build: check shapes before multiplying.

See also: Information Theory and Entropy, which sits close to Linear Algebra for Programmers in the area of Mathematics for Computing.
