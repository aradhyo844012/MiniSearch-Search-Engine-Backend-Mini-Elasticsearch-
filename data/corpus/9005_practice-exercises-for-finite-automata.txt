Practice Exercises for Finite Automata

These exercises build real understanding of Finite Automata. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Finite Automata and include the term states.
Exercise 2: Explain how accepting states and regular languages differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick JFLAP and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: missing transitions. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Compare Python dictionaries and regex engines for protocol design and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Finite Automata is simple abstract machines that read input and move between a fixed set of states.
