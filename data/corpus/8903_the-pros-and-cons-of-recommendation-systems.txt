The Pros and Cons of Recommendation Systems

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Recommendation Systems is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports streaming services
- Makes online shops easier
- Helps with news feeds

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: feedback loops
- Risk: popularity bias
- Risk: ignoring new users

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as streaming services, and you can manage the risks, Recommendation Systems is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
