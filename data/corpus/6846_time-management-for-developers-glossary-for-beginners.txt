Time Management for Developers Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Time Management for Developers feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Time Management for Developers: habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.
prioritization: a core term, most visible in managing coursework and projects.
time blocking: a core term that beginners often confuse with breaking down tasks.
breaking down tasks: a core term that connects to avoiding context switches.
avoiding context switches: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
calendar blocking: a tool commonly used for meeting deadlines.
