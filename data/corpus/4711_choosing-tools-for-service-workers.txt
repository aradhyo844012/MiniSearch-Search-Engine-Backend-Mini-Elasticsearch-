Choosing Tools for Service Workers

There is no single best tool for Service Workers. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Workbox is a common first choice, especially for offline support. the Chrome DevTools Application panel suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Lighthouse is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle faster repeat visits well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: caching stale files forever.

As a rule of thumb, version your caches so old files can be removed, no matter which tool you use.
