The Pros and Cons of Dependency Injection

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Dependency Injection is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports easier unit testing
- Makes swapping implementations easier
- Helps with configuring services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: hiding too much behind containers
- Risk: circular dependencies
- Risk: injecting too many dependencies

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as easier unit testing, and you can manage the risks, Dependency Injection is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
