The Pros and Cons of CSS Variables

No technology is free of trade-offs, and CSS Variables is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports theme switching
- Makes design systems easier
- Helps with spacing scales

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: missing fallbacks
- Risk: overriding at the wrong scope
- Risk: expecting preprocessor features

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as theme switching, and you can manage the risks, CSS Variables is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, define theme values on the root element.
