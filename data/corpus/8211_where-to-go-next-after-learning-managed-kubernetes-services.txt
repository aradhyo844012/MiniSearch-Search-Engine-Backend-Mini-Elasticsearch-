Where to Go Next After Learning Managed Kubernetes Services

You have met the basics of Managed Kubernetes Services. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about API Gateways, which belongs to the same area as Managed Kubernetes Services (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of the control plane, node pools, upgrades or integrated load balancers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around scaling services with Amazon EKS, and note every problem you meet, starting with neglecting version upgrades.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Managed Kubernetes Services to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
