Mistakes to Avoid with Data Augmentation

Everyone learns Data Augmentation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: changes that alter the label. It usually appears when someone skips transformations and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: augmenting the test set. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of image models. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unrealistic transformations. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on augmentation pipelines to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: augment only the training data.
