Blockchain Fundamentals for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Blockchain Fundamentals.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Blockchain Fundamentals in your own words.
2. Describe blocks, hashes, consensus, and decentralization.
3. Give examples from digital currencies and supply chain tracking.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes. Then draw a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is treating it as a database replacement a problem? How does hashes relate to consensus? When would you choose Bitcoin Core or Ethereum?

Study habit: ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.

When you feel ready, compare Blockchain Fundamentals with Biometric Authentication and write down three differences.
