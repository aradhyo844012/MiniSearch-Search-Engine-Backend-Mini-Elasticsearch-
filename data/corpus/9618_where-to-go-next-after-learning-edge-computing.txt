Where to Go Next After Learning Edge Computing

You have met the basics of Edge Computing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Digital Twins, which belongs to the same area as Edge Computing (Emerging and Specialized Technology) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of edge nodes, latency, bandwidth savings or synchronization, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around video analytics with AWS IoT Greengrass, and note every problem you meet, starting with hard-to-update devices.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Edge Computing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
