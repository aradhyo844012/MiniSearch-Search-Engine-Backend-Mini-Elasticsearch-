Encryption at Rest Explained Simply

If Encryption at Rest sounds intimidating, relax. It is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Encryption at Rest as a locked safe inside a locked room. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: symmetric keys, key management, disk encryption, and envelope encryption. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with symmetric keys, then add key management.

People reach for Encryption at Rest when they work on protecting backups, meeting compliance rules, and securing laptops.

One practical tip is to keep keys in a separate service from the data.
