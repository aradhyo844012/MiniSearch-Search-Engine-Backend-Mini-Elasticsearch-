Why 5G Networks Matters for Developers

Developers who understand 5G Networks make better decisions, because 5G Networks is the fifth generation of mobile networks offering higher speeds and lower latency.

Three situations show why it matters. First, mobile broadband. Second, connected vehicles. Third, industrial automation. In each case, a solid grasp of spectrum bands and latency separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: limited coverage on the fastest bands, battery drain, and marketing exaggeration.

You will not need to build everything yourself; speed test tools, network simulators, and 3GPP documents cover most needs.
