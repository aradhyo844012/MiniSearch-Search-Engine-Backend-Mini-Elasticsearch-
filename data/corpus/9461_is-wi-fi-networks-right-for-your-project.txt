Is Wi-Fi Networks Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Wi-Fi Networks, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Wi-Fi Networks is wireless networks that connect devices to a local network using radio signals.

It is a good fit when your work involves home internet or office coverage, and when your team understands SSIDs.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn channels and bands, or when you cannot manage these risks: overlapping channels, weak passwords, and poor access point placement.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether WPA encryption and signal strength are part of the answer. If they are, Wi-Fi Networks is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with public hotspots and use WPA3, or WPA2 with a long passphrase.
