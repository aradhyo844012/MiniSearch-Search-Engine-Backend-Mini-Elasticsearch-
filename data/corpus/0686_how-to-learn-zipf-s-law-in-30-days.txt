How to Learn Zipf's Law in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Zipf's Law. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Zipf's Law in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to the observation that a word's frequency is roughly inversely related to its rank in natural language.

Week 1: learn rank and frequency and power laws, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with matplotlib on a small task such as analyzing text corpora.
Week 3: build a mini project around sizing indexes.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as testing it on random text, and explain Zipf's Law to a friend.

Fast search comes from precomputing structures so that queries do very little work. Real query logs reveal what people actually look for, which is rarely what designers expect.

In practice you can start with matplotlib, then explore NumPy or pandas as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of Zipf's Law pays off. Spend time on rank and frequency and power laws, and the rest will follow.
