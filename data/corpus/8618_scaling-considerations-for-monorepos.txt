Scaling Considerations for Monorepos

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Monorepos changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding shared tooling and getting sharing libraries across apps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for slow checkouts and unclear ownership, and measure with Nx before changing anything.

Large scale: selective builds and atomic changes move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around coordinated refactors at this stage.

Before you scale, set up selective builds and tests early. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
