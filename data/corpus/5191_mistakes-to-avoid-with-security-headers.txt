Mistakes to Avoid with Security Headers

Everyone learns Security Headers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: setting headers on only some routes. It usually appears when someone skips Strict-Transport-Security and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: breaking embeds. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of hardening websites. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: copying configurations without understanding them. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on referrer policy to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test the headers on every environment.
