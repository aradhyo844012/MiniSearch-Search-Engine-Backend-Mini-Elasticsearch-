Understanding Reinforcement Learning Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture training a dog with treats for good behavior.

In that picture, agent is the first rule you follow, environment is the next step, and reward and policy keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes, and it involves details such as badly designed rewards that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Gymnasium to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain game playing to a friend.
