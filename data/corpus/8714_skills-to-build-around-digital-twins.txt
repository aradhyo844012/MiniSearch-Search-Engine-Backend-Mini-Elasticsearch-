Skills to Build Around Digital Twins

Knowing the definition of Digital Twins is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with sensor data and simulation models, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Azure Digital Twins on a Digital Twins task, and try at least one alternative such as Unity.

Judgment: learn to decide when Digital Twins fits a problem such as factory monitoring, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves.

Habits: define the question the twin must answer first, and write down what you learn after each project.
