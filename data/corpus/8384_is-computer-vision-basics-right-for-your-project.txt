Is Computer Vision Basics Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Computer Vision Basics, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video.

It is a good fit when your work involves face detection or document scanning, and when your team understands pixels.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn filters and edges, or when you cannot manage these risks: lighting changes, biased datasets, and overfitting to a small set of photos.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether feature detection and classification are part of the answer. If they are, Computer Vision Basics is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with quality inspection and visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
