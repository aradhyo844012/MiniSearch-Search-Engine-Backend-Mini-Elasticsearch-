How to Learn Unit Testing in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Unit Testing. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Unit Testing covers writing small automated tests that check one piece of code at a time. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn test cases and assertions, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with pytest on a small task such as catching regressions.
Week 3: build a mini project around documenting behavior.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as testing implementation details, and explain Unit Testing to a friend.

Keep notes on what worked. A short README explaining your decisions about Unit Testing helps the next developer. Ask a teammate to explain fixtures back to you; teaching is a quick way to find gaps. Measure before you optimize. Use pytest or a simple timer to see where time is actually spent.

In practice you can start with pytest, then explore unittest or coverage.py as your needs grow.

With steady practice, Unit Testing will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
