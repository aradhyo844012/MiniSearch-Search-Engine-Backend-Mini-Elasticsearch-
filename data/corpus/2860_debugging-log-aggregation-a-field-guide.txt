Debugging Log Aggregation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Log Aggregation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: unbounded storage growth, unstructured logs, and missing timestamps.
Step 3: revisit shippers and indexing, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Elasticsearch or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Log Aggregation in work such as troubleshooting across services come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
