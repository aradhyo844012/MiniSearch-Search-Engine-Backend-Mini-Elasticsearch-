Why Rust Ownership Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Rust Ownership make better decisions, because Rust Ownership is a set of rules that lets Rust manage memory safely without a garbage collector.

Three situations show why it matters. First, systems programming. Second, command line tools. Third, safe concurrent code. In each case, a solid grasp of ownership and borrowing separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: fighting the borrow checker with needless clones, confusing moves and copies, and overly complex lifetime annotations.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Cargo, rustc, and rust-analyzer cover most needs.
