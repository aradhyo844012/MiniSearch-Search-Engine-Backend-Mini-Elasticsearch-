The Pros and Cons of Build Systems

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Build Systems is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports compiling large projects
- Makes packaging software easier
- Helps with running repeatable pipelines

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow full rebuilds
- Risk: undeclared dependencies
- Risk: machine-specific builds

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as compiling large projects, and you can manage the risks, Build Systems is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, make builds reproducible on any machine.
