Vector Clocks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Vector Clocks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip logical time; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for Riak first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as the Amazon Dynamo paper.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit per-process counters before anything else.
4. Be careful with this one: clocks that grow with many nodes.
5. Write down why you chose Vector Clocks so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to replicated databases as well.
