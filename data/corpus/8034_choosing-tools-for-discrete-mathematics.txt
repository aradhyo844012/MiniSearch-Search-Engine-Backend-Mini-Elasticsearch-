Choosing Tools for Discrete Mathematics

There is no single best tool for Discrete Mathematics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Python sets is a common first choice, especially for algorithm analysis. proof assistants suits teams that already work with similar technology, and textbooks and problem sets is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Discrete Mathematics by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle database theory well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Discrete Mathematics, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: skipping proofs.

As a rule of thumb, practice writing small proofs by hand, no matter which tool you use.
