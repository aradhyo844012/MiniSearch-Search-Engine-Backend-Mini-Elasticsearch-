Mistakes to Avoid with Load Testing

Everyone learns Load Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: testing unrealistic traffic. It usually appears when someone skips virtual users and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring warm-up. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of capacity planning. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: using average latency only. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on ramp-up to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages.
