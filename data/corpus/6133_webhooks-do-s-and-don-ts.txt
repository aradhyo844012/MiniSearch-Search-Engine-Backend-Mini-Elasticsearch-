Webhooks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Webhooks.

Do:
- Learn event payloads before anything else.
- Try ngrok on a small example.
- Make it a habit to respond quickly and process the event in a background job.
- Test the case of payment notifications early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: not verifying signatures.
- Ignore this: slow handlers causing timeouts.
- Ignore this: processing duplicate events.
- Adopt Webhooks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Webhooks, a little patience with signature verification pays off more than any clever shortcut.
