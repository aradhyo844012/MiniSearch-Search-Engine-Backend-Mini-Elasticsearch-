Digital Audio Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Digital Audio. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.
Key terms: sampling rate, bit depth, waveforms, and the Nyquist limit.
Typical uses: music production, voice calls, and podcasts.
Risks: clipping from too high a level, aliasing at low sample rates, and mixing sample rates.
Tools: Audacity, SoX, and the Web Audio API.

Mental picture: taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later.

Habit to build: keep recordings below the clipping level.
