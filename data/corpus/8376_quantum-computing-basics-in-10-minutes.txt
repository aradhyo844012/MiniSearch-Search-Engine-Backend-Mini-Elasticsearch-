Quantum Computing Basics in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Quantum Computing Basics.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Quantum Computing Basics is computing that uses qubits and the rules of quantum mechanics to solve certain problems.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn qubits, superposition, entanglement, and quantum gates.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a coin spinning in the air before it lands. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through research on cryptography, simulating molecules, and optimization studies.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for expecting speedups for every problem and noise and errors, then try Qiskit for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to start by simulating small circuits on a laptop.
