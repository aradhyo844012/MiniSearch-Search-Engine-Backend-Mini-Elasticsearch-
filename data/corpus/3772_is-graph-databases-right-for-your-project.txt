Is Graph Databases Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Graph Databases, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Graph Databases is databases that store data as nodes and relationships and query connections directly.

It is a good fit when your work involves social networks or recommendation engines, and when your team understands nodes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn edges, or when you cannot manage these risks: using them for simple tabular data, unbounded traversals, and supernodes with millions of edges.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether properties and traversal queries are part of the answer. If they are, Graph Databases is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with fraud detection and limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
