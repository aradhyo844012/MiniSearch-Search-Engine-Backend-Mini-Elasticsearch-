VPNs in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through VPNs.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn tunneling, encryption, authentication, and split tunneling.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a private tunnel under a public road. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through remote work access, linking offices, and protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for assuming it makes you anonymous and slow routing through distant servers, then try WireGuard for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to test for DNS leaks after connecting.
