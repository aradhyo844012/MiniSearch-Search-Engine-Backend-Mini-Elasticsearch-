Mistakes to Avoid with BGP and Internet Routing

Everyone learns BGP and Internet Routing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: route leaks. It usually appears when someone skips autonomous systems and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: hijacked prefixes. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of connecting ISPs. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: misconfigured filters. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on path selection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: filter route announcements strictly.
