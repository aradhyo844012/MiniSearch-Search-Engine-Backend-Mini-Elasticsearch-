Debugging Object-Relational Mappers: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Object-Relational Mappers, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: N+1 queries, loading entire tables, and hiding slow SQL.
Step 3: revisit models and queries, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use SQLAlchemy or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Object-Relational Mappers in work such as building CRUD apps quickly come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, log the generated SQL during development. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
