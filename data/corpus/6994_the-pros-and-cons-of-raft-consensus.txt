The Pros and Cons of Raft Consensus

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Raft Consensus is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports configuration stores
- Makes distributed locks easier
- Helps with replicated state machines

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: even-sized clusters
- Risk: slow disks causing elections
- Risk: network delays above election timeouts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as configuration stores, and you can manage the risks, Raft Consensus is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use an odd number of nodes.
