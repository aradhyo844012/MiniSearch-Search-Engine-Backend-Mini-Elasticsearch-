The Pros and Cons of Responsible AI and Bias

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Responsible AI and Bias is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports hiring tools
- Makes lending models easier
- Helps with healthcare decisions

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming data is neutral
- Risk: testing only on average performance
- Risk: giving people no way to appeal

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as hiring tools, and you can manage the risks, Responsible AI and Bias is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test performance separately for different groups.
