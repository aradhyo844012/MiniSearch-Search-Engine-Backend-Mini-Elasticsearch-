Canary Releases Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Canary Releases regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for Argo Rollouts first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Istio.
2. Do not skip traffic percentages; everything else builds on it.
3. Be careful with this one: too little traffic to reveal problems.
4. Test the case of safer releases early, before the design hardens.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit health metrics before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to testing with real traffic as well.
