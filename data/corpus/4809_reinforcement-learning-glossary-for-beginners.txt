Reinforcement Learning Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Reinforcement Learning feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Reinforcement Learning: a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.
agent: a core term, most visible in game playing.
environment: a core term that beginners often confuse with reward.
reward: a core term that connects to policy.
policy: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Gymnasium: a tool commonly used for robot control.

Come back to this Reinforcement Learning list whenever documentation for Stable-Baselines3 uses a word you do not recognize.
