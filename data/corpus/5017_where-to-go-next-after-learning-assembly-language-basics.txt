Where to Go Next After Learning Assembly Language Basics

You have met the basics of Assembly Language Basics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Memory Hierarchy, which belongs to the same area as Assembly Language Basics (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of registers, instructions, memory addressing or the stack, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around debugging crashes with GDB, and note every problem you meet, starting with wrong calling conventions.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Assembly Language Basics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
