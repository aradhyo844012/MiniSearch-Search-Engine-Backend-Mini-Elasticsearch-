Scaling Considerations for DHCP

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how DHCP changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding leases and getting home and office networks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for rogue DHCP servers and exhausted address pools, and measure with dnsmasq before changing anything.

Large scale: reservations and discover and offer messages move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around guest Wi-Fi at this stage.

Before you scale, reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
