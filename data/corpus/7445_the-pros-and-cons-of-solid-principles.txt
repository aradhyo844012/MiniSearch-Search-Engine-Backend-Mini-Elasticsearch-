The Pros and Cons of SOLID Principles

No technology is free of trade-offs, and SOLID Principles is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports structuring classes
- Makes reviewing designs easier
- Helps with reducing coupling

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: applying them rigidly to tiny scripts
- Risk: creating too many tiny classes
- Risk: confusing interfaces with inheritance

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as structuring classes, and you can manage the risks, SOLID Principles is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, apply them when a design starts to hurt, not before.
