Entity Component Systems Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Entity Component Systems regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: overengineering small games.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit components before anything else.
3. Make sure you can keep components as plain data.
4. Test the case of large simulations early, before the design hardens.
5. Write down why you chose Entity Component Systems so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to games with thousands of objects as well as to Entity Component Systems in general.
