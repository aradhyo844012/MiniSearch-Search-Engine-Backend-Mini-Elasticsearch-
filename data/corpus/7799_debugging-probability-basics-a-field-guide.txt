Debugging Probability Basics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Probability Basics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: confusing independent and exclusive events, ignoring base rates, and falling for the gambler's fallacy.
Step 3: revisit events and conditional probability, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python random or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Probability Basics in work such as randomized algorithms come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, simulate the problem in code to check your reasoning. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
