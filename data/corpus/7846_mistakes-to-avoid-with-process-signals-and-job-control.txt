Mistakes to Avoid with Process Signals and Job Control

Everyone learns Process Signals and Job Control partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using kill -9 first. It usually appears when someone skips SIGTERM and SIGKILL and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: orphaned child processes. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of stopping runaway programs. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring cleanup in handlers. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on process groups to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.
