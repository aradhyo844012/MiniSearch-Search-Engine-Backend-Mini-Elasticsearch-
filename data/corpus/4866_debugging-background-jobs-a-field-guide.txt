Debugging Background Jobs: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Background Jobs, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: jobs that are not idempotent, unbounded retries, and losing jobs on restarts.
Step 3: revisit queues and workers, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Celery or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Background Jobs in work such as sending emails come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, give every job a timeout and a retry limit. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
