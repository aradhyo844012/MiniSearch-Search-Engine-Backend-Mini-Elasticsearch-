Virtual Private Clouds in Real Projects

Reading about Virtual Private Clouds is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: separating public and private services. Here, subnets drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against overlapping address ranges.

Scenario two: connecting to offices. This time route tables matters most, and tools such as AWS VPC help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: securing databases. The key idea is security groups, and the biggest risk is databases in public subnets.

Across all three scenarios, Virtual Private Clouds stays the same idea: isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources. Only the details change.
