Where to Go Next After Learning Regular Expressions

You have met the basics of Regular Expressions. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Garbage Collection, which belongs to the same area as Regular Expressions (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of character classes, quantifiers, groups or anchors, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around searching logs with the Python re module, and note every problem you meet, starting with catastrophic backtracking.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Regular Expressions to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
