Mistakes to Avoid with A* Pathfinding

Everyone learns A* Pathfinding partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overestimating the heuristic. It usually appears when someone skips open and closed sets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow searches on huge maps. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of game characters. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring moving obstacles. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on grids and graphs to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
