Time Management for Developers Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Time Management for Developers. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule.
Key terms: prioritization, time blocking, breaking down tasks, and avoiding context switches.
Typical uses: managing coursework and projects, meeting deadlines, and reducing stress.
Risks: multitasking, underestimating tasks, and working without breaks.
Tools: calendar blocking, Trello, and the Pomodoro technique.

Mental picture: packing a suitcase with the big items first.

Habit to build: break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
