Android Basics Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Android Basics.

Q: Explain Android Basics in simple words.
A: Android Basics refers to the core building blocks used to create apps for Android phones. I would add an example involving building simple apps.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Android Studio, Kotlin, and Gradle.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as activities, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: activities, layouts, intents, and lifecycle.

Q: How does it connect to Mobile App Performance?
A: Both belong to the area of Mobile Development, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with accessing sensors, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.
