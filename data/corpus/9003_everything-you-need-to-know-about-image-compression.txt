Everything You Need to Know About Image Compression

This page collects the essentials about Image Compression in one place. In short, Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail.

Core concepts:
- lossy and lossless formats
- quantization
- transform coding
- quality settings

Where it is used:
- web images
- photo storage
- sending pictures over slow networks

Common problems:
- repeated recompression
- the wrong format for graphics
- excessive quality loss

Tools to know:
- ImageMagick
- mozjpeg
- Squoosh

Remember this above all: packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.
