Pair Programming for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Pair Programming.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Pair Programming in your own words.
2. Describe driver and navigator, rotation, communication, and shared ownership.
3. Give examples from teaching new teammates and solving hard bugs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Pair Programming is two developers working together at one computer on the same code. Then draw a pilot and co-pilot who check each other's decisions on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is one person dominating a problem? How does rotation relate to communication? When would you choose VS Code Live Share or Tuple?

Study habit worth keeping: swap roles every twenty to thirty minutes.

When you feel ready, compare Pair Programming with API Design Principles and write down three differences.
