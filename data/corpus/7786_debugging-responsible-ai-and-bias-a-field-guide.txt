Debugging Responsible AI and Bias: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Responsible AI and Bias, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: assuming data is neutral, testing only on average performance, and giving people no way to appeal.
Step 3: revisit bias in data and fairness metrics, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Fairlearn or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Responsible AI and Bias in work such as hiring tools come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test performance separately for different groups. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
