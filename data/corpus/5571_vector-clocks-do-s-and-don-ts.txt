Vector Clocks Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Vector Clocks.

Do:
- Learn logical time before anything else.
- Try Riak on a small example.
- Make it a habit to compare clocks entry by entry to detect true concurrency.
- Test the case of detecting concurrent edits early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: clocks that grow with many nodes.
- Ignore this: confusing them with wall-clock time.
- Ignore this: never pruning old entries.
- Adopt Vector Clocks just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Vector Clocks, a little patience with per-process counters pays off more than any clever shortcut.
