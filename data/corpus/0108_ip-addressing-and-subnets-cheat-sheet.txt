IP Addressing and Subnets Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of IP Addressing and Subnets.

- Definition: the rules for numbering devices on a network and dividing networks into smaller parts.
- Core ideas: IPv4 and IPv6; subnet masks; CIDR notation; private ranges.
- Use it for: planning office networks; configuring cloud networks; troubleshooting connectivity.
- Watch out for: overlapping address ranges; miscounting host bits; exhausting small subnets.
- Tools: ipcalc, ping, traceroute.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for subnet masks before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice IPv4 and IPv6 until it feels automatic.
