The Pros and Cons of SSH and Remote Access

No technology is free of trade-offs, and SSH and Remote Access is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports administering servers
- Makes copying files securely easier
- Helps with tunneling to private services

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: password logins left enabled
- Risk: sharing private keys
- Risk: ignoring host key warnings

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as administering servers, and you can manage the risks, SSH and Remote Access is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use key-based login and disable password authentication.
