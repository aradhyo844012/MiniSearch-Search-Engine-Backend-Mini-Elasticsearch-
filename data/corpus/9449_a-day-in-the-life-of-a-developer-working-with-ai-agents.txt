A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with AI Agents

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on AI Agents.

Morning: Arjun starts by reviewing a ticket about research assistants. Before touching any code, Arjun re-reads the notes on planning to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Arjun pairs with a teammate to explore tool calls with LangGraph. A small test exposes the problem of runaway loops, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving workflow automation. Arjun sketches an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Arjun writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.

Days like this are typical. AI Agents is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
