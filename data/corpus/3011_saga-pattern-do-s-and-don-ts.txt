Saga Pattern Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Saga Pattern.

Do:
- Learn local transactions before anything else.
- Try Temporal on a small example.
- Make it a habit to make every step and its compensation idempotent.
- Test the case of order processing early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: missing compensations.
- Ignore this: steps that are not idempotent.
- Ignore this: flows that are hard to trace.
- Adopt Saga Pattern just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Saga Pattern, a little patience with compensating actions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
