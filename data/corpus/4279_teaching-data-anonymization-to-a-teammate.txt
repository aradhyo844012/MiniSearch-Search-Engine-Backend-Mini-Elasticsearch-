Teaching Data Anonymization to a Teammate

Explaining Data Anonymization to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sharing datasets with researchers, naming masking and generalization as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain k-anonymity in Data Anonymization back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, re-identification through combined fields, and suggest this habit: test whether combining fields could identify a person.
