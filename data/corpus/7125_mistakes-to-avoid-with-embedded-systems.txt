Mistakes to Avoid with Embedded Systems

Everyone learns Embedded Systems partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: memory leaks on tiny devices. It usually appears when someone skips microcontrollers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no plan for updates. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of appliances. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring power use. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on memory limits to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
