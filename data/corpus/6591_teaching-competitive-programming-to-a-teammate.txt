Teaching Competitive Programming to a Teammate

Explaining Competitive Programming to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: athletic training for problem solving. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Competitive Programming is solving algorithmic problems under time limits to build speed and problem-solving skills. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving interview preparation, naming problem patterns and time limits as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain contests in Competitive Programming back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, memorizing solutions without understanding, and suggest this habit: review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
