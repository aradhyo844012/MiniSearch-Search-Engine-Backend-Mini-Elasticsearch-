Scaling Considerations for Video Codecs

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Video Codecs changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding frames and getting streaming services right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mismatched codec support and too low a bitrate, and measure with FFmpeg before changing anything.

Large scale: bitrate and containers move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around video calls at this stage.

Before you scale, test playback on the weakest target device. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
