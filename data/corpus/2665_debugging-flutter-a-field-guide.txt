Debugging Flutter: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Flutter, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: deep widget trees, poor state organization, and large app sizes.
Step 3: revisit widgets and state, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Dart or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Flutter in work such as cross-platform mobile apps come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, split big widgets into small reusable ones. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
