Where to Go Next After Learning Open Source Contribution

You have met the basics of Open Source Contribution. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Reading Documentation, which belongs to the same area as Open Source Contribution (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of forks, pull requests, issues or contributing guides, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around learning from experienced maintainers with GitHub, and note every problem you meet, starting with opening huge pull requests.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Open Source Contribution to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
