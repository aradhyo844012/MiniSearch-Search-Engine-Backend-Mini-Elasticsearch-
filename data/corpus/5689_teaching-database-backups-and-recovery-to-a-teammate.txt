Teaching Database Backups and Recovery to a Teammate

Explaining Database Backups and Recovery to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving recovering deleted tables, naming full and incremental backups and recovery point objective as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain recovery time objective in Database Backups and Recovery back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, never testing restores, and suggest this habit: schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
