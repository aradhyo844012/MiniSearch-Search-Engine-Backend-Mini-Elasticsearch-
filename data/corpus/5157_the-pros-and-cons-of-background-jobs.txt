The Pros and Cons of Background Jobs

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Background Jobs is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports sending emails
- Makes resizing images easier
- Helps with generating reports

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: jobs that are not idempotent
- Risk: unbounded retries
- Risk: losing jobs on restarts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as sending emails, and you can manage the risks, Background Jobs is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, give every job a timeout and a retry limit.
