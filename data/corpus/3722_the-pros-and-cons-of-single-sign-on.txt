The Pros and Cons of Single Sign-On

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Single Sign-On is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports company intranets
- Makes reducing password fatigue easier
- Helps with centralized access control

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: a single point of failure
- Risk: misconfigured redirects
- Risk: long sessions on shared devices

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as company intranets, and you can manage the risks, Single Sign-On is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, plan for identity provider outages.
