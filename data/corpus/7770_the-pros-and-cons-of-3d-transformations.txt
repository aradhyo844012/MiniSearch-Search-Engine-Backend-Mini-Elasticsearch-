The Pros and Cons of 3D Transformations

No technology is free of trade-offs, and 3D Transformations is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports games
- Makes modeling tools easier
- Helps with robotics

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: wrong multiplication order
- Risk: gimbal lock
- Risk: mixing coordinate systems

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as games, and you can manage the risks, 3D Transformations is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
