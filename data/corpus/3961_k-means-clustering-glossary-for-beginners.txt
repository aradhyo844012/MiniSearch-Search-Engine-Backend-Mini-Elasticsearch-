K-Means Clustering Glossary for Beginners

New words can make K-Means Clustering feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

K-Means Clustering: an algorithm that groups data points into a chosen number of clusters around central points.
centroids: a core term, most visible in customer segmentation.
distance measures: a core term that beginners often confuse with choosing k.
choosing k: a core term that connects to iterations.
iterations: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
scikit-learn: a tool commonly used for image compression.

Come back to this K-Means Clustering list whenever documentation for NumPy uses a word you do not recognize.
