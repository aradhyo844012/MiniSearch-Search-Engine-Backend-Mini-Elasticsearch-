How to Learn Serverless Computing in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Serverless Computing. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Serverless Computing in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to running code in managed functions without maintaining servers yourself.

If you are comfortable with Serverless Computing, explore Monitoring and Observability next. The two often appear in the same project.

Week 1: learn functions and event triggers, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with AWS Lambda on a small task such as webhooks.
Week 3: build a mini project around scheduled jobs.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as cold start delays, and explain Serverless Computing to a friend.

In practice you can start with AWS Lambda, then explore Cloud Functions or Azure Functions as your needs grow.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Serverless Computing.
