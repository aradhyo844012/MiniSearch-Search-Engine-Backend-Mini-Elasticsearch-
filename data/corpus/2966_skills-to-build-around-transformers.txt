Skills to Build Around Transformers

Knowing the definition of Transformers is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with self-attention and positional encoding, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Hugging Face on a Transformers task, and try at least one alternative such as PyTorch.

Judgment: learn to decide when Transformers fits a problem such as language translation, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a reader who can look back at any word of a paragraph while reading each new word.

Habits: begin with small pretrained models, and write down what you learn after each project.
