Write-Ahead Logging Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Write-Ahead Logging. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a technique that records changes in a log before they are applied to the main data.
Key terms: log records, checkpoints, crash recovery, and fsync.
Typical uses: surviving crashes, feeding replication streams, and point-in-time recovery.
Risks: disabling fsync for speed, log files filling the disk, and forgetting to archive logs.
Tools: PostgreSQL WAL, SQLite journal, and Apache Kafka.

Mental picture: writing a plan in a notebook before changing the real blueprint.

Habit to build: test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose.
