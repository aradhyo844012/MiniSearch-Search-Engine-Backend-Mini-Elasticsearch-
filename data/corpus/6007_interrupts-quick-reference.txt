Interrupts Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Interrupts. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event.
Key terms: interrupt handlers, interrupt vectors, priorities, and context saving.
Typical uses: keyboard and mouse input, timers, and network packets arriving.
Risks: long handlers, race conditions with shared data, and lost interrupts.
Tools: Linux /proc/interrupts, logic analyzers, and embedded debuggers.

Mental picture: a phone call interrupting your reading.

Habit to build: keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.
