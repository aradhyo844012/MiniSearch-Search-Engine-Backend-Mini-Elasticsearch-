Teaching TypeScript Generics to a Teammate

Explaining TypeScript Generics to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a labeled box whose label type is filled in when you use it. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: TypeScript Generics is a feature that lets functions and types work with many types while keeping type safety. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving reusable utility functions, naming type parameters and constraints as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain type inference in TypeScript Generics back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, overusing any, and suggest this habit: add constraints so that error messages become clearer.
