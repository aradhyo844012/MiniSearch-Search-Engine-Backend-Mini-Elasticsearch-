The Pros and Cons of Raspberry Pi

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Raspberry Pi is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports home automation
- Makes learning Linux easier
- Helps with small servers

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: SD card corruption
- Risk: inadequate power supplies
- Risk: exposing the device without hardening

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as home automation, and you can manage the risks, Raspberry Pi is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
