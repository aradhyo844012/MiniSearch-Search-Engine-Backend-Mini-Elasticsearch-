Why Encryption at Rest Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Encryption at Rest make better decisions, because Encryption at Rest is protecting stored data by encrypting it on disk or in a database.

Three situations show why it matters. First, protecting backups. Second, meeting compliance rules. Third, securing laptops. In each case, a solid grasp of symmetric keys and key management separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: storing keys next to the data, no key rotation, and assuming it stops every kind of attack.

You will not need to build everything yourself; LUKS, AWS KMS, and BitLocker cover most needs.
