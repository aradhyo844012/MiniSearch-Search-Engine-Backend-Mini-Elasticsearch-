Practice Exercises for A* Pathfinding

These exercises build real understanding of A* Pathfinding. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble game characters or robot navigation.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing A* Pathfinding and include the term open and closed sets.
Exercise 3: Explain how heuristics and grids and graphs differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Unity NavMesh and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: overestimating the heuristic. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: A* Pathfinding is a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining.
