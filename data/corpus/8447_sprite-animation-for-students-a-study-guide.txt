Sprite Animation for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Sprite Animation.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Sprite Animation in your own words.
2. Describe sprite sheets, frames, frame rate, and animation states.
3. Give examples from 2D platformers and user interface effects.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Sprite Animation is showing a sequence of 2D images quickly to make a character appear to move. Then draw a flip book drawn one page at a time on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is inconsistent frame timing a problem? How does frames relate to frame rate? When would you choose Aseprite or Unity Animator?

Study habit worth keeping: pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.

When you feel ready, compare Sprite Animation with A* Pathfinding and write down three differences.
