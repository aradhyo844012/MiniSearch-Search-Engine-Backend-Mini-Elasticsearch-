Packet Switching for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Packet Switching.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Packet Switching in your own words.
2. Describe packets, headers, routing, and reassembly.
3. Give examples from internet traffic and voice over IP.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Packet Switching is a method of sending data as small packets that travel independently across a network. Then draw mailing a book one page per envelope and reassembling it on arrival on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is packet loss a problem? How does headers relate to routing? When would you choose Wireshark or tcpdump?

Study habit worth keeping: capture a few packets to see the headers for yourself.

When you feel ready, compare Packet Switching with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 and write down three differences.
