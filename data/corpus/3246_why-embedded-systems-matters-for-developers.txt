Why Embedded Systems Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Embedded Systems make better decisions, because Embedded Systems is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits.

Three situations show why it matters. First, appliances. Second, medical devices. Third, vehicles. In each case, a solid grasp of microcontrollers and firmware separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: memory leaks on tiny devices, no plan for updates, and ignoring power use.

Good tools exist, including C, FreeRTOS, and PlatformIO, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
