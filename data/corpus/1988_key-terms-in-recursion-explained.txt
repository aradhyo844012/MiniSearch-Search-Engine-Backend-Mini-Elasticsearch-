Key Terms in Recursion Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Recursion much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. When people talk about Recursion, they usually mean a technique where a function solves a problem by calling itself on smaller inputs. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

base case: a key term in Recursion. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
recursive case: a key term in Recursion. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
call stack: a key term in Recursion. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
tail calls: a key term in Recursion. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.

Measure before you optimize. Use Python sys.setrecursionlimit or a simple timer to see where time is actually spent. Practice base case with small exercises until it feels natural. Write a few tests that show how Recursion should behave. Tests double as documentation for your teammates.
