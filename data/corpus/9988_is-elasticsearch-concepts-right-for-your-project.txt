Is Elasticsearch Concepts Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Elasticsearch Concepts, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.

It is a good fit when your work involves site search or log analytics, and when your team understands indexes.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn shards, or when you cannot manage these risks: too many shards, mapping explosions, and treating it as a primary database.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether analyzers and queries are part of the answer. If they are, Elasticsearch Concepts is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with product catalogs and define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
