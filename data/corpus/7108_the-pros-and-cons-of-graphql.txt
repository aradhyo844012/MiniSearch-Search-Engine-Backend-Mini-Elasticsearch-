The Pros and Cons of GraphQL

No technology is free of trade-offs, and GraphQL is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports mobile apps with limited bandwidth
- Makes aggregating many services easier
- Helps with flexible dashboards

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: the N+1 query problem
- Risk: unbounded query depth
- Risk: weak caching

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as mobile apps with limited bandwidth, and you can manage the risks, GraphQL is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
