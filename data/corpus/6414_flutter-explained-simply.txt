Flutter Explained Simply

Here is Flutter without the jargon: it is a toolkit for building natively compiled apps from a single Dart codebase.

Think of Flutter as a box of customizable building blocks drawn by its own engine. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: widgets, state, hot reload, and the render tree. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with widgets, then add state.

You will meet Flutter in work such as cross-platform mobile apps, desktop apps, and custom user interfaces.

One practical tip is to split big widgets into small reusable ones.

Once Flutter feels comfortable, read about Push Notifications, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
