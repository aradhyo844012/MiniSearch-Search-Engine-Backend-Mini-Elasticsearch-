Feature Stores Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Feature Stores.

Do:
- Learn offline store before anything else.
- Try Feast on a small example.
- Make it a habit to define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.
- Test the case of sharing features across models early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: leaking future data.
- Ignore this: duplicate definitions.
- Ignore this: stale online values.
- Adopt Feature Stores just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Feature Stores, a little patience with online store pays off more than any clever shortcut.
