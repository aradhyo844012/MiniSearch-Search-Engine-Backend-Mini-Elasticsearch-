Data Quality Checks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Data Quality Checks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip null checks; everything else builds on it.
2. Reach for Great Expectations first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as dbt tests.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit uniqueness before anything else.
4. Write down why you chose Data Quality Checks so the next person understands the decision.
5. Be careful with this one: alerts nobody reads.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to validating ingestion as well.
