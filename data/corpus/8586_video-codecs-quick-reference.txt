Video Codecs Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Video Codecs. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: programs that compress and decompress video so it can be stored and streamed.
Key terms: frames, motion compensation, bitrate, and containers.
Typical uses: streaming services, video calls, and archiving footage.
Risks: mismatched codec support, too low a bitrate, and ignoring hardware decoding.
Tools: FFmpeg, H.264, and AV1.

Mental picture: a flip book that only redraws what changed between pages.

Habit to build: test playback on the weakest target device.
