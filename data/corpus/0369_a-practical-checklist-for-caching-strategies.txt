A Practical Checklist for Caching Strategies

Use this checklist before you rely on Caching Strategies in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: stale data.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers cache-aside.
3. Review how Caching Strategies interacts with JWT.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as Redis or Memcached.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example speeding up read-heavy APIs.
6. Confirm that your team agrees on what Caching Strategies should accomplish.
7. Review the core ideas: cache-aside, write-through, time to live, and invalidation.
8. Guard against this risk: cache stampedes.
