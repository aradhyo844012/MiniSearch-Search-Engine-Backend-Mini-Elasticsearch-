Scaling Considerations for Views and Materialized Views

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Views and Materialized Views changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding view definitions and getting simplifying repeated queries right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for stale materialized data and views stacked on views, and measure with PostgreSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: permissions and dependencies move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around hiding sensitive columns at this stage.

Before you scale, document how and when each materialized view is refreshed. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
