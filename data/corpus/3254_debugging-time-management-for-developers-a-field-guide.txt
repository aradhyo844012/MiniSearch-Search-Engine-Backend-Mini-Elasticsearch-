Debugging Time Management for Developers: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Time Management for Developers, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: multitasking, underestimating tasks, and working without breaks.
Step 3: revisit prioritization and time blocking, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use calendar blocking or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Time Management for Developers in work such as managing coursework and projects come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
