Practice Exercises for Anomaly Detection

These exercises build real understanding of Anomaly Detection. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble fraud detection or server monitoring.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Anomaly Detection and include the term baselines.
Exercise 3: Compare PyOD and Prometheus for manufacturing quality and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Pick scikit-learn and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Explain how isolation methods and false positives differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Anomaly Detection is finding data points or events that differ sharply from normal behavior.
