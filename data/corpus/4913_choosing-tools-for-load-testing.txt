Choosing Tools for Load Testing

There is no single best tool for Load Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

k6 is a common first choice, especially for capacity planning. Locust suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Apache JMeter is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle finding bottlenecks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: testing unrealistic traffic.

As a rule of thumb, report p95 and p99 latency, not only averages, no matter which tool you use.
