Where to Go Next After Learning Content Security Policy

You have met the basics of Content Security Policy. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Penetration Testing, which belongs to the same area as Content Security Policy (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of directives, nonces, report-only mode or trusted sources, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around blocking unwanted scripts with browser DevTools, and note every problem you meet, starting with allowing unsafe-inline.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Content Security Policy to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
