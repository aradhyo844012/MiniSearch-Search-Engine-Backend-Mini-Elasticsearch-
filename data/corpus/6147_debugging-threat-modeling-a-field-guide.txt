Debugging Threat Modeling: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Threat Modeling, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: doing it once and never again, ignoring data flows, and focusing only on technical threats.
Step 3: revisit assets and attackers, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use STRIDE or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Threat Modeling in work such as designing new features come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, draw the data flow diagram first. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
