Choosing Tools for Game AI Behavior Trees

There is no single best tool for Game AI Behavior Trees. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Unreal Engine behavior trees is a common first choice, especially for enemy behavior. py_trees suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Behavior Designer is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Game AI Behavior Trees by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle companion characters well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Game AI Behavior Trees, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: trees that become tangled.

As a rule of thumb, visualize the tree while the game is running, no matter which tool you use.
