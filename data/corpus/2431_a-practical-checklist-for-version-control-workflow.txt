A Practical Checklist for Version Control Workflow

Use this checklist before you rely on Version Control Workflow in a project.

1. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on branches.
2. Measure results before and after any change.
3. Review the core ideas: commits, branches, tags, and release process.
4. Guard against this risk: vague commit messages.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example collaborating on shared code.
6. Guard against this risk: committing large unrelated changes.
7. Confirm that your team agrees on what Version Control Workflow should accomplish.
