Where to Go Next After Learning Regression Testing

You have met the basics of Regression Testing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about End-to-End Testing, which belongs to the same area as Regression Testing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of test suites, automation, test selection or bug reproduction tests, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around verifying bug fixes with pytest, and note every problem you meet, starting with slow suites that nobody runs.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Regression Testing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
