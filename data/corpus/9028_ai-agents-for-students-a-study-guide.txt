AI Agents for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover AI Agents.

Learning goals:
1. Explain AI Agents in your own words.
2. Describe planning, tool calls, memory, and guardrails.
3. Give examples from research assistants and workflow automation.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal. Then draw an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is runaway loops a problem? How does tool calls relate to memory? When would you choose LangGraph or AutoGen?

Study habit: limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.

When you feel ready, compare AI Agents with Responsible AI and Bias and write down three differences.
