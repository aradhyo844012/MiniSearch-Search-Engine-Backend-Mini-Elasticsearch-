Recursion Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Recursion.

- Definition: a technique where a function solves a problem by calling itself on smaller inputs.
- Core ideas: base case; recursive case; call stack; tail calls.
- Use it for: traversing trees; divide and conquer algorithms; parsing nested data.
- Watch out for: missing base cases; exceeding the recursion limit; recomputing the same values.
- Tools: Python sys.setrecursionlimit, debuggers, memoization decorators.
- Quick tip: practice base case until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for recursive case before building anything large.
