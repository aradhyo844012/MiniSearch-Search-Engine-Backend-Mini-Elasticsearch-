Mistakes to Avoid with End-to-End Testing

Everyone learns End-to-End Testing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: flaky tests. It usually appears when someone skips browser automation and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow runs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of checking login and checkout flows. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: brittle selectors. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on selectors to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: cover only the most important user journeys.
