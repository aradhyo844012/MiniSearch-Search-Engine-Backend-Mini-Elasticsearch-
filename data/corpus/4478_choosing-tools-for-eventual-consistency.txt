Choosing Tools for Eventual Consistency

There is no single best tool for Eventual Consistency. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Amazon DynamoDB is a common first choice, especially for global social feeds. Apache Cassandra suits teams that already work with similar technology, and CRDT libraries is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle shopping cart services well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: assuming reads are always fresh.

As a rule of thumb, decide up front how your app handles stale reads, no matter which tool you use.
