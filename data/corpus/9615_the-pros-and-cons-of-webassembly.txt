The Pros and Cons of WebAssembly

No technology is free of trade-offs, and WebAssembly is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running heavy computation in the browser
- Makes porting existing C or Rust code easier
- Helps with games and image tools

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: crossing the JavaScript boundary too often
- Risk: large binaries
- Risk: limited access to browser APIs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running heavy computation in the browser, and you can manage the risks, WebAssembly is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, measure before moving code to WebAssembly.
