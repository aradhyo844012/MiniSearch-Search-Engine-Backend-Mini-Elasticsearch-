Mistakes to Avoid with System Design Interviews

Everyone learns System Design Interviews partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements. It usually appears when someone skips requirements and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring trade-offs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of preparing for senior roles. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: overcomplicating designs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on components to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes.
