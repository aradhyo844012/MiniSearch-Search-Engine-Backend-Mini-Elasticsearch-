Robotics and ROS Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Robotics and ROS regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit actuators before anything else.
2. Make sure you can test in simulation before moving real hardware.
3. Test the case of warehouse robots early, before the design hardens.
4. Reach for ROS 2 first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Gazebo.
5. Do not skip sensors; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to drones as well as to Robotics and ROS in general.
