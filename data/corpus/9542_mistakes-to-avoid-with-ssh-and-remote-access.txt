Mistakes to Avoid with SSH and Remote Access

Everyone learns SSH and Remote Access partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: password logins left enabled. It usually appears when someone skips key pairs and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: sharing private keys. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of administering servers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring host key warnings. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on config files to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use key-based login and disable password authentication.
