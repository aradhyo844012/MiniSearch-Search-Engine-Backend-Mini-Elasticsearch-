Questions to Ask About Digital Audio in Code Review

Reviewing code that involves Digital Audio is easier with a short list of questions. Keep these in mind as you read the diff.

- Would a test catch aliasing at low sample rates if it appeared later?
- Have we avoided this problem: mixing sample rates?
- Have we considered clipping from too high a level?
- Is bit depth clear to someone reading the code for the first time?
- Is Audacity used consistently, or are we mixing approaches?
- Does this change respect sampling rate, or does it work around it?

Good reviews of work on Digital Audio are specific, kind and focused on waveforms, not on personal style.
