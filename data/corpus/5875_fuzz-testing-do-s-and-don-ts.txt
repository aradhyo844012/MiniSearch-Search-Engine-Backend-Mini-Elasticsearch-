Fuzz Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Fuzz Testing.

Do:
- Learn input generation before anything else.
- Try AFL++ on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep every crashing input as a regression test.
- Test the case of testing parsers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: running without sanitizers.
- Ignore this: crashes that cannot be reproduced.
- Ignore this: ignoring slow inputs.
- Adopt Fuzz Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Fuzz Testing, a little patience with coverage guidance pays off more than any clever shortcut.
