The Pros and Cons of Time Management for Developers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Time Management for Developers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports managing coursework and projects
- Makes meeting deadlines easier
- Helps with reducing stress

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: multitasking
- Risk: underestimating tasks
- Risk: working without breaks

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as managing coursework and projects, and you can manage the risks, Time Management for Developers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, break every task into pieces that fit in an hour or less.
