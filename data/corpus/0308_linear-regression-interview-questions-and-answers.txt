Linear Regression Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Linear Regression.

Q: Explain Linear Regression in simple words.
A: Linear Regression refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data. I would add an example involving predicting prices.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: extrapolating far beyond the data; ignoring outliers; and using correlated features carelessly.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: coefficients, residuals, least squares, and assumptions.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: extrapolating far beyond the data.

Q: How does it connect to Large Language Models?
A: Both belong to the area of Machine Learning, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
