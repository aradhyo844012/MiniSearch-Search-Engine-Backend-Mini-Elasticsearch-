Debugging Dependency Vulnerabilities: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Dependency Vulnerabilities, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: ignoring transitive dependencies, never updating, and trusting packages without checking them.
Step 3: revisit CVE identifiers and version pinning, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Dependabot or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Dependency Vulnerabilities in work such as auditing projects come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, automate update pull requests. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
