Debugging Process Signals and Job Control: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Process Signals and Job Control, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: using kill -9 first, orphaned child processes, and ignoring cleanup in handlers.
Step 3: revisit SIGTERM and SIGKILL and foreground and background jobs, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use kill or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Process Signals and Job Control in work such as stopping runaway programs come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
