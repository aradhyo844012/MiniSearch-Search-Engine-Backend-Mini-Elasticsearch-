Mistakes to Avoid with Code Coverage

Everyone learns Code Coverage partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: chasing 100 percent. It usually appears when someone skips line coverage and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: treating coverage as quality. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of finding untested code. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring branch coverage. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on thresholds to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use coverage to find gaps, not to judge quality.
