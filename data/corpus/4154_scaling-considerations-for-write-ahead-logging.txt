Scaling Considerations for Write-Ahead Logging

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Write-Ahead Logging changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding log records and getting surviving crashes right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for disabling fsync for speed and log files filling the disk, and measure with PostgreSQL WAL before changing anything.

Large scale: crash recovery and fsync move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around feeding replication streams at this stage.

Before you scale, test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
