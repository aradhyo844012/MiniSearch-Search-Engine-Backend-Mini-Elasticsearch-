Practice Exercises for Hashing vs Encryption

These exercises build real understanding of Hashing vs Encryption. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare AES and OpenSSL for protecting data in transit and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Hashing vs Encryption and include the term one-way functions.
Exercise 3: Explain how collision resistance and reversibility differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble storing passwords or verifying file integrity.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Hashing vs Encryption is the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data.
