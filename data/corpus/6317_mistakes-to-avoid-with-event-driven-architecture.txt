Mistakes to Avoid with Event-Driven Architecture

Everyone learns Event-Driven Architecture partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: hard-to-trace flows. It usually appears when someone skips producers and consumers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: duplicate events. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of order pipelines. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unclear event ownership. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on asynchronous handling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: document every event name and schema.
