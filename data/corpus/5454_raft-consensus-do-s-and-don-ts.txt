Raft Consensus Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Raft Consensus.

Do:
- Learn leader election before anything else.
- Try etcd on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use an odd number of nodes.
- Test the case of configuration stores early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: even-sized clusters.
- Ignore this: slow disks causing elections.
- Ignore this: network delays above election timeouts.
- Adopt Raft Consensus just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Raft Consensus, a little patience with log replication pays off more than any clever shortcut.
