Where to Go Next After Learning Rust Ownership

You have met the basics of Rust Ownership. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Regular Expressions, which belongs to the same area as Rust Ownership (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of ownership, borrowing, lifetimes or moves, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around command line tools with Cargo, and note every problem you meet, starting with fighting the borrow checker with needless clones.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Rust Ownership to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
