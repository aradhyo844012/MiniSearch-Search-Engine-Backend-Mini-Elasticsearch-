A Practical Checklist for ETL Pipelines

Use this checklist before you rely on ETL Pipelines in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what ETL Pipelines should accomplish.
2. Document your decisions in the project README.
3. Review the core ideas: extraction, transformation, loading, and scheduling.
4. Review how ETL Pipelines interacts with Data Cleaning.
5. Guard against this risk: poor error reporting.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on transformation.
7. Measure results before and after any change.
8. Guard against this risk: no retry logic.
