Log Aggregation in Real Projects

Reading about Log Aggregation is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: troubleshooting across services. Here, shippers drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against unbounded storage growth.

Scenario two: security investigations. This time indexing matters most, and tools such as Elasticsearch help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: usage analysis. The key idea is retention, and the biggest risk is unstructured logs.

Across all three scenarios, Log Aggregation stays the same idea: collecting logs from many machines into one searchable place. Only the details change.
