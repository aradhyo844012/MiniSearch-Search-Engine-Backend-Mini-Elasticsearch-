Mistakes to Avoid with DHCP

Everyone learns DHCP partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: rogue DHCP servers. It usually appears when someone skips leases and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: exhausted address pools. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of home and office networks. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: conflicting static addresses. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on discover and offer messages to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.
