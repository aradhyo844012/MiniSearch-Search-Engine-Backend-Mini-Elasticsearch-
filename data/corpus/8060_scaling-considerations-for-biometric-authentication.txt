Scaling Considerations for Biometric Authentication

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Biometric Authentication changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding enrollment and getting phone unlock right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for spoofing attacks and biased recognition, and measure with Android BiometricPrompt before changing anything.

Large scale: false accept and false reject rates and liveness detection move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around building access at this stage.

Before you scale, offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
