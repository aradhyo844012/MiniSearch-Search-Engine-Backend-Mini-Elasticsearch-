AI Agents Explained Simply

Here is AI Agents without the jargon: it is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.

Think of AI Agents as an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: planning, tool calls, memory, and guardrails. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with planning, then add tool calls.

You will meet AI Agents in work such as research assistants, workflow automation, and customer support.

One practical tip is to limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
