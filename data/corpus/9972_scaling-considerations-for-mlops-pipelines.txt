Scaling Considerations for MLOps Pipelines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how MLOps Pipelines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding data versioning and getting repeatable model releases right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for untracked data changes and manual deployment steps, and measure with MLflow before changing anything.

Large scale: the model registry and continuous delivery move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around tracking experiments at this stage.

Before you scale, version both the code and the data. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
