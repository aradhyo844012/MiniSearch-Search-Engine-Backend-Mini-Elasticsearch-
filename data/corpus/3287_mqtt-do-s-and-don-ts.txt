MQTT Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with MQTT.

Do:
- Learn publish and subscribe before anything else.
- Try Mosquitto on a small example.
- Make it a habit to design topic names like a clear folder structure.
- Test the case of sensor networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: unsecured brokers.
- Ignore this: messy topic names.
- Ignore this: wrong quality of service choices.
- Adopt MQTT just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with MQTT, a little patience with topics pays off more than any clever shortcut.
