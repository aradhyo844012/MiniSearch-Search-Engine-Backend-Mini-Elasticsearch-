Skills to Build Around Graph Theory Basics

Knowing the definition of Graph Theory Basics is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with vertices and edges and degree, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with NetworkX on a Graph Theory Basics task, and try at least one alternative such as Graphviz.

Judgment: learn to decide when Graph Theory Basics fits a problem such as social networks, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a map of towns and the roads between them.

Habits: draw a small example before writing code, and write down what you learn after each project.
