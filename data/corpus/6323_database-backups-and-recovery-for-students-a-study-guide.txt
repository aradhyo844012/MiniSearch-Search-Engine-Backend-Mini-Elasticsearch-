Database Backups and Recovery for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Database Backups and Recovery.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Database Backups and Recovery in your own words.
2. Describe full and incremental backups, recovery point objective, recovery time objective, and restore testing.
3. Give examples from recovering deleted tables and migrating servers.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Database Backups and Recovery is the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures. Then draw a spare house key kept at a trusted neighbor's home on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is never testing restores a problem? How does recovery point objective relate to recovery time objective? When would you choose pg_dump or mysqldump?

Study habit: schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.

When you feel ready, compare Database Backups and Recovery with LSM Trees and write down three differences.
