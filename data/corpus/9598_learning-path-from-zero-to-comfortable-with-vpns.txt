Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with VPNs

This path is for a learner who has never studied VPNs before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (VPNs is encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet) and learn tunneling and encryption.

Stage 2, hands-on: install WireGuard, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving remote work access.

Stage 3, depth: study authentication and split tunneling, then deliberately explore this problem: assuming it makes you anonymous.

Stage 4, application: apply VPNs to linking offices, compare OpenVPN with Tailscale, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach VPNs to someone else, and then move on to HTTP/2 and HTTP/3.
