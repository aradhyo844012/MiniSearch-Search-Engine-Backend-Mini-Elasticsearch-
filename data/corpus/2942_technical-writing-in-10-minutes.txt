Technical Writing in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Technical Writing.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn audience, structure, examples, and editing.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine giving directions to a stranger. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through documentation, blog posts, and design proposals.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for assuming too much knowledge and burying the main point, then try Markdown for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to state the main point in the first two sentences.
