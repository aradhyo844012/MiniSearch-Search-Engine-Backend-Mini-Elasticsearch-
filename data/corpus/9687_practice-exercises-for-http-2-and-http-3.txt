Practice Exercises for HTTP/2 and HTTP/3

These exercises build real understanding of HTTP/2 and HTTP/3. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick curl with HTTP/3 support and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble faster page loads or improving mobile performance.
Exercise 3: Compare Chrome DevTools and NGINX for reducing connection overhead and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 and include the term multiplexing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 is newer versions of the web protocol that load pages faster over fewer connections.
