Combining Data Lakes with Data Anonymization in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Data Lakes and Data Anonymization can work together in a single project.

Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. Data Anonymization is removing or altering personal details in data so people cannot be identified.

Imagine a project that involves storing logs and events and sharing datasets with researchers. Data Lakes brings raw storage, while Data Anonymization brings masking.

A sensible plan is to start with Amazon S3 for the Data Lakes part and ARX for the Data Anonymization part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: no catalog or ownership on one side, and removing too little on the other.

Picture the whole as a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment, working alongside blurring faces in a crowd photo while keeping the scene.

Two habits help: first, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner; second, test whether combining fields could identify a person.
