Time Management for Developers Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Time Management for Developers regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Time Management for Developers so the next person understands the decision.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit time blocking before anything else.
3. Test the case of managing coursework and projects early, before the design hardens.
4. Do not skip prioritization; everything else builds on it.
5. Be careful with this one: multitasking.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to meeting deadlines as well.
