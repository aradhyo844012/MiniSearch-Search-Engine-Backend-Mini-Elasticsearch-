Mistakes to Avoid with CQRS

Everyone learns CQRS partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: adding it to simple apps. It usually appears when someone skips commands and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: read models drifting out of sync. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of high-read dashboards. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: duplicated logic. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on eventual consistency to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge.
