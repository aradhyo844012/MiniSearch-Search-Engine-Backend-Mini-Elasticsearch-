Mistakes to Avoid with Combinatorics

Everyone learns Combinatorics partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: double counting. It usually appears when someone skips permutations and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: mixing order and no-order cases. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of analyzing algorithms. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring repetition. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on inclusion-exclusion to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test counting formulas on tiny cases first.
