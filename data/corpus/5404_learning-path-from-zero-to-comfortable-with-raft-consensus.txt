Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Raft Consensus

This path is for a learner who has never studied Raft Consensus before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Raft Consensus is an algorithm that lets a group of servers agree on a shared log of operations) and learn leader election and log replication.

Stage 2, hands-on: install etcd, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving configuration stores.

Stage 3, depth: study terms and majority quorum, then deliberately explore this problem: even-sized clusters.

Stage 4, application: apply Raft Consensus to distributed locks, compare Consul with HashiCorp Raft library, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Raft Consensus to someone else, and then move on to CAP Theorem.
