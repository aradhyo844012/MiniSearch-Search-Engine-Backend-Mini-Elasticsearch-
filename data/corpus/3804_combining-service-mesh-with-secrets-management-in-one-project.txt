Combining Service Mesh with Secrets Management in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Service Mesh and Secrets Management can work together in a single project.

Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.

Imagine a project that involves securing service traffic and database credentials. Service Mesh brings sidecar proxies, while Secrets Management brings vaults.

A sensible plan is to start with Istio for the Service Mesh part and HashiCorp Vault for the Secrets Management part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: adopting it too early on one side, and long-lived shared credentials on the other.

Picture the whole as a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings, working alongside a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet.

Two habits help: first, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful; second, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.
