Where to Go Next After Learning Bluetooth Low Energy

You have met the basics of Bluetooth Low Energy. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Native, which belongs to the same area as Bluetooth Low Energy (Mobile, Embedded and IoT) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of advertising, GATT services, characteristics or pairing, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around smart locks with nRF Connect, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring connection limits.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Bluetooth Low Energy to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
