The Pros and Cons of Mocking and Stubbing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Mocking and Stubbing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports isolating units
- Makes simulating failures easier
- Helps with avoiding network calls

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: over-mocking
- Risk: tests that mirror the implementation
- Risk: mocks that drift from reality

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as isolating units, and you can manage the risks, Mocking and Stubbing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
