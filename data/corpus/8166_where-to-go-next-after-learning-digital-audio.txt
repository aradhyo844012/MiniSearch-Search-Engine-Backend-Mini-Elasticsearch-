Where to Go Next After Learning Digital Audio

You have met the basics of Digital Audio. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Video Codecs, which belongs to the same area as Digital Audio (Graphics and Multimedia) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sampling rate, bit depth, waveforms or the Nyquist limit, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around voice calls with Audacity, and note every problem you meet, starting with clipping from too high a level.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Digital Audio to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
