Debugging Finite Automata: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Finite Automata, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: missing transitions, confusing deterministic and non-deterministic machines, and state explosion.
Step 3: revisit states and transitions, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use JFLAP or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Finite Automata in work such as tokenizers come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, draw the state diagram before writing any code. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
