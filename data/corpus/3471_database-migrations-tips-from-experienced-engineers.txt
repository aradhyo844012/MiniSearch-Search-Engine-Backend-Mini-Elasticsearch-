Database Migrations Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Database Migrations regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of adding columns safely early, before the design hardens.
2. Do not skip up and down scripts; everything else builds on it.
3. Write down why you chose Database Migrations so the next person understands the decision.
4. Make sure you can split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
5. Be careful with this one: locking large tables.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to renaming tables as well.
