Scaling Considerations for Computer Vision Basics

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Computer Vision Basics changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding pixels and getting face detection right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for lighting changes and biased datasets, and measure with OpenCV before changing anything.

Large scale: feature detection and classification move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around document scanning at this stage.

Before you scale, visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
