Where to Go Next After Learning Hyperparameter Tuning

You have met the basics of Hyperparameter Tuning. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Random Forests, which belongs to the same area as Hyperparameter Tuning (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of search spaces, grid search, random search or validation scores, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around comparing algorithms with Optuna, and note every problem you meet, starting with tuning on the test set.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Hyperparameter Tuning to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
