Choosing Tools for CAP Theorem

There is no single best tool for CAP Theorem. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Apache Cassandra is a common first choice, especially for choosing database types. etcd suits teams that already work with similar technology, and ZooKeeper is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle designing multi-region services well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: treating it as a three-way menu.

As a rule of thumb, ask what your users prefer during a failure: stale answers or errors, no matter which tool you use.
