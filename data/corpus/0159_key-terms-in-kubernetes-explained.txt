Key Terms in Kubernetes Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Kubernetes much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Kubernetes. The term refers to a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications.

pods: a key term in Kubernetes. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
deployments: a key term in Kubernetes. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
services: a key term in Kubernetes. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
ingress: a key term in Kubernetes. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.

With steady practice, Kubernetes will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
