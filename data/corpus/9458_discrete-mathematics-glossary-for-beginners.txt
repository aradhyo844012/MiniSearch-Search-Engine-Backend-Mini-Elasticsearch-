Discrete Mathematics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Discrete Mathematics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Discrete Mathematics: the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.
sets: a core term, most visible in algorithm analysis.
logic: a core term that beginners often confuse with relations.
relations: a core term that connects to proofs.
proofs: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python sets: a tool commonly used for database theory.

Come back to this Discrete Mathematics list whenever documentation for proof assistants uses a word you do not recognize.
