Understanding Service Workers Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager.

In that picture, registration is the first rule you follow, install and activate events is the next step, and cache storage and fetch handlers keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Service Workers is scripts that run in the background of a browser and intercept network requests, and it involves details such as caching stale files forever that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Workbox to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain offline support to a friend.
