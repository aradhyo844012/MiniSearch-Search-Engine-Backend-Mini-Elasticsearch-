Mistakes to Avoid with Software Estimation

Everyone learns Software Estimation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: treating estimates as promises. It usually appears when someone skips story points and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring unknowns. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sprint planning. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: estimating alone. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on velocity to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: give ranges instead of single numbers.
