Feature Flags Explained Simply

If Feature Flags sounds intimidating, relax. It is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Feature Flags as light switches wired to every room of a house. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: flag evaluation, targeting rules, gradual rollout, and flag cleanup. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with flag evaluation, then add targeting rules.

You will meet Feature Flags in work such as dark launches, A/B tests, and emergency kill switches.

One practical tip is to give every flag an owner and a removal date.

Once Feature Flags feels comfortable, read about Autoscaling, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
