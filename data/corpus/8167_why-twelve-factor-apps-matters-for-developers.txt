Why Twelve-Factor Apps Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Twelve-Factor Apps make better decisions, because Twelve-Factor Apps is a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly.

Three situations show why it matters. First, building web services. Second, deploying to platforms. Third, keeping environments consistent. In each case, a solid grasp of config in the environment and stateless processes separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: storing state on local disks, hard-coded config, and long start-up times.

Good tools exist, including Heroku, Docker, and Cloud Foundry, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
