Mistakes to Avoid with Configuration Management

Everyone learns Configuration Management partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: committing secrets. It usually appears when someone skips environment variables and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: inconsistent defaults. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of separating development and production. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: undocumented settings. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on secrets handling to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing.
