Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Greedy Algorithms

This path is for a learner who has never studied Greedy Algorithms before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Greedy Algorithms is algorithms that build a solution by always taking the locally best choice) and learn greedy choice property and optimal substructure.

Stage 2, hands-on: install pen and paper proofs, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving interval scheduling.

Stage 3, depth: study exchange arguments and local versus global optimum, then deliberately explore this problem: assuming greedy always works.

Stage 4, application: apply Greedy Algorithms to Huffman coding, compare Python sorted with unit tests, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Greedy Algorithms to someone else, and then move on to Backtracking.
