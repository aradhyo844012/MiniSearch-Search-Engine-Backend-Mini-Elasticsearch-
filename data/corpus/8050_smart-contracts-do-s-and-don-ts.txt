Smart Contracts Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Smart Contracts.

Do:
- Learn contract code before anything else.
- Try Solidity on a small example.
- Make it a habit to audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
- Test the case of token systems early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: bugs that cannot be patched.
- Ignore this: reentrancy attacks.
- Ignore this: high gas costs.
- Adopt Smart Contracts just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Smart Contracts, a little patience with transactions pays off more than any clever shortcut.
