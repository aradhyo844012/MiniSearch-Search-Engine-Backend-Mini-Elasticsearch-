Single Sign-On in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Single Sign-On.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Single Sign-On is letting users log in once and access several applications.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn identity provider, SAML, OpenID Connect, and session lifetime.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a festival wristband that opens every gate. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through company intranets, reducing password fatigue, and centralized access control.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for a single point of failure and misconfigured redirects, then try Okta for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to plan for identity provider outages.
