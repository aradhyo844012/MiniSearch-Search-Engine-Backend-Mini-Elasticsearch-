The Pros and Cons of Rust Ownership

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Rust Ownership is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports systems programming
- Makes command line tools easier
- Helps with safe concurrent code

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: fighting the borrow checker with needless clones
- Risk: confusing moves and copies
- Risk: overly complex lifetime annotations

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as systems programming, and you can manage the risks, Rust Ownership is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, read the compiler's error messages closely because they often explain the fix.
