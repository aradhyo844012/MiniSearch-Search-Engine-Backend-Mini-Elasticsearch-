Practice Exercises for Raspberry Pi

These exercises build real understanding of Raspberry Pi. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Python GPIO libraries and SSH for small servers and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Raspberry Pi and include the term GPIO pins.
Exercise 3: Explain how SD card storage and headless setup differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Pick Raspberry Pi OS and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: List three situations from your own experience that resemble home automation or learning Linux.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects.
