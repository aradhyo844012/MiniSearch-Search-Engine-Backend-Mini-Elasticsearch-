Is Linear Algebra for Programmers Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Linear Algebra for Programmers, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Linear Algebra for Programmers is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning.

It is a good fit when your work involves 3D graphics or machine learning, and when your team understands vectors.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn matrices, or when you cannot manage these risks: mixing row and column conventions, shape mismatches, and forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether dot products and eigenvalues are part of the answer. If they are, Linear Algebra for Programmers is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with recommendation systems and check shapes before multiplying.
