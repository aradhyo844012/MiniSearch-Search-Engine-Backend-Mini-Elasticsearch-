High Availability Design Explained Simply

Here is High Availability Design without the jargon: it is designing systems so they keep working when parts fail.

Think of High Availability Design as a bridge with several cables so one breaking does not matter. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: redundancy, failover, health checks, and no single point of failure. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with redundancy, then add failover.

You will meet High Availability Design in work such as online stores, banking systems, and healthcare software.

One practical tip is to list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
