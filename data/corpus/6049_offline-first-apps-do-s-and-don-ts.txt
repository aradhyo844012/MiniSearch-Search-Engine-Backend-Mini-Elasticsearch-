Offline-First Apps Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Offline-First Apps.

Do:
- Learn local storage before anything else.
- Try SQLite on a small example.
- Make it a habit to show users which changes have not synced yet.
- Test the case of field service tools early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: unresolved sync conflicts.
- Ignore this: unbounded local data.
- Ignore this: poor feedback about pending changes.
- Adopt Offline-First Apps just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Offline-First Apps, a little patience with sync queues pays off more than any clever shortcut.
