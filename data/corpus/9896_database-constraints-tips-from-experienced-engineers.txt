Database Constraints Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Database Constraints regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip primary keys; everything else builds on it.
2. Test the case of preventing bad data early, before the design hardens.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit foreign keys before anything else.
4. Be careful with this one: skipping constraints for speed.
5. Write down why you chose Database Constraints so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to protecting relationships as well as to Database Constraints in general.
