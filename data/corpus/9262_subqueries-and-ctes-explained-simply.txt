Subqueries and CTEs Explained Simply

Here is Subqueries and CTEs without the jargon: it is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.

Think of Subqueries and CTEs as solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: subqueries, WITH clauses, correlated queries, and readability. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with subqueries, then add WITH clauses.

You will meet Subqueries and CTEs in work such as breaking complex reports into steps, filtering with computed results, and walking recursive hierarchies.

One practical tip is to name each CTE after what it returns.
