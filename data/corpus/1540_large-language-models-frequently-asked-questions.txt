Large Language Models: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Large Language Models?
A: The term Large Language Models refers to very large neural networks trained on text that can generate and analyze language.

Q: Why do developers use Large Language Models?
A: Common uses include drafting text, answering questions, and assisting with code.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with tokens and attention, then move on to pretraining and prompts.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: trusting outputs without checking; vague prompts; and sending sensitive data carelessly.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Hugging Face, API playgrounds, and evaluation harnesses.
