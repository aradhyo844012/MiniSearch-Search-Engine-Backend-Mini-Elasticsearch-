Choosing Tools for Disk Partitioning and Mounting

There is no single best tool for Disk Partitioning and Mounting. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

lsblk is a common first choice, especially for adding a new drive. fdisk suits teams that already work with similar technology, and mount is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Disk Partitioning and Mounting by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle separating data from the system well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Disk Partitioning and Mounting, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: wrong device names.

As a rule of thumb, double-check device names with lsblk before formatting, no matter which tool you use.
