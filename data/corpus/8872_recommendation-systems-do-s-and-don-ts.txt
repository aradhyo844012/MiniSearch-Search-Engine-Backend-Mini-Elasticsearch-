Recommendation Systems Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Recommendation Systems.

Do:
- Learn collaborative filtering before anything else.
- Try Surprise on a small example.
- Make it a habit to evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
- Test the case of streaming services early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: feedback loops.
- Ignore this: popularity bias.
- Ignore this: ignoring new users.
- Adopt Recommendation Systems just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Recommendation Systems, a little patience with content-based filtering pays off more than any clever shortcut.
