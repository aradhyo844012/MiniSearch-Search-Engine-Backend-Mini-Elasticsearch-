Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with DHCP

This path is for a learner who has never studied DHCP before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (DHCP is a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings) and learn leases and address pools.

Stage 2, hands-on: install dnsmasq, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving home and office networks.

Stage 3, depth: study reservations and discover and offer messages, then deliberately explore this problem: rogue DHCP servers.

Stage 4, application: apply DHCP to guest Wi-Fi, compare ISC Kea with Wireshark, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach DHCP to someone else, and then move on to Network Troubleshooting.
