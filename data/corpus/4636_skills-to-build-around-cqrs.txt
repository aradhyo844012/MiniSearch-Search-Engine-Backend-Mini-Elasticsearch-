Skills to Build Around CQRS

Knowing the definition of CQRS is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with commands and queries, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Axon Framework on a CQRS task, and try at least one alternative such as MediatR.

Judgment: learn to decide when CQRS fits a problem such as high-read dashboards, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a restaurant with one counter for orders and another for pickup.

Habits: start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge, and write down what you learn after each project.
