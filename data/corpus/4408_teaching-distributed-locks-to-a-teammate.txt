Teaching Distributed Locks to a Teammate

Explaining Distributed Locks to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a single key to a shared meeting room that expires automatically. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Distributed Locks is mechanisms that ensure only one process at a time performs a critical action across machines. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving running scheduled jobs once, naming lease times and fencing tokens as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain lock owners in Distributed Locks back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, locks that never expire, and suggest this habit: always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
