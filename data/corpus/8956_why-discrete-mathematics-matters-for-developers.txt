Why Discrete Mathematics Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Discrete Mathematics make better decisions, because Discrete Mathematics is the mathematics of countable structures such as sets, logic and graphs.

Three situations show why it matters. First, algorithm analysis. Second, database theory. Third, cryptography. In each case, a solid grasp of sets and logic separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: skipping proofs, confusing sets with sequences, and careless notation.

Good tools exist, including Python sets, proof assistants, and textbooks and problem sets, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
