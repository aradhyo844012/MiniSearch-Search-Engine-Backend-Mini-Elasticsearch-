Random Forests Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Random Forests. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: ensembles of many decision trees whose predictions are combined.
Key terms: bagging, feature randomness, voting, and out-of-bag error.
Typical uses: tabular predictions, feature importance ranking, and strong baseline models.
Risks: treating importance scores as causes, very large forests being slow, and ignoring class imbalance.
Tools: scikit-learn, XGBoost, and LightGBM.

Mental picture: asking many different experts and taking the majority view.

Habit to build: tune the number of trees only after the basics work.

See also: Reinforcement Learning, which sits close to Random Forests in the area of AI and Machine Learning.
