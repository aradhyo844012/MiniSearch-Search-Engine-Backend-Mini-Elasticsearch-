NoSQL Databases Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about NoSQL Databases.

Q: Explain NoSQL Databases in simple words.
A: NoSQL Databases refers to a broad family of databases that do not use the traditional relational table model. I would add an example involving flexible schemas.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: MongoDB, Cassandra, and Redis.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with high write throughput, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as document stores, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: How does it connect to Database Replication?
A: Both belong to the area of Databases, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
