How to Learn SQL Joins in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with SQL Joins. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about SQL Joins, they usually mean operations that combine rows from two or more tables based on related columns. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn inner join and left join, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with PostgreSQL on a small task such as linking customers to orders.
Week 3: build a mini project around building reports.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as forgetting the join condition, and explain SQL Joins to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include PostgreSQL, MySQL Workbench, and DBeaver.

In short, careful study of SQL Joins pays off. Spend time on inner join and left join, and the rest will follow.
