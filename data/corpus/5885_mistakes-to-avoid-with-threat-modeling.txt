Mistakes to Avoid with Threat Modeling

Everyone learns Threat Modeling partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: doing it once and never again. It usually appears when someone skips assets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: ignoring data flows. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of designing new features. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: focusing only on technical threats. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on mitigations to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: draw the data flow diagram first.
