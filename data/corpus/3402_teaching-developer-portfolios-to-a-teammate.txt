Teaching Developer Portfolios to a Teammate

Explaining Developer Portfolios to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a shop window displaying your best work. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving applying for internships, naming projects and README quality as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain live demos in Developer Portfolios back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, too many unfinished projects, and suggest this habit: feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
