Mistakes to Avoid with Progressive Web Apps

Everyone learns Progressive Web Apps partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: ignoring platform limitations. It usually appears when someone skips web app manifest and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: poor offline fallbacks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of offline-friendly tools. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: heavy first loads. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on responsive design to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test the app in airplane mode.
