Why Wearable Technology Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Wearable Technology make better decisions, because Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.

Three situations show why it matters. First, fitness tracking. Second, notifications. Third, health monitoring. In each case, a solid grasp of sensors and low-power design separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: battery drain, inaccurate sensor readings, and privacy of personal data.

Good tools exist, including Wear OS, watchOS, and Bluetooth Low Energy, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
