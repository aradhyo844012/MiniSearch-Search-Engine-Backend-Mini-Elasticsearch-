GPUs and Parallelism Glossary for Beginners

New words can make GPUs and Parallelism feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

GPUs and Parallelism: processors with thousands of small cores that handle many similar calculations at once.
threads and blocks: a core term, most visible in training neural networks.
kernels: a core term that beginners often confuse with memory transfers.
memory transfers: a core term that connects to SIMD.
SIMD: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
CUDA: a tool commonly used for rendering graphics.

Come back to this GPUs and Parallelism list whenever documentation for PyTorch uses a word you do not recognize.
