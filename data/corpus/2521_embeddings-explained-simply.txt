Embeddings Explained Simply

Here is Embeddings without the jargon: it is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.

Think of Embeddings as coordinates on a map where similar places are close together. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: vectors, cosine similarity, dimensionality, and nearest neighbor search. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with vectors, then add cosine similarity.

People reach for Embeddings when they work on semantic search, recommendations, and clustering text.

One practical tip is to use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.

After Embeddings, a natural next topic is Convolutional Neural Networks.
