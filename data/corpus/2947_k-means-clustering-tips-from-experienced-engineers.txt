K-Means Clustering Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with K-Means Clustering regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose K-Means Clustering so the next person understands the decision.
2. Reach for scikit-learn first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as NumPy.
3. Make sure you can scale features before clustering.
4. Be careful with this one: choosing k blindly.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit distance measures before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to image compression as well.
