Scaling Considerations for End-to-End Testing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how End-to-End Testing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding browser automation and getting checking login and checkout flows right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for flaky tests and slow runs, and measure with Playwright before changing anything.

Large scale: test data and selectors move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around release verification at this stage.

Before you scale, cover only the most important user journeys. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
