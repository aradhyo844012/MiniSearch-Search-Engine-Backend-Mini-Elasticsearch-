Mistakes to Avoid with Vector Graphics (SVG)

Everyone learns Vector Graphics (SVG) partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: overly complex paths. It usually appears when someone skips paths and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: a missing viewBox. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of logos. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: inconsistent stroke widths. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on transforms to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly.
