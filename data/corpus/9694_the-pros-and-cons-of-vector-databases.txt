The Pros and Cons of Vector Databases

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Vector Databases is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports semantic search
- Makes recommendations easier
- Helps with retrieval for AI assistants

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mixing embedding models
- Risk: ignoring recall trade-offs
- Risk: oversized vectors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as semantic search, and you can manage the risks, Vector Databases is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, measure recall on a small labeled test set.
