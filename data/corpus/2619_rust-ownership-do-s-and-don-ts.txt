Rust Ownership Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Rust Ownership.

Do:
- Learn ownership before anything else.
- Try Cargo on a small example.
- Make it a habit to read the compiler's error messages closely because they often explain the fix.
- Test the case of systems programming early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: fighting the borrow checker with needless clones.
- Ignore this: confusing moves and copies.
- Ignore this: overly complex lifetime annotations.
- Adopt Rust Ownership just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Rust Ownership, a little patience with borrowing pays off more than any clever shortcut.
