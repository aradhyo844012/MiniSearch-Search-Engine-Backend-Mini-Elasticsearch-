Scaling Considerations for Reinforcement Learning

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Reinforcement Learning changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding agent and getting game playing right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for badly designed rewards and slow training, and measure with Gymnasium before changing anything.

Large scale: reward and policy move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around robot control at this stage.

Before you scale, start with simple environments. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
