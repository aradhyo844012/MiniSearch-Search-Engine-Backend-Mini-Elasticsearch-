Combinatorics Explained Simply

Here is Combinatorics without the jargon: it is the art of counting arrangements and selections.

Think of Combinatorics as counting the ways to seat guests at a table. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: permutations, combinations, the pigeonhole principle, and inclusion-exclusion. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with permutations, then add combinations.

You will meet Combinatorics in work such as analyzing algorithms, probability, and estimating password strength.

One practical tip is to test counting formulas on tiny cases first.

Once Combinatorics feels comfortable, read about Number Theory and RSA, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
