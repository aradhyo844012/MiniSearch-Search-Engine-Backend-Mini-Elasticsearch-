Where to Go Next After Learning Package Managers

You have met the basics of Package Managers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Process Signals and Job Control, which belongs to the same area as Package Managers (Linux and System Administration) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of repositories, dependency resolution, versions or signed packages, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around keeping servers updated with apt, and note every problem you meet, starting with mixing sources that conflict.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Package Managers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
