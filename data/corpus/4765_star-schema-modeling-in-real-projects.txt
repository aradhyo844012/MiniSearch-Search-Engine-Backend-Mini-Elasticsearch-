Star Schema Modeling in Real Projects

Reading about Star Schema Modeling is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: business intelligence reports. Here, fact tables drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against mixing different grains.

Scenario two: data warehouses. This time dimension tables matters most, and tools such as dbt help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: fast aggregations. The key idea is surrogate keys, and the biggest risk is over-normalized dimensions.

Across all three scenarios, Star Schema Modeling stays the same idea: a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables. Only the details change.
