Combining Object-Relational Mappers with Dependency Injection in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Object-Relational Mappers and Dependency Injection can work together in a single project.

Object-Relational Mappers is libraries that map database tables to objects in code. Dependency Injection is a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them.

Imagine a project that involves building CRUD apps quickly and easier unit testing. Object-Relational Mappers brings models, while Dependency Injection brings constructor injection.

A sensible plan is to start with SQLAlchemy for the Object-Relational Mappers part and Spring for the Dependency Injection part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: loading entire tables on one side, and circular dependencies on the other.

Picture the whole as a translator between the language of tables and the language of objects, working alongside a chef who is handed ingredients instead of shopping for them.

Two habits help: first, log the generated SQL during development; second, inject interfaces so tests can use simple fakes.
