Combining Data Lakes with A/B Testing in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Data Lakes and A/B Testing can work together in a single project.

Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. A/B Testing is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better.

Imagine a project that involves storing logs and events and testing page designs. Data Lakes brings raw storage, while A/B Testing brings control and variant.

A sensible plan is to start with Amazon S3 for the Data Lakes part and Optimizely for the A/B Testing part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: no catalog or ownership on one side, and testing too many variants on the other.

Picture the whole as a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment, working alongside taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests.

Two habits help: first, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner; second, decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
