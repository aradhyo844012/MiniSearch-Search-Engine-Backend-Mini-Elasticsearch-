Choosing Tools for System Design Interviews

There is no single best tool for System Design Interviews. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

whiteboard sketches is a common first choice, especially for preparing for senior roles. Excalidraw suits teams that already work with similar technology, and system design primers is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle practicing architectural thinking well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: jumping into solutions before clarifying requirements.

As a rule of thumb, clarify requirements and scale before drawing boxes, no matter which tool you use.
