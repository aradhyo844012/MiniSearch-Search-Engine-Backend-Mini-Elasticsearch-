Mistakes to Avoid with Column-Oriented Storage

Everyone learns Column-Oriented Storage partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow single-row lookups. It usually appears when someone skips column chunks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: many small writes. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of analytics dashboards. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: wide tables with unused columns. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on predicate pushdown to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: select only the columns you really need.
