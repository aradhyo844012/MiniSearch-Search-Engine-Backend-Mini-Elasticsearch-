Is Idempotency Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Idempotency, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once.

It is a good fit when your work involves payment APIs or message processing, and when your team understands idempotency keys.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn safe retries, or when you cannot manage these risks: keys that expire too soon, side effects outside the guarded step, and reusing keys for different requests.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether deduplication and at-least-once delivery are part of the answer. If they are, Idempotency is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with retrying failed requests and store the result with the key so retries return the same response.
