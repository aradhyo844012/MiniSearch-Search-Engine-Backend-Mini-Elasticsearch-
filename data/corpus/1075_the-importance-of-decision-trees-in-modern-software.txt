The Importance of Decision Trees in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Decision Trees until something goes wrong without it. The term Decision Trees refers to models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Simple baselines are worth building first because they show what improvement is realistic.

People use Decision Trees for classifying customers, for explaining predictions, and for baseline models.

Three issues come up again and again: deep trees that overfit; ignoring class imbalance; and unstable results from small data changes. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

Decision Trees and Overfitting are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Decision Trees.
