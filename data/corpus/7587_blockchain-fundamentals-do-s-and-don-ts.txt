Blockchain Fundamentals Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Blockchain Fundamentals.

Do:
- Learn blocks before anything else.
- Try Bitcoin Core on a small example.
- Make it a habit to ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
- Test the case of digital currencies early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: treating it as a database replacement.
- Ignore this: slow throughput.
- Ignore this: the energy use of some designs.
- Adopt Blockchain Fundamentals just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Blockchain Fundamentals, a little patience with hashes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
