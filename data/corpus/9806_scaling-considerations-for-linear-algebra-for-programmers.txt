Scaling Considerations for Linear Algebra for Programmers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Linear Algebra for Programmers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding vectors and getting 3D graphics right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mixing row and column conventions and shape mismatches, and measure with NumPy before changing anything.

Large scale: dot products and eigenvalues move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around machine learning at this stage.

Before you scale, check shapes before multiplying. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
