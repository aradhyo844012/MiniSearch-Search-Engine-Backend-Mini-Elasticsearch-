Wearable Technology in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Wearable Technology.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn sensors, low-power design, companion apps, and data syncing.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through fitness tracking, notifications, and health monitoring.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for battery drain and inaccurate sensor readings, then try Wear OS for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to design interactions that take only a few seconds.
