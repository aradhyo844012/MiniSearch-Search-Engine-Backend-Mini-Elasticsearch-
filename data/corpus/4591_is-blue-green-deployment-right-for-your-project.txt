Is Blue-Green Deployment Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Blue-Green Deployment, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Blue-Green Deployment is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one.

It is a good fit when your work involves zero-downtime releases or quick rollbacks, and when your team understands blue and green environments.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn traffic switching, or when you cannot manage these risks: database schema mismatches, doubling infrastructure costs, and forgetting to drain old connections.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether rollback and database compatibility are part of the answer. If they are, Blue-Green Deployment is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with testing in production-like settings and keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
