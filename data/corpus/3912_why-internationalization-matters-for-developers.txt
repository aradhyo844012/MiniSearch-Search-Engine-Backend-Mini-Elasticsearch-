Why Internationalization Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Internationalization make better decisions, because Internationalization is designing software so it can be adapted to different languages and regions.

Three situations show why it matters. First, global products. Second, multi-language websites. Third, regional pricing. In each case, a solid grasp of locale detection and translation files separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: hard-coded strings, text that overflows layouts, and assuming left-to-right text.

Good tools exist, including i18next, the Intl API, and Crowdin, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
