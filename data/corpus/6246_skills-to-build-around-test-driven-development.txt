Skills to Build Around Test-Driven Development

Knowing the definition of Test-Driven Development is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with red, green, refactor and small steps, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with pytest on a Test-Driven Development task, and try at least one alternative such as JUnit.

Judgment: learn to decide when Test-Driven Development fits a problem such as designing small modules, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example writing the exam questions before teaching the lesson.

Habits: keep each red-green cycle to a few minutes, and write down what you learn after each project.
