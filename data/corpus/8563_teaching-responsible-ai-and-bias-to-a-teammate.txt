Teaching Responsible AI and Bias to a Teammate

Explaining Responsible AI and Bias to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a referee who checks that the game is played fairly for every team. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Responsible AI and Bias is practices for building AI systems that are fair, transparent and safe. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving hiring tools, naming bias in data and fairness metrics as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain transparency in Responsible AI and Bias back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, assuming data is neutral, and suggest this habit: test performance separately for different groups.
