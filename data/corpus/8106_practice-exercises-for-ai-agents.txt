Practice Exercises for AI Agents

These exercises build real understanding of AI Agents. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how memory and guardrails differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Pick LangGraph and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble research assistants or workflow automation.
Exercise 4: Compare AutoGen and tool-calling APIs for customer support and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: runaway loops. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.
