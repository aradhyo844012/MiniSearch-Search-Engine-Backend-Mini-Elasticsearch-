The Pros and Cons of Feature Flags

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Feature Flags is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports dark launches
- Makes A/B tests easier
- Helps with emergency kill switches

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: never removing old flags
- Risk: flags that depend on each other
- Risk: untested flag combinations

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as dark launches, and you can manage the risks, Feature Flags is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, give every flag an owner and a removal date.
