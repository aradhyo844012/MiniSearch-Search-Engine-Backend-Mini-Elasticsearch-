Build Systems in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Build Systems.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Build Systems is tools that turn source code into runnable programs automatically.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn targets, dependencies, incremental builds, and build caches.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine an assembly line that only rebuilds the parts that changed. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through compiling large projects, packaging software, and running repeatable pipelines.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for slow full rebuilds and undeclared dependencies, then try Make for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to make builds reproducible on any machine.
