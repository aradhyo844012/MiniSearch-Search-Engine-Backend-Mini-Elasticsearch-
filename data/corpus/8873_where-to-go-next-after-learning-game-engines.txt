Where to Go Next After Learning Game Engines

You have met the basics of Game Engines. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Physics Engines, which belongs to the same area as Game Engines (Game Development) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of scenes, assets, scripting or build pipelines, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around simulations with Unity, and note every problem you meet, starting with choosing an engine by hype.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Game Engines to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
