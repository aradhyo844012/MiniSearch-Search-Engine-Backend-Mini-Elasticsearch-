Object-Relational Mappers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Object-Relational Mappers.

Do:
- Learn models before anything else.
- Try SQLAlchemy on a small example.
- Make it a habit to log the generated SQL during development.
- Test the case of building CRUD apps quickly early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: N+1 queries.
- Ignore this: loading entire tables.
- Ignore this: hiding slow SQL.
- Adopt Object-Relational Mappers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Object-Relational Mappers, a little patience with queries pays off more than any clever shortcut.
