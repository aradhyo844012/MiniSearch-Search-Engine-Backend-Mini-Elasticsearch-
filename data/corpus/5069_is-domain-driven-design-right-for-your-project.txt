Is Domain-Driven Design Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Domain-Driven Design, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Domain-Driven Design is an approach to software that models the code around the real business domain.

It is a good fit when your work involves complex business systems or aligning developers with domain experts, and when your team understands ubiquitous language.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn bounded contexts, or when you cannot manage these risks: using it for simple CRUD apps, models that never match the business, and ignoring context boundaries.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether aggregates and entities and value objects are part of the answer. If they are, Domain-Driven Design is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with splitting large systems and agree on shared terms with domain experts before drawing classes.
