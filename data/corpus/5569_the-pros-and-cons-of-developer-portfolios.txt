The Pros and Cons of Developer Portfolios

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Developer Portfolios is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports applying for internships
- Makes showing skills to recruiters easier
- Helps with tracking personal growth

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: too many unfinished projects
- Risk: no explanation of decisions
- Risk: broken demo links

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as applying for internships, and you can manage the risks, Developer Portfolios is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
