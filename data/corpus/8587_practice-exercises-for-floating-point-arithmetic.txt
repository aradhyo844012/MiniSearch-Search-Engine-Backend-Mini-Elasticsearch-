Practice Exercises for Floating-Point Arithmetic

These exercises build real understanding of Floating-Point Arithmetic. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: comparing floats with equality. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Explain how precision and special values differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Pick Python decimal and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble scientific computing or graphics.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Floating-Point Arithmetic and include the term mantissa and exponent.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Floating-Point Arithmetic is the way computers approximate real numbers with a fixed number of bits.
