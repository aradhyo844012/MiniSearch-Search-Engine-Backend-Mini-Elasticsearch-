Everything You Need to Know About Wearable Technology

This page collects the essentials about Wearable Technology in one place. In short, Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.

Core concepts:
- sensors
- low-power design
- companion apps
- data syncing

Where it is used:
- fitness tracking
- notifications
- health monitoring

Common problems:
- battery drain
- inaccurate sensor readings
- privacy of personal data

Tools to know:
- Wear OS
- watchOS
- Bluetooth Low Energy

Remember this above all: a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to design interactions that take only a few seconds.
