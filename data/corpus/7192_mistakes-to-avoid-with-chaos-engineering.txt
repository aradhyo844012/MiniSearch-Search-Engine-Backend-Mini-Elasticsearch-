Mistakes to Avoid with Chaos Engineering

Everyone learns Chaos Engineering partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: experimenting without safeguards. It usually appears when someone skips steady state and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: having no steady-state metrics. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of testing resilience. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: running first in production. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fault injection to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with small experiments in a test environment.
