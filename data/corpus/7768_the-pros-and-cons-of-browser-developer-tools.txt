The Pros and Cons of Browser Developer Tools

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Browser Developer Tools is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports debugging layouts
- Makes inspecting API calls easier
- Helps with finding slow scripts

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: editing the page and forgetting to copy the changes
- Risk: leaving throttling on
- Risk: ignoring console warnings

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as debugging layouts, and you can manage the risks, Browser Developer Tools is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, keep the network panel open while testing features.
