A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with Dev Containers

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on Dev Containers.

Morning: Sneha starts by reviewing a ticket about onboarding new developers. Before touching any code, Sneha re-reads the notes on devcontainer.json to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Sneha pairs with a teammate to explore images with VS Code Dev Containers. A small test exposes the problem of slow image builds, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving matching production tools. Sneha sketches a ready-made workshop that opens identically for everyone on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Sneha writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to pin tool versions inside the container definition.

Days like this are typical. Dev Containers is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
