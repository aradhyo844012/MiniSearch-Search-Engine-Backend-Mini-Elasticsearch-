Managed Kubernetes Services in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Managed Kubernetes Services.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Managed Kubernetes Services is cloud services that run the Kubernetes control plane for you.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn the control plane, node pools, upgrades, and integrated load balancers.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine renting an apartment building where the landlord handles the plumbing. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through running containerized apps, scaling services, and reducing operations work.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for neglecting version upgrades and oversized node pools, then try Amazon EKS for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to plan regular cluster upgrades.
