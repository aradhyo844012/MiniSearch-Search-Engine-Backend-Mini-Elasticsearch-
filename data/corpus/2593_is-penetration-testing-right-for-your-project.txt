Is Penetration Testing Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Penetration Testing, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Penetration Testing is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do.

It is a good fit when your work involves pre-launch security checks or compliance audits, and when your team understands scoping.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn reconnaissance, or when you cannot manage these risks: testing without written permission, ignoring the findings afterward, and relying only on automated scans.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether exploitation and reporting are part of the answer. If they are, Penetration Testing is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with validating defenses and get written authorization before any test.
