Where to Go Next After Learning Reading Documentation

You have met the basics of Reading Documentation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about System Design Interviews, which belongs to the same area as Reading Documentation (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of search, tutorials versus references, examples or version numbers, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around solving errors with MDN Web Docs, and note every problem you meet, starting with reading the wrong version.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Reading Documentation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
