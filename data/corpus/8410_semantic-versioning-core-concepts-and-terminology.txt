Semantic Versioning: Core Concepts and Terminology

This page defines the vocabulary you need to read about Semantic Versioning with confidence. The subject itself is a numbering scheme where major, minor and patch numbers signal how much changed.

major, minor and patch numbers: one of the building blocks of Semantic Versioning. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
breaking changes: one of the building blocks of Semantic Versioning. It shows up whenever you work on publishing libraries.
pre-release tags: one of the building blocks of Semantic Versioning. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
version ranges: one of the building blocks of Semantic Versioning. Mixing it up with neighboring terms is a common source of confusion.
