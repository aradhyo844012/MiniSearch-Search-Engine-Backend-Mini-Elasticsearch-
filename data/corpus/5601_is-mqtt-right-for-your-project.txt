Is MQTT Right for Your Project?

Before adopting MQTT, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks.

It is a good fit when your work involves sensor networks or smart home devices, and when your team understands publish and subscribe.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn topics, or when you cannot manage these risks: unsecured brokers, messy topic names, and wrong quality of service choices.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether brokers and quality of service levels are part of the answer. If they are, MQTT is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with telemetry and design topic names like a clear folder structure.
