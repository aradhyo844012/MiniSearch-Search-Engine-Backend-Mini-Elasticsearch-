Heaps and Priority Queues: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Heaps and Priority Queues?
A: The term Heaps and Priority Queues refers to structures that always give quick access to the smallest or largest element.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with heap property and sift up and sift down, then move on to array representation and top-k selection.

Q: Why do developers use Heaps and Priority Queues?
A: Common uses include scheduling tasks, selecting the top results from many candidates, and Dijkstra's shortest path algorithm.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Python heapq, Java PriorityQueue, and C++ priority_queue.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: assuming a heap is fully sorted; using a full sort when only the top k are needed; and mixing up min-heaps and max-heaps.
