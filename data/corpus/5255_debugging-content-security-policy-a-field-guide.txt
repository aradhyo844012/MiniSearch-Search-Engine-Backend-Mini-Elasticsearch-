Debugging Content Security Policy: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Content Security Policy, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: allowing unsafe-inline, policies too broad to help, and breaking third-party widgets.
Step 3: revisit directives and nonces, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use browser DevTools or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Content Security Policy in work such as reducing the impact of XSS come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start in report-only mode to learn what would break. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
