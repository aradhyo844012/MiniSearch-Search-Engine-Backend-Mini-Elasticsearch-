Scaling Considerations for Interrupts

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Interrupts changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding interrupt handlers and getting keyboard and mouse input right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for long handlers and race conditions with shared data, and measure with Linux /proc/interrupts before changing anything.

Large scale: priorities and context saving move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around timers at this stage.

Before you scale, keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
