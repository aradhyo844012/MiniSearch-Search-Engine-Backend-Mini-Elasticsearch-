Makefiles in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Makefiles.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Makefiles is plain files that describe how to build programs and run common tasks with the make tool.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn targets, prerequisites, recipes, and phony targets.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a to-do list that knows which steps must come first. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through compiling C projects, shortcuts for common commands, and automating documentation builds.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for tabs versus spaces and missing phony declarations, then try GNU Make for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use phony targets for tasks like test and clean.
