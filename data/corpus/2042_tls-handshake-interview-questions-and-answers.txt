TLS Handshake Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about TLS Handshake.

Q: Explain TLS Handshake in simple words.
A: TLS Handshake refers to the exchange that lets a client and server agree on keys and set up an encrypted connection. I would add an example involving securing websites.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as certificates, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: certificates, cipher suites, session keys, and certificate authorities.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: expired certificates; disabling certificate validation; and using outdated protocol versions.
