Dependency Vulnerabilities for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Dependency Vulnerabilities.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Dependency Vulnerabilities in your own words.
2. Describe CVE identifiers, version pinning, transitive dependencies, and software bills of materials.
3. Give examples from auditing projects and automating updates.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Dependency Vulnerabilities is security weaknesses that come from third-party libraries a project uses. Then draw checking every ingredient supplier for recalls on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is ignoring transitive dependencies a problem? How does version pinning relate to transitive dependencies? When would you choose Dependabot or pip-audit?

Study habit: automate update pull requests.

When you feel ready, compare Dependency Vulnerabilities with Encryption at Rest and write down three differences.
