The Trade-offs of BM25 Ranking

Every design choice has a cost, and BM25 Ranking is no exception. The term BM25 Ranking refers to a ranking function that improves on plain term frequency with saturation and length normalization. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

On the positive side, BM25 Ranking is well suited to ranking search results and enterprise search. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: untuned parameters; ignoring field boundaries; comparing scores across different queries. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much term saturation and document length normalization matter to your project.
