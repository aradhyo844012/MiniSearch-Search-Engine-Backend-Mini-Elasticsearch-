Time-Series Databases for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Time-Series Databases.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Time-Series Databases in your own words.
2. Describe timestamps, retention policies, downsampling, and tags and fields.
3. Give examples from server metrics and sensor readings.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Time-Series Databases is databases optimized for storing and querying data points ordered by time. Then draw a long logbook where every line starts with the time on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is high-cardinality tags a problem? How does retention policies relate to downsampling? When would you choose InfluxDB or TimescaleDB?

Study habit: include a time range in every query.

When you feel ready, compare Time-Series Databases with Write-Ahead Logging and write down three differences.
