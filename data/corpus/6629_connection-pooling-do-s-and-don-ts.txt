Connection Pooling Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Connection Pooling.

Do:
- Learn pool size before anything else.
- Try PgBouncer on a small example.
- Make it a habit to always return connections in a finally block.
- Test the case of web backends early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: pools larger than the database allows.
- Ignore this: leaked connections.
- Ignore this: long transactions that hold connections.
- Adopt Connection Pooling just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Connection Pooling, a little patience with idle connections pays off more than any clever shortcut.
