Everything You Need to Know About A* Pathfinding

This page collects the essentials about A* Pathfinding in one place. In short, A* Pathfinding is a search algorithm that finds short routes by combining distance traveled with an estimate of distance remaining.

Core concepts:
- open and closed sets
- cost so far
- heuristics
- grids and graphs

Where it is used:
- game characters
- robot navigation
- map routing

Common problems:
- overestimating the heuristic
- slow searches on huge maps
- ignoring moving obstacles

Tools to know:
- Unity NavMesh
- Python heapq
- Recast Navigation

Remember this above all: a hiker who prefers trails that seem to head toward the summit is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to choose an admissible heuristic so paths stay optimal.
