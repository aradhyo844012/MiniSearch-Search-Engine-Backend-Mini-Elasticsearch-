Data Cleaning Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Data Cleaning.

Q: Explain Data Cleaning in simple words.
A: Data Cleaning refers to finding and fixing errors, gaps and inconsistencies in raw data. I would add an example involving preparing logs.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: pandas, OpenRefine, and regular expressions.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with merging data sources, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: dropping data without checking why it is missing; cleaning by hand without a record; and changing the original files.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: dropping data without checking why it is missing.
