Scaling Considerations for Compilers and Interpreters

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Compilers and Interpreters changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding lexing and getting running Python and JavaScript right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for assuming interpreted means slow without measuring and ignoring compiler warnings, and measure with GCC before changing anything.

Large scale: code generation and bytecode move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around building native programs at this stage.

Before you scale, turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
