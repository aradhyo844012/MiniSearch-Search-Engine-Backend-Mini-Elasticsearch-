Everything You Need to Know About Color Spaces

This page collects the essentials about Color Spaces in one place. In short, Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.

Core concepts:
- RGB
- HSL
- CMYK
- gamma

Where it is used:
- web design
- photo editing
- printing

Common problems:
- ignoring gamma
- mixing color spaces
- poor contrast choices

Tools to know:
- browser color pickers
- ImageMagick
- Coolors

Remember this above all: different ways of giving directions to the same destination is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to work in one color space and convert only at the end.
