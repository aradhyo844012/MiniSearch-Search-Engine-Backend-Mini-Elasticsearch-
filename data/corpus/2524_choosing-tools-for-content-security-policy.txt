Choosing Tools for Content Security Policy

There is no single best tool for Content Security Policy. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

browser DevTools is a common first choice, especially for reducing the impact of XSS. CSP Evaluator suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Report URI is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle blocking unwanted scripts well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: allowing unsafe-inline.

As a rule of thumb, start in report-only mode to learn what would break, no matter which tool you use.
