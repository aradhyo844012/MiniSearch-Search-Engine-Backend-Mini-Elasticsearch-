Teaching MQTT to a Teammate

Explaining MQTT to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a radio station with channels that devices can tune into. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving sensor networks, naming publish and subscribe and topics as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain brokers in MQTT back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, unsecured brokers, and suggest this habit: design topic names like a clear folder structure.
