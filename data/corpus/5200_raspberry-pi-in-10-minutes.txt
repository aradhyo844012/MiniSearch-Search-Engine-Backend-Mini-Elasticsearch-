Raspberry Pi in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Raspberry Pi.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn GPIO pins, Linux, SD card storage, and headless setup.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a pocket-sized workshop for experiments. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through home automation, learning Linux, and small servers.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for SD card corruption and inadequate power supplies, then try Raspberry Pi OS for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
