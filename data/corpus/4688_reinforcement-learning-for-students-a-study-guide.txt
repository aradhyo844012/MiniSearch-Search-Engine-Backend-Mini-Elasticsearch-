Reinforcement Learning for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Reinforcement Learning.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Reinforcement Learning in your own words.
2. Describe agent, environment, reward, and policy.
3. Give examples from game playing and robot control.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes. Then draw training a dog with treats for good behavior on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is badly designed rewards a problem? How does environment relate to reward? When would you choose Gymnasium or Stable-Baselines3?

Study habit: start with simple environments.

When you feel ready, compare Reinforcement Learning with Random Forests and write down three differences.
