Choosing Tools for Blue-Green Deployment

There is no single best tool for Blue-Green Deployment. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

AWS CodeDeploy is a common first choice, especially for zero-downtime releases. Kubernetes services suits teams that already work with similar technology, and NGINX is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle quick rollbacks well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: database schema mismatches.

As a rule of thumb, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven, no matter which tool you use.
