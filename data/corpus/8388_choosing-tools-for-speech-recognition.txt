Choosing Tools for Speech Recognition

There is no single best tool for Speech Recognition. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Whisper is a common first choice, especially for voice assistants. Kaldi suits teams that already work with similar technology, and cloud speech-to-text services is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Speech Recognition by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle captioning well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Speech Recognition, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: accents and background noise.

As a rule of thumb, measure word error rate on audio like your real users', no matter which tool you use.
