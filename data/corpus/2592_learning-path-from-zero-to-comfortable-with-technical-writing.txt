Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Technical Writing

This path is for a learner who has never studied Technical Writing before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience) and learn audience and structure.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Markdown, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving documentation.

Stage 3, depth: study examples and editing, then deliberately explore this problem: assuming too much knowledge.

Stage 4, application: apply Technical Writing to blog posts, compare Grammarly with Vale, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Technical Writing to someone else, and then move on to Open Source Contribution.
