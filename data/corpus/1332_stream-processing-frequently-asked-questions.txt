Stream Processing: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Stream Processing?
A: The term Stream Processing refers to handling data continuously as it arrives rather than in large batches.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with events and windows, then move on to offsets and state.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: late events; losing state after crashes; and duplicate processing.

Q: Why do developers use Stream Processing?
A: Common uses include live dashboards, fraud detection, and real-time alerts.

Q: How does it relate to ETL Pipelines?
A: Stream Processing and ETL Pipelines often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Data Engineering.
