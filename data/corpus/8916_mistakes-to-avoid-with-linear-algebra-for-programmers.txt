Mistakes to Avoid with Linear Algebra for Programmers

Everyone learns Linear Algebra for Programmers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: mixing row and column conventions. It usually appears when someone skips vectors and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: shape mismatches. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of 3D graphics. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting that matrix multiplication is not commutative. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on eigenvalues to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: check shapes before multiplying.
