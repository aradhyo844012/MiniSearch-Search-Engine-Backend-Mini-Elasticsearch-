Mobile App Security for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Mobile App Security.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Mobile App Security in your own words.
2. Describe secure storage, certificate pinning, obfuscation, and permissions.
3. Give examples from banking apps and health apps.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Mobile App Security is practices that protect mobile apps and their data from attacks. Then draw locking an app's valuables in a hotel safe on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is storing tokens in plain text a problem? How does certificate pinning relate to obfuscation? When would you choose OWASP MASVS or Android Keystore?

Study habit worth keeping: store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore.

When you feel ready, compare Mobile App Security with Embedded Systems and write down three differences.
