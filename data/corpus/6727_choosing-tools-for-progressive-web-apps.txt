Choosing Tools for Progressive Web Apps

There is no single best tool for Progressive Web Apps. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Lighthouse is a common first choice, especially for offline-friendly tools. Workbox suits teams that already work with similar technology, and PWABuilder is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle lightweight alternatives to native apps well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: ignoring platform limitations.

As a rule of thumb, test the app in airplane mode, no matter which tool you use.
