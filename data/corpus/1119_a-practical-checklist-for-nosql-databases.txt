A Practical Checklist for NoSQL Databases

Use this checklist before you rely on NoSQL Databases in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: weak consistency surprises.
2. Review how NoSQL Databases interacts with SQL Joins.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Review the core ideas: document stores, key-value stores, column families, and graph databases.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what NoSQL Databases should accomplish.
6. Guard against this risk: assuming no schema means no design.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on key-value stores.
8. Pick a tool to start with, such as MongoDB or Cassandra.
9. Measure results before and after any change.
