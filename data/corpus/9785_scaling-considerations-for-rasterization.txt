Scaling Considerations for Rasterization

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Rasterization changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding triangles and getting real-time games right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for aliasing along edges and overdraw, and measure with OpenGL before changing anything.

Large scale: the depth buffer and interpolation move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around user interfaces at this stage.

Before you scale, draw simple shapes first to understand the pipeline. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
