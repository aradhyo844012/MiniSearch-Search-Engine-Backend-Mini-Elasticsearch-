Cloud Cost Optimization for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Cloud Cost Optimization.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Cloud Cost Optimization in your own words.
2. Describe right-sizing, reserved capacity, tagging, and usage reports.
3. Give examples from cutting idle resources and budgeting by team.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability. Then draw turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is forgotten test environments a problem? How does reserved capacity relate to tagging? When would you choose AWS Cost Explorer or Infracost?

Study habit worth keeping: tag every resource with an owner.

When you feel ready, compare Cloud Cost Optimization with Secrets Management and write down three differences.
