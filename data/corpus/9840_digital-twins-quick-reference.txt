Digital Twins Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Digital Twins. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.
Key terms: sensor data, simulation models, synchronization, and analytics.
Typical uses: factory monitoring, building management, and predictive maintenance.
Risks: stale data, over-detailed models, and unclear goals.
Tools: Azure Digital Twins, Unity, and MQTT.

Mental picture: a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves.

Habit to build: define the question the twin must answer first.
