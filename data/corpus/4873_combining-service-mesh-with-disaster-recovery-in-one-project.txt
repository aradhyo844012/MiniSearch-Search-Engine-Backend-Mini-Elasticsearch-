Combining Service Mesh with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Service Mesh and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves securing service traffic and surviving regional outages. Service Mesh brings sidecar proxies, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with Istio for the Service Mesh part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: adopting it too early on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
