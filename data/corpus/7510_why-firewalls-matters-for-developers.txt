Why Firewalls Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Firewalls make better decisions, because Firewalls is systems that allow or block network traffic according to rules.

Three situations show why it matters. First, protecting servers. Second, segmenting networks. Third, logging suspicious traffic. In each case, a solid grasp of rules and stateful inspection separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: rules in the wrong order, overly broad allow rules, and locking yourself out remotely.

You will not need to build everything yourself; ufw, iptables, and pfSense cover most needs.
