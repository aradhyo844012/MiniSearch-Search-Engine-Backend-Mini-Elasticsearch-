A Practical Checklist for Autocomplete

Use this checklist before you rely on Autocomplete in a project.

1. Review how Autocomplete interacts with Stop Words.
2. Guard against this risk: ignoring typos in the prefix.
3. Pick a tool to start with, such as tries or Redis sorted sets.
4. Review the core ideas: prefixes, ranking suggestions, latency, and query logs.
5. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on ranking suggestions.
6. Document your decisions in the project README.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers prefixes.
8. Try the approach on a small case, for example search boxes.
