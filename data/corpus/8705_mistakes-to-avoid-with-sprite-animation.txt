Mistakes to Avoid with Sprite Animation

Everyone learns Sprite Animation partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: inconsistent frame timing. It usually appears when someone skips sprite sheets and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: huge uncompressed sheets. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of 2D platformers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: animation states that never end. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on animation states to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
