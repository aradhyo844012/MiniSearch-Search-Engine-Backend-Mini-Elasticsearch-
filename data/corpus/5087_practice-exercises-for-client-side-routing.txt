Practice Exercises for Client-Side Routing

These exercises build real understanding of Client-Side Routing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble single-page apps or dashboards.
Exercise 2: Compare Vue Router and Next.js for multi-step flows and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Explain how nested routes and route guards differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: broken deep links. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Client-Side Routing and include the term history API.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Client-Side Routing is changing the visible page in the browser without a full page reload.
