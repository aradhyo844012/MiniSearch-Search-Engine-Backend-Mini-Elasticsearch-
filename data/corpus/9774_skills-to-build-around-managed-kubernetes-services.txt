Skills to Build Around Managed Kubernetes Services

Knowing the definition of Managed Kubernetes Services is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with the control plane and node pools, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Amazon EKS on a Managed Kubernetes Services task, and try at least one alternative such as Google GKE.

Judgment: learn to decide when Managed Kubernetes Services fits a problem such as running containerized apps, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example renting an apartment building where the landlord handles the plumbing.

Habits: plan regular cluster upgrades, and write down what you learn after each project.
