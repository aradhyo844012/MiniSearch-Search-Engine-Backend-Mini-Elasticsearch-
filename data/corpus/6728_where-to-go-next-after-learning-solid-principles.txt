Where to Go Next After Learning SOLID Principles

You have met the basics of SOLID Principles. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Test-Driven Development, which belongs to the same area as SOLID Principles (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of single responsibility, the open-closed principle, Liskov substitution or dependency inversion, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around reviewing designs with UML diagrams, and note every problem you meet, starting with applying them rigidly to tiny scripts.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining SOLID Principles to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
