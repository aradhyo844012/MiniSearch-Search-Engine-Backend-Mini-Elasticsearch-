Practice Exercises for Logging Best Practices

These exercises build real understanding of Logging Best Practices. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Logging Best Practices and include the term log levels.
Exercise 2: Pick the Python logging module and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble debugging production issues or auditing actions.
Exercise 4: Explain how correlation IDs and log rotation differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Compare the ELK stack and Grafana Loki for measuring usage and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Logging Best Practices is recording useful, structured information about what an application is doing.
