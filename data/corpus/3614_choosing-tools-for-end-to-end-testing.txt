Choosing Tools for End-to-End Testing

There is no single best tool for End-to-End Testing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Playwright is a common first choice, especially for checking login and checkout flows. Cypress suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Selenium is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle release verification well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: flaky tests.

As a rule of thumb, cover only the most important user journeys, no matter which tool you use.
