Cloud Cost Optimization in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Cloud Cost Optimization.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn right-sizing, reserved capacity, tagging, and usage reports.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine turning off the lights in rooms nobody is using. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through cutting idle resources, budgeting by team, and choosing storage tiers.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for forgotten test environments and over-provisioned databases, then try AWS Cost Explorer for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to tag every resource with an owner.
