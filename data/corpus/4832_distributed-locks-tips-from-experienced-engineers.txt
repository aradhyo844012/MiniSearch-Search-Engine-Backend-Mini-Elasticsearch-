Distributed Locks Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Distributed Locks regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit fencing tokens before anything else.
2. Write down why you chose Distributed Locks so the next person understands the decision.
3. Make sure you can always use a timeout together with a fencing token.
4. Reach for Redis first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as ZooKeeper.
5. Do not skip lease times; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to protecting shared resources as well.
