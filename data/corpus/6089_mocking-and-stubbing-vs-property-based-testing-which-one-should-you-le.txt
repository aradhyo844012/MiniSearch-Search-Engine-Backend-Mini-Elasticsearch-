Mocking and Stubbing vs Property-Based Testing: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Mocking and Stubbing or Property-Based Testing first. Both sit in the area of Testing and Quality, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests, and it is closely tied to mocks and stubs. Property-Based Testing, by contrast, is testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold, and it is closely tied to properties and generators.

Choose Mocking and Stubbing first if you care about isolating units or simulating failures. Choose Property-Based Testing first if you care about testing parsers or checking sorting and math functions.

Think of Mocking and Stubbing as a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger, and of Property-Based Testing as a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Mocking and Stubbing, avoid over-mocking. With Property-Based Testing, avoid vague properties.

A good habit for Mocking and Stubbing is to prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains. A good habit for Property-Based Testing is to save failing examples as regular tests.
