Model Deployment Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Model Deployment. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: making a trained model available to serve predictions in a real application.
Key terms: serving APIs, batch versus online inference, containers, and latency budgets.
Typical uses: recommendation endpoints, fraud checks, and mobile app features.
Risks: training-serving mismatch, slow cold starts, and no fallback when the model fails.
Tools: FastAPI, TorchServe, and BentoML.

Mental picture: moving a recipe from the test kitchen into the restaurant.

Habit to build: log inputs and outputs so predictions can be audited.
