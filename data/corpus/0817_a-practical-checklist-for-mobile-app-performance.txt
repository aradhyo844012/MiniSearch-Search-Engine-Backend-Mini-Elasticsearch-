A Practical Checklist for Mobile App Performance

Use this checklist before you rely on Mobile App Performance in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: loading everything at startup.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example smoother scrolling.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers startup time.
4. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on frame rate.
5. Pick a tool to start with, such as Android Profiler or Xcode Instruments.
6. Review how Mobile App Performance interacts with Android Basics.
7. Document your decisions in the project README.
