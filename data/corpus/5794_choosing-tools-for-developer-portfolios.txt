Choosing Tools for Developer Portfolios

There is no single best tool for Developer Portfolios. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

GitHub Pages is a common first choice, especially for applying for internships. Vercel suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Netlify is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle showing skills to recruiters well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too many unfinished projects.

As a rule of thumb, feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones, no matter which tool you use.
