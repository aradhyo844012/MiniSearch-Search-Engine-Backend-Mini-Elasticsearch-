Security Headers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Security Headers.

Do:
- Learn Strict-Transport-Security before anything else.
- Try securityheaders.com on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test the headers on every environment.
- Test the case of hardening websites early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: setting headers on only some routes.
- Ignore this: breaking embeds.
- Ignore this: copying configurations without understanding them.
- Adopt Security Headers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Security Headers, a little patience with X-Content-Type-Options pays off more than any clever shortcut.
