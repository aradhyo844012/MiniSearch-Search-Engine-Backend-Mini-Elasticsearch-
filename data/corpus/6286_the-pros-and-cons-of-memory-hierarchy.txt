The Pros and Cons of Memory Hierarchy

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Memory Hierarchy is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports designing efficient programs
- Makes explaining slow I/O easier
- Helps with choosing data structures

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: ignoring locality
- Risk: excessive paging
- Risk: treating all memory accesses as equal

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as designing efficient programs, and you can manage the risks, Memory Hierarchy is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, reduce how often your program goes down to the slower layers.
