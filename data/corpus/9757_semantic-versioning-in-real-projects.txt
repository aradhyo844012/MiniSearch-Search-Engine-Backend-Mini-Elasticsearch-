Semantic Versioning in Real Projects

Reading about Semantic Versioning is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: publishing libraries. Here, major, minor and patch numbers drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against breaking changes in minor releases.

Scenario two: declaring dependencies. This time breaking changes matters most, and tools such as semver.org help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: planning upgrades. The key idea is pre-release tags, and the biggest risk is overly loose ranges.

Across all three scenarios, Semantic Versioning stays the same idea: a numbering scheme where major, minor and patch numbers signal how much changed. Only the details change.
