Mistakes to Avoid with C Pointers

Everyone learns C Pointers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: dereferencing null pointers. It usually appears when someone skips addresses and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: memory leaks. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of dynamic memory allocation. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: dangling pointers. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on null pointers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: set pointers to NULL after freeing them.
