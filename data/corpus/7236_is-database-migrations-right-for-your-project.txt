Is Database Migrations Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Database Migrations, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Database Migrations is versioned scripts that change a database schema in a controlled and repeatable way.

It is a good fit when your work involves adding columns safely or renaming tables, and when your team understands up and down scripts.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn version tables, or when you cannot manage these risks: locking large tables, editing old migrations, and data loss on rollbacks.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether backward compatibility and zero-downtime changes are part of the answer. If they are, Database Migrations is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with keeping environments in sync and split risky changes into small steps that can each be rolled back.
