Environment Variables Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Environment Variables.

Do:
- Learn variable names before anything else.
- Try env on a small example.
- Make it a habit to print a variable to confirm its value before blaming the code.
- Test the case of storing configuration early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: committing secrets in dotfiles.
- Ignore this: variables not visible to child processes.
- Ignore this: typos in names.
- Adopt Environment Variables just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Environment Variables, a little patience with scope pays off more than any clever shortcut.
