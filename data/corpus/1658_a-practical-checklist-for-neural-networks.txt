A Practical Checklist for Neural Networks

Use this checklist before you rely on Neural Networks in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: poor learning rate choices.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers layers.
3. Try the approach on a small case, for example image recognition.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Review how Neural Networks interacts with Linear Regression.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on weights.
7. Review the core ideas: layers, weights, activation functions, and backpropagation.
8. Guard against this risk: not monitoring validation loss.
