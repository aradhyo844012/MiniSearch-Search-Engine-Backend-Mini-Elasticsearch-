Microservices Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Microservices.

Q: Explain Microservices in simple words.
A: Microservices refers to an architecture that splits an application into small services that can be deployed independently. I would add an example involving large teams working in parallel.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Docker, Kubernetes, and API gateways.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with scaling parts separately, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: service boundaries, APIs, independent deployment, and distributed data.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as service boundaries, and add tests aimed at the known risks.
