Linear Algebra for Programmers Explained Simply

If Linear Algebra for Programmers sounds intimidating, relax. It is the math of vectors and matrices used in graphics and machine learning, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Linear Algebra for Programmers as a set of arrows and stretching machines acting on space. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: vectors, matrices, dot products, and eigenvalues. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with vectors, then add matrices.

People reach for Linear Algebra for Programmers when they work on 3D graphics, machine learning, and recommendation systems.

One practical tip is to check shapes before multiplying.

After Linear Algebra for Programmers, a natural next topic is Information Theory and Entropy.
