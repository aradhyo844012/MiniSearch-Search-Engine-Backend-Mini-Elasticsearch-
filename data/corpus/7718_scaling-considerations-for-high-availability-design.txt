Scaling Considerations for High Availability Design

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how High Availability Design changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding redundancy and getting online stores right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for hidden single points of failure and untested failover, and measure with load balancers before changing anything.

Large scale: health checks and no single point of failure move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around banking systems at this stage.

Before you scale, list every component and ask what happens if it dies. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
