Mistakes to Avoid with Encryption at Rest

Everyone learns Encryption at Rest partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: storing keys next to the data. It usually appears when someone skips symmetric keys and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: no key rotation. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting backups. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: assuming it stops every kind of attack. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on envelope encryption to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep keys in a separate service from the data.
