Teaching Augmented and Virtual Reality to a Teammate

Explaining Augmented and Virtual Reality to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: wearing glasses that add a new layer to the world. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Augmented and Virtual Reality is technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving training simulations, naming head tracking and rendering at high frame rates as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain spatial mapping in Augmented and Virtual Reality back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, motion sickness from low frame rates, and suggest this habit: keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
