Debugging Embeddings: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Embeddings, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: mixing models between indexing and querying, ignoring normalization, and stale embeddings after data changes.
Step 3: revisit vectors and cosine similarity, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use sentence-transformers or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Embeddings in work such as semantic search come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use the same embedding model for indexing and querying. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
