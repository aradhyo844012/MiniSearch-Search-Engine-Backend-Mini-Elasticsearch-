Practice Exercises for Zero Trust Security

These exercises build real understanding of Zero Trust Security. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how micro-segmentation and continuous checks differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Compare Tailscale and identity-aware proxies for limiting breach damage and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble remote work access or protecting internal services.
Exercise 4: Pick Okta and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Zero Trust Security and include the term identity verification.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Zero Trust Security is a model that treats every request as untrusted until it is verified.
