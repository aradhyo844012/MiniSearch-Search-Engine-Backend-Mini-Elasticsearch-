Teaching GraphQL to a Teammate

Explaining GraphQL to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: ordering a la carte instead of picking a fixed set menu. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving mobile apps with limited bandwidth, naming schema and queries and mutations as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain resolvers in GraphQL back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, the N+1 query problem, and suggest this habit: use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
