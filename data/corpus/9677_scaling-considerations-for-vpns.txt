Scaling Considerations for VPNs

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how VPNs changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding tunneling and getting remote work access right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for assuming it makes you anonymous and slow routing through distant servers, and measure with WireGuard before changing anything.

Large scale: authentication and split tunneling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around linking offices at this stage.

Before you scale, test for DNS leaks after connecting. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
