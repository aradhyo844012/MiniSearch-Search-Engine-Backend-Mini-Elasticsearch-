Neural Networks vs Feature Engineering: Key Differences

Neural Networks and Feature Engineering both belong to the area of Machine Learning, but they solve different problems. This article compares them side by side so you can choose with confidence.

The term Neural Networks refers to models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. Its core ideas are layers, weights, and activation functions.

The term Feature Engineering refers to creating and transforming input variables so that models can learn from them more easily. Its core ideas are scaling, encoding categories, and interaction features.

The main difference is focus. Neural Networks puts the emphasis on layers and weights, while Feature Engineering puts the emphasis on scaling and encoding categories.

Reach for Neural Networks when your task involves image recognition or speech recognition. Reach for Feature Engineering when your task involves improving model accuracy or preparing tabular data.

Both have risks. With Neural Networks, watch for this: too little training data. With Feature Engineering, watch for this: data leakage.

In practice you do not always have to choose. Many projects use Neural Networks alongside Feature Engineering, with tools such as PyTorch and pandas. Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.
