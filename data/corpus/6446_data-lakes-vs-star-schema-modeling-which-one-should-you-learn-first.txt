Data Lakes vs Star Schema Modeling: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Data Lakes or Star Schema Modeling first. Both sit in the area of Data and Analytics, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed, and it is closely tied to raw storage and schema on read. Star Schema Modeling, by contrast, is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables, and it is closely tied to fact tables and dimension tables.

Choose Data Lakes first if you care about storing logs and events or machine learning datasets. Choose Star Schema Modeling first if you care about business intelligence reports or data warehouses.

Think of Data Lakes as a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment, and of Star Schema Modeling as a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Data Lakes, avoid turning into a data swamp. With Star Schema Modeling, avoid mixing different grains.

A good habit for Data Lakes is to register every dataset in a catalog with an owner. A good habit for Star Schema Modeling is to state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
