Debugging Convolutional Neural Networks: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Convolutional Neural Networks, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: too little training data, ignoring data augmentation, and overly deep models for simple tasks.
Step 3: revisit filters and pooling, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use PyTorch or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Convolutional Neural Networks in work such as image classification come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start from a pretrained model. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
