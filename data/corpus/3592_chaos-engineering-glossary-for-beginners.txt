Chaos Engineering Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Chaos Engineering feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Chaos Engineering: deliberately causing failures in a system to learn how it responds.
steady state: a core term, most visible in testing resilience.
hypotheses: a core term that beginners often confuse with blast radius.
blast radius: a core term that connects to fault injection.
fault injection: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Chaos Monkey: a tool commonly used for validating failover.

Come back to this Chaos Engineering list whenever documentation for Gremlin uses a word you do not recognize.
