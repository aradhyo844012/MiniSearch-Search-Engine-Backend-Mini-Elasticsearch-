TypeScript Generics Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with TypeScript Generics.

Do:
- Learn type parameters before anything else.
- Try tsc on a small example.
- Make it a habit to add constraints so that error messages become clearer.
- Test the case of reusable utility functions early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: overusing any.
- Ignore this: overly complex generic types.
- Ignore this: forgetting constraints.
- Adopt TypeScript Generics just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with TypeScript Generics, a little patience with constraints pays off more than any clever shortcut.
