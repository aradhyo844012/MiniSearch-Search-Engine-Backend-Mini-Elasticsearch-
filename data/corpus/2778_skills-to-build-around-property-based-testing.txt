Skills to Build Around Property-Based Testing

Knowing the definition of Property-Based Testing is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with properties and generators, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Hypothesis on a Property-Based Testing task, and try at least one alternative such as QuickCheck.

Judgment: learn to decide when Property-Based Testing fits a problem such as testing parsers, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs.

Habits: save failing examples as regular tests, and write down what you learn after each project.
