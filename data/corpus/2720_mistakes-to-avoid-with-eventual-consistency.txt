Mistakes to Avoid with Eventual Consistency

Everyone learns Eventual Consistency partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming reads are always fresh. It usually appears when someone skips replication delay and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: unresolved write conflicts. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of global social feeds. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: no user feedback during delays. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on convergence to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: decide up front how your app handles stale reads.
