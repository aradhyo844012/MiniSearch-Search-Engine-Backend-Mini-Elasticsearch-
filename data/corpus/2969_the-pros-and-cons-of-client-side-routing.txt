The Pros and Cons of Client-Side Routing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Client-Side Routing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports single-page apps
- Makes dashboards easier
- Helps with multi-step flows

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: broken deep links
- Risk: unhandled 404 pages
- Risk: lost scroll position

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as single-page apps, and you can manage the risks, Client-Side Routing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test refreshing the page on every route.
