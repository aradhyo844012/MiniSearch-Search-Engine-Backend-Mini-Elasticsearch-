Mistakes to Avoid with Service Mesh

Everyone learns Service Mesh partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: added latency and complexity. It usually appears when someone skips sidecar proxies and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: adopting it too early. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of securing service traffic. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hard-to-debug proxy configs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on observability to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful.
