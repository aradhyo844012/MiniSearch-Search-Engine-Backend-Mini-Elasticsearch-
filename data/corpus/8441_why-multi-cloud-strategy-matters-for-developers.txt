Why Multi-Cloud Strategy Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Multi-Cloud Strategy make better decisions, because Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.

Three situations show why it matters. First, avoiding single-provider risk. Second, meeting regional rules. Third, using best-of-breed services. In each case, a solid grasp of portability and vendor lock-in separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: doubling operational complexity, lowest-common-denominator designs, and hidden data transfer costs.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Terraform, Kubernetes, and Crossplane cover most needs.
