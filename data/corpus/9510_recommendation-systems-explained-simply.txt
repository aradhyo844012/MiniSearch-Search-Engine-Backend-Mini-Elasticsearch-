Recommendation Systems Explained Simply

If Recommendation Systems sounds intimidating, relax. It is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Recommendation Systems as a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, ranking, and the cold start problem. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with collaborative filtering, then add content-based filtering.

People reach for Recommendation Systems when they work on streaming services, online shops, and news feeds.

One practical tip is to evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.

Once Recommendation Systems feels comfortable, read about Anomaly Detection, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
