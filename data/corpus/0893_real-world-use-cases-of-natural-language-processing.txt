Real-World Use Cases of Natural Language Processing

The best way to appreciate Natural Language Processing is to see where it is actually used. When people talk about Natural Language Processing, they usually mean the field of teaching computers to work with human language. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Natural Language Processing and Decision Trees are closely related, so learning both gives a fuller picture of Machine Learning.

People use Natural Language Processing for search engines, for chatbots, and for sentiment analysis.

Consider search engines. In a case like this, Natural Language Processing gives the work a clear structure, and understanding tokenization makes the design easier to explain. A different example is chatbots, where embeddings often decides how well the solution scales.

Results should always be checked on data the model has never seen.

Popular tools and libraries include spaCy, NLTK, and Hugging Face.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Natural Language Processing whenever a new project gives you a reason.
