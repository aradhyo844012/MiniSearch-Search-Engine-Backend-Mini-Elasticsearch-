Practice Exercises for Recommendation Systems

These exercises build real understanding of Recommendation Systems. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble streaming services or online shops.
Exercise 2: Pick Surprise and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: feedback loops. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: Write a short paragraph describing Recommendation Systems and include the term collaborative filtering.
Exercise 5: Compare implicit and TensorFlow Recommenders for news feeds and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.
