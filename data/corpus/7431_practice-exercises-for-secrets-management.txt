Practice Exercises for Secrets Management

These exercises build real understanding of Secrets Management. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: secrets in source control. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Pick HashiCorp Vault and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Secrets Management and include the term vaults.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble database credentials or API keys.
Exercise 5: Explain how access policies and audit logs differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.
