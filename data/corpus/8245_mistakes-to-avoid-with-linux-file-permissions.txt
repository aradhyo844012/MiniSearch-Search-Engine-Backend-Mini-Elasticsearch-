Mistakes to Avoid with Linux File Permissions

Everyone learns Linux File Permissions partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using chmod 777 as a quick fix. It usually appears when someone skips owner, group and others and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: wrong ownership after copying files. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of protecting private keys. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting directory execute bits. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on umask to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: grant the least permission that works.
