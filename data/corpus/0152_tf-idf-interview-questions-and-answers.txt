TF-IDF Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about TF-IDF.

Q: Explain TF-IDF in simple words.
A: TF-IDF refers to a scoring method that weights terms by how often they appear in a document and how rare they are overall. I would add an example involving ranking search results.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with finding keywords, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: over-weighting common words; ignoring document length; and using it on tiny corpora.

Q: How does it connect to Zipf's Law?
A: Both belong to the area of Search and Retrieval, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer, Lucene, and Gensim.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: term frequency, inverse document frequency, weighting, and normalization.
