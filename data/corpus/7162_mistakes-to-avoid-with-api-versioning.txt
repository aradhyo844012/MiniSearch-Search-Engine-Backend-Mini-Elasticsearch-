Mistakes to Avoid with API Versioning

Everyone learns API Versioning partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: versioning too often. It usually appears when someone skips version in the URL and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: removing fields without notice. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of public APIs. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: having no deprecation schedule. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on backward compatibility to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: announce deprecations with a clear sunset date.
