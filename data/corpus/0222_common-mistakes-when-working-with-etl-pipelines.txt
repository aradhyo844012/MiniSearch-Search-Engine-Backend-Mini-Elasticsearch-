Common Mistakes When Working with ETL Pipelines

Almost everyone makes a few avoidable errors when first working with ETL Pipelines. The term ETL Pipelines refers to processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Watch out for these problems: silent data loss; no retry logic; and poor error reporting.

Consider the first problem: silent data loss. A short test or a careful review comment usually catches it. The second problem is similar: no retry logic. The third problem is this: poor error reporting. It tends to appear only once a project grows, so it is worth planning for early.

Practice extraction with small exercises until it feels natural. Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce. Keep notes on what worked. A short README explaining your decisions about ETL Pipelines helps the next developer.

A solid grasp of ETL Pipelines also makes Pandas DataFrames easier to understand.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit ETL Pipelines whenever a new project gives you a reason.
