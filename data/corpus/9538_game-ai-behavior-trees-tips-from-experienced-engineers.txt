Game AI Behavior Trees Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Game AI Behavior Trees regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: trees that become tangled.
2. Reach for Unreal Engine behavior trees first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as py_trees.
3. Write down why you chose Game AI Behavior Trees so the next person understands the decision.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit sequence nodes before anything else.
5. Test the case of enemy behavior early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to companion characters as well as to Game AI Behavior Trees in general.
