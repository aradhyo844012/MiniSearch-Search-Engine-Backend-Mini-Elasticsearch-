Choosing Tools for Image Compression

There is no single best tool for Image Compression. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ImageMagick is a common first choice, especially for web images. mozjpeg suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Squoosh is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Image Compression by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle photo storage well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Image Compression, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: repeated recompression.

As a rule of thumb, pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics, no matter which tool you use.
