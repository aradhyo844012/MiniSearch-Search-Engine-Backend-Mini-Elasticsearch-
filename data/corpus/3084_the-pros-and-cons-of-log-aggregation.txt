The Pros and Cons of Log Aggregation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Log Aggregation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports troubleshooting across services
- Makes security investigations easier
- Helps with usage analysis

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: unbounded storage growth
- Risk: unstructured logs
- Risk: missing timestamps

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as troubleshooting across services, and you can manage the risks, Log Aggregation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use structured JSON logs so that fields are searchable.
