Teaching Feature Flags to a Teammate

Explaining Feature Flags to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: light switches wired to every room of a house. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving dark launches, naming flag evaluation and targeting rules as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain gradual rollout in Feature Flags back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, never removing old flags, and suggest this habit: give every flag an owner and a removal date.
