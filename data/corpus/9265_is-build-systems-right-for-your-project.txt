Is Build Systems Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Build Systems, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Build Systems is tools that turn source code into runnable programs automatically.

It is a good fit when your work involves compiling large projects or packaging software, and when your team understands targets.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn dependencies, or when you cannot manage these risks: slow full rebuilds, undeclared dependencies, and machine-specific builds.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether incremental builds and build caches are part of the answer. If they are, Build Systems is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with running repeatable pipelines and make builds reproducible on any machine.
