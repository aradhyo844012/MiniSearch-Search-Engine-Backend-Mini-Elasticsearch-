Myths and Facts About Containers

A lot of folklore has built up around Containers. Some of it is true, and some of it is not. Many students first meet Containers in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to lightweight packages that bundle an application with its dependencies and run in isolation.

Myth: Containers is only for experts.
Fact: beginners can use it effectively once they understand images and layers.

Myth: Containers is only useful in very large systems.
Fact: small projects such as consistent development environments benefit as well.

Myth: one tool does everything.
Fact: Docker, Podman, and containerd make different trade-offs, so the right choice depends on the project.

Myth: mistakes with Containers are always obvious.
Fact: subtle problems happen, for example running as root.

Understanding what the kernel does explains many surprising performance problems.

In short, careful study of Containers pays off. Spend time on images and layers, and the rest will follow.
