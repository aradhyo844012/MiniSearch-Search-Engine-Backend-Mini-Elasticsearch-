Wearable Technology for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Wearable Technology.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Wearable Technology in your own words.
2. Describe sensors, low-power design, companion apps, and data syncing.
3. Give examples from fitness tracking and notifications.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers. Then draw a tiny assistant strapped to your wrist on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is battery drain a problem? How does low-power design relate to companion apps? When would you choose Wear OS or watchOS?

Study habit: design interactions that take only a few seconds.

When you feel ready, compare Wearable Technology with Digital Twins and write down three differences.
