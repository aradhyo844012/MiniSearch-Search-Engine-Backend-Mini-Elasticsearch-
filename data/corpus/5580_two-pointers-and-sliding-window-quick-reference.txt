Two Pointers and Sliding Window Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Two Pointers and Sliding Window. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: techniques that scan arrays with two moving positions to avoid nested loops.
Key terms: left and right pointers, window boundaries, invariants, and shrinking and expanding.
Typical uses: finding pairs with a given sum, longest substring problems, and merging sorted arrays.
Risks: moving the wrong pointer, forgetting empty inputs, and breaking the invariant.
Tools: Python lists, collections.deque, and LeetCode.

Mental picture: a small frame that slides along a line of text.

Habit to build: write down the window invariant before coding the loop.
