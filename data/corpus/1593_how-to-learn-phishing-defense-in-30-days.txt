How to Learn Phishing Defense in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Phishing Defense. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term Phishing Defense refers to recognizing and reducing attacks that trick people into revealing information. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn suspicious links and sender verification, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with SPF on a small task such as protecting employees.
Week 3: build a mini project around securing shared mailboxes.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as clicking before checking, and explain Phishing Defense to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

Ask a teammate to explain email filtering back to you; teaching is a quick way to find gaps. Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce.

In short, careful study of Phishing Defense pays off. Spend time on suspicious links and sender verification, and the rest will follow.
