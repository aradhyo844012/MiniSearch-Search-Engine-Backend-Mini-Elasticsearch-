Understanding Embeddings Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture coordinates on a map where similar places are close together.

In that picture, vectors is the first rule you follow, cosine similarity is the next step, and dimensionality and nearest neighbor search keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together, and it involves details such as mixing models between indexing and querying that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for sentence-transformers to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain semantic search to a friend.
