CPU Caches for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover CPU Caches.

Learning goals:
1. Explain CPU Caches in your own words.
2. Describe cache lines, hit and miss rates, levels L1 to L3, and locality.
3. Give examples from speeding up loops and explaining performance differences.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: CPU Caches is small, fast memory close to the processor that stores recently used data. Then draw keeping your most-used books on your desk instead of in the library on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is false sharing a problem? How does hit and miss rates relate to levels L1 to L3? When would you choose perf or Valgrind cachegrind?

Study habit worth keeping: access arrays in the order they are stored.

When you feel ready, compare CPU Caches with Boolean Logic and Gates and write down three differences.
