Feature Stores for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Feature Stores.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Feature Stores in your own words.
2. Describe offline store, online store, point-in-time correctness, and feature definitions.
3. Give examples from sharing features across models and serving real-time predictions.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Feature Stores is systems that store and serve the input variables used by machine learning models. Then draw a shared pantry of prepared ingredients for many recipes on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is leaking future data a problem? How does online store relate to point-in-time correctness? When would you choose Feast or Tecton?

Study habit worth keeping: define each feature once and reuse it in training and serving.

When you feel ready, compare Feature Stores with Data Lakes and write down three differences.
