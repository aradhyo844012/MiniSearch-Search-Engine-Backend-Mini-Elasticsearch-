Where to Go Next After Learning Object-Relational Mappers

You have met the basics of Object-Relational Mappers. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Configuration Management, which belongs to the same area as Object-Relational Mappers (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of models, queries, relationships or migrations, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around switching databases with SQLAlchemy, and note every problem you meet, starting with N+1 queries.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Object-Relational Mappers to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
