The Pros and Cons of Service Workers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Service Workers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports offline support
- Makes faster repeat visits easier
- Helps with push notifications

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: caching stale files forever
- Risk: bugs surviving across deployments
- Risk: forgetting that HTTPS is required

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as offline support, and you can manage the risks, Service Workers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, version your caches so old files can be removed.
