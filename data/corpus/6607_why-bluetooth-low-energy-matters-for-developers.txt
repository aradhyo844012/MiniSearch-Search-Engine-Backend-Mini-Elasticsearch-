Why Bluetooth Low Energy Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Bluetooth Low Energy make better decisions, because Bluetooth Low Energy is a short-range wireless technology designed for very low power use.

Three situations show why it matters. First, fitness trackers. Second, smart locks. Third, proximity beacons. In each case, a solid grasp of advertising and GATT services separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: ignoring connection limits, weak pairing security, and assuming long range.

Good tools exist, including nRF Connect, BlueZ, and Android Bluetooth APIs, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
