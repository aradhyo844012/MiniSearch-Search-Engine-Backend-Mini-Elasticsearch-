Teaching Dijkstra's Algorithm to a Teammate

Explaining Dijkstra's Algorithm to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a GPS that keeps expanding to the nearest unvisited intersection. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Dijkstra's Algorithm is a method for finding the shortest path from one node to all others in a graph with non-negative weights. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving route planning, naming priority queue and distance table as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain edge relaxation in Dijkstra's Algorithm back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, using it with negative edge weights, and suggest this habit: stop as soon as the destination is popped from the queue.
