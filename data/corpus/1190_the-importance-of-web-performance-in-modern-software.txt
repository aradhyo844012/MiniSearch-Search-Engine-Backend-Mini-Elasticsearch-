The Importance of Web Performance in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Web Performance until something goes wrong without it. When people talk about Web Performance, they usually mean the practice of making websites load quickly and respond smoothly. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Browser developer tools are the fastest way to understand why a page behaves as it does. Small improvements in markup and styling often have a large effect on usability.

You will see Web Performance whenever people work on shopping sites or news pages. It also helps with search interfaces.

Three issues come up again and again: shipping large JavaScript bundles; unoptimized images; and too many network requests. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

If you are comfortable with Web Performance, explore Bootstrap next. The two often appear in the same project.

With steady practice, Web Performance will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
