Why Game Engines Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Game Engines make better decisions, because Game Engines is software frameworks that provide graphics, physics, input and tools for building games.

Three situations show why it matters. First, indie games. Second, simulations. Third, interactive demos. In each case, a solid grasp of scenes and assets separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: choosing an engine by hype, loading everything at start-up, and ignoring platform limits.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Unity, Godot, and Unreal Engine cover most needs.
