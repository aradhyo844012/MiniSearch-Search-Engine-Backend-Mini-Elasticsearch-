Number Theory and RSA Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Number Theory and RSA.

Do:
- Learn prime numbers before anything else.
- Try Python pow on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
- Test the case of public key cryptography early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using small primes.
- Ignore this: implementing crypto by hand in production.
- Ignore this: ignoring padding.
- Adopt Number Theory and RSA just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Number Theory and RSA, a little patience with modular arithmetic pays off more than any clever shortcut.
