Debugging Game Engines: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Game Engines, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: choosing an engine by hype, loading everything at start-up, and ignoring platform limits.
Step 3: revisit scenes and assets, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Unity or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Game Engines in work such as indie games come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
