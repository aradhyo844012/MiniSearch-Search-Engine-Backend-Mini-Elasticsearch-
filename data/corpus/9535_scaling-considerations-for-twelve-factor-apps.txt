Scaling Considerations for Twelve-Factor Apps

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Twelve-Factor Apps changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding config in the environment and getting building web services right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for storing state on local disks and hard-coded config, and measure with Heroku before changing anything.

Large scale: backing services and disposability move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around deploying to platforms at this stage.

Before you scale, keep config out of the code. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
