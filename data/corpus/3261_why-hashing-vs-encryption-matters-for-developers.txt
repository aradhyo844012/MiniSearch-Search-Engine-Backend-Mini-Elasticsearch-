Why Hashing vs Encryption Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Hashing vs Encryption make better decisions, because Hashing vs Encryption is the difference between one-way digests and reversible protection of data.

Three situations show why it matters. First, storing passwords. Second, verifying file integrity. Third, protecting data in transit. In each case, a solid grasp of one-way functions and keys separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them, using broken hash functions, and confusing encoding with encryption.

Good tools exist, including SHA-256, AES, and OpenSSL, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
