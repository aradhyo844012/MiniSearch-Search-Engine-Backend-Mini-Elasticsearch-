Augmented and Virtual Reality in Real Projects

Reading about Augmented and Virtual Reality is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: training simulations. Here, head tracking drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against motion sickness from low frame rates.

Scenario two: games. This time rendering at high frame rates matters most, and tools such as Unity XR help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: remote collaboration. The key idea is spatial mapping, and the biggest risk is heavy assets.

Across all three scenarios, Augmented and Virtual Reality stays the same idea: technologies that overlay digital content on the real world or immerse users in a virtual one. Only the details change.
