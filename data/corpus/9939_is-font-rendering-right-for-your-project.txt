Is Font Rendering Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Font Rendering, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen.

It is a good fit when your work involves web pages or operating systems, and when your team understands glyphs.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn hinting, or when you cannot manage these risks: missing font fallbacks, blurry text from scaling, and large font files.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether anti-aliasing and kerning are part of the answer. If they are, Font Rendering is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with PDF viewers and subset fonts to the characters you need.
