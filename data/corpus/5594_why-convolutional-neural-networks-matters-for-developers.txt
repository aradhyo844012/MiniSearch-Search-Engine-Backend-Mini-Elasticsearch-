Why Convolutional Neural Networks Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Convolutional Neural Networks make better decisions, because Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns.

Three situations show why it matters. First, image classification. Second, medical image analysis. Third, object detection. In each case, a solid grasp of filters and pooling separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: too little training data, ignoring data augmentation, and overly deep models for simple tasks.

Good tools exist, including PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Keras, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
