Debugging Raspberry Pi: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Raspberry Pi, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: SD card corruption, inadequate power supplies, and exposing the device without hardening.
Step 3: revisit GPIO pins and Linux, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Raspberry Pi OS or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Raspberry Pi in work such as home automation come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
