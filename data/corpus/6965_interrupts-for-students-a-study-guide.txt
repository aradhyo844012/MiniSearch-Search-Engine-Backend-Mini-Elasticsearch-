Interrupts for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Interrupts.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Interrupts in your own words.
2. Describe interrupt handlers, interrupt vectors, priorities, and context saving.
3. Give examples from keyboard and mouse input and timers.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Interrupts is signals that make a processor pause its work to handle an urgent event. Then draw a phone call interrupting your reading on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is long handlers a problem? How does interrupt vectors relate to priorities? When would you choose Linux /proc/interrupts or logic analyzers?

Study habit: keep interrupt handlers short and defer the heavy work.

When you feel ready, compare Interrupts with Compilers and Interpreters and write down three differences.
