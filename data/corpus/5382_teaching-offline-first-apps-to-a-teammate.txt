Teaching Offline-First Apps to a Teammate

Explaining Offline-First Apps to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: writing in a notebook and mailing the pages later. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Offline-First Apps is apps designed to work without a network and sync when a connection returns. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving field service tools, naming local storage and sync queues as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain conflict resolution in Offline-First Apps back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, unresolved sync conflicts, and suggest this habit: show users which changes have not synced yet.
