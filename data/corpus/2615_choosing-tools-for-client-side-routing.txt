Choosing Tools for Client-Side Routing

There is no single best tool for Client-Side Routing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

React Router is a common first choice, especially for single-page apps. Vue Router suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Next.js is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle dashboards well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: broken deep links.

As a rule of thumb, test refreshing the page on every route, no matter which tool you use.
