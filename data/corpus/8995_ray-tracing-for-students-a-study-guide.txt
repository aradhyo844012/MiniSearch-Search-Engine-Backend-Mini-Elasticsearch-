Ray Tracing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Ray Tracing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Ray Tracing in your own words.
2. Describe rays, intersections, reflection and refraction, and shadows.
3. Give examples from movie visuals and architectural visualization.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Ray Tracing is a rendering technique that follows rays of light to produce realistic images. Then draw tracing a flashlight beam backward from your eye into the scene on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is slow render times a problem? How does intersections relate to reflection and refraction? When would you choose Blender Cycles or NVIDIA OptiX?

Study habit worth keeping: start with spheres and a single light before adding features.

When you feel ready, compare Ray Tracing with Color Spaces and write down three differences.
