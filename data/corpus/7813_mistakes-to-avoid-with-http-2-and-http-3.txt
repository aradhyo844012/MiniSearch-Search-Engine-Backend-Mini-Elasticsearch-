Mistakes to Avoid with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3

Everyone learns HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: assuming old tricks like sprite sheets still help. It usually appears when someone skips multiplexing and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: middleboxes blocking UDP. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of faster page loads. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: misconfigured fallbacks. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on server push to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep a working HTTP/1.1 fallback.
