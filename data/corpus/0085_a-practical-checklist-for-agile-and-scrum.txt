A Practical Checklist for Agile and Scrum

Use this checklist before you rely on Agile and Scrum in a project.

1. Confirm that your team agrees on what Agile and Scrum should accomplish.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example student team projects.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Guard against this risk: treating estimates as promises.
5. Guard against this risk: overloading sprints.
6. Measure results before and after any change.
7. Write at least one automated test that covers sprints.
