Scaling Considerations for Random Forests

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Random Forests changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding bagging and getting tabular predictions right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for treating importance scores as causes and very large forests being slow, and measure with scikit-learn before changing anything.

Large scale: voting and out-of-bag error move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around feature importance ranking at this stage.

Before you scale, tune the number of trees only after the basics work. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
