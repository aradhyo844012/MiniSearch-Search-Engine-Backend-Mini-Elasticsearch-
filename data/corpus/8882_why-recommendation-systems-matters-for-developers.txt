Why Recommendation Systems Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Recommendation Systems make better decisions, because Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.

Three situations show why it matters. First, streaming services. Second, online shops. Third, news feeds. In each case, a solid grasp of collaborative filtering and content-based filtering separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: feedback loops, popularity bias, and ignoring new users.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Surprise, implicit, and TensorFlow Recommenders cover most needs.
