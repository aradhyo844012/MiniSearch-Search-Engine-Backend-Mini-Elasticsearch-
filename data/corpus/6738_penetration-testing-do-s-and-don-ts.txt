Penetration Testing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Penetration Testing.

Do:
- Learn scoping before anything else.
- Try OWASP ZAP on a small example.
- Make it a habit to get written authorization before any test.
- Test the case of pre-launch security checks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: testing without written permission.
- Ignore this: ignoring the findings afterward.
- Ignore this: relying only on automated scans.
- Adopt Penetration Testing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Penetration Testing, a little patience with reconnaissance pays off more than any clever shortcut.
