Teaching Physics Engines to a Teammate

Explaining Physics Engines to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a stage manager who enforces gravity on every prop. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Physics Engines is software that simulates forces, motion and collisions of objects. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving vehicles and ragdolls, naming rigid bodies and forces and impulses as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain constraints in Physics Engines back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, variable time steps, and suggest this habit: use a fixed time step for stable results.
