Practice Exercises for Cloud Cost Optimization

These exercises build real understanding of Cloud Cost Optimization. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how tagging and usage reports differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Cloud Cost Optimization and include the term right-sizing.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble cutting idle resources or budgeting by team.
Exercise 4: Pick AWS Cost Explorer and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 5: Compare Infracost and Kubecost for choosing storage tiers and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Cloud Cost Optimization is the practice of reducing cloud spending while keeping performance and reliability.
