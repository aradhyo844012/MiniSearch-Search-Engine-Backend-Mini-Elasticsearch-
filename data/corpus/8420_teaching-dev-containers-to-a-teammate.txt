Teaching Dev Containers to a Teammate

Explaining Dev Containers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a ready-made workshop that opens identically for everyone. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Dev Containers is containerized development environments that every teammate can start in the same way. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving onboarding new developers, naming devcontainer.json and images as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain mounted folders in Dev Containers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, slow image builds, and suggest this habit: pin tool versions inside the container definition.
