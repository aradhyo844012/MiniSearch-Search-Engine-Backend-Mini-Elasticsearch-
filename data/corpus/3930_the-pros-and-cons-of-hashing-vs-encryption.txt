The Pros and Cons of Hashing vs Encryption

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Hashing vs Encryption is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports storing passwords
- Makes verifying file integrity easier
- Helps with protecting data in transit

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: encrypting passwords instead of hashing them
- Risk: using broken hash functions
- Risk: confusing encoding with encryption

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as storing passwords, and you can manage the risks, Hashing vs Encryption is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, ask whether you will ever need the original value back.
