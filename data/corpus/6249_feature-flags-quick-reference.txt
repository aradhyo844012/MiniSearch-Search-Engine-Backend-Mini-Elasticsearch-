Feature Flags Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Feature Flags. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code.
Key terms: flag evaluation, targeting rules, gradual rollout, and flag cleanup.
Typical uses: dark launches, A/B tests, and emergency kill switches.
Risks: never removing old flags, flags that depend on each other, and untested flag combinations.
Tools: LaunchDarkly, Unleash, and OpenFeature.

Mental picture: light switches wired to every room of a house.

Habit to build: give every flag an owner and a removal date.

See also: Disaster Recovery, which sits close to Feature Flags in the area of DevOps and Cloud.
