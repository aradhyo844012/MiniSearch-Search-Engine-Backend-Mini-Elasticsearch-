Apache Spark Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Apache Spark. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines.
Key terms: DataFrames, transformations and actions, partitions, and lazy execution.
Typical uses: large ETL jobs, machine learning on big data, and log analysis.
Risks: data skew, too many small partitions, and collecting huge results onto the driver.
Tools: PySpark, Databricks, and the Spark UI.

Mental picture: a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers.

Habit to build: avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.

See also: Star Schema Modeling, which sits close to Apache Spark in the area of Data and Analytics.
