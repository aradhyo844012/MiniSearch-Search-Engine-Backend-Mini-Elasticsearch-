Digital Twins Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Digital Twins regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip sensor data; everything else builds on it.
2. Be careful with this one: stale data.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit simulation models before anything else.
4. Test the case of factory monitoring early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can define the question the twin must answer first.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to building management as well as to Digital Twins in general.
