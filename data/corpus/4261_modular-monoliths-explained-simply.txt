Modular Monoliths Explained Simply

Here is Modular Monoliths without the jargon: it is single deployable applications built from well-separated internal modules.

Think of Modular Monoliths as an apartment building with separate flats under one roof. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: module boundaries, public interfaces, shared database rules, and dependency checks. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with module boundaries, then add public interfaces.

You will meet Modular Monoliths in work such as small teams, early-stage products, and preparing for later service extraction.

One practical tip is to enforce boundaries with automated checks.
