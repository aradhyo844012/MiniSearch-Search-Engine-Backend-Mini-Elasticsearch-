How to Learn Web Performance in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Web Performance. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Many students first meet Web Performance in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to the practice of making websites load quickly and respond smoothly.

Week 1: learn page weight and caching, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Lighthouse on a small task such as shopping sites.
Week 3: build a mini project around news pages.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as shipping large JavaScript bundles, and explain Web Performance to a friend.

In practice you can start with Lighthouse, then explore WebPageTest or Chrome DevTools as your needs grow.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Web Performance whenever a new project gives you a reason.
