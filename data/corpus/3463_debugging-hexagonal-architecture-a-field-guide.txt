Debugging Hexagonal Architecture: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Hexagonal Architecture, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: too many layers for small apps, leaking framework types into the core, and confusing ports with adapters.
Step 3: revisit ports and adapters, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Spring or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Hexagonal Architecture in work such as testable business logic come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, keep framework imports out of the core domain. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
