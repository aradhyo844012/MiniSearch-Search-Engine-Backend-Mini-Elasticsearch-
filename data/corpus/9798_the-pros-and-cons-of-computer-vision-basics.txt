The Pros and Cons of Computer Vision Basics

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Computer Vision Basics is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports face detection
- Makes document scanning easier
- Helps with quality inspection

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: lighting changes
- Risk: biased datasets
- Risk: overfitting to a small set of photos

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as face detection, and you can manage the risks, Computer Vision Basics is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.
