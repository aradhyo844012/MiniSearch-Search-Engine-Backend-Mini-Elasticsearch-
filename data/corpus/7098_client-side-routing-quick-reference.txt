Client-Side Routing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Client-Side Routing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: changing the visible page in the browser without a full page reload.
Key terms: history API, route matching, nested routes, and route guards.
Typical uses: single-page apps, dashboards, and multi-step flows.
Risks: broken deep links, unhandled 404 pages, and lost scroll position.
Tools: React Router, Vue Router, and Next.js.

Mental picture: swapping the picture in a frame without replacing the frame.

Habit to build: test refreshing the page on every route.
