CQRS Glossary for Beginners

New words can make CQRS feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

CQRS: a pattern that separates the models used to change data from the models used to read it.
commands: a core term, most visible in high-read dashboards.
queries: a core term that beginners often confuse with read models.
read models: a core term that connects to eventual consistency.
eventual consistency: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Axon Framework: a tool commonly used for complex domains.

Come back to this CQRS list whenever documentation for MediatR uses a word you do not recognize.
