Mocking and Stubbing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Mocking and Stubbing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Mocking and Stubbing: replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests.
mocks: a core term, most visible in isolating units.
stubs: a core term that beginners often confuse with spies.
spies: a core term that connects to fakes.
fakes: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
unittest.mock: a tool commonly used for simulating failures.

Come back to this Mocking and Stubbing list whenever documentation for Mockito uses a word you do not recognize.
