Debugging Model Drift and Monitoring: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Model Drift and Monitoring, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: monitoring only system health, having no ground truth labels, and alert fatigue.
Step 3: revisit data drift and concept drift, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Evidently or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Model Drift and Monitoring in work such as catching degrading models come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, monitor input data as well as accuracy. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
