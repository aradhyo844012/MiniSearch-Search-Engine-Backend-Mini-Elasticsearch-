Embeddings Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Embeddings regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: mixing models between indexing and querying.
2. Test the case of semantic search early, before the design hardens.
3. Do not skip vectors; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
5. Write down why you chose Embeddings so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to recommendations as well.
