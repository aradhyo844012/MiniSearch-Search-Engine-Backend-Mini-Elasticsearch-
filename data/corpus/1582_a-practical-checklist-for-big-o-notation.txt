A Practical Checklist for Big O Notation

Use this checklist before you rely on Big O Notation in a project.

1. Review the core ideas: upper bounds, constant, linear and logarithmic growth, worst case, and amortized cost.
2. Guard against this risk: mixing up best and worst case.
3. Confirm that your team agrees on what Big O Notation should accomplish.
4. Guard against this risk: measuring only tiny inputs.
5. Document your decisions in the project README.
6. Write at least one automated test that covers upper bounds.
7. Try the approach on a small case, for example comparing algorithms.
8. Review how Big O Notation interacts with Tries.
9. Measure results before and after any change.
