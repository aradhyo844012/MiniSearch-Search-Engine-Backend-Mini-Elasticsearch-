Practice Exercises for Database Schema Design for Beginners

These exercises build real understanding of Database Schema Design for Beginners. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble starting new projects or reviewing existing schemas.
Exercise 2: Explain how keys and naming conventions differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Database Schema Design for Beginners and include the term entities.
Exercise 4: Compare draw.io and PostgreSQL for preparing for migrations and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Pick dbdiagram.io and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Database Schema Design for Beginners is planning tables, columns and relationships before building an application.
