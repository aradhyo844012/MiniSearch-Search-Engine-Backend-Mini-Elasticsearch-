Skills to Build Around Multi-Cloud Strategy

Knowing the definition of Multi-Cloud Strategy is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with portability and vendor lock-in, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Terraform on a Multi-Cloud Strategy task, and try at least one alternative such as Kubernetes.

Judgment: learn to decide when Multi-Cloud Strategy fits a problem such as avoiding single-provider risk, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example shopping at several stores to avoid depending on one.

Habits: be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve, and write down what you learn after each project.
