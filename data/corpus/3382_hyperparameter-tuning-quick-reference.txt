Hyperparameter Tuning Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Hyperparameter Tuning. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the process of searching for the best settings that control how a model learns.
Key terms: search spaces, grid search, random search, and validation scores.
Typical uses: improving model accuracy, comparing algorithms, and running automated experiments.
Risks: tuning on the test set, searching too many options, and ignoring training cost.
Tools: Optuna, scikit-learn GridSearchCV, and Ray Tune.

Mental picture: adjusting the knobs on a radio until the signal is clear.

Habit to build: try random search before anything fancier.
