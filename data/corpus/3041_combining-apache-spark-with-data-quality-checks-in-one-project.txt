Combining Apache Spark with Data Quality Checks in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Apache Spark and Data Quality Checks can work together in a single project.

Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines. Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent.

Imagine a project that involves large ETL jobs and protecting dashboards. Apache Spark brings DataFrames, while Data Quality Checks brings null checks.

A sensible plan is to start with PySpark for the Apache Spark part and Great Expectations for the Data Quality Checks part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: too many small partitions on one side, and checks only at the end of pipelines on the other.

Picture the whole as a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers, working alongside a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line.

Two habits help: first, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine; second, check data as early as it enters the pipeline.
