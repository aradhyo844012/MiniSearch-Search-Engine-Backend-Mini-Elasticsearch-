Practice Exercises for Stored Procedures and Triggers

These exercises build real understanding of Stored Procedures and Triggers. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble enforcing rules close to the data or audit logging.
Exercise 2: Write a short paragraph describing Stored Procedures and Triggers and include the term procedures.
Exercise 3: Pick PL/pgSQL and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Compare MySQL Workbench and pgTAP for batch updates and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how parameters and side effects differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Stored Procedures and Triggers is code stored inside the database that runs on demand or automatically when data changes.
