Managed Cloud Databases Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Managed Cloud Databases.

Do:
- Learn automated backups before anything else.
- Try Amazon RDS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test restoring a backup before you need it.
- Test the case of launching apps quickly early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: surprise costs.
- Ignore this: limited configuration control.
- Ignore this: ignoring maintenance windows.
- Adopt Managed Cloud Databases just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Managed Cloud Databases, a little patience with read replicas pays off more than any clever shortcut.
