Scaling Considerations for Security Headers

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Security Headers changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding Strict-Transport-Security and getting hardening websites right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for setting headers on only some routes and breaking embeds, and measure with securityheaders.com before changing anything.

Large scale: frame protections and referrer policy move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around reducing clickjacking at this stage.

Before you scale, test the headers on every environment. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
