Responsible AI and Bias Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Responsible AI and Bias.

Do:
- Learn bias in data before anything else.
- Try Fairlearn on a small example.
- Make it a habit to test performance separately for different groups.
- Test the case of hiring tools early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: assuming data is neutral.
- Ignore this: testing only on average performance.
- Ignore this: giving people no way to appeal.
- Adopt Responsible AI and Bias just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Responsible AI and Bias, a little patience with fairness metrics pays off more than any clever shortcut.
