Database Sharding: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Database Sharding?
A: The term Database Sharding refers to splitting a large dataset across several databases so each holds only part of it.

Q: How does it relate to Database Replication?
A: Database Sharding and Database Replication often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Databases.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Vitess, MongoDB sharding, and Citus.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Why do developers use Database Sharding?
A: Common uses include scaling beyond one server, isolating tenants, and spreading write load.
