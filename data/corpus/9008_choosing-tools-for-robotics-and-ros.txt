Choosing Tools for Robotics and ROS

There is no single best tool for Robotics and ROS. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ROS 2 is a common first choice, especially for warehouse robots. Gazebo suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Arduino is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Robotics and ROS by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle drones well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Robotics and ROS, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: ignoring sensor noise.

As a rule of thumb, test in simulation before moving real hardware, no matter which tool you use.
