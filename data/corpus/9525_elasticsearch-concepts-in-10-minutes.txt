Elasticsearch Concepts in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Elasticsearch Concepts.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Elasticsearch Concepts is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn indexes, shards, analyzers, and queries.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a giant library with an index card for every word. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through site search, log analytics, and product catalogs.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for too many shards and mapping explosions, then try Elasticsearch for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
