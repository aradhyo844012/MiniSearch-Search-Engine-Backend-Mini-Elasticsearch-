Relational Databases Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Relational Databases.

Q: Explain Relational Databases in simple words.
A: Relational Databases refers to systems that store data in tables with rows, columns and defined relationships. I would add an example involving storing orders and customers.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with reporting and analytics, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to NoSQL Databases?
A: Both belong to the area of Databases, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: skipping constraints; storing lists in a single column; and missing indexes on filtered columns.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: skipping constraints.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: PostgreSQL, MySQL, and SQLite.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as tables and rows, and add tests aimed at the known risks.
