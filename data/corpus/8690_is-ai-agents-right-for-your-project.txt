Is AI Agents Right for Your Project?

Before adopting AI Agents, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.

It is a good fit when your work involves research assistants or workflow automation, and when your team understands planning.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn tool calls, or when you cannot manage these risks: runaway loops, unchecked tool permissions, and hard-to-debug behavior.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether memory and guardrails are part of the answer. If they are, AI Agents is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with customer support and limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
