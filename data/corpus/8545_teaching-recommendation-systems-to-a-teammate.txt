Teaching Recommendation Systems to a Teammate

Explaining Recommendation Systems to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving streaming services, naming collaborative filtering and content-based filtering as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain ranking in Recommendation Systems back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, feedback loops, and suggest this habit: evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
