Debugging Views and Materialized Views: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Views and Materialized Views, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: stale materialized data, views stacked on views, and surprising performance costs.
Step 3: revisit view definitions and refresh strategies, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use PostgreSQL or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Views and Materialized Views in work such as simplifying repeated queries come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, document how and when each materialized view is refreshed. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
