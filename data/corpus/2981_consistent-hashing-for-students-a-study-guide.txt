Consistent Hashing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Consistent Hashing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Consistent Hashing in your own words.
2. Describe hash ring, virtual nodes, key placement, and rebalancing.
3. Give examples from distributed caches and sharded databases.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Consistent Hashing is a way of mapping keys to servers so that adding or removing servers moves very few keys. Then draw seats around a round table where a new guest only displaces their neighbor on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too few virtual nodes a problem? How does virtual nodes relate to key placement? When would you choose Redis Cluster or Memcached clients?

Study habit: use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load.

When you feel ready, compare Consistent Hashing with Vector Clocks and write down three differences.
