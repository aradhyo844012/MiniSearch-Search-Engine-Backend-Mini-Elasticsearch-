Where to Go Next After Learning Speech Recognition

You have met the basics of Speech Recognition. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Recommendation Systems, which belongs to the same area as Speech Recognition (AI Engineering and MLOps) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of audio features, acoustic models, language models or word error rate, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around captioning with Whisper, and note every problem you meet, starting with accents and background noise.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Speech Recognition to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
