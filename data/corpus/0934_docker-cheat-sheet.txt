Docker Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Docker.

- Definition: a platform for building and running applications in containers.
- Core ideas: Dockerfile; image layers; volumes; networks.
- Use it for: reproducible development setups; shipping applications; CI jobs.
- Watch out for: huge images; secrets baked into images; running as root.
- Tools: Docker Compose, Docker Hub, Podman.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for image layers before building anything large.
- Related topic: Serverless Computing.
