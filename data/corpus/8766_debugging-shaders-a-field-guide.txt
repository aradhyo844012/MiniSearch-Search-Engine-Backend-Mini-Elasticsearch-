Debugging Shaders: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Shaders, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: branches that slow the GPU, precision errors, and platform differences.
Step 3: revisit vertex shaders and fragment shaders, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use GLSL or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Shaders in work such as lighting effects come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
