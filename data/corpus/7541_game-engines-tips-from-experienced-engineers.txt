Game Engines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Game Engines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip scenes; everything else builds on it.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit assets before anything else.
3. Be careful with this one: choosing an engine by hype.
4. Test the case of indie games early, before the design hardens.
5. Make sure you can build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to simulations as well as to Game Engines in general.
