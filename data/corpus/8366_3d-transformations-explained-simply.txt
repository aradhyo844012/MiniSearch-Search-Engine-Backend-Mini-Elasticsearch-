3D Transformations Explained Simply

If 3D Transformations sounds intimidating, relax. It is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of 3D Transformations as moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: translation, rotation, scaling, and matrices. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with translation, then add rotation.

You will meet 3D Transformations in work such as games, modeling tools, and robotics.

One practical tip is to write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
