Scaling Considerations for Star Schema Modeling

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Star Schema Modeling changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding fact tables and getting business intelligence reports right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for mixing different grains and over-normalized dimensions, and measure with dbt before changing anything.

Large scale: surrogate keys and grain move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around data warehouses at this stage.

Before you scale, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
