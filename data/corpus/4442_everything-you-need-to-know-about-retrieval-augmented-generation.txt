Everything You Need to Know About Retrieval-Augmented Generation

This page collects the essentials about Retrieval-Augmented Generation in one place. In short, Retrieval-Augmented Generation is a technique that gives a language model relevant documents to read before it answers.

Core concepts:
- retrieval
- chunking
- prompts with context
- grounding

Where it is used:
- company knowledge assistants
- question answering over documents
- support bots

Common problems:
- poor chunking
- irrelevant retrieved text
- no evaluation of the answers

Tools to know:
- LangChain
- FAISS
- Elasticsearch

Remember this above all: an open-book exam where the student first looks up the right pages is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to evaluate retrieval quality separately from answer quality.
