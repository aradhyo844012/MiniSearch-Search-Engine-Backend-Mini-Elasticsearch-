Mistakes to Avoid with Raft Consensus

Everyone learns Raft Consensus partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: even-sized clusters. It usually appears when someone skips leader election and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow disks causing elections. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of configuration stores. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: network delays above election timeouts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on majority quorum to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use an odd number of nodes.
