Where to Go Next After Learning Hashing vs Encryption

You have met the basics of Hashing vs Encryption. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CSRF, which belongs to the same area as Hashing vs Encryption (Application Security) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of one-way functions, keys, collision resistance or reversibility, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around verifying file integrity with SHA-256, and note every problem you meet, starting with encrypting passwords instead of hashing them.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Hashing vs Encryption to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
