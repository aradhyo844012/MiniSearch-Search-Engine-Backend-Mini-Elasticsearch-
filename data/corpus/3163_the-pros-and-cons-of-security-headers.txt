The Pros and Cons of Security Headers

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Security Headers is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports hardening websites
- Makes reducing clickjacking easier
- Helps with enforcing HTTPS

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: setting headers on only some routes
- Risk: breaking embeds
- Risk: copying configurations without understanding them

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as hardening websites, and you can manage the risks, Security Headers is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test the headers on every environment.
