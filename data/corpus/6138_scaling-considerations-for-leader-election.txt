Scaling Considerations for Leader Election

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Leader Election changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding heartbeats and getting database primaries right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for split brain and flapping leaders, and measure with ZooKeeper before changing anything.

Large scale: candidates and failover move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around job schedulers at this stage.

Before you scale, tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
