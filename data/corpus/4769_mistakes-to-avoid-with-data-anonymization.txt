Mistakes to Avoid with Data Anonymization

Everyone learns Data Anonymization partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: re-identification through combined fields. It usually appears when someone skips masking and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: removing too little. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of sharing datasets with researchers. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: destroying usefulness by removing too much. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on differential privacy to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test whether combining fields could identify a person.
