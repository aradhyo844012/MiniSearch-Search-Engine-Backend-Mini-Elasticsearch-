Distributed Tracing Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Distributed Tracing feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Distributed Tracing: following a single request as it passes through many services.
spans: a core term, most visible in finding slow services.
trace IDs: a core term that beginners often confuse with context propagation.
context propagation: a core term that connects to sampling.
sampling: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
OpenTelemetry: a tool commonly used for understanding dependencies.

Come back to this Distributed Tracing list whenever documentation for Jaeger uses a word you do not recognize.
