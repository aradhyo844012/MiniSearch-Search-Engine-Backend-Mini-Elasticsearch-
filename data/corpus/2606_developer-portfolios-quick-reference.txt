Developer Portfolios Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Developer Portfolios. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.
Key terms: projects, README quality, live demos, and clear descriptions.
Typical uses: applying for internships, showing skills to recruiters, and tracking personal growth.
Risks: too many unfinished projects, no explanation of decisions, and broken demo links.
Tools: GitHub Pages, Vercel, and Netlify.

Mental picture: a shop window displaying your best work.

Habit to build: feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
