Boolean Logic and Gates for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Boolean Logic and Gates.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Boolean Logic and Gates in your own words.
2. Describe AND, OR and NOT, truth tables, De Morgan's laws, and logic gates.
3. Give examples from writing conditions and designing circuits.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Boolean Logic and Gates is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from. Then draw light switches wired in series or in parallel on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is operator precedence mistakes a problem? How does truth tables relate to De Morgan's laws? When would you choose Logisim or Python bitwise operators?

Study habit: write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.

When you feel ready, compare Boolean Logic and Gates with CPU Caches and write down three differences.
