The Pros and Cons of Penetration Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Penetration Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports pre-launch security checks
- Makes compliance audits easier
- Helps with validating defenses

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: testing without written permission
- Risk: ignoring the findings afterward
- Risk: relying only on automated scans

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as pre-launch security checks, and you can manage the risks, Penetration Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, get written authorization before any test.
