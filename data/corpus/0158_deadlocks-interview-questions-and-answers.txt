Deadlocks Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Deadlocks.

Q: Explain Deadlocks in simple words.
A: Deadlocks refers to situations where two or more tasks wait for each other forever. I would add an example involving database lock conflicts.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as mutual exclusion, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: mutual exclusion, hold and wait, circular wait, and lock ordering.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: thread dumps, database deadlock logs, and static analyzers.

Q: How does it connect to File Systems?
A: Both belong to the area of Operating Systems, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
