Go Goroutines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Go Goroutines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Reach for the go command first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as the race detector.
2. Do not skip goroutines; everything else builds on it.
3. Make sure you can run your tests with the race detector turned on.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit channels before anything else.
5. Test the case of servers handling many connections early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to parallel data processing as well.
