Arduino Basics Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Arduino Basics regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit digital and analog pins before anything else.
2. Do not skip sketches; everything else builds on it.
3. Be careful with this one: wiring errors.
4. Make sure you can test each sensor on its own before combining them.
5. Write down why you chose Arduino Basics so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to driving motors and LEDs as well.
