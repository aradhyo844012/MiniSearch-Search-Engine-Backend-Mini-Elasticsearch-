Where to Go Next After Learning Secrets Management

You have met the basics of Secrets Management. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Autoscaling, which belongs to the same area as Secrets Management (DevOps and Cloud) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of vaults, rotation, access policies or audit logs, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around API keys with HashiCorp Vault, and note every problem you meet, starting with secrets in source control.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Secrets Management to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
