Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Prompt Engineering

This path is for a learner who has never studied Prompt Engineering before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Prompt Engineering is the practice of writing clear instructions and examples to get useful output from language models) and learn instructions and examples.

Stage 2, hands-on: install model playgrounds, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving drafting content.

Stage 3, depth: study context and output format, then deliberately explore this problem: vague requests.

Stage 4, application: apply Prompt Engineering to extracting data from text, compare LangChain with evaluation sets, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Prompt Engineering to someone else, and then move on to Responsible AI and Bias.
