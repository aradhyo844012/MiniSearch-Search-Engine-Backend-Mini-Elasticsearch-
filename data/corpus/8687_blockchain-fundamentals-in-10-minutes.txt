Blockchain Fundamentals in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Blockchain Fundamentals.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Blockchain Fundamentals is a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn blocks, hashes, consensus, and decentralization.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through digital currencies, supply chain tracking, and public records.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for treating it as a database replacement and slow throughput, then try Bitcoin Core for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
