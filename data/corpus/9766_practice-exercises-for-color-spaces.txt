Practice Exercises for Color Spaces

These exercises build real understanding of Color Spaces. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble web design or photo editing.
Exercise 2: Compare ImageMagick and Coolors for printing and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick browser color pickers and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Explain how CMYK and gamma differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: ignoring gamma. Then fix it.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.
