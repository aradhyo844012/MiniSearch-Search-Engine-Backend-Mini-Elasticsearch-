NoSQL Databases Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of NoSQL Databases.

- Definition: a broad family of databases that do not use the traditional relational table model.
- Core ideas: document stores; key-value stores; column families; graph databases.
- Use it for: flexible schemas; high write throughput; caching and sessions.
- Watch out for: assuming no schema means no design; weak consistency surprises; expensive cross-collection queries.
- Tools: MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis.
- Related topic: Database Replication.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for key-value stores before building anything large.
