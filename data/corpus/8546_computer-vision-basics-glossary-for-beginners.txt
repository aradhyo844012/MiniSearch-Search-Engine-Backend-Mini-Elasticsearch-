Computer Vision Basics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Computer Vision Basics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Computer Vision Basics: teaching computers to extract information from images and video.
pixels: a core term, most visible in face detection.
filters and edges: a core term that beginners often confuse with feature detection.
feature detection: a core term that connects to classification.
classification: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
OpenCV: a tool commonly used for document scanning.
