Secrets Management Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Secrets Management. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely.
Key terms: vaults, rotation, access policies, and audit logs.
Typical uses: database credentials, API keys, and TLS certificates.
Risks: secrets in source control, long-lived shared credentials, and no rotation plan.
Tools: HashiCorp Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, and SOPS.

Mental picture: a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet.

Habit to build: rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.

See also: Log Aggregation, which sits close to Secrets Management in the area of DevOps and Cloud.
