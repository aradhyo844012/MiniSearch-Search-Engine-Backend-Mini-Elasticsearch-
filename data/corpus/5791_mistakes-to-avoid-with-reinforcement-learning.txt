Mistakes to Avoid with Reinforcement Learning

Everyone learns Reinforcement Learning partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: badly designed rewards. It usually appears when someone skips agent and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: slow training. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of game playing. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: unstable results across runs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on policy to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with simple environments.
