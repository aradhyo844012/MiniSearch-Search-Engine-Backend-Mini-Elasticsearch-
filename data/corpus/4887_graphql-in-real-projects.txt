GraphQL in Real Projects

Reading about GraphQL is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: mobile apps with limited bandwidth. Here, schema drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against the N+1 query problem.

Scenario two: aggregating many services. This time queries and mutations matters most, and tools such as Apollo help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: flexible dashboards. The key idea is resolvers, and the biggest risk is unbounded query depth.

Across all three scenarios, GraphQL stays the same idea: a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need. Only the details change.
