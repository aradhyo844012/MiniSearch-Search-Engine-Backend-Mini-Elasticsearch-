Where to Go Next After Learning Python Decorators

You have met the basics of Python Decorators. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Python Asyncio, which belongs to the same area as Python Decorators (Programming Languages) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of higher-order functions, wrapper functions, functools.wraps or decorator arguments, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around caching results with functools, and note every problem you meet, starting with losing function metadata.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Python Decorators to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
