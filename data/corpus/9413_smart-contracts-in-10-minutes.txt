Smart Contracts in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Smart Contracts.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Smart Contracts is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn contract code, transactions, gas fees, and immutability.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a vending machine that follows its rules with no clerk. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through token systems, automated escrow, and decentralized applications.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for bugs that cannot be patched and reentrancy attacks, then try Solidity for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
