Skills to Build Around Subqueries and CTEs

Knowing the definition of Subqueries and CTEs is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with subqueries and WITH clauses, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with PostgreSQL on a Subqueries and CTEs task, and try at least one alternative such as SQLite.

Judgment: learn to decide when Subqueries and CTEs fits a problem such as breaking complex reports into steps, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers.

Habits: name each CTE after what it returns, and write down what you learn after each project.
