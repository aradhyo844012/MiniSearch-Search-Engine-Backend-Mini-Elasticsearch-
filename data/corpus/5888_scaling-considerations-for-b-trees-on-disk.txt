Scaling Considerations for B-Trees on Disk

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how B-Trees on Disk changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding pages and getting database indexes right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for random writes on fragmented pages and oversized keys, and measure with PostgreSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: node splits and sorted keys move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around file system directories at this stage.

Before you scale, keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
