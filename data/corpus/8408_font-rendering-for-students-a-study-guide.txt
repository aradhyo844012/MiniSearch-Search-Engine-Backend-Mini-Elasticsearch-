Font Rendering for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Font Rendering.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Font Rendering in your own words.
2. Describe glyphs, hinting, anti-aliasing, and kerning.
3. Give examples from web pages and operating systems.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Font Rendering is turning font outlines into readable text on screen. Then draw a calligrapher tracing letter shapes onto a grid of pixels on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is missing font fallbacks a problem? How does hinting relate to anti-aliasing? When would you choose FreeType or Google Fonts?

Study habit worth keeping: subset fonts to the characters you need.

When you feel ready, compare Font Rendering with Image Compression and write down three differences.
