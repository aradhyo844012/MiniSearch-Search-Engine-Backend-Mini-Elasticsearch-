Choosing Tools for Consistent Hashing

There is no single best tool for Consistent Hashing. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Redis Cluster is a common first choice, especially for distributed caches. Memcached clients suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Apache Cassandra is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle sharded databases well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too few virtual nodes.

As a rule of thumb, use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load, no matter which tool you use.
