Where to Go Next After Learning Discrete Mathematics

You have met the basics of Discrete Mathematics. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Information Theory and Entropy, which belongs to the same area as Discrete Mathematics (Mathematics for Computing) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of sets, logic, relations or proofs, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around database theory with Python sets, and note every problem you meet, starting with skipping proofs.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Discrete Mathematics to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
