Practice Exercises for Digital Twins

These exercises build real understanding of Digital Twins. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Compare Unity and MQTT for predictive maintenance and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 2: Pick Azure Digital Twins and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Explain how synchronization and analytics differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: stale data. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Digital Twins and include the term sensor data.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data.
