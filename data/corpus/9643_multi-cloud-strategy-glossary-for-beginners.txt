Multi-Cloud Strategy Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Multi-Cloud Strategy feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Multi-Cloud Strategy: using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.
portability: a core term, most visible in avoiding single-provider risk.
vendor lock-in: a core term that beginners often confuse with abstraction layers.
abstraction layers: a core term that connects to cost comparison.
cost comparison: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Terraform: a tool commonly used for meeting regional rules.

Come back to this Multi-Cloud Strategy list whenever documentation for Kubernetes uses a word you do not recognize.
