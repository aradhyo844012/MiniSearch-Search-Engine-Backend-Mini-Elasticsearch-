Data Lakes for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Data Lakes.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Data Lakes in your own words.
2. Describe raw storage, schema on read, partitioning, and metadata catalogs.
3. Give examples from storing logs and events and machine learning datasets.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. Then draw a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is turning into a data swamp a problem? How does schema on read relate to partitioning? When would you choose Amazon S3 or Delta Lake?

Study habit: register every dataset in a catalog with an owner.

When you feel ready, compare Data Lakes with Data Quality Checks and write down three differences.
