Debugging CSRF: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around CSRF, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: using GET requests for state changes, skipping tokens on cookie-based APIs, and misconfigured SameSite settings.
Step 3: revisit session cookies and anti-CSRF tokens, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Django CSRF middleware or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving CSRF in work such as protecting forms come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
