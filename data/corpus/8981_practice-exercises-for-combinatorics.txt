Practice Exercises for Combinatorics

These exercises build real understanding of Combinatorics. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Python itertools and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Compare math.comb and SymPy for estimating password strength and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: double counting. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble analyzing algorithms or probability.
Exercise 5: Explain how the pigeonhole principle and inclusion-exclusion differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Combinatorics is the art of counting arrangements and selections.
