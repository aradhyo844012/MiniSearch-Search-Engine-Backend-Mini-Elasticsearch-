Hands-On Tutorial: Getting Started with Neural Networks

This hands-on tutorial takes you from zero to a small working experiment with Neural Networks. Put simply, Neural Networks covers models made of layers of connected units that learn patterns from data. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Step 1: Install or open PyTorch and create a new empty project folder.
Step 2: Read about layers and write down a definition in your own words.
Step 3: Build the smallest possible example that uses weights.
Step 4: Add one test or print statement that proves it works.
Step 5: Extend the example to handle a case such as image recognition.
Step 6: Break your example on purpose and see what happens. A good experiment: too little training data.

In practice you can start with PyTorch, then explore TensorFlow or Keras as your needs grow.

Watch out for these problems: too little training data; poor learning rate choices; and not monitoring validation loss.

With steady practice, Neural Networks will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
