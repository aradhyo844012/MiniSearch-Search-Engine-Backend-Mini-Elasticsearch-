The Future of Database Normalization

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of Database Normalization is easy to describe in general terms. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Database Normalization. The term refers to the practice of organizing tables to reduce duplicated data and update anomalies.

Most slow pages in data-driven applications trace back to a handful of slow queries. Good schema design is hard to change once real data and users depend on it.

Expect tools such as ER diagrams and dbdiagram.io to keep improving, and expect newer ideas to keep building on first normal form. The basics, though, tend to stay the same, so time spent on second normal form and third normal form is rarely wasted. Anyone working on designing clean schemas will keep meeting Database Normalization for years to come.
