Where to Go Next After Learning Data Augmentation

You have met the basics of Data Augmentation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Random Forests, which belongs to the same area as Data Augmentation (AI and Machine Learning) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of transformations, label preservation, synthetic samples or augmentation pipelines, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around text classification with Albumentations, and note every problem you meet, starting with changes that alter the label.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Data Augmentation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
