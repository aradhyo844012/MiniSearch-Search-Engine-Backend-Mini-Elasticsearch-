MQTT for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover MQTT.

Learning goals:
1. Explain MQTT in your own words.
2. Describe publish and subscribe, topics, brokers, and quality of service levels.
3. Give examples from sensor networks and smart home devices.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: MQTT is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for small devices and unreliable networks. Then draw a radio station with channels that devices can tune into on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is unsecured brokers a problem? How does topics relate to brokers? When would you choose Mosquitto or paho-mqtt?

Study habit: design topic names like a clear folder structure.

When you feel ready, compare MQTT with Push Notifications and write down three differences.
