Where to Go Next After Learning Greedy Algorithms

You have met the basics of Greedy Algorithms. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Backtracking, which belongs to the same area as Greedy Algorithms (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of greedy choice property, optimal substructure, exchange arguments or local versus global optimum, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around Huffman coding with pen and paper proofs, and note every problem you meet, starting with assuming greedy always works.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Greedy Algorithms to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
