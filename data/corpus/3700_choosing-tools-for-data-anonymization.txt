Choosing Tools for Data Anonymization

There is no single best tool for Data Anonymization. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

ARX is a common first choice, especially for sharing datasets with researchers. Faker suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Google's differential privacy library is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle testing with production-like data well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: re-identification through combined fields.

As a rule of thumb, test whether combining fields could identify a person, no matter which tool you use.
