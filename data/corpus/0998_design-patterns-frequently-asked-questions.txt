Design Patterns: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Design Patterns?
A: The term Design Patterns refers to reusable solutions to common problems in software design.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with singleton and factory, then move on to observer and strategy.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: forcing patterns where they are unnecessary; over-engineering; and copying patterns without understanding the problem.

Q: How does it relate to Code Review?
A: Design Patterns and Code Review often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Software Engineering.
