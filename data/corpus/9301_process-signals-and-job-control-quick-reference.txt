Process Signals and Job Control Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Process Signals and Job Control. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: messages and shell features used to stop, pause and resume running programs.
Key terms: SIGTERM and SIGKILL, foreground and background jobs, kill and trap, and process groups.
Typical uses: stopping runaway programs, shutting down gracefully, and running long tasks in the background.
Risks: using kill -9 first, orphaned child processes, and ignoring cleanup in handlers.
Tools: kill, jobs and fg, and nohup.

Mental picture: a traffic officer's hand signals for running programs.

Habit to build: try SIGTERM before SIGKILL so programs can clean up.
