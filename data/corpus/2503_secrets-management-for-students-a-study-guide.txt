Secrets Management for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Secrets Management.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Secrets Management in your own words.
2. Describe vaults, rotation, access policies, and audit logs.
3. Give examples from database credentials and API keys.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely. Then draw a bank's safe deposit room with a sign-in sheet on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is secrets in source control a problem? How does rotation relate to access policies? When would you choose HashiCorp Vault or AWS Secrets Manager?

Study habit: rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak.

When you feel ready, compare Secrets Management with Feature Flags and write down three differences.
