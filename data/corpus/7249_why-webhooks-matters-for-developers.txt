Why Webhooks Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Webhooks make better decisions, because Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens.

Three situations show why it matters. First, payment notifications. Second, CI triggers. Third, chat integrations. In each case, a solid grasp of event payloads and signature verification separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: not verifying signatures, slow handlers causing timeouts, and processing duplicate events.

Good tools exist, including ngrok, the Stripe CLI, and Postman, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
