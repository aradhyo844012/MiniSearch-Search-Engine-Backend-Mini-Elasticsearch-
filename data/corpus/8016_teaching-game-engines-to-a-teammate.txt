Teaching Game Engines to a Teammate

Explaining Game Engines to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a fully equipped workshop with the tools for building a game. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Game Engines is software frameworks that provide graphics, physics, input and tools for building games. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving indie games, naming scenes and assets as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain scripting in Game Engines back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, choosing an engine by hype, and suggest this habit: build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing.
