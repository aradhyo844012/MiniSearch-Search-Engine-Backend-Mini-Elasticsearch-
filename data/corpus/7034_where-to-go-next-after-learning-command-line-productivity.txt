Where to Go Next After Learning Command Line Productivity

You have met the basics of Command Line Productivity. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Technical Writing, which belongs to the same area as Command Line Productivity (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of aliases, pipes, history search or scripts, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around exploring files and logs with bash, and note every problem you meet, starting with copying commands without understanding them.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Command Line Productivity to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
