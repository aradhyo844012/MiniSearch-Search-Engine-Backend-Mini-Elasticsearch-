The Pros and Cons of Digital Twins

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Digital Twins is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports factory monitoring
- Makes building management easier
- Helps with predictive maintenance

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: stale data
- Risk: over-detailed models
- Risk: unclear goals

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as factory monitoring, and you can manage the risks, Digital Twins is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, define the question the twin must answer first.
