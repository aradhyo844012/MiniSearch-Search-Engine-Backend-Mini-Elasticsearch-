Scaling Considerations for Color Spaces

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Color Spaces changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding RGB and getting web design right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring gamma and mixing color spaces, and measure with browser color pickers before changing anything.

Large scale: CMYK and gamma move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around photo editing at this stage.

Before you scale, work in one color space and convert only at the end. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
