Deadlocks Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Deadlocks.

- Definition: situations where two or more tasks wait for each other forever.
- Core ideas: mutual exclusion; hold and wait; circular wait; lock ordering.
- Use it for: database lock conflicts; multi-threaded programs; resource allocation.
- Watch out for: acquiring locks in inconsistent order; holding locks while waiting on I/O; no timeouts on locks.
- Tools: thread dumps, database deadlock logs, static analyzers.
- Quick tip: practice mutual exclusion until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for hold and wait before building anything large.
