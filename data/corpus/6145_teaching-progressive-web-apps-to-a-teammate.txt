Teaching Progressive Web Apps to a Teammate

Explaining Progressive Web Apps to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a website that packs its own suitcase so it still works when the network is gone. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Progressive Web Apps is web applications that can be installed and work offline like native apps. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving offline-friendly tools, naming web app manifest and service worker as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain installability in Progressive Web Apps back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, ignoring platform limitations, and suggest this habit: test the app in airplane mode.
