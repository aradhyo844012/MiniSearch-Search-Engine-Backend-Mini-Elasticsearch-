Debugging Eventual Consistency: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Eventual Consistency, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: assuming reads are always fresh, unresolved write conflicts, and no user feedback during delays.
Step 3: revisit replication delay and conflict resolution, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Amazon DynamoDB or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Eventual Consistency in work such as global social feeds come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, decide up front how your app handles stale reads. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
