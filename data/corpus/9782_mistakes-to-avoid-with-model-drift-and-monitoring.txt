Mistakes to Avoid with Model Drift and Monitoring

Everyone learns Model Drift and Monitoring partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: monitoring only system health. It usually appears when someone skips data drift and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: having no ground truth labels. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of catching degrading models. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: alert fatigue. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on alerts to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: monitor input data as well as accuracy.
