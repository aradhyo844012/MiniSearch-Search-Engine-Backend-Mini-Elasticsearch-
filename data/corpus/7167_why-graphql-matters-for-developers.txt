Why GraphQL Matters for Developers

Developers who understand GraphQL make better decisions, because GraphQL is a query language for APIs that lets clients ask for exactly the fields they need.

Three situations show why it matters. First, mobile apps with limited bandwidth. Second, aggregating many services. Third, flexible dashboards. In each case, a solid grasp of schema and queries and mutations separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: the N+1 query problem, unbounded query depth, and weak caching.

Good tools exist, including Apollo, GraphQL Playground, and Hasura, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
