Where to Go Next After Learning Competitive Programming

You have met the basics of Competitive Programming. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Debugging Strategies, which belongs to the same area as Competitive Programming (Developer Career and Tools) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of problem patterns, time limits, contests or upsolving, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around learning algorithms with Codeforces, and note every problem you meet, starting with memorizing solutions without understanding.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Competitive Programming to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
