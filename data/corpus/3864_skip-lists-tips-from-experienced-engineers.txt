Skip Lists Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Skip Lists regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: poor random number choices.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit random level assignment before anything else.
3. Do not skip multiple levels; everything else builds on it.
4. Reach for Redis sorted sets first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Java ConcurrentSkipListMap.
5. Test the case of ordered in-memory indexes early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to sorted sets in caches as well.
