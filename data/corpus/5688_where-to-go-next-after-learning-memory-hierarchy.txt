Where to Go Next After Learning Memory Hierarchy

You have met the basics of Memory Hierarchy. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Instruction Pipelines, which belongs to the same area as Memory Hierarchy (Computer Systems) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of registers, cache, main memory or disk, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around explaining slow I/O with lscpu, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring locality.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Memory Hierarchy to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
