Understanding Technical Writing Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture giving directions to a stranger.

In that picture, audience is the first rule you follow, structure is the next step, and examples and editing keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience, and it involves details such as assuming too much knowledge that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for Markdown to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain documentation to a friend.
