Debugging systemd Services: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around systemd Services, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: editing unit files without reloading the daemon, missing restart policies, and ignoring dependency ordering.
Step 3: revisit unit files and targets, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use systemctl or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving systemd Services in work such as starting services at boot come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, run systemctl daemon-reload after every unit file change. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
