Practice Exercises for Image Compression

These exercises build real understanding of Image Compression. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Image Compression and include the term lossy and lossless formats.
Exercise 2: Pick ImageMagick and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: List three situations from your own experience that resemble web images or photo storage.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: repeated recompression. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Compare mozjpeg and Squoosh for sending pictures over slow networks and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Image Compression is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail.
