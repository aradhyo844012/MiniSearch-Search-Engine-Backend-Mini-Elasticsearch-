Debugging Database Constraints: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Database Constraints, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: skipping constraints for speed, missing indexes on foreign keys, and rules enforced only in the app.
Step 3: revisit primary keys and foreign keys, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use PostgreSQL or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Database Constraints in work such as preventing bad data come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, put critical rules in the database as well as in the app. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
