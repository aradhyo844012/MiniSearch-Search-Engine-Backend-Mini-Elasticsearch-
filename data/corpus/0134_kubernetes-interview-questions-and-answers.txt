Kubernetes Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Kubernetes.

Q: Explain Kubernetes in simple words.
A: Kubernetes refers to a system for deploying, scaling and managing containerized applications. I would add an example involving running many containers.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: resource limits missing.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: pods, deployments, services, and ingress.

Q: How would you test it?
A: I would write small unit tests for each core concept, such as pods, and add tests aimed at the known risks.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: resource limits missing; over-complicated setups; and ignoring health probes.

Q: Where would you apply it first?
A: I would start with rolling updates, because it is small enough to test and large enough to show the benefit.

Q: How does it connect to Git Branching?
A: Both belong to the area of Cloud and DevOps, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
