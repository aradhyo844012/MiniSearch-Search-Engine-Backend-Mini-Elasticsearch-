How to Learn Inverted Index in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Inverted Index. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Inverted Index covers a data structure that maps each term to the documents that contain it. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn vocabulary and postings lists, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Lucene on a small task such as full-text search.
Week 3: build a mini project around log search.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as scanning very long postings lists, and explain Inverted Index to a friend.

In practice you can start with Lucene, then explore Elasticsearch or custom Python dictionaries as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of Inverted Index pays off. Spend time on vocabulary and postings lists, and the rest will follow.
