Probability Basics Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Probability Basics feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Probability Basics: the study of how likely events are to happen.
events: a core term, most visible in randomized algorithms.
conditional probability: a core term that beginners often confuse with independence.
independence: a core term that connects to expected value.
expected value: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Python random: a tool commonly used for machine learning.

Come back to this Probability Basics list whenever documentation for NumPy uses a word you do not recognize.
