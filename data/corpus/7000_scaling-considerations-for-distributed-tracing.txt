Scaling Considerations for Distributed Tracing

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Distributed Tracing changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding spans and getting finding slow services right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for missing context propagation and sampling too aggressively, and measure with OpenTelemetry before changing anything.

Large scale: context propagation and sampling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around understanding dependencies at this stage.

Before you scale, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
