Why Regular Expressions Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Regular Expressions make better decisions, because Regular Expressions is compact patterns for matching and extracting text.

Three situations show why it matters. First, validating input. Second, searching logs. Third, extracting fields from text. In each case, a solid grasp of character classes and quantifiers separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: catastrophic backtracking, patterns that are too greedy, and using them to parse HTML.

You will not need to build everything yourself; the Python re module, regex101, and grep cover most needs.
