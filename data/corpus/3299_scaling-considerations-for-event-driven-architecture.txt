Scaling Considerations for Event-Driven Architecture

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Event-Driven Architecture changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding producers and consumers and getting order pipelines right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for hard-to-trace flows and duplicate events, and measure with Apache Kafka before changing anything.

Large scale: loose coupling and asynchronous handling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around real-time notifications at this stage.

Before you scale, document every event name and schema. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
