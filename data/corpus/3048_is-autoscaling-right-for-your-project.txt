Is Autoscaling Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Autoscaling, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

It is a good fit when your work involves handling traffic spikes or saving money overnight, and when your team understands scaling metrics.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn cooldown periods, or when you cannot manage these risks: scaling too slowly, flapping because of short spikes, and ignoring database limits.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether minimum and maximum limits and horizontal scaling are part of the answer. If they are, Autoscaling is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with keeping latency steady and load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
