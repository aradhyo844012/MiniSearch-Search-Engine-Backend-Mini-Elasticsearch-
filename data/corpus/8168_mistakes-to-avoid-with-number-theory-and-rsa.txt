Mistakes to Avoid with Number Theory and RSA

Everyone learns Number Theory and RSA partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: using small primes. It usually appears when someone skips prime numbers and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: implementing crypto by hand in production. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of public key cryptography. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring padding. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on modular exponentiation to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use vetted crypto libraries instead of writing your own.
