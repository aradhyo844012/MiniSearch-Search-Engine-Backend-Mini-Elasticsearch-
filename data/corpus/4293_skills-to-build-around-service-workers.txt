Skills to Build Around Service Workers

Knowing the definition of Service Workers is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with registration and install and activate events, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Workbox on a Service Workers task, and try at least one alternative such as the Chrome DevTools Application panel.

Judgment: learn to decide when Service Workers fits a problem such as offline support, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a receptionist who answers common requests before they reach the manager.

Habits: version your caches so old files can be removed, and write down what you learn after each project.
