3D Transformations for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover 3D Transformations.

Learning goals:
1. Explain 3D Transformations in your own words.
2. Describe translation, rotation, scaling, and matrices.
3. Give examples from games and modeling tools.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space. Then draw moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is wrong multiplication order a problem? How does rotation relate to scaling? When would you choose GLM or three.js?

Study habit worth keeping: write down the order of transformations before multiplying.

When you feel ready, compare 3D Transformations with Image Compression and write down three differences.
