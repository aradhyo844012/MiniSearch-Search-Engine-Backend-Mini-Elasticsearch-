The Pros and Cons of Greedy Algorithms

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Greedy Algorithms is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports interval scheduling
- Makes Huffman coding easier
- Helps with making change with standard coins

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming greedy always works
- Risk: missing counterexamples
- Risk: skipping the correctness proof

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as interval scheduling, and you can manage the risks, Greedy Algorithms is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, try to break your greedy idea with a small counterexample before trusting it.
