Where to Go Next After Learning React Hooks

You have met the basics of React Hooks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about CSS Variables, which belongs to the same area as React Hooks (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of useState, useEffect, dependency arrays or custom hooks, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around fetching data on load with React DevTools, and note every problem you meet, starting with missing dependencies in effects.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining React Hooks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
