Elasticsearch Concepts Explained Simply

If Elasticsearch Concepts sounds intimidating, relax. It is the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Elasticsearch Concepts as a giant library with an index card for every word. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: indexes, shards, analyzers, and queries. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with indexes, then add shards.

You will meet Elasticsearch Concepts in work such as site search, log analytics, and product catalogs.

One practical tip is to define explicit mappings instead of relying on defaults.
