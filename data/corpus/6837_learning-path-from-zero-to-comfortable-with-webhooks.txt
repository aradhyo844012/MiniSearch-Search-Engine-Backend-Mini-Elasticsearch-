Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Webhooks

This path is for a learner who has never studied Webhooks before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens) and learn event payloads and signature verification.

Stage 2, hands-on: install ngrok, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving payment notifications.

Stage 3, depth: study retries and endpoints, then deliberately explore this problem: not verifying signatures.

Stage 4, application: apply Webhooks to CI triggers, compare the Stripe CLI with Postman, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Webhooks to someone else, and then move on to Background Jobs.
