Debugging Robotics and ROS: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Robotics and ROS, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: ignoring sensor noise, unsafe motion commands, and tight coupling between nodes.
Step 3: revisit sensors and actuators, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ROS 2 or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Robotics and ROS in work such as warehouse robots come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, test in simulation before moving real hardware. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
