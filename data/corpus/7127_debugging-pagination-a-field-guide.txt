Debugging Pagination: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Pagination, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: offsets getting slow on huge tables, unstable sort order, and missing total counts when users need them.
Step 3: revisit offset pagination and cursor pagination, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use SQL LIMIT and OFFSET or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Pagination in work such as search results come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, prefer cursors for large or fast-changing data. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
