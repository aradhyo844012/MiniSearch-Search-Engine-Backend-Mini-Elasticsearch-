Data Quality Checks in Real Projects

Reading about Data Quality Checks is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: protecting dashboards. Here, null checks drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against alerts nobody reads.

Scenario two: validating ingestion. This time uniqueness matters most, and tools such as Great Expectations help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: catching pipeline bugs. The key idea is freshness, and the biggest risk is checks only at the end of pipelines.

Across all three scenarios, Data Quality Checks stays the same idea: automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent. Only the details change.
