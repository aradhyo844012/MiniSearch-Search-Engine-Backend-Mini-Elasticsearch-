Video Codecs Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Video Codecs regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip frames; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose Video Codecs so the next person understands the decision.
3. Be careful with this one: mismatched codec support.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit motion compensation before anything else.
5. Test the case of streaming services early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to video calls as well as to Video Codecs in general.
