Understanding Statistical Significance Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture asking how surprised you should be if a coin were actually fair.

In that picture, p-values is the first rule you follow, the null hypothesis is the next step, and confidence intervals and effect size keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Statistical Significance is a measure of how unlikely a result would be if there were no real effect, and it involves details such as mistaking significance for importance that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for SciPy to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain judging experiment results to a friend.
