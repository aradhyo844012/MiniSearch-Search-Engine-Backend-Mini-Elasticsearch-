The Pros and Cons of 5G Networks

No technology is free of trade-offs, and 5G Networks is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports mobile broadband
- Makes connected vehicles easier
- Helps with industrial automation

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: limited coverage on the fastest bands
- Risk: battery drain
- Risk: marketing exaggeration

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as mobile broadband, and you can manage the risks, 5G Networks is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test real-world coverage rather than trusting peak speeds.
