A Practical Checklist for Gradient Descent

Use this checklist before you rely on Gradient Descent in a project.

1. Try the approach on a small case, for example training neural networks.
2. Guard against this risk: getting stuck on plateaus.
3. Document your decisions in the project README.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as PyTorch or NumPy.
5. Confirm that your team agrees on what Gradient Descent should accomplish.
6. Guard against this risk: unscaled features.
7. Guard against this risk: learning rates that are too large.
