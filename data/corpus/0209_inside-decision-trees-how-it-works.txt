Inside Decision Trees: How It Works

This article walks through what happens inside Decision Trees, step by step. Many students first meet Decision Trees in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to models that make predictions by asking a sequence of simple questions about the data.

To understand Decision Trees, start with splits. Then move on to entropy and Gini impurity, and finally look at leaf nodes and pruning. Understanding entropy and Gini impurity first often makes leaf nodes much easier to reason about.
