Scaling Considerations for Database Constraints

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Database Constraints changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding primary keys and getting preventing bad data right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for skipping constraints for speed and missing indexes on foreign keys, and measure with PostgreSQL before changing anything.

Large scale: unique constraints and check constraints move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around protecting relationships at this stage.

Before you scale, put critical rules in the database as well as in the app. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
