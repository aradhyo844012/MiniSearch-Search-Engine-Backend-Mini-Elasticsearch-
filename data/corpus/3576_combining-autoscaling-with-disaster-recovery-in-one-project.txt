Combining Autoscaling with Disaster Recovery in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Autoscaling and Disaster Recovery can work together in a single project.

Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes. Disaster Recovery is the plans and systems that restore services after major failures.

Imagine a project that involves handling traffic spikes and surviving regional outages. Autoscaling brings scaling metrics, while Disaster Recovery brings recovery time objective.

A sensible plan is to start with Kubernetes HPA for the Autoscaling part and AWS Backup for the Disaster Recovery part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: flapping because of short spikes on one side, and outdated runbooks on the other.

Picture the whole as a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives, working alongside a fire drill for a whole system.

Two habits help: first, load test the system to find realistic thresholds; second, run a failover exercise on a regular schedule.
