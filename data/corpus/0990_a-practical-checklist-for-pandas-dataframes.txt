A Practical Checklist for Pandas DataFrames

Use this checklist before you rely on Pandas DataFrames in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: forgetting to check data types.
2. Write at least one automated test that covers rows and columns.
3. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on indexing.
4. Document your decisions in the project README.
5. Try the approach on a small case, for example loading CSV files.
6. Review how Pandas DataFrames interacts with ETL Pipelines.
7. Measure results before and after any change.
8. Review the core ideas: rows and columns, indexing, groupby, and merging.
