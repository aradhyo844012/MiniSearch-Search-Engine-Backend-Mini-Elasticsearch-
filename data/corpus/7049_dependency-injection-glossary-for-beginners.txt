Dependency Injection Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Dependency Injection feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Dependency Injection: a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them.
constructor injection: a core term, most visible in easier unit testing.
interfaces: a core term that beginners often confuse with containers.
containers: a core term that connects to inversion of control.
inversion of control: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Spring: a tool commonly used for swapping implementations.

Come back to this Dependency Injection list whenever documentation for FastAPI Depends uses a word you do not recognize.
