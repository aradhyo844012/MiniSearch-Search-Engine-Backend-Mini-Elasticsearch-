Where to Go Next After Learning Software Estimation

You have met the basics of Software Estimation. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about API Design Principles, which belongs to the same area as Software Estimation (Software Design) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of story points, ranges, uncertainty or velocity, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around setting deadlines with planning poker, and note every problem you meet, starting with treating estimates as promises.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Software Estimation to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
