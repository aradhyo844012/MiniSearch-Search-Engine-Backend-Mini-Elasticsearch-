Understanding Color Spaces Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture different ways of giving directions to the same destination.

In that picture, RGB is the first rule you follow, HSL is the next step, and CMYK and gamma keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK, and it involves details such as ignoring gamma that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for browser color pickers to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain web design to a friend.
