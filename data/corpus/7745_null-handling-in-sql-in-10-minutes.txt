NULL Handling in SQL in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through NULL Handling in SQL.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: NULL Handling in SQL is understanding how missing or unknown values behave in queries.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn three-valued logic, IS NULL, COALESCE, and NULL in aggregates.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine an empty box on a form that means unknown, not zero. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through optional fields, outer joins, and data cleaning.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for comparing with equals instead of IS NULL and NOT IN with NULLs, then try PostgreSQL for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use IS NULL and COALESCE instead of comparing with equals.
