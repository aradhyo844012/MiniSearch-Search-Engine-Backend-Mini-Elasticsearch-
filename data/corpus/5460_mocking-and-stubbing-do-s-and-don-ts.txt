Mocking and Stubbing Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Mocking and Stubbing.

Do:
- Learn mocks before anything else.
- Try unittest.mock on a small example.
- Make it a habit to prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
- Test the case of isolating units early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: over-mocking.
- Ignore this: tests that mirror the implementation.
- Ignore this: mocks that drift from reality.
- Adopt Mocking and Stubbing just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Mocking and Stubbing, a little patience with stubs pays off more than any clever shortcut.
