How to Learn DNS in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with DNS. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. The term DNS refers to the system that translates human-friendly domain names into IP addresses. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Week 1: learn resolvers and records, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with dig on a small task such as mapping domains to servers.
Week 3: build a mini project around email routing.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as forgetting TTLs when migrating, and explain DNS to a friend.

In practice you can start with dig, then explore nslookup or Cloudflare DNS as your needs grow.

In short, careful study of DNS pays off. Spend time on resolvers and records, and the rest will follow.
