Combining Blue-Green Deployment with Autoscaling in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Blue-Green Deployment and Autoscaling can work together in a single project.

Blue-Green Deployment is a release method that keeps two identical environments and switches traffic from the old one to the new one. Autoscaling is automatically adding or removing computing resources as demand changes.

Imagine a project that involves zero-downtime releases and handling traffic spikes. Blue-Green Deployment brings blue and green environments, while Autoscaling brings scaling metrics.

A sensible plan is to start with AWS CodeDeploy for the Blue-Green Deployment part and Kubernetes HPA for the Autoscaling part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: doubling infrastructure costs on one side, and flapping because of short spikes on the other.

Picture the whole as swapping to a fully prepared spare stage while the show goes on, working alongside a restaurant opening extra tables when a crowd arrives.

Two habits help: first, keep the old environment running until the new one is proven; second, load test the system to find realistic thresholds.
