The Pros and Cons of Write-Ahead Logging

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Write-Ahead Logging is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports surviving crashes
- Makes feeding replication streams easier
- Helps with point-in-time recovery

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: disabling fsync for speed
- Risk: log files filling the disk
- Risk: forgetting to archive logs

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as surviving crashes, and you can manage the risks, Write-Ahead Logging is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose.
