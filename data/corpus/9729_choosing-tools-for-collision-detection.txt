Choosing Tools for Collision Detection

There is no single best tool for Collision Detection. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Box2D is a common first choice, especially for platformers. Unity colliders suits teams that already work with similar technology, and quadtrees is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Collision Detection by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle shooters well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Collision Detection, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: tunneling through thin objects.

As a rule of thumb, use simple shapes first and refine only when needed, no matter which tool you use.
