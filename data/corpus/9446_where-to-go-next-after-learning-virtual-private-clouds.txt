Where to Go Next After Learning Virtual Private Clouds

You have met the basics of Virtual Private Clouds. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Cloud-Native Applications, which belongs to the same area as Virtual Private Clouds (Cloud Platforms and Architecture) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of subnets, route tables, security groups or gateways, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around connecting to offices with AWS VPC, and note every problem you meet, starting with overlapping address ranges.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Virtual Private Clouds to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
