Practice Exercises for Vector Graphics (SVG)

These exercises build real understanding of Vector Graphics (SVG). Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how fill and stroke and transforms differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Pick Inkscape and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: overly complex paths. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble logos or icons.
Exercise 5: Compare SVGO and Figma for charts and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Vector Graphics (SVG) is images described by shapes and paths so they stay sharp at any size.
