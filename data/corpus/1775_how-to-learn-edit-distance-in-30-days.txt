How to Learn Edit Distance in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Edit Distance. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. If you build software for long enough, you will run into Edit Distance. The term refers to a measure of how many single-character changes turn one word into another.

Week 1: learn insertions and deletions, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Levenshtein libraries on a small task such as spell checking.
Week 3: build a mini project around fuzzy matching.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as comparing against every word in a large vocabulary, and explain Edit Distance to a friend.

In practice you can start with Levenshtein libraries, then explore deletion-neighbour indexes or difflib as your needs grow.

With steady practice, Edit Distance will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
