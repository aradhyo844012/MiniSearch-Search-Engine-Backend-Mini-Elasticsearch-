The Pros and Cons of Collision Detection

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Collision Detection is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports platformers
- Makes shooters easier
- Helps with physics puzzles

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: tunneling through thin objects
- Risk: checking every pair of objects
- Risk: inconsistent hitboxes

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as platformers, and you can manage the risks, Collision Detection is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
