Developer Portfolios Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Developer Portfolios.

Do:
- Learn projects before anything else.
- Try GitHub Pages on a small example.
- Make it a habit to feature a few polished projects rather than many half-finished ones.
- Test the case of applying for internships early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: too many unfinished projects.
- Ignore this: no explanation of decisions.
- Ignore this: broken demo links.
- Adopt Developer Portfolios just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Developer Portfolios, a little patience with README quality pays off more than any clever shortcut.
