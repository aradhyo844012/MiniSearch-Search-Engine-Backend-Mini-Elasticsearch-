Scaling Considerations for Minimum Spanning Trees

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Minimum Spanning Trees changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding weighted graphs and getting designing cable networks right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for using it on directed graphs and forgetting that the graph must be connected, and measure with NetworkX before changing anything.

Large scale: Prim's algorithm and the cut property move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around clustering data at this stage.

Before you scale, sort the edges once and reuse the order when testing variations. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
