Why High Availability Design Matters for Developers

Developers who understand High Availability Design make better decisions, because High Availability Design is designing systems so they keep working when parts fail.

Three situations show why it matters. First, online stores. Second, banking systems. Third, healthcare software. In each case, a solid grasp of redundancy and failover separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: hidden single points of failure, untested failover, and correlated failures.

You will not need to build everything yourself; load balancers, Kubernetes, and Patroni cover most needs.
