GraphQL Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with GraphQL regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip schema; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose GraphQL so the next person understands the decision.
3. Reach for Apollo first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as GraphQL Playground.
4. Make sure you can use a data loader to avoid N+1 queries.
5. Be careful with this one: the N+1 query problem.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to aggregating many services as well.
