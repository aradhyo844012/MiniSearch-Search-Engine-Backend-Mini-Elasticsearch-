Database Backups and Recovery Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Database Backups and Recovery feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Database Backups and Recovery: the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures.
full and incremental backups: a core term, most visible in recovering deleted tables.
recovery point objective: a core term that beginners often confuse with recovery time objective.
recovery time objective: a core term that connects to restore testing.
restore testing: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
pg_dump: a tool commonly used for migrating servers.

Come back to this Database Backups and Recovery list whenever documentation for mysqldump uses a word you do not recognize.
