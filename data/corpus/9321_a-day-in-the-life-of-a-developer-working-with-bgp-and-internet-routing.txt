A Day in the Life of a Developer Working with BGP and Internet Routing

This is a fictional but realistic day for a developer whose project relies on BGP and Internet Routing.

Morning: Arjun starts by reviewing a ticket about connecting ISPs. Before touching any code, Arjun re-reads the notes on autonomous systems to make sure the plan is sound.

Midday: Arjun pairs with a teammate to explore route announcements with bgpq4. A small test exposes the problem of route leaks, and they fix it together.

Afternoon: the team discusses a new request involving directing traffic between large networks. Arjun sketches the postal service agreeing on which depot handles each region on the whiteboard to help everyone follow the idea.

End of day: Arjun writes a short note describing what changed and adds a reminder to filter route announcements strictly.

Days like this are typical. BGP and Internet Routing is not glamorous, but understanding it keeps small problems from growing.
