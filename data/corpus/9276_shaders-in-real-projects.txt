Shaders in Real Projects

Reading about Shaders is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: lighting effects. Here, vertex shaders drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against branches that slow the GPU.

Scenario two: water and fire visuals. This time fragment shaders matters most, and tools such as GLSL help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: post-processing. The key idea is uniforms, and the biggest risk is precision errors.

Across all three scenarios, Shaders stays the same idea: small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn. Only the details change.
