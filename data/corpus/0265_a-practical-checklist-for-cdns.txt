A Practical Checklist for CDNs

Use this checklist before you rely on CDNs in a project.

1. Review the core ideas: edge servers, cache rules, origin servers, and cache purging.
2. Try the approach on a small case, for example serving images and scripts.
3. Write at least one automated test that covers edge servers.
4. Pick a tool to start with, such as Cloudflare or Akamai.
5. Review how CDNs interacts with IP Addressing and Subnets.
6. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on cache rules.
7. Guard against this risk: caching private data.
8. Measure results before and after any change.
9. Document your decisions in the project README.
