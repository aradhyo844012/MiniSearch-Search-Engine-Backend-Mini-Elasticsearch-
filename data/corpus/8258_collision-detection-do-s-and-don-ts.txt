Collision Detection Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Collision Detection.

Do:
- Learn bounding boxes before anything else.
- Try Box2D on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
- Test the case of platformers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: tunneling through thin objects.
- Ignore this: checking every pair of objects.
- Ignore this: inconsistent hitboxes.
- Adopt Collision Detection just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Collision Detection, a little patience with broad and narrow phases pays off more than any clever shortcut.
