The Pros and Cons of Threat Modeling

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Threat Modeling is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports designing new features
- Makes reviewing architecture easier
- Helps with prioritizing security work

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: doing it once and never again
- Risk: ignoring data flows
- Risk: focusing only on technical threats

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as designing new features, and you can manage the risks, Threat Modeling is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, draw the data flow diagram first.
