Scaling Considerations for Progressive Web Apps

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Progressive Web Apps changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding web app manifest and getting offline-friendly tools right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring platform limitations and poor offline fallbacks, and measure with Lighthouse before changing anything.

Large scale: installability and responsive design move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around lightweight alternatives to native apps at this stage.

Before you scale, test the app in airplane mode. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
