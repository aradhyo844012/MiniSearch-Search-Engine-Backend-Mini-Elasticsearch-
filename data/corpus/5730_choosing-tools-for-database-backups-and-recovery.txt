Choosing Tools for Database Backups and Recovery

There is no single best tool for Database Backups and Recovery. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

pg_dump is a common first choice, especially for recovering deleted tables. mysqldump suits teams that already work with similar technology, and AWS Backup is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle migrating servers well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: never testing restores.

As a rule of thumb, schedule a restore drill on a regular basis, no matter which tool you use.
