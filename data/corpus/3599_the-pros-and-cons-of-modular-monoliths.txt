The Pros and Cons of Modular Monoliths

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Modular Monoliths is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports small teams
- Makes early-stage products easier
- Helps with preparing for later service extraction

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: modules reaching into each other's tables
- Risk: no enforced boundaries
- Risk: treating modules as just folders

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as small teams, and you can manage the risks, Modular Monoliths is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, enforce boundaries with automated checks.
