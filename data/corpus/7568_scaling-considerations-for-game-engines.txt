Scaling Considerations for Game Engines

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Game Engines changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding scenes and getting indie games right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for choosing an engine by hype and loading everything at start-up, and measure with Unity before changing anything.

Large scale: scripting and build pipelines move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around simulations at this stage.

Before you scale, build a tiny prototype in two engines before choosing. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
