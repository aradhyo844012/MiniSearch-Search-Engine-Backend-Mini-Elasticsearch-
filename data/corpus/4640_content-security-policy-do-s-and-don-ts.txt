Content Security Policy Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Content Security Policy.

Do:
- Learn directives before anything else.
- Try browser DevTools on a small example.
- Make it a habit to start in report-only mode to learn what would break.
- Test the case of reducing the impact of XSS early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: allowing unsafe-inline.
- Ignore this: policies too broad to help.
- Ignore this: breaking third-party widgets.
- Adopt Content Security Policy just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Content Security Policy, a little patience with nonces pays off more than any clever shortcut.
