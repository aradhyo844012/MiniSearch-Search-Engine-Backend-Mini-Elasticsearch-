Responsive Design Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of Responsive Design.

- Definition: building pages that adapt smoothly to phones, tablets and large screens.
- Core ideas: media queries; fluid grids; flexible images; mobile-first thinking.
- Use it for: websites used on many devices; dashboards; e-commerce pages.
- Watch out for: testing only on one screen size; tiny touch targets; horizontal scrolling.
- Tools: Chrome DevTools device mode, Bootstrap grid, Lighthouse.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for fluid grids before building anything large.
- Quick tip: practice media queries until it feels automatic.
