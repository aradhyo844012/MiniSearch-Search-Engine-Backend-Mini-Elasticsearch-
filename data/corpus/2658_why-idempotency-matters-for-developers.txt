Why Idempotency Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Idempotency make better decisions, because Idempotency is the property that repeating an operation gives the same result as doing it once.

Three situations show why it matters. First, payment APIs. Second, message processing. Third, retrying failed requests. In each case, a solid grasp of idempotency keys and safe retries separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: keys that expire too soon, side effects outside the guarded step, and reusing keys for different requests.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Stripe idempotency keys, Redis, and database unique constraints cover most needs.
