Procedural Generation Explained Simply

If Procedural Generation sounds intimidating, relax. It is creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Procedural Generation as a chef inventing endless dishes from a fixed set of ingredients. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: random seeds, noise functions, rules and constraints, and reproducibility. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with random seeds, then add noise functions.

You will meet Procedural Generation in work such as roguelike dungeons, infinite terrain, and varied item drops.

One practical tip is to log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.

Once Procedural Generation feels comfortable, read about Shaders, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
