Understanding Subqueries and CTEs Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture solving a puzzle by writing down intermediate answers.

In that picture, subqueries is the first rule you follow, WITH clauses is the next step, and correlated queries and readability keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Subqueries and CTEs is queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets, and it involves details such as correlated subqueries that run slowly that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for PostgreSQL to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain breaking complex reports into steps to a friend.
