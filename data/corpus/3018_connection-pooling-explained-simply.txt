Connection Pooling Explained Simply

Here is Connection Pooling without the jargon: it is reusing a limited set of open database connections instead of opening a new one for every request.

Think of Connection Pooling as a rental desk that lends a few shared bikes instead of buying one per visitor. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: pool size, idle connections, timeouts, and connection leaks. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with pool size, then add idle connections.

You will meet Connection Pooling in work such as web backends, serverless functions that call databases, and high-traffic APIs.

One practical tip is to always return connections in a finally block.
