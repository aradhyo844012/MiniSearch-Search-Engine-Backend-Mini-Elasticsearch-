Webhooks for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Webhooks.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Webhooks in your own words.
2. Describe event payloads, signature verification, retries, and endpoints.
3. Give examples from payment notifications and CI triggers.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Webhooks is HTTP callbacks that let one service notify another when an event happens. Then draw a doorbell that rings when a delivery arrives instead of you checking the porch on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is not verifying signatures a problem? How does signature verification relate to retries? When would you choose ngrok or the Stripe CLI?

Study habit worth keeping: respond quickly and process the event in a background job.

When you feel ready, compare Webhooks with Background Jobs and write down three differences.
