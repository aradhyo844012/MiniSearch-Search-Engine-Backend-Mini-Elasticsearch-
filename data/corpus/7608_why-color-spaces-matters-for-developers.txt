Why Color Spaces Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Color Spaces make better decisions, because Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.

Three situations show why it matters. First, web design. Second, photo editing. Third, printing. In each case, a solid grasp of RGB and HSL separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: ignoring gamma, mixing color spaces, and poor contrast choices.

Good tools exist, including browser color pickers, ImageMagick, and Coolors, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
