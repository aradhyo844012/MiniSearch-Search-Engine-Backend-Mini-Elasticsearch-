Arduino Basics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Arduino Basics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Arduino Basics in your own words.
2. Describe sketches, digital and analog pins, setup and loop functions, and the serial monitor.
3. Give examples from reading sensors and driving motors and LEDs.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Arduino Basics is a beginner-friendly microcontroller platform for reading sensors and controlling devices. Then draw a tiny brain that watches inputs and flips switches on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is wiring errors a problem? How does digital and analog pins relate to setup and loop functions? When would you choose Arduino IDE or PlatformIO?

Study habit worth keeping: test each sensor on its own before combining them.

When you feel ready, compare Arduino Basics with Bluetooth Low Energy and write down three differences.
