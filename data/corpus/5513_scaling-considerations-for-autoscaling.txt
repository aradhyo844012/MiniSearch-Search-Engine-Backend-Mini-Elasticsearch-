Scaling Considerations for Autoscaling

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Autoscaling changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding scaling metrics and getting handling traffic spikes right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for scaling too slowly and flapping because of short spikes, and measure with Kubernetes HPA before changing anything.

Large scale: minimum and maximum limits and horizontal scaling move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around saving money overnight at this stage.

Before you scale, load test the system to find realistic thresholds. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
