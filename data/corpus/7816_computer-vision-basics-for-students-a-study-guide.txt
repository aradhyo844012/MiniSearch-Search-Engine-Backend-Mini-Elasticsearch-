Computer Vision Basics for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Computer Vision Basics.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Computer Vision Basics in your own words.
2. Describe pixels, filters and edges, feature detection, and classification.
3. Give examples from face detection and document scanning.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Computer Vision Basics is teaching computers to extract information from images and video. Then draw teaching a computer to notice shapes the way eyes do on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is lighting changes a problem? How does filters and edges relate to feature detection? When would you choose OpenCV or Pillow?

Study habit worth keeping: visualize intermediate steps such as edges or masks.

When you feel ready, compare Computer Vision Basics with Rasterization and write down three differences.
