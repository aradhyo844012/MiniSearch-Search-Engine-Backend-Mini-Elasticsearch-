Practice Exercises for CPU Caches

These exercises build real understanding of CPU Caches. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: false sharing. Then fix it.
Exercise 2: Compare Valgrind cachegrind and Intel VTune for designing data layouts and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick perf and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble speeding up loops or explaining performance differences.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing CPU Caches and include the term cache lines.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: CPU Caches is small, fast memory close to the processor that stores recently used data.
