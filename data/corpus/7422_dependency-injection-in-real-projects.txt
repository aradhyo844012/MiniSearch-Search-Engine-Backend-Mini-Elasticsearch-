Dependency Injection in Real Projects

Reading about Dependency Injection is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: easier unit testing. Here, constructor injection drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against hiding too much behind containers.

Scenario two: swapping implementations. This time interfaces matters most, and tools such as Spring help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: configuring services. The key idea is containers, and the biggest risk is circular dependencies.

Across all three scenarios, Dependency Injection stays the same idea: a design approach where objects receive their dependencies instead of creating them. Only the details change.
