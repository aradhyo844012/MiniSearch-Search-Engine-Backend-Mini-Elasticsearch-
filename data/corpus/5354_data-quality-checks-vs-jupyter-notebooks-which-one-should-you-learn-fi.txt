Data Quality Checks vs Jupyter Notebooks: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Data Quality Checks or Jupyter Notebooks first. Both sit in the area of Data and Analytics, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Data Quality Checks is automated tests that verify data is complete, valid and consistent, and it is closely tied to null checks and uniqueness. Jupyter Notebooks, by contrast, is interactive documents that combine code, results and notes, and it is closely tied to cells and kernels.

Choose Data Quality Checks first if you care about protecting dashboards or validating ingestion. Choose Jupyter Notebooks first if you care about exploring data or teaching and demos.

Think of Data Quality Checks as a quality inspector at the end of an assembly line, and of Jupyter Notebooks as a lab notebook that can run its own experiments. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Data Quality Checks, avoid alerts nobody reads. With Jupyter Notebooks, avoid running cells out of order.

A good habit for Data Quality Checks is to check data as early as it enters the pipeline. A good habit for Jupyter Notebooks is to restart the kernel and run all cells before sharing.
