Mistakes to Avoid with Content Security Policy

Everyone learns Content Security Policy partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: allowing unsafe-inline. It usually appears when someone skips directives and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: policies too broad to help. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of reducing the impact of XSS. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: breaking third-party widgets. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on trusted sources to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start in report-only mode to learn what would break.
