Practice Exercises for Virtual Private Clouds

These exercises build real understanding of Virtual Private Clouds. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Explain how security groups and gateways differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 2: Compare Terraform and VPC Flow Logs for securing databases and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Pick AWS VPC and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: overlapping address ranges. Then fix it.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Virtual Private Clouds and include the term subnets.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Virtual Private Clouds is isolated private networks inside a cloud provider where you place your resources.
