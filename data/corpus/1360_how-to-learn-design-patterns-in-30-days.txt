How to Learn Design Patterns in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Design Patterns. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Design Patterns covers reusable solutions to common problems in software design. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn singleton and factory, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with UML diagrams on a small task such as structuring applications.
Week 3: build a mini project around decoupling components.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as forcing patterns where they are unnecessary, and explain Design Patterns to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include UML diagrams, refactoring guides, and code examples.

With steady practice, Design Patterns will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
