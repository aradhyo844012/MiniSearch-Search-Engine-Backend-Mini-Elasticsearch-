Mistakes to Avoid with Single Sign-On

Everyone learns Single Sign-On partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: a single point of failure. It usually appears when someone skips identity provider and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: misconfigured redirects. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of company intranets. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: long sessions on shared devices. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on session lifetime to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: plan for identity provider outages.
