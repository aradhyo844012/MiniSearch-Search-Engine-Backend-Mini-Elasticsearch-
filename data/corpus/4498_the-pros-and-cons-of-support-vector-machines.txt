The Pros and Cons of Support Vector Machines

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Support Vector Machines is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports text classification
- Makes small high-dimensional datasets easier
- Helps with image recognition baselines

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow training on large data
- Risk: poor kernel choices
- Risk: unscaled features

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as text classification, and you can manage the risks, Support Vector Machines is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, scale features and try a linear kernel first.
