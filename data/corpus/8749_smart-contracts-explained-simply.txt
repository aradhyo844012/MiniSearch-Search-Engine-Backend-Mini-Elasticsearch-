Smart Contracts Explained Simply

If Smart Contracts sounds intimidating, relax. It is programs stored on a blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Smart Contracts as a vending machine that follows its rules with no clerk. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: contract code, transactions, gas fees, and immutability. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with contract code, then add transactions.

You will meet Smart Contracts in work such as token systems, automated escrow, and decentralized applications.

One practical tip is to audit and test contracts thoroughly before deployment.
