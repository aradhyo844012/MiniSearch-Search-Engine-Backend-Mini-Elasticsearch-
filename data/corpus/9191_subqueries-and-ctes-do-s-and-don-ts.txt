Subqueries and CTEs Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Subqueries and CTEs.

Do:
- Learn subqueries before anything else.
- Try PostgreSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to name each CTE after what it returns.
- Test the case of breaking complex reports into steps early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: correlated subqueries that run slowly.
- Ignore this: deep nesting.
- Ignore this: assuming CTEs are always materialized.
- Adopt Subqueries and CTEs just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Subqueries and CTEs, a little patience with WITH clauses pays off more than any clever shortcut.
