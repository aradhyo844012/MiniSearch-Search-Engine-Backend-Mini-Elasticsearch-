Competitive Programming Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Competitive Programming regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of interview preparation early, before the design hardens.
2. Reach for Codeforces first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as LeetCode.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit time limits before anything else.
4. Make sure you can review problems you could not solve and study the editorial.
5. Write down why you chose Competitive Programming so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to learning algorithms as well.
