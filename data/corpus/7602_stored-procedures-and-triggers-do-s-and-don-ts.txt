Stored Procedures and Triggers Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Stored Procedures and Triggers.

Do:
- Learn procedures before anything else.
- Try PL/pgSQL on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep triggers small and well documented.
- Test the case of enforcing rules close to the data early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: hidden logic that surprises developers.
- Ignore this: hard-to-test code.
- Ignore this: trigger chains.
- Adopt Stored Procedures and Triggers just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Stored Procedures and Triggers, a little patience with triggers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
