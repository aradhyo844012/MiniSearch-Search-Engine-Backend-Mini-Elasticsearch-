Mistakes to Avoid with Code Formatters

Everyone learns Code Formatters partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: reformatting entire files at once. It usually appears when someone skips style rules and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: conflicting tool settings. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of ending style debates. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: ignoring generated files. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on pre-commit hooks to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: run the formatter in a pre-commit hook.
