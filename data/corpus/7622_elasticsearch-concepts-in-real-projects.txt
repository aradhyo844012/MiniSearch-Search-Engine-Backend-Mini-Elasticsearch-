Elasticsearch Concepts in Real Projects

Reading about Elasticsearch Concepts is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: site search. Here, indexes drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against too many shards.

Scenario two: log analytics. This time shards matters most, and tools such as Elasticsearch help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: product catalogs. The key idea is analyzers, and the biggest risk is mapping explosions.

Across all three scenarios, Elasticsearch Concepts stays the same idea: the building blocks of a distributed search engine that indexes and ranks documents. Only the details change.
