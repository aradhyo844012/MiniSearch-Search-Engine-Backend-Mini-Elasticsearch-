Message Queues: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Message Queues?
A: The term Message Queues refers to systems that let services send work to each other asynchronously through a buffer.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: losing messages without acknowledgements; poison messages that retry forever; and duplicate processing.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with producers and consumers, then move on to acknowledgements and retries.

Q: How does it relate to JSON?
A: Message Queues and JSON often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Backend and APIs.

Q: Why do developers use Message Queues?
A: Common uses include sending emails, processing uploads, and smoothing traffic spikes.
