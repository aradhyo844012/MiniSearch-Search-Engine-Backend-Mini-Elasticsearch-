Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Recommendation Systems

This path is for a learner who has never studied Recommendation Systems before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want) and learn collaborative filtering and content-based filtering.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Surprise, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving streaming services.

Stage 3, depth: study ranking and the cold start problem, then deliberately explore this problem: feedback loops.

Stage 4, application: apply Recommendation Systems to online shops, compare implicit with TensorFlow Recommenders, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Recommendation Systems to someone else, and then move on to Fine-Tuning Models.
