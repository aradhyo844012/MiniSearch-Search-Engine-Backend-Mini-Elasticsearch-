Why Number Theory and RSA Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Number Theory and RSA make better decisions, because Number Theory and RSA is the study of integers, whose properties underpin much of modern encryption.

Three situations show why it matters. First, public key cryptography. Second, hash functions. Third, digital signatures. In each case, a solid grasp of prime numbers and modular arithmetic separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: using small primes, implementing crypto by hand in production, and ignoring padding.

Good tools exist, including Python pow, OpenSSL, and SageMath, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
