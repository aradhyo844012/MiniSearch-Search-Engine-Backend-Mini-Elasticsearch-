Everything You Need to Know About Recommendation Systems

This page collects the essentials about Recommendation Systems in one place. In short, Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want.

Core concepts:
- collaborative filtering
- content-based filtering
- ranking
- the cold start problem

Where it is used:
- streaming services
- online shops
- news feeds

Common problems:
- feedback loops
- popularity bias
- ignoring new users

Tools to know:
- Surprise
- implicit
- TensorFlow Recommenders

Remember this above all: a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.
