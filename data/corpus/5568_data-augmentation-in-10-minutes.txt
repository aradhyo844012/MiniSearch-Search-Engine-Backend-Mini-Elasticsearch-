Data Augmentation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Data Augmentation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn transformations, label preservation, synthetic samples, and augmentation pipelines.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through image models, text classification, and small datasets.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for changes that alter the label and augmenting the test set, then try Albumentations for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to augment only the training data.
