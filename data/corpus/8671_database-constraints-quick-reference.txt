Database Constraints Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Database Constraints. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: rules the database enforces to keep stored data valid.
Key terms: primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and check constraints.
Typical uses: preventing bad data, protecting relationships, and documenting business rules.
Risks: skipping constraints for speed, missing indexes on foreign keys, and rules enforced only in the app.
Tools: PostgreSQL, SQLite, and ER diagram tools.

Mental picture: guard rails along a mountain road.

Habit to build: put critical rules in the database as well as in the app.
