Teaching Configuration Management to a Teammate

Explaining Configuration Management to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: the settings dial on a machine that stays the same on every site. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Configuration Management is keeping application settings separate from code so they can change per environment. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving separating development and production, naming environment variables and config files as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain defaults in Configuration Management back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, committing secrets, and suggest this habit: fail fast at startup if a required setting is missing.
