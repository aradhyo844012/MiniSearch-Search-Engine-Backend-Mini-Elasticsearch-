How to Learn Database Normalization in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Database Normalization. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. Put simply, Database Normalization covers the practice of organizing tables to reduce duplicated data and update anomalies. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Week 1: learn first normal form and second normal form, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with ER diagrams on a small task such as designing clean schemas.
Week 3: build a mini project around avoiding update anomalies.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as over-normalizing reporting tables, and explain Database Normalization to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include ER diagrams, dbdiagram.io, and MySQL Workbench.

In short, careful study of Database Normalization pays off. Spend time on first normal form and second normal form, and the rest will follow.
