Graph Databases Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Graph Databases.

Do:
- Learn nodes before anything else.
- Try Neo4j on a small example.
- Make it a habit to limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
- Test the case of social networks early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: using them for simple tabular data.
- Ignore this: unbounded traversals.
- Ignore this: supernodes with millions of edges.
- Adopt Graph Databases just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Graph Databases, a little patience with edges pays off more than any clever shortcut.
