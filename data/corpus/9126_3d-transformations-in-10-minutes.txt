3D Transformations in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through 3D Transformations.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: 3D Transformations is the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn translation, rotation, scaling, and matrices.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through games, modeling tools, and robotics.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for wrong multiplication order and gimbal lock, then try GLM for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
