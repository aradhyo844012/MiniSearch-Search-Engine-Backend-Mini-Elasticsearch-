Choosing Tools for Graph Theory Basics

There is no single best tool for Graph Theory Basics. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

NetworkX is a common first choice, especially for social networks. Graphviz suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Gephi is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Graph Theory Basics by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle maps well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Graph Theory Basics, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: mixing directed and undirected graphs.

As a rule of thumb, draw a small example before writing code, no matter which tool you use.
