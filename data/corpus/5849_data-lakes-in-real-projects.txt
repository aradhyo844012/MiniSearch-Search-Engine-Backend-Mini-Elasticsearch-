Data Lakes in Real Projects

Reading about Data Lakes is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: storing logs and events. Here, raw storage drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against turning into a data swamp.

Scenario two: machine learning datasets. This time schema on read matters most, and tools such as Amazon S3 help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: analytics across many sources. The key idea is partitioning, and the biggest risk is no catalog or ownership.

Across all three scenarios, Data Lakes stays the same idea: large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. Only the details change.
