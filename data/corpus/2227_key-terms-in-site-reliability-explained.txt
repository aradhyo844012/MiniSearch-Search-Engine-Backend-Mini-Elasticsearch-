Key Terms in Site Reliability Explained

Clear vocabulary makes Site Reliability much easier to learn, so here are the key terms in plain language. The term Site Reliability refers to the discipline of keeping services dependable using engineering practices. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

service level objectives: a key term in Site Reliability. Practice with a small example to make the meaning stick.
error budgets: a key term in Site Reliability. It matters because it shapes how you design, test and explain your solution.
incident response: a key term in Site Reliability. You will meet it constantly in documentation and in code reviews.
postmortems: a key term in Site Reliability. Beginners often confuse it with related terms, so define it in your own words early.
