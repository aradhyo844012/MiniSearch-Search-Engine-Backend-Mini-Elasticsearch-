How to Learn IP Addressing and Subnets in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with IP Addressing and Subnets. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. If you build software for long enough, you will run into IP Addressing and Subnets. The term refers to the rules for numbering devices on a network and dividing networks into smaller parts.

Week 1: learn IPv4 and IPv6 and subnet masks, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with ipcalc on a small task such as planning office networks.
Week 3: build a mini project around configuring cloud networks.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as overlapping address ranges, and explain IP Addressing and Subnets to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include ipcalc, ping, and traceroute.

With steady practice, IP Addressing and Subnets will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
