Mistakes to Avoid with Ray Tracing

Everyone learns Ray Tracing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: slow render times. It usually appears when someone skips rays and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: noise from too few samples. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of movie visuals. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: numerical precision errors. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on shadows to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: start with spheres and a single light before adding features.
