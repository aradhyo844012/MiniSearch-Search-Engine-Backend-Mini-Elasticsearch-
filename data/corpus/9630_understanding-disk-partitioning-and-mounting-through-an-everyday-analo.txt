Understanding Disk Partitioning and Mounting Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture building rooms inside a warehouse and putting door numbers on them.

In that picture, partitions is the first rule you follow, file system types is the next step, and mount points and fstab keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Disk Partitioning and Mounting is dividing storage into sections and attaching them to the file tree, and it involves details such as wrong device names that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for lsblk to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain adding a new drive to a friend.
