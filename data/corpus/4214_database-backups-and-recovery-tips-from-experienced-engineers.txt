Database Backups and Recovery Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Database Backups and Recovery regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
2. Reach for pg_dump first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as mysqldump.
3. Write down why you chose Database Backups and Recovery so the next person understands the decision.
4. Be careful with this one: never testing restores.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit recovery point objective before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to migrating servers as well.
