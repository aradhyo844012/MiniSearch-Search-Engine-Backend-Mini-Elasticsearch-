The Pros and Cons of Integration Testing

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Integration Testing is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports verifying API and database interaction
- Makes catching contract mismatches easier
- Helps with testing third-party integrations

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: slow suites
- Risk: flaky external dependencies
- Risk: unclear failures

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as verifying API and database interaction, and you can manage the risks, Integration Testing is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use disposable containers for test dependencies.
