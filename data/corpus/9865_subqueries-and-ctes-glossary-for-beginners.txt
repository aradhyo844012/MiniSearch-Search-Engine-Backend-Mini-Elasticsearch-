Subqueries and CTEs Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Subqueries and CTEs feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Subqueries and CTEs: queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets.
subqueries: a core term, most visible in breaking complex reports into steps.
WITH clauses: a core term that beginners often confuse with correlated queries.
correlated queries: a core term that connects to readability.
readability: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
PostgreSQL: a tool commonly used for filtering with computed results.
