Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Time Management for Developers

This path is for a learner who has never studied Time Management for Developers before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Time Management for Developers is habits for protecting focus and finishing technical work on schedule) and learn prioritization and time blocking.

Stage 2, hands-on: install calendar blocking, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving managing coursework and projects.

Stage 3, depth: study breaking down tasks and avoiding context switches, then deliberately explore this problem: multitasking.

Stage 4, application: apply Time Management for Developers to meeting deadlines, compare Trello with the Pomodoro technique, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Time Management for Developers to someone else, and then move on to Command Line Productivity.
