AI Agents Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for AI Agents. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.
Key terms: planning, tool calls, memory, and guardrails.
Typical uses: research assistants, workflow automation, and customer support.
Risks: runaway loops, unchecked tool permissions, and hard-to-debug behavior.
Tools: LangGraph, AutoGen, and tool-calling APIs.

Mental picture: an assistant that makes a plan and uses a toolbox to carry it out.

Habit to build: limit what tools the agent may use and log every action.
