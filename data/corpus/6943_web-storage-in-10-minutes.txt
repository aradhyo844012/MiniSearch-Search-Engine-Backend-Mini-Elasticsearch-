Web Storage in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Web Storage.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Web Storage is browser features for keeping small amounts of data on the user's device.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, and IndexedDB.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a sticky note on the fridge that only your household can read. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through saving user preferences, remembering form drafts, and caching small API results.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for storing sensitive data and assuming storage is always available, then try browser DevTools for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to wrap every read and write in error handling.
