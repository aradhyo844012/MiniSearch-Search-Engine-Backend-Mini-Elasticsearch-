The Future of DOM Manipulation

Predicting the future is risky, but the direction of DOM Manipulation is easy to describe in general terms. Put simply, DOM Manipulation covers reading and changing the structure and content of a web page from JavaScript. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Web pages must work across many browsers, devices and network conditions.

Expect tools such as querySelector and browser console to keep improving, and expect newer ideas to keep building on selectors. The basics, though, tend to stay the same, so time spent on events and nodes is rarely wasted. Anyone working on updating search results will keep meeting DOM Manipulation for years to come.

Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce. Start small by applying DOM Manipulation to one case, such as updating search results, before rolling it out more widely.

None of this needs special hardware or expensive software. A laptop, curiosity and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with DOM Manipulation.
