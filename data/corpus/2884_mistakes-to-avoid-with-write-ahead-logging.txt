Mistakes to Avoid with Write-Ahead Logging

Everyone learns Write-Ahead Logging partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: disabling fsync for speed. It usually appears when someone skips log records and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: log files filling the disk. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of surviving crashes. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting to archive logs. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fsync to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: test crash recovery by killing the process on purpose.
