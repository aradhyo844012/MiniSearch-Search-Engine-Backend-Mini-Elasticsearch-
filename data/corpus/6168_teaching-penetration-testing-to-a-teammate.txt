Teaching Penetration Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Penetration Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: hiring a locksmith to test your doors. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Penetration Testing is authorized attempts to find and exploit weaknesses in a system before real attackers do. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving pre-launch security checks, naming scoping and reconnaissance as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain exploitation in Penetration Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, testing without written permission, and suggest this habit: get written authorization before any test.
