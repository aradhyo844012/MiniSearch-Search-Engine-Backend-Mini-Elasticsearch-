MongoDB Aggregation Glossary for Beginners

New words can make MongoDB Aggregation feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

MongoDB Aggregation: a pipeline of stages for filtering, grouping and reshaping documents in MongoDB.
pipeline stages: a core term, most visible in summarizing orders.
$match and $group: a core term that beginners often confuse with projection.
projection: a core term that connects to indexes.
indexes: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
MongoDB Compass: a tool commonly used for building analytics queries.
