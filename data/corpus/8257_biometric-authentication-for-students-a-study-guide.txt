Biometric Authentication for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Biometric Authentication.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Biometric Authentication in your own words.
2. Describe enrollment, matching, false accept and false reject rates, and liveness detection.
3. Give examples from phone unlock and building access.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Biometric Authentication is verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices. Then draw a door that recognizes you by your face instead of a key on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is spoofing attacks a problem? How does matching relate to false accept and false reject rates? When would you choose Android BiometricPrompt or Face ID APIs?

Study habit worth keeping: offer a fallback method and protect the stored templates.

When you feel ready, compare Biometric Authentication with Digital Twins and write down three differences.
