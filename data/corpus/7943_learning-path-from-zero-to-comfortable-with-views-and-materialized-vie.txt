Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Views and Materialized Views

This path is for a learner who has never studied Views and Materialized Views before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Views and Materialized Views is saved queries that behave like tables, with materialized views storing the results) and learn view definitions and refresh strategies.

Stage 2, hands-on: install PostgreSQL, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving simplifying repeated queries.

Stage 3, depth: study permissions and dependencies, then deliberately explore this problem: stale materialized data.

Stage 4, application: apply Views and Materialized Views to hiding sensitive columns, compare MySQL with Snowflake, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Views and Materialized Views to someone else, and then move on to Database Schema Design for Beginners.
