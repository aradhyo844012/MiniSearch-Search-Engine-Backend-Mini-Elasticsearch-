Everything You Need to Know About Network Troubleshooting

This page collects the essentials about Network Troubleshooting in one place. In short, Network Troubleshooting is a step-by-step process for finding out why a network connection fails or is slow.

Core concepts:
- protocol layers
- ping and traceroute
- DNS checks
- packet captures

Where it is used:
- fixing office connectivity
- diagnosing slow apps
- verifying firewall changes

Common problems:
- changing several things at once
- blaming the application too early
- ignoring DNS

Tools to know:
- ping
- traceroute
- Wireshark

Remember this above all: a doctor who checks the heart, lungs and blood pressure in order is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to work from the bottom layer upward.
