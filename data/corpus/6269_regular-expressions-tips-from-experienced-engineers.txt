Regular Expressions Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Regular Expressions regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Be careful with this one: catastrophic backtracking.
2. Reach for the Python re module first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as regex101.
3. Make sure you can build patterns in small steps and test each one.
4. Test the case of validating input early, before the design hardens.
5. Write down why you chose Regular Expressions so the next person understands the decision.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to searching logs as well.
