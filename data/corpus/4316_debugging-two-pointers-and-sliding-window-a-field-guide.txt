Debugging Two Pointers and Sliding Window: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Two Pointers and Sliding Window, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: moving the wrong pointer, forgetting empty inputs, and breaking the invariant.
Step 3: revisit left and right pointers and window boundaries, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Python lists or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Two Pointers and Sliding Window in work such as finding pairs with a given sum come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, write down the window invariant before coding the loop. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
