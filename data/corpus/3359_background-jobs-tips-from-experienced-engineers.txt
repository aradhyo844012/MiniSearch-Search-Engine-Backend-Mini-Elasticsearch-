Background Jobs Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Background Jobs regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit workers before anything else.
2. Test the case of sending emails early, before the design hardens.
3. Reach for Celery first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Sidekiq.
4. Write down why you chose Background Jobs so the next person understands the decision.
5. Do not skip queues; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to resizing images as well.
