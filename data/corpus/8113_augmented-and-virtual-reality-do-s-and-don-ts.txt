Augmented and Virtual Reality Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Augmented and Virtual Reality.

Do:
- Learn head tracking before anything else.
- Try Unity XR on a small example.
- Make it a habit to keep frame rates high and test comfort with real users.
- Test the case of training simulations early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: motion sickness from low frame rates.
- Ignore this: heavy assets.
- Ignore this: poor interface design in 3D.
- Adopt Augmented and Virtual Reality just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Augmented and Virtual Reality, a little patience with rendering at high frame rates pays off more than any clever shortcut.
