The Importance of TCP and UDP in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate TCP and UDP until something goes wrong without it. Put simply, TCP and UDP covers the two main transport protocols, one reliable and ordered and the other fast and connectionless. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Simple command-line tools can reveal a lot about what is happening on the wire. Network failures are normal, so well-designed software expects timeouts and retries.

If you are comfortable with TCP and UDP, explore HTTP and HTTPS next. The two often appear in the same project.

Typical applications include web traffic, video calls, and online games. Small projects and large systems both benefit.

Three issues come up again and again: assuming UDP guarantees delivery; ignoring timeouts; and opening too many connections. Knowing them in advance saves hours of debugging.

In short, careful study of TCP and UDP pays off. Spend time on handshakes and acknowledgements, and the rest will follow.
