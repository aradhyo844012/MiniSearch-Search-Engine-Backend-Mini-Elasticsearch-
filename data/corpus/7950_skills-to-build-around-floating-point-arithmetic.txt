Skills to Build Around Floating-Point Arithmetic

Knowing the definition of Floating-Point Arithmetic is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with mantissa and exponent and rounding, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with Python decimal on a Floating-Point Arithmetic task, and try at least one alternative such as NumPy.

Judgment: learn to decide when Floating-Point Arithmetic fits a problem such as scientific computing, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example writing numbers in scientific notation with a limited number of digits.

Habits: compare floats with a tolerance and use decimals for money, and write down what you learn after each project.
