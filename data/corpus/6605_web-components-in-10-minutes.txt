Web Components in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Web Components.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Web Components is browser-native custom elements with their own markup, style and behavior.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn custom elements, shadow DOM, templates, and slots.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a Lego brick in its own sealed packaging. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through design system components, widgets reused across frameworks, and embeddable tools.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for styling surprises across the shadow boundary and poor accessibility, then try Lit for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to document the attributes and events like a public API.
