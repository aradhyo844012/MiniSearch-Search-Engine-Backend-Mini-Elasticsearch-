The Pros and Cons of Information Theory and Entropy

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Information Theory and Entropy is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports data compression
- Makes error-correcting codes easier
- Helps with communication systems

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: confusing information with meaning
- Risk: ignoring noise
- Risk: misreading entropy values

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as data compression, and you can manage the risks, Information Theory and Entropy is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, compute the entropy of a short text by hand.
