Embeddings in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Embeddings.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn vectors, cosine similarity, dimensionality, and nearest neighbor search.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine coordinates on a map where similar places are close together. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through semantic search, recommendations, and clustering text.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for mixing models between indexing and querying and ignoring normalization, then try sentence-transformers for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to use the same embedding model for indexing and querying.
