Debugging Quantum Computing Basics: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Quantum Computing Basics, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: expecting speedups for every problem, noise and errors, and hype about current machines.
Step 3: revisit qubits and superposition, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Qiskit or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Quantum Computing Basics in work such as research on cryptography come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start by simulating small circuits on a laptop. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
