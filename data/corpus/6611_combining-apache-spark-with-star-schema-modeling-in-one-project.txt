Combining Apache Spark with Star Schema Modeling in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Apache Spark and Star Schema Modeling can work together in a single project.

Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines. Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.

Imagine a project that involves large ETL jobs and business intelligence reports. Apache Spark brings DataFrames, while Star Schema Modeling brings fact tables.

A sensible plan is to start with PySpark for the Apache Spark part and dbt for the Star Schema Modeling part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: too many small partitions on one side, and over-normalized dimensions on the other.

Picture the whole as a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers, working alongside a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions.

Two habits help: first, avoid collecting large results onto a single machine; second, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
