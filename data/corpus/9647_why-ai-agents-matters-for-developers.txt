Why AI Agents Matters for Developers

Developers who understand AI Agents make better decisions, because AI Agents is software systems that use a language model to plan steps and call tools to reach a goal.

Three situations show why it matters. First, research assistants. Second, workflow automation. Third, customer support. In each case, a solid grasp of planning and tool calls separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: runaway loops, unchecked tool permissions, and hard-to-debug behavior.

You will not need to build everything yourself; LangGraph, AutoGen, and tool-calling APIs cover most needs.
