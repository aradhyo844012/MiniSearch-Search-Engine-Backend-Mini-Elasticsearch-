Bootstrap: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Bootstrap?
A: The term Bootstrap refers to a popular front-end toolkit with ready-made layout, components and utilities.

Q: Why do developers use Bootstrap?
A: Common uses include prototypes and dashboards, admin pages, and responsive layouts.

Q: How does it relate to DOM Manipulation?
A: Bootstrap and DOM Manipulation often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Web Development.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Bootstrap.
