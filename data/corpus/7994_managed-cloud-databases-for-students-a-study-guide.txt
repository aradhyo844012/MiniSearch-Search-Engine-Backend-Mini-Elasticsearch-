Managed Cloud Databases for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Managed Cloud Databases.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Managed Cloud Databases in your own words.
2. Describe automated backups, read replicas, maintenance windows, and scaling options.
3. Give examples from launching apps quickly and reducing database administration.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Managed Cloud Databases is database services where the cloud provider handles backups, patching and failover. Then draw a restaurant that hires a kitchen crew to handle the cleaning on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is surprise costs a problem? How does read replicas relate to maintenance windows? When would you choose Amazon RDS or Cloud SQL?

Study habit: test restoring a backup before you need it.

When you feel ready, compare Managed Cloud Databases with Regions and Availability Zones and write down three differences.
