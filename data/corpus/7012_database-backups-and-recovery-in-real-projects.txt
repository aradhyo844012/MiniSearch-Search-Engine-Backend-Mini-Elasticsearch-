Database Backups and Recovery in Real Projects

Reading about Database Backups and Recovery is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: recovering deleted tables. Here, full and incremental backups drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against never testing restores.

Scenario two: migrating servers. This time recovery point objective matters most, and tools such as pg_dump help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: meeting compliance rules. The key idea is recovery time objective, and the biggest risk is storing backups on the same disk.

Across all three scenarios, Database Backups and Recovery stays the same idea: the practice of copying data regularly so that it can be restored after failures. Only the details change.
