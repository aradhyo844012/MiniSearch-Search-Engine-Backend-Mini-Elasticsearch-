Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Secrets Management

This path is for a learner who has never studied Secrets Management before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Secrets Management is storing and distributing passwords, keys and tokens securely) and learn vaults and rotation.

Stage 2, hands-on: install HashiCorp Vault, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving database credentials.

Stage 3, depth: study access policies and audit logs, then deliberately explore this problem: secrets in source control.

Stage 4, application: apply Secrets Management to API keys, compare AWS Secrets Manager with SOPS, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Secrets Management to someone else, and then move on to Service Mesh.
