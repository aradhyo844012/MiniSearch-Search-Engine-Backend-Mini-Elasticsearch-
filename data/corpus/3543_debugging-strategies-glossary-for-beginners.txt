Debugging Strategies Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Debugging Strategies feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Debugging Strategies: systematic ways of finding and fixing the cause of unexpected program behavior.
reproducing the bug: a core term, most visible in fixing production issues.
isolating the cause: a core term that beginners often confuse with forming hypotheses.
forming hypotheses: a core term that connects to binary search through code.
binary search through code: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
debuggers: a tool commonly used for learning unfamiliar code.

Come back to this Debugging Strategies list whenever documentation for logging uses a word you do not recognize.
