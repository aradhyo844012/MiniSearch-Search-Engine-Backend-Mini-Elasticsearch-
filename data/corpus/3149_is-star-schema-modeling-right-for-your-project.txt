Is Star Schema Modeling Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Star Schema Modeling, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.

It is a good fit when your work involves business intelligence reports or data warehouses, and when your team understands fact tables.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn dimension tables, or when you cannot manage these risks: mixing different grains, over-normalized dimensions, and missing date dimensions.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether surrogate keys and grain are part of the answer. If they are, Star Schema Modeling is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with fast aggregations and state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
