Two Pointers and Sliding Window in Real Projects

Reading about Two Pointers and Sliding Window is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: finding pairs with a given sum. Here, left and right pointers drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against moving the wrong pointer.

Scenario two: longest substring problems. This time window boundaries matters most, and tools such as Python lists help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: merging sorted arrays. The key idea is invariants, and the biggest risk is forgetting empty inputs.

Across all three scenarios, Two Pointers and Sliding Window stays the same idea: techniques that scan arrays with two moving positions to avoid nested loops. Only the details change.
