Property-Based Testing Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Property-Based Testing. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: testing with many automatically generated inputs to check that general rules always hold.
Key terms: properties, generators, shrinking, and invariants.
Typical uses: testing parsers, checking sorting and math functions, and finding edge cases.
Risks: vague properties, slow generators, and ignoring random seed reports.
Tools: Hypothesis, QuickCheck, and fast-check.

Mental picture: a robot tester trying thousands of odd inputs.

Habit to build: save failing examples as regular tests.
