Mocking and Stubbing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Mocking and Stubbing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Mocking and Stubbing in your own words.
2. Describe mocks, stubs, spies, and fakes.
3. Give examples from isolating units and simulating failures.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Mocking and Stubbing is replacing real dependencies with controlled fakes during tests. Then draw a crash test dummy standing in for a real passenger on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is over-mocking a problem? How does stubs relate to spies? When would you choose unittest.mock or Mockito?

Study habit: prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.

When you feel ready, compare Mocking and Stubbing with Chaos Engineering and write down three differences.
