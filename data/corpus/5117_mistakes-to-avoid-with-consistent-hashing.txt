Mistakes to Avoid with Consistent Hashing

Everyone learns Consistent Hashing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: too few virtual nodes. It usually appears when someone skips hash ring and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: uneven load. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of distributed caches. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: hot keys. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on rebalancing to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: use many virtual nodes per server to even out the load.
