Where to Go Next After Learning Mocking and Stubbing

You have met the basics of Mocking and Stubbing. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Chaos Engineering, which belongs to the same area as Mocking and Stubbing (Testing and Quality) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of mocks, stubs, spies or fakes, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around simulating failures with unittest.mock, and note every problem you meet, starting with over-mocking.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Mocking and Stubbing to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
