Teaching Event-Driven Architecture to a Teammate

Explaining Event-Driven Architecture to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a notice board where people post news and others react when they see it. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Event-Driven Architecture is a style in which components communicate by publishing events and reacting to them. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving order pipelines, naming producers and consumers and event brokers as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain loose coupling in Event-Driven Architecture back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, hard-to-trace flows, and suggest this habit: document every event name and schema.
