Teaching Managed Kubernetes Services to a Teammate

Explaining Managed Kubernetes Services to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: renting an apartment building where the landlord handles the plumbing. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Managed Kubernetes Services is cloud services that run the Kubernetes control plane for you. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving running containerized apps, naming the control plane and node pools as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain upgrades in Managed Kubernetes Services back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, neglecting version upgrades, and suggest this habit: plan regular cluster upgrades.
