Blockchain Fundamentals Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Blockchain Fundamentals. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a shared ledger where blocks of records are chained together using cryptographic hashes.
Key terms: blocks, hashes, consensus, and decentralization.
Typical uses: digital currencies, supply chain tracking, and public records.
Risks: treating it as a database replacement, slow throughput, and the energy use of some designs.
Tools: Bitcoin Core, Ethereum, and Hyperledger.

Mental picture: a notebook passed around a group where every page refers to the previous page.

Habit to build: ask whether a normal database would solve the problem.
