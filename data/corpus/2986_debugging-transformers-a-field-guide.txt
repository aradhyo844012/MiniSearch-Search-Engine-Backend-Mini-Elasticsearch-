Debugging Transformers: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Transformers, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: huge compute requirements, limited context windows, and biased training data.
Step 3: revisit self-attention and positional encoding, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Hugging Face or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Transformers in work such as language translation come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, begin with small pretrained models. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
