Integration Testing for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Integration Testing.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Integration Testing in your own words.
2. Describe test databases, service boundaries, fixtures, and test environments.
3. Give examples from verifying API and database interaction and catching contract mismatches.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Integration Testing is tests that check how several parts of a system work together. Then draw checking that the gears of a watch mesh before closing the case on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is slow suites a problem? How does service boundaries relate to fixtures? When would you choose Testcontainers or pytest?

Study habit worth keeping: use disposable containers for test dependencies.

When you feel ready, compare Integration Testing with Fuzz Testing and write down three differences.
