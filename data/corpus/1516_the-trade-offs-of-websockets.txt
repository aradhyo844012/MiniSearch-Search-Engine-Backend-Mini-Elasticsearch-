The Trade-offs of WebSockets

Every design choice has a cost, and WebSockets is no exception. Many students first meet WebSockets in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to a protocol that keeps a two-way connection open between a browser and a server.

On the positive side, WebSockets is well suited to chat applications and live dashboards. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: forgetting reconnection logic; unbounded message queues; proxies closing idle connections. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much persistent connections and messages matter to your project.
