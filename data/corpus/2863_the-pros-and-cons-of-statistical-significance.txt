The Pros and Cons of Statistical Significance

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Statistical Significance is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports judging experiment results
- Makes comparing model performance easier
- Helps with reporting research findings

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: mistaking significance for importance
- Risk: p-hacking
- Risk: ignoring sample size

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as judging experiment results, and you can manage the risks, Statistical Significance is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, always report the effect size alongside the p-value.
