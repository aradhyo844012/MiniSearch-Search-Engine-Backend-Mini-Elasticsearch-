Mistakes to Avoid with Mocking and Stubbing

Everyone learns Mocking and Stubbing partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: over-mocking. It usually appears when someone skips mocks and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: tests that mirror the implementation. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of isolating units. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: mocks that drift from reality. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fakes to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: prefer simple fakes over deep mock chains.
