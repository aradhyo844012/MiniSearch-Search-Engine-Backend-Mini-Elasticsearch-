Cron Jobs and Scheduling Explained Simply

Here is Cron Jobs and Scheduling without the jargon: it is a Unix tool that runs commands automatically at scheduled times.

Think of Cron Jobs and Scheduling as an alarm clock that starts chores for you. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: crontab syntax, schedule fields, environment differences, and output redirection. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with crontab syntax, then add schedule fields.

People reach for Cron Jobs and Scheduling when they work on nightly backups, log rotation, and periodic reports.

One practical tip is to redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths.
