Vector Databases Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Vector Databases. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: databases built to store embeddings and find the most similar ones quickly.
Key terms: embeddings, approximate nearest neighbors, distance metrics, and metadata filters.
Typical uses: semantic search, recommendations, and retrieval for AI assistants.
Risks: mixing embedding models, ignoring recall trade-offs, and oversized vectors.
Tools: FAISS, pgvector, and Pinecone.

Mental picture: a map where similar ideas sit near each other.

Habit to build: measure recall on a small labeled test set.
