Choosing Tools for Command Line Productivity

There is no single best tool for Command Line Productivity. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

bash is a common first choice, especially for automating repetitive tasks. tmux suits teams that already work with similar technology, and fzf is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle exploring files and logs well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: copying commands without understanding them.

As a rule of thumb, turn commands you repeat into small scripts, no matter which tool you use.
