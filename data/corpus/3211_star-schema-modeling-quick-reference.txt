Star Schema Modeling Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Star Schema Modeling. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.
Key terms: fact tables, dimension tables, surrogate keys, and grain.
Typical uses: business intelligence reports, data warehouses, and fast aggregations.
Risks: mixing different grains, over-normalized dimensions, and missing date dimensions.
Tools: dbt, Snowflake, and Kimball techniques.

Mental picture: a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions.

Habit to build: state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.

See also: SQL Window Functions, which sits close to Star Schema Modeling in the area of Data and Analytics.
