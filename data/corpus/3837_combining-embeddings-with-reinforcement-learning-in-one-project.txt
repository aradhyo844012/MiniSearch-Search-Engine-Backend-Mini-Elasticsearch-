Combining Embeddings with Reinforcement Learning in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Embeddings and Reinforcement Learning can work together in a single project.

Embeddings is lists of numbers that represent words, images or items so that similar things sit close together. Reinforcement Learning is a way of training agents to choose actions by rewarding good outcomes.

Imagine a project that involves semantic search and game playing. Embeddings brings vectors, while Reinforcement Learning brings agent.

A sensible plan is to start with sentence-transformers for the Embeddings part and Gymnasium for the Reinforcement Learning part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: ignoring normalization on one side, and slow training on the other.

Picture the whole as coordinates on a map where similar places are close together, working alongside training a dog with treats for good behavior.

Two habits help: first, use the same embedding model for indexing and querying; second, start with simple environments.
