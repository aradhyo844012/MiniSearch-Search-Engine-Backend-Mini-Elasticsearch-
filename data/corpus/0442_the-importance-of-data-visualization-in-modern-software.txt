The Importance of Data Visualization in Modern Software

It is easy to underestimate Data Visualization until something goes wrong without it. The term Data Visualization refers to presenting data as charts and graphics so that patterns are easy to see. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

Data work is mostly preparation, and cleaning often takes longer than analysis. Writing down assumptions about the data prevents confusing surprises later.

You will see Data Visualization whenever people work on explaining results or exploring data. It also helps with reporting performance.

Watch out for these problems: truncated axes; unreadable labels; and too many colors.
