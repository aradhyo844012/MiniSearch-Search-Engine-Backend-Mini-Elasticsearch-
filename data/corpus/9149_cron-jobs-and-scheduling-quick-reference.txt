Cron Jobs and Scheduling Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Cron Jobs and Scheduling. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a Unix tool that runs commands automatically at scheduled times.
Key terms: crontab syntax, schedule fields, environment differences, and output redirection.
Typical uses: nightly backups, log rotation, and periodic reports.
Risks: assuming cron has your shell environment, overlapping runs of slow jobs, and no logging of failures.
Tools: crontab, systemd timers, and flock.

Mental picture: an alarm clock that starts chores for you.

Habit to build: redirect output to a log file and use absolute paths.
