Choosing Tools for B-Trees on Disk

There is no single best tool for B-Trees on Disk. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PostgreSQL is a common first choice, especially for database indexes. SQLite suits teams that already work with similar technology, and MySQL InnoDB is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle file system directories well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: random writes on fragmented pages.

As a rule of thumb, keep indexed keys short so more of them fit on a page, no matter which tool you use.
