Troubleshooting Monitoring and Observability Problems

When something breaks around Monitoring and Observability, a calm and systematic approach saves the most time. Many students first meet Monitoring and Observability in a classroom, but it matters just as much in production systems. The term refers to collecting metrics, logs and traces so you can understand how a system behaves.

Start by reproducing the problem with the smallest possible example. Then change one thing at a time and note what happens. Logs, tests and Prometheus usually reveal the cause faster than guessing.

Common culprits: noisy alerts; logs without context; dashboards nobody reads. If none of these explains the symptom, re-read the basics of metrics and logs.

Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes.

In practice you can start with Prometheus, then explore Grafana or OpenTelemetry as your needs grow.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit Monitoring and Observability whenever a new project gives you a reason.
