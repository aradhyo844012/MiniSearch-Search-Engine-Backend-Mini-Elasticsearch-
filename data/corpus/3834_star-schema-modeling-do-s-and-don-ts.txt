Star Schema Modeling Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Star Schema Modeling.

Do:
- Learn fact tables before anything else.
- Try dbt on a small example.
- Make it a habit to state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
- Test the case of business intelligence reports early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: mixing different grains.
- Ignore this: over-normalized dimensions.
- Ignore this: missing date dimensions.
- Adopt Star Schema Modeling just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Star Schema Modeling, a little patience with dimension tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
