Raspberry Pi for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Raspberry Pi.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Raspberry Pi in your own words.
2. Describe GPIO pins, Linux, SD card storage, and headless setup.
3. Give examples from home automation and learning Linux.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer popular for learning, prototypes and home projects. Then draw a pocket-sized workshop for experiments on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is SD card corruption a problem? How does Linux relate to SD card storage? When would you choose Raspberry Pi OS or Python GPIO libraries?

Study habit worth keeping: use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.

When you feel ready, compare Raspberry Pi with Mobile App Security and write down three differences.
