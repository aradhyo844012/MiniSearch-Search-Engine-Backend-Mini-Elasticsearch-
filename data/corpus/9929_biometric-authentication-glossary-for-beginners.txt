Biometric Authentication Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Biometric Authentication feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Biometric Authentication: verifying identity using traits such as fingerprints, faces or voices.
enrollment: a core term, most visible in phone unlock.
matching: a core term that beginners often confuse with false accept and false reject rates.
false accept and false reject rates: a core term that connects to liveness detection.
liveness detection: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
Android BiometricPrompt: a tool commonly used for building access.

Come back to this Biometric Authentication list whenever documentation for Face ID APIs uses a word you do not recognize.
