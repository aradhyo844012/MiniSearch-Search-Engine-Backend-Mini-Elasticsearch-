CPU Caches Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with CPU Caches.

Do:
- Learn cache lines before anything else.
- Try perf on a small example.
- Make it a habit to access arrays in the order they are stored.
- Test the case of speeding up loops early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: false sharing.
- Ignore this: poor access patterns.
- Ignore this: assuming memory is uniformly fast.
- Adopt CPU Caches just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with CPU Caches, a little patience with hit and miss rates pays off more than any clever shortcut.
