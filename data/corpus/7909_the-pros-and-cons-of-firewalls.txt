The Pros and Cons of Firewalls

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Firewalls is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports protecting servers
- Makes segmenting networks easier
- Helps with logging suspicious traffic

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: rules in the wrong order
- Risk: overly broad allow rules
- Risk: locking yourself out remotely

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as protecting servers, and you can manage the risks, Firewalls is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, start with default deny and open only what you need.
