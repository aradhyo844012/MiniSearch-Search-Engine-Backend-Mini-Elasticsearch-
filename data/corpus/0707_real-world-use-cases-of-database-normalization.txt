Real-World Use Cases of Database Normalization

The best way to appreciate Database Normalization is to see where it is actually used. The term Database Normalization refers to the practice of organizing tables to reduce duplicated data and update anomalies. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

People use Database Normalization for designing clean schemas, for avoiding update anomalies, and for keeping data consistent.

Consider designing clean schemas. In a case like this, Database Normalization gives the work a clear structure, and understanding first normal form makes the design easier to explain. A different example is avoiding update anomalies, where second normal form often decides how well the solution scales.
