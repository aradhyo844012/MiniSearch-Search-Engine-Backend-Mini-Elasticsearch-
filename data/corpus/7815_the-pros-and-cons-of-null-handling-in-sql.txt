The Pros and Cons of NULL Handling in SQL

No technology is free of trade-offs, and NULL Handling in SQL is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports optional fields
- Makes outer joins easier
- Helps with data cleaning

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: comparing with equals instead of IS NULL
- Risk: NOT IN with NULLs
- Risk: forgetting NULLs in counts

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as optional fields, and you can manage the risks, NULL Handling in SQL is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, use IS NULL and COALESCE instead of comparing with equals.
