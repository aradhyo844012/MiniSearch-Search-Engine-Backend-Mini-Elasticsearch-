Combining Service Mesh with Distributed Tracing in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Service Mesh and Distributed Tracing can work together in a single project.

Service Mesh is an infrastructure layer that manages communication between services through proxies. Distributed Tracing is following a single request as it passes through many services.

Imagine a project that involves securing service traffic and finding slow services. Service Mesh brings sidecar proxies, while Distributed Tracing brings spans.

A sensible plan is to start with Istio for the Service Mesh part and OpenTelemetry for the Distributed Tracing part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: adopting it too early on one side, and sampling too aggressively on the other.

Picture the whole as a postal system that applies delivery rules between buildings, working alongside a parcel tracking page that shows every stop on the route.

Two habits help: first, adopt it only once the number of services makes manual setup painful; second, propagate trace IDs through every outgoing call.
