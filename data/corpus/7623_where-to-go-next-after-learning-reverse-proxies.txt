Where to Go Next After Learning Reverse Proxies

You have met the basics of Reverse Proxies. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about NAT, which belongs to the same area as Reverse Proxies (Networking and Internet Infrastructure) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of upstream servers, proxy headers, TLS termination or caching, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around terminating HTTPS with NGINX, and note every problem you meet, starting with losing the client IP address.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Reverse Proxies to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
