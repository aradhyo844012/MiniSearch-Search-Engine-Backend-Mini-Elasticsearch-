Reverse Proxies Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Reverse Proxies.

Do:
- Learn upstream servers before anything else.
- Try NGINX on a small example.
- Make it a habit to forward the real client IP in a trusted header.
- Test the case of hiding backend servers early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: losing the client IP address.
- Ignore this: wrong timeouts.
- Ignore this: exposing internal paths.
- Adopt Reverse Proxies just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Reverse Proxies, a little patience with proxy headers pays off more than any clever shortcut.
