Debugging Strategies in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Debugging Strategies.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Debugging Strategies is systematic ways of finding and fixing the cause of unexpected program behavior.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn reproducing the bug, isolating the cause, forming hypotheses, and binary search through code.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a detective narrowing down suspects. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through fixing production issues, learning unfamiliar code, and reducing repeat bugs.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for changing many things at once and guessing without evidence, then try debuggers for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to change one thing at a time and record the result.
