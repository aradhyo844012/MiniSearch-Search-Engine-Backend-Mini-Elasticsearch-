Choosing Tools for Convolutional Neural Networks

There is no single best tool for Convolutional Neural Networks. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

PyTorch is a common first choice, especially for image classification. TensorFlow suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Keras is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle medical image analysis well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: too little training data.

As a rule of thumb, start from a pretrained model, no matter which tool you use.
