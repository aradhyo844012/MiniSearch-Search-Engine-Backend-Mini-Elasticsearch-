The Pros and Cons of Sprite Animation

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Sprite Animation is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports 2D platformers
- Makes user interface effects easier
- Helps with visual novels

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: inconsistent frame timing
- Risk: huge uncompressed sheets
- Risk: animation states that never end

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as 2D platformers, and you can manage the risks, Sprite Animation is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, pack frames into one sprite sheet to reduce draw calls.
