Where to Go Next After Learning Data Quality Checks

You have met the basics of Data Quality Checks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Data Lakes, which belongs to the same area as Data Quality Checks (Data and Analytics) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of null checks, uniqueness, freshness or schema validation, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around validating ingestion with Great Expectations, and note every problem you meet, starting with alerts nobody reads.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Data Quality Checks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
