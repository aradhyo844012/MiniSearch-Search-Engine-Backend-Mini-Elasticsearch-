Convolutional Neural Networks for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Convolutional Neural Networks.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Convolutional Neural Networks in your own words.
2. Describe filters, pooling, feature maps, and stride.
3. Give examples from image classification and medical image analysis.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Convolutional Neural Networks is neural networks that scan images with small filters to detect patterns. Then draw a magnifying glass sweeping across a photo looking for edges and shapes on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is too little training data a problem? How does pooling relate to feature maps? When would you choose PyTorch or TensorFlow?

Study habit: start from a pretrained model.

When you feel ready, compare Convolutional Neural Networks with Random Forests and write down three differences.
