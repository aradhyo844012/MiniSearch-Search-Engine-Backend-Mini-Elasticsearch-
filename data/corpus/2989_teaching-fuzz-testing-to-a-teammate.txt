Teaching Fuzz Testing to a Teammate

Explaining Fuzz Testing to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: shaking a machine hard to see what rattles loose. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Fuzz Testing is feeding programs random or malformed input to uncover crashes and security bugs. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving testing parsers, naming input generation and coverage guidance as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain crash triage in Fuzz Testing back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, running without sanitizers, and suggest this habit: keep every crashing input as a regression test.
