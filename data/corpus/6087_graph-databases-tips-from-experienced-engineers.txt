Graph Databases Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Graph Databases regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Graph Databases so the next person understands the decision.
2. Make sure you can limit traversal depth in every exploratory query.
3. Reach for Neo4j first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as Cypher.
4. Be careful with this one: using them for simple tabular data.
5. Test the case of social networks early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to recommendation engines as well.
