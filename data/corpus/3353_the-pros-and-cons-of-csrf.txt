The Pros and Cons of CSRF

No technology is free of trade-offs, and CSRF is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports protecting forms
- Makes securing account settings easier
- Helps with hardening admin panels

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: using GET requests for state changes
- Risk: skipping tokens on cookie-based APIs
- Risk: misconfigured SameSite settings

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as protecting forms, and you can manage the risks, CSRF is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, combine SameSite cookies with anti-CSRF tokens.
