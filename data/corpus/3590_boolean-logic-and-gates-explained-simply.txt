Boolean Logic and Gates Explained Simply

If Boolean Logic and Gates sounds intimidating, relax. It is the basic true-or-false operations that digital circuits and program conditions are built from, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Boolean Logic and Gates as light switches wired in series or in parallel. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: AND, OR and NOT, truth tables, De Morgan's laws, and logic gates. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with AND, OR and NOT, then add truth tables.

People reach for Boolean Logic and Gates when they work on writing conditions, designing circuits, and simplifying expressions.

One practical tip is to write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.

Once Boolean Logic and Gates feels comfortable, read about Memory Hierarchy, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
