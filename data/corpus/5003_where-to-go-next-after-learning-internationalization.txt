Where to Go Next After Learning Internationalization

You have met the basics of Internationalization. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about React Hooks, which belongs to the same area as Internationalization (Frontend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of locale detection, translation files, pluralization or date and number formatting, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around multi-language websites with i18next, and note every problem you meet, starting with hard-coded strings.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Internationalization to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
