3D Transformations Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with 3D Transformations.

Do:
- Learn translation before anything else.
- Try GLM on a small example.
- Make it a habit to write down the order of transformations before multiplying.
- Test the case of games early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: wrong multiplication order.
- Ignore this: gimbal lock.
- Ignore this: mixing coordinate systems.
- Adopt 3D Transformations just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with 3D Transformations, a little patience with rotation pays off more than any clever shortcut.
