Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Model Drift and Monitoring

This path is for a learner who has never studied Model Drift and Monitoring before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Model Drift and Monitoring is watching deployed models for changes in data or performance over time) and learn data drift and concept drift.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Evidently, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving catching degrading models.

Stage 3, depth: study performance metrics and alerts, then deliberately explore this problem: monitoring only system health.

Stage 4, application: apply Model Drift and Monitoring to scheduling retraining, compare Prometheus with Grafana, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Model Drift and Monitoring to someone else, and then move on to Model Deployment.
