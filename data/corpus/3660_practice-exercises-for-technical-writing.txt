Practice Exercises for Technical Writing

These exercises build real understanding of Technical Writing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick Markdown and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Compare Grammarly and Vale for design proposals and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: assuming too much knowledge. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble documentation or blog posts.
Exercise 5: Explain how examples and editing differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Technical Writing is explaining technical ideas clearly in writing for a chosen audience.
