Game AI Behavior Trees Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Game AI Behavior Trees.

Do:
- Learn selector nodes before anything else.
- Try Unreal Engine behavior trees on a small example.
- Make it a habit to visualize the tree while the game is running.
- Test the case of enemy behavior early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: trees that become tangled.
- Ignore this: expensive conditions checked every frame.
- Ignore this: no way to debug decisions.
- Adopt Game AI Behavior Trees just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Game AI Behavior Trees, a little patience with sequence nodes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
