Mistakes to Avoid with Regions and Availability Zones

Everyone learns Regions and Availability Zones partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: putting everything in one zone. It usually appears when someone skips regions and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: cross-region transfer fees. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of placing services near users. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting data residency rules. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on data residency to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: spread critical services across at least two zones.
