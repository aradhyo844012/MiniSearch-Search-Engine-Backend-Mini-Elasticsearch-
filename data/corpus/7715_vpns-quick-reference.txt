VPNs Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for VPNs. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: encrypted tunnels that connect devices or networks across the public internet.
Key terms: tunneling, encryption, authentication, and split tunneling.
Typical uses: remote work access, linking offices, and protecting traffic on public Wi-Fi.
Risks: assuming it makes you anonymous, slow routing through distant servers, and leaking DNS requests.
Tools: WireGuard, OpenVPN, and Tailscale.

Mental picture: a private tunnel under a public road.

Habit to build: test for DNS leaks after connecting.
