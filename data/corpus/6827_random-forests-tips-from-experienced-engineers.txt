Random Forests Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Random Forests regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Make sure you can tune the number of trees only after the basics work.
2. Reach for scikit-learn first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as XGBoost.
3. Do not skip bagging; everything else builds on it.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit feature randomness before anything else.
5. Be careful with this one: treating importance scores as causes.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to feature importance ranking as well.
