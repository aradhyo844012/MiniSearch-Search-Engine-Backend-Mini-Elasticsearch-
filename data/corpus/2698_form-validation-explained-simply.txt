Form Validation Explained Simply

If Form Validation sounds intimidating, relax. It is checking user input for correctness before it is accepted, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Form Validation as a proofreader checking a form before it is submitted. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: client-side checks, server-side checks, error messages, and constraint attributes. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with client-side checks, then add server-side checks.

You will meet Form Validation in work such as sign-up forms, payment pages, and search filters.

One practical tip is to validate on the server even when the browser checks too.

After Form Validation, a natural next topic is Service Workers.
