Where to Go Next After Learning Webhooks

You have met the basics of Webhooks. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Logging Best Practices, which belongs to the same area as Webhooks (Backend Engineering) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of event payloads, signature verification, retries or endpoints, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around CI triggers with ngrok, and note every problem you meet, starting with not verifying signatures.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Webhooks to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
