Raspberry Pi Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Raspberry Pi.

Do:
- Learn GPIO pins before anything else.
- Try Raspberry Pi OS on a small example.
- Make it a habit to use a quality power supply and a reliable memory card.
- Test the case of home automation early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: SD card corruption.
- Ignore this: inadequate power supplies.
- Ignore this: exposing the device without hardening.
- Adopt Raspberry Pi just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Raspberry Pi, a little patience with Linux pays off more than any clever shortcut.
