Skills to Build Around Stored Procedures and Triggers

Knowing the definition of Stored Procedures and Triggers is only the start. These skills turn that knowledge into something useful.

Foundations: be comfortable with procedures and triggers, and able to explain them without notes.

Hands-on practice: spend real time with PL/pgSQL on a Stored Procedures and Triggers task, and try at least one alternative such as MySQL Workbench.

Judgment: learn to decide when Stored Procedures and Triggers fits a problem such as enforcing rules close to the data, and when something simpler is better.

Communication: practice describing it with an everyday picture, for example a house rule that fires automatically whenever something is placed on a shelf.

Habits: keep triggers small and well documented, and write down what you learn after each project.
