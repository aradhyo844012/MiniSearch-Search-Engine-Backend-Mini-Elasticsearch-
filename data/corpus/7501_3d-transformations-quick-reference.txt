3D Transformations Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for 3D Transformations. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: the matrix operations that move, rotate and scale objects in 3D space.
Key terms: translation, rotation, scaling, and matrices.
Typical uses: games, modeling tools, and robotics.
Risks: wrong multiplication order, gimbal lock, and mixing coordinate systems.
Tools: GLM, three.js, and Blender.

Mental picture: moving a toy around a table by sliding, turning and resizing it.

Habit to build: write down the order of transformations before multiplying.

See also: Rasterization, which sits close to 3D Transformations in the area of Graphics and Multimedia.
