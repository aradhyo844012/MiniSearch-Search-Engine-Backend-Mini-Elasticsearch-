Semantic Versioning Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Semantic Versioning.

Do:
- Learn major, minor and patch numbers before anything else.
- Try semver.org on a small example.
- Make it a habit to write a changelog entry with every release.
- Test the case of publishing libraries early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: breaking changes in minor releases.
- Ignore this: overly loose ranges.
- Ignore this: forgetting to document changes.
- Adopt Semantic Versioning just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Semantic Versioning, a little patience with breaking changes pays off more than any clever shortcut.
