Single Sign-On Explained Simply

Here is Single Sign-On without the jargon: it is letting users log in once and access several applications.

Think of Single Sign-On as a festival wristband that opens every gate. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: identity provider, SAML, OpenID Connect, and session lifetime. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with identity provider, then add SAML.

You will meet Single Sign-On in work such as company intranets, reducing password fatigue, and centralized access control.

One practical tip is to plan for identity provider outages.

After Single Sign-On, a natural next topic is Zero Trust Security.
