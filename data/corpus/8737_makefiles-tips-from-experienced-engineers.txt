Makefiles Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Makefiles regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of compiling C projects early, before the design hardens.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit prerequisites before anything else.
3. Be careful with this one: tabs versus spaces.
4. Write down why you chose Makefiles so the next person understands the decision.
5. Do not skip targets; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to shortcuts for common commands as well as to Makefiles in general.
