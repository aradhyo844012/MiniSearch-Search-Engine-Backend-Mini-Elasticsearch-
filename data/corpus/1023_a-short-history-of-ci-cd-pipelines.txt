A Short History of CI/CD Pipelines

Ideas behind CI/CD Pipelines developed gradually as engineers ran into practical limits and shared what they learned. Put simply, CI/CD Pipelines covers automated workflows that build, test and deliver code whenever it changes. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Automation turns risky manual steps into repeatable and reviewable processes.

The vocabulary of CI/CD Pipelines is small. Learn build stage, test stage, artifacts, and deployment stage, and most documentation will start to make sense. Sketching deployment stage on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.

A solid grasp of CI/CD Pipelines also makes Site Reliability easier to understand.

With steady practice, CI/CD Pipelines will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
