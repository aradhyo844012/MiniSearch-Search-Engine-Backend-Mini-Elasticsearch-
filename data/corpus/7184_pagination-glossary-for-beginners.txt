Pagination Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Pagination feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Pagination: splitting large result sets into smaller pages that clients request one at a time.
offset pagination: a core term, most visible in search results.
cursor pagination: a core term that beginners often confuse with page size.
page size: a core term that connects to stable ordering.
stable ordering: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
SQL LIMIT and OFFSET: a tool commonly used for activity feeds.

Come back to this Pagination list whenever documentation for keyset queries uses a word you do not recognize.
