Load Balancing: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Load Balancing?
A: The term Load Balancing refers to spreading incoming requests across several servers so no single server is overwhelmed.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: the balancer becoming a single point of failure; ignoring health checks; and session data stored only on one server.

Q: How does it relate to WebSockets?
A: Load Balancing and WebSockets often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Networking.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Load Balancing.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with round robin and health checks, then move on to sticky sessions and layer 4 and layer 7.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.
