Linear Regression: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Linear Regression?
A: The term Linear Regression refers to a method for predicting a number by fitting a straight line or plane to data.

Q: Why do developers use Linear Regression?
A: Common uses include predicting prices, estimating demand, and finding trends.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Linear Regression.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include scikit-learn, NumPy, and statsmodels.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.
