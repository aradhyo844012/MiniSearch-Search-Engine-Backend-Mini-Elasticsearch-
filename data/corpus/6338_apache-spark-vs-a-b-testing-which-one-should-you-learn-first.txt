Apache Spark vs A/B Testing: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Apache Spark or A/B Testing first. Both sit in the area of Data and Analytics, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Apache Spark is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines, and it is closely tied to DataFrames and transformations and actions. A/B Testing, by contrast, is an experiment that compares two versions of something to see which performs better, and it is closely tied to control and variant and random assignment.

Choose Apache Spark first if you care about large ETL jobs or machine learning on big data. Choose A/B Testing first if you care about testing page designs or comparing ranking algorithms.

Think of Apache Spark as a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers, and of A/B Testing as taste-testing two recipes on randomly chosen guests. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Apache Spark, avoid data skew. With A/B Testing, avoid stopping tests early.

A good habit for Apache Spark is to avoid collecting large results onto a single machine. A good habit for A/B Testing is to decide the success metric and sample size before you begin.
