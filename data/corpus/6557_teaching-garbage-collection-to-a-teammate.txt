Teaching Garbage Collection to a Teammate

Explaining Garbage Collection to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a cleaner who removes items nobody has touched in a while. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Garbage Collection is automatic memory management that finds and frees objects a program no longer uses. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving managed languages like Java and Python, naming reachability and generations as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain mark and sweep in Garbage Collection back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, creating huge numbers of short-lived objects, and suggest this habit: measure allocation rates before tuning collector settings.
