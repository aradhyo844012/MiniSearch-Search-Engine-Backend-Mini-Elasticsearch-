Debugging Secrets Management: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Secrets Management, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: secrets in source control, long-lived shared credentials, and no rotation plan.
Step 3: revisit vaults and rotation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use HashiCorp Vault or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Secrets Management in work such as database credentials come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, rotate secrets on a schedule and after every suspected leak. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
