Everything You Need to Know About High Availability Design

This page collects the essentials about High Availability Design in one place. In short, High Availability Design is designing systems so they keep working when parts fail.

Core concepts:
- redundancy
- failover
- health checks
- no single point of failure

Where it is used:
- online stores
- banking systems
- healthcare software

Common problems:
- hidden single points of failure
- untested failover
- correlated failures

Tools to know:
- load balancers
- Kubernetes
- Patroni

Remember this above all: a bridge with several cables so one breaking does not matter is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to list every component and ask what happens if it dies.
