Scaling Considerations for Hyperparameter Tuning

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Hyperparameter Tuning changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding search spaces and getting improving model accuracy right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for tuning on the test set and searching too many options, and measure with Optuna before changing anything.

Large scale: random search and validation scores move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around comparing algorithms at this stage.

Before you scale, try random search before anything fancier. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
