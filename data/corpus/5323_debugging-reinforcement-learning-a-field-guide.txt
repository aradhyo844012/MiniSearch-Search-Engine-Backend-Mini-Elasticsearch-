Debugging Reinforcement Learning: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Reinforcement Learning, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: badly designed rewards, slow training, and unstable results across runs.
Step 3: revisit agent and environment, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Gymnasium or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Reinforcement Learning in work such as game playing come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start with simple environments. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
