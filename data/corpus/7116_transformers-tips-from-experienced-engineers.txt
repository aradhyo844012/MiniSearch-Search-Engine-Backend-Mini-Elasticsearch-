Transformers Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Transformers regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Transformers so the next person understands the decision.
2. Test the case of language translation early, before the design hardens.
3. Make sure you can begin with small pretrained models.
4. Be careful with this one: huge compute requirements.
5. Reach for Hugging Face first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as PyTorch.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to text generation as well.
