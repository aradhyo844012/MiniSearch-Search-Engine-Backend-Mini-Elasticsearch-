Recommendation Systems for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Recommendation Systems.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Recommendation Systems in your own words.
2. Describe collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, ranking, and the cold start problem.
3. Give examples from streaming services and online shops.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Recommendation Systems is systems that suggest items a user is likely to want. Then draw a shopkeeper who remembers what similar customers liked on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is feedback loops a problem? How does content-based filtering relate to ranking? When would you choose Surprise or implicit?

Study habit worth keeping: evaluate with held-out interactions, not training data.

When you feel ready, compare Recommendation Systems with Responsible AI and Bias and write down three differences.
