MLOps Pipelines Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with MLOps Pipelines regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip data versioning; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose MLOps Pipelines so the next person understands the decision.
3. Test the case of repeatable model releases early, before the design hardens.
4. When something behaves strangely, revisit training pipelines before anything else.
5. Make sure you can version both the code and the data.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to tracking experiments as well as to MLOps Pipelines in general.
