Teaching Content Security Policy to a Teammate

Explaining Content Security Policy to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: a guest list at the door of a building. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Content Security Policy is a browser feature that limits which scripts and resources a page may load. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving reducing the impact of XSS, naming directives and nonces as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain report-only mode in Content Security Policy back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, allowing unsafe-inline, and suggest this habit: start in report-only mode to learn what would break.
