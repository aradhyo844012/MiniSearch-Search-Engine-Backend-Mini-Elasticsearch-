Practice Exercises for Developer Portfolios

These exercises build real understanding of Developer Portfolios. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble applying for internships or showing skills to recruiters.
Exercise 2: Compare Vercel and Netlify for tracking personal growth and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Developer Portfolios and include the term projects.
Exercise 4: Explain how live demos and clear descriptions differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Pick GitHub Pages and complete its getting-started tutorial.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Developer Portfolios is a collection of projects and writing that shows what a developer can build.
