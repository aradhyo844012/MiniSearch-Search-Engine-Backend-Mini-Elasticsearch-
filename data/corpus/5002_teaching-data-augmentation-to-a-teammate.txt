Teaching Data Augmentation to a Teammate

Explaining Data Augmentation to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: practicing a sport in different weather and on different fields. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving image models, naming transformations and label preservation as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain synthetic samples in Data Augmentation back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, changes that alter the label, and suggest this habit: augment only the training data.
