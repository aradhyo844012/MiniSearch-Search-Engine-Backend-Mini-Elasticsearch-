Scaling Considerations for Bluetooth Low Energy

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Bluetooth Low Energy changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding advertising and getting fitness trackers right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for ignoring connection limits and weak pairing security, and measure with nRF Connect before changing anything.

Large scale: characteristics and pairing move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around smart locks at this stage.

Before you scale, test with several phone models. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
