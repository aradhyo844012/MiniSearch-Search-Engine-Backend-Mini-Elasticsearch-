Image Compression Explained Simply

If Image Compression sounds intimidating, relax. It is reducing the size of image files by removing redundancy or less visible detail, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Image Compression as packing a suitcase by folding clothes tightly. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: lossy and lossless formats, quantization, transform coding, and quality settings. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with lossy and lossless formats, then add quantization.

People reach for Image Compression when they work on web images, photo storage, and sending pictures over slow networks.

One practical tip is to pick the format by content: photos versus flat graphics.

Once Image Compression feels comfortable, read about Color Spaces, which lives in the same neighborhood of ideas.
