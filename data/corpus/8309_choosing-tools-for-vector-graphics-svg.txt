Choosing Tools for Vector Graphics (SVG)

There is no single best tool for Vector Graphics (SVG). The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

Inkscape is a common first choice, especially for logos. SVGO suits teams that already work with similar technology, and Figma is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Vector Graphics (SVG) by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle icons well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Vector Graphics (SVG), test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: overly complex paths.

As a rule of thumb, set a viewBox so the image scales cleanly, no matter which tool you use.
