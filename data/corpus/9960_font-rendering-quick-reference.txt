Font Rendering Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Font Rendering. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: turning font outlines into readable text on screen.
Key terms: glyphs, hinting, anti-aliasing, and kerning.
Typical uses: web pages, operating systems, and PDF viewers.
Risks: missing font fallbacks, blurry text from scaling, and large font files.
Tools: FreeType, Google Fonts, and font inspector tools.

Mental picture: a calligrapher tracing letter shapes onto a grid of pixels.

Habit to build: subset fonts to the characters you need.
