Why Hexagonal Architecture Matters for Developers

Developers who understand Hexagonal Architecture make better decisions, because Hexagonal Architecture is a design that keeps core logic separate from outside systems through ports and adapters.

Three situations show why it matters. First, testable business logic. Second, swapping databases. Third, supporting several interfaces. In each case, a solid grasp of ports and adapters separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: too many layers for small apps, leaking framework types into the core, and confusing ports with adapters.

Good tools exist, including Spring, FastAPI, and pytest, so you rarely have to start from nothing.
