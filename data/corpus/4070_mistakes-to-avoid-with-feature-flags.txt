Mistakes to Avoid with Feature Flags

Everyone learns Feature Flags partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: never removing old flags. It usually appears when someone skips flag evaluation and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: flags that depend on each other. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of dark launches. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: untested flag combinations. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on flag cleanup to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: give every flag an owner and a removal date.
