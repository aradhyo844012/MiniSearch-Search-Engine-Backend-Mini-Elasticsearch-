Push Notifications vs Embedded Systems: Which One Should You Learn First?

Learners often ask whether to study Push Notifications or Embedded Systems first. Both sit in the area of Mobile, Embedded and IoT, so the honest answer is that it depends on your goals.

Push Notifications is messages sent to a user's device from a server even when the app is closed, and it is closely tied to device tokens and push services. Embedded Systems, by contrast, is computers built into devices to perform dedicated tasks within tight limits, and it is closely tied to microcontrollers and firmware.

Choose Push Notifications first if you care about order updates or chat alerts. Choose Embedded Systems first if you care about appliances or medical devices.

Think of Push Notifications as a courier who knocks on your door when a package arrives, and of Embedded Systems as a small brain inside an appliance that only knows one job. The two pictures are very different, which is a useful reminder that the topics solve different problems.

Both have traps. With Push Notifications, avoid asking for permission too early. With Embedded Systems, avoid memory leaks on tiny devices.

A good habit for Push Notifications is to ask for permission at a moment when the user understands the value. A good habit for Embedded Systems is to plan how firmware updates will be delivered from the start.
