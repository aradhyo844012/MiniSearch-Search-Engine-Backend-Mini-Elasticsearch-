Debugging Leader Election: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Leader Election, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: split brain, flapping leaders, and slow failure detection.
Step 3: revisit heartbeats and terms or epochs, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use ZooKeeper or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Leader Election in work such as database primaries come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, tune heartbeat intervals against real network delays. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
