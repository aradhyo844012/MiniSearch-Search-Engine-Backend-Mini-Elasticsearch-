Teaching Security Headers to a Teammate

Explaining Security Headers to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: safety instructions printed on the packaging of every page. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Security Headers is HTTP response headers that tell browsers to enforce extra protections. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving hardening websites, naming Strict-Transport-Security and X-Content-Type-Options as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain frame protections in Security Headers back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, setting headers on only some routes, and suggest this habit: test the headers on every environment.
