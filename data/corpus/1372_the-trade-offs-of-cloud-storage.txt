The Trade-offs of Cloud Storage

Every design choice has a cost, and Cloud Storage is no exception. The term Cloud Storage refers to storing files and objects on remote servers that scale on demand. This article explains the idea in plain language and points out where it fits in real projects.

On the positive side, Cloud Storage is well suited to backups and serving images. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: public buckets by mistake; surprise egress costs; no lifecycle rules. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much buckets and objects matter to your project.

Automate repetitive steps so that results are easy to reproduce. Practice buckets with small exercises until it feels natural. Ask a teammate to explain access policies back to you; teaching is a quick way to find gaps.
