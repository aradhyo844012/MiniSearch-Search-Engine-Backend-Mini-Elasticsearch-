Is Wearable Technology Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Wearable Technology, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Wearable Technology is small computing devices worn on the body, such as watches and fitness trackers.

It is a good fit when your work involves fitness tracking or notifications, and when your team understands sensors.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn low-power design, or when you cannot manage these risks: battery drain, inaccurate sensor readings, and privacy of personal data.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether companion apps and data syncing are part of the answer. If they are, Wearable Technology is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with health monitoring and design interactions that take only a few seconds.
