Is Data Augmentation Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Data Augmentation, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Data Augmentation is creating modified copies of training data to help models generalize.

It is a good fit when your work involves image models or text classification, and when your team understands transformations.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn label preservation, or when you cannot manage these risks: changes that alter the label, augmenting the test set, and unrealistic transformations.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether synthetic samples and augmentation pipelines are part of the answer. If they are, Data Augmentation is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with small datasets and augment only the training data.
