Debugging Environment Variables: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Environment Variables, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: committing secrets in dotfiles, variables not visible to child processes, and typos in names.
Step 3: revisit variable names and scope, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use env or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Environment Variables in work such as storing configuration come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, print a variable to confirm its value before blaming the code. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
