Everything You Need to Know About Collision Detection

This page collects the essentials about Collision Detection in one place. In short, Collision Detection is the process of finding out when objects in a game touch or overlap.

Core concepts:
- bounding boxes
- broad and narrow phases
- spatial partitioning
- collision response

Where it is used:
- platformers
- shooters
- physics puzzles

Common problems:
- tunneling through thin objects
- checking every pair of objects
- inconsistent hitboxes

Tools to know:
- Box2D
- Unity colliders
- quadtrees

Remember this above all: two people checking whether their personal space bubbles overlap is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to use simple shapes first and refine only when needed.
