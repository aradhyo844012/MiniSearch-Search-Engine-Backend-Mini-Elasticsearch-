Debugging CQRS: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around CQRS, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: adding it to simple apps, read models drifting out of sync, and duplicated logic.
Step 3: revisit commands and queries, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Axon Framework or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving CQRS in work such as high-read dashboards come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, start with a single model and split only when reads and writes truly diverge. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
