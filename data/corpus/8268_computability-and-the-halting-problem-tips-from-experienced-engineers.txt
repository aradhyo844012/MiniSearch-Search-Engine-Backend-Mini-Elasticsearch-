Computability and the Halting Problem Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Computability and the Halting Problem regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. When something behaves strangely, revisit decidability before anything else.
2. Test the case of understanding the limits of tools early, before the design hardens.
3. Do not skip Turing machines; everything else builds on it.
4. Make sure you can learn the diagonal argument step by step.
5. Be careful with this one: assuming everything is solvable.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to proofs of impossibility as well as to Computability and the Halting Problem in general.
