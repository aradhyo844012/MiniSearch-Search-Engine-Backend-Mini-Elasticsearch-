Digital Twins for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Digital Twins.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Digital Twins in your own words.
2. Describe sensor data, simulation models, synchronization, and analytics.
3. Give examples from factory monitoring and building management.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Digital Twins is virtual copies of physical systems that are kept updated with real data. Then draw a mirror image of a machine that moves when the machine moves on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is stale data a problem? How does simulation models relate to synchronization? When would you choose Azure Digital Twins or Unity?

Study habit: define the question the twin must answer first.

When you feel ready, compare Digital Twins with Blockchain Fundamentals and write down three differences.
