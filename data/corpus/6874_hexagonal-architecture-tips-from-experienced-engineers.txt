Hexagonal Architecture Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Hexagonal Architecture regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Hexagonal Architecture so the next person understands the decision.
2. Be careful with this one: too many layers for small apps.
3. Test the case of testable business logic early, before the design hardens.
4. Reach for Spring first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as FastAPI.
5. Do not skip ports; everything else builds on it.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to swapping databases as well.
