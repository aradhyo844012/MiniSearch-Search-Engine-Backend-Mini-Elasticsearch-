Mistakes to Avoid with Service Workers

Everyone learns Service Workers partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: caching stale files forever. It usually appears when someone skips registration and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: bugs surviving across deployments. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of offline support. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting that HTTPS is required. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on fetch handlers to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: version your caches so old files can be removed.
