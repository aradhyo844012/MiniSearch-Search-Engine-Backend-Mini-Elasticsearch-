Understanding Graph Theory Basics Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a map of towns and the roads between them.

In that picture, vertices and edges is the first rule you follow, degree is the next step, and paths and cycles and connectivity keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Graph Theory Basics is the study of nodes and the connections between them, and it involves details such as mixing directed and undirected graphs that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for NetworkX to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain social networks to a friend.
