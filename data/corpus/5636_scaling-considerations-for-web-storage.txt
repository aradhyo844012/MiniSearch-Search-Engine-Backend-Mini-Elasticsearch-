Scaling Considerations for Web Storage

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Web Storage changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding localStorage and getting saving user preferences right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for storing sensitive data and assuming storage is always available, and measure with browser DevTools before changing anything.

Large scale: cookies and IndexedDB move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around remembering form drafts at this stage.

Before you scale, wrap every read and write in error handling. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
