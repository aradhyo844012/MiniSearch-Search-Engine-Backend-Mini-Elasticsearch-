Mistakes to Avoid with Database Backups and Recovery

Everyone learns Database Backups and Recovery partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: never testing restores. It usually appears when someone skips full and incremental backups and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: storing backups on the same disk. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of recovering deleted tables. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: keeping credentials inside backup scripts. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on restore testing to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: schedule a restore drill on a regular basis.
