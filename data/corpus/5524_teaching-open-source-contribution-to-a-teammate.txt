Teaching Open Source Contribution to a Teammate

Explaining Open Source Contribution to someone else is one of the best ways to test your own understanding. Use this simple outline.

Start with a picture: helping repair a shared public library. Ask your teammate what the picture suggests.

Then give the plain definition: Open Source Contribution is helping improve public software projects by fixing bugs, writing documentation or adding features. Avoid jargon at this stage.

Walk through an example involving building a portfolio, naming forks and pull requests as they come up.

Ask your teammate to explain issues in Open Source Contribution back to you. Gaps in their explanation show you what to clarify.

Finish by pointing out a trap, opening huge pull requests, and suggest this habit: start with documentation fixes or small bugs.
