Practice Exercises for Managed Kubernetes Services

These exercises build real understanding of Managed Kubernetes Services. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Write a short paragraph describing Managed Kubernetes Services and include the term the control plane.
Exercise 2: List three situations from your own experience that resemble running containerized apps or scaling services.
Exercise 3: Pick Amazon EKS and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 4: Explain how upgrades and integrated load balancers differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 5: Compare Google GKE and Azure AKS for reducing operations work and write down your conclusion.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Managed Kubernetes Services is cloud services that run the Kubernetes control plane for you.
