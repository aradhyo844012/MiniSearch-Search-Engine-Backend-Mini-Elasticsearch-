Semantic Versioning Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for Semantic Versioning. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a numbering scheme where major, minor and patch numbers signal how much changed.
Key terms: major, minor and patch numbers, breaking changes, pre-release tags, and version ranges.
Typical uses: publishing libraries, declaring dependencies, and planning upgrades.
Risks: breaking changes in minor releases, overly loose ranges, and forgetting to document changes.
Tools: semver.org, npm semver, and release notes.

Mental picture: the edition number on a textbook that tells you whether you must relearn anything.

Habit to build: write a changelog entry with every release.

See also: Monorepos, which sits close to Semantic Versioning in the area of Developer Tooling and Build Systems.
