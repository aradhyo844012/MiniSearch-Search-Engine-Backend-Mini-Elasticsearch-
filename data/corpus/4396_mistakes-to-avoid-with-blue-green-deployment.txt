Mistakes to Avoid with Blue-Green Deployment

Everyone learns Blue-Green Deployment partly through mistakes. Here are three worth avoiding.

Mistake one: database schema mismatches. It usually appears when someone skips blue and green environments and jumps straight to implementation. The fix is to slow down and review the basics.

Mistake two: doubling infrastructure costs. It tends to be noticed late, often during testing of zero-downtime releases. A short checklist during review catches it early.

Mistake three: forgetting to drain old connections. It is easy to miss because everything seems to work at first. Keep an eye on database compatibility to spot it.

If you remember only one rule, make it this: keep the old environment running until the new one is proven.
