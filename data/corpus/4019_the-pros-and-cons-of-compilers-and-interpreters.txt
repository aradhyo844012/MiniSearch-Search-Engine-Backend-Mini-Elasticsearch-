The Pros and Cons of Compilers and Interpreters

No technology is free of trade-offs, and Compilers and Interpreters is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports running Python and JavaScript
- Makes building native programs easier
- Helps with creating tools like linters

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: assuming interpreted means slow without measuring
- Risk: ignoring compiler warnings
- Risk: confusing compile-time and run-time errors

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as running Python and JavaScript, and you can manage the risks, Compilers and Interpreters is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, turn on compiler warnings and treat them seriously.
