The Pros and Cons of React Native

No technology is free of trade-offs, and React Native is no exception. Here is an honest summary.

Advantages:
- Supports cross-platform apps
- Makes rapid prototypes easier
- Helps with sharing code with web teams

Drawbacks and risks:
- Risk: heavy re-renders
- Risk: poorly tuned lists
- Risk: relying on outdated native modules

Weigh the two lists against your own project. If the advantages match your needs, such as cross-platform apps, and you can manage the risks, React Native is a sound choice. Whatever you decide, test on real devices for both platforms.
