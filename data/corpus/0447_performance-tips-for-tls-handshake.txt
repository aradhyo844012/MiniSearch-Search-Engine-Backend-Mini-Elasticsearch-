Performance Tips for TLS Handshake

Fast software rarely happens by accident, and TLS Handshake offers several places to gain speed. Put simply, TLS Handshake covers the exchange that lets a client and server agree on keys and set up an encrypted connection. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

Start by measuring. Use OpenSSL or a simple timer to find out where time is really spent, rather than guessing. Pay special attention to certificates and cipher suites, because they usually decide how well things scale. For workloads such as securing websites, even small improvements add up quickly.

To understand TLS Handshake, start with certificates. Then move on to cipher suites, and finally look at session keys and certificate authorities. Sketching certificate authorities on paper before coding helps many learners see how the pieces fit.
