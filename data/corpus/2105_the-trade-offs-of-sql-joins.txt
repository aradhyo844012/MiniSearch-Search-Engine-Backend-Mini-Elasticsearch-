The Trade-offs of SQL Joins

Every design choice has a cost, and SQL Joins is no exception. Put simply, SQL Joins covers operations that combine rows from two or more tables based on related columns. Learning it well pays off across many areas of computing.

On the positive side, SQL Joins is well suited to linking customers to orders and building reports. On the other hand, it is not free. Keep these risks in mind: forgetting the join condition; duplicating rows by joining on non-unique keys; filtering in the wrong clause of a left join. Whether the trade is worth it depends on how much inner join and left join matter to your project.
