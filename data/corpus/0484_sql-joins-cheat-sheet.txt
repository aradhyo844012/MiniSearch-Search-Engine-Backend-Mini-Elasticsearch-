SQL Joins Cheat Sheet

Use this page as a quick reminder of the essentials of SQL Joins.

- Definition: operations that combine rows from two or more tables based on related columns.
- Core ideas: inner join; left join; join conditions; cartesian products.
- Use it for: linking customers to orders; building reports; finding unmatched rows.
- Watch out for: forgetting the join condition; duplicating rows by joining on non-unique keys; filtering in the wrong clause of a left join.
- Tools: PostgreSQL, MySQL Workbench, DBeaver.
- Quick tip: practice inner join until it feels automatic.
- Quick tip: write a tiny test for left join before building anything large.
