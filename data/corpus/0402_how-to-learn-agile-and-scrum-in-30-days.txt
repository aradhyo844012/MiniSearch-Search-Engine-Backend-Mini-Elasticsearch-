How to Learn Agile and Scrum in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Agile and Scrum. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Agile and Scrum, they usually mean iterative ways of planning and delivering software in short cycles. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn sprints and backlogs, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Jira on a small task such as student team projects.
Week 3: build a mini project around product development.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as skipping retrospectives, and explain Agile and Scrum to a friend.

A solid grasp of Agile and Scrum also makes Version Control Workflow easier to understand.

Popular tools and libraries include Jira, Trello, and GitHub Projects.

With steady practice, Agile and Scrum will stop feeling abstract and become a tool you reach for naturally.
