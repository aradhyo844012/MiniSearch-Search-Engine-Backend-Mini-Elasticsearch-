Practice Exercises for Feature Flags

These exercises build real understanding of Feature Flags. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: List three situations from your own experience that resemble dark launches or A/B tests.
Exercise 2: Pick LaunchDarkly and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 3: Write a short paragraph describing Feature Flags and include the term flag evaluation.
Exercise 4: Compare Unleash and OpenFeature for emergency kill switches and write down your conclusion.
Exercise 5: Explain how gradual rollout and flag cleanup differ, using a drawing.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Feature Flags is switches in configuration that turn features on or off without redeploying code.
