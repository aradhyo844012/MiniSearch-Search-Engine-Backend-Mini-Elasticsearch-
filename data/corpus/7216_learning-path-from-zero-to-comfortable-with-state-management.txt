Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with State Management

This path is for a learner who has never studied State Management before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (State Management is the practice of organizing where an application keeps and updates its data) and learn single source of truth and actions.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Redux, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving large single-page apps.

Stage 3, depth: study reducers and derived state, then deliberately explore this problem: putting everything in global state.

Stage 4, application: apply State Management to shared shopping carts, compare Zustand with Vue Pinia, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach State Management to someone else, and then move on to Web Storage.
