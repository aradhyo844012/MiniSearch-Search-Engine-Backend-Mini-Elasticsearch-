Is Twelve-Factor Apps Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Twelve-Factor Apps, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Twelve-Factor Apps is a set of twelve guidelines for building apps that deploy and scale cleanly.

It is a good fit when your work involves building web services or deploying to platforms, and when your team understands config in the environment.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn stateless processes, or when you cannot manage these risks: storing state on local disks, hard-coded config, and long start-up times.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether backing services and disposability are part of the answer. If they are, Twelve-Factor Apps is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with keeping environments consistent and keep config out of the code.
