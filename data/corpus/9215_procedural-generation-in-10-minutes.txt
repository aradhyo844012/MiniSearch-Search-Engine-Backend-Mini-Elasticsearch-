Procedural Generation in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Procedural Generation.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Procedural Generation is creating game content such as levels and terrain automatically with algorithms.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn random seeds, noise functions, rules and constraints, and reproducibility.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine a chef inventing endless dishes from a fixed set of ingredients. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through roguelike dungeons, infinite terrain, and varied item drops.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for levels that are unfair or unwinnable and repetitive results, then try Perlin noise for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to log the seed so any generated level can be reproduced.
