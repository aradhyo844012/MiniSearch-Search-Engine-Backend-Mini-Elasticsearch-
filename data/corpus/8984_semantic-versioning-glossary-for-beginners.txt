Semantic Versioning Glossary for Beginners

New words can make Semantic Versioning feel harder than it is. This glossary keeps the key ones in one place.

Semantic Versioning: a numbering scheme where major, minor and patch numbers signal how much changed.
major, minor and patch numbers: a core term, most visible in publishing libraries.
breaking changes: a core term that beginners often confuse with pre-release tags.
pre-release tags: a core term that connects to version ranges.
version ranges: a core term worth revisiting after you try a real example.
semver.org: a tool commonly used for declaring dependencies.

Come back to this Semantic Versioning list whenever documentation for npm semver uses a word you do not recognize.
