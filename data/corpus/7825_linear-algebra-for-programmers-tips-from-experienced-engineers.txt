Linear Algebra for Programmers Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Linear Algebra for Programmers regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Do not skip vectors; everything else builds on it.
2. Write down why you chose Linear Algebra for Programmers so the next person understands the decision.
3. When something behaves strangely, revisit matrices before anything else.
4. Test the case of 3D graphics early, before the design hardens.
5. Be careful with this one: mixing row and column conventions.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to machine learning as well as to Linear Algebra for Programmers in general.
