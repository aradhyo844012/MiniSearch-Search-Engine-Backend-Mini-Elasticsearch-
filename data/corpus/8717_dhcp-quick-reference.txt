DHCP Quick Reference

A one-screen reminder for DHCP. Bookmark it and return when the details fade.

What it is: a service that automatically gives devices IP addresses and network settings.
Key terms: leases, address pools, reservations, and discover and offer messages.
Typical uses: home and office networks, guest Wi-Fi, and booting machines over a network.
Risks: rogue DHCP servers, exhausted address pools, and conflicting static addresses.
Tools: dnsmasq, ISC Kea, and Wireshark.

Mental picture: a hotel desk that hands out room numbers for a limited stay.

Habit to build: reserve fixed addresses for servers and printers.
