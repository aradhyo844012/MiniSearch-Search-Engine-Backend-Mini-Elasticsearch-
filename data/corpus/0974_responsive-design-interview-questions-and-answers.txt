Responsive Design Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Responsive Design.

Q: Explain Responsive Design in simple words.
A: Responsive Design refers to building pages that adapt smoothly to phones, tablets and large screens. I would add an example involving websites used on many devices.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: testing only on one screen size; tiny touch targets; and horizontal scrolling.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: testing only on one screen size.

Q: What are the key concepts?
A: media queries, fluid grids, flexible images, and mobile-first thinking.

Q: How does it connect to Single Page Applications?
A: Both belong to the area of Web Development, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.
