Web Performance: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Web Performance?
A: The term Web Performance refers to the practice of making websites load quickly and respond smoothly.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with page weight and caching, then move on to lazy loading and critical rendering path.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: shipping large JavaScript bundles; unoptimized images; and too many network requests.

Q: How does it relate to Responsive Design?
A: Web Performance and Responsive Design often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Web Development.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with Web Performance.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Why do developers use Web Performance?
A: Common uses include shopping sites, news pages, and search interfaces.
