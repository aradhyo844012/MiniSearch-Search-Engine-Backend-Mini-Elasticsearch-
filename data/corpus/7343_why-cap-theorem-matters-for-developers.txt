Why CAP Theorem Matters for Developers

Developers who understand CAP Theorem make better decisions, because CAP Theorem is a result stating that a distributed system cannot guarantee consistency, availability and partition tolerance all at once.

Three situations show why it matters. First, choosing database types. Second, designing multi-region services. Third, explaining system behavior in interviews. In each case, a solid grasp of consistency and availability separates a quick fix from a lasting solution.

Ignoring it has a cost. Teams run into these problems: treating it as a three-way menu, ignoring latency trade-offs in normal operation, and assuming partitions never happen.

You will not need to build everything yourself; Apache Cassandra, etcd, and ZooKeeper cover most needs.
