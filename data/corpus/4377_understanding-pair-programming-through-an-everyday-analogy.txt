Understanding Pair Programming Through an Everyday Analogy

Abstract ideas are easier to hold onto when you attach them to something familiar. So picture a pilot and co-pilot who check each other's decisions.

In that picture, driver and navigator is the first rule you follow, rotation is the next step, and communication and shared ownership keep everything running smoothly.

Every analogy breaks down eventually. Real Pair Programming is two developers working together at one computer on the same code, and it involves details such as one person dominating that no everyday picture captures.

Use the analogy to remember the shape of the idea, and use documentation for VS Code Live Share to learn the exact behavior. The same picture helps when you explain teaching new teammates to a friend.
