How to Learn WebSockets in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with WebSockets. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about WebSockets, they usually mean a protocol that keeps a two-way connection open between a browser and a server. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn persistent connections and messages, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with Socket.IO on a small task such as chat applications.
Week 3: build a mini project around live dashboards.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as forgetting reconnection logic, and explain WebSockets to a friend.

Every web request crosses several layers of protocols before it reaches an application. Simple command-line tools can reveal a lot about what is happening on the wire.

In practice you can start with Socket.IO, then explore ws library or Python websockets as your needs grow.

Keep experimenting, keep notes, and revisit WebSockets whenever a new project gives you a reason.
