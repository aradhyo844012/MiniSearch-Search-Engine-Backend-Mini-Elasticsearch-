Scaling Considerations for Software Estimation

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Software Estimation changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding story points and getting sprint planning right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for treating estimates as promises and ignoring unknowns, and measure with planning poker before changing anything.

Large scale: uncertainty and velocity move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around setting deadlines at this stage.

Before you scale, give ranges instead of single numbers. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
