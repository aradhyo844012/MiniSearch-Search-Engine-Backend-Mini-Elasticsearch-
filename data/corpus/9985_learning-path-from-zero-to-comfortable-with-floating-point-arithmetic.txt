Learning Path: From Zero to Comfortable with Floating-Point Arithmetic

This path is for a learner who has never studied Floating-Point Arithmetic before. Move at your own speed.

Stage 1, the basics: read a short definition (Floating-Point Arithmetic is the way computers approximate real numbers with a fixed number of bits) and learn mantissa and exponent and rounding.

Stage 2, hands-on: install Python decimal, follow a tutorial, and build a small example involving scientific computing.

Stage 3, depth: study precision and special values, then deliberately explore this problem: comparing floats with equality.

Stage 4, application: apply Floating-Point Arithmetic to graphics, compare NumPy with IEEE 754 references, and write up your results.

Stage 5, review: teach Floating-Point Arithmetic to someone else, and then move on to Graph Theory Basics.
