Choosing Tools for Font Rendering

There is no single best tool for Font Rendering. The right choice depends on your project, your team and your timeline.

FreeType is a common first choice, especially for web pages. Google Fonts suits teams that already work with similar technology, and font inspector tools is worth a look when you need something different.

Judge any tool for Font Rendering by four questions: Is the documentation clear? Is the community active? Does it handle operating systems well? Can you leave it later if you need to?

Whichever tool you pick for Font Rendering, test it on a small problem first, and watch for this: missing font fallbacks.

As a rule of thumb, subset fonts to the characters you need, no matter which tool you use.
