Scaling Considerations for Mobile App Security

A design that works for a classroom project can behave very differently under real load. Here is how Mobile App Security changes as things grow.

Small scale: almost any approach works. Focus on understanding secure storage and getting banking apps right.

Medium scale: problems start to appear. Watch for storing tokens in plain text and hard-coded API keys, and measure with OWASP MASVS before changing anything.

Large scale: obfuscation and permissions move from background details to central design questions. Teams often revisit their choices around health apps at this stage.

Before you scale, store secrets in the platform keychain or keystore. It is cheaper to build that habit early than to fix a crisis later.
