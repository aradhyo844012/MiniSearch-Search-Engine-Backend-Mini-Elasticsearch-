Combining Data Lakes with Star Schema Modeling in One Project

Real projects rarely use one idea in isolation. This article shows how Data Lakes and Star Schema Modeling can work together in a single project.

Data Lakes is large storage systems that keep raw data in its original format until it is needed. Star Schema Modeling is a way of organizing analytics data into a central fact table surrounded by dimension tables.

Imagine a project that involves storing logs and events and business intelligence reports. Data Lakes brings raw storage, while Star Schema Modeling brings fact tables.

A sensible plan is to start with Amazon S3 for the Data Lakes part and dbt for the Star Schema Modeling part, and to keep the two sides connected through a small, well-tested interface.

Be prepared for combined risks: no catalog or ownership on one side, and over-normalized dimensions on the other.

Picture the whole as a big reservoir where water from many rivers is stored before treatment, working alongside a wheel with a hub of numbers and spokes of descriptions.

Two habits help: first, register every dataset in a catalog with an owner; second, state the grain of the fact table before designing anything else.
