Everything You Need to Know About Saga Pattern

This page collects the essentials about Saga Pattern in one place. In short, Saga Pattern is a way of managing long business operations across services using a sequence of local steps and compensating actions.

Core concepts:
- local transactions
- compensating actions
- orchestration
- choreography

Where it is used:
- order processing
- travel bookings
- payment workflows

Common problems:
- missing compensations
- steps that are not idempotent
- flows that are hard to trace

Tools to know:
- Temporal
- Camunda
- Apache Kafka

Remember this above all: booking a trip with separate hotel, flight and car reservations that can each be cancelled is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to make every step and its compensation idempotent.
