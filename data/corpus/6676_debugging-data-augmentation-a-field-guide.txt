Debugging Data Augmentation: A Field Guide

When something goes wrong around Data Augmentation, resist the urge to guess. Work through the problem in order.

Step 1: reproduce the problem with the smallest possible example.
Step 2: list the usual suspects: changes that alter the label, augmenting the test set, and unrealistic transformations.
Step 3: revisit transformations and label preservation, since misunderstandings there cause many bugs.
Step 4: use Albumentations or similar tools to look inside.
Step 5: write a test that fails because of the bug and passes after the fix.

Many bugs involving Data Augmentation in work such as image models come down to the first suspect on the list. Check the simple causes before suspecting anything exotic.

Finally, augment only the training data. It prevents a surprising number of repeat problems.
