Is Multi-Cloud Strategy Right for Your Project?

Before adopting Multi-Cloud Strategy, ask whether it fits the problem. Remember that Multi-Cloud Strategy is using services from more than one cloud provider on purpose.

It is a good fit when your work involves avoiding single-provider risk or meeting regional rules, and when your team understands portability.

Be cautious when the project is very small, when deadlines leave no room to learn vendor lock-in, or when you cannot manage these risks: doubling operational complexity, lowest-common-denominator designs, and hidden data transfer costs.

A simple decision test: write down the problem in one sentence, then check whether abstraction layers and cost comparison are part of the answer. If they are, Multi-Cloud Strategy is probably worth a closer look.

If you decide to go ahead, start small with using best-of-breed services and be clear about the problem multi-cloud is meant to solve.
