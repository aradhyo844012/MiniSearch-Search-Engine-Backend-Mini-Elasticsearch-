Debugging Strategies Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Debugging Strategies.

Do:
- Learn reproducing the bug before anything else.
- Try debuggers on a small example.
- Make it a habit to change one thing at a time and record the result.
- Test the case of fixing production issues early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: changing many things at once.
- Ignore this: guessing without evidence.
- Ignore this: not writing a test afterward.
- Adopt Debugging Strategies just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Debugging Strategies, a little patience with isolating the cause pays off more than any clever shortcut.
