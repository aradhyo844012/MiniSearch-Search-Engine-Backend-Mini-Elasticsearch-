Subqueries and CTEs in Real Projects

Reading about Subqueries and CTEs is useful, but the ideas make more sense in context. These three scenarios show how it appears in real projects.

Scenario one: breaking complex reports into steps. Here, subqueries drives the main design choices, and the team needs to guard against correlated subqueries that run slowly.

Scenario two: filtering with computed results. This time WITH clauses matters most, and tools such as PostgreSQL help keep things manageable.

Scenario three: walking recursive hierarchies. The key idea is correlated queries, and the biggest risk is deep nesting.

Across all three scenarios, Subqueries and CTEs stays the same idea: queries nested inside other queries or named as temporary result sets. Only the details change.
