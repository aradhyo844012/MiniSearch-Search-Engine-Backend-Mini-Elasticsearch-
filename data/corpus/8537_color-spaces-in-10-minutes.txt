Color Spaces in 10 Minutes

You have ten minutes, so here is the fast route through Color Spaces.

Minutes 1 and 2, the definition: Color Spaces is mathematical models for describing colors, such as RGB, HSL and CMYK.

Minutes 3 and 4, the vocabulary: learn RGB, HSL, CMYK, and gamma.

Minutes 5 and 6, the picture: imagine different ways of giving directions to the same destination. That is the intuition.

Minutes 7 and 8, the use cases: skim through web design, photo editing, and printing.

Minutes 9 and 10, the traps and the tools: watch for ignoring gamma and mixing color spaces, then try browser color pickers for a first experiment.

If you have a spare minute, remember to work in one color space and convert only at the end.
