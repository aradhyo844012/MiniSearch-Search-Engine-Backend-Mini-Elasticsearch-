Network Troubleshooting Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Network Troubleshooting regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Write down why you chose Network Troubleshooting so the next person understands the decision.
2. When something behaves strangely, revisit ping and traceroute before anything else.
3. Make sure you can work from the bottom layer upward.
4. Reach for ping first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as traceroute.
5. Test the case of fixing office connectivity early, before the design hardens.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to diagnosing slow apps as well as to Network Troubleshooting in general.
