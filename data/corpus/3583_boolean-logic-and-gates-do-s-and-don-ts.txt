Boolean Logic and Gates Do's and Don'ts

A quick list of habits for working with Boolean Logic and Gates.

Do:
- Learn AND, OR and NOT before anything else.
- Try Logisim on a small example.
- Make it a habit to write a truth table whenever a condition feels confusing.
- Test the case of writing conditions early.

Don't:
- Ignore this: operator precedence mistakes.
- Ignore this: forgetting short-circuit behavior.
- Ignore this: negating complex conditions incorrectly.
- Adopt Boolean Logic and Gates just because it is fashionable.

Most of the Don'ts come from rushing. When you work with Boolean Logic and Gates, a little patience with truth tables pays off more than any clever shortcut.
