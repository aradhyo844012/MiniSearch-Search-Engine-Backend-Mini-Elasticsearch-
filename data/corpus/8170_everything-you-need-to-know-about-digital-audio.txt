Everything You Need to Know About Digital Audio

This page collects the essentials about Digital Audio in one place. In short, Digital Audio is representing sound as numbers by sampling it many times per second.

Core concepts:
- sampling rate
- bit depth
- waveforms
- the Nyquist limit

Where it is used:
- music production
- voice calls
- podcasts

Common problems:
- clipping from too high a level
- aliasing at low sample rates
- mixing sample rates

Tools to know:
- Audacity
- SoX
- the Web Audio API

Remember this above all: taking rapid snapshots of a wave so it can be redrawn later is a good way to picture it, and the best habit is to keep recordings below the clipping level.
