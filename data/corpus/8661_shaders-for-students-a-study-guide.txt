Shaders for Students: A Study Guide

This study guide prepares you for exams, labs and interviews that cover Shaders.

Learning goals:
1. Explain Shaders in your own words.
2. Describe vertex shaders, fragment shaders, uniforms, and GLSL.
3. Give examples from lighting effects and water and fire visuals.
4. Name three problems to watch for.

Start with the definition: Shaders is small programs that run on the graphics card to control how things are drawn. Then draw a tiny painter that colors each pixel following your instructions on paper and label the four terms from the goals above.

Self-test questions: Why is branches that slow the GPU a problem? How does fragment shaders relate to uniforms? When would you choose GLSL or Shadertoy?

Study habit: start from a minimal shader and add one effect at a time.

When you feel ready, compare Shaders with Collision Detection and write down three differences.
