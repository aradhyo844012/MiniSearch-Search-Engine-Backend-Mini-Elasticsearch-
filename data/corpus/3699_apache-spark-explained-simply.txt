Apache Spark Explained Simply

If Apache Spark sounds intimidating, relax. It is a distributed engine for processing large datasets across many machines, and the idea is simpler than the name suggests.

Think of Apache Spark as a team of workers each processing a share of a huge pile of papers. Keep that picture in mind as you read on.

The vocabulary has four main pieces: DataFrames, transformations and actions, partitions, and lazy execution. You do not need to master them all at once. Start with DataFrames, then add transformations and actions.

People reach for Apache Spark when they work on large ETL jobs, machine learning on big data, and log analysis.

One practical tip is to avoid collecting large results onto a single machine.

After Apache Spark, a natural next topic is Data Lakes.
