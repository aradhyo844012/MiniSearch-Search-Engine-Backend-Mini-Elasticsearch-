A Practical Checklist for JWT

Use this checklist before you rely on JWT in a project.

1. Guard against this risk: skipping signature checks.
2. Confirm that your team agrees on what JWT should accomplish.
3. Measure results before and after any change.
4. Review the core ideas: header, payload and signature, claims, expiry, and signing keys.
5. Guard against this risk: very long expiry times.
6. Guard against this risk: storing tokens in insecure places.
7. Ask a teammate to review the work, focusing on claims.
8. Write at least one automated test that covers header, payload and signature.
9. Pick a tool to start with, such as jwt.io or PyJWT.
