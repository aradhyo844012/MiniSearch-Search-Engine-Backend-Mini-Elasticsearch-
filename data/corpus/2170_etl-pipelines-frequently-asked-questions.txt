ETL Pipelines: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is ETL Pipelines?
A: The term ETL Pipelines refers to processes that extract data from sources, transform it and load it into a destination.

Q: How does it relate to Data Visualization?
A: ETL Pipelines and Data Visualization often appear in the same project, and both belong to the area of Data Engineering.

Q: Which tools help?
A: Popular choices include Apache Airflow, dbt, and Python scripts.

Q: What should a beginner learn first?
A: Start with extraction and transformation, then move on to loading and scheduling.

Q: How long does it take to learn?
A: Most people grasp the basics in a few weeks of steady practice, then deepen their understanding by building projects.

Q: Do I need special hardware to practice?
A: No. A normal laptop and a few small experiments are enough to get comfortable with ETL Pipelines.

Q: What mistakes are most common?
A: Watch for these: silent data loss; no retry logic; and poor error reporting.
