Containers Interview Questions and Answers

Prepare for these questions about Containers.

Q: Explain Containers in simple words.
A: Containers refers to lightweight packages that bundle an application with its dependencies and run in isolation. I would add an example involving consistent development environments.

Q: When would you avoid it?
A: I would avoid it when the problem is small and a simpler approach works, or when this risk is hard to manage: running as root.

Q: What problems have you seen in practice?
A: running as root; bloated images; and storing data inside the container.

Q: How does it connect to Linux Command Line?
A: Both belong to the area of Operating Systems, and understanding one makes the other easier to learn.

Q: Which tools have you used?
A: Docker, Podman, and containerd.
