Where to Go Next After Learning Topological Sorting

You have met the basics of Topological Sorting. Here is how to keep the momentum going.

Go wider: read about Union-Find, which belongs to the same area as Topological Sorting (Algorithms in Practice) and often appears in the same projects.

Go deeper: choose one of directed acyclic graphs, in-degree counts, Kahn's algorithm or cycle detection, and study it until you can explain it to a beginner.

Go practical: build something around course prerequisite planning with Python graphlib, and note every problem you meet, starting with ignoring cycles.

Go social: share what you learned. Explaining Topological Sorting to others, and hearing their questions, is the quickest way to find the gaps in your knowledge.
