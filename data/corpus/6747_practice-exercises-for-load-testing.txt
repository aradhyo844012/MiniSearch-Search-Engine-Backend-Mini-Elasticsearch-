Practice Exercises for Load Testing

These exercises build real understanding of Load Testing. Do them in order, and write down your answers.

Exercise 1: Pick k6 and complete its getting-started tutorial.
Exercise 2: Explain how latency percentiles and ramp-up differ, using a drawing.
Exercise 3: Create a small example that deliberately triggers this problem: testing unrealistic traffic. Then fix it.
Exercise 4: List three situations from your own experience that resemble capacity planning or finding bottlenecks.
Exercise 5: Write a short paragraph describing Load Testing and include the term virtual users.

When you finish, compare your answers with the definition: Load Testing is measuring how a system behaves under expected and heavy traffic.
