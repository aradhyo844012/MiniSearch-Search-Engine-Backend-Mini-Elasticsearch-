How to Learn Refactoring in 30 Days

You do not need months to become comfortable with Refactoring. A focused plan of thirty days is enough to build a solid base. When people talk about Refactoring, they usually mean improving the structure of existing code without changing its behavior. It appears in almost every serious software project, whether or not developers notice it.

Week 1: learn small steps and tests as a safety net, and write a one-page summary.
Week 2: practice with IDE refactoring tools on a small task such as reducing duplication.
Week 3: build a mini project around preparing for new features.
Week 4: review common mistakes such as refactoring without tests, and explain Refactoring to a friend.

Popular tools and libraries include IDE refactoring tools, pytest, and linters.

In short, careful study of Refactoring pays off. Spend time on small steps and tests as a safety net, and the rest will follow.
