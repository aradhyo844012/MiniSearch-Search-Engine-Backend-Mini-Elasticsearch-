Event Sourcing Tips from Experienced Engineers

Engineers who work with Event Sourcing regularly tend to repeat the same advice. Here it is.

1. Test the case of audit trails early, before the design hardens.
2. Do not skip event log; everything else builds on it.
3. Reach for Apache Kafka first, and only then evaluate alternatives such as EventStoreDB.
4. Write down why you chose Event Sourcing so the next person understands the decision.
5. When something behaves strangely, revisit projections before anything else.

None of these tips needs special equipment, and all of them apply to financial ledgers as well.
