CORPUS ANALYSIS

This graph illustrates the relationship between the frequency of terms and their rank in the MiniSearch document collection. A small number of terms occur frequently, while many terms appear less often.

Term rank Term frequency
Understanding the distribution

The downward trend indicates that a relatively small number of terms occur frequently, while most terms occur less often. This is consistent with a Zipf-like distribution in natural language.
